# Primary Capital Market 

## Meaning :
It is a market of newly issue securities. So, primary market is called market of newly issued securities. The investors buy only newly issued securities as it is a market for new issued securities. Primary market means a market for selling new securities in order to raise capital fund.

## Characteristics :

(1) It is a market for newly issued securities.
(2) New securities are sold and investor can buy.
(3) There are numerous intermediaries in primary market like book running lead manager, registrar of issue, sharebroker, etc.
(4) New capital is issued through prospectus in primary market.
