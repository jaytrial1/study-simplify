# Difference between Formal and Informal Communication

| No. | Points | Formal Communication | Informal Communication |
| :--: | :--: | :--: | :--: |
| 1. | Meaning | Communication system which <br> depends on rules, regulations <br> and accomplishment of <br> business objectives, is known <br> as Formal Communication. | Communication system which <br> depends on human relation <br> and friendship is known as <br> Informal Communication. |
| 2. | Purpose | The purpose of formal <br> communication is control and <br> co-ordination. | The purpose of informal <br> communication is to give more <br> co-ordination and credibility to <br> the organisation. |
| 3. | Base | Due to rules and regulations, <br> exchange of informations <br> between two people takes <br> place. | Due to personal and friendly <br> relations, exchange of <br> informations between two <br> people takes place. |
| 4. | Control and <br> Supervision | It requires control and <br> supervision. | It does not require control <br> and supervision. |
| 5. | Flexibility | It is less flexible. | It is more flexible. |
| 6. | Time | It takes place at the work <br> place during working hours. | It takes place at any place or <br> during working hours. |
| 7. | Information <br> in advance | Here, sender and receiver of <br> information as well as form of <br> communication are decided <br> before hand. | Form of communication and <br> procedure are not decided <br> before hand. It takes place as <br> per time and situation. |
