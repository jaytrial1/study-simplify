# Difference between Marketing and Selling : 

| Sr. <br> No. | Point of <br> Difference | Marketing | Selling |
| :--: | :--: | :-- | :-- |
| (1) | Meaning | Marketing is an identification <br> of the needs of the consumer <br> through the process of creating <br> profit and consumer <br> satisfaction. | Exchange of goods or service <br> for money is known as selling. |
| (2) | Area of <br> Work / <br> Scope | The area of work is very wide <br> which includes understanding <br> the requirement of consumer, <br> develop new products, <br> determining price and provide <br> after sale service. | The area of work is limited <br> which includes the transfer of <br> ownership of products from <br> the seller to the consumer. |
| (3) | Objective | Earning profit through <br> customer satisfaction. | Earning profit by selling <br> products and services. |
| (4) | Parties | Suppliers of product, <br> middlemen involved in sale <br> distribution, consumers are the <br> parties involved in marketing. | Buyers and sellers are the <br> parties involved. |
| (5) | Beginning <br> and End | Marketing process begins with <br> market research and goes till <br> after sales service. | Sales starts after production <br> and ends with providing goods <br> or service to consumer. |
| (6) | Need for <br> Capital | Storing products, gradation, <br> packing, labeling, transportation <br> of goods requires considerable <br> working capital. | Due to the restricted area of <br> operation, working capital <br> requirement is less. |
| (7) | Direction <br> of Efforts | Efforts are made in <br> manufacturing the product as <br> per the need of consumer and <br> making them reachable to <br> consumer. | Making efforts towards <br> preparing consumers to accept <br> the existing product. |
