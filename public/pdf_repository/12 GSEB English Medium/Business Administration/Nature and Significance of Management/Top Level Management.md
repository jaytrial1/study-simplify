# Top Level Management
The top level management is the supreme authority for the management of an enterprise. Board of Directors, Managing Directors, General Manager, Chief Executive Officers are included in this level.

Important policy decisions of business unit are taken by top level management. It is known as Top Level or Higher Level of Management.

## Functions : 

(1) To lay down primary and subsidiary objectives of business.
(2) Directors act as the trustees of the business enterprise.
(3) To select Chief Executive Officer and higher officers and to assign them authority and responsibility.
(4) To sanction the budget prepared by different departments.
(5) To abide by law and to take care of interest of different stakeholders of the business.
(6) To take strategic decisions by making long term plans.
(7) To analyse and resolve complex problems of management as per legal provisions.
(8) To plan for the enterprise, its implementation and to supervise it.
(9) To do the functions like distribution of profit, dividend, reserve fund, re-investment of profit, etc.
(10) To analyse reports of different activities and to give instructions accordingly.
