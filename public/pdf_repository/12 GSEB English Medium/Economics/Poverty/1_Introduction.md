# Understanding Poverty

Poverty is a situation in an economy where a segment of the population is unable to fulfill their minimum basic needs. In many developing economies, characterized by low per capita income and significant income inequality, a substantial portion of the population struggles to maintain a healthy life due to a lack of essential resources such as nutritious food, clothing, and adequate housing.

## The Dichotomy of Wealth

Conversely, affluent and resourceful segments of society enjoy high incomes and elevated standards of living. This economic disparity within a country can lead to dissatisfaction, unrest, animosity, and class conflict.

## The Relationship Between Economic and Human Development

According to the Human Development Report (HDR) 1997, economic development is a resource, while human development is the ultimate goal. This implies that economic progress should ideally translate into a reduction in poverty. True development, therefore, necessitates addressing income inequality and poverty.

## Poverty Eradication in India's Five-Year Plans

Poverty eradication has been a central objective in several of India's Five-Year Plans. Notably, the Fifth Five-Year Plan and subsequent plans introduced specific programs and schemes aimed at poverty reduction and alleviation. However, despite these efforts, poverty levels have not decreased to the targeted extent.
