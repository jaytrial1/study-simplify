3_Meaning, Characteristics and Limitations of Economic Development

We need to understand what Economic Development actually means, especially as the economies of Asia, Africa, and Latin America have become proactive in achieving it. Economic Development is a continuous process aimed at achieving economic well-being. Hence, the term "economic development" has a very wide meaning, which includes economic growth, economic welfare, and economic progress.

Even in Economic Development, there is an increase in real national income and real per capita income. However, economic development is not just about quantitative changes, but also about qualitative changes. Development is different from growth; it is a multidimensional process.

During the process of development, the economic and social structure of the economy changes. Along with economic progress, progress takes place in society too. Generally, the structure of national income also undergoes a change. The contribution of agriculture as a percentage of the total national income decreases, while the contribution of industry and the service sector increases. The disguised unemployed in the agricultural sector find employment in other sectors. Modern technology is implemented, saving time and money. New seeds are innovated, and as a result, the structure of institutions pertaining to production and distribution changes. Poverty, unemployment, and inequalities fall due to such systematic changes.

# Definitions

## Definitions of Economic Development

### Michael Todaro

"Economic Development is a multidimensional process."

### G.M. Meier

"Economic Development is such a process in which there is no increase in the population living below the poverty line, the distribution of income does not further increase the inequalities and there is a continuous rise in the real per capita income of the country for a long period of time."

### Machlup

"Economic Development is a process which increases the factors of production and creates change in the technique of production due to which per capita income continuously increases and the standard of living continuously rises irrespective of whether population remains constant or increases."

# Characteristics

## Characteristics of Economic Development

### Economic Development is a Continuous Process

Economic Development is a slow but strong process in a predetermined order and direction. In any country, development is a process which is easy to start but difficult to maintain. The process of development is usually fast initially but slows down over a period of time.

### Quantitative and Qualitative Change Takes Place

In Economic Development, output increases which is quantitative, and due to research, the quality of the product improves which is qualitative. But there is more of qualitative improvement.

### Change in Demand

There is a rise in the income of the people due to development, which changes their taste. In the initial stages of development, the demand for basic or primary goods rises, but subsequently, there is a rise in the demand for comforts and luxury goods.

### Labour Becomes More Dynamic

Development leads to an increase in the education of labor, which makes them more dynamic.

### Increases Capital Formation

Development leads to an increase in demand for different commodities. New enterprises come forward, due to which the rate of investment and capital formation increase enormously.

### Change in Technology

There is a shift in dependence from fire and water-based technology to coal & iron-based technology, which results in faster development.

### Self-Motivating Development

After a particular stage, development becomes self-motivating.

# Limitations

## Limitations of the Concept of Economic Development

### Limited Scope

Economic development indicates the progress of a nation. It shows the economic scenario, but in a real sense, it cannot discuss human development. It cannot become the index of human progress.

### Difficult to Measure

Economic development cannot be measured as economic growth. It is very difficult to measure economic development because it includes those changes that have happened in the society. It is very difficult to derive a measurement for this.

### Standard of Living May Not Improve

When economic development takes place, the standard of living of people improves. Though economic development is taking place in India today, there is not much improvement in the standard of living of the people, and therefore we cannot say that economic development means improvement in the standard of living.
