2_Importance of Industrial Sector

Industrialization is necessary for the rapid growth of the economy, the development of the agriculture sector, the generation of more employment opportunities, the optimum utilization of internal resources, a quick increase in income, and the improvement of people's living standards. Therefore, it can be said that to overcome serious issues like unemployment and poverty in developing economies like India, industrialization is a must. Industrialization is not only necessary for economic development, but also for social and political stability. This can be proven from the factors mentioned below.

# Contribution in National Income

## Contribution Details

At the time of independence, the agriculture sector dominated the economy, which gradually reduced with the development of industries. Due to planning efforts, the share of industries has increased in national income, but it is still not enough. In 1951, the industrial sector contributed 16.6% to the national income, which rose to 27% (at constant prices) in 2013-14. Based on this, it can be said that the contribution of the industrial sector has increased in national income. It is also noticeable that the industrial sector has played an extraordinary role in the growth of the service sector.

# Employment

## Employment Opportunities

India is a highly populated nation, where the entire labor supply is not being utilized in productive activities. This indicates that a serious issue of unemployment is being created due to a lack of enough employment opportunities. Planned efforts have increased employment opportunities in the industrial sector. In 1951, 10.6% of laborers were employed in industries, which rose to 24.3% in 2011-12. Small-scale industries in the industrial sector generally use labor-intensive production techniques; therefore, an increase in small-scale industries could substantially increase employment.

# Export Income

## Earning Foreign Exchange

Similar to the agriculture sector, the industrial sector can also increase its production volume and generate a surplus in the economy. These surplus items can be exported to earn foreign exchange. This foreign exchange is very useful to import scarce items into the economy. In 2013-14, industries earned about 2/3 of the share of export income. Thus, the industrial sector of the economy does not only satisfy the requirements of citizens, but it also earns foreign exchange from exports of surplus production. Thus, it indirectly helps in satisfying the requirements of other items in the economy.

# Balanced Economic Development

## Achieving Balanced Development

The industrial sector is very useful in attaining rapid and balanced economic development. Along with the development of the economy, there is a rise in the demand for primary commodities. In addition to this, excess income remains as savings. This increases the demand for luxurious and entertainment products. This demand can also be satisfied by industries. In addition, the government can also establish several public sector enterprises in less developed or backward areas to increase employment and income in order to make speedy and balanced development of the economy.

# Modernization of Agriculture

## Providing Technology to Agriculture

Modernization of agriculture is needed to increase the development of the agriculture sector and to increase land and labor productivity. Industries can provide technology to help the agriculture sector. Tractors, threshers, submersible pumps, equipment to spray pesticides, etc., and such other modern instruments can be provided by the industry. Even chemical-based fertilizers, pesticides, etc., are also produced by industries. Finally, it can be said that by using the modern technology of manufacturing industries, the development of agriculture could be made possible.

# Strengthens Economic Structure

## Building a Solid Structure

The industrial sector is essential to creating a strong solid structure of the economy. The industrial sector produces products like steel (Iron), cement which are useful to build irrigation projects, roads, bridges, etc. In addition to that, it provides vehicles of transportation such as buses, trucks, railways, planes, cars, two-wheelers, etc. which strengthen the economy. Even instruments of safety protection (Rifles, bullets, tanks, etc.) are also produced by the industrial sector which reduces dependence on other nations and makes India much stronger economically.

# Change in Social Structure

## Emergence of New Industrial Culture

Due to industrialization, a new industrial culture emerged. Due to which new values like discipline, hard work, competition, team work, self-dependency, co-operation, understanding, innovation skills, and institutional capability gets developed and stands against superstitions, fatalism, narrow psychology, orthodox behavior gets reduced. These types of social changes inspire economic development.
