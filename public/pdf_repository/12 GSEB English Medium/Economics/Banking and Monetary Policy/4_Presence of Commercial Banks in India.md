4_Presence of Commercial Banks in India

# Overview of Commercial Banks in India

Commercial banks in India operate in both the public and private sectors.

## Post-1991 Reforms

Following the economic reforms of 1991, foreign banks began entering the private sector.

## Other Financial Institutions

Besides scheduled commercial banks, there are also:

*   Financial companies functioning like banks in the unorganized and organized money markets.
*   Co-operative banks.

# Definition of Banks in India

According to the official definition, only companies listed in the 2nd schedule of the RBI Act, 1934, are considered scheduled banks and are recognized as the "real banks".

## Regulatory Framework

All rules and regulations of the Reserve Bank of India apply to these scheduled banks.
