# Economic Reforms of 1991

Major economic changes were brought about in 1991, which are identified as economic reforms. Liberalisation, privatisation, and globalisation have changed the Indian economy completely, and as a result, the Indian economy is being considered one of the fastest-growing economies in the world. Migration and urbanisation are considered gifts of the development process in the country, and this is not an exaggerated statement.

# Infrastructural Facilities

In the same way, infrastructural facilities are said to be prerequisites for economic development and hence are directly related to migration and urbanisation. Infrastructural facilities are a combination of facilities essential for the economic development and economic prosperity of a country.
