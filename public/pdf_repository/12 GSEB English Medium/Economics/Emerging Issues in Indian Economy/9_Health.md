# Meaning and Importance of Health

The WHO - World Health Organisation has given the definition of health in this way.

## Definition

Health is not just the absence of diseases or physical strength, but an individual's physical, mental and social well-being.

The productivity of a laborer is dependent on their health. A sound mind can be in a sound body. Education nurtures the mind while health nurtures the body.

National Income is directly dependent on the public health of the country.
If the health of the laborer is not good and if they fall ill again and again it adversely affects production and productivity. If the health of the laborer improves, automatically there will be growth in production. Improvement in health leads to an increase in economic growth in three ways.

(1) Rise in productivity leads to a rise in output.
(2) Natural resources can be better utilized and wastage of resources can be stopped.
(3) Rise in incomes of labor, leads to higher standards of living.

There are two aspects necessary for good health: (1) balanced diet (2) good medical treatment.

The average life expectancy of the people in a country is an important indicator of people's health. In 1951, the life expectancy of an average Indian was 32 years which rose to 63.5 years in 2011 is due to development of balanced diet, nutritional food and good treatment. Similarly, the infant mortality rate was 146 in 1951 which fell to 44 in 2012.

# Health Situation

Even today $70 \%$ of India's total population live in villages, but out of the total number of hospitals, only $1 / 5$ th are situated in rural areas and thus required medical services are not available in rural areas. There is a vast difference between the medical services in rural and urban areas.

There is a shortage of specialized treatments in rural areas as specialists are not available like Child specialists (Pediatricians), specialists for women (Gynaecologists) anaesthetists, eye specialists (Ophthalmologists) like M.D., M.S and such highly qualified doctors. Good treatment and timely treatment is not available for them.

A healthy child cannot be born to an unhealthy mother. In India due to a lack of nutritional food, $50 \%$ of females between the age of 15 and 49 suffer from iron deficiency creating anaemia, out of whom $19 \%$ die.

This problem can be eliminated by the spread of education, expansion and spread of health services. The World Bank Report shows that India spends $4.4 \%$ on health, out of the gross domestic product, while America spends $20.3 \%$ and China $12.5 \%$.

The government is consistently increasing the expenditure on health. There is a direct relationship between health and country's economic development and thus the government can build a healthy nation by expanding and extending health services to rural areas.
