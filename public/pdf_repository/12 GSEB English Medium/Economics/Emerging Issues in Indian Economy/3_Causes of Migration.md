3_Causes of Migration

After studying the types of migration, to understand migration in a better way, it becomes necessary to study its causes. We can explain migration under four heads:

# Economic Causes

The main reason for migration is economic. Economic reasons are:

## For Employment, Occupation, and Business

When a person moves for employment, occupation, or business to another place.

## Transfer

When a person is transferred from one place to another distant place, he is forced to move to that place.

## Extent of Natural Resources

When a particular place has an abundance of natural resources, but relatively the population is low in that area, people migrate to that place. e.g., gold, diamond, metallic mines, petroleum products, mining, and refining require technicians in large numbers. So people migrate to such places leaving their village or native place for this purpose. e.g., Migration to countries like UAE, Australia, New Zealand, Canada, etc.

## To Attain Better Quality Education

When educational opportunities are limited in any particular place and a person has a hunger for better education, he migrates to a distant place which, in due course, becomes permanent in nature.

## To Get Modern Health Services

When a person cannot acquire required health services in his own native place, he is forced to migrate to a better place for acquiring those services.

## Planned Migration

When a family plans to send one or more persons away from their native place for performing economic activity to a distant place, it can be called planned or organised migration.

# Social Causes

Along with economic factors, social factors also play an important role in migration, which are:

## Marriage

After marriage, when a woman leaves her native place and migrates to live in a distant place with her husband, it is known as social migration.

## To Get Respite From Social Rituals

A society in a village is traditional and orthodox to a great extent, while urban society has liberal thinking and a modern lifestyle. The youth of the villages get attracted to such a lifestyle and like to settle in urban areas to escape from the rigidity and rituals of rural areas.

# Political Causes

There are two political factors which can be considered for migration:

## War and Unrest

In whichever area war takes place time and again, there will be unrest in that area and people of that place will prefer to migrate to another area where they need not have to live in constant fear and insecurity and can live a peaceful and secured life. So they migrate from a disturbed place to a peaceful place.

## Avoid Friction

Wherever rioting and friction occur, people will prefer not to stay there and they will migrate to a peaceful place.

# Natural Calamities or Environmental Factors

The places which are affected frequently by natural calamities like famine, earthquakes, volcanoes, etc., see large-scale migration of the people in search of safety.

Due to developmental activity, the migration that takes place is also included in migration due to environmental reasons. e.g., the migration that happened because of the Sardar Sarovar Yojana in Gujarat can be called developmental migration. Likewise, if a National Park or afforestation is adopted, people are made to move to different areas, which is also known as developmental migration.
