# Railways Worldwide

World over, railways is considered to be a revolutionary development in the field of transportation. The British Government developed railways in India for their own benefits. Indian Railway was started during British Rule on April 16, 1853, between Bombay and Thane, a distance of 22 miles (approximately 34 kilometers).

## Railways in India

After Independence, the administration of railways came into the hands of the Indian Government and is administered through a separate department.

Today, the spread of the railway network is number one in Asia and number four in the entire world. Railway is India's biggest public enterprise, and today more than 1.4 million people are employed by it.

In 2012, 8200 million passengers traveled, and 970 million tonnes were transported by railways.

## Role of Railways in India's Economic Development

The development of railways has played an important role in India's economic development because:

(1) Industrialization and business have become faster because of occupational dynamism, which is due to the faster movement of heavy machines.
(2) Regional dynamism of labor has improved due to the increase in speed, safety, and comfort levels of travel. This has resulted in an increase in labor supply to industries.
(3) The development of railways has resulted in the commercialization of agriculture, and it also enables the transportation of fertilizers, tools, and other inputs from distant places to agriculture, and the agricultural produce is transported to the market.
(4) India's foreign trade has developed through the development of railways.
(5) A new sector has developed, which is tourism, entirely due to the development of railways.
(6) Railways have acted as a link for national unity and integrity.

Thus, it can be said that Indian Railways, as an infrastructural facility, has contributed to the development of agriculture, industry, and the service sector, due to which the entire economy has attained development. Hence, in every five-year plan, encouragement is being given to the development of railways and its modernization, and the following aspects were taken care of:

(1) More modernization is undertaken in every plan to speed up gauge conversion.
(2) More facilities are being created to make rail travel safer.
(3) Modernization of railway stations is also being undertaken for providing various facilities.
(4) Railway compartments are also being modernized to make passengers feel more comfortable during their travel.
(5) More and more electrification of railways is taking place so that travel can become faster.
(6) Through modernization, the work of increasing the speed of the train has already started so that the time taken to reach the destination for passengers and goods can be reduced.
(7) Talgo and bullet trains are forms of modernization of trains.

## Challenges Faced by Indian Railways

Despite all these efforts, railways face a number of challenges like:

(1) Modern technology is still insufficient.
(2) Railway facilities are still insufficient in relation to their demand.
(3) Shortage of finance and problems of administration.
(4) Problems of shortage of facilities for travelers.
(5) Regional imbalance in the development of railways, etc.
