# Importance of Petroleum

Petroleum can be considered an important source of energy and a driving force for the movement of all vehicles. Modern technology depends on petroleum for production. However, India is dependent on imports because the production of petroleum is relatively limited in the country.

## Rising Demand

The demand for petroleum is rising greatly because of rapid industrialization. Transportation is increasing rapidly, resulting in more private and public vehicles, which continuously increases the demand for petroleum.

# Petroleum Production in India

In India, oil reserves were first found in Assam. Realizing the importance of petroleum for economic development, ONGC (Oil and Natural Gas Commission Limited) was set up in 1959. Later, the Government converted it into a corporation to intensify the exploration of crude oil.

## ONGC's Role

Through ONGC, petroleum resources have been found in Kadi, Kalol, Ankleshwar, etc., in Gujarat, and in Bombay High, where an offshore drilling platform has been erected. Despite these efforts, India's contribution to petroleum production is just 0.4% of the total.

## Limited Reserves and Alternative Sources

The extent of world production at present and a continuous rise in demand shows that petroleum reserves are only enough to meet the demand for a limited number of years. Hence, countries worldwide have started thinking seriously in terms of alternative energy sources. Research programs are being undertaken in this direction, and India has also started encouraging research.

# Natural Gas

Natural gas is also considered to be of petroleum origin, predominantly used in thermal power stations, as cooking gas, and as fuel for running vehicles. The total gas production in India is only 0.5% of the total gas production of the world.

## Environmental Benefits

Using more natural gas can reduce environmental pollution to a remarkable extent. Hence, it should be used increasingly in electricity production and for vehicle operation. The use of gas is considered environmentally friendly.
