5_Types of Money

Since the time of the barter system, animals or precious stones were used as the means of exchange and storage of value. Thereafter, metal coins came into existence, which were followed by government-regulated notes and currency coins. This was succeeded by the banking system, credit cards, and the e-banking system. The types of money are:

# Commodity Money

# Animal Money

# Metal Money

# Paper Money

# Plastic Money

# Banking Money (Invisible or E-Money)
