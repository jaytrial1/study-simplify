1_Introduction

Money is at the center of all economic activities in a modern world. To satisfy their needs, an individual requires money to buy goods and services. Money is not only the medium of exchange, but it is also used to store value. People save money for their future needs.

# Human Wants and Exchange

Human wants are unlimited, and a person cannot satisfy all wants by their own produce. Thus, a person tries to satisfy the maximum possible wants by way of mutual exchange of goods and services.

## Barter System

In ancient times, human beings used to satisfy their wants by exchanging goods and services among themselves. Such a system of exchange for satisfying wants was called a barter system.
