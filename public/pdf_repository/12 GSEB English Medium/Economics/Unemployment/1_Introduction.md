# Global Economic and Social Challenges

In recent years, most nations worldwide have faced economic and social problems such as poverty, unemployment, inflation, and depression. The world is concerned about these issues. Each nation has attempted to solve these problems in its own way, but they persist. Developed and developing countries have achieved economic growth but have struggled to attain full employment. As a result, unemployment has become a global problem.

## Unemployment in India

In India, unemployment is a long-term issue. Consequently, plans prioritize the removal of unemployment. However, despite economic development, the problem of unemployment has worsened instead of declining. This highlights a limitation of our planning system.

### Factors Contributing to Unemployment

In developing India, a high population growth rate coupled with insufficient economic development has made the problem of unemployment more serious.
