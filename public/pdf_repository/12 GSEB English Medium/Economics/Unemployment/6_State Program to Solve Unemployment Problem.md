6_State Program to Solve Unemployment Problem

In 1951, five-year plans started in India. It was thought at that time that with the steps of planning and economic development, the problem of unemployment could be solved, but in the first four five-year plans, this thought proved to be wrong. As a result, from the fifth five-year plan, various employment-oriented schemes like the Integrated Rural Development Programme, Food against Work, Jawahar Rojgar Yojana, Nehru Rojgar Yojana, Suvarna Jayanti Gram Swarojgar Yojana, Suvarna Jayanti Sheher Rojgar Yojana, and Training to Rural Youth for Self-Employment were started to solve the problem of unemployment. The National Rural Employment Guarantee Programme, MGNREGA, Skill Development Programme, Skill India, Make in India and Mudra, and many employment-oriented schemes were implemented. Some of them are as below:

# Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act (MGNREGA)

On February 2006, the National Rural Employment Guarantee Act was started with the objective of providing employment to rural people residing in backward districts of the country. The name NREGA was changed to MGNREGA on 2nd October 2009. To make this programme successful, the government declared 2nd February as "Employment Day". In this programme, at least one person from each family is guaranteed employment for 100 days a year.

$1 / 3$ rd employment is reserved for females under this scheme. It was recommended to provide minimum wages for physical labour. Moreover, labourers should be provided wages within seven days. Labourers should be given employment within 5 km from their residence. If labourers are given employment beyond that distance, then $10 \%$ extra wages are given to them. Labourers working under this scheme are provided job cards which are valid for five years. After receiving a job card, if the labourer does not get employment, then he is paid an employment allowance.

# Pandit Deendayal Upadhyay Shramev Jayate Yojana (PDUSJY)

This scheme was started on 16th October 2014. Some of the objectives of this scheme were to provide health and security along with good management, skill development and welfare to the labourers of the unorganized sector and to develop a conducive environment for industrial development.

# Deendayal Upadhyay Gramjyoti Yojana (DUGJY)

Instead of the earlier Rural Electrification programme, this programme started with the objective of providing constant $24 \times$ 7 electricity services in rural areas.

# Deendayal Upadhyay Gramin Kaushalya Yojana (DUGKY)

This scheme was started on 25th September 2014. The main objective of this programme was to provide employment to youth between the age group of 18 to 35.

# Prime Minister Agricultural Irrigation Programme

This programme was started on 1st July 2015 with the objective of "Water to every field" to increase field productivity, optimum use of available resources and planning of irrigational facilities to agricultural areas.
