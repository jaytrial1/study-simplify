## Time Series Graphs

In a trade cycle or in cycles of economic activities, short term and long term regular or irregular (erratic) changes are observed. The trends of such changes are obtained with the help of statistical tools; and when graphs are drawn taking these changes as variable and time period as base then such graphs are called time series graphs.
