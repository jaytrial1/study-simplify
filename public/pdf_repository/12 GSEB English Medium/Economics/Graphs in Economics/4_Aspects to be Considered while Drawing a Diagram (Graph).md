4_Aspects to be Considered while Drawing a Diagram (Graph)

While making diagrams and graphs, the following aspects must be considered, as the purpose of drawing pictures is to make analysis simpler and more effective:

# Key Considerations

## Choice of Diagram/Graph and Presentation

The right type of diagram or graph must be chosen to make the picture more effective. Certain data can be presented in a bar diagram as well as in a pie diagram. The user must make the right choice to make the picture more effective.

## Clarity

A picture must look neat and clear. Different colors or shades may be used to denote the various components of a picture.

## Scales and Measures

To make a picture look appropriate in size, scales must be taken in accordance with the data.

## Representation of Axes

Both axes of a diagram or graph must be denoted/represented with appropriate details of what they represent.

## Data Table and Source of Data

Diagrams and graphs must be accompanied by the data table from which they are created. By mentioning the source of data, the picture becomes more reliable and authentic.

## Method of Calculating the Data

When the picture is not drawn from self-explanatory data but is drawn from data simplified with statistical formulae, it is appropriate to state the statistical method briefly.
