8_Internet Technology

Internet is another facility created by digital technology, which all of us have used from time to time. The use of the internet in economics can be summarized in the following chart.

# Uses of the Internet in Economics

## Tutorials

Some educational websites put PowerPoint presentations and study material along with worksheets on open access links. We can view such presentations as tutorials.

## Active Learning

Some videos of lectures by experts are put on open access sites by some educational institutions; some institutions create live lectures available to students. We can register on these sites and listen to a lecture on the net like we do in our classroom. For example, lectures by experts are available in economics and other subjects.

## Reading Material

Numerous books are available online free of cost for reading. Some good research articles, copies of journals, etc., are also available for reading. Some articles, books, and journals can be accessed by paying annual fees on those websites. These materials are called e-books, e-journals, etc.

## Information

By merely searching, we can get information on universities offering degrees in economics or any other information about any subject.

## Miscellaneous

We can get quotes by economists and names of reference books on the internet by using a search engine.

## Data

Economics uses a lot of secondary data. For instance, we use data on the budget of a government, banking, agricultural production, value of exports and imports, poverty, employment, industrial production, and many others. This data can be accessed from authentic websites. For example, an authentic site for data on banking in India is the official website of the Reserve Bank of India; for exports and imports, it is the website of the Ministry of Commerce and Industries of the Government of India; budget-related information from the Ministry of Finance, Government of India, and so on. Some organizations and agencies functioning at national and international levels also collect, publish, and put data on websites. For example, CSO, NSSO, WHO, UNO, CMIE, ILO, IMF, World Bank, and so on. By authentic data, we mean data published by recognized research institutes after doing comprehensive research.

# Caution in Using the Internet for Education

Like a computer, the internet is also only a tool. It cannot replace books and teachers or our own thinking and reasoning. A lot of substandard material, irrelevant information, misleading information, and plagiarized articles are found on the internet. We must avoid using those. Readers must have the wisdom to identify authentic material on the internet. Only authentic websites must be referred to; otherwise, we may end up getting misled.
