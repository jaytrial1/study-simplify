6_Measures to Control Population

# Mass Education and Awareness

It is necessary to make people realize the importance of small families to reduce the birth rate. For this, it is necessary to propagate and spread education, especially by making society aware through various programs. Use telecasts through visual media. In schools and colleges, expert lectures need to be arranged, and awareness can be created through plays, mimes, songs, etc. In the year 2000 population policy, emphasis was placed on women's upliftment. Demographers believed that education is the best method for population control.

# Effectiveness of Family Planning Programme

To make the family planning program more effective, along with public awareness, family planning services and incentives were also increased. It is essential to create simple, easy, and affordable availability of contraceptives. In the population policy of 2000, changes were made in the programs related to family planning by reducing the importance of sterilization and emphasis was placed on preventing unwanted pregnancy through preventive methods.

# Increasing the Age of Marriage and Raising the Status of Women

## Increasing the Age of Marriage

By raising the legal age of marriage, particularly for women, a reduction in the birth rate can be achieved. As per the population policy of 2000, encouragement was given to raising the age of marriage for women from 18 to 20 years if possible.

## Raising the Status of Women

If women's status is raised in society in comparison to men, and if equal opportunities are given to them in matters of education and employment, such women will control the family size.

# Incentives and Disincentives

The incentives and disincentives that are offered by the Government play a very important role in family planning. For instance, those couples who undergo sterilization are given financial compensation by the Government.

China has adopted disincentives or discouragement to control the rising population, in which a number of facilities are withdrawn from those couples who have two or more children. But recently, some relaxations have been given. In India, in the elections of local self-government, couples with more than two children cannot contest in elections.

# Expansion in Medical Services and its Growing Effectiveness

In India, the death rate has come down, but it is still higher than some of the developed countries. With the help of science, there has been an increase in the services and facilities for childbirth and the health of the newborn. Universalization and effectiveness of vaccination and to make it effective, awareness regarding communicable diseases like "AIDS" is to be increased; reduction in various infectious diseases and sex-related diseases, etc. can bring about a reduction in the death rate and infant mortality rate.

India was the first country in the world to introduce a population policy to control population. A committee was set up to frame the New Population Policy of 2000 under the Chairmanship of Dr. M. S. Swaminathan.

The various measures in the population policy will result in an increase in social welfare, which in turn will improve awareness against population growth.
