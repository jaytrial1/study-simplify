3_Meaning of Birth-rate and Death-rate

# Meaning of Birth-rate

The birth-rate depicts the number of children born for every 1000 people during the given year.

$$
\text { Birth-rate }=\frac{\text { The number of live-births during a given year }}{\text { Total population }} \times 1000
$$

Birth-rate is not depicted as a percentage, but it shows how much addition is taking place for every 1000 people. On the basis of birth-rate, the increase in the population is known, and the data on birth-rate helps in deciding the population policy.

## Birth-rate in India

Table 7.6 Extent of birth-rate in India

| Year | Birth-rate (per $\mathbf{1 0 0 0}$ persons) |
| :--: | :--: |
| 1951 | 39.9 |
| 2011 | 21.8 |

Source : Census of India, 2011

## Analysis and Conclusions

### Birth-rate Analysis

(1) In 1951, the extent of birth-rate in India was 39.9 which decreased to 21.8 (39.9 - 21.8 $=18.1$ difference) in 2011, which shows a slow reduction in the birth-rate. The main reasons for this are low level of education, preference for a male child, low income levels, etc.

# Meaning of Death-rate

The death rate depicts the number of people who die per every 1000 people during a given year.

$$
\text { Death-rate }=\frac{\text { No. of people who die in a given year }}{\text { Total population }} \times 1000
$$

The decrease in population can be understood from the death-rate. The reduction in the total population in a particular manner can be understood by measuring the total number of deaths in a year.

## Death-rate in India

Table 7.7 : The extent of death-rate in India

| Year | Death-rate (per $\mathbf{1 0 0 0}$ persons) |
| :--: | :--: |
| 1951 | 27.4 |
| 2011 | 07.1 |

Source : Census of India, 2011

## Analysis and Conclusions

### Death-rate Analysis

(1) The death-rate in India in 1951 was 27.4 which fell to 7.1 in 2011 (27.4 - $7.1=$ difference 20.3). The fall in death-rate is much higher than the fall in birth-rate. The main causes are control of famines, improvement in quality of life, nutritious food, increase and improvement in medical services, spread of education, improvement in medical science and research in the field of surgery, control of infectious diseases, etc.
