# International Trade

## Defining International Trade

The geographical boundaries of our country, like all countries, are determined. There are legal restrictions and sometimes prohibitions on crossing the geographical boundaries of a country. Then, have we ever thought about how we have a Korean refrigerator, a Japanese TV in our homes, German cars on our roads, American tractors on our farms, and UK-made shoes and garments in our malls? Do we know that the bulk of the petrol and diesel with which our vehicles run comes from Gulf countries?

## Overview of the Chapter

Hence, we will understand in this chapter that the activity of exchanging goods, services, technology, etc., by a country across its geographical boundary is called international trade. Besides, we will also understand some important aspects of international trade.
