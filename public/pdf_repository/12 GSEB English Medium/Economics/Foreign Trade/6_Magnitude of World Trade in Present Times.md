6_Magnitude of World Trade in Present Times

The World Trade Report 2013 highlights trends in world trade, drawing on the work of historian Angus Maddison.

# Historical Growth

## Population, Output, and Trade

Since the mid-1800s:

*   World population has grown approximately six-fold.
*   World output has grown 60-fold.
*   World trade has grown over 140-fold.

## Driving Forces

According to the report, significant reductions in transport and communication costs have been key drivers of the modern global trading system. Geopolitics has also significantly contributed to these trends.

# Recent Trends

## Trade Growth

Over the last 30 years, global merchandise and commercial services trade has increased by about 7% per year on average.

## Developing Economies

Between 1980 and 2011:

*   The share of developing economies in world trade rose from 34% to 47%.
*   Their share in world imports increased from 29% to 42%.
*   Asian countries are playing an increasingly important role in global trade.

## Trade vs. Production

For many decades, world trade has grown nearly twice as fast as world production (World GDP growth), reflecting the increasing importance of international supply chains and the value-added approach to measuring trade.
