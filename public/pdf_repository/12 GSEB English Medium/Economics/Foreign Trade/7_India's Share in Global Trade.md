7_India's Share in Global Trade

# Overview

India's share in world trade has remained low considering the size and growth of world trade. However, as a developing nation, India plays an important role in determining the direction of world trade. India's exports have grown significantly and are rising significantly as a percentage of GDP.

## Trade Growth

Though India's exports are also rising significantly, the growth rate of imports has been higher than the growth rate of exports. Imports also constitute a greater percentage of GDP than exports.

### Factors Influencing Trade

India is able to import more because the rising pace of growth and development has increased the demand for imports and the ability to import. India is able to export more because it is now able to produce better and desirable quality of goods at a competitive price and is able to generate more exportable surplus.

# India's Share in World Trade

The share of India's total trade in world trade in 2014-15 was about 2.07%.
