4\. BASES FOR MARKET SEGMENTATION :

Market segmentation means to divide a market into different groups of customers. There can be various criteria of market segmentation. If a market is divided into three groups such as child buyers, young buyers and aged buyers, the criterion used is age of the buyers. If a market is divided into two segments, one consisting of male customers and the other consisting of female customers, the criterion adopted is sex of the customers. Thus, there are different variables which can be used as criteria of market segmentation. These variables can be studied in the context of two categories of products:

(a) Consumer product and (b) Industrial product.

By consumer product we mean a product which can directly satisfy the wants of the final consumers. The examples are cloth, sugar, kerosene, talcum powder, vegetables, milk etc. Some of the consumer products last for a longer period of time. They are known as durable consumer products. The examples are refrigerators, washing machines, television sets, electric grinders etc. On the other hand, industrial product refers to that product which is used for further production in industries. Machines, equipment, raw cotton etc. are examples of industrial products.

Since only segmentation of the consumer market is included in the syllabus, we shall be dealing only with this in the following paragraphs.
