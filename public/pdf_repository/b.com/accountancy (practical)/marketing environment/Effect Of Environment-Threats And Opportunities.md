2\. EFFECT OF ENVIRONMENT-THREATS AND OPPORTUNITIES:\*

Whenever a marketing manager studies the environment, he is interested in knowing how the environment and firm are affecting each other. The firm and environment are so interconnected that any change in the environment will disturb the harmony of the inter relations. e.g. the workers of the motor-car industry, petrol pumps etc. are closely related to the sales of the firm manufacturing motor-cars. Now, if the situation changes and a new motor engine is found which works on electricity instead of petrol, then the industry dependent on petrol will be disturbed. Thus changing and changing the environment has definite notable effects on certain industries.

Because of such effects, environmental threats put certain industries into difficulties. If the firm is not prepared to face such threats, then it will be thrown out of business. The business which is prosperous at present; but does not adjust to new changes necessary to face environmental threats, then business may have to be closed. The 'Life' magazine of America got closed under the impact of Television. In the Indian car industry once the Fiat car was listed for 10 to 12 years. Now, the situation has changed so that the customers are not available for the same car though the same is sold on installments. Because of this certain American companies go for 'Threats Analysis continuously so as to make decisions about how to react to the threats.

According to Kotler "an environmental threat is a challenge posed by an unfavourable trend or specific disturbance in the environment which would lead, in the absence of purposeful marketing action, to the stagnation or demise of a company, product or brand."

Still however, it may happen that with the change in environment, the firm may get new opportunities and if the firm takes proper advantage of the same, then the firm may become more prosperous. At this stage let us clarify the concept of 'Marketing Opportunity'. Marketing opportunity means a challenge to take steps for the object oriented sales function, wherein there is a group of suitable environments and there is probability of success. Thus, marketing opportunity is considered when (1) Somebody presents the concept of a better alternative situation than the current situation. (2) Similarly, this type of alternative situation should be more attractive than the present situation. (3) The firm should have enough financial and other resources that it can take advantage of such an opportunity. When the firm takes advantage of such an opportunity it can run the business more profitably; not only that but the total social satisfaction increases.

Economical forces are the forces related to the nature and direction of the economy with which the firm is functional. It includes consumption pattern, level of savings, average income level, per capita income of the people, volume of foreign exchange etc. By keeping all these factors under consideration the marketing manager should plan marketing strategy.

Social forces affecting the firm include belief of people, social values, attitudes, opinions, life style, thinking pattern etc. Especially in the modern age the number of serving women increases, which may result in increased demand for washing machines, vacuum cleaner, grinding machine, refrigerator, ready spices etc.

The political environment provides threats and challenges for the companies.

e.g. because of liberalisation and privatisation, in many businesses the degree of competition has increased. Thus the government role affects the functioning of the company.

There is a notable effect of technological change on many companies.

There is a rapid development of new technology. If accordingly the strategies will not be changed, then there are chances that one after the other the companies may advance for closure. The marketing manager has to keep on studying all these matters.

Natural forces affect the business environment. Certain industries increase pollution. The pollution can be from air, water or noise. If it is not taken care of by the managers, then the business may be required to be closed down. The company has to plan a strategy to control the natural environment. Such a company can earn recognition from customers and society.

Global forces simultaneously raise threats and opportunities for industries.

In the international market India, China, Korea, Taiwan etc. countries have raised threats before American companies. American companies ignore international changes. e.g. American car manufacturing companies had ignored Japanese car manufacturing companies. As a result the Japanese companies controlled a large share of the small car market. Because of research at the international level, price reduction, quality improvement, new inventions etc. are encouraged.
