6\. FUNCTION OR SCOPE OF MARKETING MANAGEMENT 

(1) Market research: To make market research regarding the changing fashions, trends, customs, likes and dislikes of the customers, competition etc. is also one of the important functions coming under the scope of marketing management.

(2) Sales planning To decide the sales target on the basis of the estimate of sales of particular product in a particular market during definite future period and to organise different activities to achieve the targeted sales is one of the important functions of marketing research.

(3) Product decision: Market research decides product design, shape, size, colour, packing etc. according to the customers. It is known as a product decision.

(4) Inspecting raw material: After taking the product decision, necessary raw material is to be processed. Necessary inquiry and inspection is to be made regarding the quality, quantity etc. of the raw material.

(5) Pricing Pricing is a very important and difficult task various factors such as cost of production, competitors price, likes of customers their purchasing power, return on investment etc. have to be taken into consideration,

(6) Sales Organisation In order to undertake the various marketing activities efficiently to set up a sales organisation by creating different department in marketing department such as market research, department, advertisement department, sales promotion department, dispatch department, service department etc. is also one of the important functions.

(7) Determining channels of distribution Marketing management also performs the function of determining channels of distribution on the bases of type of product, types of customer size of market, product demand etc.

(8) Advertisement & publicity: To launch a new product or to make the customers aware about the product, advertisement and publicity play a very important role. In modern times advertisement a publicity has become inevitable.

(9) Sales promotion: Different sales promotion programmes such as advertisement, special discount offer, sales competition, prize distribution luck draw, sample distribution etc. are undertaken to attract the customers and increase the sales.

(10) After sales service Listening to customers grievances, and producing different after sales services is also one of the function of marketing management.

(11) Budgeting: The budget is prepared for various marketing activities so as to exercise control over them.

(12) Managing sales force It includes different functions such as appointment of salesmen, their braining motivation and guidance and control over item.

(13) Marketing strategy: Different policies such as pricing policy, credit policy, cash & trade discount policy etc. are to be decided.

(14) Market control: In order to undertake all the marketing activities effectively and efficiently control is exercised.
