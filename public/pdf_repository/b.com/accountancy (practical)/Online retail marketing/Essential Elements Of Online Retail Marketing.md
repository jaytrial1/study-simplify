5\. ESSENTIAL ELEMENTS OF ONLINE RETAIL MARKETING:

(A) Buyers/Consumers Point of View:

(1) Pricing:  
Price is the amount of money which funds the consideration for the transfer of legal title to a good or service. It is the first important element of online retail marketing. It plays a very important role. A lot of distribution costs are gone if consumers purchase online directly from the supplier or any distributor. If consumers invest some time looking out different websites for the same products, they can get amazing prices. E-consumers may go to various websites and know different prices of the same products and services.  
A lot of "Price comparison websites or shopping engines" are available to help consumers. Price comparison shopping engine is a vertical search engine that consumers use to filter and compare products based on price and other key features. Well-known price comparison websites like Google Shopping, Nextag, Amazon Product Ads, Shopping.com, Bizrate.com, etc.

(2) Ease of shopping:  
It is the second important element of online retail marketing. It is also an important element of online retail marketing for any e-consumerio A company's website should be very much user friendly. A consumer should not feel tension while surfing and searching for the products I. should be easy to operate. The process of purchasing is the same on every website. But few people provide very good interfaces and very good support at every stage so that the consumer feels very good about it. The way of presentation and the support services make shopping easy. From this point of view amazon.com, bluenile.com, etc. and very good websites.

(3) Security of Transaction:  
Recently every big brand has set up their own e-commerce sites. While big brands set-up e-commerce websites, they always keep the issue of security of transactions.  
A consumer should be taken in confidence that whatever information he is going to disclose for thje shopping, will be kept confidential and will not be disclosed. Security of transaction protection may result in consumer satisfaction. On other hand any leak in this information may result in huge financial loss to the consumer. Before any transaction, consumers look for various security certificates, their validity, certifying authority, etc.

(4) Guarantee of the Product:  
Guarantee of the product and quality of product are other important elements. Guarantee of the product appeals to consumers emotional triggers like security and trust. Consumers should know all the features of a product which they are going to purchase through e-commerce. It is advisable to check and ask for the guarantee and warranty of the product before placing the order from the distributor who is selling it online.

(5) Return Policy:  
Good return policy inspires confidence in consumers and shows distributors are committed to consumer service. Consumers return products when they are disappointed with their purchase.  
Return policy is very important if the product is costly. Consumers like the websites with precisely defined return policies. If the products distributors are selling have informative descriptions and proper pictures (or even product video), they greatly reduce the number of returns.

(6) Customer Care Services:  
Customer care services are the backbone to any online retail marketing for e-commerce business. They are the mouth piece of any online web store.  
This is the front which can decide which website is better in the present scenario. Almost all the websites have good security, all have good quality of products, but the point where one scores over the other is the class of customer service. Consumers like websites with chat support and very less turn around time for query posts.

\[B\] Seller/Retailer/Distributor Point of View:

(1) Website:  
A website is the first and most necessary element of any online retail marketing campaign. A website should be consumer oriented. The main objective of a website should be to convert visitors into paying consumers.  
A website should be user friendly and attractive also. It covers all the details regarding company, products, purchase, return policy, conditions etc. It shows the features and benefits of products and services in a clear way.  
Some websites have an opt-in box where visitors can sign up for a free sample or newsletter while other sites just sell products directly.

(2) Design Matters \[Web Design\]:  
Design matters is the second important element from a seller point of view. Attractive web design has always played a key role in successful online sales and marketing. That is because it increases the perceived value of products and works to make business website seem more trustworthy  
Look good, feel good is true. It is applicable in web designing also. The design of the website is most important, don't be sloppy. Success of company depends on it.

(3) Strategic Contents:  
Strategic content means creating content that drives the visitor to a specific goal. Each and every page on the company website should have one goal.  
Objectives of company could be a number of things:  
(i) Purchase of product  
(ii) Sign up for Email updates  
(iii) Download a document  
(iv) Watch a video  
(v) Click a link  
If these objectives are fulfilled by visitors, it is the success of the company. Strategic content tells visitors what to do with company' website and, more importantly, how they can do business with company D or distributors.

While making content on home pages and web pages following issues must be taken into consideration:  
(i) Each and every page on the website should have one goal.  
(ii) Each word of content should have a purpose.  
(iii) Make content easy to read.  
(iv) Keep paragraphs short.  
(v) Do not use one web page to sell products. Keep more than ones web page.  
(vi) All the contents should be clear and visible.

(4) Give Things Away:  
Giving things away is another important issue. Give people a sample of product or service. This does not have to be anything physical, but give people your knowledge. When distributors give people free content that solves their problems and also help them understand company clearly which ultimately leads to the sale.

(5) Make it Simple (Simplification\]:  
Simplification is the important element of any online retail marketing. Recently, time has come to make it simple. It is necessary that the content and marketing materials of the company are working together. It avoids confusion during online shopping by e-consumers.  
When consumers are confused, they are faced with too many decisions and too much information, they do nothing. The company may suffer a lost prospect. On the other hand, consumers are not confused, they are not faced with too many things, they are buying. It is clear that time to simplify.

(6) Teach Visitors / Users:  
If distributors actually want to sell their products and services online, concentrate on delivering value to visitors or users. Teach them what you know. Selling online is about education and clear communication. Sell benefits and big ideas at distributors via the internet.

(7) Nurture, Support and Encourage:  
If a distributor has used strategic content to get their visitors to take specific action, they must now follow-up. In marketing, the word “nurturing list" is the very first time few people are going to buy your website.  
The distributor will get a lot of leads. People who will gladly give distributors their email address in exchange for valuable information.  
What distributors do with these leads is nurture them, support and encourage them. Distributors do this with email marketing.  
Nurturing distributor list means they send content geared towards solving your consumer's problems.

(8) Search Engine Optimisation (SEO):  
Search engine optimization is not difficult, but it is time consuming.  
Google is always with you and helps you. Find keywords that best suit your business and start working on them. Build content around these search terms and spread the word. Ranking in Google is time consuming. You have to earn it. There is no easy way in.

How fast you climb Google depends on a number of factors  
(i) Strength of your corapetition  
(ii) Quality of the content on your website  
(iii) Number and quality of back links pointing back to your website  
(iv) On-page SEO and clear code.  
So, it is clear that SEO is the important element that needs to be considered if a distributor wants people to find their website. It is one of the most cost effective marketing strategies.

(9) Make Search Engine (i.e. Google) Happy:  
If distributors want more traffic and sales, they need to rank better on Google's search page. This includes SEO and keyword optimisation, but there are more distributors that can improve the quality of websites on Google. It is necessary to improve your page load times. To improve page load times, write good HTML code, compress image files and take advantage of caching plugins if distributors are running their site on a content management system.   
If distributors do not have a Google Local Listing, make one. Pay attention to Google and do the things required to expand your Google reach.

Social media is the last and important element of online retail marketing. It is a very popular element. It is a computer and internet based tool that allows people to create and share information, ideas, pictures, videos in virtual communities and networks.
