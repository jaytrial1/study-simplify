8\. ADVANTAGES OF ONLINE RETAIL MARKETING :\*

Now, let us discuss the points regarding the benefits of online retail marketing.

(A) The Benefits to Companies or Distributors or Organisations or Producers: \*\*

(1) Easy access to market:  
For entrepreneurs it is not easy to access the market. They can easily access the market by using online market places. Such as Amazon and e Bay allow anyone to set up a simple online shop and sell products within minutes.  
An online marketplace is a website where small businessman can sell products in return for a fee or commission.

(2) Cost Control:  
Selling online can remove the need for expensive retail premises and customer-facing staff and allow businesses to invest in better marketing and customer experience on their e-commerce site.

3\) No necessity for shop or show-rooms:  
In online marketing, a business concern is not required to establish shops or show-rooms at different cities and hence, it can manage the whole business with a limited investment also.

(4) No necessity for holding the huge stock:  
In online marketing, it is not necessary to keep the whole stock in one place. He can entrust this responsibility to different agencies at different places. e.g. American company "amazon" does not require to keep a huge stock at one place and also it facilitates the customers purchasing from nearby shop.

(5) Enhancement in sale:  
On placing a web-site of the products on the internet, a company can get the customers from any corner of the world and he can attract the customers from the whole world. He can then achieve an increase of his sales and can earn a good amount of revenue.

(6) Facility of global market :  
Online retail marketing is managed through an internet and the internet is spread over the whole world. In this way a facility of world-wide market is available in online marketing. Such a huge world-wide market is known as a "global market." Company can easily enter in global merket. Company can expand his market beyond local customers very quickly by using online retail marketing. Company can focus on target audience globally. Company can put his website in a different languages and take advantage of different nations people.

(7) Development of business:  
The new business of any kind can be developed or can be introduced in the global market very easily.

(8) Providing good services:  
By using the website more information can be given to customers and better services can be offered before, during and after the sale.

(9) Customer intelligence:  
A company can target potential customers by identifying their different needs and getting insight into the customers. By that way, companies can know what kind of product / services, features, qualities, contents, delivery etc.

(B) The Benefits to Consumers / Users:

(1) Multiple choice:  
On looking at the websites of many companies, a consumer can select his item from the internet, keeping in view the multiple factors for selection. He can compare the items by considering its price, quality and get up.

(2) Economy in purchase:  
On looking at the web-sites of many distributors, a consumer can compare the prices of the items of his choice and can select one which is affordable to him.  
Consumers may go to various price comparison websites and he can easily get the price of the same product from different distributors. For e.g., Flipkart, amazon, snapdeal, etc. provide discount on various products and consumers get information about these products from these websites or also from price comparison websites.

(3) Saving of time:  
A consumer is not required to visit shops or show-rooms situated at different places. He can do this only by looking at the different websites on the internet. In this way, a consumer can save his time in searching a particular item.

(4) Best services:  
A consumer can become well-versed with the services before, during and after purchase given by different distributors and he can select the best distributor who can provide the best services and informations.

(5) Foreign items:  
By looking at the internet website, a consumer can book an order for an item available from the distributors in foreign countries.

(6) 24 hours service:  
By using the internet the distributors or companies can be contacted at any time on all the days. Thi s types of services are known as 24×7 selling or purchasing services.

(7) Chatting with member of company:  
There is also a facility of chatting with members of the company directly. It is useful in purchasing the products of a company.

(C) The Benefits to Community:

(1) Opportunities for employment:  
Increasing demand of online retail marketing has created the opportunities of employment in the new fields such as computer industries, internet service providers, website design, cyber-cafe, etc. The problem of unemployment in the community has been reduced to some extent.

(2) Increases in government revenue:  
e In online retail marketing a large number of selling transactions are performed and hence the growth of the government revenue also increases.

(3) New scope of business:  
From the community anyone can start his own website or start-ups. Sometime, people can set up a simple online shop in "amazon" or "e- Bay":

(4) Industrial and scientific progress:  
As the spread of online retail marketing has been extended globally, the different kinds of problems have evolved. For this purpose new techniques or researches are to be made in the corporate field. Further technological research is to be carried over for the field of internet technology.

3.9. DISADVANTAGES OF ONLINE RETAIL MARKETING:

In India, at present only 5 to 10% houses are having internet connections, while in America 60-70% houses are having internet connections. In India, therefore the spread of online marketing is too limited. But there are some other disadvantages of the online retail marketing also.

(1) Lack of personal contact:  
In online marketing business transactions are carried over an internet and people purchase simply by looking at the web-site. The buyer does not contact or know the distributor or seller and the distributor / seller does not know the buyer. The buyer suspects the authenticity of a distributor or seller. The buyer thinks that if the items happen to be defective, then there is a risk for its replacing by a new one.

(2) Web-site costs:  
Planning, designing, creating, hosting, securing and maintaining a professional e-commerce, web-site is not cheap, especially if distributors expect large and growing sales volumes.

(3) Infrastructure costs:  
There are huge infrastructure costs to establish proper online retail marketing structure. It includes costs like developing websites, tie-up with banks, logistics, warehousing facilities etc.

(4) Security and fraud:  
One of the most dangerous things in online retail marketing is hacking of systems and frauds. Sometimes, confidential data may be stolen by hackers and the entire system gets disturbed. The reputation of the business of the company / distributor could be totally damaged if he do not invest in the latest security systems to protect his website and transaction processes.

(5) Legal issues:  
Legal issues and complexities of e-commerce can prove problematic for distributors or companies. Moreover, one should also know the solution of such issues and additional information about the right of e-consumers.

(6) Lack of customer trust:  
It can be difficult to establish a trusted brand name, especially without a physical business with a track record and face-to-face interaction between consumers and sales staff. Companies need to consider the costs of setting up a good customer service system as a part of his online offering.

(7) Advertising costs:  
Good online retail marketing strategies demand a generous budget. A company should spend more amount on advertisements to reach scattered customers across the world.

(8) Problems of returning the goods:  
Usually, there is no provision of returning the goods or item once purchased and hence, people are reluctant to purchase through the internet. This creates a hindrance in the development of online retail marketing.

(9) Inexperience distributor or seller:  
We know that anyone can enter the on-line marketing and sometimes it may happen that an inexperienced seller may enter it who does not know the pros and cons of the items which he is selling. \-He does not possess the knowledge of even the internet website. Under this, the business fails to progress.

(10) Limited up to well-to-do persons:  
Since online marketing is based on the internet and the payment is also made through E-process, the common people and the poor cannot afford to buy through the internet.

There are a lot of consumers who are not proficient in using the internet and focusing only on online marketing can cause companies to lose these consumers. It is limited only up to the well-to-do persons.

(11) Constant monitoring:  
Online retail marketing depends heavily on information technology. Information technology is changing fast within a short period of time. So, it requires constant attention and monitoring to ensure that the marketing strategy of the company does not look out-of-date.
