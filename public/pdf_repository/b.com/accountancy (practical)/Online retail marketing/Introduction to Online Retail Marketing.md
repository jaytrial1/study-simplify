1\. INTRODUCTION:

In the 21st century, online retail marketing is becoming necessary and also a fashion. Businesses of all sizes are using online retail marketing to increase awareness of their company's goods and services. Online retail marketing allows the smallest firm to compete in a global market.

Online retail marketing is defined as the sale of goods and services to the consumer market over the internet. It is a collective name for marketing activity carried out online, as opposed to traditional marketing. Compared to traditional methods of marketing, online retail marketing offers realistic costs. It also offers accurate targeting and excellent reporting. It is important for small and medium size firms and start-ups.

Online retail marketing means many things; it can include email campaigns, search engine optimisation (SEO), online advertisements, webinars, virtual events and blogging.

Online Retail Marketing is also known as digital retail marketing, internet retail marketing and web-retail marketing.
