6\. E-MARKETING STRATEGIES \[E-MARKETING MIX\]: (\*Not in syllabus but necessary to understand this topic)  
E-marketing strategy is normally based and built upon the traditional 4 P's (Product, Price, Place and Promotion).

E-marketing strategies are as follows:

(1) E-Product Strategies:

In traditional marketing consumers see a product, assess it and touch it. Online, this immediate tangibility disappears. But, it is not a disadvantage in the 21st century. Within the U.K. e-commerce sales are increasing at extremely high rates. Why? Firstly there are clear online facts about the product you are purchasing. The buyer knows immediately about product features, the facts, not a sales person's assumptions.

The buying process is also customised for returning users, making repeat purchases easier. Distributors can also offer immediately ancillary products along with the main purchase.

The product can also be customised to consumers needs. www.nike.com offer customised trainers to users online. Users can design and see their trainers online before they order.

(2) E- Price Strategies:

E-price is a unique element in the e-marketing mix since it relates directly to the generation of revenue. E-pricing occupies an important place in the marketing of goods and services by a distributor over the internet.

Recently , e-pricing has been very competitive. Online retail marketing gives consumers the power to shop around for the best deal at a click of a button. Websites such www.kelkoo.com compare products from different websites informing consumers of where the best deal is. Such easy access to information helps to maintain prices within the online world.

The growth of online auctions also helps consumers to dictate price. The online auction company www.ebay.com has grown in popularity with thousands of buyers and sellers bidding daily.

E-pricing can also easily reward loyal consumers. Payment is also easy, paypal or online credit cards use allows for easy payments. However, the downside to this is internet fraud which is growing rapidly around the world.

(3) E-place Strategies:

One of the biggest changes to the marketing mix is online purchasing. Consumers can buy directly from distributors cutting out retailers totally. The challenge for online distributors is to ensure that the products are delivered to the consumer within a proper time. Online location is important in e-place strategies. Google is helpful to find online locations.

(4) E-promotion Strategies:

Promoting goods and services online is concerned with a number of issues. These issues are:

(1) Having a domain name  
(2) Banner promotions  
(3) Web Public Relations (WPR)  
(4) E-leaflets.

Having a recognisable domain name is the first stage towards e-promotion.

The e-marketing mix must work together and support each other if the distributor or owner is to have a successful online marketing strategy.
