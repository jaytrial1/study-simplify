7\. DEFINITION AND CHARACTERISTICS OF PERSONAL SELLING :

After getting an elementary idea of what personal selling means, let us examine some of its definitions:

(1) According to Stanton "Personal selling consists of individual, personal communication, in contrast to the mass, impersonal communication of advertising, sales promotion and the other promotional tools".

(2) According to the definition given by the National Association of Marketing Teachers "Personal selling is the ability to persuade people to buy goods or services at a profit to the seller and with benefits to the buyer. Stated a little differently, it is the ability to induce others to accept a benefit at a fair price, the benefit being the services rendered."

(3) Russel and Beach are of the opinion that, "Personal selling is the art of persuading the people to purchase goods which will give off lasting satisfaction, by using methods which consume the least time and effort."

7.1 CHARACTERISTICS:\*

Following are the main characteristics of personal selling:

(1) Personal Contact Other constituents of promotion-mix require no personal contact, whereas here a salesman personally meets the buyers individually and persuades them to buy. It is possible for a salesman to remove any objections that customers have in mind against the product. Thus, the psychological hurdle against the product can be removed.

(2) Flexible Process: Personal selling is a flexible process. As discussion with the customers proceeds a salesman can change his presentation, if necessary. He varies his presentation in accordance with buyers' needs and behaviour.

(3) Quick Results: This is the only medium of sales promotion which yields quick results. As the salesman tackles buyers' objections against the product successfully during conversation, they would decide to buy the goods. There is no guarantee of immediate purchase in case of other means of sales promotion.

(4) Minimum Waste: There is much scope of wastes in the field of advertising as it reaches those persons also who are not even potential buyers. There is hardly any possibility of such a waste in personal selling. A salesman prepares a list of potential buyers. Hence, appeal gets directed only to right persons. The expenditure is therefore likely to yield good returns.

(5) Genuine Form of Marketing: Since salesmen are in personal contact with buyers, they come to know their requirements well, and try to sell products accordingly. The idea of selling refrigerators to Eskimos has become outdated. The real form of marketing is that products are sold according to the requirements of the customers and production is also adjusted accordingly. The salesmen can convey the feelings of the buyers to manufacturers, who make necessary changes in production structure in response to it.

(6) Difficult Task: Personal selling is a tough job as compared to other media of selling. A human being is to be dealt with, and hence salesmen have to be very cautious, tolerant and friendly. The salesman has to undergo physical strain also. He has to be alert in presenting his views before the customers. He has to provide after-sale services. He has to collect relevant information and convey it to the manufacturer.
