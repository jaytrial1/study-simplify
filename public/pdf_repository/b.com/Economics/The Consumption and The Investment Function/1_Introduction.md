
We have seen in the earlier chapter that in Keynesian analysis, income and employment depend on the total effective demand. The total effective demand is the sum of consumption expenditure and investment expenditure. Accordingly it becomes necessary to analyse:

(a) the factors underlying consumption outlays which involve a study of consumption function and
(b) the factors underlying investment outlays which involve a study of investment function. In this chapter, in the first place we shall study both these concepts.
