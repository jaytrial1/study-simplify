# 5_Importance of the Concept of Incidence of Taxation
Inspite of certain limitations, the study of the concept of incidence of taxation is of considerable significance. The tax system is not merely aimed at raising a certain amount of revenue, but the aim is to raise it from those sections of the society who can best bear the burden of the tax. The aim, in short, is to secure a fair and just distribution of the tax burden. This obviously cannot be done unless an effort is made to trace the incidence of each tax levied by the State. We must know who exactly pays the tax ultimately in order to find out whether it is just to ask him to pay it or whether the burden imposed on him is according to the ability to pay of the tax payer or not.. If the tax system is to confirm to Adam smith's first canon of taxation, namely, the canon of equity, it becomes imperative for us to make a careful study of the reactions and repercussions of each tax and find out its final resting place.

If public finance is to serve as an instrument of social justice, the question of incidence of taxation assumes great importance. The rich have to be taxed and the proceeds have to be spent for the benefit of the poor. And if we have to tax the rich, we have to see that the incidence must be on
