# 1_Introduction
Public finance deals with the finances of public bodies or public authorities, such as goverments (central or state), local bodies, public corporations etc. The study of public finance as a branch of economics has come to occupy a very important place in economic literature since last nine decades. During these decades, as witnessed in the field of science and techonlogy, significant and far reaching changes have taken place in the sphere of public finance as a branch of economic science. Upto the end of the $19^{\text {th }}$ century, the general view held by the economists was that the main functions of the State were to defend the country against foreign aggression and to maintain law and order at home. The state was not supposed to interfere in the economic activities of the people relating to production, exchange, distribution etc., these were to be left to the private sector and private enterprise. And that is why, the concept of state prevalent at that time was known as the police state. Thus, in view of the limited functions of the State, it did not require funds on a large scale and hence it was not necessary to raise large resources from the people.

But in recent years, things have changed substantially, Modern State is no longer a Police State, instead the concept of Welfare State has come to accepted by governments all over the world. In modern times, the governments do not confine themselves to defence and maintenance of law

and order. There has been a manifold increase in the functions of the government. Apart from defence and internal security, the government in modern times initiates a number of measures for the economic and social develpoment of the country. The government, therefore, needs more revenues to discharge its ever increasing functions and responsibilities. Hence the importance of public finance has greatly increased in recent years. Indeed, there is not a single aspect of a modern citizen's life that is not touched by public finance. Public finance deals with the income and expenditure of the government.
