3. Determination of the Rate of Exchange

As said above, the rate of exchange is the price of a country's currency expressed in terms of a foreign currency. There are three important theories for the determination of exchange rate under different monetary standards.

# (1) Mint Par Theory :

When the two countries are on gold standard, the rate of exchange is determined according to mint par theory.

# (2) Purchasing Power Parity Theory :

When the two countries are on inconvertible paper currency standard, the rate of exchange is determined according to the purchasing power parity theory.

# (3) Balance of Payments Theory :
