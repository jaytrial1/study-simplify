1. Introduction

**What is Foreign Exchange?**

The problem of foreign exchange arises in international trade because different countries have different systems of currency. In the case of domestic trade or inter-regional trade, however, as there is only one currency system which operates throughout the country, the problem of foreign exchange does not arise. But in the case of international trade, because of the prevalence of different systems of currencies in the different countries, there arises the problem of converting the currency of one country into the currency of another country. Foreign exchange, therefore, refers to the mechanism of the ways and means by which payments in connection with international trade are effected.

In the words of S.E. Thomas, "Foreign exchange is that branch of the science of economics in which we seek to determine the principles on which the people of the world settle their debts one to the other."

According to Hartley Withers, "Foreign exchanges are a mechanism by which international indebtedness is settled between one country and another." Likewise, Chapman has stated that, the mechanism where payments are effected in international trade is known as foreign exchange. short, foreign exchange is the science and art of international money changing.
