1\. INTRODUCTION:

Market research is an integral part of marketing management. The producer who produces on large scale on the basis of anticipated demand bears immense risks which he may avoid if he carefully estimates the magnitude of demand by gathering reliable information through systematic market research. In the modern age, market research has become an indispensable tool for decision making with respect to such matters as placing a new product in the existing market, placing existing product in a new market, preferences of the customers and their purchasing capacity etc. To formulate business policy merely on the basis of baseless information and inferences is extremely disastrous for the present day producers and firms.

At present an intense competition prevails everywhere for capturing new markets for the products. Consequently, local as well as national markets have receded in the background and global markets have become a major goal for the progressive and dynamic companies. The whole world has turned into a unified market.
