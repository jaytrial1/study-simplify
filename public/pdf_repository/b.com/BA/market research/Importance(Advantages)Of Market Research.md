3\. IMPORTANCE (ADVANTAGES) OF MARKET RESEARCH:\*

A most glaring aspect of the modern production process is that production is carried on in anticipation of demand. Hence, market research becomes necessary to have a routine idea of the existing and potential needs of the consumers. If this information is not obtained through market research, there is every possibility that the commodity produced on a large scale remains unsold and a huge loss is suffered by the producer. In reality, market research has played a significant role in expanding the area of sales in present day circumstances.

(1) Solves Marketing Problems: Market research provides much information about the market and thereby it helps solve various problems of marketing. These are the problems pertaining to customers, traders, competitors, nature of competition, advertising, distribution etc. Market research serves as an effective tool in resolving all these problems. (2) Increases Market Efficiency: Market research increases market efficiency also by answering the questions such as which channels should be used to distribute the goods, how should the product be designed, what should be its price, what should be the quality of the product, all these problems can be solved effectively on the basis of the information obtained through market research.

(3) Facilitates Sales Forecasting: Market research provides valuable information for sales forecasting. Sales forecasting is based on a thorough study of seasonal, cyclical and secular changes in market demand. Hence, it can be fruitfully used for the planning of production, marketing and sales.  
4\) Authenticity of Decisions: The utility of market research in raising the authenticity of business decisions is indisputable. The marketing manager is constantly in touch with the functions of market research. He gathers information about current market situation, the factors affecting market situation, the efforts made to influence these factors and their results etc. and equips himself to arrive at prompt and most reliable decisions regarding production and sales.

(5) Helpful to Management: Market research helps management get better solutions to complex problems about marketing processes. Through advertising and sales promotion activities, a balance can be achieved between demand and supply in the market. But it is market research which helps management in planning and executing advertisement and sales promotion activities.

(6) Useful to Production: Market research is beneficial to the producer in various ways. It guides him in the selection of production plan, distribution method, size of marketing organisation, the instruments of publicity and advertisement etc.

(7) Link between Producer and Consumer: Market research acts as a link between producers and customers. Obviously there can be no direct contact and communication between producers and customers when goods are produced on a large scale and at far off places in the world. However, it is possible to fill the gap between producers and customers through market research even in this situation.
