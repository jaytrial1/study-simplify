3\. FEATURES OF ONLINE RETAIL MARKETING:  
(Not in syllabus but necessary to understand in detail online marketing.)

Features of online retail marketing are as follows:

(1) Online retail marketing can deliver the same message in a wide variety of contexts.

(2) Online retail marketing is a unique tool of mass communication.

(3) It is powerful tools and methods used for promoting goods and services through a digital channel.

(4) It is measurable.

(5) It is a better tool for direct response.

(6) It may generate more immediate sales.

(7) It is less expensive than traditional retail marketing.

(8) It provides for immediate reporting and feedback while using the internet. Email, social media, etc.

(9) It has four broad phases  strategies, implement, benchmark and optimise.

(10) It is flexible.

(11) It covers a wide geographic area.

(12) It makes marketing easier.
