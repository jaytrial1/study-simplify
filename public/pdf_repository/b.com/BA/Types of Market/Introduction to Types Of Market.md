1\. INTRODUCTION:\*

The objective of each business enterprise is to earn profit by selling goods. Hence, they are supposed to know their market i.e. customers, to study its characteristics and to formulate suitable marketing policy and strategy. Buyers of all these types of markets have similar behaviour. Still however, their purchasing behaviour is different. Why their behaviour is different; for that what types of change the company has to introduce in its marketing policy etc. matters have to be understood by the marketing manager. Especially, in all types of markets, which type of forces influence the buyers' decisions? If the marketing manager has the knowledge of this, he can make a proper decision. Four types of markets have been recognised by Mr. Kotler.

1\. Consumer Market

2\. Industrial Market or Producer Market

3\. The Reseller Market

4\. The Government Market

Some of the writers describe the International Market as the fifth market.

But in our syllabus, the first four only are mentioned and hence, we will discuss only those markets.
