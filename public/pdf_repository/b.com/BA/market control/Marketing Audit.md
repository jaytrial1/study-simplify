2\. MARKETING AUDIT:\*

To keep control over marketing it is necessary to exercise control over marketing strategy in its entirety and to evaluate it thoroughly. The method used for controlling marketing in its totality, instead of controlling its components separately one by one is known as marketing audit.

Till now different elements of marketing such as quality of the product, sales promotion efforts, distribution channels etc. were regulated separately, but the trend at present is to evaluate marketing as a whole at a time.

According to A. R. Oxenfelt, "Marketing audit refers to impartial critical observation and evaluation of the organisation, methods, procedures and personnel employed to implement the policies so that the goals of marketing function are realised."

The definition of marketing audit given by Harwood Merryle is more concise and simple: "Marketing audit means systematic, critical and objective observation and evaluation of marketing operations as a whole."
