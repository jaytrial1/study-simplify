9\. CHARACTERISTICS OF SALES PROMOTION :

The characteristics that follow from the above definitions may be summarised as below:

(1) Sales promotion refers to the non-recurrent measures of increasing sales. This means that these measures are not repeated again and again. For instance, sales contests or discount sales are organised only once a year.

(2) Various methods and parties are employed for this purpose. Among the parties are salesmen, dealers and consumers, while methods include such practices as exhibitions, monetary incentives, price reduction, demonstrations, bonus etc.

(3) Sales promotion is not indispensable; selling does not stop in the absence of sales promotion efforts. On the other hand, advertising and personal selling are to be continued without interruption. Sales are not possible without these activities. Sales promotion refers to special additional marketing activities aimed at increasing sales volume.

(4) The results of sales promotion can be ascertained. Of course, the effects of advertising and personal selling are difficult to measure. But the effects of sales promotion on sales can be estimated in most cases. For instance, a discount sale programme organised by a saree dealer at some well-known hall in the Navrangpura area results in sales which can be estimated. It can be known even on the basis of coupons collected.

(5) Parties to the transactions are offered some additional benefit than what they receive in ordinary circumstances. For instance, consumers are offered such benefits as lower prize and award of prizes, salesmen are offered bonus, higher commission, trophies etc. Special schemes may be designed for dealers and traders if they sell more than a prescribed quota.

(6) Sales promotion differs from other constituents of the promotion mix. Advertising and personal selling are continuous activities, while sales promotion is a non-recurring and specific additional activity.
