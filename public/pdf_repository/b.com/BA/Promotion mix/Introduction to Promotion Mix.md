1\. INTRODUCTION:

Promotion mix is an important constituent of marketing mix. Promotion mix refers to the combination of various instruments of sales promotion. If attempts are not made to promote sales continuously, business may become stagnant and in the long run it may become necessary to close down the business. Some efforts are necessary even to maintain the business at its current level. These efforts can be divided into four groups: advertisement, personal selling, sales promotion and publicity. The combination of these four is collectively known as 'promotion mix'. Buyers are to be continuously informed of the name and uses of a product; they are to be explained how the firm's products are better than competitor's. They are to be informed of the new uses and newly added attributes of the product. These measures are essential if a business is to survive. It is observed that only those business units survive and grow, which constantly try for the expansion of markets. We shall consider in detail the objectives of the promotion mix and factors affecting it in the present chapter. Then each of the elements of the promotion mix will be dealt with in detail in the later chapters.
