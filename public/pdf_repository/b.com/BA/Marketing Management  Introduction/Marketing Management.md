4\. MARKETING MANAGEMENT:

Let us examine some definitions of Marketing Management:

(1) According to Philip Kotler, "Marketing management is the analysis, planning, implementation and control of the programme designed to create, build and maintain beneficial exchanges with target buyers for the purpose of achieving organisational objectives."

(2) In the words of Still and Cundif, 'Marketing management is concerned with directing purposeful activities for the achievement of sales objective.'

(3) According to Davar, Marketing management is a process of ascertaining consumer needs, converting them into products and services and then moving the products or services to final consumer or user to satisfy such needs and wants of specific customer segment or segments with emphasis on profitability ensuring the optimum use of the services available to the organisation." Thus, Shri Davar considers profitability and optimum utilisation of available resources, as the foundations of the structure of marketing management.

(4) Generally planning, organising, staffing, directing and controlling are the functions of management. The definition of marketing management given by Levis K. Johnson will be helpful in grasping the scope of marketing management. According to him, "marketing management includes not only those activities which are directly related to the functions of sales force but such activities also as sales planning, market research, publicity and sales control etc."

(5) An outstanding author on management has stated, "Marketing management inculdes all those activities which are required for converting people's purchasing power into effective and real demand."
