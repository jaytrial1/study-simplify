# Independent Inheritance of Traits

Mendel also investigated what happens when pea plants with *two different characteristics* are bred together. Consider crossing a *tall plant* with *round seeds* and a *short plant* with *wrinkled seeds*.

### The Dihybrid Cross: Law of Independent Assortment

When a pure-breeding *tall plant* with *round seeds* is crossed with a pure-breeding *short plant* with *wrinkled seeds*, the *F1 progeny* are all *tall* and have *round seeds*. This confirms that *tallness* and *round seeds* are *dominant traits*.

When these *F1 progeny* (which are heterozygous for both traits) are self-pollinated to generate *F2 progeny*, a Mendelian experiment reveals a variety of combinations:
*   Some *F2 progeny* are *tall plants* with *round seeds* (parental combination).
*   Some *F2 progeny* are *short plants* with *wrinkled seeds* (parental combination).
*   Crucially, there are also *new combinations* in the *F2 progeny*:
    *   Some are *tall* with *wrinkled seeds*.
    *   Others are *short* with *round seeds*.

*This image illustrates a dihybrid cross, specifically focusing on the inheritance of two traits in pea plants: seed shape (round vs. wrinkled) and seed color (yellow vs. green).

**1. Parental (P) Generation:**
*   One parent is homozygous dominant for seed shape and homozygous recessive for seed color (RR yy), expressed phenotypically as "round, green".
*   The other parent is homozygous recessive for seed shape and homozygous dominant for seed color (rr YY), expressed phenotypically as "wrinkled, yellow".

**2. Gamete Formation in P Generation:**
*   The "round, green" parent (RR yy) produces only one type of gamete: Ry.
*   The "wrinkled, yellow" parent (rr YY) produces only one type of gamete: rY.

**3. F1 Generation:**
*   The cross between Ry and rY gametes results in all F1 individuals having the genotype Rr Yy.
*   Phenotypically, all F1 individuals are "round, yellow", indicating that round (R) is dominant over wrinkled (r) and yellow (Y) is dominant over green (y).

**4. F2 Generation (Dihybrid Cross):**
*   The F2 generation is produced by self-crossing two F1 individuals (Rr Yy x Rr Yy).
*   Each F1 parent (Rr Yy) can produce four types of gametes due to independent assortment: RY, Ry, rY, and ry.

**5. Punnett Square for F2 Generation:**
*   A 4x4 Punnett square is used to predict the genotypes and phenotypes of the F2 offspring.
*   The four gamete types from one parent (RY, Ry, rY, ry) are listed across the top row, and the four gamete types from the other parent (RY, Ry, rY, ry) are listed down the first column.
*   Each cell within the Punnett square represents a possible genotype resulting from the combination of a paternal and maternal gamete.
    *   Cells with at least one 'R' and at least one 'Y' allele (e.g., RRYY, RrYy) are phenotypically "round, yellow".
    *   Cells with at least one 'R' but two 'y' alleles (e.g., RRyy, Rryy) are phenotypically "round, green".
    *   Cells with two 'r' alleles and at least one 'Y' allele (e.g., rrYY, rrYy) are phenotypically "wrinkled, yellow".
    *   The single cell with two 'r' and two 'y' alleles (rryy) is phenotypically "wrinkled, green".

**6. Phenotypic Ratios in F2 Generation:**
*   The image summarizes the observed and theoretical phenotypic ratios for the F2 generation:
    *   **Round, yellow:** 315 observed seeds, corresponding to a theoretical ratio of 9 out of 16. These are genotypes with at least one dominant allele for both traits (R_Y_).
    *   **Round, green:** 108 observed seeds, corresponding to a theoretical ratio of 3 out of 16. These are genotypes with at least one dominant allele for shape and homozygous recessive for color (R_yy).
    *   **Wrinkled, yellow:** 101 observed seeds, corresponding to a theoretical ratio of 3 out of 16. These are genotypes with homozygous recessive for shape and at least one dominant allele for color (rrY_).
    *   **Wrinkled, green:** 32 observed seeds, corresponding to a theoretical ratio of 1 out of 16. These are genotypes that are homozygous recessive for both traits (rryy).
*   The total number of observed seeds is 556.
*   The Mendelian dihybrid cross phenotypic ratio of 9:3:3:1 is clearly demonstrated by the observed counts, which approximate these theoretical proportions.*

This demonstrates that the *tall/short trait* and the *round seed/wrinkled seed trait* are *independently inherited*. This is known as Mendel's *Law of Independent Assortment*.
