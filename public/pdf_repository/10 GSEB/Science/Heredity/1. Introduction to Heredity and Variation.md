# Introduction to Heredity and Variation

Reproductive processes result in new individuals that are similar to their parents but possess *subtle differences*. Even during *asexual reproduction*, some *variation* is produced. However, the number of successful *variations* is maximized through *sexual reproduction*. For example, a field of sugarcane shows very little *variation* among individual plants, but sexually reproducing animals, including humans, exhibit distinct *variations* among individuals. This chapter explores the mechanism by which *variations* are created and *inherited*.
