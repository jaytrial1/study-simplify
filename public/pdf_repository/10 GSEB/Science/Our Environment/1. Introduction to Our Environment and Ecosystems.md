# Our Environment

We often hear the word 'environment' used in various contexts, from daily conversations to global discussions. Our elders recall a different 'environment' from the past, while others emphasize the need for a healthy 'environment' for well-being. Global summits frequently address 'environmental' issues, highlighting their critical importance. In this chapter, we will explore the intricate interactions among different components of the environment and analyze the significant impact human activities have on it.
