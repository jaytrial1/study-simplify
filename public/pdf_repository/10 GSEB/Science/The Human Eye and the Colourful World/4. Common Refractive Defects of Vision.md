# Common Refractive Defects of Vision

Over time, the human eye may gradually lose its power of accommodation. In such cases, a person might find it difficult to see objects distinctly and comfortably, leading to blurred vision. These issues are primarily due to *refractive defects* of the eye.

There are three main common refractive defects of vision:

1.  **Myopia** (or near-sightedness)
2.  **Hypermetropia** (or far-sightedness)
3.  **Presbyopia**

These defects can generally be corrected by using suitable spherical lenses in spectacles or contact lenses, or through surgical interventions.
