# Energy Resources

**Energy resources** hold an important place in the economic development of any nation. They keep industries and the economy thriving. These minerals include **Coal**, **Mineral oil**, **Natural gas**, and **Atomic minerals**.

## Classification of Energy Resources

Energy resources can be classified in different ways:

*   **Conventional / Commercial Energy Resources:**
    *   These are generally **non-renewable resources**.
    *   Examples: Coal, Mineral oil, Natural gas, Atomic minerals.
    *   Electricity is often generated using these resources.
*   **Non-Conventional / Renewable Energy Resources:**
    *   These are generally **renewable resources** and sometimes called inexhaustible.
    *   Examples: Hydro energy, Wind energy, Solar energy, Bio-gas, Geothermal energy, Tidal energy.
*   **Non-Commercial Energy Resources:**
    *   Examples: Peat coal, firewood, dung.
