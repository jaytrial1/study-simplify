# Introduction to Minerals and Their Importance

Requirements of primitive man were limited; he worked only to sustain his life. Man has since conquered the heights of evolution, and **minerals** have played a large role in this progress. A mineral is a **natural resource**.

Human evolution is often divided into various ages, such as the **Stone Age**, **Bronze Age**, **Iron Age**, and the **modern age** (atomic age). In the Stone Age, man used stones for hunting, but now he is travelling into space. Man's relation with minerals is very old and strong.

Today, minerals are considered to be the **backbone of economic development** of any nation. Countries like the U.S. and Russia have become world powers due to the proper use of minerals. Both countries are rich in variety of minerals and prosperity. Even though India has potentially huge reserves of minerals, long-term dependence and the absence of technical knowledge have resulted in lesser economic development.
