# Common Metallic Minerals in India

## Iron Ore

**Iron** is considered to be the **base of industrial development** of the modern world. It is used widely in making items from small pins to large machines, cars, trucks, ships, railways, bridges, buildings, and weapons. It is cheaper, strong, and durable, and easily available in most countries. It has the virtue to mix with other minerals, making it an important mineral.

Iron is found in an impure condition and is refined by mixing it with **coke** and **lime** and heating it in large furnaces. The pure iron obtained is known as **Pig iron**.

There are four types of iron ore found in India:
1.  **Hematite**
2.  **Magnetite**
3.  **Limonite**
4.  **Siderite**

Maximum iron ore in India is obtained from **Karnataka**. Other major producers are **Odisha**, **Jharkhand**, **Chhattisgarh**, and **Andhra Pradesh**. Iron ore is also found in Goa, Rajasthan, Tamil Nadu, Maharashtra, Madhya Pradesh, Kerala, Uttar Pradesh, and Assam.

## Manganese

**Manganese** is an important mineral for the **iron and steel industry**. It is mainly used to make steel from iron.

Its other uses include:
*   Chemical industry (bleaching powder, insecticides)
*   Dry battery cells
*   Tiles
*   Leather industry
*   Glass industry
*   Matchbox industry
*   Photography
*   China clay utensils
*   Coloured bricks

When manganese is mixed with steel, it makes rails and rods more flexible and strong. Steel mixed with manganese is used in machines to break rocks and in grinding machines.

Major manganese-producing states are **Odisha**, **Karnataka**, **Madhya Pradesh**, and **Goa**. It is also obtained from Andhra Pradesh, Jharkhand, Rajasthan, and Gujarat.

## Copper

**Copper** has been used since primitive times and was the **first mineral used by man**. It is important due to its mixing property.
*   When mixed with **Tin**, it forms **bell metal**.
*   When mixed with **Zinc**, it makes **Brass**.

Copper is mostly used in making:
*   Electric equipment
*   Telephone, radio, television, refrigerator, and air conditioners (as it is a good conductor of electricity)
*   Insecticides
*   Explosives
*   Coloured glass
*   Coins
*   Printing

Copper is mainly produced by **Jharkhand**, **Madhya Pradesh**, and **Rajasthan** in India. Sikkim and Andhra Pradesh also produce it. Copper is also found in Bihar, Karnataka, Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu, Himachal Pradesh, and Uttarakhand.

## Bauxite

**Bauxite** is an **aluminium ore**. It was first found at Les Baux in France in 1921. **Aluminium** is obtained from Bauxite. It is useful due to its special properties:
*   Very light in weight
*   Strong
*   Durable
*   Good conductor of electricity
*   Rust-free
*   Easily ductile

It is widely used in making:
*   Domestic utensils
*   Electrical gadgets
*   Colours
*   Ship building

Bauxite is obtained from **Odisha**, **Chhattisgarh**, **Maharashtra**, **Jharkhand**, and **Gujarat**. This mineral is found in the geological structure of the **Deccan Trap**. Ranchi in Jharkhand and Jamnagar, Bhavnagar, Junagadh, Amreli, Surat, and Sabarkantha districts in Gujarat produce Bauxite.

## Lead

The ore of **Lead** is called **galena**. It is soft and heavy. It is used to make:
*   Alloys
*   Electric wires
*   Colours
*   Weapons
*   Glass
*   Rubber
*   Storage batteries

In India, lead is available in **Rajasthan**, **Andhra Pradesh**, and **Tamil Nadu**. It is also found in West Bengal, Madhya Pradesh, Uttar Pradesh, Odisha, Maharashtra, Meghalaya, and Sikkim.
