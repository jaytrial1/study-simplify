This section describes key non-metallic minerals found in India, focusing on their properties, uses, and production areas.

# Important Non-Metallic Minerals

## Mica

India ranks **first in the world** in **Mica** production. It is **fire resistant** and a **bad conductor of electricity**, making it useful in:
*   Making electrical goods (electric motors, dynamo, radio, telephone, cars, steamers, etc.)

**Bihar**, **Jharkhand**, **Andhra Pradesh**, and **Rajasthan** are major producers of Mica in India. Mica is also found in Karnataka, West Bengal, and Tamil Nadu. A large reserve of **muscovite** type of Mica is found in India.

Even though Mica is produced in sufficient quantity, demand is high, and it has to be imported.

## Limestone

**Limestone** is used in large quantity in the production of **cement**.

Besides cement, it is also used for:
*   Iron melting
*   Chemical industry
*   Soda ash
*   Soap
*   Colour-chemicals
*   Building construction
*   Paper
*   Sugar refining

**Andhra Pradesh**, **Rajasthan**, **Madhya Pradesh**, **Gujarat**, and **Tamil Nadu** produce about 70% of the country's Limestone. Other producing states include Chhattisgarh, Karnataka, Maharashtra, and Himachal Pradesh.

In Gujarat, Jamnagar, Kachchh, Amreli, and Kheda districts are major Limestone-producing districts. Other districts with reserves include Banaskantha, Mahesana, Sabarkantha, Vadodara, Panchmahal, Bharuch, Narmada, Surat, Bhavnagar, and Rajkot. Rocks in Jamnagar district contain about 97% lime content.
