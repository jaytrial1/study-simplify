This section discusses the necessity of preserving minerals due to their limited availability and outlines various methods for their efficient and sustainable use.

# Mineral Preservation

Minerals are necessary for the existence and development of human beings. It is crucial to think about their preservation. **Preservation** means the **efficient and well-planned use of minerals**. Today, nations often export minerals extensively to earn foreign exchange, leading to unrestricted use. This makes mineral preservation necessary.

## Remedies for Mineral Preservation

1.  **Use of Proper Technology:** Using appropriate technology to obtain minerals can reduce wastage.
2.  **Recycling:** Scrap minerals like iron, copper, aluminium, and tin should be reused.
3.  **Alternate Use of Minerals:** Find alternatives for minerals found in less quantity (e.g., solar energy instead of electricity, aluminium instead of copper, CNG instead of petrol).
4.  **Use of Non-Conventional Means:** Increase the use of non-conventional sources like water (hydro), solar, wind, bio-gas, etc.
5.  **Sustainability:** Maintain environmental purity and pass on a clean environment to future generations. Work towards a pollution-free environment.
6.  **Planned Usage:** After calculating mineral reserves, plan their use properly to ensure availability for a long period.

It is necessary to preserve and enhance minerals.
