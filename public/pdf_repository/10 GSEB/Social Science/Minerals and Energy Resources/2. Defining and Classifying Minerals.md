# Defining and Classifying Minerals

## What is a Mineral?

A **mineral** is matter formed due to an **inorganic process** that has a certain chemical composition.

This is a result of an **abiotic process** that took place in the interior of the earth over infinite time. The solid, liquid, or gaseous matter, formed due to specific abiotic processes within the rocks in the interior of the earth, having a certain chemical composition, homogeneous constitution, and specific atomic structure is called a 'mineral'.

*   **Solid minerals:** Iron, Manganese, Gold, Silver, etc.
*   **Liquid minerals:** Mercury, Petroleum, etc.
*   **Gaseous minerals:** Natural gas

The type of minerals available from the interior of the earth depends on how the earth's crust originated. For example, Iron, Copper, Zinc, Gold, and Silver are found in **igneous rocks**. Diamonds are found in **metamorphic rocks**.

## Classification of Minerals

Minerals can be broadly classified into the following categories:

*   **Metallic Minerals:**
    *   Precious Metallic Minerals: Gold, Silver, Platinum
    *   Light Metallic Minerals: Magnesium, Bauxite, Titanium
    *   Minerals Used for Common Purpose: Iron, Copper, Lead, Zinc, Tin, Nickel
    *   Minerals Used as Alloys: Chromium, Manganese, Tungsten, Vanadium
*   **Non-Metallic Minerals:** Limestone, Chalk, Asbestos, Mica, Fluorspar, Gypsum, Sulphur, Diamond
*   **Minerals Used as Energy Resources:** Coal, Mineral Oil, Natural Gas, Uranium, Thorium
