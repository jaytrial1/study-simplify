This section defines anti-social activities and categorizes crimes, focusing on the global problem of corruption, its definition, and various forms.

# Anti-social Activities and Corruption

An *anti-social activity* is defined as "an individual or group activity or behavior which is against rules proposed by laws in the society".

Common criminal or anti-social activities include murder, theft, kidnapping, robbery, cheating, trickery, and cyber crime. These are often referred to as **blue-collar crimes**.

On the other hand, **white-collar crimes** are prevalent in society, such as bribery, corruption, tax evasion, hoarding, adulteration, land encroachment, and black marketing.

## Corruption

*Corruption* is a global problem. According to the World Bank, "Corruption means the misuse of the public position for personal benefits." It originates from the misuse of status and power. Corruption exists in both public and private sectors in India. Both the giver and the taker involved in corruption are considered guilty and punishable.

## Corruption - In our Country and Foreign country

Corruption prevails in many countries to varying degrees. It manifests in diverse forms:
*   Giving and taking money, gifts, expensive ornaments, or foreign trips.
*   Partial behavior.
*   Influencing decisions.
*   Favoring relatives and friends.

Corruption has adverse effects on the economy and society:
*   It degrades moral values and social policy regulation.
*   It leads to the problem of *black money* in the economy, hindering development.
*   It erodes public trust in state laws, judicial processes, government, and administration. Honest people experience frustration and disappointment.
*   It causes injustice and disparity in income, leading to class struggle and violation of human rights.
*   It puts morality and national character at risk and contributes to a fall in the level of the economic system.
