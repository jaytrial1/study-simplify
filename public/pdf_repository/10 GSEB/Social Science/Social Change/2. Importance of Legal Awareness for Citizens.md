# Importance of Legal Awareness for Citizens

Due to factors like low literacy rates and lack of information, many people in our country lack knowledge about everyday laws. Ignorance of the law does not excuse one from punishment or conviction if they violate it. Therefore, it is essential to provide basic legal understanding and information to every citizen.

Why is it necessary to have general knowledge about law?

> **Answer:** It is very essential to have general knowledge, information, and understanding of law for several reasons:
> (1) People can avoid committing crimes and thus be saved from punishment or conviction if they have general knowledge about the law.
> (2) They can seek guidance regarding exploitation and take legal steps against injustice.
> (3) They can enjoy their constitutional rights, protect their individual interests, and flourish.
> (4) They can become informed about various legal provisions made for their protection and well-being.
> (5) Their loyalty towards society, state, and nation increases.
> (6) They can contribute to the formation of society, are not deprived of their rights as citizens, and are able to perform their duties.
> (7) If everyone has proper knowledge of laws, they can lead a life of esteem and pride. Thus, knowledge and understanding of laws are essential.
