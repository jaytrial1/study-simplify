# Understanding Social Change

*Social change* refers to the alterations that occur in the social structure and its institutions. It also includes changes in the composition and functions of society.

Social changes have been observed in social relations, family and marriage arrangements, culture, lifestyle, literature, art, music, and dance. These changes are often attributed to processes like *westernization*, *globalization*, and *urbanization*, which facilitate cultural exchange. Physical objects, luxury items, modern tools, and daily facilities have become accessible even in rural areas. Changes in housing styles and construction have occurred. As a result of these physical changes, the living standard of people has improved. The influence of western culture is particularly evident in lifestyle changes.
