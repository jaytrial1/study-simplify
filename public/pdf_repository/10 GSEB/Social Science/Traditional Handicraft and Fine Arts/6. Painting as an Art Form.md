# Painting as an Art Form

Among all arts, painting holds a foremost position. It is an art expressed through lines and colours, aiming to express emotions by imitating animate and inanimate objects from nature. Evidence of Indian painting dates back 5000 years to the Harappan culture. Archaeological excavations have uncovered many examples.

Cave paintings by primitive humans from the Stone Age at Bhimbetka (Madhya Pradesh) show pictures of animals and birds. People also drew geometrical designs and sketches. Notable examples include elephants, rhinoceroses, and deer. The paintings at Ajanta and Ellora are considered incomparable specimens. Drawing *swastik*, *Pitcher*, and Ganesha sketches during festivals and celebrations is an old tradition in India. *Rangoli* making is also a very old tradition in some regions.
