# Understanding the HDI Score

For calculating the Human Development Index, highest and lowest values for every criterion are decided. Every index (LEI, EI, II) is calculated according to a formula. Based on these indices, the Human Development Index is obtained.

*   Its rate is between **0 to 1** for any country.
*   The HDI score depicts the difference that has to be covered to reach the maximum value of 1 (one).

This difference is useful in comparing human development between various nations. Based on it, one can understand the direction of a country's development and its relative position compared to others.
