This section lists various aspects that contribute to human development.

# Human Development Means

*   Ability of human beings to lead successful and creative lives as per their own interest, abilities, and wisdom.
*   Creation of human capabilities, securing equality, and getting an opportunity to select in various fields.
*   Man living a healthy, fine, fit, and long life.
*   Acquiring knowledge through information and education.
*   Getting opportunities for monetary benefit.
*   Natural resources should be available to all to improve living standards.
*   Achieving a qualitative lifestyle.
*   Removal of squalor and improvement of health-related conditions.
*   Obtaining personal and social security.
*   Utilizing Human rights, which are inherent to all human beings.

Human development is related to the creation and expansion of many such opportunities.

Thus, human life includes peace, economic, social, political, and cultural fields. Developing countries have sometimes struggled to achieve economic and social development due to indifference or disgust towards improvement, low aspirations, illiteracy, lack of entrepreneurship, suspicion, superstition, antiquatedness, orthodox traditions, along with improper use of natural resources.
