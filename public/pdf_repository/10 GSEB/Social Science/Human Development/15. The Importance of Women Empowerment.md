# Women Empowerment

Women are central to the whole development process. In any developing country, **economic empowerment** is a main aspect of women empowerment. Economic freedom is essential for women's empowerment. An educated woman can educate her household, society, and ultimately the entire nation.

India has started taking steps in this direction. Women have served at the posts of Prime Minister, President, and Chief Minister of different states. Women are engaged in various jobs, from taxi drivers to pilots. We see women working as social workers, literateurs, journalists, sportspersons, educators, and actors on TV and news channels. Women's employment is expanding from manual labor to work in business, commerce, communication, and many other jobs because of increasing education, training, and skilled programs. However, there is still a lot of scope for development, requiring significant efforts.
