# Human Development Report Analysis

Since 1990, the Human Development Report (HDR) is published every year. The Human Development Index of 188 countries included in the Human Development Report 2015 is divided into four sections based on HDI score ranges.

*   **Norway (0.944)** tops the list.
*   **Australia (0.935)** is placed in 2nd position.
*   **Switzerland (0.930)** is in 3rd position.
*   Asian country **Singapore (0.912)** is on 11th position.
