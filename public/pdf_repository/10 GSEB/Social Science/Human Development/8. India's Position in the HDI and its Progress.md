# India's Position in HDI

India, with an HDI of **0.609** in the 2015 report, is at the **130th position** among 188 countries. This places India in the category of **medium human development** countries.

India's Human Development Index has shown progressive improvement over the years:
*   1990: 0.428
*   2000: 0.496
*   2010: 0.586
*   2014: 0.604
*   2015: 0.609
