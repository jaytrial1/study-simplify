This section elaborates on the meaning and scope of human development, emphasizing its multi-dimensional nature.

# Meaning of Human Development

The term *human development* is frequently used by media, political leaders, institutions, and governments. It refers to the **expansion of human capacities**, the **prevalence of preferences**, and the **development of freedom and human rights**. Human development indicates many things beyond just education, health, nutrition, and controlling resources.

According to the UNDP, human development is a process of **extending human aspirations and essential subsistence services**.

Human development is a **man-centered approach** towards development. Its objective is to create equal conditions for everyone's life, enabling people to lead significant and creative lives according to their talent. Earlier, human development was evaluated solely on the basis of economic development, with *per capita income* considered the only measure. Nations concentrated on increasing their *Gross National Product (GNP)*. However, despite achieving economic success, there was often no significant change in people's lifestyle and quality of life. Thus, it is incorrect to believe that human development is solely achieved through economic development. Human development is not just related to income but also to how that income is used.

There are four important pillars of human development:
*   **Equality**
*   **Stability**
*   **Production**
*   **Empowerment**

Human development, therefore, means getting the opportunity to lead a successful and creative life according to one's own taste, interest, skill, intelligence, and capabilities.
