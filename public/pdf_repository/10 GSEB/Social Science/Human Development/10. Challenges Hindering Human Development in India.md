# Challenges of Human Development

Comparing India with the top five nations in the Human Development Report reveals that India still needs to make significant progress across all parameters: health, education, and income (per capita income and National income). Human development is not primarily related to materialistic resources but to live human resources.

The challenges that specifically hinder the progress of human development in India, according to the Human Development Index, and which require special focus are:
1.  **Health**
2.  **Sex Ratio (Gender Equality)**
3.  **Women Empowerment**
