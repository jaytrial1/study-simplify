This section outlines various schemes and policies implemented by the Indian government for women's development and empowerment.

# Women Welfare Schemes

Since the 1980s, many plans and programs related to women's development in India have been implemented.
*   The **National Commission for Women** was set up in 1992.
*   The **National Policy for Empowerment of Women - 2001** was set up by the Department of Women and Child Development.
*   Programs have been undertaken focusing on strength building, employment, income generation, welfare, supportive services, and gender sensitization.

Internationally:
*   The United Nations declared **1975** as **"Women's Year"**.
*   The decade **1975-1985** was declared **"Women's Decade"**.
*   **2002** was celebrated as **"Women Empowerment Year"**.

An Act has also been amended in India to give women equal rights in family property.
