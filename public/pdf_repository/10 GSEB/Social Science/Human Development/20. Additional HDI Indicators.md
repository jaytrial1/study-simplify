This section lists additional indicators included in the Human Development Report beyond the core three, providing a broader view of human well-being.

# Additional HDI Indicators

The Human Development Report 2015, published by the United Nations Development Programme (UNDP) on December 14, 2015, included the following indicators along with the core Human Development Index for a more comprehensive picture:
1.  Gender Development Index
2.  Child and Youth
3.  Adult Health and Health Expenditure
4.  Education
5.  Command Over Allocation of Resources
6.  Social Competencies
7.  Personal Insecurity
8.  International Integration
9.  Environment
10. Population Trends
11. Supplementary Indicators: Perceptions of Well-being

This report and its data are available on the UNDP website (http://hdr.undp.org/en/2015 report) and the India-specific site (http://www.in.undp.org/). The report is published in Hindi, English, and many other languages.
