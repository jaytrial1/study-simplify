This section discusses the ongoing health challenges in India and the need for continued focus on health initiatives.

# Addressing Health Issues

Despite progress, waterborne diseases, other diseases, and malnutrition continue to pose problems for the population. Lack of nutritious food means that women, children, and poor people often suffer from a lack of nutrients, basic minerals, vitamins, and proteins, which can stunt their development or make it incomplete.

Environmental pollution and poisonous gases are another challenge in daily life. Increasing urbanization and crowded habitats have created more problems despite newly raised health facilities. To face these new challenges, it has become especially important to focus on the health agenda and implement necessary changes, including addressing respiratory issues.
