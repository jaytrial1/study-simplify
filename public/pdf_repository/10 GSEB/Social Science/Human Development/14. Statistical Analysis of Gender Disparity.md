Generally, in all states in India, males dominate high posts, high-income jobs, and receive more profit and salary in industries and jobs. The proportion of female Members of Parliament was only 12.2% in 2014. A lower proportion of females is seen in Parliament, state legislative assemblies, senior officer roles, managers, company directors, and commercial and technical fields. Discrimination in other fields is also clearly evident.
