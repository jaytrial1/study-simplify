# Human Development Index (HDI)

The concept of the Human Development Index was proposed by Nobel Prize winner, economist of Indian origin, **Amartya Sen**. The first human development report was published in 1990 with his help. Since then, under the **United Nations Development Programme (UNDP)**, a report is published every year, presenting a worldwide analysis of the development of different nations across various dimensions.

The idea of the Human Development Index was presented in the first Human Development Report published by UNDP, which collectively used three dimensions:
1.  **Average age (Health)**
2.  **Knowledge**
3.  **Living standard (Per capita income)**

The Human Development Index of any nation is determined based on these three dimensions. Until 2009, these indicators were used by UNDP to prepare the report. A new technique has been used since 2010.
