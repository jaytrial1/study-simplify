# Various Schemes of Gujarat Government for Providing Equality to Women

The Gujarat government established the Women and Child Development Department in 2001 for the social, economic, and educational progress of women. The Gujarat Government has emphasized women's empowerment by stressing three aspects of women's development for uplifting women from poor and common families and rural areas: **Educational empowerment, Women's health, and Women's safety**. For this purpose, the government has implemented various schemes included in the women-centered gender budget.

Key schemes include:
*   **Encouraging Girls' Education:** Initiating school entrance ceremonies and girl education chariot festivals, resulting in nearly 100% admission and an increase in women's literacy.
*   **Vidyalaxmi Bond:** Providing a bond to girl children in villages with less than 35% women literacy or from BPL families in urban areas upon admission to primary or secondary school.
*   **Saraswati Sadhna Yojna:** Providing free bicycles to girls (one lakh fifty thousand girls mentioned). Girls traveling outside their village for study are given free travel facility in ST buses.
*   **Sabla Yojna:** Providing nutritional food to adolescent girls and developing their skills.
*   **Reservation:** Making provision for 33% reservation for women in government jobs and increasing women's reservation in local self-government institutions from 33% to 50%.
*   **Rastriya Swavlamban Yojna:** Providing pensions to poor and destitute old people. Destitute widows are given economic help to prevent them from living in poverty.
*   **Mission Mangalam Yojna:** Providing economic help to women through *Sakhi Mandal* (self-help groups) to make them self-reliant.
*   **E-mamta programme:** Registering pregnant women through mobile technology and issuing *mamta cards* to control maternal mortality. Regular health checkups are done, and after childbirth, the health of the mother and child is cared for through vaccination programs.
*   **Abolition of Gender Discrimination:** Launching the **"Save Girl Child"** (*Beti Bachhao Abhiyan*) and the **"Beti Bachao, Beti Badhao, and Beti Padhao"** (Save Daughter, Empower Daughter, and Educate Daughter) programs, which have contributed significantly to women's empowerment.
*   **Chiranjivi Yojna:** Providing medicines, laboratory tests, and operation facilities free of cost to Scheduled Caste and Scheduled Tribe pregnant women.

Various other schemes have been started for women's welfare by the Women and Child Development Department of Gujarat. Extending the coverage of these schemes will help face the challenges of women's empowerment.
