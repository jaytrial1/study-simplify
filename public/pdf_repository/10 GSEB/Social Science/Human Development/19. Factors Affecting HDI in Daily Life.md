This section reflects on how everyday situations observed in communities relate to and affect the Human Development Index of the country.

# Factors Affecting HDI in Daily Life

If we look closely into our streets and villages, we find various situations that directly impact human development:
*   Pregnant women not getting proper nutrition.
*   Underweight children being born.
*   Children suffering from malnutrition.
*   Children not attending *anganwadi* or school.
*   Children studying in school but unable to read or write.
*   Studies being abandoned midway.
*   Daughters not being sent for higher studies.
*   Youth not getting employment.
*   Somebody dying because of an accident.
*   Somebody suffering from a serious disease.

All these things affect the Human Development Index of our country. Thus, if not only the government but also the citizens of the country become active in the direction of development, only then will India be able to compete with nations having high human development in the future.
