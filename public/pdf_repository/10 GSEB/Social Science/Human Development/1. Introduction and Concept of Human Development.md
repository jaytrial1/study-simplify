# Human Development

Change is the rule of life. An infant grows slowly into an adult. From the phase of primitive man, humanity has developed into the present complicated social arrangement. Social and economic systems have also developed in different forms. Economics, which initially developed to maintain records of economic production, consumption, income, expenditure, and profit-loss, has started becoming a measure of physical well-being and human development over the last 60-70 years.

Significant differences exist in the quality of life among people in different countries and even between different regions within a country. To improve the quality of life, basic requirements like food, clothing, and shelter must be satisfied, followed by needs for education, health, entertainment, etc. This chapter studies Human Development and India's condition in the Human Development Index.
