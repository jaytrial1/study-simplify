# Health

Health is an important and valuable asset. For an individual's family and social life to be great, it is essential that the person is healthy. In developing countries like India, attention has been focused on issues like population growth, common diseases, malnutrition, disabilities, contagious diseases like AIDS, mental disorders, and related matters.

Expenditure on health is not only for improving the quality of life but is also an **investment in human resource development**. India has made significant progress in the field of health.

India has achieved considerable improvement in child health and child death rates through child vaccination programs, providing vaccinations for Polio (OPV), Tuberculosis (BCG), Hepatitis-B, Diphtheria, Pertussis, and Tetanus (DPT), Measles, MMR, and Typhoid.

Campaigns have been started against deficiencies of iodine, vitamins, and iron. Diseases like plague, smallpox, and leprosy have been eradicated. Diseases like measles, chickenpox, malaria, dengue, hepatitis, tuberculosis, diabetes, cancer, and heart diseases have been substantially controlled. As a result, people are leading longer, healthier lives. A decline in birth rate, mortality rate, and child mortality rate has been recorded, and the life expectancy rate has increased.
