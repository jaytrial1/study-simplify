# Sectors of the Economy

This section describes the three main sectors into which various occupations and economic activities are classified.

Various occupations and economic activities are classified in three sections:
1.  **Primary sector**
2.  **Secondary sector**
3.  **Service sector**

This classification of economic activities is recognized as commercial structure. All the three sections and activities included in them may be discussed as follows:

-   **Primary sector:** Agriculture and agriculture related activities such as cattle rearing, cattle breeding, fishing, poultry farm, collection of forest products, mining of raw metal etc. are included in primary sector section.
-   **Secondary sector:** Small and large scale industries, factories, construction, electricity, gas and water supply are included in this section. This section is also acknowledged as industry, which includes production from small pin to gigantic machines.
-   **Service sector:** Different types of services are included in this section. Services like trade, communication, airways, waterways, education, health, banking and insurance, tourism and entertainment are included in this sector.

Normally the primary sector dominates in developing countries. Primary sector contributes the most in employment generation and national income. However, as there is rise in economic development, significance of primary sector decreases in comparison to secondary and service sector, and importance of industrial sector and service sector increases.
