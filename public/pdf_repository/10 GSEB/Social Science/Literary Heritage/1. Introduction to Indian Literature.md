# Introduction to Indian Literature

This section introduces the concept of literature as a form of communication and highlights the diversity and uniqueness of Indian literature from ancient times.

Man used expressions, symbols, drawings, and sounds to communicate thoughts, ideas, feelings, and emotions. This led to the invention of dialects and scripts, which contributed to the development of language. Over time, language was used to express thoughts, and the constructive use of language resulted in the creation of literature.

From ancient times, Indian literature has been known for its diversity and uniqueness. Indian scholars divided ancient literature into **Vedic** and **classical literature**. Besides these, some **folk literature** was also popular.
