# The Birth and Development of Urdu Literature

This section focuses on the emergence of Urdu as a new language and its growth into a rich literary tradition during the medieval period.

One of the most significant developments during the medieval period was the birth of the **Urdu language**. This new language quickly developed into one of the richest modern Indian languages, producing great poets such as:
*   Wali
*   Mir Dard
*   Mir Taqi Mir
*   Nazir Akbarabadi
*   Abdullahkhan
*   Galib
*   Iqbal

**Urdu prose** also developed in the early 18th century with the translation of historical works from Sanskrit into Urdu and the writing of original prose. **Muhammad Hussain Azad's 'Darbar-e-Akbari'** is considered one of the best works in Urdu language.
