# Early Regional Literature: Gujarati and Dravidian

This section explores the development of literature in early forms of Gujarati and the four major Dravidian languages.

## Early Gujarati Literature

Verses were composed in the earlier form of Gujarati language. Over time, Gujarati developed, and many literary works were created. The heritage of Gujarati literature was enriched by contributions from:
*   Narsinh Mehta
*   Mirabai
*   Dayaram
*   Akho
*   Premchand
*   Pritam

These poets composed beautiful verses, songs, garba, narrative poems, and chhappas. Later scholars who enriched Gujarati literature through their works include:
*   Narmad
*   Navalram
*   Kishorlal Mashruwala
*   Pannalal Patel
*   Umashankar Joshi
*   Mahipatram Ruparam Nilkanth
*   Goverdhanram Tripathi

## Dravidian Literature

The four Dravidian languages—**Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, and Malayalam**—developed their own scripts and literature. **Tamil** is the oldest, with literature dating back to early centuries CE.

According to tradition, three literary gatherings (sangams) were held where sages and poets recited compositions on themes like politics, war, and love. Famous works from this period include:
*   **"Ethutokoi"** (composition of 8 poems)
*   **"Tolkappiyam"** (Grammar)
*   **"Patthuppattu"** (The 10 songs)

**Thiruvalluvar** wrote the famous **'Kural'**, a verse work dealing with many aspects of life and religion. **'Shilppadikaram'** and **'Manimekhalai'** are among the most famous works of early Tamil literature.
