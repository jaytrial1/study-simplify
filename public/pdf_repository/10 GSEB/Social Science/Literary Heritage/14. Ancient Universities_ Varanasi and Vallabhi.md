# Ancient Universities: Varanasi and Vallabhi

This section describes the significance of Varanasi (Kashi) and Vallabhi as ancient centers of learning and religious importance.

## Varanasi (Kashi)

**Varanasi** was famous as a place of pilgrimage and a renowned education center by the 7th century. It grew as a religious center for Aryan culture during the Upanishadic period. **King Ajatshatru** of Varanasi was a great philosopher of the Upanishadic age and a strong supporter of education. The *Vyaas Samhita* mentions that **Maharshi Vedvyas** had his ashram in Varanasi.

Lord Buddha chose Varanasi for propagating his teachings. The philosopher **Adishankaracharya** moved to Kashi to adopt new principles of Vedantas. **Vallabhacharyaji** and **Chaitanya Mahaprabhu**, pioneers of Pushtimarg, gained prestige for their Vaishnav sects in Kashi. Many scholar families migrated to Kashi. Many princes also came for education in Varanasi. The monastery at Sarnath became a famous education center under Emperor Ashoka's patronage.

## Vallabhi

This university in Gujarat was a very famous education center in the 7th century. The contemporary rulers of the Vansh Dynasty and the people contributed significantly to making Vallabhi a well-renowned and large center of education. Vallabhi was a center of the **Hinayana cult** of Buddhism. In the mid-7th century, Buddhist scholars like **Sthirmati** and **Gunmati** were leading principals. Students from the distant Ganga-Yamuna areas came for higher studies. Chinese traveler **It sing** noted that Vallabhi competed with the famous Nalanda in Eastern India.

Vallabhi was a capital and an international port from 480 AD to 775 AD. The Maitrak Dynasty rulers patronized education and the university, despite not being Buddhist themselves. They helped the institution. In 775 AD, Arabians attacked, the Maitraks were defeated, and the university closed down. The names of famous scholars were written on the gate, and scholars demonstrating their knowledge gained higher rights in the royal assembly.

The university's knowledge, worship practices, and particular education system made it famous nationally and internationally. Students from India and abroad came for education. It was an international university teaching almost all branches of knowledge. It was maintained through charity from kings and landlords, showing people's loyalty towards the university. It was world-renowned for its knowledge of arts and science.
