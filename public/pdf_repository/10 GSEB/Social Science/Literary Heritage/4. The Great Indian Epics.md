# The Great Indian Epics

This section focuses on the two major Indian epics, the Ramayana and the Mahabharata, highlighting their content and influence.

The **Ramayana** and the **Mahabharata** are two great Indian epics. Their present form can be traced back to the second century.

## The Ramayana

The Ramayana tells the story of Ramchandra, the king of Ayodhya. It is smaller than the Mahabharata and narrates many attractive stories of adventure.

## The Mahabharata

The Mahabharata is the world's largest epic, containing one lakh verses. It describes the war between the Kauravas and Pandavas and includes numerous other stories. The **Bhagwad Gita**, a part of the Mahabharata, expounds deep philosophical principles, conveying the message of achieving 'Moksha' (salvation) through 'Gyana' (knowledge), 'Karma' (action), and 'Bhakti' (devotion).

Both epics have long influenced millions of Indians and Indian literature, implanting motivational 'sanskaras' (values) in people.
