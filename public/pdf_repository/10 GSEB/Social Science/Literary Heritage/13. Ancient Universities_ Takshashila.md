# Ancient Universities: Takshashila

This section details the ancient Takshashila University, its location, subjects, and famous alumni and visitors.

The ancient university of **Takshashila** was located at Rawalpindi in present-day Pakistan, serving as the capital city of the ancient Gandhar region. This university imparted education in 64 subjects. Most students stayed in ashrams to practice with their guru. **'Jivak'**, a disciple of Lord Buddha, learned Ayurveda here. According to belief, it was named after Taksha, son of Bharat, brother of Lord Ram. It was a renowned education center in the 7th century.

Students were free to study subjects of interest. Although limited to 20 students per teacher, they often taught more. Students from distant cities like Varanasi, Rajgruha, Mithila, and Ujjain attended in large numbers.

Princes from Varanasi and Prasenjit, the king of Kaushal, studied here. The great grammarian **Panini** and the expert politician **Kautilya** also received education from this university. Takshashila was considered the best center for higher education. Students typically stayed with their guru for practice. Education included:
*   Vedas
*   Military science
*   Gajvidhya
*   Archery
*   Grammar
*   Philosophy
*   Warlore
*   Astronomy
*   Astrology

**Chanakya**, the mentor of Chandragupta Maurya, also studied here. The Chinese scholar **Fa-Hien** visited this university in the beginning of the 5th century.
