# Classical Sanskrit and Other Ancient Literature

This section covers Sanskrit literature produced during the ancient period beyond the Vedas and Epics, including scriptures, Smrutis, and highlights from the Gupta period.

During the period of Sanskrit literature production, there was a combination of religious and worldly aspects of life. The **Puranas** played an important role in explaining the initial Vedic religion. Many scriptures and **Smrutigranthas** were composed during this time. These scriptures dealt with science and philosophy. For example, Kautilya's **'Arthshastra'** is a book on management, and there were also books on arts, mathematics, and other sciences. The Smrutigranthas explain religious teachings, laws, and customs.

The earlier **Buddhist literature** was written in **Pali**. It is divided into three sections, known as **'Tripitika'**:
*   Sukta pitika
*   Vinaya pitika
*   Abhidhamma pitika

Besides the Tripitika, many other works were composed in Buddhist literature.

The **Gupta period** is known as the **golden period** for the growth of Sanskrit poems and dramas. Great writers from this period include:
*   **Kalidas** (considered renowned worldwide)
*   Bhavbhuti
*   Bharvi
*   Bhartrihari
*   Banabhatt
*   Mangha

Kalidas's renowned works, known for their best poetic and dramatic style, include:
*   *Kumarsambhava*
*   *Raghuvansham*
*   *Meghdoot*
*   *Abhigyan Shankuntalam*
*   *Rutusamhar*

Bana wrote the *Kadambari* and **'Harsha charit'** (a biography of King Harsha). Other notable works from this period include:
*   Bhavbhuti's *Uttar Ramcharit*
*   Bharavi's *Kiratarjuniyam*
*   Vishakhadatta's *Mudra Raksha*
*   Shudrak's *Mrichchhkatikam*
*   Dandi's *Dashkumarcharita*

The subjects of these works included political events, romance, allegories, comedies, and philosophy.
