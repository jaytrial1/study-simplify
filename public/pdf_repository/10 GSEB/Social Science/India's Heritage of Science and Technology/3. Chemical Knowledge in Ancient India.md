# Chemistry (Alchemical Lore)

Chemistry is an experimental science useful for various applications:

*   Minerals, plants, seeds for agriculture
*   Making or altering various metals
*   Making medicine

**Acharya Nagarjun**, a learned Buddhist from Nalanda University, is known as an Acharya in Chemistry. He wrote books like '**Rasaratnakar**' and '**Arogyamanjari**'. Acharya Nagarjuna advocated using *Alopathy* alongside herbal medicines and is believed to have initiated the use of *mercury ash* as medicine.

Nalanda University had its own school of chemistry and a furnace for study and research. Chemistry texts describe main *rasa*, *uprasa*, ten types of poisons, various salts, and ash of minerals.

Examples of advanced chemical knowledge are seen in large copper statues:

*   A copper statue of Buddha from Sultangunj (Bhagalpur, Bihar) is 7.5 feet high and weighs one tonne.
*   A statue of Buddha at Nalanda is 18 feet high.

The corrosion resistance of the Vijay Stambh also exemplifies the high level of alchemical knowledge.
