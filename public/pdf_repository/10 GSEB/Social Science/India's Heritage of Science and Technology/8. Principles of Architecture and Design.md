This section details the ancient Indian science of architecture and design, known as Vastushastra, and its continued relevance.

# Vastushastra

Ancient India made immense contributions to the field of Vastushastra, which is considered an inseparable part of astrology. Vastushastra from India is gaining recognition and praise globally.

Pioneers in Vastushastra include **Brahma, Narad, Bruhspati, Bhrugu, Vashishtha, and Vishwakarma**. They propagated principles for the construction of:

*   Dwellings
*   Temples
*   Palaces
*   Ashwashala (stables)
*   Forts
*   Store-house of ammunition
*   Town planning

Descriptions of Vastushastra are found in '**Brihat Samhita**'. **Rana Kumbha** of Mewar revived this science in the 15th century.

According to tradition, **Vishwakarma** was the first architect of the Gods. He divided Vastushastra into eight sections. Vastushastra provides various information, including:

*   Selection of place
*   Shapes
*   Structure
*   Proper planning of things
*   Layout of temples
*   Location of Bhramsthan (central area), dining room, bedroom, etc.

Principles and understanding of Vastushastra have evolved over time, and it is now being adopted by foreigners.
