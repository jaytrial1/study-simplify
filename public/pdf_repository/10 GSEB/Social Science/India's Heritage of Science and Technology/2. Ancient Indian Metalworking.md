# Metallurgy

Indians have used metallurgy since ancient times. Ancient India made extraordinary progress in this field.

*   A metallic idol of a female dancer from the Indus Valley Civilization is an example of early achievement.
*   Statues of Buddha from the Kushan period were found at Takshshila.
*   During the Chola period in South India, numerous metal idols were created.
*   The statue of **Nataraja** is a world-famous masterpiece, preserved in a museum in Chennai.
*   Another well-known metal idol is **'Ram - The archer'**.
*   Artistic statues of Gods, Goddesses, birds, animals, and betel-nut cutters are considered excellent examples of metallic art.
*   The **Vijay Stambh**, a 24-feet high, 7-tonne iron pillar built by Chandragupta II, is an exquisite example of ancient alchemical lore. It has not corroded despite centuries of exposure to nature, demonstrating advanced knowledge of metals and their properties.
