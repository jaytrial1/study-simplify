# Astronomy And Astrology

Astronomy is a very ancient science. Many works (*Grantha*) related to Astronomy were written in India. Ancient Indian universities conducted organized and deep study of Astrology.

Planets, their movements, constellations, and other celestial objects were used for calculations, leading to the remarkable development of astrology and astronomy. Predictions were made based on planetary movements.

**Aryabhatta** made a remarkable contribution to Astronomy. The first Indian satellite was named "Aryabhatta" in his honor. He declared that the Earth rotates on its own axis and proved that the basic reason for a lunar eclipse is the shadow of the Earth, a concept that some scholars initially addressed as 'Ajarbhar'.

**Brahmagupta** popularized the laws of gravitation in his book "**Brahmasiddhant**".

**Varahmihir** was a great astrologer and astronomer. He divided astrology into 3 sections: *Tantra*, *Hora*, and *Samhita*. His *Grantha* named '**Brihadsamhita**' provides information on:

*   Effects of planets on a person's future and characteristics.
*   Various classes of animals.
*   Auspicious times for marriage.
*   Information regarding ponds, wells, and gardens.
*   Good omens for sowing.

We should feel pride in the expertise of our ancestors in various sciences.
