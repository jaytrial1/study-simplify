This section lists specific measures that can be taken to reduce or stop soil erosion.

## Measures to prevent soil erosion

*   Control the grazing activity on the land.
*   Plantation should be carried out in contour method in sloppy regions.
*   Plant trees in fallow land.
*   Construct check dams where there are streams.
*   Cultivate very deeply in the field to reduce the speed of the water.
