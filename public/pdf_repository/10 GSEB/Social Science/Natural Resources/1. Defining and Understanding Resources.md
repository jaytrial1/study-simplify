This section introduces the concept of resources, explaining what makes something a resource and the interaction between nature, humans, and culture in this process.

# Natural Resources

Human needs can be satisfied partially or completely through resources. There are thousands of elements in nature, but we cannot call all of them resources. These elements can be called resources only when they are utilised by man through his special knowledge and expertise. In other words, the things on which man is dependent, which can fulfill human needs, and which man has the physical and intellectual capacity to utilise can be called resources. In this way, anything which is used to fulfill human needs becomes a resource. In ancient times, man did not know about the minerals buried within the land, so these were not resources at that time, but today these are extremely useful due to their utility and the developed techniques of mining. The natural resources should have both the virtues of *utility* and *capability to work*. The resources are made of the interactions of the three, i.e., **Nature**, **man**, and the **culture**.
