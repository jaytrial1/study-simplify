This section outlines the essential responsibilities and precautions that drivers and riders must follow for safe road use.

# Key Duties and Cautions for Drivers and Riders

*   A driver should drive only if physically and mentally fit.
*   Every driver should always drive with utmost safety and caution.
*   Drive with full concentration, keeping a constant eye on the road and traffic, avoiding distractions.
*   Drive with special care for pedestrians, cyclists, children, the elderly, and disabled people (**vulnerable road users**).
*   Avoid causing discomfort or inconvenience to others when driving or stopping.
*   Do not be inattentive or distracted by passengers, animals, goods, equipment, or the vehicle's condition.
*   Ensure both the driver and passengers wear **seat belts**.
*   Motorcycle riders and pillion riders must wear a **helmet**.
*   Take care of your own safety and the safety of other road users while boarding and alighting from the vehicle.
*   Do not drive under any kind of intoxication.
*   Do not drive a vehicle with defects that endanger safety.
*   Before starting, adjust rear and side view mirrors for clear visibility. Check tyre pressure and the mechanical condition, especially brakes.
*   A rider should not catch or push any other vehicle while on a motorcycle or three-wheeled vehicle.
*   Remove your foot from the pedal or foot rest only when road conditions require it for safety.
*   When opening the door and getting out, be careful not to collide with vehicles coming from behind.
*   Never stop your vehicle in a way that obstructs traffic or its smooth flow.
*   Do not honk unnecessarily or continuously, or in designated quiet areas.
