This section discusses the increasing risk of road accidents and how seat belts protect occupants from serious injury or death during a crash.

## Importance of Seat belt

The increase in vehicles on the road has led to a higher chance of accidents. Using a **seat belt** and the **"Child Restraint System"** is the most effective way to protect drivers and passengers from serious injury during road accidents.

If a driver or passenger is not wearing a seat belt during a crash, they may collide forcefully with internal parts of the car, such as the steering wheel or windscreen. This can result in severe external and internal injuries, potentially causing immediate death. Wearing a seat belt helps prevent fatal injuries in such accidents.
