This section provides guidelines for approaching and moving through intersections, junctions, and roundabouts.

# Things to Keep in Mind at Intersection and Roundabout

*   Slow down your vehicle when approaching a crossroad, junction, or roundabout.
*   At an intersection, junction, or roundabout, give priority to vehicles coming from the right.
*   Indicate necessary signals when changing lanes at a roundabout.
*   Observe all precautions taken while turning left when exiting a roundabout.
