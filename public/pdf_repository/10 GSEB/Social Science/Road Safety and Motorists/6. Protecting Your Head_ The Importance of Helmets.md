# Importance of Helmet and Seat belt

## Importance of Helmet

When a two-wheeler is involved in an accident, the riders are often thrown off. If a rider's head hits the road or an object, the head's forward motion stops abruptly. However, the brain continues moving, striking the inside of the skull and colliding with opposite sides.

Such collisions can cause severe head injuries, potentially leading to immediate death. Wearing a standard **helmet** can protect against these serious injuries. The main function of a helmet is to reduce the impact force on the head and shield the head and brain from severe injury.
