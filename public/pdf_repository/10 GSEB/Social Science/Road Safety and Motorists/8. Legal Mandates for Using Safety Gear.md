This section outlines the legal requirements under the Motor Vehicle Act regarding the mandatory use of helmets and seat belts and the penalties for non-compliance.

# Legal Mandates for Using Safety Gear

## Do you know? (Helmet)

*   According to Section 129 of the Motor Vehicle Act, every motorcycle (two-wheeler) driver and any person above the age of four sitting behind must always wear a helmet of approved standards on public roads.
*   According to Section 194 (D) of the Motor Vehicle Act, riding or sitting on the back of a two-wheeler without wearing a helmet can result in a penalty, and the driving license can be suspended for three months.

## Do you know? (Seat belt)

*   As per Section 194 (B) of the Motor Vehicle Act, driving or traveling in a motorcar without wearing a seat belt is punishable.
