# Lane Traffic

When a road is divided into sections by lines for ease of traffic movement, each section is called a **'Lane'**.

Points to keep in mind while driving in a lane:

*   Light, medium-speed, and heavy vehicles should generally be driven in the leftmost lane.
*   Always drive in your own lane. Change lanes only when it is safe and after giving the appropriate signal in advance.

*   When a lane is marked for a particular class of vehicle, drive only that type of vehicle in that lane (e.g., cars or heavy vehicles cannot use a two-wheeler lane).
*   When a road is divided by a continuous yellow or white stripe, drive within that stripe and do not cross it.
*   Do not drive over a continuous yellow or white stripe unless there is an obstacle ahead.
*   When a broken line (**fault line**) is marked parallel to a continuous lane, a vehicle to the left of the broken line may cross it to overtake the vehicle in front. After overtaking, the vehicle must safely return to its original lane immediately.
