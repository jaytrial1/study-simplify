# Understanding Driving Licenses and Age Requirements

Driving any motor vehicle requires a **Driving Licence**. It is mandatory to have the correct type of license for the vehicle you intend to drive (e.g., motorcycle, car, heavy vehicle).

Age limits to get a driving license:

*   A person who has completed 18 years can get a license for all types of motor vehicles except heavy transport vehicles.
*   A person aged 16 years or older can get a license to drive a two-wheeler without gear (**Motor Cycle Without Gear - MCWoG**) with an engine capacity up to 50 CC.
*   A minor under the age of 18 years requires the written consent of a guardian.
