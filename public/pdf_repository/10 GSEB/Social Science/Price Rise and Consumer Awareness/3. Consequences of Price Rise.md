# Consequences of Price Rise

This section explains the negative and far-reaching effects of continuous price increases on the economy and the daily lives of people, highlighting the necessity of price control.

Continuous price rise causes massive, far reaching and adverse effects on economy and social life of people. To avoid these effects the need of price control has arisen. We will understand the effects of price rise as follows:

*   The vicious circle of price rise keeps on moving continuously because of increase in profit, increase in income, increase in purchasing power, increase in demand of goods and services; lives of poor and middle class people becomes miserable.
*   Rate of savings and capital generation decreases due to price rise. Production of necessary commodities decrease. New business, industries, employment stops.
*   Foreign investment decreases. Because of increase in import of goods, foreign exchange is spent, which gives rise to new problem.
*   Production of necessary goods decreases so scarcity is created. Living standard of the people deteriorates, the poor become poorer.
*   As the production cost of goods increases in the country, they become expensive, so the cost of export goods increases. Comparatively imported goods become cheap in the market. As a result exports decrease and import increases, balance of exchange is disturbed. Imbalance is created in import and export.
*   Living standard of poor or middle class deteriorates because of price rise. In order to fulfill requirements, immoral activities like theft, robbery, murder, crime, betting, hoarding, profiteering, black marketing, corruption, suicide increase in the society. Moral degradation occurs in society.

Thus, inflationary price rise is a hindrance for economy. So there is a desperate need to control price rise.
