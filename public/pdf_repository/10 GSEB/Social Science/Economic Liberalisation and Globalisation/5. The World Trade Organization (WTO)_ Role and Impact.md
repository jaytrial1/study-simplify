# The World Trade Organization (WTO)

The World Trade Organization (WTO) was established on January 1, 1995, by member nations of the United Nations. Its headquarters are located in Geneva, Switzerland.

## Objectives of the WTO

*   To remove obstacles affecting global trade.
*   To eliminate protectionist policies countries use for their domestic industries in foreign trade.
*   To coordinate global trade policies with economic policies.
*   To resolve trade-related disputes that arise internationally.

## Functions of the WTO

*   Establishing the necessary structure for implementing multinational trade agreements and related accords.
*   Playing a crucial role in debates and negotiations concerning multinational trade.
*   Promoting unbiased international trade practices.
*   Observing the national policies of different countries and suggesting necessary improvements.

## Impact of WTO on the Indian Economy

India has been a member of the WTO since its inception. The organization has had several impacts and offered benefits to India:
*   India's contribution to world trade has increased. Due to the rise in international trade, India's exports have grown significantly, increasing its share in world trade.
*   Membership in the WTO has boosted the export of readymade clothes from India.
*   India has been able to increase the export of its agricultural products.
*   Increased exports have reduced pressure on imports, and foreign investment has increased in foreign exchange reserves.

However, being a WTO member means India receives benefits but also has to comply with certain conditions. The ultimate impact depends significantly on how quickly India improves its infrastructure and how developed countries engage with India.
