This section defines privatization, outlines the processes involved, and examines its advantages and disadvantages.

# Defining and Implementing Privatization

**Privatization** is the process by which ownership and management of industrial units previously controlled by the state are transferred to the private sector.

This process can occur in different ways:
*   Areas previously reserved exclusively for the public sector are opened up for private sector participation.
*   The state may retain ownership of state-owned companies but transfer the management responsibilities to private companies.
*   Alternatively, the state may keep management control while transferring ownership to a private company.

## Advantages of Privatization

*   The number of production units in the industrial sector increased.
*   Production of capital-intensive and consumable goods rose due to privatization.
*   The working efficiency of the public sector improved, influenced by the privatization of public sector enterprises.

## Disadvantages of Privatization

*   Economic power became centralized, leading to an increase in the problem of monopoly.
*   Cottage industries did not develop adequately; primarily large industries benefited.
*   Prices did not remain under control, contributing to the problem of price rise.
