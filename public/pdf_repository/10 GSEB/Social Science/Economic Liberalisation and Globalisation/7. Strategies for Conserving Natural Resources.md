# Strategies for Resource Conservation and Preservation

To conserve and preserve natural resources effectively, the following strategies should be adopted:

*   **Renewable Resources:** Renewable natural resources like cultivable land, forests, and water resources should be used in a way that maintains their quality.
*   **Non-Renewable Resources:** Non-renewable resources such as coal, petroleum, and minerals should be used judiciously and efficiently.
*   **Industrial Location:** Decisions on industrial locations should aim to minimize transportation costs and promote "Eco-friendly development".
*   **Multi-purpose Resource Use:** Resources that can serve multiple purposes should be utilized in different ways. For example, irrigation systems can be used for electricity generation, flood control, and transportation.
*   **Waste Control:** Natural resources should not be wasted. Strict control should be placed on unplanned disposal of industrial waste, poisonous chemicals, and the expansion of slums.
*   **Renewable Energy:** Emphasis should be placed on using non-conventional energy resources like solar and wind energy.
