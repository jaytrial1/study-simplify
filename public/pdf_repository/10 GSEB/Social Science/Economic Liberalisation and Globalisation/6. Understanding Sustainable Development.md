This section defines sustainable development and explains the context and concerns that led to its emergence as a concept.

# The Concept of Sustainable Development

The idea of **Sustainable Development** arose due to the significant impact of human activities on nature and the environment.

According to a widely accepted definition, sustainable development is "development which meets the needs of the present generation without compromising with the ability of future generations to meet their own needs."

The current pace and pattern of development are raising concerns that the benefits and resources enjoyed by the present generation might not be available for future generations. Economic development has led to a tremendous decrease in the quantity of natural resources, and their quality is also deteriorating. This situation highlights the need to change the traditional idea of development and focus on sustainable practices.
