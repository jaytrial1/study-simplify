# Global and National Efforts for Environmental Protection

Recognizing the need for environmental protection, significant steps have been taken worldwide. The first "Earth Conference" was held in 1972 in Stockholm, Sweden. Since then, conventions and meetings have been organized periodically at the global level to decide on various steps for environmental protection.

India actively participates in these global efforts and is also making significant efforts at the national level. These include:

*   Providing information about pollution levels in different cities across the nation.
*   Establishing Central and state pollution control boards to monitor and control pollution.
*   Observing June 5th as "World Environment Day".
*   Passing the "Air Pollution Control Act" in India in 1981.
*   Signing international agreements concerning the deteriorating ozone layer, disposal of nuclear waste, and maintenance of bio-diversity.

Conservation of the environment is a shared responsibility. Failing to control pollution poses a significant threat to life on Earth.
