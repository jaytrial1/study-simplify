This section details steps and strategies for protecting wild animals and their habitats.

# Measures of Preservation of Wild Life

Preserving wildlife requires changing our approach and implementing specific measures:

*   **Change Attitude**: We must stop viewing forests solely as sources of unlimited income and prioritize their preservation to save natural wildlife habitats.
*   **Maintain Ecological Balance**: Steps should be taken to maintain the balance between herbivore and carnivore populations in forests. This includes preserving water sources and restricting domestic animal grazing.
*   **Strict Anti-Hunting Laws**: Implement and enforce strict laws banning hunting, imposing harsh punishments and fines for illegal activities like mining in forest areas.
*   **Protect Breeding Periods**: Make arrangements to ensure animals are not disturbed during their reproductive periods.
*   **Study Human Impact**: Study the potential effects of activities like fisheries, forest gatherings, and tourism on forests and wildlife, and take appropriate steps based on findings.
*   **Raise Awareness**: Organize widespread awareness programs in society about wildlife preservation.
*   **Ensure Protection Force Activity**: If wildlife protection forces are inactive, pressure should be applied to prioritize their work.
