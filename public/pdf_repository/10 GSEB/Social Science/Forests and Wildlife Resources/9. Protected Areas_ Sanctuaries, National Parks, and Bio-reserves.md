# Protected Areas: Sanctuaries, National Parks, and Bio-reserves

Sanctuaries, national parks, and bio-reserves are established to protect wildlife, though they have distinct differences.

## 1. Sanctuary

*   Human activities are permitted within certain limits.
*   Domesticated animals may graze with permission from authorities.
*   Established primarily for the protection of specific species.
*   Can be established by the Government through a defined process.
*   Famous examples include Periyar, Chandraprabha, and Eturnagaram.

## 2. National Park

*   Offers a higher level of protection compared to sanctuaries.
*   Often includes more than one ecosystem.
*   There is a complete ban on animal grazing.
*   Protection is not centered around a single species but encompasses the entire ecosystem.
*   Established through coordination between State and Central Governments.
*   Important examples include Kaziranga, Corbett, Velavadar, Marine National Park, Gir, and Dachigam.

## 3. Bio-reserve Zone

*   Formed according to international standards.
*   Aims to protect the physical and cultural diversity of a region.
*   Protects not only vegetation, insects, and land but also the lifestyle of local inhabitants.
*   Special arrangements are made for research and training related to bio-reserves.
*   External human movement is totally restricted in the core proclaimed area.
*   The average area is large, exceeding 5000 sq. kilometers.
*   Important national bio-reserves include Nilgiri, Gulf of Mannar, Great Nicobar, Sundarban, and Pachmadhi.
*   The Rann of Kachchh in Gujarat was declared a Bio-reserve zone in 2008 to protect its unique environmental conditions.

Development is necessary, but planning must consider its negative effects on the entire life system. The complete destruction or endangerment of even one species disturbs the food chain, with far-reaching consequences. Every organism plays a specific role; the loss of one disrupts the natural system in the long run. These effects on humans are often delayed, making us less aware. Current environmental dangers are results of past carelessness. Planned development and an environmentally friendly approach are essential today.
