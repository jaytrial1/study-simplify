## Red Panda

The Red Panda is found in the cold forests of the eastern Himalayas. Its diet includes bamboo sprouts, eggs, small birds, and insects. It is less active during the day. Besides India, it is also found in China, Nepal, Bhutan, and Myanmar.

## Ganga River Dolphin (Platanista Gangetica)

This freshwater dolphin is found in the Brahmaputra and Ganga rivers, typically in deep, calm water. It exists in the densely populated Ganga basin. Its existence is threatened by pollution from dirty water, sedimentation from deforestation, fisheries, river navigation, and industrial waste. Few remain outside the Ganga and Brahmaputra, with some found in the Chambal river. It surfaces frequently to breathe, making a "Suu-Suu" sound, earning it names like Sauns, Susu, or Suis. It is also found in rivers in Nepal and Bangladesh. The Ganga River Dolphin's existence is currently in danger.

## Leopard

The Leopard belongs to the cat family and is smaller than lions and tigers. It is found throughout India, sometimes in a dark black color. Large numbers are found in Gujarat's forests. Leopards often approach human settlements and are sometimes mistakenly called Cheetahs.

## Cheetah

The Cheetah is now totally extinct from India's forests. Its natural habitat is currently found in Africa. In India, cheetahs are only found in captivity (Zoos).

## One Horned Indian Rhino

This rhino species is seen in the marshy regions of Brahmaputra and Sundarban. It is hunted for its horns, used in medicine. It is a herbivorous animal. Conservation efforts have led to an increase in its numbers.

## Henotaro

This animal lives in arid and semi-arid regions, including alkaline forests, grasslands, deserts, and semi-desert areas. It is found in Gujarat's Greater and Smaller Rann of Kachchh, Banni, and Narayan Sarovar sanctuaries. Slightly taller than a fox, it has a fleshy round mouth and high ears. It hunts smaller birds and animals. Its presence can be identified by its footprints.

## Dugang

The Dugang is an aquatic animal found in very small numbers along India's western coast, as well as the coasts of East Africa, South-East Asia, and northern Australia. Its main food is sea grass and vegetation, though it occasionally eats aquatic animals. It is heavily hunted for its meat and fat. Once frequent along the Gujarat coast, especially Saurashtra, it is now rarely seen there.
