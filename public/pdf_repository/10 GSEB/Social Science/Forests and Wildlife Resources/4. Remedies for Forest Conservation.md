This section outlines various measures and strategies to protect and conserve forests.

# Remedies for Forest Conservation

Several steps can be taken to conserve forests:

*   **Find Alternatives to Wood**: Research should be conducted to find substitutes for wood to reduce its usage.
*   **Compensatory Afforestation**: Wherever trees must be cut for development or necessity, new trees of the same species should be planted. There should be a total ban on cutting immature trees.
*   **Industry Responsibility**: Industries relying on forest raw materials should be required to undertake afforestation for future needs.
*   **Control Eco-tourism**: Eco-tourism activities should be strictly controlled to prevent damage to forest cover.
*   **Public Awareness**: Organize widespread public awareness programs among local communities about the importance of forests.
*   **Education**: Include the importance of forests and conservation strategies in school and college syllabi.
*   **Develop Social and Agro-forestry**: Intensively develop these practices with planned steps to meet the needs for fodder and firewood.
*   **Alternative Energy Sources**: Implement alternative options like solar energy and natural gas in place of firewood.
*   **Sparse Resource Use**: Use forest resources sparingly. Remove infested trees to promote faster, healthier growth.
*   **Forest Fire Management**: Create a dedicated national system or force to quickly extinguish forest fires, which cause heavy damage.
*   **Manage Tourism/Pilgrimages**: Properly manage litter and impact from large gatherings (fairs, pilgrimages) in forested religious places to prevent pollution.
*   **Demarcate Pasture Areas**: Reserve specific areas for animal grazing.
