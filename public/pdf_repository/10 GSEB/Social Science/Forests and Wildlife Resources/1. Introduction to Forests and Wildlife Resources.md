# Introduction to Forests and Wildlife Resources

Human existence and development are heavily reliant on natural resources, with forests being particularly significant. Forests are generally understood as accumulations of trees, shrubs, or dense grass. Natural vegetation, also known as virgin vegetation, grows without human intervention. In India, true virgin vegetation is now primarily found in areas like the Himalayas, Sundarbans, and remote parts of the Thar desert.
