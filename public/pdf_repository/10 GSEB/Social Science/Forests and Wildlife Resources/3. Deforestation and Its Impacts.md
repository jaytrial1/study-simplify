# Deforestation and Its Impacts

Deforestation, the destruction of forests, is a global problem, not just confined to India. While natural tree destruction occurs, the loss caused by human activities is significantly higher.

## Impact of Deforestation

The effects of deforestation are extensive:

*   Increases the quantity of carbon dioxide in the atmosphere, intensifying the *greenhouse effect*.
*   Removal of forest cover leads to *soil erosion*, reducing farm fertility.
*   Forest area, especially in peninsular India, has significantly decreased.
*   Many organisms lose their habitats.
*   Wild animals may approach human settlements in search of food and water.
*   Increased instances of carnivorous animals killing domestic animals near forest edges are recorded.
