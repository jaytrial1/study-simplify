# Classification of Forests

Forests can be classified using various criteria. While classification based on climate was covered previously, this chapter focuses on types based on administration, ownership, and management.

## Types of Forests According to Administration

1.  **Reserved Forests**: These forests are under direct government control. Activities like lumbering, wood collection, and grazing are restricted.
2.  **Protected Forests**: These are looked after by the administration. Local people are allowed to collect wood and graze animals, provided they do not harm the trees.
3.  **Unclassified Forests**: These forests are not yet classified. There are generally no restrictions on tree felling or animal grazing.

## Classification of Forests According to Ownership, Administration, and Management

Forests in India are classified into three types based on these aspects:

1.  **State Forest**: Controlled by either the Central or State Government. Most of the country's forests fall under this category.
2.  **Communal (Community) Forest**: Controlled by Local Self-Government institutions like Gram Panchayats, Municipalities, Corporations, and District Panchayats.
3.  **Private Forest**: Owned by individuals. These are more common in states like Odisha, Meghalaya, Punjab, and Himachal Pradesh. Some are in poor condition or totally barren.
