# Rain Water Harvesting

This section describes methods for collecting and storing rainwater and outlines the main goals of this practice.

*Rain Water Harvesting* refers to special methods to collect the rainwater and conserve it. These include the construction of wells, small dams, khet talavadi (farm ponds) etc. Water is conserved through these mediums. This also raises the ground water level. This may fulfil the requirements of domestic use and agriculture.

## Main objectives of rain water harvesting:

*   To increase the capacity to conserve the ground water and increase the underground water table.
*   Reduce the water pollution.
*   Improve the quality of ground water.
*   To save the land routes from water logging.
*   Reduce the surface run off of water.
*   Fulfil the requirement of domestic water requirements during summer and during long dry spells.
*   Fulfil the increasing demand of water.
*   Make arrangement to store rain water in underground tanks in multi-storey residence in large cities or arrangement should be made so that the water percolates in the ground.
