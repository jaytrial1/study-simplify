# Conclusion: India's Global Identity and UNESCO Sites

India is a treasure house of rich cultural heritage. The places of Indian cultural heritage have given India a unique identity in the world. Tourists from India and abroad regularly visit these sites of Indian art and architecture. This significantly boosts the economic benefits of the tourism industry in India. Consequently, 32 monuments of Indian cultural and natural heritage have been included in the world heritage list by **UNESCO**.
