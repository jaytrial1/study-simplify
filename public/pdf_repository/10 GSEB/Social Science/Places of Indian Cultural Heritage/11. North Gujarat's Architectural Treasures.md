# North Gujarat's Architectural Treasures

## Patan (North Gujarat)

In **Patan**, the **Sahastralinga lake**, the **step-well of the Queen (Rani ni Vav)**, and the **Rudra Mahalaya** of Siddhpur are worth seeing architectures. The ruins of **Rudra Mahalaya** in Siddhpur, situated 26 km away from Patan, suggest the grandeur of the palace.

The **Rani ni Vav** (step-well of the Queen) was built by **Udaymati**, queen of Bhimdev-I, to provide water for her subjects. UNESCO included this *vav* as a **world heritage monument in 2014**. This step-well demonstrates the beautiful arrangement of water harvesting for public use in ancient times. **Siddhraj Jaysingh** built the **Sahastralinga lake** in 1140 A.D.

### Step-well of Queen (Rani ni Vav)

Apart from the fort at **Vadnagar**, the **Sharmishta lake** and **Kirti Arches** are worth seeing monuments. Kirti Arches are created on two erected pillars, forming a bow shape. The **Shamlaji Temple**, situated on the bank of the Meshwo river, is an ancient place of pilgrimage with marvelous architecture.

### Things to Know: Vav (Step-wells)

A **Vav** is a well with steps, typically having one, two, three, or four openings and three, six, nine, or twelve floors. **Nanda, Bhadra, Jaya, and Vijaya** are the main types of *vav*. Examples of *vav* architecture include the **Step-well of Adalaj** near Gandhinagar, the **Step-well of the Queen of Patan**, the **Adi-Kadi step-well** at Junagadh, and others in Nadiad, Mehmedabad, Umrath, Kapadwanj, Vadhwan, and Kaleshwari (Mahisagar district).
