# Prominent Pilgrimage Destinations in India

India has historically been a land of pilgrimages. People undertake various journeys, including the **Chardham** and **Twelve Jyotirlingas**. The Chardham pilgrimage includes **Badrinath** (Uttarakhand), **Rameshwar** (Tamil Nadu), **Dwarka** (Gujarat), and **Jagannathpuri** (Odisha). Additionally, the pilgrimage to the **51 Shaktipeeths** and the **Amarnath Yatra** are considered highly important.

**Pradakshinas** (circumambulations) like the **Girnar (Lili Parikrama)**, **Shetrunjya Pradakshina**, and **Narmada Pradakshina** also hold great significance.
