# Agra's Mughal Masterpieces

## Fort of Agra

The **Fort of Agra** is located in Agra city, Uttar Pradesh. It is made up of **red stones**, leading to its name, the Red Fort. It was built by **Akbar** in 1565 AD. The architecture reflects a blend of **Hindu and Iranian styles**. The fort has a 70-foot tall wall with a 1.5-mile circumference. The red stones are skillfully embedded, leaving no visible cracks. The **Jahangir Palace** within this fort was built by Akbar and shows the architectural style of Bengal and Gujarat. **Shahajahan** spent the last days of his life in this fort.

## Taj Mahal

The **Taj Mahal** is located at Agra in Uttar Pradesh, on the bank of the Yamuna river. It is recognized as one of the **Seven Wonders of the World**. It was constructed by Mughal King **Shahajahan** in memory of his queen, **Mumtaz Mahal**, who died in 1630 AD. Construction began in 1631 AD and was completed in 1653 AD, taking 22 years. Shahajahan employed expert Indian sculptors, as well as Iranian, Arabian, Turkish, and European artisans. The Taj Mahal is considered one of the best tombs globally. Shahajahan's deepest wish was for Mumtaz Mahal's name to become immortal through this tomb. The building is spread in a rectangular shape from North to South. Mumtaz Mahal's grave lies in the center, surrounded by a beautiful artistic **octagonal fence** carved out of stone. An inscription on one of its arches reads: "Pious hearts are welcomed in the garden of paradise". The architecture of the Taj glorifies India's rich heritage. Its grandeur attracts thousands of visitors annually.
