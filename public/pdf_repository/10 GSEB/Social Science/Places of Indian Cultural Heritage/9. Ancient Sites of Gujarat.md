This section focuses on two significant ancient cities from the Indus Valley Civilization located in Gujarat.

# Ancient Sites of Gujarat

## Dholaveera and Lothal

**Dholaveera** and **Lothal** were main cities of the **Indus Valley civilization**. Dholaveera is situated in Khadirbet at Bhachau taluka in Kuchchh district. Dholaveera is known for its **ideal town planning**, whereas it was also a renowned center of trade and commerce for the Harappan culture. Evidence of bead making factories and ornament making using shells, dating back 5000 years, has been found at Dholaveera.

**Lothal** is situated near the Ahmedabad-Bhavnagar highway. It is an architectural place that served as a main center of trade and commerce and a well-facilitated port of the Harappan culture in ancient times.
