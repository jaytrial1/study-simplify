# Junagadh and Ahmedabad's Historical Sites

## Junagadh

In **Junagadh**, several places are worth seeing, including the **stone inscription of Ashoka**, Buddhist caves of **Khapra Kodiya**, *Vav* (Step-well), old **Rajmahal**, **Navghan Well**, the tomb of **Mahabatkhan**, and the tomb of **Bahauddin Vazir's**. The **Bhavnath fair** is held in the foothills of Girnar on Mahashivratri.

## Ahmedabad

**Ahmedabad** is a historical city with many noteworthy architectures. Worth seeing sites include the **Fort of Bhadra**, **Jama Masjid**, **Mosque of Queen Sipri**, **Roza of Sarkhej**, **Kankariya Lake**, **Shaking Minarets (Jhulta Minara)**, **Sidi Saiyed Jali (Grill)**, **Temples of Hathi Sinh**, and the **Mosque of Queen Roopmati**. The **Shaking Minarets** outside Sarangpur Darwaza in Raipur-Gomtipur are famous for the unsolved mystery of their vibration. The **Sidi Saiyed Grill** is renowned for its intricate vegetational and geometrical carvings.
