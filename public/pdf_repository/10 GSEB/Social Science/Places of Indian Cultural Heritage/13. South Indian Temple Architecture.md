# South Indian Temple Architecture

Temples in South India are famous for their distinct style, known as the **Dravid Style** of art. They are characterized by their **pyramidal shape** with multiple stories. An attractive stone is typically placed on the very top. The temple yards were usually very huge. Many South Indian temples built in ancient times are listed below:

| :---------------------- | :----------------------- |
| Mahabalipuram           | Mahabalipuram-Tamil Nadu |
| Kailash Temple          | Kanchipuram-Tamil Nadu   |

| Virupaksha Temple       | Pattadakal-Karnataka     |
| Parashurameshwar Temple | Bhuvneshwar-Odisha       |
