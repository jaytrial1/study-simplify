# Vijaynagar and Fatehpur Sikri Architecture

## Hampi

The town of **Hampi** is situated on the bank of the Tungabhadra river near Hospet, in Bellary district of Karnataka. Hampi was the capital city of the **Vijaynagar kingdom**. The rulers of this kingdom were great lovers of art, and a peculiar type of architecture developed during their reign. The period of **Krishnadevaraya** is considered the golden age of this art, as it reached its peak during his time. The main feature of Vijaynagar architectural style was the carving of huge and magnificent **pillars from stones**. These pillars and columns were artistically adorned with figures of Gods, Goddesses, humans, animals, warriors, and dancers. In Hampi, during Krishnadevaraya's reign, the **Vithala temple** and **Hajra temples** were built. The **Virupaksha temple** and **Achyutaray temple of Lord Krishna** are also excellent examples of this architecture.

## Fatehpur Sikri

**Fatehpur Sikri** is located 26 miles away from Agra in Uttar Pradesh. Akbar founded this city in memory of the Sufi saint **Shaikh Salim Chisti**, making it his capital city. Building construction began in 1569 AD and was completed by 1572 AD. Among the best buildings here are the palace of **Birbal**, the Golden Palace of **Bibi Mariam**, the palace of the **Turkey Sultan**, **Jama Masjid**, and **Buland Darwaza**.

### Buland Darwaza

The **Buland Darwaza** of Fatehpur Sikri is 41 m wide and 50 m high. Other well-known and remarkable buildings in Sikri include the palace of **Jodhabai**, **Panch Mahal**, the **Tomb of Shaikh Salim Chisti**, **Diwan-e-Aam**, **Diwan-e-Khas**, and **Jyotish Mahal**.
