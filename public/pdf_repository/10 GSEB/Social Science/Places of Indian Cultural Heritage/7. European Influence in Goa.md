# European Influence in Goa

## Churches of Goa

Christian missionaries arrived in India with the Portuguese to spread Christianity. **Goa** served as the capital of the Portuguese. Many churches were built in Goa, such as the church of **Besalika of Bom Jesus** and **Besalika of God Jesus** in Old Goa. The relics of **St. Francis Xavier** are kept in a coffin in a church in Goa, and his body is said to have not decayed after many years. Besides these, there are many other churches in Goa. Goa is renowned for its beautiful beaches and its artistic churches.
