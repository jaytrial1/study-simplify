# Buddhist and Other Religious Sites in Gujarat

Many Stupas and Viharas were built in Gujarat during the **Kshatrap period**. Remains of Buddhist Stupas have been found at **Bordevi** of Junagadh district, **Dev ni Mori** near Shamlaji, and **Intva stupa** in Girnar near Junagadh. Besides these, many other architectures at Dev ni Mori, **Bavapyara**, **Uparkot**, **Khapra Kodiya**, **Khambhaliya**, **Talaja**, **Sana**, **Dhank**, **Jhinjhurijhar**, and **Kadiya Dungar** are seen in Gujarat.

There are many **Jain temples** on the Shetrunjya mountains at **Palitana** in Bhavnagar district. Some of these temples were built in the 11th century. The **Taranga Pilgrim** site is situated on the hills near Timba village at Kheralu taluka in Mehsana district, where the temple of **Taramati** is located. The **Somnath temple** at Gir Somnath district and the **Dwarkadhish temple** of Devbhoomi, Dwarka, possess gorgeous historical heritage. This list is not exhaustive, as many other places in Gujarat contribute to its rich and prosperous cultural heritage.
