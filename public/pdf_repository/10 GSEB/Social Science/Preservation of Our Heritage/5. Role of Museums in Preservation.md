# Role of Museums in Preservation

This section highlights the function of museums in safeguarding valuable historical artifacts and documents, including the legal framework supporting this.

According to the **Indian Treasure Trove Act, 1876**, any citizen finding ancient artistic or antique articles while digging must immediately notify the archaeological department officer. This helps preserve valuable items that could otherwise be lost. Under a 1972 act, the government has also sought information regarding private museums holding expensive artistic pieces.

Systematically archived documents for each state provide valuable guidance to historians and researchers. Museums play a crucial role in carefully preserving valuable items. Examples of significant museums in India include:
