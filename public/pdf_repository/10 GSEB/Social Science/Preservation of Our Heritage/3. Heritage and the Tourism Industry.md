# Heritage and the Tourism Industry

This section discusses how India's heritage sites contribute to the tourism industry and its associated economic and cultural benefits.

Many of India's natural and cultural heritage sites are recognized on UNESCO's World Heritage list. These sites attract numerous visitors from India and abroad who come to explore, visit, and research. This significantly boosts the tourism and transportation industries. Beyond economic benefits for the respective states, tourism provides opportunities to showcase India's diverse cultural arts and traditions globally.

Furthermore, tourism creates employment for local communities and helps keep vanishing arts and traditions alive. Tourism is now included as an educational subject, promoting tourism guiding as an independent profession. India also earns foreign currency from international visitors. By introducing diverse cultures, tourism highlights India's talent on an international platform. Infrastructure like roads, railways, water supply, and communication facilities develop around heritage sites. Tourism encourages activities such as photography, horse riding, and boating, simultaneously providing jobs for street vendors. Varied cultures, indigenous arts, and their unique features gain a platform for development through tourism.
