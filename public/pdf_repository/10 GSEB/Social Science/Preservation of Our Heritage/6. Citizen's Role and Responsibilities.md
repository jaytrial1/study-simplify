# Citizen's Role and Responsibilities

This section discusses the individual's part in preserving heritage, complementing the efforts of government and organizations.

While central and state governments, UNESCO, and NGOs work to preserve valuable heritage, individuals also have a significant role. Teachers should educate students about India's glorious heritage. Students and the public should learn about historical places, monuments, and ancient sites and help in their preservation. Everyone should care for these monuments, perhaps by listing heritage sites. Valuable artifacts should not be destroyed, broken, or stolen. Schools, colleges, and NGOs should organize programs like visits to historical places, speeches, exhibitions, discussions, and seminars to raise awareness. Once architectural, sculptural, or historical specimens are destroyed, restoring them to their original form is very difficult. Therefore, it is our moral duty to prevent their destruction or unauthorized movement. Our country possesses a rich and unique heritage.

India has many ancient ponds, lakes, wells, step wells, streams, and springs. Special care should be taken of sites like Malav lake (Dholka), Rani ni Vav (Patan), the Well of Champaner, Bhamario Well (Mehemdabad), and Navghan well (Junagadh), especially during monsoon. Their preservation requires constant attention.
