# Maintaining Cleanliness at Heritage Sites

This section provides guidelines for maintaining cleanliness and preserving the condition of picnic and tourist spots, particularly historical sites.

Central and state governments are taking steps to ensure the cleanliness and preservation of picnic spots, implementing special arrangements. Religious and historical places are major attractions for both local and foreign tourists, prompting government attention to their preservation.

To help preserve these sites:

*   Avoid using plastic.
*   Do not litter; use dustbins at picnic spots.
*   Do not damage historical monuments by writing or drawing on them.
*   Dispose of waste properly.
*   Do not spit after consuming tobacco or paan.
*   Do not pollute the areas surrounding historical monuments.
*   Exercise great care during monsoon for historical monuments like step-wells, lakes, ponds, and streams.
*   Be cautious when cleaning monuments, especially with archaeological chemical methods.
*   Educate domestic and foreign visitors to avoid damaging historical heritage.
*   If natural disasters damage historical places, make efforts to restore them to their original form.

Maintaining the beauty and cleanliness of tourist places is our responsibility, as they contribute to the nation's fame and prosperity. We should utilize science and technology to help maintain the originality of our ancient heritage.
