# Need and Necessity for Preserving Cultural Heritage

This section explains why preserving India's diverse cultural heritage is essential for national identity, future guidance, and preventing degradation.

India's multifaceted heritage brings glory to the nation. Cultural heritage includes sculpture, architecture, literature, various arts, and natural heritage. Both natural and human-made factors negatively impact our heritage, making preservation necessary. Our heritage is a source of pride for the people, who connect it with their identity. Thus, developing a positive approach to its management is important. Preserving vanishing arts and architecture allows future generations to be inspired by the work of ancestors. As we are not always fully aware of preservation needs, and our negligence contributes significantly to its degradation, preservation has become vital.

Our heritage serves as the nation's identity and a guide for the future. By understanding past errors, we can correct them in the present and plan effectively for the future. Heritage helps determine the direction of our development and is necessary for spreading new consciousness across the country.

Raising awareness among people about the damage to cultural and natural heritage sites is crucial. Both the government and the public must actively protect our heritage. Since heritage is considered an ideal for people, its degradation must be stopped. Significant damage has occurred due to invasions and our lack of awareness. Therefore, it is the moral duty of both the government and the general public to conserve and preserve our heritage.
