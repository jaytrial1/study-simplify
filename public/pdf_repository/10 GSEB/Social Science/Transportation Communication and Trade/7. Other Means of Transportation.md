This section covers alternative transportation methods like pipelines and ropeways, used for specific purposes and terrains.

# Other Means of Transportation

## Pipelines
Pipelines are used for transporting liquid materials such as water, mineral oil, natural gas, and other liquids. Examples include an oil pipeline from Naharkatia in Assam to Nunmati-Barauni, one from Kalol to Koyali in Gujarat, and from Salaya to Mathura. A pipeline also connects Bombay High to the Mumbai coast. In Gujarat, natural gas is transported by pipeline to Khambhat-Dhuvaran-Koyali-Ahmedabad. Cooking gas is supplied via pipelines to cities like Surat, Bharuch, Vadodara, Ahmedabad, Jamnagar, Morbi, Rajkot, and Gandhinagar.

## Ropeway
In mountainous regions, ropeways connect summits to transport goods and passengers. India has about 100 ropeways, found in places like Darjeeling, Kullu-Manali, Cherrapunji, and Haridwar in North India, and in mountainous areas near Chennai and Malai. In Gujarat, ropeway services are available at Pavagadh, Saputara, and Ambaji. Work has started on a ropeway at Girnar in Junagadh.
