# Introduction to Transportation, Communication, and Trade

The movement of people and goods from one place to another is called **transportation**. It is essential for economic and physical progress, enabling the exchange of goods and people and linking distant places. Transportation facilitates national integration and processes like industrialization and urbanization.

Communication involves the exchange of messages. In modern times, communication equipment like telephones, cell phones, and the internet are widely used. Satellites launched for space research have significantly improved telecommunication services in India.

**Trade** is a tertiary economic activity that encourages production. No country can be entirely self-reliant, necessitating exchanges with other countries. For example, India exports agro-products and imports items like date-palms and mineral oil.
