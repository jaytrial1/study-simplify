# International Trade - Exports

India exports a portion of its production, carefully managing quantities to avoid price increases within the country. India also imports raw materials for some products, manufactures goods, and then exports them.

Indian exports include:
*   Iron ore and minerals
*   Engineering goods (e.g., cycles, fans, sewing machines, cars, railway coaches)
*   Computer software
*   Chemicals and related products
*   Gemstones
*   Hide and leather goods
*   Cotton textiles
*   Fish and its products
*   Handicrafts
*   Tea and coffee
*   Jute and its products
*   Readymade clothes
