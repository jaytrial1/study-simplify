# International Trade - Imports

India imports various goods based on its requirements and domestic production levels. When domestic iron production is insufficient, India imports iron and copper. Due to high demand for transportation and machinery, India imports petroleum mineral oil and lubricants. Other significant imports include machines, pearls, gemstones, and edible oils. These items are imported from countries like the USA, Germany, Russia, Myanmar, and Iran.
