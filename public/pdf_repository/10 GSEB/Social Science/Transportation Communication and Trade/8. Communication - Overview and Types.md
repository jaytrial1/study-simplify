This section defines communication, highlights its importance, traces its historical methods, and classifies modern communication systems into individual and mass communication.

# Communication - Overview and Types

**Communication** is the arrangement to send or receive information from one place to another. The communication system is vital in routine life and extremely useful during natural hazards like floods, droughts, cyclones, or tsunamis for relief and rescue operations. It plays a significant role in economic, social, and cultural progress, and in maintaining national integration and unity.

Historically, messages were sent using methods like playing the *dhol* (drum), smoke signals, pigeons, and other animals. Modern communication, facilitated by science and technology, uses mail services, telephones, mobile phones, smartphones, and satellites, making communication very speedy and easy. Today, important events can be viewed live.

Communication can be divided into two main parts:
1.  Individual Communication System
2.  Mass Communication System

## Individual Communication System
Internet and smartphones are highly effective tools in the individual communication system. Services like email, e-commerce, and currency exchange have become very fast due to the internet. Social media applications have revolutionized telecommunication, allowing people, including villagers, to stay in touch locally and internationally.

## Mass Communication System
Mass communication systems have two mediums:
1.  **Print Media:** Includes newspapers, magazines, and pamphlets.
2.  **Electronic Media:** Includes Akashwani (radio) and Doordarshan (television).

Prasarbharti is an autonomous body responsible for transmissions in India, with Akashwani and Doordarshan as its two divisions. There are 415 radio stations in the country broadcasting programs in 23 languages. Radio is convenient and can be used even in remote areas, serving as an important contact method during natural disasters. With the use of satellites, Doordarshan telecasts news, weather reports, and programs on entertainment and education. Many private channels now also offer similar programs.
