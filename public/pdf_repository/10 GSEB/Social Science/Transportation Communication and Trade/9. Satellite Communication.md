This section explains the role of artificial satellites in communication and highlights India's achievements in satellite technology.

# Satellite Communication

Artificial satellites possess their own communication capabilities and also help control other communication mediums. The Indian National Satellite (INSAT) series are multi-purpose satellites launched by India. They assist Doordarshan with weather forecasting, warning about cyclones and thunderstorms, disaster warnings, research, and other telecasts. India has also developed indigenous launch vehicles like the Polar Satellite Launch Vehicle (PSLV) for deploying Indian Remote Sensing (IRS) satellites.
