This section discusses the historical evolution of transportation methods, from early human and animal power to modern vehicles, and explores the factors influencing transportation types.

# Transportation - Overview and Early Methods

Historically, humans lived a nomadic life, carrying their belongings themselves. With the development of agriculture and animal husbandry, animals became beasts of burden. In the modern era, auto vehicles are primarily used for transportation.

The types of transportation used are influenced by various factors:
*   **Physical Factors:** Location, climate, and relief (terrain).
*   **Cultural Factors:** Technical development, economic development, market demand, capital investments, and political decisions.

In plain regions, transportation relies heavily on roads and railways. In mountainous areas, animals and humans are still used. For instance, Yaks are used in the Himalayas, and Bhotia people assist in carrying goods during Everest ascents. Elephants, mules, and horses are used in mountainous forests, while camels are ideal for desert transportation. Manual carrying, like woodcutters carrying wood or coolies at railway stations, is also a form of transportation.
