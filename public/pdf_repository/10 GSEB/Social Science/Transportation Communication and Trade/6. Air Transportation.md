# Air Transportation

Airways are the speediest and most expensive mode of transportation. They are used to reach distant places, remote and dense forests, and areas difficult to access by road. India's weather is generally favorable for aviation year-round. Air travel is increasingly being utilized today.

Air services in India began with air mail services between Allahabad and Naini, operated by a private company. Currently, "Air-India" and other private companies provide air transportation services.

The Airport Authority of India manages 127 airports, including 15 international, 87 domestic, and 25 civilian airport terminals. Major international airports are located in Kolkata, Mumbai, Chennai, New Delhi, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, and Ahmedabad. Pavanhanse Helicopters provides helicopter services, particularly to ONGC and the Government.
