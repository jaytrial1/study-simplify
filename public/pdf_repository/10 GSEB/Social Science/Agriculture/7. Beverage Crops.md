# Beverage Crops

## Tea

**Tea** is a plant of both tropical and temperate zones. The processed leaf and tender sprouts are used for drinks. India produces the second most tea after China. Sri Lanka, China, and India are leading tea exporters. Favourable conditions include sloppy land for water drainage, good iron content in soil, 20°C to 30°C temperature, and about 200 cm of rainfall spread throughout the year.

Tea is grown more in Assam, West Bengal, Uttarakhand, Uttar Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, and Karnataka. Assam and West Bengal produce about 75% of the nation's tea. Tea leaves are skilfully plucked.

## Coffee

**Coffee** is grown on mountain slopes in the shade of larger trees to protect the leaves from direct sunlight. It needs 150-200 cm rainfall, 15°C to 28°C temperature, and sloppy mountain land. Coffee is grown abundantly in Karnataka, Kerala, and Tamil Nadu. The Coorg area of Karnataka is well-known for coffee production. Coffee seeds are extracted from the nuts, ground, and used as a drink.

## Cocoa

**Cocoa** is prepared from the seeds of the cocoa fruit and is used as a beverage. Chocolate is made from cocoa. It requires hot and humid climate and excessive rainfall. African countries are major producers. Cocoa production is increasing in Kerala, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, and Tamil Nadu.
