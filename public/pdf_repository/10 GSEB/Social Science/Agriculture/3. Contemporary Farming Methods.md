# Contemporary Farming Methods

Organic farming, sustainable farming, mixed farming, etc., are some current methods of farming in India.

## Organic Farming

The increasing use of chemical fertilizers and insecticides has led to destructive effects. The presence of these chemicals in grains, vegetables, and fruits adversely affects human health and the environment. The productive capacity and fertility of the land have reduced over time.

**Organic farming** is a method where urea or other chemical fertilizers and insecticides are not used. For crop nutrition, dung, earthworm fertilizer, composite fertilizer, etc., are used. Cow urine, neem solvents, buttermilk, etc., are used for crop protection. Organic products are nutritious, have natural taste, sweetness, and fragrance, and contain minerals, vitamins, and life-energising elements. There is now high demand for organic farm products, leading to good returns for farmers.

## Sustainable Farming

To retain soil fertility for longer periods, care is taken through crop rotation, using chemical fertilizers only when necessary, employing biotic controls for pests, and conserving water.

## Mixed Farming

Here, cattle rearing, poultry, sericulture, and fishery are carried out simultaneously with farming.
