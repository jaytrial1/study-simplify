# Major Cash Crops

## Cotton

**Cotton** is a **kharif crop** and holds a significant place as a cash crop in Gujarat and India. India ranks second globally in cotton production, usage, and export. Cotton (without seed) is obtained from the plant and is known as '**White Gold**' in India. Cotton seed oil is used as edible oil, and the seeds and oil-cake are used as animal food.

Favourable conditions for cotton include black lava soil that retains humidity, soil with plenty of minerals, hot and humid climate, 20°C to 35°C temperature, and 30-70 cm rainfall. The growing season is about 6-8 months. Frost damages the cotton crop. Major producing states are Gujarat, Maharashtra, Telangana, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Haryana, Rajasthan, Punjab, Tamil Nadu, and Odisha. Gujarat stands first in India for area sown, productivity, total production, and quality due to farmers adopting BT cotton seeds. Cotton is grown extensively in districts like Surendranagar, Rajkot, Vadodara, Ahmedabad, Sabarkantha, Mahesana, Botad, Bharuch, Kheda, Surat, Panchmahal, Amreli, Bhavnagar, Patan, Junagadh, and Jamnagar.

## Sugarcane

**Sugarcane** is a major crop in India, which has the largest area sown under sugarcane globally. India ranks second in production after Brazil. Sugar, gur (jaggery), khandsari (raw sugar), and ethanol are produced from sugarcane. Black fertile alluvial lava soil, hot and humid climate, 21°C-27°C temperature, and 75-100 cm rainfall are necessary. Uttar Pradesh leads in area sown, but Maharashtra leads in production. South Gujarat and Saurashtra produce more sugarcane in Gujarat.

## Jute

India produces the maximum **jute** globally. The jute fibre is called '**Golden Fibre**'. Jute is used to make cloth, gunny bags, mattresses, ropes, bags, footwear, handicrafts, etc. India faces strong competition from Bangladesh due to cheap labour in the jute industry. Jute requires fertile soil of delta regions with annual sediment deposition, hot and humid climate, 30°C to 40°C temperature, and more than 100 cm rainfall. Jute is grown more in the Ganga delta in West Bengal, Assam, Bihar, Odisha, and Uttar Pradesh.

## Tobacco

**Tobacco** is a **kharif crop**. Sandy loamy soil, 20°C temperature, and 100 cm rainfall are suitable. Soil is a more determining factor than climate. The four major global producers and exporters are China, Brazil, India, and U.S.A. In India, major states are Gujarat, Andhra Pradesh, Uttar Pradesh, and Karnataka. The Charotar region of Kheda and Anand districts, Mahesana, Vadodara, Panchmahal grow more tobacco. About 80% of India's bidi-tobacco is produced in Gujarat. Tobacco is used to make gutkha, bidi, cigarette, snuff, etc. Tobacco consumption is harmful to health. Sikkim is the first state to ban tobacco-gutkha.

## Rubber

**Rubber** is prepared from the latex milk oozing from rubber trees. Acetic acid is mixed with the collected milk and heated at a low temperature to obtain rubber. It is used in many industrial products like tyres and tubes. Rubber plantations require hot and humid climate and heavy rainfall. Malaysia ranks first globally. In India, Kerala, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Assam, and Tripura are rubber-producing states.
