This section outlines the policy and legal changes implemented by the government to improve land ownership, provide financial support, and regulate the sale of farm produce for the benefit of farmers.

# Institutional Reforms in Agriculture

Reforms related to land ownership, crop subsidy, and the sale of farm produce are considered **institutional reforms** in India.

*   The government abolished **landlordism** and stopped farmer exploitation. Under the '**land to the tiller**' law (Land Tenancy Act), land tillers were given ownership rights.
*   The **Land Ceiling Act** removed disparity among land owners.
*   Farmers receive financial help for crop subsidy through **Kisan Credit Card** and nationalized/cooperative banks.
*   Full crop insurance protection is provided through the **Prime Minister Crop Insurance Scheme**.
*   Financial help is given when crops fail due to drought or excessive rain.
*   Legal support promotes open auction for selling farm products in marketing yards.
*   Facilities like cooperative societies, market associations, cooperative warehouses, cold storages, transportation, and communication help farmers get the minimum support price.
*   Organizations purchasing farm products at support prices include:
    1.  **National Agricultural Co-operative Marketing Federation of India (NAFED)**
    2.  **Gujarat Co-operative Oil Seeds Growers' Federation (GROFED)**
    3.  **National Dairy Development Board (NDDB)**
