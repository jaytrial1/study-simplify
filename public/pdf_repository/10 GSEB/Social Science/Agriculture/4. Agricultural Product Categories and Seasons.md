This section outlines how agricultural crops in India are categorized based on seasons and provides a general classification of major agricultural products.

# Agricultural Product Categories and Seasons

Agricultural crops in India are grouped into three categories based on seasons:
1.  **Kharif crop**
2.  **Rabi crop**
3.  **Zaid crop**

| Kharif (Monsoon) Crops                                                                                                 | Rabi (Winter) Crops                                                                                                  | Zaid (Summer) Crops                                                                                                      |
| :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| - Crops taken during the rainy season. <br> - Time: June-July to October-November. <br> - Examples: **Paddy**, **maize**, **jowar**, **millet**, **cotton**, **til**, **groundnut**, **mung**, **matth**. | - Crops taken during winter. <br> - Time: October-November to March-April. <br> - Examples: **Wheat**, **gram**, **barley**, **mustard**, **linseed**. | - Crops grown during summer. <br> - Time: March to June. <br> - Examples: **Paddy**, **maize**, **groundnut**, **til**, **millet**, and fruits like **watermelon**, **cucumber**, **musk melon**. |
