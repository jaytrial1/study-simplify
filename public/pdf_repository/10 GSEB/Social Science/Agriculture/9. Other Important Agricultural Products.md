This section covers the cultivation of medicinal plants, condiments, spices, fruits, vegetables, and flowers, highlighting India's position in their production and export.

# Other Important Agricultural Products

## Medicinal Condiments and Spices

Gujarat ranks first globally in the production of **cumin seed**, **fennel seed**, and **isabgul**. India leads in the production and export of dried **coriander**, **fenugreek**, **mustard**, **suva**, and **ajvain**. India contributes about 35% to the total world production of condiments. Indian **black pepper**, **cinnamon**, **clove**, etc., are in high demand nationally and internationally.

Examples of medicinal plants include **Ashwagandha**, **tulsi**, **kariyatu**, **mindhi aaval**, **white musli**, **madhunashini**, **ashok**, **garmal**, **lindi piper**, **galo**, **allovera**. Flavoured crops include **mint**, **menthol**, **palmroza**, and **lemon grass**.

## Fruits, Vegetables and Flowers

India ranks second globally in **fruit** production after China. Fruits like **banana**, **mango**, **apple**, **grape**, **naspati** (pear), **orange**, etc., are grown. Bananas are grown in Tamil Nadu, Gujarat, and Maharashtra; apples in Jammu-Kashmir and Himachal Pradesh; and grapes in Uttarakhand, Maharashtra, Himachal Pradesh, Jammu-Kashmir, Punjab, Tamil Nadu, and Andhra Pradesh. Grape production is less than demand, leading to imports from Afghanistan, Pakistan, and Australia. Various **vegetables** and **flowers** like rose, jui, mogro, galgota are also cultivated. Thus, a variety of crops are grown in India.

### Things to know: Fodder Crops

With increasing cultivation of cash crops, there is a shortage of grass (fodder). Various types of grass like **Dharaf** (GujaraatDharaf-1), **Anjan** (Pusa Yellow Anjan), **Marcel** (Gujarat marvel grass-1), **Shaniar** (Gujarat shaniar - 1), **jinjvo**, **dhaman**, **hemeta**, and **clataria** are grown in Gujarat. Generally, all grass types should be harvested in October, about four months after sowing.
