# Oil Seeds in India

**Groundnut**, **til**, **soyabean**, **castor**, **mustard**, **sunflower**, etc., are considered oil seeds and hold an important position in the Indian meal. After extracting edible oil, the remaining oil-cake is used as animal fodder and biotic manure.

## Groundnut

Groundnut is a key oil seed. Favourable conditions include black or a mixture of loamy and sandy soil that doesn't retain water, 20°C to 25°C temperature, and 50-70 cm rainfall. It is sown as a **kharif crop** and also during summer where irrigation is available. Gujarat, Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, and Maharashtra are major producers. India ranks second globally after China. Gujarat leads India in groundnut production, with districts like Junagadh, Gir, Somnath, Amreli, Rajkot, Bhavnagar, etc., being major producers. Groundnut oil is widely used as edible oil in Gujarat.

## Sesam/Til

Til oil has been used in India since ancient times. In North India, it's a rain-based **kharif crop**. In South India, it's sown as a **rabi crop** and sometimes a **zaid crop**. Almost all states grow til. It has a high oil content compared to other oil seeds.

In almost all parts of the world, it is used as an edible oil. Major producers are Gujarat, Tamil Nadu, West Bengal, Karnataka, and Madhya Pradesh. Gujarat ranks first in India in til production and area sown, with Banaskantha growing the maximum. India is the largest exporter of til globally.

## Mustard

This is a **rabi crop** and important in North India. Mustard seed and its oil are used for medicine and as edible oil. Rajasthan, Uttar Pradesh, West Bengal, Gujarat, and Madhya Pradesh are major producers.

## Coconut

Coconut is a **plantation crop** of hot and humid coastal climate and saline land. Plantations are found in Karnataka, Kerala, Tamil Nadu, Andaman-Nicobar, etc. In Gujarat, it's grown in coastal regions. A shorter, higher-yielding variety has been developed. In South India, coconut oil extracted from the kernel is used as edible oil. Coconut water is a healthy drink.

## Castor Oil

**Castor** is known locally as **Divela**. It is both a **kharif** and **rabi crop**. India is the largest producer globally, with 64% of world production, followed by China and Brazil. About 80% of India's production comes from Gujarat. Andhra Pradesh and Rajasthan are other producers. In Gujarat, districts like Banaskantha, Patan, Sabarkantha, Rajkot, Junagadh, and Amreli produce it in smaller quantities. Oils from cotton seeds, sunflower, paddy, and maize are also increasingly used as edible oils.
