This section describes the advancements and changes in agricultural technology, including implements, seeds, fertilizers, and farmer guidance systems in India.

# Technical Reforms in Agriculture

Earlier, farming used simple implements like sickles, spades, hoes (pavdo), drill ploughs, ploughs, and bullock carts. Now, modern implements like tractors, trailers, rotavators, threshers, and harvesters are common. There is increased usage of chemical fertilizers, hybrid seeds, BT seeds, insecticides, drip irrigation, and green houses.

The changes in seeds, fertilizers, and farm implements in India are known as **technical reforms**.

*   Farmers who used leather buckets and water wheels now use submersible pumps, mono bloc pumps, solar pumps, drip irrigation, and sprinklers.
*   Chemical fertilizers like di-ammonia phosphate (D.A.P.), nitrogen, phosphorous, potash (N.P.K.), urea, and bio-fertilizers, liquid bio-fertilizers, and bio-tec seeds are used.
*   Farmers receive guidance through radio, television, newspapers, DD Kisan channel, SMS via the kisan toll-free number 18001801551 (Kisan call centre), Government Farm web portals, i-khedut, and mobile apps like 'agri market'.
*   New agricultural research and techniques are conveyed to farmers by **Gram Sevaks** in villages.
*   Farmer training centres are established in every district headquarter to train farmers.
*   Recent information and guidance are provided through agricultural fairs in Gujarat.
*   Agricultural Universities and colleges are established in every state. In Gujarat, Agricultural Universities are at Dantiwada, Junagadh, Anand, and Navsari. These universities conduct research and train experts.
*   At the national level, the **Indian Council for Agricultural Research (ICAR)** and the **Department of Agricultural Research and Education (DARE)** are active.
