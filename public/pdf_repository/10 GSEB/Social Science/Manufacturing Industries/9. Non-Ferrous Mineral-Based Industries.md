# Mineral Based Industries (Continued)

## Aluminium Refining Industry

Aluminium refining is another significant industry after iron and steel. Aluminium is valued for being lightweight, strong, malleable, an efficient conductor of electricity, and rustproof. Its ore is bauxite. Aluminium is often mixed with other minerals to create alloys useful in manufacturing cars, railways, aeroplanes, and various machines. Electricity accounts for a significant portion (about 40-50%) of the production cost. Therefore, this industry is typically located in areas where hydro-electricity and bauxite are easily available. Aluminium factories are established in Odisha, West Bengal, Kerala, Uttar Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Maharashtra, and Tamil Nadu.

## Copper Refining

The use of copper has increased due to its excellent electrical conductivity and its ability to mix easily with other minerals. Copper is essential in the electricity industry and is used in the manufacturing of refrigerators, air-conditioners, automobiles, radiators, and domestic utensils. India's first copper refining unit was founded by the Indian Copper Corporation (ICC) at Ghatshila in Jharkhand. In 1972, the ICC was taken over by Hindustan Copper Limited (HCL). Besides HCL, copper is also produced by the private sector today. However, domestic production is insufficient to meet demand, requiring imports.
