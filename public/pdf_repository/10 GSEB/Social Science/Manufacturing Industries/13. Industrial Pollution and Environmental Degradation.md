# Industrial Pollution and Environmental Degradation

While industrial production significantly contributes to a nation's economic development, it also increases pollution and leads to environmental degradation. Environmental degradation occurs when the quality of the environment deteriorates due to natural or human-induced reasons. Industries primarily cause four types of pollution:
*   Air pollution
*   Water pollution
*   Land pollution
*   Noise pollution

In today's competitive age, industries have heavily polluted air and water. Air pollution is exacerbated by harmful gases like carbon monoxide and sulphur dioxide emitted by industries. Water pollution has increased due to industrial effluents; many factories release their waste into rivers, disregarding environmental laws. Water pollution is currently more severe than in the past.

Noise pollution is a contributing factor to deafness in humans. The use of old machinery in industries and transportation increases noise pollution. Excessive noise can also cause mental tension.
