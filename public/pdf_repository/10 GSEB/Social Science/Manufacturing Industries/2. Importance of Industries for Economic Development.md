# Importance of Industries

Today, the survival and strength of nations depend significantly on industrial development. Economic growth is impossible without industrial progress. Countries with advanced industrial sectors, such as the United States of America, Russia, Japan, and South Korea, are prosperous and developed. Nations with undeveloped or less developed industries struggle to utilize their natural resources effectively as raw materials. They are often compelled to sell raw materials cheaply and repurchase finished goods made from the same materials at higher prices from other countries. Manufacturing industries currently contribute about 29% to India's national production.
