This section explains the different ways industries can be categorized based on various factors.

# Classification of Industries

Industries are classified based on factors such as human labour, ownership, and the source of raw material.

Based on human labour, industries can be divided into:
*   **Small scale industries:** These are typically under individual ownership and employ fewer labourers (e.g., sugar (khandsari) industry).
*   **Large scale industries:** These provide employment to a larger number of people (e.g., cotton textile industry).

Based on ownership, industries can be:
*   Private
*   Public
*   Collaborative
*   Cooperative groups

Based on the source of raw material, industries are classified into:
*   Agro-based industries
*   Mineral-based industries
