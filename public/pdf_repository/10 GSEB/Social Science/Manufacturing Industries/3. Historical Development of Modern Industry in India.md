This section discusses the state of Indian industry under British rule and the early attempts at establishing modern industrial complexes.

# Historical Development of Modern Industry in India

The administrative policies of British rule undermined India's traditional industrial structure. During the period of colonial rule, establishing industries using modern methods was difficult in India. The first industrial complex based on a charcoal iron furnace was founded in 1853 but failed. The first successful modern industrial venture was in the cotton textile industry in 1854. Following this, a jute mill was established in 1855 at Risra near Kolkata. In 1874, a factory for making iron was founded at Kulti, which closed after a few years but was restarted in 1881. A significant milestone was the founding of the Tata Iron and Steel Company at Jamshedpur in 1907, which marked a new era for Indian industry.
