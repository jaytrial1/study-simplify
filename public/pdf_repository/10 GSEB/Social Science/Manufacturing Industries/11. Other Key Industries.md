# Mineral Based Industries (Continued)

## Plastic Industry

The plastic industry is often referred to as the 'Sunrise Industry' due to the continuous increase in demand for plastic raw materials. Because plastic is waterproof and easily mouldable, it is widely used in packaging chemicals, textiles, building construction, vehicle manufacturing, and electronics. Important centres for plastic production include Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Bengaluru, Vadodara, Vapi, Kanpur, Coimbatore, and Chennai.

## Cement Industry

Cement is crucial for construction activities like building houses, roads, and dams. India ranks second globally in cement production, after China, contributing about 6% of the world's output. The raw materials for the cement industry include limestone, coal, gypsum, bauxite, and clay. Since the raw materials and the finished product are heavy, cement factories are typically located in areas where raw materials are readily available in abundance. The industry is well-developed in Gujarat.
