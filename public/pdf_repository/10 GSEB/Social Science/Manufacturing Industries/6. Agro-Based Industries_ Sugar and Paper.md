This section covers other important industries that rely on agricultural products as their primary raw material.

# Agro-based Industries (Continued)

## Sugar Industry

The production of jaggery from sugarcane juice is a very old industry in India. In terms of agro-based industries, the sugar industry ranks second after textiles in India. Sugarcane needs to be processed (squeezed) within twenty-four hours of harvesting to prevent the reduction of its water content and subsequent decrease in sugar content. This necessity is why sugar factories are typically located close to sugarcane growing areas. Sugar factories are found in states like Maharashtra, Uttar Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, and Gujarat. In Gujarat, developed centres include Bardoli, Gandevi, Surat, Navsari, Vyara, Bharuch, Kodinar, and Talala Gir.

## Paper Industry

Paper is manufactured from raw materials such as soft wood, bamboo, grass, and molasses. Paper is produced in states like Andhra Pradesh, Maharashtra, West Bengal, Odisha, Karnataka, Gujarat, Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Tamil Nadu, and Haryana.
