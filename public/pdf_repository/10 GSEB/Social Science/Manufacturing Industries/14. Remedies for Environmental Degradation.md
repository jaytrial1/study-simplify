This section outlines potential solutions and measures to control and reduce environmental degradation caused by industrial activities.

# Remedies to Curb Environmental Degradation

National progress should be pursued in a manner that does not degrade the environment. The extent of pollution can be reduced through proper planning for industrial development. Pollution can also be minimized by selecting quality equipment and fuel sources. Air pollution can be controlled using devices like filters, scrubbers, and precipitators. Water pollution can be prevented by treating industrial effluents before releasing them. Industrial water can be purified through appropriate treatment processes.
