## Chemical Industry

The chemical industry holds an important position in India. Chemicals are broadly classified into two types:
*   **Organic chemicals:** Petrochemicals are the main organic chemicals used in artificial fibres, synthetic rubber, plastic goods, colours, chemicals, and medicines. Organic chemical industries are located near oil refineries and petrochemical centres.
*   **Inorganic chemicals:** These include substances like phosphoric acid, nitric acid, salts, soda ash, caustic soda, and chlorine.

India is a significant player in the global insecticide industry. Gujarat is the leading state in India's chemical industry, with major centres including Ahmedabad, Vadodara, Ankleshwar, and Bharuch.

## Chemical Fertilizer Industry

India's first chemical fertilizer factory was established in 1906 at Ranipet in Tamil Nadu. The industry's development accelerated when the Fertilizer Corporation of India started its factory at Sindri. This industry is concentrated in Gujarat, Tamil Nadu, Uttar Pradesh, Punjab, and Kerala. In Gujarat, chemical fertilizer factories are located at Kalol, Kandla, Hajira, Bharuch, and Vadodara.
