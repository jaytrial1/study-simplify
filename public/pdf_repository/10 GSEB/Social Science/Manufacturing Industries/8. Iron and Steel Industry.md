## Iron and Steel Industry

The Iron and Steel Industry is considered the backbone of modern industrial and economic development, often referred to as a base or key industry. Its products are essential for manufacturing machines and constructing various structures.

The process of making iron has a long history in India; iron from India was historically imported to make swords in Damascus. The first factory in India specifically for producing iron was established at Portonova in Tamil Nadu but was later closed. A successful production of raw iron was achieved at Kulti. In 1907, a factory established at Jamshedpur began producing iron and steel on a large scale. Factories were also set up at Burnpur in West Bengal and Bhadravati in Karnataka. Iron and steel factories were later established in Bhilai, Rourkela, and Durgapur. Other large-scale factories were founded at Bokaro, Vishakapatnam, and Salem. The primary raw materials used for producing iron and steel are iron ore, coal, limestone, and manganese. A mini steel plant is located near Hajira in Gujarat. The administration of all major iron and steel factories, except for Tata Steel, is managed by the Steel Authority of India Limited (SAIL). India ranks fifth in the world in iron and steel production.
