This section covers industries involved in manufacturing various modes of transport and the electronic industry, noting their development and key centres.

# Transport Equipment Industry

You see many types of vehicles used for travel. Historically, animal-drawn vehicles were used, which were slow. In the modern age, the development of roads has coincided with the use of faster vehicles. The industry that produces these vehicles is known as the transport industry.

## Railway

Railway services are highly appreciated for travel in India. The railways manufacture much of their required equipment themselves, such as railway engines, passenger coaches, and goods coaches. These are also produced by the private sector. There are three types of railway engines: steam, diesel, and electric. Steam engines are now primarily used only for Heritage Trains operated for tourism. Diesel and electric engines are produced at:
*   Chittaranjan Locomotives Works in Mihijam, West Bengal
*   Diesel Locomotives Works at Varanasi
*   Tata Locomotive Works at Jamshedpur
Passenger coaches are manufactured at Perambur, Bengaluru, Kapurthala, and Kolkata.

## Road Vehicles

Before India's independence, cars were manufactured by assembling imported parts. Today, factories manufacturing trucks, buses, cars, motorcycles, scooters, and cycles are established within the country. Road vehicles are mostly manufactured by the private sector. India ranks fifth globally in the production of commercial vehicles. Indian-made vehicles and spare parts are now exported to foreign countries. Tractors and cycles are produced on a very large scale and exported overseas.

## Ship Building Industry

This is an ancient industry in India. In modern times, there are centres capable of building ships of modern design. These are primarily in the public sector and located at:
*   Vishakhapatnam
*   Kolkata
*   Kochi
*   Mumbai
*   Marmagao
Kochi and Vishakhapatnam build larger ships. Private dockyards mainly serve local needs.

## Aircraft Manufacturing

India has also started producing helicopters. Aircraft building units are located at Bengaluru, Koraput, Nasik, Hyderabad, and Lucknow. The manufacturing of aircraft specifically for passenger travel has not yet commenced in India.

## Electronic Industry

The establishment of the radio set and telephone industry in India in 1905 can be considered the beginning of the electronic industry. Bharat Electronics Limited (BEL) was founded in Bengaluru in 1956 with the aim of manufacturing equipment for Akashwani (All India Radio) and the weather department. Today, BEL produces various electronic equipment in collaboration with the Indian Space Research Organization (ISRO).

This industry has profoundly impacted people's daily lives, lifestyles, and the national economy. Bengaluru has become the major hub for this industry, often called the "Silicon Valley of India." Software parks, science parks, and technological parks have been established to support its growth. The future prospects for this industry in India are very positive.
