# Defining and Identifying Poverty

A significant portion of the population is unable to meet their basic and essential needs for life, such as food, clothing, shelter, education, and health services. This situation is termed '*comprehensive poverty*', and the individuals experiencing this condition are referred to as '*poor*'.

People Living Below Poverty Line (BPL): Poverty is a qualitative concept. In India, it is understood as the lowest level of life. Common characteristics of people living below the poverty line include:

*   Not being able to secure two meals a day.
*   Suffering from a lack of adequate housing.
*   Being compelled to reside in substandard or slum areas.
*   Having an income below a determined expected level.
*   Possessing a life span shorter than the national average life expectancy.

Further characteristics of people living below the poverty line (BPL) include:

*   Generally being illiterate.
*   Frequently suffering from various diseases due to insufficient nutritious food.
*   Children taking on family responsibilities at an age when they should be studying.
*   A high death rate resulting from malnutrition.
