# Poverty Alleviation Programme (PAP)

To ensure the success of poverty alleviation solutions, direct financial benefits from various welfare schemes should reach rural and urban people living below the poverty line. While program names may change, the basic objective remains: creating employment opportunities for poor families, improving agriculture, rural upliftment, ensuring food security, providing education, and developing skills through training to improve economic conditions, lead to social security, and lift them above the poverty line. Present central and state governments have implemented numerous welfare programs, aiming to improve the living standards of the urban poor by enhancing geographical and infrastructural services. Reducing unemployment is expected to reduce poverty.

Poverty alleviation programs or schemes can be broadly divided into five categories:
1.  Wage-based employment Programmes
2.  Self-employment Programmes
3.  Programmes related to food security
4.  Programmes related to social safety
5.  Programmes for elimination of poverty in urban areas.

Many programs are currently implemented across these categories. A compiled discussion of the main programs follows, focusing on objectives like providing employment in agriculture/irrigation/roads, food security, organic farming, and agricultural product sales to generate direct income and improve financial conditions for the poor.
