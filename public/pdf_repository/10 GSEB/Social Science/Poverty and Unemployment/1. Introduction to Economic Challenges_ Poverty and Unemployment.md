# Introduction to Economic Challenges: Poverty and Unemployment

The Indian economy confronts numerous significant and complex economic and social challenges. Among these, major problems include population growth, price rise, black money, poverty, unemployment, starvation, corruption, and terrorism. This chapter will focus specifically on the economic problems of *poverty* and *unemployment*.
