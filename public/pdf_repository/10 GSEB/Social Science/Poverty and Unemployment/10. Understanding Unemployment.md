This section introduces the problem of unemployment in India, defines what constitutes unemployment, and clarifies who is *not* considered unemployed under this definition.

# Understanding Unemployment

Among the current problems in India, unemployment is crucial. Poverty often originates from unemployment. This is a long-term, deeply rooted economic problem faced by many countries worldwide.

Meaning of Unemployment: An individual is considered *unemployed* if they are an adult (aged 15-60 years), ready to work at the prevailing market wage rate, possess the energy and qualification to work, are actively seeking employment, but are unable to find it. A situation where a group of people faces this condition is called *unemployment*. Unemployment faced against one's wish is mandatory unemployment.

Individuals who are *not* considered unemployed include those demanding wages higher than the market rate, those outside the 15-60 age group, those who are handicapped, weak, sick, or old, those who are lazy, housewives not seeking external employment, and those unwilling to work despite being capable.
