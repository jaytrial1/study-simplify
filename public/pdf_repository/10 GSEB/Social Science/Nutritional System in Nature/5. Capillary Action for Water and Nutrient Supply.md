# Capillary Action for Water and Nutrient Supply

Rainwater is stored underground due to **gravitational force**. After the rainy season, this groundwater rises to plant roots via **capillary action**. This natural system is well-planned and self-governing.

According to natural principles, deep soil acts as a reservoir of nutrients. As moisture rises from the ground by capillary action, it brings minerals stored in the deep soil upwards. These minerals accumulate near the roots, making nutrients available to them.
