This section describes an experiment to illustrate the concept that elements taken from nature by vegetation eventually return to the earth.

# Experiment: Demonstrating Material Cycles

## Material required for the experiment:

*   10 green leaves
*   10 dry leaves of the same plant
*   Matchbox
*   Container

## Method:

1.  Weigh the green leaves and dry leaves separately and record their weights.
2.  Note the difference in weight between the two.
3.  Burn the dried leaves in a suitable container using a matchstick and observe.
4.  Weigh the remaining ashes.

## Summary:

The difference in weight between green and dry leaves represents the water content in the green leaves. Burning the dry leaves releases fire (energy taken from solar energy) and smoke (carbon taken from air as carbon dioxide). The remaining ashes represent the elements the vegetation took from the earth, which have returned to the earth.

## Conclusion:

The experiment concludes that the elements utilized from nature by vegetation eventually return to their original state, helping to maintain balance in nature.
