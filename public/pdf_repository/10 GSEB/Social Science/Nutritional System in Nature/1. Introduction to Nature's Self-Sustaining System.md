This section introduces the concept that nature provides for vegetation without human intervention, highlighting its self-governing and self-sustaining characteristics.

# Nature's Self-Sustaining System

Man has not contributed to providing nourishment to natural vegetation. Nature provides essential elements like earth, water, air, sky, and fire to vegetation, enabling forests to produce fruits and flowers. Examining a leaf reveals no nutrient deficiency, indicating plants obtain all necessary nutrients naturally from nature. This suggests that nature is a well-planned, self-governing, and self-sustaining system. The process of obtaining seed from a tree and a tree from seed is a continuous natural cycle.
