# Classification of Natural Arrangements

Padmashri Dr. Subhash Palekar has classified nature's system into four types of arrangements:

1.  Regulation of nutrients through the food cycle.
2.  Provision of nutrients to plant roots by capillary action.
3.  Rain by Cyclone (Thunderstorm).
4.  Activities of local earthworms.
