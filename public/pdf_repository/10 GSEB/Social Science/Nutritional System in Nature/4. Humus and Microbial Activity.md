# Humus and Microbial Activity

**Humus** (organic matter) is a significant component connected with the food cycle. It is an organic substance where biological, biochemical, and chemical processes continuously occur. As biomass forms in living matter, older substances disintegrate, leading to constant creation and decay.

Biomass is formed by the **decomposition** of organic matter, carried out by millions of microbial species. These microbes require **sucrose** (raw sugar) for energy, which they obtain from plant roots. In nature, humus forms near the roots, not away from them.

Green leaves produce sucrose through photosynthesis. About 25% of this sucrose reaches microbes through the roots. In return, microbes create humus, making nutrients available to the roots.
