This section details the negative consequences of terrorism on the social fabric and economic stability of a region or nation, covering aspects like fear, distrust, disrupted services, and financial costs.

# Socio-Economic Impacts of Terrorism

Terrorism has significant negative effects on both society and the economy.

## Social effects of Terrorism

*   Terrorism contributes to the **disintegration of the nation**.
*   It creates feelings of **fear and suspicion** among people due to acts like robbery, violence, etc., affecting all age groups. Education suffers in affected areas.
*   Mutual **faith and brotherhood diminish**.
*   It often leads to **communal conflicts**, fragmenting social arrangements. Chaos and unrest are generated. People cannot celebrate social functions with enthusiasm, disrupting inter-relations.

## Economic Impact of Terrorism

*   Terrorism prevents the creation of a conducive atmosphere for **business and industry development**, negatively affecting trade relations.
*   Due to adverse effects on business and industries, people may **migrate** to other places.
*   Some terrorist organizations **extort money** from wealthy individuals, industrialists, employees, and businessmen.
*   Terrorists engage in anti-social activities like **drug trafficking and black money**, creating socio-economic problems.
*   Terrorists destroy infrastructure like railways, radio stations, roads, bridges, and government offices. **Crores of rupees** must be spent on rebuilding.
*   The government spends significant amounts on **safety and security**.
*   State and national **transportation and tourism industries** suffer great losses.

Thus, terrorism is harmful both socially and economically, making its resolution necessary.
