# Introduction to Social Challenges in India

India, a large democracy with a vast population, is characterized by people of diverse religions, languages, and cultures living together. This coexistence and secularism are defining features. As Indian society transitions from traditional to modern, numerous changes have occurred, giving rise to various social, economic, political, and cultural problems. This chapter will focus on two significant challenges: **communalism** and **casteism**.

Despite the collective efforts of people from different backgrounds during the freedom struggle, the harmony, unity, and tolerance that once prevailed seem to have diminished after independence. Today, negative factors like communal conflicts, caste-based quarrels, and regional violence pose serious challenges to social goodwill, secularism, democratic values, and national integrity.
