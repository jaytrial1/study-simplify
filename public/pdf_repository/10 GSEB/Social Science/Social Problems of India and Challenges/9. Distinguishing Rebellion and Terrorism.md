| Rebellion                                                                                             | Terrorism                                                                                                                                |
| :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| - Rebellion is a national problem. <br> - It is spread on the regional level against own government. <br> - It is carried out with the help of local people. <br> - Development of state suffering from rebellion is hindered. | - It is a global problem. <br> - It is either against own country or against other countries.
