This section details constitutional provisions specifically aimed at addressing the unique challenges faced by Scheduled Castes, particularly concerning untouchability and access to religious institutions.

# Specific Constitutional Provisions for Scheduled Castes

*   **Article 17:** **Untouchability is totally eradicated**, and its practice in any form is prohibited. Any disability imposed due to untouchability is a legally punishable crime.
*   **Article 25:** Grants states the right to frame or continue laws that allow public institutions for social welfare or reform to keep Hindu religious institutions open to all Hindus. The term "Hindu" here includes Sikhs, Jains, and Buddhists, and "Hindu religious institution" includes their respective religious institutions.
