This section outlines constitutional provisions specifically designed to protect the interests of Scheduled Tribes, focusing on restricting external interference in their areas and safeguarding their land and resources.

# Specific Constitutional Provisions for Scheduled Tribes

*   **Article 19(5):** Empowers state governors to impose **restrictions** on the right to unrestricted movement, settlement, property acquisition, or business/trade in the interest of Scheduled Tribes. These rights allow for framing special laws to restrict exploitation in tribal regions concerning land transactions, money lending, and other forms of exploitation.
