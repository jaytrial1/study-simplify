# Progress and Development Initiatives for Vulnerable Groups

Various schemes and programs initiated by the government for the development of Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes, and backward classes are leading to improved conditions for these groups. Many individuals have gained opportunities for social, economic, and political progress.

Other initiatives mentioned in the text include:
*   Opening **hostels** under five-year plans.
*   Offering **scholarships**.
*   Providing counselling and training classes for **competitive examinations**.
*   Setting up **Residential schools (Ashram Shala)** for educational progress.
*   Relaxing minimum requirements for age, fees, and qualifications for government jobs for candidates from these groups.
*   The **Dr. Baba Saheb Ambedkar Organization** felicitates those working for social understanding, upliftment, transformation, justice, and human dignity of weaker sections.
*   Separate divisions and special officers are appointed at state and central levels for their welfare and protection.
*   A **National Commission** exists at the central level.
*   Various social, educational, and economic development schemes are run by state and central governments.
