This section defines communalism, explains its harmful effects on society and the nation, and outlines various strategies to address and eradicate it.

# Understanding and Combating Communalism

Religion is a matter of personal faith. India is a secular country, and therefore, communal behavior is against the spirit of its constitution. Communal tension arises when a group of people from one religion or sect oppose another. When people prioritize their religious interests, assert the superiority of their religion, and view citizens through a communal lens rather than as individuals, it divides society.

Even decades after independence, communalism remains a challenge. It is harmful in several ways:
*   It creates **social tension**, turning friends into rivals and fostering an atmosphere of difference and hatred.
*   It leads to **communal riots** or quarrels.
*   These consequences are detrimental to **democracy**, **national integrity**, and **national development**.

## Struggle Against Communalism

Communalism hinders individual, societal, and national progress. Various steps are necessary to eradicate it:
*   The government should take **punitive actions** against communal elements.
*   **Education** is crucial. Curriculum should include positive aspects, and activities like organizing prayers of all religions and celebrating social functions can help children develop respect for all faiths.
*   **Political parties** based on communal ideas should not be recognized. Election ethics must be implemented and followed.
*   **Media** (Radio, TV, Cinema) should spread messages of secularism and tolerance, broadcasting programmes that promote national interest and nationalism.
*   **Religious and political leaders** should collaborate to combat communalism.
*   **Youth** must develop a secular and scientific attitude at social and educational levels, with efforts from both government and society.
*   Recognizing that **national interest** and **national pride** are above religion, caste, state, or language can unite people and nurture nationalism and national integrity.
