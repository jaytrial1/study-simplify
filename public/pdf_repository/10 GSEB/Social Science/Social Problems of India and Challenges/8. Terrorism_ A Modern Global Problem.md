# Terrorism: A Modern Global Problem

In the 21st century, terrorism has emerged as a significant problem for human society, affecting the globe. Terrorism causes destruction of human rights, bloodshed, fear, chaos, violence, and unrest. Although terrorism has no inherent connection to any religion, terrorists often link it to religion to justify their actions. Terrorism hinders national progress and violates the principle of "live and let live."

Terrorism is characterized as an **organized, planned, and deliberate violent act**. Terrorists engage in activities such as suicide attacks, bombing, hiding and using weapons, kidnapping, plane hijacking, suicide attacks, and drug smuggling.
