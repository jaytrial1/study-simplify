This section provides an overview of the diverse architecture found in Gujarat, encompassing various religious structures and public utility buildings.

# Architecture of Gujarat

The sculpture and architecture of Gujarat include temples of various religions, mosques, Buddhist viharas, Maths, Stupas, Chaityas, Cave-temples, and Jain temples. Besides these, different types of building construction useful for society are seen, such as:
*   Royal palaces
*   Forts
*   Domes
*   Gates
*   Kirtistambh (tower of fame)
*   Inns (sanctuary)
*   Upashrya, Visamas, Varandas
*   Skylights (Jharookho)
*   Watch towers
*   Arches
*   Wells
*   Step-wells
*   Lakes and ponds
*   Figures of animals and birds

These structures are considered beautiful and magnificent.

## Mosques in Gujarat

*   **Jama Masjid (Ahmedabad):** Near Teen Darwaja, built by Sultan Ahmad Shah-I in 1424 A.D. It has 260 pillars and 15 domes.
*   Other structures include **Sidi Saiyyad grill** with minute carving, **Roza of Sarkhej**, **Shaking towers** (Ahmedabad), **Mosque of Queen Sipri** (also known as 'Mosque of Nageena'), and **Jama Masjid at Champaner**. Many such mosques exist in big cities of Gujarat.

## Jain Temples in Gujarat

Jain temples of Hathi Singh, Kumbhariyaji, Shankheshwar, Siddhgiri, Shetrunjya giri (Palitana) are well-known for their sculpture, architecture, and carving.

## Step-wells of Gujarat

Step-wells (vav) are splendid examples of construction.
*   Step-well of Adalaj
*   Step-well of Dada Hari (Hari ni Vav, Ahmedabad)
*   Rani ni step-well of Patan
*   Step-well of Dabhoi
