# Introduction to Indian Cultural Heritage

India is world-famous for its rich cultural heritage. The art of **sculpture** and **architecture** has brought a unique identity to India. The town planning of the Indus culture of ancient India was remarkable and considered greater than contemporary town planning elsewhere.

## Sculpture

The art of shaping figures or designs with facial expression in round or in relief, professionally performed by a sculptor with the help of chisel and hammer, is known as **sculpture**.

## Architecture

The simple meaning of architecture is the **art of construction**. The word '*vastu*' is used for architecture in the Sanskrit language and is very renowned. In this context, the art of constructing buildings, houses, towns, wells, forts, minarets, temples, mosques, and tombs is called architecture. The skill of the architect is seen in their architectural work.
