# Gopuram and Temple Components

## Architecture of Gopuram

**Gopuram** means the entrance of the temple. The construction of temples gained momentum due to the encouragement of Pandya rulers of South India. They built high outer walls and beautifully decorated gates outside the temple. These gates are known as 'Gopuram'. The artistic glory of the Gopuram has become more popular than the temples themselves. Gopurams of Kanchi and Madurai provide aesthetic pleasure even today when viewed from a distance.

## Linear Sketch of Temple Components

*   **Garbha Gruh:** A small, dark rectangular room in which the idol is kept. In Gujarat, it is known as 'Gabharo'.
*   **Gopuram:** A gateway of a South Indian temple. The two lower storeys are convex shaped, and the other two storeys are aloft to make a pyramidal structure, making the Gopuram strong.
*   **Mandap:** A big hall constructed on pillars or a huge area in front of the main gate.
*   **Pinnacle (Shikhar):** The pointed exterior part of the Garbha Gruh at the top. It is sometimes plated with gold or brass.
*   **Viman:** A part of the temple structure, semi-circular or sloping in shape, with many storeys, looking like a pyramid.
