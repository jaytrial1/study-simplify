# Cave Architecture

Cave architecture is considered a manmade place of beauty. Famous specimens of cave architecture include:
*   Caves of Ajanta and Ellora at Aurangabad
*   Elephanta caves near Mumbai
*   Udayagiri and Bagh near Gwalior
*   Bhuvneshwari near Odisha
*   Khandagiri and Nilgiri

Cave inscriptions of Ashoka have been carved on the walls of three caves of Berber mountain, 16 km away from Gaya, describing works of charity done by Ashoka. Caves of Assam and Darjeeling, Sudama cave of Bihar, and cave of Seeta are also well-known cave architecture sites.
