Here is information about the components of Mosque architecture:

*   **Galiyara:** The way to enter and exit from the mosque.
*   **Kibla:** This part of the architecture is a section of the mosque or a hall wall where namaz (prayer) is performed. It is always in the direction of Kaba.
*   **Livan:** The room in a mosque with pillars, typically the prayer hall.
*   **Maksura:** The end of the mosque wall, often separated by a railing.
*   **Mahrab:** The interior part of the mosque wall, typically of human height, which indicates the correct direction to Mecca for prayer. (In India, Mahrab is generally kept in the west).
*   **Sahan:** The campus or courtyard of the mosque where followers of Islam gather for prayer.
