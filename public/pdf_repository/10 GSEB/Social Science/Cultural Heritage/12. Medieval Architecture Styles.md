This section explores the architectural developments during the medieval period in India, including the influence of Islamic styles and regional variations.

# Medieval Architecture

The architecture of mosques, minarets, royal palaces, bridges, and inns is seen in the medieval age.

*   **Delhi Sultanate:** Qutub-ud-din Aibak built **Qutub Minar** and **Quwwat-ul-Islam mosque**. A similar mosque named **Adhai-Din-ka Jhopara** was built by him in Ajmer.
*   **Bengal:** Adina Mosque at Pandua, Tomb of Jalal-ud-Muhammad Shah, and Tantipara mosque were constructed. This region developed its own special style.
*   **Jaunpur:** Sultans of Turkey built **Atala Mosque**. It features an artistic grill around the dome and walls/ceilings decorated with Indian designs, including lotus.
*   **Malwa:** A peculiar style of Mandu buildings is seen under the protection of Sultans. Many tombs were created there with huge and impressive domes and attractive balcony carving. The Tomb of Hoshang Shah is completely made of marble in Indian style.
*   **Other Provinces:** Rulers of Kashmir and Bahmani built the dome of Bijapur, buildings at Bidar and Gulbarga, and the 'Madrasa' of Mahmud Gava. The Vijayanagara Empire is known for temples of Hampi (Vithala Swami and Hazar Rama), Gopurams, and artistic Pillars.
