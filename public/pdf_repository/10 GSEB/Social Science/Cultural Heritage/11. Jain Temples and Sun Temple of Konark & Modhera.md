# Jain Temples and Sun Temples

## Jain Temples (Derasar)

Jain temples are found at various places in India. Notable sites include:
*   Vaibhar at Rajgruha
*   Vipulachal, Ratnagiri, Udayagiri, and Shramgiri (five Jain temples)
*   Siddhakshetra Pilgrim in Samet Shikharji, Bihar (Madhuvan), where Aadinath Bhagwan and 20 other Tirthankars attained Nirvana. There are temples of Abhinandan Nathji and Parshwa Nathji. Bhagwan Mahavir reached here, and many sages attained salvation (Moksha).

In Gujarat, there is a Jain temple at Palitana and Shankheshwar temple at Panchasara.

Jain temples at Delwara (Mount Abu) and Ranakpur in Rajasthan are excellent and wonderful examples of construction carving, artistic skill, and sculpture.

### Delwara Temples, Mount Abu

Especially the Delwara temples of Abu, constructed with white marble, are famous in India and abroad for their attractive, minute, and artistic sculptural work.
*   **Vimal Vasahi:** Constructed by minister Vimal Shah.
*   **Luna Vasahi:** Constructed by minister Vastupal.

These temples are wonderful and memorable gifts of Jain religion to Indian culture. Jain temples are worldwide famous due to their art of sculpture and architecture.

## Sun Temple of Modhera

Modhera temple in Gujarat (Mahesana District) was built in 1026 A.D. during the reign of Solanki King Bhimdev-I.
*   A gem was studded in the middle of the crown of Sun God's idol.
*   The entrance gate on the Eastern side is constructed so that the first ray of Sun falls directly on the gem, lighting up the entire sanctum and creating a divine atmosphere.
*   Twelve different images of Sun God and erotic sculptures are seen.
*   The carving is done in the **Iranian school of Art**.
*   There are 108 small temples surrounding the outside tank, creating a pleasant sight at dusk and dawn.
