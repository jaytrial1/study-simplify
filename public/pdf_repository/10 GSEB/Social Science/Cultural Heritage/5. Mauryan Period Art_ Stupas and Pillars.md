This section covers the art and architecture of the Mauryan period, focusing on stupas, their components, pillar inscriptions, and the famous Lion Capital of Sarnath.

# Mauryan Period Art

The period of King Ashoka was considered a golden period for Buddhist religion and an age of sculpture and architecture. Buddhist religion significantly contributed caves, viharas, chaityas, and stupas to architecture.

## Stupa

An oval-shaped construction under which the remains of Lord Buddha's body were kept in a box is known as a **Stupa**. Five famous stupas from King Ashoka's time are:
1.  Stupa of Sanchi
2.  Stupa of Sarnath
3.  Stupa of Berat
4.  Stupa of Nandangadh
5.  Stupa of Devanimori in Gujarat

Besides these, Chaityas, Viharas, and Maths were also constructed.

## Stupa of Sanchi

The Sanchi stupa was built during the Mauryan Period and is situated in Madhya Pradesh. The original stupa was made of bricks and was half the size of the present stupa. This Buddhist stupa is a precious specimen of the art of architecture.

## Linear Sketch of Stupa Components

*   **Harmika:** The railing around the top of the oval-shaped stupa.
*   **Medhi:** Elevated circular path around the stupa, used for pradakshina.
*   **Pradakshina Path:** A slightly elevated circular path around a temple or place of worship. Pradakshina is done with the place of worship always to the right side.
*   **Toran:** A gateway built on two high pillars with an artistic horizontal beam on it, used by devotees as an entrance.

## Pillar Inscription

Stone inscriptions were made from a single rock. Stone inscriptions carved out by the religious order of Emperor Ashoka are considered the best specimens of engraving on stone. They were polished so well that they gleamed. Such pillars were erected in places like Ambala, Meerut, Allahabad, Sarnath, Loriya near Nandangadh, Sanchi, Kashi, Patna, and near the Bodhivruksha near Bodh-Gaya. They were carved in **Brahmi Script**.

## Pillar at Sarnath

The stone inscription at Sarnath is the best specimen of sculpture. The pillar has **four lions** facing four directions. Sarnath was the preaching place of Lord Buddha. **Dharmachakras** are carved below the lion images, indicating the triumph of religion. This is why it is called Dharmachakra. Apart from this, it has sculptures of an elephant, horse, and bullock. This chakra has been placed in the national flag of Republic India. The images of four lions have been placed as our national emblem. This is considered one of the best specimens of sculpture in the world.

## Stone Inscription Locations

Stone inscriptions carved by religious orders of Emperor Ashoka are best specimens of engraving on stone. Wooden art and stone sculpting show excellent specimens of art and architecture. Along with the erection of pillars of wood and stone, beautiful arches are engraved on doors, emphasizing religious behavior. Such stone inscriptions have been found from Peshawar, Dehradun, Thane, Mumbai, Dhauli, Jaugada (Odisha), and Chennai.

In Gujarat, such a stone inscription has been found from the foothill of Girnar mountain on the way to Junagadh. Other stone inscriptions providing information in Sanskrit and Gujarati languages include those at Palitana Jain temples on Shetrunjay mountain and the Jain temple of Hathisinh in Ahmedabad (1847 A.D.).
