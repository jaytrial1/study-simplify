# Ancient Indian Town Planning

India acquired expertise in the field of **town planning**. Many such towns have been found during archaeological excavations. The towns were typically divided into three sections:
1.  **Fort of rulers (Citadel)**
2.  **Houses of administrative officers** situated on the upper part of the town.
3.  **Residences of common people** situated on the lower part of the town.

People of the Indus valley civilization developed many beautiful and systematic towns, considered more advanced than many other contemporary civilizations from an architectural point of view. Among these, Harappa and Mohenjo-Daro had the best town planning.
