This section discusses the evolution of temple architecture in India, including the unique chariot temples and contributions of dynasties like the Pallavas and Cholas.

# Temple Architecture

In the field of temple architecture, many temples are seen with high pedestals and pinnacles with stairs. Some are flat with a pradakshina path encircling the 'Garbha Gruha' (innermost part of the temple). Examples include the Bhoomara Shiva temple near Jabalpur and temples of Larkhan in Bijapur district. Copper statues like the Lord Buddha statue in Nalanda (Sultangunj) and Jain temples of Mathura are unique specimens of architecture and sculpture.

## Chariot Temples

A unique feature of the Pallava age can be seen in the famous chariot temples of South India, which are carved out from a single rock. Kailashnath temple of Kanchi and the temple of Vaikuntha Perumal are considered best examples of architectural art.

Apart from these, Mandapas at Mahabalipuram and the chariot temples of Mahabalipuram are world-famous. Each one is a **rock-cut temple**. These chariots are named after the Pandavas. The chariot temple of Dharmaraja is the biggest, whereas the chariot temple of Draupadi is the smallest.

There is a greater contribution of **Pallava kings** to temple architecture. Temples constructed in Kanchi, their capital city, are very famous.

Tanjavur was the capital of the **Chola Dynasty**. The great **Brahadeshwar temple** was the first temple constructed by Rajaraja of the Chola dynasty. It is about 200 metres high and is considered an extraordinary temple of ancient India. Metallic and stone idols of this age have special characteristic features. The bronze statue of Natraj is the best example of idol sculpting of that time. Chola temples have their own splendor in the field of art and architecture.

Meenakshi temple in Madurai is one of the huge temples of India, spread over a vast area. It has four main 'Gopurams' from an architectural point of view and is considered a wonderful temple.

In the medieval period, Khajuraho was the capital city of the Chandela rulers of Bundelkhand. Chandela rulers built beautiful temples at **Khajuraho**. The style of the pinnacle varies from others. Khajuraho temples are considered among the finest temples.
