This section specifically details various cave sites located within the state of Gujarat, highlighting their characteristics and historical periods.

# Cave Architecture in Gujarat

Several caves have been found in Gujarat, showcasing ancient architecture.

## Caves at Junagadh

There are three groups of caves in Junagadh:
1.  **Group of Bavapyara caves:** Situated near Bavapyara math, these 16 caves are in three layers intersecting at right angles (4 in the first line, 7 in the second, 5 in the third). They were possibly carved during the first and second century A.D.
2.  **Caves of Uperkot:** These are double-storeyed caves. Their remains suggest they might have been carved during the end of the second century and the beginning of the fourth century A.D.
3.  **Caves of Khapra and Kediya (Caves of Kund):** These were floored caves, possibly carved in the third century A.D. There are twenty pillars, now in ruins.

## Caves of Khambhalida

Discovered in 1959 A.D., these caves are situated at Khambhalida near Gondal, 70 km from Rajkot. Three are remarkable. A Chaitya Gruh with a stupa is in the middle cave. Figures of Bodhisattvas and statues of devotees stand on both sides of the entrance path, supported by trees. They are from the second or third century A.D.

## Talaja Cave

The mountain of Talaja is situated near the mouth of the Setrunji river in Bhavnagar district, famous as the pilgrimage site of Taaldhwajgiri. Thirty caves have been carved from stones with huge gates. Ebhal Mandap (Sabhakhand) and Chaitya Gruh are excellent examples from sculpture and protection points of view. This Buddhist cave architecture belongs to the third century A.D.

## Sana Cave

This group of caves is situated on the top of Sana mountain in Vankiya village, Una taluka in Gir Somnath district. These caves are spread on Sana mountain like a bee-hive.

## Dhank Cave

Dhank giri is situated in Dhank village of Upleta taluka at Rajkot district. It is considered that they might have been built in the beginning of the fourth century A.D.

## Jhinjhuri Jhar

There are many Buddhist caves in the west of Dhank, about 7 km from Siddhsar, in the valley of Jhinjharijhar. It is considered that they were built in the second century A.D.

## Khapra - Kodiya Caves at Kachchh

These two caves are situated on the top of a mountain near old Paatgadh, in Lakhpat taluka of Kachchh. They were discovered by K. K. Shastri in 1967 A.D.

## Caves at Kaliya Dungar

There are three caves in Kaliya Dungar at Jhagadiya taluka in Bharuch district. These are considered old architectured specimens of Buddhist religion. The architecture is marvelous. An eleven-foot-high statue of a lion with two bodies and one mouth, carved as a single rock pillar, is a notable feature.
