This section discusses the physical properties of heat and electrical conductivity, highlighting how metals are good conductors.

Can you hold a hot metallic pan which is without a plastic or a wooden handle and not get hurt? Perhaps not! Why?
> **Answer:** No, you cannot hold a hot metallic pan without a protective handle because metals are good conductors of heat, and the heat from the pan would quickly transfer to your hand, causing a burn.

Try to list some other experiences in which a wooden or plastic handle protects you from being hurt while handling hot things. On the basis of these experiences what can you say about the conduction of heat by wood and plastic?
> **Answer:** Other experiences include holding a hot iron box with a plastic handle, using wooden spoons for cooking, or holding the plastic handle of a soldering iron. Based on these, wood and plastic are poor conductors of heat.

You must have seen an electrician using his screw driver. What kind of handle does it have? Why?
> **Answer:** An electrician's screwdriver typically has a plastic or rubber handle. This is because plastic and rubber are poor conductors (insulators) of electricity, protecting the electrician from electric shock.
