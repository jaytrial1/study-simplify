This section describes the process of cutting mature crops (harvesting) and separating grain from chaff (threshing and winnowing).

# Harvesting

**Harvesting** of a crop is an important task. The cutting of crop after it is mature is called harvesting. In harvesting, crops are pulled out or cut close to the ground. It usually takes 3 to 4 months for a cereal crop to mature.

Harvesting in our country is either done manually by sickle (Fig. 1.7) or by a machine called **harvester**. In the harvested crop, the grain seeds need to be separated from the chaff. This process is called **threshing**.
