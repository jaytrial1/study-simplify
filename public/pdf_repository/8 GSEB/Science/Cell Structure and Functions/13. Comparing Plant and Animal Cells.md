**Image Description:**

The image depicts two types of cells, labeled (a) and (b), commonly used to illustrate basic cell structure in introductory biology.

*   **(a) Plant Cell:** This cell has a regular, almost rectangular shape. It possesses a prominent cell wall, a rigid outer layer outside the cell membrane. Inside the cell, the key features are:
    *   **Cell Membrane:** A boundary inside the cell wall.
    *   **Chloroplasts:** Small, green oval structures, primarily located along the periphery of the cell, representing the organelles responsible for photosynthesis.
    *   **Cytoplasm:** The general internal substance of the cell, surrounding all organelles.
    *   **Vacuole:** A large, empty-looking space taking up a substantial portion of the cell's volume.
    *   **Nucleus:** A prominent organelle near the vacuole.

*   **(b) Animal Cell:** The shape is somewhat irregular and rounded. It features:
    *   **Cell Membrane:** An outer boundary that encapsulates the cell.
    *   **Cytoplasm:** The fluid content where other structures are embedded.
    *   **Nucleus:** A noticeable, often central structure, with a textured appearance inside.
    *   **Vacuoles** are shown as smaller, less conspicuous structures compared to the plant cell.*
