# Artificial Satellites

Many **artificial satellites** orbit Earth. They are man-made and launched from Earth. They orbit Earth much closer than the moon.

India has built and launched several artificial satellites, starting with Aryabhata. Others include INSAT, IRS, Kalpana-1, EDUSAT, etc.

Artificial satellites have many uses: weather forecasting, transmitting TV and radio signals, telecommunication, and **remote sensing** (collecting information from a distance).
