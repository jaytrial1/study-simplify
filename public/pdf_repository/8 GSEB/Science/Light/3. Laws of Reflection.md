# Laws of Reflection

A ray of light, which strikes any surface, is called the **incident ray**. The ray that comes back from the surface after reflection is known as the **reflected ray**. A ray of light is an idealisation; in reality, we have a narrow beam of light made up of several rays. For simplicity, we use the term ray for a narrow beam of light.

Draw a line making an angle of $90^{\circ}$ to the line representing the mirror at the point where the incident ray strikes the mirror. This line is known as the **normal** to the reflecting surface at that point. The angle between the normal and incident ray is called the **angle of incidence** ($\angle i$). The angle between the normal and the reflected ray is known as the **angle of reflection** ($\angle r$).
