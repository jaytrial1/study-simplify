# Specific Notable Painters: Rabindranath Tagore
This section covers the life and artistic contributions of Rabindranath Tagore, known for his unique style and influence on modern Indian art.

**Rabindranath Tagore** (1861-1941 AD), a Nobel Prize-winning poet for *Gitanjali*, was also a talented painter. He made **Shantiniketan** a hub for literature, music, dance, and painting. He developed a distinct painting style, free from traditional Indian and Western influences, earning him the title of **Bhishma Pitamah of modern Indian painting**.

He created over 2000 paintings characterized by a flow in lines, freshness in colors, and varied emotional depictions. His paintings are archived in Shantiniketan, Rabindra Bhawan, and the National Gallery of Modern Art in Delhi.
