# Notable Painters of India and Gujarat
This section lists some renowned painters from India and specifically from Gujarat.

Among the renowned painters of India are **Raja Ravivarma** and **Rabindranath Tagore**.

Prominent painters from Gujarat include **Kalaguru Shri Ravi Shankar M. Raval** and **Rameshbhai Pandya**. Many other well-known and anonymous painters have also contributed significantly to Indian painting.
