# Specific Notable Painters: Abanindranath Tagore
This section details the life and work of Abanindranath Tagore, focusing on his role in establishing the Bengal School of Art.

**Abanindranath Tagore** (1871-1941 AD), born in Bengal, deeply studied Indian, Mughal, Tanjore, Chinese, and Japanese painting styles. By learning from western painters, he created a new style that gave a new direction to Indian painting. His style reflects the **Indian environment and sentimentality**. His paintings, often using the **Japanese wash method**, show atmospheric depth and expressive characters.

He founded the **'Bengal School of Art'** to promote painting. The prevalent painting style of that era became famous under the name 'Bengal School of Art'.
