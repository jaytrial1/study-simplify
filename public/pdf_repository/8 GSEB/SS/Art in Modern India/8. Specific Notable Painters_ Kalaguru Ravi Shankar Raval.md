# Specific Notable Painters: Kalaguru Ravi Shankar Raval
This section focuses on Kalaguru Ravi Shankar Raval, a significant figure in promoting art in Gujarat.

**Ravi Shankar Rawal**, known as **Kalaguru**, received his art education in Mumbai and established Ahmedabad as his workplace, guiding the development of painting in Gujarat. His painting **'Bilvamangal'** was presented at the Bombay Art Society's annual exhibition, making him the first Gujarati artist to do so. The painting, done in the Rajput style using flat colours, won a gold medal.

In 1924 AD, Ravi Shankar Rawal started the monthly magazine **'Kumar'**, which introduced people to paintings and artists, fostering art culture, interest, and awareness in Gujarat. He founded the **Gujarat Kala Sangh** and provided free painting training to young people.

He painted a historical event: the trial of Gandhiji and Shankarlal Banker for treason on March 12, 1922, in a court in Ahmedabad (now the circuit house in Shahibaug).
