This section outlines the overall structure of the court system in India, from the highest court down to the local level.

# Structure of the Judiciary

The Indian Judiciary administers Justice in case of both **civil matters** and **criminal matters**.
*   **Civil matters** include complaints about the ownership of house, land or other properties.
*   **Criminal matters** include complaints about theft, loot, murder, combating, etc.

In order to administer justice to the citizens of India, **Taluka Courts**, **District Courts**, **High Courts**, and **Supreme Court** have been established. There exists a uniform judicial system in India. The **Supreme Court** is at the top of the Judiciary.
