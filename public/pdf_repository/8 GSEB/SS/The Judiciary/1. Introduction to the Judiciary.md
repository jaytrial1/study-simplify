This section introduces the role of the Judiciary in India's democratic system and its importance in administering justice.

# The Judiciary

India is a democratic country. In order to run the administration of the country in an appropriate way, laws have been formed in accordance with certain principles and ideals. When a person breaks a law, it becomes an offence. When a person commits a crime, s/he is depriving someone of his/her rights. **Justice** is required at such time and the task of providing justice is carried out by the **Judiciary**. Thus the Judiciary holds a prominent place.
