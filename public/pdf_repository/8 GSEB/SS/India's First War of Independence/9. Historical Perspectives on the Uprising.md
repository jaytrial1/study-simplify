# The form of the War of Independence

Various historians and scholars offer different interpretations of the 1857 War of Independence.

*   The British often called it simply a **mutiny of soldiers**.
*   Some Indians consider it a **mass rebellion**.
*   **Disraeli**, a British politician, called it a **political and religious War of Independence**.
*   **Vinayak Damodar Savarkar** called it **'India's first War of Independence'**.
*   **Pattabhi Sitaramaiah** agreed with Savarkar's view.
*   **Dr. Sen** also considered it a **'War of Independence'**.

It is certain that its form was more extensive and influential than just a sepoy mutiny. The bravery shown by kings, jagirdars, peasants, and tribals indicates it was a significant event and a great source of inspiration for future national movements in India.
