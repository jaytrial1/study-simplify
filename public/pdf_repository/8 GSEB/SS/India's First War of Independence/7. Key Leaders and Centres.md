This section identifies the important leaders and geographical locations where the 1857 uprising was prominent.

## The Eminent Leaders and Places of the First War of Independence

Apart from Delhi, major centres of the struggle included:

*   **Kanpur**
*   **Lucknow**
*   **Jhansi**
*   **Patna**
*   **Jagdishpur**
*   **Bareilly** in North India.

Key leaders in these areas included:

*   **Begum Hazrat Mahal** led the War of Independence in Lucknow.
*   **Rani Lakshmibai** of Jhansi bravely joined with a huge army and led forces against the British in places like Kalpi and Gwalior.

*Rani of Jhansi Lakshmibai: A prominent leader who fought bravely against the British.*
*   **Kunwar Singh**, the Jagirdar of Bihar, led the struggle at the age of 82.
*   **Bahadur Khan** was the main leader in Bareilly.
*   **Nanasaheb Peshwa** and his minister **Tatya Tope** led the struggle in Kanpur.

The War of Independence also spread to other regions:

*   **Uttar Pradesh**: Banaras, Faizabad, Agra, Jhansi.
*   **Rajasthan**: Abu.
*   **Madhya Pradesh**: Gwalior, Indore.
*   **Maharashtra**: Satara, Kolhapur, Sawantwadi.
*   **Gujarat**: Ahmedabad, Lunawada, Patan, Anand, Dwarka, Okha, Vijapur, Kheralu, Sabarkantha.

Leaders in Gujarat included:

*   **Garbaddas Mukhi** of Anand.
*   **Jodha Manek** and **Mulu Manek** who offered tremendous resistance in Dwarka and the Okhamandal region.
*   Tribals of the Pandarwada area of Mahisagar district also fought bravely.

Participants in the War of Independence came from diverse backgrounds, including kings, landlords, jagirdars, women leaders, tribals, farmers, people from rural areas, and people of both Hindu and Muslim religions. In this respect, they all took on the leadership for India.
