This section explores the administrative and economic policies of the British that caused discontent among the Indian population.

## Administrative Reasons

Indians had no place in the administration of the company. Only English people were appointed to high posts. The British administrative system was seen as painful by the people.

*   They levied **heavy taxes** on common and poor people.
*   Revenue was collected from farmers with strictness.
*   The judiciary was extremely expensive.
*   The police force was inactive.
*   There was a huge difference in the salaries of Indian and English employees.

All these matters fueled the First War of Independence of 1857 AD.

## Economic Reasons

The British terribly exploited India economically.

*   They destroyed India's foreign trade.
*   They aimed to make India a source of raw materials and a market for finished goods.
*   They adopted a policy of compulsory production of goods essential for them, such as cotton, indigo, and silk, by Indians.
*   This policy led to a decrease in the production of cereals and pulses, causing food shortages.
*   The peasantry was ruined due to unjust customs policies.
*   Many of India's important trading centres were destroyed.
*   Frequent droughts added to the suffering, leading to millions of deaths from food shortage.
*   India's craft industries, rural industries, and handicrafts were destroyed.

When the War of Independence took place, along with kings and landlords, the peasants and artisans also participated on a very large scale.
