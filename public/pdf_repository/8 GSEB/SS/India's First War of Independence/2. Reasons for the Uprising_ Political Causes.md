This section details the political factors that contributed to the outbreak of the 1857 War, focusing on British expansionism and policies.

# Reasons for the First War of Independence of 1857 AD

Let us try to understand the specific reasons for this struggle in chronological order.

## Political Reasons

The main political reason for this struggle was the establishment of British power in India. The **Battle of Plassey in 1757 AD** marked the beginning of British rule. They expanded their empire by:

*   Waging Mysore wars and ending the empire of Tipu Sultan.
*   Defeating Dutch and French companies.
*   Defeating Indian rulers like the Nawab of Awadh, the Nizam of Hyderabad, the Mughal emperor, the Nawab of Bengal, and Tanjore, one by one.
*   Defeating the Marathas in the Maratha wars.

By 1818 AD, they had established their political power over almost the whole of India. Due to British imperialism, Indians were excluded from political power. Surviving native kingdoms accepted **Wellesley's Subsidiary Alliance system**, effectively subjugating their states to the British. A foreign company started ruling India instead of Indians, leading to exploitation and becoming a main cause of the struggle.

**Dalhousie** annexed states like Satara, Sambhalpur, Jhansi, Nagpur, and Awadh to the British Empire through an aggressive **Doctrine of Lapse**. He also stopped pensions to rulers like Peshwa Nanasaheb and other kings. Lands of several landlords were confiscated. As a result, this widespread displeasure fueled the First War of Independence.
