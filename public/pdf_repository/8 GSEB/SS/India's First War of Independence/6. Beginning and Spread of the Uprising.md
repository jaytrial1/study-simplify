This section describes how the revolt started and quickly spread from military barracks to other areas.

# The Beginning and Development of the War of Independence

The initial refusal to use the controversial cartridges occurred on March 29, 1857, in Barrackpore, Bengal.

*   Sepoys refused to use the cartridges with animal fat.
*   When ordered not to protest, **Mangal Pandey** defied the British officer Major Hewson.
*   Hewson ordered his arrest, but Mangal Pandey shot and killed Hewson and also Lieutenant Baugh.
*   Mangal Pandey was arrested and **hanged on April 8, 1857**.
*   Mangal Pandey is considered the **first martyr** of the 1857 War of Independence.

The real beginning of the War of Independence took place on **May 10, 1857, in Meerut**.

*   Indian soldiers in Meerut refused to use the cartridges and were imprisoned.
*   Other soldiers revolted, killed British officers, and rescued their imprisoned comrades.
*   They started a large-scale War of Independence.
*   The next day, they marched from Meerut to Delhi.
*   These sepoys killed many British officers in Delhi and conquered the city, making it the main centre of the struggle.
*   They proclaimed the Mughal emperor **Bahadur Shah II** as the emperor of India.
