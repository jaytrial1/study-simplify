This section focuses on the specific event that triggered the revolt – the controversy surrounding the new rifle cartridges.

## Immediate Reason

One of the immediate causes of the 1857 AD War of Independence was the 'cartridges with fat'.

*   The British government decided to use a new **Enfield rifle** instead of the old **Brown Bess rifle**.
*   The cap on the top of the new rifle cartridge had to be bitten off with teeth before loading.
*   In January 1857 AD, a rumour spread among Indian troops in Bengal that **cow and pig fat** was used in these cartridge caps.
*   Soldiers of both Hindu and Muslim faiths were outraged, as cows are sacred to Hindus and pork is forbidden for Muslims.
*   Using these cartridges would make them feel religiously impure (apostasy or *dharmabhrsht*).

The sepoys refused to use these cartridges, which sparked the War of Independence. Some historians believe this was not just a rumour.
