# India's First War of Independence

Since childhood, we are acquainted with the names of Rani Lakshmibai of Jhansi, Mangal Pandey, Tatya Tope, Nanasaheb, etc. They have been our role models. These great individuals fought in the First War of Independence, known as the War of Independence of 1857 AD. It was a great challenge against the British and a commendable attempt was made to drive them out of India. They sacrificed their lives to shake the British rule and make India independent.
