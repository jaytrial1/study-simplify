# Factors Affecting Agriculture and Farm Systems

Favorable conditions such as suitable soil, adequate water availability, and a supportive climate are essential for agricultural activities. As observed on maps showing agricultural distribution, farming is concentrated in regions worldwide where these suitable factors for growing crops exist.

## Farm System

Agriculture or farming can be viewed as a system with distinct components:
*   **Inputs:** Important inputs include seeds, fertilizers, machinery, and labor.
*   **Operations:** The processes involved in the system are operations like ploughing, sowing, irrigation, weeding, and harvesting.
*   **Outputs:** The results or outputs from this system are crops, wool, dairy products, and poultry products.
