# Agricultural Development and Support

Agricultural development progresses at varying rates globally. Developing countries with large populations often focus on *intensive agriculture*.

Agricultural development involves efforts to boost production to meet increasing population demands. This can be achieved by:
*   Expanding the area under cultivation.
*   Growing more crops per year.
*   Improving irrigation systems.
*   Using fertilizers and high-yielding variety seeds.
*   *Mechanization* of agriculture (using machinery).

The main goal of agricultural development is to enhance *food security*, ensuring enough food is available for everyone.

Cooperative activities in agriculture have been encouraging. Financial aid is provided to farmers through schemes like *Kisan Credit Cards* and loans from nationalized and cooperative banks. *Warehouses* are established to store agricultural produce.

The government provides farmers with continuous information, new technologies, and guidance through various channels:
*   Radio, television, newspapers.
*   Kisan channel.
*   Kisan SMS on mobile.
*   Toll-free number 18001801551 (Kisan Call Center).
*   Government's e-khedut farmer webportal.
*   Agricultural fairs ('Krushi Mela') in Gujarat.
