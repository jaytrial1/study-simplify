# Major Agricultural Crops

A variety of crops are grown in different parts of India and the world, influenced by diverse geographical conditions, climate, soil, and rainfall.

## Rice

*Image: Scene depicting people working in a rice paddy field, with mountains and trees in the background.*
*   Importance: Important and major food crop worldwide and in India, consumed by most people.
*   Requirements: Hot and humid climate, high rainfall, mainly grown in irrigated fields.
*   Labor: Requires more workers for farming.
*   Leading Producers: China (leads), followed by India, Japan, Sri Lanka.
*   Major Indian States: West Bengal, Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Bihar, Odisha.
*   Major Districts in Gujarat: Kheda, Vadodara, Ahmedabad, Surat.

## Wheat


---
**Image:**
public/images/8 GSEB/SS/Agriculture/5. Major Agricultural Crops/img_img-3.png
---


*Image: Close-up view of a field of mature wheat stalks.*
*   Importance: Second most important cereal crop in India after rice.
*   Requirements: Moderate temperature and rainfall, bright sunshine during growth. Grows well in black clayey soils that retain moisture.
*   Distribution: Grown extensively in USA, Canada, Argentina, Russia, Ukraine, Australia, and India.
*   Major Indian States: Punjab, Haryana, Western Uttar Pradesh. Punjab is called 'Wheat Bowl of India' due to large production.
*   Major Districts in Gujarat: Ahmedabad, Bhavnagar, Surendranagar. Bhal Pradesh of Ahmedabad is famous for Bhaliya wheat.
*   Growing Season in India: Winter.

## Millets
*   Requirements: Sandy and loamy soils, low rainfall, moderate temperature, adequate sunlight.
*   Also known as: Coarse grain.
*   Other Producers: Nigeria, China, Niger.
*   Major Indian States: Rajasthan, Gujarat, Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra.
*   Production in India: Highest production in Rajasthan, followed by Gujarat.
*   Highest Production in Gujarat: Banaskantha district.

## Know This

Ragi (*naagli*) is a leading cereal crop in Gujarat and India. It is mostly cultivated in Dang and Valsad districts in Gujarat. Ragi is nutrient-rich, high in protein, minerals, and vitamins, especially calcium and iron, making it useful for treating malnutrition and in baby food. Its high fiber content is beneficial for diabetic and heart patients.

## Groundnut


---
**Image:**
public/images/8 GSEB/SS/Agriculture/5. Major Agricultural Crops/img_img-4.png
---


*Image: A plant showing groundnuts attached to its roots, freshly dug from the soil.*
*   Importance: Important oilseed crop. Edible oil is obtained from groundnut.
*   Requirements: Black sandy, loamy, and lava sandy soil where water does not log.
*   Leading Producers: China (leads), followed by India (second largest).
*   Production in India: Gujarat ranks first in total production.
*   Highest Production in Gujarat: Junagadh district.

## Castor
*   Importance: Oilseed crop.
*   Requirements: Moderately dark, well-drained, and sandy soil.
*   Leading Producers: India (ranks first), followed by China and Brazil.
*   Production in India: Mostly grown in Gujarat.
*   Major Districts in Gujarat: Junagadh, Amreli, Banaskantha, Patan, Mehsana, Sabarkantha, Rajkot, Surendranagar, etc.

## Cotton


---
**Image:**
public/images/8 GSEB/SS/Agriculture/5. Major Agricultural Crops/img_img-6.png
---


*Image: Close-up view of cotton bolls on a plant, showing the fluffy white fiber.*
*   Importance: Important raw material for the cotton textile industry.
*   Requirements: High temperature, light rainfall. Black soil is most suitable.
*   Growth Period: Takes six to eight months to be ready. Requires a clear sky when ready for harvest.
*   Leading Producers: China, USA, India, Pakistan, Brazil, Egypt.
*   Major Indian States: Gujarat, Maharashtra, Telangana, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Haryana, Rajasthan, Punjab, Tamil Nadu, Odisha.
*   Production in India: Gujarat ranks first.
*   Famous Region in Gujarat: Kanam region near Bharuch for long-stringed cotton.
*   Major Districts in Gujarat: Surendranagar, Rajkot, Vadodara, Ahmedabad, Sabarkantha, Mehsana, Botad, Bharuch, Kheda, Surat, Panchmahal, Amreli, Bhavnagar, Patan, Junagadh, Jamnagar, etc.
