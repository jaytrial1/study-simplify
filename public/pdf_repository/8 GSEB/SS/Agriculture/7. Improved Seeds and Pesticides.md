This section covers the importance of using improved seeds for higher yields and discusses the use of pesticides, including the issues with chemical pesticides and the benefits of biological alternatives.
# Improved Seeds and Pesticides

## Improved Seeds

Seeds are crucial for agricultural production. To increase yields, farmers now use improved and treated seeds. Government-established seed development centers work on this. Seedlings for horticultural crops are specifically prepared using *biotech methods*, helping develop early maturing, high-yielding varieties. Higher production of cereals using these methods has helped India become *self-sufficient* in foodgrains.

## Pesticides

Pesticides are important for protecting crops from damage. However, excessive use of chemical pesticides leads to soil and water pollution and can have long-term negative effects on human health due to toxins in produce.

To control pests, biological pesticides using bacteria, viruses, fungi, worms, and plant extracts are used. About 90% of these are based on microorganisms. Germs that produce spores are used for pest control. *Bacillus species* is a leading bacterium used this way. Organic pesticides are commercially available. Biological pesticides cause diseases in pests, leading to their destruction.

Bacterial biological pesticides act as a *gastric toxin*, meaning the insect must ingest them. When sprayed, dormant live bacteria enter the caterpillar's intestines with its food. The toxic protein causes paralysis, especially in the mouth, eventually killing the caterpillar.

Using biological pesticides instead of chemical ones helps avoid side effects. Natural materials like neem, karanj, mahudo, tulsi, ratanjyot, mint, bitter gourd, tobacco, sevanti, etc., are used for pest control.
