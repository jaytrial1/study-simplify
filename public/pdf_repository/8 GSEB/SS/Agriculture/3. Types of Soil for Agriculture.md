# Types of Soil for Agriculture

Different types of soil are found across India, each with unique properties influencing agricultural potential.

## Alluvial Soil
*   Formed by silt deposited by rivers.
*   Characteristics: Sticky, dark color, generally very fertile, though fertility varies locally.
*   Distribution: Found in large areas of India, covering over 50% of Gujarat.
*   Suitable Crops: Paddy, sugarcane, jute, cotton, maize, oil seeds.

## Red Soil
*   Formed in areas with *igneous* and *metamorphic rocks*.
*   Characteristics: Red color due to iron content (turns yellow deeper down), relatively porous and fertile.
*   Suitable Crops: Millet, cotton, wheat, jowar, linseed, groundnut, potato.

## Black Soil
*   Characteristics: Clayey and loamy, high *moisture-storage capacity*, develops *fissures* or cracks when dry. Also known as 'Regur'.
*   Suitability: Highly suitable for cotton crop, hence called 'Black Cotton Soil'.
*   Suitable Crops: Cotton, linseed, mustard, groundnut, tobacco, pulses like urad.

## Laterite Soil
*   Formation: Created in areas with severe erosion and excess rainfall, which washes nutrients from the upper layer to the lower layer (*leaching*).
*   Characteristics: Red color due to iron oxide, can become hard and uncultivable when dry, low organic content makes it less fertile naturally.
*   Suitable Crops (after fertilizing): Cotton, paddy, ragi, sugarcane, tea-coffee, cashew nuts.

## Desert Soil
*   Distribution: Found in arid and semi-arid climates (Rajasthan, Gujarat, Haryana, South Punjab; in Gujarat: Saurashtra, Kutch, parts of North Gujarat).
*   Characteristics: Sandy, less fertile, high content of soluble salts.
*   Suitable Crops (with irrigation): Millet, jowar.

## Mountain Soil
*   Distribution: Primarily found in the Himalayan region, particularly in areas with deodar, chid, pine trees.
*   Characteristics: Thin and immature layer.

## Forest Soil
*   Formation: Upper part becomes black due to decaying fallen leaves and high organic matter; turns brown or red underneath.
*   Distribution: Found only in certain areas.
*   Suitable Crops: Tea-coffee, spices, wheat, maize, barley, paddy.

## Marshy and Peaty Soil
*   Formation: Accumulation of organic matter in moist areas.
*   Characteristics: Submerged during monsoon, excess alkaline and biological substances.
*   Distribution: Found in very limited areas.
*   Suitable Crops: Paddy (cultivated as water recedes).
