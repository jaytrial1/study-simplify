# Introduction to Agriculture

Agriculture is a vital occupation globally, engaging about 50% of the world's population. In India, it is particularly crucial, with approximately two-thirds of the population still relying on it. The prosperity of India is closely linked to the development of its agricultural sector. Various crops, including both food crops and cash crops used in industries, are grown across the country. Agriculture forms the main base of the Indian economy.

Farming encompasses the cultivation of cereals, oil seeds, pulses, fruits, vegetables, flowers (*floriculture*), and the raising of animals (*animal husbandry*). Farming is also referred to as agriculture.
