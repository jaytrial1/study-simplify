# Social Reforms During the British Rule

During the English rule in India, several social evils or vices were prevalent. These practices were considered anti-human and hindered individual development. Key social evils included **child marriage, untouchability, female infanticide, slavery, Sati-pratha, caste discrimination, prohibition of widow remarriage, illiteracy, and superstition**.

In the 19th century, prominent reformers campaigned against these social evils individually and through institutions. The 19th century in India is often called the century of socio-religious renaissance. These reformers aimed to transform Indian society by eradicating superstitions and harmful practices.
