# Rabindranath Tagore's Thoughts on Education

Rabindranath Tagore, known as 'Kavivar' or 'Gurudev', aimed to establish educational institutions in Bengal based on his ideas. A devotee of nature, he believed students should understand Indian ideology and culture. He advocated for nature education, believing knowledge is best acquired close to nature. He felt education should foster a child's creativity, freeing them from rigid discipline, and developing imagination and curiosity. He encouraged children to learn arts like music, acting, and painting, and stressed the importance of moral values and spirituality. He also believed teachers should understand child psychology.

In 1901 AD, he founded the **'Shantiniketan'** institute based on his philosophy, which produced many scholars. Later, it became known as **Shantiniketan Visva-Bharati University**.

### Know This (Tagore's Nobel Prize)

Poet Rabindranath Tagore received the Nobel Prize for literature in 1913 AD for his collection of poems 'Gitanjali'.
