# Mahatma Gandhi's Thoughts on Education

Mahatma Gandhi significantly contributed to education alongside the freedom struggle. His educational plan is known as the **Vardha Shikshan Yojana of 1936 AD**. He formed an education committee led by **Dr. Zakir Hussain**.

Gandhi believed that literacy is merely a tool to educate men and women, not an end in itself. He sought to strengthen the Indian education system with ideas like **'Nai Talim'**, **'Buniyadi Shikshan'**, and **'Basic education'**, emphasizing skill-based vocational training as an alternative to Western education.

He felt the prevailing system failed to prepare students for life and employment. He advocated for basic education to be self-sufficient and vocational. His primary education curriculum proposal was for 7 years, teaching various subjects and skills. He strongly supported primary education in the mother tongue and promoted skills like handicrafts, carpentry, blacksmithing, weaving, and spinning.

By 1937 AD, schools based on his ideas were established in many provinces. Alongside the freedom struggle, Mahatma Gandhi founded **Gujarat Vidyapeeth** in Ahmedabad in 1920 AD. Inspired by him, **Jamia Millia Islamia University** in Delhi and **Aligarh University** were also established. Many of Gandhi's grassroots workers set up ashrams and campaigned for student education, including figures like **Thakkar Bapa**, **Jugatram Dave**, and **Purnimabahen Pakwasa**.

### Know This (Gandhi's Views)

*   According to Gandhiji, 'Education is the discovery of the best of the body, mind and soul of a child and a human being.'
