This section focuses on the development of vocational and technical education, particularly in the 20th century.

# Vocational Education

The **Wood's dispatch of 1854 AD** included provisions for vocational education. However, a significant number of institutions focusing on science, technology, and business emerged in the 20th century. Notable institutions included the **Indian Institute of Science in Bangalore (Bengaluru)**, the interdisciplinary **Bose Research Centre in Calcutta (Kolkata)**, a research center linked to the Forest Department in Dehradun, the **Centre for Agriculture in Delhi**, the **Centre for Engineering in Roorkee**, and the **Bhandarkar Institute in Poona (Pune)**.
