# Efforts to Fulfil Necessities

After independence, the government implemented many developmental schemes. Changes were needed in both industrial and agricultural sectors.

## Agricultural Development

Although ample land was available for agriculture, traditional farming methods resulted in low production. Significant efforts were made to improve agriculture, eventually leading to India becoming self-sufficient in food production.

### Activity

*   Get the details of efforts made for 'Green Revolution' and discuss them in your classroom.
    > **Answer:** The Green Revolution involved introducing high-yielding varieties of seeds, improved irrigation methods, fertilizers, and pesticides to increase food grain production, especially wheat and rice, in India during the 1960s and 1970s. This significantly boosted agricultural output.
*   Get the information from the elders of the village about farming and farmers' condition that prevailed 50 years back.
    > **Answer:** Asking elders would likely reveal that farming methods were more traditional, relying heavily on manual labor and natural rainfall. Farmers might have faced more uncertainty regarding yields and market access compared to today. Houses and living conditions were generally simpler.
*   Many parts of Gujarat are facing the problem of groundwater in recent times. Get information about how to harvest water to eliminate this problem.
    > **Answer:** Water harvesting involves collecting and storing rainwater or surface runoff for later use. Techniques include building check dams, tanks, ponds, or implementing rooftop rainwater harvesting systems. These methods help recharge groundwater levels and provide water during dry periods.

Agricultural reforms have improved the socio-economic status of farmers. Cooperative activities have greatly helped agriculture and guided farmers. Efforts are also made to connect farm production to large markets.

Cooperative activities were also started in animal husbandry and milk production, leading to the 'White Revolution', which was very successful.

## Industrial Development

Industrial development is also crucial for a country's balanced growth. Industries often develop where raw materials and transport facilities are available. However, developing industries in all areas based on local conditions provides employment opportunities everywhere, leading to economic progress for people in different regions.

The government has made notable efforts in this area. 'Special Industrial Zones' (SIZ) have been established in some areas. The government provides facilities like land, power, and water at reasonable rates to industries starting in SIZs. They also receive tax exemptions for a certain period. These schemes have accelerated industrial development, economic growth, and improved living standards.
