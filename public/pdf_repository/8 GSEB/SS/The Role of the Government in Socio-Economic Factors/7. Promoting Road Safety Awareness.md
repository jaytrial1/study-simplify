This section covers government initiatives, public awareness campaigns, and the role of various entities in educating people about road safety.

# Promoting Road Safety Awareness

Various programs are conducted annually to raise public awareness about traffic rules and reduce accident damage. These include distributing educational materials like banners, posters, pamphlets, and booklets.

The Government of India and state governments like Gujarat usually organize "Road Safety Week / Month" every January to create awareness.

## World Remembrance Day

World Remembrance Day for Road Traffic Victims is observed on the third Sunday of November each year. It started in 1993 and was recognized by the UN in 2005. Programs are organized by government departments to raise awareness on this day.

## Role of Media

Media platforms like radio, TV, movies, and advertisements play a role in spreading awareness about road safety.

## Role of Parents and Teachers

Parents and teachers are important in teaching children about road safety from a young age. Children often learn by example, so parents following traffic rules is crucial. Parents should discourage video/computer games that promote unsafe driving habits. They should teach children about traffic signals, rules, using zebra crossings, waiting before crossing, using pavements, and avoiding playing on or near roads.

## Personal Responsibility

Road safety is largely a personal responsibility. Drivers must be trained and licensed, and vehicle documents must be carried. Essential personal responsibilities include:

*   Allowing pedestrians to use zebra crossings first.
*   Never using a cell phone while driving.
*   Wearing seat belts, which significantly reduce death risk in car accidents.
*   Wearing a good quality helmet on a two-wheeler to protect against head injury.
*   Never driving dangerously fast.
*   Obeying all traffic rules and signs.
*   Not driving under the influence of alcohol.
*   Ensuring the vehicle is in good condition and regularly maintained.
*   Using the road considerately so everyone can move smoothly.
*   Following the speed limit.

### Advice/Instructions Regarding Road Safety

*   Automatic road barriers should be installed at unmanned railway crossings.
*   Separate routes for cyclists would be beneficial and help reduce accidents involving them.
