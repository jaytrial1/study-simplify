# Other Fields and the Government

The government's role impacts many aspects of life. Measures are taken to ensure everyone has access to nutritious food, clean air, and clean drinking water.

In unusual situations, like disasters such as earthquakes or floods, public cooperation is essential as the government alone cannot manage relief efforts for large numbers of people.

Even in normal times, significant efforts are made to provide adequate nutritious food and potable water to people of all classes, granting these amenities as rights.

### Activity

Get details about various governmental schemes meant to provide services to people. Note the details about schemes like making water available to each house, housing, sanitation, health services and education.
> **Answer:** Governmental schemes aim to provide essential services. Examples include Jal Jeevan Mission (water to every house), Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (housing), Swachh Bharat Abhiyan (sanitation), Ayushman Bharat (health services), and Sarva Shiksha Abhiyan / Samagra Shiksha Abhiyan (education). These schemes provide financial aid, infrastructure development, and direct services to citizens.
