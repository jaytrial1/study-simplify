This section discusses how the understanding of basic needs expanded over time and the link between these needs and employment.

# Basic Necessities

Initially, basic necessities were defined as 'food, clothes, and shelter'. However, as citizens gained more freedom and development, the definition expanded to include:

*   Education
*   Health
*   Clean water
*   Clean air
*   Nutritious food
*   Entertainment

To access these necessities, families need sufficient financial resources, which requires employment. This situation highlighted the need to create new job opportunities.
