# India's Goal: A Welfare State

India decided to become a 'welfare state' after independence. This goal is based on the ancient concept of 'सर्वश्रध्दंवश्रधंवश्रधं सुप्यां' (for the happiness of all, for the welfare of all).

Before independence, India was ruled by others for a long time, which affected the mindset of the Indian people. Society faced challenges like ill traditions and significant social inequality. Political freedom alone was not enough; economic and intellectual development was also crucial. Therefore, the Indian government had to undertake significant efforts and long-term steps to achieve this vision.
