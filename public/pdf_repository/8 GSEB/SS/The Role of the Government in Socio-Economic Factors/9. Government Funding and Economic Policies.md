# Government Schemes and the Economy

The government spends a lot of money to improve people's living standards and provide basic amenities. This money is primarily collected through various taxes.

Consider a bus service reaching a small village. The government invests significantly to make this transport available, even if it doesn't earn a profit on that specific route. The intention is not profit but providing essential services to people.

Similarly, essential items like food grains, edible oil, and sugar are provided to lower-income families at subsidized rates, meaning the price is lower than the actual production cost. These subsidies are part of the system to provide basic amenities.

To fund these welfare schemes, the government may need to impose higher taxes in certain areas, as taxes are the main source of revenue for such initiatives.

### Activity

*   Visit a nearby colony and get details about the land and help given by the government for construction of houses.
    > **Answer:** Government schemes like Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana provide financial assistance or subsidies for constructing or buying houses, especially for economically weaker sections. Sometimes, the government also helps with land allocation or regularization in certain areas. Visiting a colony built under such a scheme could provide specific examples of the help received by residents.
*   In an ordinary restaurant, no taxes are charged and one needs to pay only for the meals eaten. However, in bigger restaurants, taxes are also charged in addition to the meal charges. Why can this not be considered as discrimination? Discuss.
    > **Answer:** This is generally not considered discrimination because tax laws often differentiate based on factors like the size of the business, turnover, or type of service offered. Bigger restaurants might fall under different tax brackets or service tax regulations compared to smaller, basic eateries. This differentiation is usually based on economic principles or policy objectives, not personal discrimination.
*   Which type of small industries should be given concession by the government? Try to know this from some expert.
    > **Answer:** Experts might suggest that the government should give concessions (like tax breaks, subsidies, easier loans) to small industries that: promote employment in rural or backward areas, use local resources, produce essential goods, adopt environmentally friendly practices, or are run by disadvantaged groups.
*   Get information about the schemes such as Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act (MGNREGA), Jawahar Rozgar Yojana, Atmanirbhar Bharat etc.
    > **Answer:** MGNREGA guarantees 100 days of wage employment in a financial year to adult members of rural households willing to do unskilled manual work. Jawahar Rozgar Yojana was an earlier rural employment scheme. Atmanirbhar Bharat Abhiyan (Self-Reliant India) is a movement promoting self-sufficiency across various sectors through economic packages and reforms to support businesses, especially MSMEs, and boost domestic production.
