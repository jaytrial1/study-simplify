This section explains the government's efforts to uplift historically disadvantaged groups and promote a vision of equality.

# Social Development through Change in Life

Certain groups in India's social system were historically deprived of facilities, considered backward, and lacked fundamental human rights. These classes are listed in the Indian Constitution's Appendix.

To help these groups achieve equality with others, the government has granted them certain facilities in education and employment. The spread of education has also helped change people's mindsets, fostering a vision of seeing all humans as equal. However, obstacles still exist in achieving full equality.
