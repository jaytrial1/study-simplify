# Government Rules and Public Interest

Just as the government works to provide basic needs, education, and health, it also makes rules in different areas for the public interest. It is everyone's responsibility to follow these rules. Road safety is one important area governed by such rules.

## Road Safety

Vehicles are a vital part of modern life, making travel easier but also causing millions of injuries and deaths through road accidents. Road safety regulations aim to protect all travellers.

Road accidents are unplanned events leading to injury or death, causing significant social and economic harm. Accidents happen due to issues with roads and, importantly, due to negligence.

### Causes of Road Accidents

*   **Speeding:** Most fatal accidents are caused by excessive speed, which increases the risk and severity of injury.
*   **Driver Behavior:** Uncontrolled speeding, violating traffic rules, not understanding signs, fatigue, and driving under the influence of alcohol.
*   **Pedestrian Negligence:** Pedestrians being careless, illiterate about rules, or crossing roads at unsafe places. Actions like putting limbs out of windows, talking to drivers, or catching moving buses also contribute.
*   **Vehicle Condition:** Mechanical failures like brake or steering failure, tyre bursts, inadequate headlights, or carrying excessive weight.
*   **Road Conditions:** Damaged roads, potholes, washed-out roads, sudden diversions, or village roads poorly connected to highways.
*   **Environmental Factors:** Reduced visibility or difficult conditions due to fog, snowfall, heavy rains, wind, storms, or hail.
*   **Traffic Signal Violations:** Crossing the road when the traffic light is red.

### Steps to Prevent Road Accidents

The Ministry of Road Transport and Highways has taken serious steps:

*   Improving roads in accident-prone areas.
*   Maintaining traffic signals and road signs on busy roads.
*   Constructing proper roads in cities and villages.
*   Installing CCTV cameras to catch speeding vehicles.
*   Framing rules for the public to follow traffic regulations and setting fines/penalties for violations.
