This section focuses on the government's role in expanding access to education and healthcare across the country, particularly in rural areas.

# Fields of Education and Health

Before independence, awareness and facilities for education and health were limited, especially in villages. Many villages lacked basic primary education and health facilities.

Spreading education was essential for the success of democracy. The government and public institutions have made significant efforts in this regard. The goal of having a school in every village has largely been achieved.

Making health facilities available to everyone was also necessary. Despite efforts, issues like child and maternal deaths still occur, sometimes due to misbeliefs preventing people from using available health services. Nevertheless, sincere efforts continue in this field.

### Activity

*   Try to know what kinds of services are available by dialling 108 at the time of accident or sudden illness.
    > **Answer:** Dialing 108 in India connects you to an emergency ambulance service that provides free transportation to a hospital during medical emergencies, accidents, or sudden illnesses. It's a critical service for accessing immediate medical help.
*   Hold discussion about what is done by the organization of local self-government to keep your town/ village clean? And what are the duties of citizens?
    > **Answer:** Local self-government bodies (like Panchayats or Municipalities) are responsible for waste collection, street cleaning, maintaining public hygiene, and providing sanitation facilities. Citizens' duties include properly disposing of waste, not littering, participating in community cleanliness drives, and maintaining hygiene in their surroundings.
