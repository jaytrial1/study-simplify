# Information Technology Industry

The **Information Technology (IT) industry** involves the collection, processing, and distribution of information. It has become a global industry, facilitated by technology, politics, and socio-economic changes.

Key factors determining the geographical location of the IT industry include:
*   Facilities for **research**.
*   Availability of **money (capital)**.
*   Structure supporting **research and development**.

The main global centres for the information technology industry are **Silicon Valley in California (USA)** and **Bengaluru in India**.

## Think and Tell

*   How do industries thrive in certain areas?
*   How does facility of transportation help in development of Industries?

> **Answer:** Industries thrive in certain areas where factors like availability of raw materials, land, water, labour, power, capital, transport, and markets are easily accessible. Sometimes government incentives also help industries develop in specific locations.
> **Answer:** Transportation facilities help in the development of industries by allowing easy movement of raw materials to the factory and finished goods from the factory to the market or consumer. Ports, roads, and railways connect industries to sources of supply and demand.

## Silicon Valley (USA) and Bengaluru (India)

Bengaluru, located on the Deccan Plateau, is known as the **'Silicon Plateau'** due to its importance as an IT hub and its moderate climate. Silicon Valley is part of the Santa Clara Valley in California, near the Rocky Mountains, with a temperate climate. Both regions are major IT centres.

Other emerging IT hubs in India include Mumbai, New Delhi, Hyderabad, and Chennai. Cities like Gurgaon, Pune, Thiruvananthapuram, Cochin, and Chandigarh are also important centres for the information technology industry.
