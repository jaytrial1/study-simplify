This section describes how industrial regions develop when multiple industries locate near each other to share resources and benefits, listing major global and Indian industrial areas.
# Industrial Regions



---
**Image:**
public/images/8 GSEB/SS/Industries/5. Industrial Regions/img_img-1.png
---



**Industrial regions** emerge when many industries are located in areas where the necessary factors (like raw materials, labour, transport) are readily available, allowing them to benefit from proximity.

Major industrial regions globally include:
*   Northeast America
*   Western and Central Europe
*   Eastern Asia

These areas are often located in temperate zones, near seaports, and close to coalfields.

In India, several industrial regions exist:
*   Mumbai-Pune cluster
*   Bengaluru-Tamil Nadu region
*   Hugli region
*   Ahmedabad-Vadodara region
*   Chhotanagpur industrial belt
*   Visakhapatnam-Guntur region
*   Gurgaon-Delhi-Meerut region
*   Kollam-Thiruvananthapuram cluster
