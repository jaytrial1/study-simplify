# Classification of Industries by Size

Industries can be classified based on their size, which is often determined by investment and the type of labour used.

*   **Cottage Industry:** Run by family members using simple tools and low investment. Examples: Weaving khadi, making papad, khakhra, agarbatti, bamboo work.
*   **Micro Industries:** Industries with investment up to 25 lakh rupees. They are labour intensive. Examples: Making artistic or useful items from metal, leather, soil.
*   **Small scale Industries:** Industries with investment between 25 lakh rupees and less than 5 crore rupees. They use labour intensive methods and often produce goods helpful for heavy industries. Examples: Implements, vehicle mending, industries producing usable items.
*   **Medium scale Industries:** Industries that are labour or capital intensive with investment between 5 crore rupees and less than 10 crore rupees. Examples: Machines, colour-chemicals, electronics implements.
*   **Large scale Industries:** Primarily capital intensive industries with an investment of 10 crores or more. Examples: Railway implements, heavy vehicles, iron and steel.
