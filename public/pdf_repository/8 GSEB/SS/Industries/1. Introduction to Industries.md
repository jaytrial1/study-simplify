# Introduction to Industries

**Industry** is related to socio-economic activity. It involves the production of goods, often associated with the arrangement of minerals and services. The **mining industry**, for instance, focuses on extracting minerals from the earth's crust.

Generally, industry refers to the outcome of any work, labour, or activity used by humans to meet their needs. Historically, any activity aimed at fulfilling needs and producing handicrafts was considered industry. As human needs grew and machines were introduced, the meaning of industry expanded. Today, the term industry is often associated with machines and involves three key activities:
1.  Collecting **raw materials**.
2.  Changing the form of the product and increasing its **usefulness**.
3.  Delivering the **finished product** to the market or consumer.

Therefore, **industry** can be defined as an activity that transforms raw materials using mechanical aid to increase their usefulness.
