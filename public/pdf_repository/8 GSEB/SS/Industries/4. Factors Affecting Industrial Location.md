# Factors Affecting Industrial Location

Several factors influence where industries are located. These include:
*   Availability of **raw materials**
*   Access to **land** and **water**
*   Availability of **labour**
*   Access to **power**
*   Availability of **capital**
*   Access to **transport** facilities
*   Proximity to the **market**

Industries are typically located where some or all of these factors are easily available. Governments sometimes encourage industrial development in certain areas by offering incentives like affordable electricity, lower transportation costs, and infrastructure facilities. This can lead to industrialization and the growth of cities and towns, even in economically backward areas.
