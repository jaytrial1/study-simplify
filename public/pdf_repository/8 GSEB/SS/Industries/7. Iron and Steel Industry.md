# Iron and Steel Industry

The **Iron and Steel Industry** requires significant investment. It is considered a **feeder industry** because its output (steel) is used as raw material by other industries.

Inputs for this industry include:
*   Labour
*   Capital
*   Suitable location
*   Infrastructural facilities
*   Raw materials such as **iron ore, coal, and limestone**.

The process of making steel from iron ore involves putting raw materials into a **blast furnace** for purification. The resulting steel is tough but can be easily shaped, cut, or drawn into wire. **Alloys of steel**, made by adding metals like Aluminium, Nickel, or Copper, can provide extra hardness, toughness, and rust resistance.

Steel is often called the **backbone of modern industry** because it is used in almost everything, from small needles to giant machines, vehicles, trains, ports, oil wells, pipelines, mining equipment, farming tools, and building structures.

In India, the iron and steel industry has grown due to access to raw materials, cheap labour, transport, and markets. Important steel centres like Bhilai, Durgapur, Burnpur, Jamshedpur, Rourkela, and Bokaro are located across West Bengal, Jharkhand, Odisha, and Chhattisgarh. Other centres like Bhadravati and Vijayanagar (Karnataka), Visakhapatnam (Andhra Pradesh), and Salem (Tamil Nadu) utilize local resources.

## Tata Iron and Steel Company (TISCO), Jamshedpur

Before India's independence (1947 AD), TISCO was the only privately owned iron and steel plant, started in 1907 AD in Sakchi (now Jamshedpur), Jharkhand, near the confluence of the Suvarnarekha and Kharkai rivers. Steel production began in 1912 AD.

Sakchi was chosen due to:
*   Proximity to the Bengal-Nagpur railway line (32 km from Kalimati station).
*   Closeness to iron ore, coal (from Jharia), and manganese mines (from Odisha and Chhattisgarh).
*   Access to a large market in Kolkata.
*   Adequate water supply from the Kharkai and Suvarnarekha rivers.
*   Government financial assistance.

The development of the iron and steel industry, including large integrated plants and mini steel mills, has significantly contributed to India's rapid industrial development by providing basic infrastructure for almost all other sectors.

## Pittsburgh (USA)

Pittsburgh is a major iron and steel centre in the United States. It benefits from local resources like coal available within Pittsburgh itself. Iron ore is transported from Minnesota's mines, often using the cheap waterway provided by the Great Lakes Waterway. Water supply comes from the Ohio, Monongahela, and Alleghery rivers.

Large iron and steel mills are located in the river valleys around Pittsburgh. Finished steel is transported to markets via both land and water routes.
