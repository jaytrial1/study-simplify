# Distribution of Major Industries

The world's major industries include the **iron and steel industry**, **cotton textile industry**, **electronic industry**, and **information technology (IT)**. Iron and steel and cotton textile industries are older, while IT is an emerging industry.

*   **Iron and steel industry** is found in Germany, USA, China, Japan, and Russia.
*   The **textile industry** is concentrated in India, Hong Kong, South Korea, Japan, and Taiwan.
*   Major **information technology** hubs are in Silicon Valley (California, USA) and Bengaluru (India).
