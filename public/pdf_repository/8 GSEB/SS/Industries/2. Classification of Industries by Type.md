This section classifies industries based on the source of their raw materials (natural resources, agro, animal, marine, forest, mineral) and by ownership structure.
# Classification of Industries by Type

Industries can be classified based on different criteria.

## Natural Resource Based Industries

These industries rely directly on natural resources. Examples include:
*   Animal husbandry
*   Dairy and meat industries
*   Wool industry
*   Fisheries
*   Forestry
*   Breeding industries (poultry, silkworm, bee)

## Raw Materials Based Industries

Classification based on the source of raw material:
*   **Agro Based Industry:** Uses raw materials from agriculture. Examples: Food industry, cotton and linen textiles, vegetable oil industry.
*   **Animal based Industry:** Uses materials from animals. Examples: Milk and products, meat industry, leather industry.
*   **Marine Based Industry:** Uses resources from the ocean. Examples: Fish processing, minerals from the ocean.
*   **Forest based Industry:** Uses resources from forests. Examples: Paper industry, pharmaceuticals, furniture, building materials.
*   **Mineral Based Industry:** Uses minerals as raw materials. Examples: Iron and steel industry, cement industry, chemical industry.

## Ownership Based Classification

Industries can be classified based on who owns and operates them:
*   **Public sector:** Owned and operated by the government. Examples: Hindustan Aeronautical Limited, Steel Authority of India.
*   **Private sector:** Owned and operated by an individual or group of individuals. Examples: Tisco, Reliance.
*   **Co-operative sector:** Owned and run by the producers of raw materials, workers, or both. Examples: Amul, Mother Dairy.
*   **Joint Sector:** Owned by both the government and an individual or group of individuals. Example: Maruti Ltd.
