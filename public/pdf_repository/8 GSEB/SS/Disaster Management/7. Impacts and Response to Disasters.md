# Impacts and Response to Disasters

Whether disasters are natural or man-made, they leave many profound effects on human life. Governments have to spend crores of rupees to repair the damage. Moreover, if the earning member of a family dies, those families face an unbearable situation. The task of rehabilitating people with permanent disabilities and helping them cope with mental depression is challenging. However, the government takes steps to provide relief and assistance to affected people.
