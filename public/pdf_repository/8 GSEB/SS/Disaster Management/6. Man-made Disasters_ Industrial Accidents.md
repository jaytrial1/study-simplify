# Man-made Disasters: Industrial Accident

We require many things to meet our needs, and numerous industries have been established for production. Accidents are likely to occur during the transfer and storage of raw materials, during the industrial process itself, or during the storage and transfer of finished goods. Precautions and rules are in place to prevent accidents in industrial units. However, accidents sometimes happen due to *human error*, *mechanical malfunction*, or *lack of maintenance* of machines.

Industrial accidents can include:
*   Gas leakage in pesticide factories.
*   Explosions in fireworks factories.
*   Fires in factories producing flammable materials like plastics.
*   Explosions or fires in refineries.
*   Fires or explosions during processing in chemical factories.
*   Accidents in nuclear industries.

Such accidents cause the death of many workers and people living nearby. Many others become permanently disabled. Toxic gas poisoning can have serious health consequences for a large number of people. In addition, the atmosphere and waterbodies are also polluted.

In case of a gas leak from an industrial accident, one should run in the opposite direction of the wind to reduce the impact. If there is a fire, move to a safe place. Help in relief and rescue operations without crowding out of curiosity. Do not participate without special rescue training or appropriate attire and equipment. Help clear the way for police, firefighters, and ambulances.
