This section details the framework of British governance in India and important legislative acts passed by the British Parliament.

# British Government Structure in India

The British Parliament gradually asserted control over the administration of India through various legislative acts, starting from **1773 AD**.

*   **The Regulating Act (1773 AD)**:
    *   Appointed a **Governor General of India**.
    *   Established a **Supreme Court** in Calcutta.
    *   The Governor General became the supreme head of Indian administration.
    *   A three-member council was appointed to assist the Governor General.
*   **Charter Act of 1813 AD**:
    *   Abolished the commercial monopoly of the East India Company (except for trade with China and tea).
    *   Placed the company's territories in India more directly under the control of the British Crown and Parliament.
*   **Charter Act of 1833 AD**:
    *   Appointed the **Governor-General of India** with vast powers.
    *   The Governor-General could now make laws for the whole of India.
    *   A legal member was added to the Governor-General's Council.
*   **Charter Act of 1853 AD**:
    *   Further increased the dominance of the British Parliament over the East India Company.
    *   Took over most of the company's powers, setting the stage for direct Crown rule.
