This section details the series of wars fought between the British and the powerful state of Mysore.

# The Mysore Wars

*   After the decline of the Vijayanagar Empire, **Mysore** emerged as a powerful state in South India by **1761 AD**, primarily under the leadership of **Hyder Ali**.
*   Hyder Ali modernized his army by training soldiers in European methods and equipping them with advanced arms.
*   The British became concerned by Hyder Ali's growing power.
*   This led to four major wars between the British and the state of Mysore:
    *   **First Mysore War (1767-69 AD)**: Ended without a decisive victory for either side.
    *   **Second Mysore War (1780-84 AD)**: Continued after Hyder Ali's death in 1782 AD by his son. Ended with a treaty between the two sides.
    *   **Third Mysore War (1790-92 AD)**: Tipu Sultan was defeated and suffered heavy losses.
    *   **Fourth Mysore War (1799 AD)**: Tipu Sultan died fighting.
*   The British overthrew Tipu Sultan, a strong ruler, and consolidated their empire in the South.
*   They restored the state of Mysore to the former **Wadiyar dynasty** and imposed a **Subsidiary Alliance** on it, bringing it under British control.
