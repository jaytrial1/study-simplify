This section covers the arrival and eventual decline of the French presence in India due to conflicts with the British.

# The French Presence and Conflicts

*   In **1639 AD**, a French officer named **Franco Martin** leased Madras (Chennai) from the King of Chandragiri and established a trading center.
*   The **French East India Company** was founded in **1664 AD**.
*   They established their first trading center in **Surat in 1668 AD**.
*   Their headquarter was at **Masulipatnam**.
*   They founded **Pondicherry (Puducherry) in 1673 AD**.
*   Wars between the Dutch and French in Europe also affected their rivalry in India. The French were initially defeated as the British supported the Dutch.
*   In **1739 AD**, they gained control over **Karaikal** and expanded commercial activities in South India.
*   The issue of establishing a monopoly in the South led to the **Karnataka Wars** with the British.
*   The French were defeated in these wars and lost most of their power in India by **1761 AD**, except for Pondicherry.
*   They were even prohibited from fortifying Pondicherry.
