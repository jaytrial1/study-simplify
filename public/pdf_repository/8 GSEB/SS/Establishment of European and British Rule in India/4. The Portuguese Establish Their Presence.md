This section focuses on the arrival and activities of the Portuguese (Firangi) in India.

# The Portuguese in India

*   **Vasco-da-Gama** arrived in **Calicut** in **1498 AD**.
*   King Samudrik (**Zamorin**) of Calicut permitted the Portuguese to trade in spices.
*   To strengthen their position and security, the Portuguese built forts in **Cochi (1503 AD)** and **Kannur (1505 AD)**.
*   In **1505 AD**, Portugal sent its first Viceroy, **Francisco-de-Almeida**, to establish Portuguese rule.
*   His successor, Viceroy **Alfonso-de-Albuquerque**, conquered several ports, including **Goa**, and solidified Portuguese rule.
*   **Goa** became their capital in **1530 AD**.
*   They further conquered **Vasai (1534 AD)**, **Diu (1535 AD)**, and **Daman (1559 AD)**, expanding their influence in the Indian Ocean.
*   The Portuguese defeated local rulers like the sultans of Ahmednagar, Calicut, and Bijapur.
*   They transported spices from India to Europe and horses from Central Asia on their return journeys.
*   They were called the **'Lord of the Sea'**, implying that other nations needed their permission to use the Indian sea route for trade.
*   They were heavily involved in **piracy**.
*   In Goa, they undertook large-scale conversions of Hindus to Christianity.
