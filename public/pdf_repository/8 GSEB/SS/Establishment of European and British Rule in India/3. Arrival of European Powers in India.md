# European Nations Arrive in India

The primary motivation for European nations to come to India was **trade**. Countries like Portugal, Spain, and Holland invested significant effort in discovering a sea route. The **Portuguese sailor Vasco-da-Gama** successfully reached Calicut in **1498 AD**, discovering a new sea route from Portugal, around Africa, to India. This discovery opened the floodgates for other Europeans to follow.
