This section describes the attempts by the Dutch and Danish to establish trade in India.

# The Dutch and Danish in India

## The Dutch (Valanda)

*   Attracted by the potential for profit, the Dutch from Holland also arrived in India.
*   They first invaded **Sri Lanka in 1658 AD** and took control of the spice trade there.
*   They established trade centers in **Bengal (1632 AD)** and the **Malabar area (1650 AD)**.
*   They secured a decree from the ruler of Golconda to establish a position in **'Masulipatnam' (Machilipatnam)**.
*   After **1650 AD**, major wars were fought between England and Holland.
*   The Dutch were defeated, ending their trade monopoly in India.

## The Danish

*   Following the Dutch, the Danish from Denmark also came to India.
*   They established a fort (Kothi) in **Sirampur, Bengal**.
*   However, they were unable to make significant progress in trade and their presence remained limited.
