# European Renaissance and the Need for a Sea Route

Significant social and religious changes, known as the **'Renaissance'**, took place in Europe during the 15th century. This period coincided with a major event affecting trade with India: in **1453 AD**, the **Turks conquered Constantinople**. Constantinople was a key hub for the trade route between India and Europe.

With the conquest, the flow of Indian goods to Europe and Western countries was disrupted. Europeans had a great need for Indian goods, especially **spices**, which were essential for preserving meat, as they were largely carnivores. **Cotton cloth** was also vital. As the traditional land trade route was blocked, Europeans began actively seeking new waterways to reach India.
