This section explains the Battle of Buxar and the establishment of the Dual System of administration in Bengal.

# The Battle of Buxar (1764 AD)

*   **Mir Qasim**, the next Nawab of Bengal, planned to expel the British.
*   He formed an alliance with the **Nawab of Awadh** and the **Mughal Emperor**.
*   Their combined army consisted of about 50,000 soldiers, significantly larger than the British East India Company's army of 7072 soldiers.
*   The **Battle of Buxar** was fought on **October 22, 1764**, between this alliance and the British army led by **Major Munro**.
*   The British won the battle decisively.
*   The victory at Buxar solidified the British position gained at Plassey. Defeating the combined forces of three major Indian rulers demonstrated the British military superiority and removed any major challenge to their power in the region.
*   The Battle of Buxar granted the British the **'diwani'** (civil rights, i.e., the right to collect revenue) of **Bengal, Bihar, and Orissa (Odisha)**. This made them the formal masters of the region's revenue.
*   However, the administrative responsibility (Nizamat) remained with the Nawab.
*   This arrangement led to the establishment of the **Dual System (Dwimukhi rule)** in Bengal, where the British controlled revenue but the Nawab controlled administration.
