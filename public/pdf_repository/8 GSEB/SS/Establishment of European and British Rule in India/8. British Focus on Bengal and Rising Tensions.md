This section details the British East India Company's increasing focus on Bengal and the resulting conflict with the local ruler.

# British Expansion and Conflict in Bengal

*   The British established their first 'factory' (trading center) in **Bengal in 1651 AD**.
*   In **1717 AD**, Mughal Emperor **Farrukh Shiyar** granted them permission for duty-free trade in Bengal, Bihar, and Odisha, and also allowed them to lease areas around Calcutta (Kolkata).
*   After **1740 AD**, the British began fortifying their factory (**Fort William**), which strained their relations with the Nawab of Bengal, **Siraj-ud-Daula**.
*   Siraj-ud-Daula viewed the fortification as a challenge to his authority.
*   On **June 15, 1756**, Siraj-ud-Daula attacked Fort William and defeated the British forces there.
