This section details the founding and initial activities of the British East India Company in India.

# The British East India Company's Foothold

*   The **East India Company** was founded in England in **1600 AD**.
*   Its primary goal was to trade with Eastern nations and establish a monopoly.
*   In **1613 AD**, the British obtained a decree from Mughal Emperor **Jahangir** to establish their first fort at **Surat**.
*   **Thomas Roe** secured permission from Jahangir to trade throughout India in **1615 AD**.
*   They started a trading company in **Balasore (1633 AD)** and a trading post on the banks of the **Hoogly River (1651 AD)**.
*   This trading post was called a **'factory'**. A factory served as a warehouse for goods intended for export and housed company officials.
*   A trading post was also set up at **Qasim Bazaar in Patna** and placed under the control of Fort St. George.
*   In **1698 AD**, the British acquired the **'Zamidari'** (land rights) of three villages: **Sutanuti, Kalighat, and Gobindapur**.
*   They built a new fortified settlement here called **'Fort William'**, which later grew into the city now known as **Kolkata**.
