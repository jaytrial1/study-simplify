This section describes the wars fought between the British and the Maratha Confederacy.

# The Maratha Wars

*   In **1761 AD**, the Marathas were defeated in the **Third Battle of Panipat** against Ahmedshah Abdali of Iran.
*   This defeat weakened the central Maratha authority and led to the empire being divided among major families: the **Sindhias, Holkars, Gaekwads, and Bhonsles**.
*   These dynasties were nominally under the control of the **Peshwa** (supreme minister), whose headquarter was in **Pune**.
*   Several wars were fought between the Marathas and the British:
    *   **First Maratha War (1775 AD to 1782 AD)**: Ended with the **Treaty of Salbai (1782 AD)**. Both sides agreed to return territories, resulting in no clear victor.
    *   **Second Maratha War (1803 AD - 1805 AD)**: Governor-General **Wellesley** established British authority over the Marathas. As a result of this war, territories like **Odisha, Agra (north of Yamuna), and Delhi** came under British control.
    *   **Third Anglo-Maratha War (1817 AD - 1819 AD)**: The Maratha power was decisively crushed. The **Peshwa** was removed from Pune and sent to **Bithur** near Kanpur with a pension.
*   With the defeat of the Marathas, the power of the British East India Company extended from the Vindhyachal range to all parts of South India. British rule was now largely established over the entire Indian subcontinent by **1818 AD**.
