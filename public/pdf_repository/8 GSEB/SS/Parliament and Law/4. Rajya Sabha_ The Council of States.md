This section details the composition, qualifications, term, and functions of the Rajya Sabha, the upper house of Parliament.

# Rajya Sabha (Upper House)

In all, there are **250 members** in our Rajya Sabha. Of these, **12 members are nominated by the President**. The members nominated by the President are well-known personalities linked with different fields of activities such as Literature, Science, Sports, Art, and Social Service and are recognised for their specific contribution. The remaining **238 members are representatives of different states, who are indirectly elected**. The Rajya Sabha is also known as the **'Council of States'**. Eleven seats are allotted to Gujarat in the Rajya Sabha.

## Qualification for the membership of Rajya Sabha:

*   S/he must be a citizen of India.
*   S/he must be aged 30 years or above.
*   S/he should not be bankrupt, a culprit, or with an unstable mind.
*   S/he should not be a salaried employee of the government.

## Term:

The term of the member of Rajya Sabha is **6 years**. Rajya Sabha is a **permanent house** as it is not completely dissolved. Every two years, one-third of the members get retired and election is held for the same number of members.

## Functions of Rajya Sabha:

*   Like Lok Sabha, the Rajya Sabha also forms, amends, and repeals laws.
*   The Rajya Sabha discusses the functioning of the government.
*   The Rajya Sabha asks questions and complementary questions to the ministers related to the functioning of the administrative machinery.
*   In terms of control over financial matters and administrative machinery, the Rajya Sabha has **lesser rights as compared to the Lok Sabha**.
