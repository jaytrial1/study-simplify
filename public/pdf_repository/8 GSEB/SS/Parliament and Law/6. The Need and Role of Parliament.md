This section explains why Parliament is essential in a diverse country like India and describes its general functions.

## The Need for a Parliament

India is a country of much diversity. It inhabits people belonging to different religions and communities. Parliament becomes necessary and important to run and manage the country amid such diversity. In our Constitution, therefore, the Parliament has been described as the biggest **democratic institution**.

We have learned that the Parliament consists of two houses and both the houses participate equally in the process of formation and making amendments in laws. For this purpose, specific guidelines have been given in the Constitution. The Parliament functions independently.

## Role of the Parliament

We have observed that the Parliament functions in accordance with the guidelines given in the Constitution. Different committees are formed to carry out these functions. One of the important functions carried out by the Parliament is **formation of new laws** and making amendments in existing laws or repealing them. Through the budget, the parliament exercises **control over the expenditure** made by the executive machinery. The **debates** on government's policies which take place in the Parliament make the government conscious about its mistakes and shortcomings. These debates also make the whole nation aware about the government policies.
