This section discusses the fundamental reasons why laws are necessary in a democratic society like India.

## Why Law is Needed?

'All are equal and all are respectable' is the policy on the basis of which the law of our country functions. Law ensures peace, safety, and unity in our country. Law is necessary for democracy and smooth running of the system. Values stated in the Indian Constitution are taken into account while forming the laws. If a law is formed against the stated norms in the Indian constitution, the Indian judiciary can nullify it.
