This section explains the general role and rights of Members of Parliament (MPs) in representing their constituencies and participating in parliamentary discussions.

## Role of a Member of Parliament

Members of both the Houses enjoy similar rights. In the Parliament, they talk about the problems or issues of the constituency they represent.

Proceedings of both the Houses are telecast so that people can watch them. Members of any of the Houses can present their ideas or views freely. The questions asked in the parliament and the answers given to them are recorded.
