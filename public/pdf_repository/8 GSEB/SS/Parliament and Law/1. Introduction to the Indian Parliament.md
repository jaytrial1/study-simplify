# The Indian Parliament

Our school is managed by the principal, teachers, and students. Your school may have Shala-Panchayat or Shala Samiti, involving students to carry out different responsibilities. Likewise, specific provisions have been made in our Constitution to run the country. This system is known as **Parliament**. There is Gram-Panchayat to manage activities at the village level. We also know about Municipalities managing small towns and Municipal Corporations managing bigger cities. Similarly, the country is managed by the Parliament. Let us study about the Parliament of our country and other related aspects.
