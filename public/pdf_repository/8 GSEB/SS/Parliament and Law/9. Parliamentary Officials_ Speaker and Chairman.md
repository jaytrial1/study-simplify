This section describes the roles of the Speaker of the Lok Sabha and the Chairman of the Rajya Sabha in managing the proceedings of the houses.

## The Speaker-Chairman

The **Speaker of the Lok Sabha** is elected from among the members of the Lok Sabha. The elected Speaker mostly belongs to the party having majority. According to the provision made in the Constitution, the **Vice President of India is also ex officio Chairman of the Rajya Sabha**. If any member behaves in an indecent manner in the House, the Speaker or the Chairperson can suspend him/her for a day or for the whole session. In both the Houses, members ask their questions addressing the Speaker or the Chairperson. They cannot ask directly to the Prime Minister or other ministers.

# Things to Know

*   Mr. Ganesh Vasudev Mavlankar was the first Speaker of the Lok Sabha.
*   Dr. Sarvapalli Radhakrishnan was the first Chairman of the Rajya Sabha.
