This section introduces the role, qualifications, powers, and term of the President of India, the constitutional head of the country.

## The President

The President is the **first citizen** of the country and also the **Constitutional head**. The whole administration of the country is carried out in the name of the President.

## Qualifications:

*   The candidate must be a citizen of India.
*   S/he must be aged 35 years or above.
*   S/he should not be a salaried employee of the government.
*   S/he should have qualification to be eligible for the membership of the Lok Sabha or the Rajya Sabha.

## Powers and functions of the President:

*   The President appoints the ministers of the Union Council of Ministers with the advice of the Prime Minister and administers the oath of office to the appointed ministers.
*   The President is the **Supreme Commander of the Armed Forces** of India and is authorised to declare war or conclude a treaty.
*   The President appoints the governors of states, the judges of the Supreme Court, chief election officials and Indian ambassadors for different countries.
*   The President is authorised to summon or to prorogue the meetings of both the Houses.
*   The President of India may issue a **Proclamation of National Emergency** when the security of any part of the country is threatened.
*   The President may dismiss the state council of ministers and take over the executive powers of the state by imposing **President's Rule** in that state.
*   A Bill becomes a Law only after it is signed by the President.

# Term:

*   The term of the President is **5 years**.
*   When in office, no criminal case can be lodged against the President. Nor any order can be passed for his arrest or imprisonment. In the absence of the President, Vice- President discharges the duties. The President gives his/her resignation to the Vice- President.

## Know This

*   Dr. Rajendra Prasad was the first President of India.
*   Mrs. Pratibha Patil was the first woman President of India.
