This section explains how laws are made in India, particularly focusing on the process of an ordinary bill becoming an act.

## Understanding Law

In India, the methods of formation of legislation are developed on the basis of the model of the British Parliament. As mentioned earlier, new laws are formed and amendments are made in existing laws when necessary. The aim of framing laws is to maintain individual as well as collective freedom. The Executive body sees to it that the laws are properly implemented. The laws of our country bestow equal rights to each citizen. If the law is not followed and results in injustice to someone, s/he can go to the judiciary. The judiciary interprets the law and confers proper justice to the victim. Constitutionally, the judiciary functions independently.

## Things to Know

### When an ordinary bill becomes an act?

(1) In the first reading, principles/broad objectives of the bill are discussed.
(2) In the second reading, thorough and serious discussion about the bill takes place.
(3) In the third reading, each clause of the bill is discussed and then it is sent to the other House. There also, the bill goes through three readings. After both the Houses pass the bill, it is sent to the President for assent. After his/her signature, the bill becomes an act.
