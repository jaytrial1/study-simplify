This section provides details about the Lok Sabha, including its composition, qualifications for membership, term, and key functions.

# Lok Sabha (Lower House)

There are, in all, **545 members** in our Lok Sabha. These members represent different Indian states and union territories. The number of seats of the parliament is decided on the basis of the population of states. Efforts are made to ensure equal representation of each state. Lok Sabha is also known as **'House of People'**, as its members are elected through direct voting of people. Certain seats are kept reserved for the representatives of scheduled castes and scheduled tribes. In Lok Sabha, 26 seats are allotted to Gujarat.

## Qualification for the membership of Lok Sabha:

*   The candidate must be a citizen of India.
*   The candidate must be aged 25 years or above.
*   The candidate should not be bankrupt, a culprit, or with an unstable mind.
*   The candidate should not be a salaried employee of the government.

## Term:

The term of the Lok Sabha is **five years**. However, in specific circumstances, the President can dissolve the Lok Sabha or can prolong its term. After the completion of the term, the Lok Sabha is dissolved and it is formed again through fresh elections.

## Functions of Lok Sabha:

*   The Lok Sabha passes bills concerning subjects which have been included in the **Union List**.
*   Considering the needs or the requirements of the people, the Lok Sabha forms new laws and makes amendments in existing laws or repeals them.
*   The Lok Sabha controls and regulates the activities of the **council of ministers**.
*   The Lok Sabha sanctions the **budget**. The Budget is first presented in the Lok Sabha and thereafter in the Rajya Sabha.
*   If the members of the Lok Sabha are not satisfied with the working of the council of ministers, they can pass a **motion of no confidence** against the council of ministers and compel the government to vacate the office.
*   Important national issues are discussed in the Lok Sabha and are brought to the government's notice.
*   For any financial matter, the central government needs sanction from the Lok Sabha.
