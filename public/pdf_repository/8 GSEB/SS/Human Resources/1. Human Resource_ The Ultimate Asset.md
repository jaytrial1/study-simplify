This section introduces the concept of human resources as the most important resource and how people transform natural elements into useful resources.

# Human Resource

People are considered the nation's greatest resource. The significance of nature's elements becomes apparent only when people find them useful. Humans utilize their demands and abilities to convert natural elements into resources. Therefore, **human resource** is recognized as the ultimate resource. A healthy, educated, and motivated population is crucial for developing resources according to needs.

Unlike other resources, human resources are not evenly distributed across the globe. They vary significantly in terms of numbers, age, gender, educational levels, and other characteristics.
