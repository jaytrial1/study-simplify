# Distribution of Population

The way people are spread across the Earth's surface is known as the **pattern of population distribution** or **population division**. In 1999 AD, the world population was approximately six billion. Notably, over 90 percent of the global population resides on only about 30 percent of the land area. This distribution is highly uneven, with some areas being densely populated and others very sparsely populated.

*   **Densely Populated Areas:** Southeast Asia, Europe, and Northeast North America.
*   **Sparsely Populated Areas:** High latitude regions, tropical deserts, mountains, and tropical forests.

More people live in the Northern Hemisphere compared to the Southern Hemisphere. Approximately three-fourths of the world's population lives in Asia and Africa. Furthermore, 60 percent of the world's population lives in just 10 countries.
