# Density of Population

**Population density** is defined as the number of people living within a specific unit geographic area of the Earth's surface, typically expressed as per square kilometer.

The formula for calculating population density is:
$$
\text{Density of Population} = \frac{\text{Total population of the country}}{\text{Total area of the country}}
$$

As per the 2011 census, India has an average population density of 382 persons per square kilometer. In contrast, the world average population density in 2011 was 54 persons per square kilometer. The highest population density globally is found in South Central Asia, followed by East Asia and Southeast Asia.
