# Transformation and Development of Major Cities

During the British rule, many old cities underwent significant **transformation**, while new cities developed rapidly. These new urban centers emerged to meet the requirements of **trade**, **industries**, and **administration**. Examples of this change include major cities like Kolkata, Surat, Madras (Chennai), Mumbai, Ahmedabad, and Indore.

- How was this town earlier? How has this city developed in the present time?
- What kind of development has taken place in this city in the present time?
