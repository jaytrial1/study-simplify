# Development of Infrastructure: Railways, Post, and Telegraph

India saw significant improvements in infrastructure during the period of **Lord Dalhousie**. Some English reformers referred to him as the creator of modern India due to these developments. The **first railway line** in India, connecting Mumbai to Thane, began operation in 1853 AD, inaugurated on April 16, 1853. The **Telegraph line** between Kolkata and Peshawar, and Mumbai and Madras (Chennai) also started during Dalhousie's time. Around the same period, the **postal system** was introduced. Initially, mail delivery fees were charged to the recipient rather than the sender. Postal rates also varied by region. Despite many obstacles and hindrances under British governance, crucial infrastructure like cotton textiles, chemicals, iron and steel industries, post and telegraph services, and railway and transport facilities began to develop.
