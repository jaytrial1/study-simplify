# Child Rights: Nurturing Future Citizens

The development of any nation depends on the overall development of its children. It is a priority to develop their physical, mental, and intellectual abilities to make them healthy and responsible citizens.

Following are the **child rights** declared by the United Nations for the development and welfare of children:
*   Right to live without discrimination based on caste, colour, gender, language, religion, or nationality.
*   Right to adequate nurturing by parents.
*   Right to education for personal development.
*   Right to a healthy life.
*   Right to live in one's own religious community and preserve its culture.
*   Right to freedom of expression.
*   Right to protection against mental or physical harassment, exploitation, and agony.
*   Right to social security and a proper standard of living.

## Things to Know

**'Child Rights Day'** is celebrated every year on November 20th.
