This section outlines the provisions made in the Indian Constitution to protect disadvantaged groups and discusses the broader societal responsibility towards achieving equality.

# Constitutional and Societal Efforts for Equality

Certain provisions have been made in the Constitution of India for the protection, welfare, and development of Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes, nomadic communities, socially and economically backward classes, and minorities.

*   The Constitution of India grants social, economic, and political justice equally to all citizens.
*   It states that each citizen is entitled to equal opportunity and status.
*   Through Constitutional rights, people from backward classes are given equal opportunity, justice, and status.
*   Specific provisions have also been made for people of these classes in Five Year Plans.

## Social Inequality and Economic Condition

Perceiving someone as superior or inferior is a common human practice. When this becomes deeply rooted, discrimination appears in society. This can lead to people only helping those in their own group and disliking others. Such a situation can cause **class conflict** with serious societal impact. Consequently, a certain class may fall behind in progress.

This deprived class may lack access to facilities for development. Their ability to speak out against injustice also weakens. They remain exploited and deprived, becoming victims of **lineal poverty**.

With growing awareness today, the development of backward classes is seen as the responsibility of the whole society. This requires efforts for social change. The government has implemented various schemes to help these groups get justice.
