# Government Schemes for Social Justice

After independence, the government of India implemented several schemes to ensure equality and equal opportunity for all citizens. Here are some examples:

*   In Parliament, state legislatures, and Panchayats, certain constituencies are reserved for classes needing social justice. This ensures their representation and gives them a voice.
*   Seats are reserved in educational institutions for certain classes to increase literacy among them and improve their social status.
*   To help socially backward classes become self-sufficient, interest-free or low-interest loans are provided for small occupations, cottage industries, and small industries. Loans are also available for purchasing vehicles like rickshaws, helping them become economically independent.

Many other schemes have been implemented to protect backward classes, significantly contributing to social justice.
