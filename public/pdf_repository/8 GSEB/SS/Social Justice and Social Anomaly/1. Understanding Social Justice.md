This section introduces the concepts of social justice and social inequality, defining what they mean in the context of a changing society and listing common forms of inequality.

# Understanding Social Justice

Indian society is undergoing rapid transformation from traditional to modern. Changes are occurring in economic, social, political, and cultural areas. A fair and consistent system is needed for society to function well. To ensure **social justice**, all people should have equal opportunities for development without discrimination based on caste, sex, race, etc.

'Social Justice' combines two words. 'Social' relates to all people in a society. 'Justice' relates to independence, equality, and rights. **Social justice** aims to ensure individual independence, equality, and protect individual rights. Its goal is to reconstruct society.

## Social Inequality

**Social inequality** refers to a situation where there is inequality in enjoying individual rights and accessing opportunities. In India, inequality is seen in education, employment, income, sex, caste, etc. Historically, sex-based discrimination was common, like paying unequal wages for similar work or discriminating in raising and educating sons and daughters.

Following are some reasons for social inequality:
*   People are deprived of social benefits due to lack of education.
*   Lack of knowledge about laws can also lead to social inequality.
*   Harmful social practices can obstruct the development of certain communities.
*   Conservative beliefs can hinder social development by preventing people from accepting new ideas or changes.
