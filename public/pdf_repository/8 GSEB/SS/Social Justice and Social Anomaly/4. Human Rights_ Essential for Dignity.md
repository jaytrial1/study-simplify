# Human Rights: Essential for Dignity

**Human Rights** are essential for a person to live with dignity. They are rights that all human beings are born with. Everyone has the right to live with dignity without facing discrimination based on caste, religion, language, nationality, etc.

Following are some Human Rights declared by the United Nations:
*   Right to adequate food, housing, water, and hygiene
*   Right to work and fair pay (remuneration)
*   Right to education
*   Right to freedom of expression
*   Right to property
*   Right to privacy

## Know This

Every year, December 10th is celebrated as **'Human Rights Day'**.

> **Answer:** A Human Rights Commission is an independent body established to protect and promote human rights. Its activities typically include investigating complaints of human rights violations, educating the public about human rights, advising the government on human rights issues, and working to ensure laws and policies comply with human rights standards.
