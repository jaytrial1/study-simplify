This section describes India's federal structure, explaining the division of power between the central (union) government and state governments due to the country's size and diversity.

# Union Territory/State

India is a federal union comprising different states. Given India's large size and great diversities, it is challenging to govern the entire country from one central location. Therefore, the Constitution provides for a **federal structure**.

The federal structure has two main parts:
1.  **Federal government** (also known as the Central government)
2.  **State government**

The work and authority of these two levels of government are clearly defined. The federal government is entrusted with important tasks and authority.
