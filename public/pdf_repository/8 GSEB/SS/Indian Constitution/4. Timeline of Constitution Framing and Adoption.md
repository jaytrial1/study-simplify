This section outlines the key dates and duration involved in the drafting and implementation of the Indian Constitution.

# Process and Timeline

The first meeting of the Constituent Assembly was held on December 9, 1946. The entire process of framing the Constitution took 2 years, 11 months, and 18 days. During this period, 166 meetings of the Constituent Assembly were held. Before finalizing the Constitution, important aspects of the constitutions of different countries were thoroughly studied.

After India's independence, the Constituent Assembly passed the Constitution on November 26, 1949, and it was enforced on January 26, 1950.
