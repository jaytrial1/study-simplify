# Secularism

India is a **secular country**. This means the country is not run based on the ideology or beliefs of any single sect or religion. No discrimination is made based on sect or community. The Constitution grants freedom related to sect and religion to every citizen, allowing them to follow and propagate their religion and beliefs.
