# Democracy

India has adopted a democratic form of governance. In simple terms, democracy is defined as the **'rule of the people, by the people and for the people'**. This means the system is authorized and managed by the people. In India, elections are held every five years where people elect their representatives. These elected representatives serve for five years. Any citizen of India can be a candidate in an election without discrimination based on sex, caste, religion, etc. Independence is a crucial aspect of democracy.

## Know This

*   At the time the Constitution was formed, the minimum age for voting was 21 years.
*   39 years after the Constitution's enforcement, the minimum age for voting was changed to 18 years through the 61st amendment.
