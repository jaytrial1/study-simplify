This section details the formation and composition of the Constituent Assembly responsible for drafting the Constitution, highlighting key members and the role of its chief architect.

# Architect of Constitution

A 'Constituent Assembly' was formed for the purpose of creating the Constitution.

The Constituent Assembly had a total of 389 members. For the Constitution-forming process, 23 committees were formed to handle different subjects. These committees included members from various communities, religions, genders, geographical areas, representatives of political parties, and scholars from different fields.

Notable members included:
*   Dr. Rajendra Prasad
*   Jawaharlal Nehru
*   Dr. Bhimrao Ambedkar
*   Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel
*   Sardar Baldev Singh
*   Frank Anthony
*   H. P. Modi
*   A.K.S. Ayer
*   Maulana Abul Kalam Azad
*   Shyama Prasad Mukherjee
*   Kanhaiyalal Munshi
*   K.T. Shah

Female representatives included:
*   Sarojini Naidu
*   Vijaya Laxmi Pandit

Dr. Rajendra Prasad was the president of the Constituent Assembly. Dr. Bhimrao Ambedkar was the chairman of the Drafting Committee. Due to his significant contribution in shaping the Constitution, he is known as the **chief architect of the Constitution of India**.
