This section explains what it means for India to be a republic, highlighting that the head of state is elected and positions are open to all citizens.

# Republic

India is a **republic country**. This signifies that the country is governed by its own people, elected directly or indirectly. The head of the State is not determined by inheritance, as in a monarchy, but is elected through indirect voting. In this republican system, all positions, from the Panchayat level to the Parliament, are open to all citizens regardless of gender, caste, or religion.
