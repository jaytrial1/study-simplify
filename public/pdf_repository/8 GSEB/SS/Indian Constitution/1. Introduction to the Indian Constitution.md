# Indian Constitution

India is a democratic country with people of diverse religions, castes, and languages. It is also notable for its geographic, social, and local diversities. The Indian Constitution was formed to ensure equal opportunities for all citizens, regardless of their background. Every country has a fundamental document in the form of its Constitution, which the government follows to administer the nation. The country must be run in accordance with its Constitution. The Indian Constitution is the longest written Constitution in the world and was created to govern the country effectively amidst its diversities and challenges.
