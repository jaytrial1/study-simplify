# Types of Minerals
This section explains the main classification of minerals into metallic and non-metallic based on their composition and properties.

There are more than three thousand different minerals on earth. Minerals are mainly classified into **metallic** and **non-metallic** on the basis of composition.

## Metallic Minerals
In metallic minerals, metals are in raw form. Metals are hard substances that conduct heat, electricity and have the characteristics of lustre or luminosity. Metallic minerals are found in large layers of igneous and metamorphic rocks. Metals obtained from these minerals can be moulded into different shapes by tapping or straining. It can not be broken by striking. Metals are obtained by melting metallic minerals. Gold, zinc, silver, copper, aluminum, iron etc are main minerals.

## Non-metallic Minerals
These minerals do not contain metals. Some non-metallic minerals can be moulded into different shapes by cutting, uprooting or breaking. Striking breaks them into pieces. Limestone, mica and gypsum are examples of these minerals. Energy resources such as coal and petroleum are also non-metallic minerals. Non-metallic minerals are found in the sedimentary rocks of the plains and the fold mountains. Mineral fuels such as coal and petroleum are also found in sedimentary layers.
