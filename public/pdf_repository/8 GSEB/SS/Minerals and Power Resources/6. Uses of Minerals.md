# Uses of Common Minerals
This section lists the various applications and uses of several common minerals in industries and daily life.

Here are some uses of common minerals:

*   **Manganese:** Used in chemical industry, pesticides, glass, varnish, and printing industry. Mainly used to make steel from iron.
*   **Copper:** Used in electrical wires, explosives, coloured glass, coins, and printing. Adding tin to copper makes **bronze**, and adding zinc makes **brass**. Used in making telephones, radios, televisions, refrigerators, and air conditioners.
*   **Aluminium:** Obtained from **bauxite**. Bauxite is used in electrical equipment, paints, manufacturing airplanes, kerosene refining, and manufacturing cement.
*   **Mica:** As Mica is a fire retardant and an electrical insulator, it is used in manufacturing electrical equipment. Used in manufacturing radio, telephone, airplane, dynamo, automobiles, electric motors etc.
*   **Fluorspar:** Used in metallurgy industry, plastics industry, hydrochloric acid, manufacture of porcelain.
*   **Limestone:** Used in industries like cement, iron, steel, soda ash, soap, paper, paint, sugar-refining.
*   **Lead:** Used in manufacturing storage batteries and zinc oxide.
*   **Zinc:** Used for plating galvanized foil and for making utensils.
*   **Iron (iron ore):** Used to make everything from pins to large machines, motor vehicles, ships, railways, bridges, houses, and weapons.
*   **Coal:** Used as fuel in the production of thermal power.
*   **Silicon:** Used in computer industry, derived from **quartz**.
