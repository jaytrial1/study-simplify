# Minerals and Ores
This section introduces the basic definitions of minerals and ores, their natural formation process, identification characteristics, and their importance to the economy.

'Organic and inorganic substances which change under heat and pressure to assume a certain chemical composition are called **minerals**.' Minerals found in the interior regions of the earth in impure forms are called **ores**. Various minerals are obtained in pure form after refining the ore. Minerals are not evenly distributed.

Minerals are a gift of nature. They are formed under different conditions in different types of geographical conditions. They are formed naturally. This treasure is created after thousands of years of natural processes in the earth's crust. They can be identified on the basis of their chemical properties such as colour, lustre, density, hardness etc. Minerals supply raw materials to industries and make the nation prosperous. So minerals are the backbone of the national economy.
