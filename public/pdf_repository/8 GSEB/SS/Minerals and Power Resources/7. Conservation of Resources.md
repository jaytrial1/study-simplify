# Conservation of Minerals and Energy
This section discusses the importance of conserving mineral and energy resources, particularly non-renewable ones, and suggests methods for conservation.

## Conservation of Minerals
*   Coal and petroleum are **non-renewable resources**. It takes thousands of years for their formation and concentration. The rate of formation of non-renewable resources is much smaller than the rate at which humans consume them. It is necessary to reduce wastage in the process of mining.
*   **Recycling of metals:** Scrap of iron, copper, aluminium and tin should be reused.
*   Alternatives to minerals found in less quantity should be explored. The use of solar-electricity instead of electricity, consumption of CNG instead of petrol should be increased.

## Conservation of Energy Resources
*   The use of non-conventional resources like water (hydro power), solar energy, wind, biogas should be increased.
*   Efforts should be made to give the gift of clean environment to future generations while maintaining the quality of environment.

You must have seen scrap dealers collecting various waste material. What must they be doing with it?
> **Answer:** Scrap dealers collect waste materials like metal, paper, and plastic to sell them to recycling facilities. Recycling allows these materials to be processed and reused, conserving natural resources and reducing waste.

Energy resources are very precious. Since they are limited, it is our duty to use them sparingly. We must be vigilant and committed to energy conservation before it becomes a major crisis in the coming days.
