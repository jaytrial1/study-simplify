# Producer's Equilibrium

In the $2^{\text {nd }}$ chapter, 'Consumer's Equilibrium', we discussed that a consumer is at equilibrium when he / she derives maximum satisfaction. The present chapter focuses on the determination of "Equilibrium level of Producer".

Like a consumer, a producer also aims to maximise his / her satisfaction. But a producer's satisfaction is maximised in terms of profit. So, this chapter deals with the determination of a level of output which yields the maximum profit. In order to clearly understand the concept of producer's equilibrium, it is necessary to understand the meaning of profit.
