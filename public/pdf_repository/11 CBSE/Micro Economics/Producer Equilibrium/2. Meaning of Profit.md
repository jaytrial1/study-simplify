## 1.2 MEANING OF PROFIT

Profit refers to the excess of receipts from the sale of goods over the expenditure incurred on producing them.
The amount received from the sale of goods is known as 'revenue' and the expenditure on production of such goods is termed as 'cost'. The difference between revenue and cost is known as 'profit'. For example, if a firm sells goods for **10** crores after incurring an expenditure of 7 crores, then profit will be 3 crores.
