# Important Formulae at a Glance

1.  $\mathrm{TFC}=\mathrm{TC}-\mathrm{TVC}$
2.  $\mathrm{TVC}=\mathrm{TC}-\mathrm{TFC}$
3.  $\mathrm{TC}=\mathrm{TVC}+\mathrm{TFC}$
4.  $\mathrm{MC}_{n}=\mathrm{TC}_{n}-\mathrm{TC}_{n-1}$
5.  $\mathrm{AFC}=\mathrm{TFC} \div$ Output
6.  $\mathrm{AVC}=\mathrm{TVC} \div$ Output
7.  $\mathrm{AC}=\mathrm{TC} \div$ Output

    Or $\quad$ TFC $=\mathrm{AFC} \times$ output
    Or $\quad$ TVC $=$ AVC $\times$ output
    Or $\quad$ TC $=$ AC $\times$ output
    Or $\quad$ MC $_{n}=\mathrm{TVC}_{n}-\mathrm{TVC}_{n-1}$
    $\mathrm{AFC}=\mathrm{AC}-\mathrm{AVC}$
    $\mathrm{AVC}=\mathrm{AC}-\mathrm{AFC}$
    $\mathrm{AC}=\mathrm{AVC}+\mathrm{AFC}$

## Example 1. Calculate Total Fixed Cost (TFC) and Total Variable Cost (TVC).

| Output (units) | 0  | 1   | 2   | 3   | 4   |
| :--------------- | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| TC (₹)          | 80  | 102 | 122 | 140 | 156 |

### Solution:

| Output  (Units) | Total Cost (₹)  TC | Total Fixed Cost (₹)  TFC | Total Variable Cost (₹)  TC-TFC = TVC |
| :---------------: | :-----------------: | :---: | :-----------------------: | :---: | :--------------------------------: |
| 0 | 80 | 80 | $80-80=0$ |
| 1 | 102 | 80 | $102-80=22$ |
| 2 | 122 | 80 | $122-80=42$ |
| 3 | 140 | 80 | $140-80=60$ |
| 4 | 156 | 80 | $156-80=76$ |

Note: TFC $=$ TC at 0 level of output.

Note: The intercept of ₹ 50 on the $Y$-axis indicates that total cost (TC) is equal to ₹ 50 at zero output. It means, TFC $=₹ 50$ as $T C=T F C$ at zero output.

```markdown
## Example 25

Calculate the weekly TC and AVC from the following particulars:

| Particulars                      |     |
| :------------------------------- | :-: |
| Number of workers employed       | 50  |
| Number of units produced per week | 100 |
| Weekly wage of each worker       | ₹ 200 |
| Weekly rent of shed              | ₹ 400 |
| Raw materials used               | ₹ 1,600 |
| Power                            | ₹ 300 |

**Solution:**

TC = TVC + TFC

TVC = Raw material used + Power + (Number of workers × Weekly wage)

TVC = 1,600 + 300 + (50 × 200) = ₹ 11,900

TFC = Weekly rent of shed = ₹ 400

TC = 11,900 + 400 = ₹ **12,300**

AVC = TVC ÷ Units produced = 11,900 ÷ 100 = ₹ 119

MC = TC = ₹ 12,300; AVC = ₹ 119

## Example 26

A firm is producing 20 units. At this level of output, ATC and AVC are respectively equal to ₹ 40 and ₹ 37. Find out the total fixed cost of the firm.

**Solution:**

Given: Units = 20; ATC = ₹ 40; AVC = ₹ 37

We know, AFC = ATC - AVC. So, AFC = ₹ 3

Also, TFC = AFC × units

So, TFC = ₹ 3 × 20 = ₹ 60

Total Fixed Cost = ₹ 60

## Example 27
```

```
## Example 30

When output increases from 40 units to 55 units, TC increases from ₹ 2,500 to ₹ 3,250. Calculate MC.

**Solution:**

```
MC = Change in Total Cost / Change in number of units = ΔTC/ΔQ = (3,250 - 2,500) / (55 - 40) = 750 / 15 = ₹ 50
```

Ans. MC = ₹ 50

## Example 31

The total cost of a firm increased by ₹ 450, when production increased from 12 units to 15 units. Calculate marginal cost of the firm.
```

```

Ans. Marginal Cost = ₹ 150
```
