# Important Formulae at a Glance

1.  $\mathrm{TP}=$ Units of variable factor $\times \mathrm{AP}=\Sigma \mathrm{MP}$
2.  $A P=T P \div$ Units of variable factor
3.  $\mathrm{MP}_{\mathrm{n}}=\mathrm{TP}_{\mathrm{n}}-\mathrm{TP}_{\mathrm{n}-1}$

# Practicals on TP, AP and MP

## Example 1
Calculate Average Product (AP) and Marginal Product (MP):

| Variable Factors | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| :--------------- | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| Total Product (TP) | 0 | 8 | 20 | 28 | 28 | 25 |

### Solution
| Variable Factors (VF) | TP (in units) | AP (in units) $T P \div V F=A P$ | MP (in units) $T P_{n}-T P_{n-1}=M P_{n}$ |
| :--------------------: | :------------: | :------------------------------: | :---------------------------------------: |
| 0 | 0 | - | - |
| 1 | 8 | $8 \div 1=8$ | $8-0=8$ |
| 2 | 20 | $20 \div 2=10$ | $20-8=12$ |
| 3 | 28 | $28 \div 3=9.33$ | $28-20=8$ |
| 4 | 28 | $28 \div 4=7$ | $28-28=0$ |
| 5 | 25 | $25 \div 5=5$ | $25-28=-3$ |

## Example 3
Calculate TP and AP:

| Variable Factor | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 |
| :-------------- | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| MP (in units) | 24 | 20 | 16 | 12 | 8 | 0 | -8 |

### Solution
| Variable Factor (VF) | MP (in units) | TP (in units) $T P=\Sigma M P$ | AP (in units) $T P \div V F=A P$ |
| :-------------------: | :------------: | :----------------------------: | :------------------------------: |
| 1 | 24 | $24+0=24$ | $24 \div 1=24$ |
| 2 | 20 | $24+20=44$ | $44 \div 2=22$ |
| 3 | 16 | $44+16=60$ | $60 \div 3=20$ |
| 4 | 12 | $60+12=72$ | $72 \div 4=18$ |
| 5 | 8 | $72+8=80$ | $80 \div 5=16$ |
| 6 | 0 | $80+0=80$ | $80 \div 6=13.33$ |
| 7 | -8 | $80-8=72$ | $72 \div 7=10.28$ |

## Example 9
Identify different phases of the Law of Variable Proportions from the following schedule. Give reasons for your answer.

| Variable Input (Units) | Total Physical Product (Units) |  |
| :----------------------: | :------------------------------: | :--: |
| 1 | 4 |  |
| 2 | 9 |  |
| 3 | 13 |  |
| 4 | 15 |  |
| 5 | 12 |  |

### Solution
| Variable Input (Units) | Total Physical Product or TPP (Units) | Marginal Physical Product or MPP (Units) $M P P_{n}=T P P_{n}-T P P_{n-1}$ | Phase |
| :----------------------: | :------------------------------------: | :-----------------------------------------------------------------------: | :---------------------: |
| 1 | 4 | 4 | $1^{\text {st }}$ Phase (IRF) |
| 2 | 9 | 5 |  |
| 3 | 13 | 4 | $2^{\text {nd }}$ Phase (DRF) |
| 4 | 15 | 2 |  |
| 5 | 12 | -3 | $3^{\text {rd }}$ Phase (NRF) |

### Reason behind each Phase
- $1^{\text {st }}$ Phase (IRF or Increasing returns to a factor): TP increases at an increasing rate and MP also increases.
- $2^{\text {nd }}$ Phase (DRF or Diminishing returns to a factor): TP increases at decreasing rate and MP falls. This phase ends when MP becomes zero and TP reaches its maximum point.
- $3^{\text {rd }}$ Phase (NRF or Negative returns to a factor): TP starts decreasing and MP not only falls, but also becomes negative.
