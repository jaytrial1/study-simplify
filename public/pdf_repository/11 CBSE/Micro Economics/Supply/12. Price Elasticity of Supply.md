# Price Elasticity of Supply

This concept is parallel to the concept of price elasticity of demand. It points out the reaction of the sellers to a particular change in the price of the commodity. It explains the quantitative changes in the supply of a commodity, due to a given change in the price of the commodity.

Price elasticity of supply refers to the degree of responsiveness of the supply of a commodity with reference to a change in the price of such commodity. For example, if the price elasticity of supply is 2, it means that a one percent fall in price leads to a 2 percent fall in supply or a one percent rise in price leads to a 2 percent rise in supply.
