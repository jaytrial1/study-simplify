# Supply Curve: Movement vs. Shift

## Movement Along Supply Curve vs. Shift in Supply Curve

| Basis                 | Movement along Supply Curve                                                                       | Shift in Supply Curve                                                                                                     |
| :-------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Meaning               | When the quantity supplied changes due to a change in price, keeping other factors constant, it leads to a movement along the supply curve. | When the supply changes due to a change in any factor other than the own price of the commodity, it leads to a shift in the supply curve. |
| Effect on supply curve | The movement is along the same supply curve (see Fig. 9.4) either upward (known as Expansion in supply) or downward (known as Contraction in supply).  | The shift in the supply curve (see Fig. 9.7) is either rightward (known as Increase in supply) or leftward (known as decrease in supply). |
| Reason                | It occurs due to an increase or decrease in the price of the given commodity.                     | It occurs due to a change in other factors, like a change in the price of inputs, change in taxes, change in technology etc. |

## Change in Quantity Supplied vs. Change in Supply

| Basis                 | Change in Quantity Supplied                                                                       | Change in Supply                                                                                                        |
| :-------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Meaning               | When the quantity supplied changes due to a change in price, keeping other factors constant, it is known as a change in quantity supplied. | When the supply changes due to a change in any factor other than the own price of the commodity, it is known as a change in supply. |
| Effect on supply curve | It leads to a movement along the same supply curve (see Fig. 9.4) either upward (known as Expansion in supply) or downward (known as Contraction in supply).  | It leads to a shift in the supply curve (see Fig. 9.7) either rightward (known as increase in supply) or leftward (known as decrease in supply). |
| Reason                | It occurs due to an increase or decrease in the price of the given commodity.                     | It occurs due to a change in other factors, like a change in the price of inputs, change in taxes, change in technology etc. |

## Expansion in Supply vs. Increase in Supply

| Basis                 | Expansion in Supply                                                                                                   | Increase in Supply                                                                                               |
| :-------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Meaning               | When the quantity supplied rises due to an increase in the price, keeping other factors constant, it is known as expansion in supply. | Increase in supply refers to a rise in the supply of a commodity caused due to any factor other than the own price of the commodity. |
| Tabular Presentation  | Price (₹) | Supply (Units) |                                                                                         | Price (₹) | Supply (Units) |
|                       | :-------- | :------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :-------- | :------------- |
|                       | 10        | 100            |                                                                                         |           |                |
|                       | 12        | 150            |                                                                                         |           |                |
| Effect on supply curve | There is an upward movement (see Fig. 9.5) along the same supply curve.                                             | There is a rightward shift (see Fig. 9.8) in the supply curve.                                                    |
| Reason                | It occurs due to an increase in the price of the given commodity.                                                    | It occurs due to other factors like a decrease in the price of inputs, decrease in taxes, technological upgradation etc. |

## Contraction in Supply vs. Decrease in Supply

| Basis                 | Contraction in Supply                                                                                                 | Decrease in Supply                                                                                                |
| :-------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Meaning               | When the quantity supplied falls due to a decrease in the price, keeping other factors constant, it is known as contraction in supply. | Decrease in supply refers to a fall in the supply of a commodity caused due to any factor other than the own price of the commodity. |
| Tabular Presentation  | Price (₹) | Supply (Units) |                                                                                         | Price (₹) | Supply (Units) |
|                       | :-------- | :------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :-------- | :------------- |
|                       | 12        | 150            |                                                                                         |           |                |
|                       | 10        | 100            |                                                                                         |           |                |
| Effect on supply curve | There is a downward movement (see Fig. 9.6) along the same supply curve.                                           | There is a leftward shift (see Fig. 9.9) in the supply curve.                                                   |
| Reason                | It occurs due to a decrease in the price of the given commodity.                                                    | It occurs due to other factors like an increase in the price of inputs, increase in taxes, technological degradation etc. |
