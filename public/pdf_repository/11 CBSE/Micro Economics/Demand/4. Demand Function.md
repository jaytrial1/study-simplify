# 4 Demand Function

## 4.1 DEMAND FUNCTION

Demand function shows the relationship between quantity demanded for a particular commodity and the factors influencing it. It can be either with respect to one consumer (individual demand function) or to all the consumers in the market (market demand function).

### 4.1.1 Individual Demand Function

Individual demand function refers to the functional relationship between individual demand and the factors affecting individual demand.
It is expressed as: $\mathrm{D}_{\mathrm{x}}=\mathrm{f}\left(\mathrm{P}_{\mathrm{x}}, \mathrm{P}_{\mathrm{r}}, \mathrm{Y}, \mathrm{T}, \mathrm{F}\right)$

Where,
$\mathrm{D}_{\mathrm{x}}=$ Demand for Commodity x ;
$\mathrm{P}_{\mathrm{x}}=$ Price of the given Commodity x ;
$\mathrm{P}_{\mathrm{r}}=$ Prices of Related Goods;
$\mathrm{Y}=$ Income of the Consumer;
$\mathrm{T}=$ Tastes and Preferences;
$\mathrm{F}=$ Expectation of Change in Price in future.

Demand function is just a short-hand way of saying that quantity demanded ( $D_{x}$ ), which is on the lefthand side, is assumed to depend on the variables that are listed on the right-hand side.

### 4.1.2 Market Demand Function

Market demand function refers to the functional relationship between market demand and the factors affecting market demand.
As mentioned before, market demand is affected by all factors affecting individual demand. In addition, it is also affected by size and composition of population, season and weather and distribution of income.
So, market demand function can be expressed as: $\mathbf{D}_{\mathrm{x}}=\mathbf{f}\left(\mathbf{P}_{\mathrm{x}}, \mathbf{P}_{\mathrm{r}}, \mathbf{Y}, \mathbf{T}, \mathbf{F}, \mathbf{P}_{\mathrm{o}}, \mathbf{S}, \mathbf{D}\right)$
Where,
$\mathrm{D}_{\mathrm{x}}=$ Market demand of commodity x ;
$\mathrm{P}_{\mathrm{x}}=$ Price of given commodity x ;
$\mathrm{P}_{\mathrm{r}}=$ Prices of Related Goods;
$\mathrm{Y}=$ Income of the consumers;
$\mathrm{T}=$ Tastes and Preferences;
$\mathrm{F}=$ Expectation of Change in Price in future;
$\mathrm{P}_{\mathrm{o}}=$ Size and Composition of population;
$\mathrm{S}=$ Season and Weather;
$\mathrm{D}=$ Distribution of Income.
