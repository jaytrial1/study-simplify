Till now, we have discussed the determination of equilibrium price through free play (without government interference) of demand and supply. In such a freely functioning market, the quantity demanded and the quantity supplied are equal to each other at the equilibrium price. However, markets are rarely free from government interference. At times, the government has to intervene in the process of price determination when the equilibrium price so determined is either too high for the consumers or too low (i.e., unprofitable) for the producers of the commodity. Let us now discuss the two types of government interventions:

1.  Price Ceiling or Maximum Price Ceiling
2.  Price Floor or Minimum Support Price (MSP)
