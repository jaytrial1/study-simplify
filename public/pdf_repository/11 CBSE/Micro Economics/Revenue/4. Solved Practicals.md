# Important Formulae at a Glance

1.  $TR = \text{Price } (AR) \times \text{Quantity (units sold) Or } TR = \Sigma MR$
2.  $AR (\text{Price}) = TR \div \text{Units sold (Q)}$
3.  $MR_{n} = TR_{n} - TR_{n-1}$

## Example 1. Calculate Average Revenue (AR) and Marginal Revenue (MR):

| Units sold | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 |
| :---------- | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| $TR(\sigma)$ | 20 | 36 | 48 | 56 | 60 | 60 | 56 |

### Solution:

| Units sold <br> (Q) | $TR(\sigma)$ | $AR(\sigma)$ <br> $TR \div Q = AR$ | $MR(\sigma)$ <br> $TR_{n} - TR_{n-1} = MR_{n}$ |
| :------------------: | :----------: | :----------------------------: | :--------------------------------: |
|          1           |      20      |        $20 \div 1 = 20$       |              $20 - 0 = 20$              |
|          2           |      36      |        $36 \div 2 = 18$       |              $36 - 20 = 16$             |
|          3           |      48      |        $48 \div 3 = 16$       |              $48 - 36 = 12$              |
|          4           |      56      |        $56 \div 4 = 14$       |              $56 - 48 = 8$               |
|          5           |      60      |        $60 \div 5 = 12$       |              $60 - 56 = 4$               |
|          6           |      60      |        $60 \div 6 = 10$       |              $60 - 60 = 0$               |
|          7           |      56      |        $56 \div 7 = 8$        |              $56 - 60 = -4$              |

# Example 17. Suppose, a book seller can sell 10 books at the price of ₹ 200 per book. His marginal revenue (MR) from the $11^{\text {th }}$ book is ₹ 255. At what price did he sell the $11^{\text {th }}$ book?

## Solution:
TR of 10 books $=200 \times 10=₹ 2,000$ and MR of $11^{\text {th }}$ book $=₹ 255$
TR of 11 books $=₹ 2,000+₹ 255=₹ 2,255$
Price (AR) of $11^{\text {th }}$ book $=$ TR of 11 books $\div 11=2,255 \div 11=₹ 205$
Ans. Price of $11^{\text {th }}$ book $=₹ 205$

# Example 18. When output increases from 50 units to 70 units, TR increases from ₹ 4,000 to ₹ 5,000. Calculate MR.

## Solution:

$$
M R=\frac{\text { Change in Total Revenue }}{\text { Change in number of units }}=\frac{\Delta T R}{\Delta Q}=\frac{5,000-4,000}{70-50}=\frac{1,000}{20}=₹ 50
$$

Ans. $M R=₹ 50$
# Example 19. The total revenue of a firm increased by ₹ 5,400, when his sale increased from 35 units to 50 units. Calculate marginal revenue of the firm.

## Solution:

Marginal Revenue $=\frac{\text { Change in Total Revenue }}{\text { Change in number of units }}=\frac{5,400}{50-35}=\frac{5,400}{15}=₹ 360$
Ans. Marginal Revenue $=₹ 360$
