# Internal or External Funds:

Financing is also available from both internal and external funds. The type of funds most frequently employed is internally generated funds, coming from several sources as follows:

(a) Past savings, help from family members
(b) Retained profits
(c) Sale of assets
(d) Squeezing (reducing) working capital
(e) Chasing up debtors or delaying payments to creditors
(f) Sales of little-used assets
(g) Procuring assets on rental basis/ lease rather than buying
(h) Sale of redundant assets
(i) Reducing short-term assets viz. inventory, cash etc.

The other general source of funds is external to the venture. This is money from outside the firm's own resources. Mostly the debt financing is all referred to as an external source of raising funds.

Each source has its own merits and demerits. No one form can be termed as 'the Best'. It's always better for the entrepreneur to compare, evaluate, and conclude 'a combination' of sources which is capable of giving an entrepreneur:

(1) Minimum cost
(2) Maximum benefit
