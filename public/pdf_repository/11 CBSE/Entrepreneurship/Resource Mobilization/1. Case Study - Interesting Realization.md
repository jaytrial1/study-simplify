# Rahul Sharma: A Story of Entrepreneurial Success

Rahul Sharma, the founder-owner of a five-store hotel, and an engineer from Benaras Hindu University, has come a long way. Hailing from a family with three generations of businessmen, Rahul had to virtually start from scratch to struggle his way up in life. His forefathers were well-settled at Batala in Punjab, where they had foundries, non-ferrous rolling mills, and machine tool units. In 1957, Rahul's father decided to separate from his family and shift to Bhandara, near Nagpur, to start a metal unit in partnership with a local businessman for manufacturing non-ferrous utensils.

## Taking Over the Family Business

In 1968, Rahul took over his father's business, only to find that the income from the business was barely enough to sustain his large family of four brothers and two sisters.

## Critical Review of the Business

Rahul critically reviewed the whole business situation, the company's structure, overheads, raw materials, suppliers, etc., to evaluate where things were going wrong.

### Identifying Key Issues

It was here that he realized that, on one hand, their basic raw materials, like brass scrap, copper, and zinc, were not only costly, but the transportation cost heavily affected their working capital as this all came from the western region of Maharashtra, mainly from Mumbai and Pune. On the other hand, the major chunk of their finished products, i.e., brass utensils, were also sold in these regions. This fact came as the turning point.

## The Turning Point: Relocation to Pune

Easy availability of raw materials and closeness to the market acted as the key agents to make him shift to Pune. Since then, there was no looking back. This not only resulted in saving the transportation cost but brought down their overall cost of production significantly.

### The Reason for Success

Could you understand the reason for Rahul Sharma's success? Yes, resource availability.
