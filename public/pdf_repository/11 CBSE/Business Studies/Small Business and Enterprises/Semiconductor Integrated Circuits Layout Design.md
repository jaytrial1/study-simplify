# 7. Semiconductor Integrated Circuits Layout Design

A semiconductor layout design means a layout of transistors and other circuitry elements and includes lead wires connecting such elements and expressed in any manner in semiconductor integrated circuits. A semiconductor is an integral part of every computer chip. Its design is to perform an electronic circuitry function.
