# 8.8 ROLE OF SMALL BUSINESS IN RURAL INDIA 

1. Employment Opportunities: Cottage and rural industries play an important role in providing employment opportunities in the rural areas, especially for the traditional artisans and the weaker sections of society.
2. Prevents migration to urban areas: The development of rural and village industries prevent migration of rural population to urban areas in search of employment.
3. Improves Economic Condition: The small scale industries in rural area help to improve the economic conditions of people by providing ample employment opportunities.
4. Rural Development: These industries lead to overall development of rural areas by reducing income inequalities and promoting balanced regional development of industries. Finally, it can be concluded that promotion of small scale industries and rural industrialisation helps to overall growth of the nation.
