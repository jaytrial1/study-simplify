# 8.13 FUTURE OF THE SMALL BUSINESS SECTOR 

As the Indian economy is getting globalised, small business enterprises will have to explore their capabilities in order to survive and grow in future.

- Government should provide its assistance to the small business sector by acting as a facilitator and promoter and not as a regulator.
- Small business sector should be ready to face competition from domestic and international firms.
- Small industries will have to adapt themselves to the fast changing needs of the market by including dynamism, flexibility and innovation in their functioning.
- Small industries should take the advantage of globalisation by getting involved in export of specialised component and parts.
The small sector can make their future bright and prosperous only when they improve their competitive strength and consider globalisation as an opportunity and not as a threat. Small sector will have to create a standing for themselves. In short, the mantra of success in this modern era has to be 'Think Global, Act Local'.
