# e-Business Vs Traditional Business 

| Basis | e-Business | Traditional Business |
| :--: | :--: | :--: |
| Ease of formation | It is easy to form. | It is difficult to form. |
| Physical presence | It does not require physical presence. | It requires physical presence. |
| Locational requirements | There is no requirement for location. | Location is preferred near raw materials and market. |
| Cost of setting up | Low cost of setting due to no need for physical facilities. | It involves high cost of setting. |
| Operating cost | The operating cost is low. | High operating cost due to investment in production, marketing, etc. |
| Direct/indirect contact with suppliers and customers | It has direct contact. | It has indirect contact through intermediaries. |
| Nature of internal communication | Communication can flow in any direction. | It involves hierarchical flow of communication. |
| Response time in meeting customer's requirement | It gives instant response. | Response takes a lot of time. |
| Organisational structure | Vertical or tall structure due to chain of command. | Horizontal or flat structure due to direct command and communication. |
| Business Processes and length of the cycle | Short business process cycle. | It is long due to number of sequential processes. |
| Opportunity for inter-personal touch | Less opportunity for inter-personal touch. | More opportunity for inter-personal touch. |
| Opportunity for physical pre-sampling of the products | It is possible only in case of books, journals, etc. | It provides many opportunities for physical pre-sampling of products. |
| Ease of going global | There are more chances of going global. | There are less chances of going global. |
| Commitment patronage | Increasing | Declining |
| Refine of human capital | Technically \& professionally qualified. | Mostly semi-skilled \& unskilled manpower. |
| Finfaction risk | High risk due to lack of personal contact between the parties. | Low risk due to personal contact between the parties. |
