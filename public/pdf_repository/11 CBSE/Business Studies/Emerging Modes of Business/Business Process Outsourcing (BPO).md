# 3. BUSINESS PROCESS OUTSOURCING (BPO) 

Business Process Outsourcing (BPO) is a subset of outsourcing that involves the contracting of a specific business task, such as human resources and customer service, to a third-party service provider.
This allows companies to focus on their core business processes. For example, an insurance company might outsource their claims processing program or a bank might outsource their loan processing system. Other common examples of BPO are call centres and payroll outsourcing.

- Business processes are often information technology based and are referred to as ITES-BPO (where ITES stands for Information Technology Enabled Service).
- BPO is often divided into two categories:
(i) Back Office Outsourcing: It includes outsourcing of internal business functions such as billing or purchasing.
(ii) Front Office Outsourcing: It includes outsourcing of customer-related services such as marketing or technical support.
- BPO that is contracted outside a company's own country is sometimes called 'offshore outsourcing'. BPO that is contracted to a company's neighboring country is called 'nearshore outsourcing' and BPO that is contracted with the company's own county is called 'onshore outsourcing'.
Evolution and maturity of the Indian BPO sector has given rise to yet another wave in the global outsourcing scenario: KPO or Knowledge Process Outsourcing.
