# Loan from Financial Institutions (Medium \& Long-term Source of Finance)

The central and state governments have established various financial institutions in the country "provide finance to business organisations. These institutions are also known as 'Development Barks' as they aim at promoting industrial development of a country.
These institutions also conduct market surveys and provide technical assistance and managerial services to people who run the enterprises. This source is suitable to raise large funds for longterm requirements for expansion, reorganisation and modernisation of an enterprise.

## Features of Loan from Financial Institutions 

The main features of raising funds through financial institutions are as follows:

1. Medium and long-term finance: Financial institutions provide medium and long-term finance.
2. Assistance in Business: Financial institutions also provide financial, managerial and technical advice and consultancy to business firms.
3. Source of Goodwill: Obtaining loan from financial institutions increases the goodwill of the borrowing company and it can raise funds easily from other sources as well.
4. Easy Repayment scheme: Loan from financial institutions does not lead to high burden on the business as loan can be easily repaid in installments.
5. Finance even during depression: Funds can be raised from financial institutions even during periods of depression or recession, when other sources of finance are not available.

## Special Financial Institutions in India

1. Industrial Finance Corporation of India (IFCI): It was established in July 1948, to provide assistance for balanced regional development and encouraging new entrepreneurs to enter into the priority sectors of the economy.
2. State Financial Corporation (SFC): It was established to provide medium and short-term finance to industries (like private limited companies, partnership firms and proprietary concerns), which are outside the scope of IFCI.
3. Industrial Credit and Investment Corporation of India (ICICI): This was established in 1955 to assist creation, expansion \& modernisation of industrial enterprises, exclusively in the private sector.
4. Industrial Development Bank of India (IDBI): It was established in 1964 to coordinate activities of other financial institutions including commercial banks. It performs three types of functions: (i) Assistance to other financial institutions; (ii) Direct assistance to industrial concerns; and (iii) Promotion and coordination of financial-technical services.
5. State Industrial Development Corporations (SIDC): It was established by different states to promote industrial development in their respective states.
6. Unit Trust of India (UTI): It was established by the Indian Government to mobilise the community's savings and channelise them into productive ventures.
7. Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC): It was set up in 1956 to mobilise the community's savings in the form of insurance premium and to make it available to industrial concerns in the form of direct loans.
