# Difference between Equity Shares and Preference Shares

| Basis | Equity Shares | Preference Shares |
| :--: | :--: | :--: |
| Face Value | The face value of equity share is generally low. | The face value of preference share is generally high. |
| Rate of dividend | The dividend rate varies depending upon the profits of the company. | Preference shareholders get a fixed rate of dividend. |
| Right to dividend | Equity dividend is paid after the payment of preference dividend. | Preference Shares get a priority over equity shares for payment of dividend. |
| Voting rights | Equity shareholders enjoy voting rights in company. | They do not carry voting rights except when dividend remains unpaid for a specified period. |
| Degree of risk | Equity shareholders assume a high degree <br> of risk. | Preference shareholders bear relatively low <br> degree of risk. |
| Refund of capital | At the time of winding up, refund of equity <br> share capital is made after the refund of <br> preference share capital. | Preference shares get priority over equity <br> shares for refund of capital. |
| Redemption | They are not redeemable during the lifetime <br> of the company (unless the company decides <br> to buy back its shares). | They may be redeemed after a fixed period <br> or at the option of the company. |
| Appeal to investors | Equity shares appeal to bold and risk-taking <br> investors. | Preference shares appeal to cautious and <br> risk-fearing investors. |
