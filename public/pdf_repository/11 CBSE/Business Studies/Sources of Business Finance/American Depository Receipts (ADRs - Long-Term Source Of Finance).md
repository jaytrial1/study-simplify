# American Depository Receipts (ADRs) (Long-term Source of Finance)

The depository receipts issued by a company in the USA are known as American Depository Receipts.

- It is issued by non-US companies to the Americans in order to obtain foreign capital.
- ADR is similar to GDR except that ADRs can be issued only in America and is traded at American stock exchange.


## Procedure for Issue of ADRs

1. First of all, the shares are issued by a Non-US company (say, Indian Company) to US intermediary, known as 'Depository'.
2. Shares are registered in the name of the depository, who appoints another intermediary, known as 'Custodian', for keeping the physical possession of such shares.
3. The depository issues ADRs to American citizens, which certifies that shares of Indian company are held by the depository's custodian.
4. Dividend on such shares, notices and reports are received by the depository.
5. Dividend is received by the depository in the home currency of the share issuing company (i.e. in ₹), while depository pays the dividend in Dollars to the ADR holders.
