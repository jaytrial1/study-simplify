# Health Insurance 

Health insurance is a type of insurance coverage that pays for medical and surgical expenses incurred by the insured. These expenses could be related to hospitalisation costs, cost of medicines or doctor consultation fees. Health insurance can reimburse such expenses to the insured or pay the care provider directly.

- Reason for Origin: In mid 80's, most of the hospitals in India were government owned and treatment was free of cost. With the advent of Private Medical Care, the Health Insurance Policy provides financial shelter in the event of medical treatment incurred out of sickness or injury.
need for Health Insurance was felt and various Insurance Companies introduced Health Insurance (or Mediclaim Insurance) as a product.

- Nature of Protection: Health Insurance is taken as a safeguard against rising medical costs. It provides risk coverage against unforeseen health expenditure that may result in financial hardship.
- Premium: Depending upon the policy, premium may be payable either in a lump sum or in installments.
- Types of Health Insurance: There are mainly three types of Health Insurance covers:
(i) Individual Mediclaim: It covers the hospitalisation expenses for an individual for up to the sum assured limit.
(ii) Family Floater Policy: It covers the hospitalisation expenses for the entire family up to the sum assured limit.
(iii) Unit Linked Health Plans: This policy combines health insurance with investment and pays back an amount at the end of the insurance term.
- Claims Settlement: There are two ways by which health insurance claims are settled:
(i) Cashless: The claim amount needs to be approved by the TPA and the hospital settles the amount with the TPA. (TPA or Third Party Administrator is a middleman between Insurer and the Customer).
(ii) Reimbursement: The insured avails the treatment and settles the hospital bills directly at the hospital. The insured can claim reimbursement later on by submitting relevant bills/documents for the claimed amount to the TPA.
