# 4.1 INTRODUCTION

Service sector includes all those economic activities which are required for uninterrupted production and exchange of goods. It includes specialised services required for removing various hindrances of trade. We know, it is very important that goods are produced in time. However, at the same time, it is also important that such goods reach the consumers at the right time. Service sector helps to achieve these twin goals. Trade and industry is not possible without the various service sectors like banking, insurance, communication, transportation, warehousing, etc. In short, service sector is the backbone of every business and Industry.
