# 4.2 BUSINESS SERVICES: MEANING AND TYPES

Business services refer to those services which are used by business enterprises to conduct their activities. For example, banking, insurance, communication, transportation and warehousing services.

## Types of Business Services

Business Services can be classified into following categories:

1. Banking: Every business enterprise needs finance to carry on its operations. Banks provide funds to the business.
2. Insurance: In order to protect the businessman from damage or risk, there is a need to get the plant, machinery, goods, etc. insured.
3. Communication: Communication is needed to educate the consumer or potential user about the availability and utility of the product.
4. Transportation: Transportation is required to carry raw material and finished goods in good shape and on time.
5. Warehousing: Warehousing is needed to store the goods from the time of their production or purchase until they are sold or consumed.
