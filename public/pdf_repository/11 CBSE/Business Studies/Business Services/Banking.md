# 4.3 BANKING 

Banking plays an important role in the economic development of a nation. The Banking Regulation Act, 1949 defines the term banking as "The accepting for the purpose of lending or investment of deposits of money from the public, repayable on demand or otherwise and withdrawable by cheque, draft, order or otherwise".
It means, the essential elements of banking are:
(i) There should be acceptance of deposits.
(ii) The deposits should be received from the public.
(iii) The deposits should be repayable on demand or on the expiry of a term or after a specified period.
(iv) The purpose of deposits should be lending or investment.
In simple words, we can say that 'Bank is a financial institution which accepts deposits from the public, repayable on demand and also earns a margin of profit by lending money'.
Banks stimulate economic activity by mobilising savings of people and making funds available instruments and provide financial services.
