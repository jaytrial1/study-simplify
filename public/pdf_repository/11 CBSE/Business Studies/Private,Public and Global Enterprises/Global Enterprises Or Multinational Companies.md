# 3.4 GLOBAL ENTERPRISES OR MULTINATIONAL COMPANIES

Global Enterprise or Multinational Company (MNC) is a company whose business operations extend beyond the country in which it has been incorporated. Global enterprises have their head office in one country, but they carry on business operations in other countries, known as host countries. For example, Pepsi and Coca Cola companies are registered in USA, whereas, they operate across the world. MNCs are also known as 'Supernatural Companies' or 'Transnational Companies'.
They extend their industrial and marketing operations through a network of branches (known as 'Majority Owned Foreign Affiliates') in several countries.
In the words of W.H. Moreland, "Multinational corporations or companies are those enterprises whose management, ownership and controls are spread in more than one foreign country".
Global enterprises do not aim to maximise profits from one or two products. Rather, they operate in several areas and produce multiple products. They significantly influence the international economy because of their huge size, large number of products, advanced technology, marketing strategies and network of operations all over the world. After the Economic Reforms of 1991, MNCs have played an important role in the Indian economy.
However, some of the MNCs are slightly exploitative in nature and concentrate more on selling consumer goods and luxury items, which are not always desirable for developing countries.

## Features of Global Enterprises or Multinational Companies 

The main features of these corporations are as follows:

1. Huge Capital Resources: Global enterprises are characterised by possessing huge financial resources and the ability to raise funds from different sources.

- They may raise funds through issue of equity shares, debentures or bonds to the public or can borrow from financial institutions and international banks.
- Due to their credibility in the capital market, investors and banks of the host country also want to invest in them. Because of their financial strength, they are able to survive under all circumstances.

2. Foreign Collaboration: Global enterprises usually collaborate with Indian companies (public or private) for production of goods, use of brand names or for sale of technology.

- They have to comply with various restrictive clauses for agreements relating to transfer of technology, pricing, dividend payments, etc.
- Big industrial houses collaborate with MNCs in order to diversify and expand in terms of patents, resources, foreign exchange, etc.
However, these foreign collaborations have given rise to growth of monopolies and concentration of power in few hands

3. Advanced Technology: These enterprises possess technological superiorities and are able to conform to international standards and quality specifications. It leads to industrial progress of the country in which such MNCs operate as they are able to make best possible use of resources. Computerization and other inventions have come due to the technological advancements of MNCs.
4. Product Innovation: Global enterprises are continuously engaged in research and development activities. It leads to development of new products and superior designs of existing products. Such research is possible only by MNCs as they have huge resources.
5. Marketing Strategies: MNCs use aggressive marketing strategies in order to increase their sales in a short period. They have more reliable and up-to-date information about the market. Moreover, their advertising and sales promotion techniques are normally very effective because of their established brand image in the global market.
6. Expansion of Market Territory: As activities of MNCs extend beyond the physical boundaries of their own countries, they have to operate through a network of subsidiaries, branches and affiliates in host countries. It expands their market territory and enables them to become international brands.
7. Centralised Control: Global enterprise exercises centralised control over all its branches and subsidiaries in the host countries from its Head Office (located in the home country). All these branches have to work within the broad policy framework of the parent company. However, the head office does not interfere in their day-to-day operations.

## Merits of Global Enterprises or Multinational Companies 

1. Employment Opportunities: Multinationals create numerous employment opportunities in host countries because they carry on business on large scale. MNCs also offer good salary package and career opportunities to its staff.
2. Advanced Technology: Multinational companies act as a source of advanced technology because they have their own Research and Development (R \& D) department, where they develop new techniques.
3. Inflow of Foreign Capital: Multinationals command huge financial resources. They make foreign direct investment (FDI), which leads to economic development of host countries.
4. Improves standard of living: By providing superior products and services, MNCs help to improve standard of living.
5. Growth of Domestic firms: The multinational companies also help in the growth of the domestic firms. These firms are encouraged to act as suppliers and ancillary units of MNCs.
6. Healthy Competition: MNCs compel the local firms to improve their efficiency in order to face competition from them.
7. World Economy: Multinationals promote the concept of global economy. They help to integrate national economies into a world economy.

## Demerits of Global Enterprises or Multinational Companies

1. Disregard of National Priorities: MNCs invest their capital only in the profitable projects without any regard to national priorities of the host country.
2. Creation of Monopoly: Multinationals give rise to concentration of economic power and monopoly in the host country.
3. Depletion of natural resources: MNCs exploit the resources of the host country to maximise their global profits.
4. Obsolete technology: The technology transferred by MNCs in host countries is usually obsolete or outdated.
5. Threat to National Sovereignty: MNCs enjoy considerable political influence due to their huge operations. They have enough power to influence and interfere in the political affairs of host countries.
