# 3.3 FORMS OF PUBLIC SECTOR ENTERPRISES 

The government has a major role to play in the formation of the public sector. However, the government acts through its people. These public enterprises are owned by the public and are accountable to the public through the Parliament. For the control and management of the day-today business of public enterprises, they can be classified into three categories.

1. Departmental Undertaking
2. Statutory Corporation or Public Corporation
3. Government Company
