# Comparison between Forms of Public Sector Enterprises

| Basis | Departmental Undertaking | Statutory Corporation | Government Company |
| :--: | :--: | :--: | :--: |
| Formation | It is created by order of the government and is attached to a particular ministry. | It is created by a Special Act of the Parliament. | It is created in accordance with the Companies Act. |
| Legal Status | No separate legal entity. | Separate legal entity. | Separate legal entity. |
| Management | Managed by government officials of the concerned ministry. | Managed by Board of Directors appointed by the government. | Managed by a board of directors nominated by the government. |
| Finance | 100\% financed by government out of funds allocated in budget. | Funds through borrowings from government or through revenue from sale of goods. | Atleast 51\% of share capital is provided by the government. |
| Staff | Government Employees. | No Government employee. All employees are appointed under contract of service. | No Government employee. All employees are appointed under contract of service. |
| Ownership | Wholly owned by the government. | Wholly owned by the government. | Atleast 51\% of the company is owned by the government. |
| Flexibility | No flexibility | Considerable scope for flexibility and initiative. | Significant flexibility in the use of the new iOS own rules and regulations. |
| Degree of Autonomy | No autonomy as there is strict control of the government. | Considerable degree of autonomy as no interference by government in day-to-day affairs. | Substantial degree of autonomy as no interference by government in day-to-day affairs. |
| Public Accountability | Fully accountable to the public and concerned ministry. | Accountable to the parliament. | Accountable to the concerned of the ministry. |
| Examples | Indian Railways, Post and Telegraph Department, etc. | RBI, FCl, LIC, Air India, etc. | SAIL, BHEL, BEML, etc. |

Difference between Private Sector and Public Sector Enterprises

| Basis | Private Sector Enterprises | Public Sector Enterprises |
| :--: | :--: | :--: |
| Objective | They work with the main objective of earning profit. | They work with the main objective of providing services to public. |
| Ownership | They are owned by one or more private individuals or corporate bodies. | They are owned by the Central Government and / or one or more state governments either singly or jointly. |
| Capital | Entire or majority of capital is contributed by private investors/owners from their own resources. | Entire or at least $51 \%$ of capital is contributed by the Government. |
| Management | They are managed by owners and professional managers. | They are managed by Board of Directors who are generally representatives of government. |
| Freedom of Management | Owners are free to manage and control the enterprise with little interference. | There is less freedom due to political interference. |
| Area of Operations | They generally operate in those areas in which steady and reasonable returns are expected. | They operate mainly in the field of strategic industries, public utility services and other areas of social benefit. |
| Flexibility | They can easily modify their policies as per the needs of the situation. | They are required to follow rigid policies and procedures, which cannot be modified easily and requires the approval of the government. |
