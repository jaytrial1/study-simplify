# Comparison of Different Forms of Business Organisations

| Basis | Sole Proprietorship | Partnership | Hindu Undivided Family Business | Cooperative Society | Company |
| :--: | :--: | :--: | :--: | :--: | :--: |
| Formation | Easiest formation with minimum legal formalities. | Easy formation as registration is optional. | Easy formation as less legal formalities and no need for registration | Compulsory registration and involves many legal formalities. | Compulsory registration with lengthy and expensive process of formation. |
| Members | Only owner. | Minimum-2 <br> Maximum-50. | Minimum-2 persons for division of property Maximum-No limit. | Minimum-10 <br> Maximum-No limit. | Private Company: <br> Minimum-2, <br> Maximum-200 <br> Public Company: <br> Minimum-7, <br> Maximum-No limit. |
| Capital <br> Contribution | Limited finance. | Limited, but more than sole proprietorship. | Limited to the amount of ancestral property. | Limited contribution due to low return on capital. | Large financial resources. |
| Liability | Unlimited | Unlimited liability Each partner is individually and jointly liable. | karta has unlimited liability, while others have limited liability. | Limited liability. | Limited liability. |
| Control and management | All business decisions are taken by owner, which leads to quick decision making. | All business decisions are taken with the consent of all partners. | All business decisions are taken by karta. | Elected managing committee takes decisions. | Decisions are taken by Board of Directors, appointed by shareholders as there is separation between ownership and management. |
| Continuity | Unstable as directly affected by status of owner. | More stable, but affected by status of partners. | Stable business and continues | Stable because of separate legal status. | Stable because of separate legal status. |
