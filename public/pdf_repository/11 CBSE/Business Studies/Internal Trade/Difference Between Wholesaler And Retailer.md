# Difference between Wholesaler and Retailer

| Basis | Wholesaler | Retailer |
| :--: | :--: | :--: |
| Nature of business | Wholesaler is a link between retailer and manufacturer. | Retailer is a link between consumers and wholesalers. |
| Scale of operation | Wholesaler operates on large scale as he/she buys and sells goods in large quantities. | Retailer operates on small scale as he/she buys and sells goods in small quantities. |
| Number of items | Wholesaler deals in few items and aims to specialise in one line. | Retailer handles a large number of items. |
| Capital | Wholesaler requires large amount of capital for his/her business. | Retailer can manage with small capital. |
| Nature of payment | Most of the dealings are done on credit. | Most of the dealings are done on cash. |
| Window display | Wholesaler does not need it as he/she is not required to attract his/her clients. | It is very much needed in order to attract his/her customers. |
| After-sale service | There is no arrangement of any sort of after-sale or repair service. | Retailer offers facilities of after-sale. |
| Profit margin | Wholesaler sells at a very low profit margin due to fast turnover. | Retailer sells at a higher profit margin because of expenses on the window dressing. |
| Scope of business | Business is spread over several places. | Local area is generally covered in the retail trade. |


## Classification of Retailers

Retailers can be classified on the following basis:

1. Size of Business: On the basis of size, retailers may be categorised into large, medium and small retailers. Some customers prefer to visit large retail houses located at central places, while some prefer small shops located in their local area.
2. Ownership: On the basis of type of ownership, retailers may be categorised into sole proprietorship, partnership, cooperative store and company. The large departmental stores generally operate as a company form of business, while small shops operate as sole proprietorship.
3. Merchandise or Product Mix: On the basis of merchandise handled (types and variety of products kept), the retailers may be classified as Speciality Store (dealing in specific product under one product line), Departmental Store (dealing in wide range of different lines of products), etc.

4. Fixed Place of Business: Retailers may also be classified on the basis whether or not they have a fixed place of business. On this basis, there are two categories of retailers: (i) Itinerant Retailers (who do not have a fixed place of business to operate); and (ii) Fixed Shop Retailers (who have a fixed place of business to operate).
