# Fixed Shop Retailers 

This is the most common type of retailing. Under this, the retail traders operate their business from permanently established shops. They do not move from place to place to serve their customers.

## Characteristics of Fixed Shop Retailers

The main characteristics of such retailers are:

1. They have greater resources and operate at a relatively large scale as compared to itinerant traders. However, fixed shop retailers may vary from very small size to very large size.
2. They deal in different products including consumer durables as well as non-durables.
3. They provide various services to customers, like home delivery, guarantees, repairs, credit facilities, etc. As a result, such retailers enjoy greater credibility in the minds of customers.

## Types of Fixed Shop Retailers

On the basis of size of operation, the fixed shop retailers can be classified into two types:

1. Fixed Shop Small Retailers
2. Fixed Shop Large Retailers
