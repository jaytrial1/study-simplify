# FIXED SHOP LARGE RETAILERS

These retailers operate on large-scale as they maintain large stock of goods and purchase goods in bulk. Such retailers have gained importance in big cities and towns. Some of the fixed shop large retailers are:

1. Departmental Stores
2. Chain Stores or Multiple Shops
3. Mail Order Houses

## Departmental Stores

A departmental store is a large establishment, which sells a wide variety of products and aim to satisfy all needs of the customers under one roof. The store is divided into a number of departments and each department deals in a particular variety of goods. For example, there may be separate departments for toiletries, fruits and vegetables, groceries, electronics and clothing. They satisfy diverse market segments with a wide variety of goods and services. Everything from 'Pin to Plane' is the spirit behind a typical department store. Such stores are common in developed countries like USA. But, in India, real departmental stores have not yet come in a big way. Still, Big Bazaar, More Megastore or Reliance Fresh Store play an important role in satisfying diverse needs of people.

### Features of Departmental Stores 

The essential features of a departmental store are as follows:

1. Central location: It is generally located at a central place in the city so that people living in different areas may reach there easily.
2. Provision of services: A modern departmental store renders a number of facilities and services to their consumers such as restaurant, travel and information bureau, telephone booth, rest rooms, etc.
3. Corporate Status: The departmental stores are generally formed as a joint stock company because of their large size. They have a Board of Directors with a managing director, assisted by a general manager and several department managers.
4. Elimination of Middlemen: A departmental store purchases goods directly from the manufacturers, which sells them to the ultimate consumers. It eliminates the middlemen and increases their profit margin.
5. Centralised Purchasing: All the purchases in a department store are made centrally by the purchase department. However, the sales are decentralised in different departments.
6. Large Variety of Goods: These stores maintain a large variety of goods and customers can purchase almost all their requirements from these stores.

### Advantages of Departmental Stores

The departmental stores offer the following advantages:

1. Attract large number of customers: These stores are generally located in the centre of the city. As a result, they are able to attract a large number of customers.
2. Convenience in Buying: A departmental store offers wide range of goods. Customers do not have to run from one shop to another because they can do all shopping under one roof.
3. Attractive Services: Most of the departmental stores provide numerous services to their customers, like liberal credit, free home delivery, execution of telephone orders, etc. All such services make shopping a pleasant experience.
4. Economy of large-scale operations: Departmental stores are in position to take the advantages of large scale operations, particularly in respect of purchase of goods. They buy large quantities of goods directly from the producers and sell them to the final consumers. It eliminates the need for middlemen and increases their profit margin.
5. Promotion of Sales: The departmental stores can afford heavy advertising because of their sound financial position, which help in increasing their sales volume.
6. Professional Management: As a departmental store is organised as a joint stock company with sound financial position, it can employ specialists with expert knowledge of buying, advertising, selling and pricing.

### Limitations of Departmental Stores 

Departmental stores also suffer from the following limitations:

1. Lack of Personal Attention: There is lack of personal touch in a departmental store as it is very difficult to give personal attention to large number of customers visiting the store every day.
2. High Operating Cost: The operating cost of running the departmental stores is very high due to extensive services and facilities offered by them to the customers. As a result, they are not attractive to lower income group of people due to high prices as compared to prices in small shops.
3. High Possibility of Loss: The chances of incurring losses in a departmental store are high because of heavy operating costs and large-scale operations. For example, if any item has become out-of-fashion, then the store has to sell the huge stock at heavy discount.
4. Inconvenient Location: The departmental stores are generally located in the centre of the city. As a result, it is inconvenient for the customers, who prefer to buy goods from shops nearer to them.

Conclusion
The departmental stores are very popular in the western countries due to their benefits to high income group people. In India, these stores are popular in metropolitan and big cities.
