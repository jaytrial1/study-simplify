# 9.8 VENDING MACHINES

A vending machine is a new form of retailing, in which goods are sold with the help of slot machine operated by coins or tokens. This method of retail selling is very popular in western countries. Vending machines are used for selling several products, like hot beverages (tea or coffee), platform tickets, milk, soft drinks, etc. Customers are required to insert necessary coins or token in a slot and press a button whereby the article is released automatically. The coins or tokens are periodically collected from the machine and articles are put in as needed.
Vending machines are widely used as Automated Teller Machines (ATMs) in the banking services. With the help of these machines, money can be withdrawn at any time without visiting any branch of a bank.

## Features of Vending Machines

1. Self-service system: Vending machines do not require salesmen or employees. It reduces the operating costs.
2. Need of coins and tokens: Vending machines require coins or tokens to operate the machine.
3. $24 \times 7$ shopping: Vending machines provide the facility of anytime shopping.
4. Suitable Products: Vending machines are useful to sell low priced products, which have been specially packed for the machines. Products should be uniform in size and weight. The goods must also have high turnover in order to avoid high operating cost.

## Advantages of Vending Machines

1. Anytime shopping: Vending machines provide the facility of $24 \times 7$ shopping at all major locations.
2. Self-service: There is no need of a salesman and customers develop the habit of self-service.
3. Fast Service: These machines provide quick service to the customers.
4. More satisfaction to customers: Customers are more satisfied about the quality and quantity of the product.

## Limitations of Vending Machines 

1. Costly: The initial cost of installing a vending machine and expenditure on its maintenance and repair is quite high.
2. No Prior Inspection: The consumers cannot feel or see the product before buying and bought goods cannot be returned.
3. Customised products: Products have to be developed according to the requirements of the machine.
With the growth in the economy, vending machines have a promising future in retail sales of high turnover and low priced consumer products.
