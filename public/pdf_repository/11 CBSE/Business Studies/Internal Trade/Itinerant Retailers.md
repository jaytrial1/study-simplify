# Itinerant Retailers

Itinerant retailers are those traders who keep on moving from place to place to sell their goods. They do not have a fixed place of business to operate from.

## Characteristics of Itinerant Retailers

The main characteristics of itinerant retailers are:
(i) They carry limited stock as they do not have fixed premises.
(ii) They operate with a very small amount of capital.
(iii) They normally deal in consumer products of daily use such as toiletry products, fruits, vegetables, etc.
(iv) They mostly sell goods on cash basis.
(v) They provide greater service to the customers by making the products available to their doorstep.
(vi) They generally sell goods at prices lower than charged by fixed shop retail traders because of minimum establishment expenses.

## Types of Itinerant Retailers 

The most common types of itinerant retailers operating in India are as follows:

1. Hawkers and Pedlars: These retailers move from place-to-place to sell their merchandise at the doorstep of the customers. The hawkers carry their goods in a wheeled vehicle while the pedlars carry the goods on their heads or backs.
Main Features of Hawkers and Pedlars
(i) They sell a variety of goods like vegetables, fruits, fabrics, etc.
(ii) They deal in local and non-standardised products.
(iii) They generally do not have a fixed price policy. Their price varies depending on the bargaining power of the customers.
(iv) They provide great convenience to the consumers as they supply goods at the customer's door step.
(v) They are also found in buses, trains, railway
It must be noted that products sold by hawkers and pedlars may not be reliable in terms of quality and price.
2. Market Traders: Market traders are the small retailers who open their shops at different places on fixed days. These retailers sell their goods at periodical markets such as weekly, monthly or annually. For example, weekly markets are held on a fixed day and are popularly known by the name of day (like 'Som Bazaar' on Monday).

## Main Features of Market Traders

(i) They deal in low-priced consumer items of daily use, such as fabrics, toys, crockery, vegetables, etc.
(ii) They mainly cater to the needs of lower-income customers.
(iii) They set up their shop with temporary structure.
(iv) They also sell their goods on gatherings on special occasions such as annual fairs, festivals, etc.
3. Street Traders or Pavement Vendors: These retailers arrange or display their goods at busy street corners, pavements and other busy public places like bus stands, railway stations, cinema halls, etc. For example, selling newspapers or magazines on stands.

## Main Features of Street Traders or Pavement Vendors

(i) They sell consumer items of common use, such as stationery items, eatables, readymade garments, newspapers and magazines.
(ii) They generally deal in low price products.

(iii) They are different from market traders as they do not change their place of business so frequently.
4. Cheap Jacks: Cheap Jack is a kind of retailer who generally hires a small shop in a residential colony only for a temporary period.
Main Features of Cheap Jacks
(i) They hire small shops where they display their goods.
(ii) They shift from locality to locality depending upon the prospects of getting business. However, they do not change the place as frequently as in case of hawkers or market traders.
(iii) They generally deal in household articles, readymade garments and other low-priced goods.
