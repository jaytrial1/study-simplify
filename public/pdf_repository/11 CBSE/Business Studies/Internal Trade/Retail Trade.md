# 9.4 RETAIL TRADE 

Retail trade refers to buying of goods and services in relatively small quantities from wholesalers and selling them to the ultimate consumers. The traders who deal in retail trade are known as retailers.

## Features of Retail Trade

(i) This trade provides link between the wholesaler and the ultimate consumer. Retailer is the last link in the distribution chain.
(ii) Retailer buys and sells in small quantities.
(iii) Retailer deals in large variety of goods.

(iv) Retailer sells goods to final consumers for their personal consumption and not for further sale.
(v) The area of operation of retailer is generally limited to a locality.
(vi) Retailer generally maintains direct and personal contact with the customers.
(vii) It is not necessary that retail trade is carried on in a shop or store. Retailing may also be done through telephone, internet, vending machines, etc.
It must be noted that retail trade is not concerned with 'how' or 'where' the goods are sold. If the goods are sold directly to the consumers, it will be considered retail trade.

## Services of Retailers 

Retailers serve as an important link between the producers and the final consumers. They provide useful services to the manufacturers, wholesalers and consumers.

## Services to Manufacturers and Wholesalers

The retailers render the following services to the wholesalers and producers:

1. Help in Distribution of Goods: Retailers provide access to the market to manufacturers and wholesalers through distribution of goods to the final consumers, who may be scattered over a large geographic area.
2. Personal Selling: The sale of consumer goods generally requires personal selling efforts. Retailers relieve the manufacturers and wholesalers from such efforts by making individual sales to consumers in small quantities.
3. Enabling Large-scale Operations: Retailers relieve the wholesalers and manufacturers from retail botherations of making individual sales to consumers in small quantities. It enables them to operate at large-scale and concentrate on other essential activities.
4. Collecting Market Information: As retailers are in direct contact with their customers, they provide valuable and reliable market information to wholesalers and manufacturers about the tastes, preferences and attitudes of customers.
5. Help in Promotion: The manufacturers and wholesalers undertake various promotional activities in order to increase the sale of their products, like advertising the products or offering incentives such as coupons, free gifts, etc. Retailers participate in these activities in various ways and help in promoting the sale of the products.

## Services to Consumers

The services rendered by retailers to the consumers are as follows:

1. Regular Availability of Products: Retailer holds stock of various goods produced by different manufacturers and is prepared to sell the goods in small quantities to the customers. It enables the buyers to choose the products as per their tastes and to buy them as and when needed.

2. New Products Information: Retailers make effective display of new products and provide significant information (like features, price, etc.) about such products to the customers. It helps the customers in their decision-making process for the purchase of those goods.
3. Convenience in Buying: Retailers offer great convenience to the customers in buying small quantities of products according to their requirements. Retail shops are usually situated near to the residential areas and remain open for long hours.
4. Wide Selection: A retailer keeps stock of large variety of goods of different manufacturers for the satisfaction of the customers. It enables the consumers to make choice out of wide selection of goods.
5. After-sales Services: Retailers provide important after-sales services in the form of home delivery or repair of goods in case of durable items. Effective services bring repeat purchase of the products.
6. Provide Credit Facilities: Sometimes, retailers also sell goods to their regular and reputed customers on credit basis. It enables the customers to increase their consumption level and standard of living.
