# 9.2 INTERNAL TRADE

Internal trade is the buying and selling of goods and services within the boundaries of a nation.
(i) Internal trade is also known as 'Home Trade' or 'Inland Trade' or 'Domestic Trade'.

(ii) The payments in internal trade are made in national home currency directly or through the national banking system.
(iii) Internal trade is free from custom duty of import duty as goods are part of domestic production and are meant for domestic consumption.
(iv) Internal trade aims at equitable distribution of goods within a nation speedily and at reasonable cost.

## Types of Internal Trade 

On the basis of scale of operations, the internal trade of a country may be divided into two main types:

1. Wholesale Trade
2. Retail Trade

Both Wholesale Trade and Retail Trade are essential to facilitate exchange of goods and services between producers and users or ultimate consumers.
