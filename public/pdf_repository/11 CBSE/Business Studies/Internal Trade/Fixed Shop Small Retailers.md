# FIXED SHOP SMALL RETAILERS

## General Stores

These shops deal in different types of goods which are needed by the local residents quite frequently in their day-to-day life. They are located in residential colonies.

### Main Features of General Stores

(i) They stock a variety of goods of daily use such as grocery items, soft drinks, toiletry products, stationery and confectionery.
(ii) General stores are mostly found in residential colonies.
(iii) These stores remain open for long hours at convenient timings.
(iv) They provide various services like free home delivery, credit facility to their regular customers.
(v) They aim to create personal relations with their customers in order to win their confidence.

## Single Line Stores 

Single line stores are those shops which deal in a single product line such as readymade garments, shoes, etc.

### Main Features of Single Line Stores

(i) These stores are generally organised in the form of sole traders or partnership firms.
(ii) They usually hold the stock of goods of different qualities, size, design etc. in the same product line. For example, a garment shop maintains stock of various sizes, styles and designs of garments for children, ladies and gents.
(iii) They are generally centrally located.
(iv) They serve needs of particular type of customers and the price range varies accordingly.

## Speciality Shops

These shops specialise in sale of a specific product under one product line. For example, garment store dealing in men's wear only.

### Main Features of Speciality Shops

(i) These retail stores specialise in the sale of specific line of products. For example. Men's Wear Shop, Stationery Shop, Medicines Store, School Uniforms Shop, etc.
(ii) They keep variety in that particular product. For example, book shop will keep books of different publishers.
(iii) Speciality shops are generally located at central places in order to attract large number of customers.
(iv) They provide a wide choice to the customers in the selection of particular type of goods.

## Street Stall Holders

These stalls are generally located at the crossings or in the busy streets. They offer convenience to the customers by providing items of their needs. An individual entrepreneur runs such shops under his own close supervision.

### Main Features of Street Stall Holders 

(i) These stalls have temporary platforms, which cover limited space.
(ii) They operate with a very small amount of capital.
(iii) They deal in inexpensive goods such as hosiery products, toys, cigarettes, soft drinks, etc.
(iv) They get their supplies from local suppliers as well as wholesalers.

## Second-hand Goods Shop

These shops deal in second-hand or used goods, such as clothes, books, furniture, etc. Such shops receive supplies from individuals who sell the goods not required by them or from private and public auctions.

### Main Features of Second-hand Goods Shop

(i) These shops may be located at street crossings or in busy streets.
(ii) These shops may either be in the form of a stall with temporary platform (in case of books) or may have reasonably good infrastructure (in case of old furniture or vehicles).
(iii) The goods are generally sold at lower prices as they are used goods.
(iv) Customers of second-hand goods are mostly poor people, who cannot afford to buy new goods.
(v) Such shops may also attract people from high income group if the shop has rare and antique items, which are sold at heavy prices.
