Brief History and Overview of Mobile Banking

-   The use of mobile phones was increasing, and banks decided to take advantage of this to communicate with customers. Indeed, banks began offering the first m-banking platforms to their customers in 1999, when the wireless application protocol (WAP) was introduced.
-   The first WAP banking appeared in Norway in 1999.
-   The Bank of Scotland is unquestionably a global mobile banking pioneer. In 2007 it announced the world's first mobile banking app for smartphones.
-   Before 2010, m-banking services were offered via SMS and WAP. While WAP banking has become a thing of the past, SMS notifications have stood the test of time and are still very popular.
-   The days customers used to go to banks to check their bank account balance or pay a bill are gone forever. Time is money - they no longer want to waste time standing in queues. But nowadays, customers do most of their banking remotely in the comfort of their place. Today almost every bank has its mobile app available on various operating systems.
-   The advancement of mobile banking can be attributed to advancement in internet services.
-   The launch of smartphones with WAP services enabled banking to be accessible via mobile web.
-   Today, most services are offered over dedicated mobile based apps of most banks.

# Market Context and Growth

-   We see that there are some variations in these estimates, but on the whole the base is steadily expanding.

-   The Internet user base in India is expected to reach 627 million in 2019.
-   Mobile use in India is steadily on the rise and India stands fourth in Mobile Banking penetration. In India, mobile phone users were 524.9 million in 2013, that increased to an estimated 730.7 million by end 2017. [statista.com]
