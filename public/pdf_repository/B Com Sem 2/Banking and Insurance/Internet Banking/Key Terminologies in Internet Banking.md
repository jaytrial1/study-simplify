Key Terminologies in Internet Banking

1.  **Encryption:** The process of encoding information to safeguard it from unauthorized access or hackers. The process of encoding information to make secure and unreadable without the appropriate decryption key.
2.  **Authentication:** Verifying the identity of users to ensure secure access to online banking services. Verifying the identity of a user, typically through passwords, biometrics, or other secure means.
3.  **Two-Factor Authentication (2FA):** A security measure that requires two forms of identification before granting access to an account or service.
4.  **Phishing:** Fraudulent attempts to acquire sensitive information, such as passwords or credit card details, by masquerading as a trustworthy entity in electronic communication.
5.  **Backend Operations:** The internal processes and systems that support the functions of a service or platform.
6.  **API (Application Programming Interface):** A set of protocols tools, and definitions that allow different software applications to communicate and interact with each other.
