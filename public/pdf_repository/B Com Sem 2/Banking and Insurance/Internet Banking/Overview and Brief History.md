Overview and Brief History

- In today's modern world it is difficult to imagine that at one time, branch banking was the only form of banking available and for every single need, customers were required to visit the bank branch.
- It may also surprise many that online banking has been around since early 1980s, though in a different form and the term online banking gained popularity only in the late 1980s.
- The inception of Internet banking traces back to the late 20th century. The early stages of online banking began with the development of telecommunication networks and the internet.
- Innovations in online banking started in early 1980s. It was in 1983 that the Bank of Scotland offered Nottingham Building Society (NBS) customers the very first internet banking service in the United Kingdom and named it "Homelink".
- Originally the work 'online banking' referred to the use of keyboard and television or computer monitor to access a bank account, through the use of telephone lines.
- In 1981, when four of major US banks viz., Citibank, Chase Manhattan and others came up with this idea, there were not enough takers, and hence it failed to gain momentum.
- The turning point came in the mid-1990s when banks began offering limited online services, primarily allowing customers to view account balances and transaction history through rudimentary websites. The next wave of online banking came in mid-1990s. It was in 1994 that Stanford Federal Credit Union became the first institution in the US to offer online banking to its customers.
- Initially customers were reluctant to use online banking because of lack of security features and lack of awareness as to how to make use of this channel of banking.

## 3. India's Milestone

India embraced Internet banking in the early 2000s, marking a significant milestone in the country's banking sector. The ICICI bank in private sector was among the pioneers, introducing its online banking services in 1998. ICICI Bank was the first bank in India to introduce Internet banking, followed by HDFC Bank and Citibank in the year 1999.
