Product Features of Internet Banking

# Detailed Features

1.  **Account Management**
    *   **Balance Inquiry:** Check your account balance anytime, anywhere.
    *   **Transaction History:** View detailed records of your transactions including deposits, withdrawals, and transfers.
    *   **Statement Download:** Download account statements for record keeping or financial analysis.

2.  **Fund Transfers**
    *   **Internal Transfers:** Easily transfer funds between your own accounts within the same bank.
    *   **External Transfers:** Transfer money to accounts in other banks securely and conveniently.
    *   **Scheduled Payments:** Set up recurring payments or schedule future transfers.

3.  **Bill Payments**
    *   **Utility Bill Payments:** Pay electricity, water, gas, and other utility bills online.
    *   **Credit Card Payments:** Settle credit card bills seamlessly through the Internet Banking platform.
    *   **Loan Payments:** Make payments towards loans or mortgages hassle-free.

4.  **Online Applications and Services**
    *   **New Account Opening:** Apply for new accounts, loans, or credit cards online.
    *   **Investment Services:** Access investment opportunities and manage portfolios.
    *   **Insurance Services:** Purchase or renew insurance policies through the online platform.

5.  **Alerts and Notifications**
    *   **Transaction Alerts:** Receive instant notifications for transactions or account activities.
    *   **Payment Reminders:** Get reminders for upcoming bill payments or due dates.
    *   **Security Alerts:** Stay informed about any suspicious activities or login attempts on your account.
