Integration with E-commerce

# Corporate Integration

For businesses, Internet banking plays a crucial role in facilitating financial operations. Corporations can seamlessly integrate their banking systems with e-commerce merchant sites to streamline payment processes.
(Also referred to as: Corporate Internet Banking Integration with e-Commerce Merchant sites.)

# Individual Integration

Individual users can also benefit from Internet banking when making online purchases. By linking their bank accounts or cards to e-commerce merchant sites, individuals can securely pay for goods and services without disclosing sensitive financial information.
(Also referred to as: Individual Internet Banking Integration with e-Commerce Merchant sites.)

# Real-Time Examples

Consider a scenario where a customer wants to buy a laptop from an online store. Using their Internet banking credentials, they select the product, proceed to the payment page, and choose the option to pay via Internet banking.
