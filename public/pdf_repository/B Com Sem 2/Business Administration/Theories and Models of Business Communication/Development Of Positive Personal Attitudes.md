# DEVELOPMENT OF POSITIVE PERSONAL ATTITUDES

(1) Meaning of Attitude :
Attitude can be defined as a mental process of evaluating particular person, situcatio, action, idea or thing. Attitude is a tendency to act towards or against some environment factors which become there by a positive or nagative value.
(2) Meaning of Positive Attitude :
Positive attitude means a person believes every thing happens for the best in the end.

It i an optimistic approach for a person to achieve good results.

- How to develop positive personal attitudes?

Person's thoughts and emotions play a large role in deteming the state of mental attitude of person.

One should try to develop the positive attitude with the help of following points :
(1) Follow the favourable thinking and behavior :

For developing the positive attitude, person should follow the favourable thinking and behavior.

(2) Look for opportunities in bad situations :

Bad situations are inevitable but by looking forward to good opportunities and proactively trying to improve a situation, creating a positive, personal attitude. It helps constructive and practical solutions of problems.

(3) Set reachable goals :

For developing positive personal attitude, it is necessary to set reachable (achievable) goals. Some people are good oriented and some to easily make deisions that lead to achieving their goals. It develops positive mental attitude.
(4) Adopt beliefs that frame events in a positive way :

Your beliefs and rules about life and work determine how you intepret events and therefore your attitude decide to adopt "Strong" beliefs that create a good attitude rather than beliefs that create a bad attitude to use saies as an example :

Situation : The first sales call of the day good poorly.
Strong beliefs : Every sales call is different, so the next will probably be better.
(5) Control your response :

Think before you speak and appreciate the comment (even if it is negative). Don't get defensive.
(6) Creating a positive environment :

Henry Ford once said, "If you think you can, you can. If you think you can't, you can't. Either way, you are right."

The conditions and events under which an organisation works are termed as business environment. This environment affects the working of an organisation. It develops positive attitude among employees or group members.
(7) Accept Every Situation :

Only give time on necessary situations. Stop wasting time on unnecessary situations, because they will give you discouragement alone.
