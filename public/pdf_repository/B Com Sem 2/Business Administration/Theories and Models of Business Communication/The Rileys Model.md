# The Rileys Model: 

John W. Riley and Matilda White Riley, a husband and wife team of sociologists pointed out the importance of the sociological view in communication. They developed a model to illustrate sociological implications in communication. The model is shown below:

The mode Indicates the cominunicator (C) emerges as part of a larger pattern, who sends messager in accordance with the expectations and actions of other persons and groups within the same social structure. it is also true in the case of the receiver (R) in the communications process. in addition, both the communicator and receiver are part of an overall social

system. The model clearly illustrates that communication is a two-way process.
