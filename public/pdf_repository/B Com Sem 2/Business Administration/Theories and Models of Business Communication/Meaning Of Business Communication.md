# MEANING OF BUSINESS COMMUNICATION
(1) Business Communication is the process of sharing information between people within and outside a company.
(2) In other terms, Communication between business parties or people for business related tasks is considered as Business Communication.
(3) According to Ricks and Gow, "Business Communication as a system that is responsible to affect change through out the whole organisation."
(4) According to W.H., "Business Communication is exchanging business related difierent views, ideas, and news within the related parties."

A model is a visual and symbolic representation of the concept or the theory. A business communication model presents before us a visual picture of the relationship of various elements of the process of business communication.

A business communication model helps manager to conceptualize the relationship of the various elements involved in the process of communication.
