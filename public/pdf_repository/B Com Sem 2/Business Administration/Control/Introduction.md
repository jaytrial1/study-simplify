# INTRODUCTION
Planning ranks first in the sequence of managerial functions, while controlling is the last among them. Yet, controlling is as important as planning. Controlling ensures that actual performance is according to the predetermined course of action. Thus, it facilitates the realisation of objectives. The importance of control as one of the functions of management has been accepted by all experts in the subject of management.

Control is an important function of management in as much as, it is through control that the management can check whether the actual performance is in accordance with the predetermined plan, instructions issued and established principles. Thus, control is indispensable for the effective implementation of plans prepared in advance. It assists management in delegating responsibility. It is through control that the management assures itself that the subordinates carry out their responsibility as planned and the whole administration goes on smoothly. It helps management in locating the causes of deviations, if actual performance is not found to be in accordance with activities planned. The analysis of variances will be useful in taking corrective action. Decentralisation is made possible in a business enterprise because of control. It is the control function which makes co-ordination possible. It leads to effectively carrying out the other managerial functions like planning, organising and motivating. It is of considerable help in formulating long term plans. Thus, control is an important tool in the hands of management to measure and improve the efficiency of management.
