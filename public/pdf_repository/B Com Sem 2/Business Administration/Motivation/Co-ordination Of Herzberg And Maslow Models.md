# CO-ORDINATION OF HERZBERG AND MASLOW MODELS
These models are similar in some respects. Maslow's first three needs are low level needs, and they are essential to maintain the level of satisfaction of the employees. These needs are equivalent to Herzberg's maintenance factors. Herzberg has opined that the U.S.A. has reached a stage of socio-economic progress such that Maslow's two higher level needs are now primarily the motivating ones. The three lower level needs are now minimally achieved so that they are no longer strong driving force to an employee. They are necessary only for his .naintenance at his current level of progress.
