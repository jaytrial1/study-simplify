# TOOLS OF MOTIVATION

The tools or methods used by management to motivate employees and to raise their morale are called tools of motivation. The popular motivational tools can be grouped into two categories as follows :
(A) Positive Tools of Motivation
(B) Negative Tools of Motivation
(A) Positive Tools of Motivation :* Positive tools are those which encourage people to do good work and when they get some of them, they are satified. e.g. Increase in pay, promotion, appreciation for good work etc. We describe them in detail as follows :
(1) Recognition and Appreciation : It is common knowledge that most people want that their good work should be recognised and appreciated by others. A few words of appreciation from management for good work can work wonders. He is motivated to perform better and better work. It is harmful to the management to scold the employee for his fault and not to say a few words of appreciation for his good work. Even though an employee may say that he is not interested in his praise, there is no doubt that he tries to perform better and better.
(2) Interest in personal problems of Employees : When we take interest in personal matters of others, they would definitely listen to us. Hence, it is not only desirable for manager to take personal interest in employee's problem, but it is absolutely essential. e.g. when the son of an employee passes his Board or University examination with excellent marks, he must be congratulated. Even manager can take interest in personal hobbies of employees. If a friendly atmosphere is created, a sense of belonging to the organisation is developed in employees. But the interest in the personal matters of employees must be genuine and not merely a show. On the other hand, the impression should not be created that the manager is a simpleton and relationship with him can be misused. When an employee is slack in his perfomance, he must be taken to task or asked to be sincere.
(3) Competition : Many employees are eager to show their worth by participating in a competition. Many of them want to improve upon his record as compared to those of his co workers and even as compared to his own performance. If a skilled supervisor arranges for some sort of competition among his subordinates, he may be able to motivate them. An American industrialist once visited his factory, wrote the performance of his worker on the floor with a chalk-stick. The workers of the other shift tried to improve upon it. Again, he wrote those figures on the floor and even the workers of the third shift tried to do better, due to sense of competition. Thus, without spending a single pie, competition could be arranged.
(4) Sense of Pride : If a sense of pride for the work done by an employee is created in him, it can prove to be a powerful tool of motivation. Even in the above points, the sense of pride is involved. When a worker's good work is recognised and appreciated, his sense of pride is satisfied. Hence, an atmosphere must be created in which he feels sense of pride for the work that he does. His work can be separated from those of others and he must be complimeted for his good work. If the work of any one worker may not thus be separated, then the work of the whole group of workers must be appreciated.
(5) Delegating Responsibility : It is seen that most of the employees prefer responsible and challenging jobs. Hence, if they are given some responsibility, they are likely to work with greater enthusiasm and sincerity. Of course, it must be seen that the subordinates have the ability to shoulder the responsibility. There are some persons who are afraid of accepting any responsibility. They do not have self-confidence. There, such delegation may not give good results. Besides, the persons who are given responsibility should have freedom to do his work, but there must be control and supervision.
(6) Financial Incentives : There are no two opinions about the fact that money is a powtful incentive. Most of the employees are interested in getting more and more money. Hence, the management generally tries incentive wage plans, bonus, profit sharing plans etc. as motivators. But it has been proved by experience and from number of studies that money is not the only incentive. Sometimes, employees are motivated more by status, prestige, just behaviour of management, sense of pride etc. rather than money.
(B) Negative Tools of Motivation :* In some unusual situations. a manager is compelled to use negative incentives. When all the positive incentives fail, the management is left with no other alternative but to use negative incentive.

Just as there are some seasoned offenders in a society, similarly there are some employees in an organisation who are always indisciplined and there is no effect of positive incentives on them. For them, use of negative incentives is absolutely necessary.

In negative incentive, we can include fear of losing the job, demotion, wage-cut, leave-cut, issuing memos, imposing penalties or ultimately dismissal. Due to fear of such negative steps, even the seasoned offender is compelled to improve his perfomance. An atmosphere of fear reigns in the organisation. Hence, the manager must very carefully and cautiously use such motivator. Once a decision is taken to use negative motivator against an employee, they must not be diluted or cancelled.

In the end, it can be said that the manager must make combined use of both positive and negative incentives. Which tools to employ depends upon the environment of the organisation, the skill of manager and the over-all management policy in this regard.
