# CHARACTERISTICS OF MOTIVATION
From the above definitions, the following characteristics of motivation become clear :
(1) Motivation is the internal feeling.
(2) Source of motivation is need.
(3) Motivation changes according to the changes in man's needs.
(4) As needs are many, so also are the motives.
(5) Motives change according to time, circumstances, age and person.
(6) Motivation plays a significant role in human behaviour.
(7) Motivation differ from person to person.
(8) Employees morale goes high due to motivation.
(9) Employees become frustrated when their needs are not satisfied.
