# MEANING \& DEFINITION OF MOTIVATION
When a worker is willing to put in more work than what he normally does, he is said to be motivated.
(1) According to Jucius, 'Motivation is the act of stimulating some one or oneself to get a desired course of action.'
(2) E.F.L. Breach has opined that "Motivation is getting the members of the teams to pull their weight effectively, to give their loyalty to the group to carry out properly the activities allocated and generally to play an effective part in the purpose or task that the organisation has undertaken."
(3) According to Dr. Terry, "Motivation is the drive within an individual that drives him towards goal-oriented action."
(4) In the words of Stanley Vance, "Motivation implies any emotion or desire which so conditions one's will that the individual is propelled into action."
(5) According to Dalton E. McFarland, "The concept of motivation is mainly psychological. It relates to those forces operating within the individual employee or subordinate which impel him to act or not to act in certain ways."
(6) In short, motivation is the sum-total of all the efforts which encourages a worker to show better performance. It would be possible only when his needs are recognised and satisfied.
