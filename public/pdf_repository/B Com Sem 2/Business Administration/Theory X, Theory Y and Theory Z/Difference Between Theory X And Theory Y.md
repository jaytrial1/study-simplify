# DIFFERENCE BETWEEN THEORY X AND THEORY Y
Theory X and Theory Y both affect the style of management. The following are the effects of both theories on management style :

| Theory X | Theory Y |
| :--: | :--: |
| 1. Only superiors set objectives and subordinates have to fulfil them | 1. Subordinates are associated with managers in setting objectives. |
| 2. Subordinates try to achieve the goals laid down due to fear of punishment. Management adopts policy accordingly. | 2. Subordinates try to achieve objectives of organisation with self-control and initiative. Management frames its policy accordingly. |
| 3. Autocratic leadership style is adopted, in which subordinates have simply to carry out policies and programmes laid down. | 3. Participative management concept is adopted which suggests democratic-style of leadership. |
| 4. Subordinates are prepared to accept responsibility and they: lack, sense of responsibility. | 4. Subordinates come forward to accept responsibilities and challenge, They are conscious of their responsibilities. |
| 5. There develops a sense of distrust among subordinates. | 5. Subordinates trust the management and are prepared to accept their plans and policies. |
| 6. There is a rigid system of control emphasis is laid on control from outside. | 6. Control system is self-imposed and no rigid control is there. Superiors are the guides of subordinates and not only the bosses. |
| 7. The chain of communication flows from upper level to lower level. | 7. Communication becomes a twoway process. It flows from above to below and from bottom to top. |
| 8. Management take punitive steps like warning, dismissal, issuing memos, monetary penalties, demotions etc. | 8. Management implements decentralisation, delegation, job enrichment, division of work according to the abilities, self evaluation etc. |
| 9. Concept of co-partnership is not acceptable. | 9. Co-partnership is adopted. |
| 10. Motivating factors are lowerlevel needs. | 10. Motivativing factors are higher level needs. |
