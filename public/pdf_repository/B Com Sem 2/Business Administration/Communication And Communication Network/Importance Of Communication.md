# IMPORTANCE OF COMMUNICATION OR ADVANTAGES
Importance of communication in management cannot be overemphasised. An ex-president of the American Management Association, Shri Alvin Dodd, once remarked' that 'the number one management problem today is communication.' Failures in operations are frequently attributed to the fact that communication is lacking; that instructions and orders and information did not reach some members of the organisation. The importance of communication in management can he judged from the following points :
(i) Smooth running of the enterprise : The management may have best planning, the most efficient personnel, but unless the policies and decisions are effectively communicated to the personnel, the business cannot succeed. Effective communication is essential for motivating the employees.
Even when two persons work together, each must understand what the other is trying to do and how. They may talk to each other, write to each other or make signs with their hands. Any how they must get their ideas conveyed from one to the other. The more complex the organisation, the more important it becomes to establish that correct information is passed on to the right person at the right time and through the right medium. The smooth running of the organisation depends on an effective system of communication.
(ii) Maximum Productivity : Effective communication of policies are to be conveyed to the employees and the beliefs and complaints of the employees in turn are to be received by managers. This is how a sound human relation gets established through communication and decisions to those who are to put them into action results in accurate and efficient performance. Communication has thus played an important role in increasing productivity and production. It is observed that production has declined in those firms where communication is defective. As one writer has put it "For this management has to sell ideas, motivate the workers to work with an a will and build up high morale in the company. Communication, as an influence process, plays a vital role here."
(iii) Assists other Functions : Communication helps other functions of management like planning and co-ordination. Top officials and lower level personnel communicate their ideas and suggestions on the formulation of a plan. Besides. co-ordination between different groups is essential for the efficient functioning of the enterprise. To a large extent this co-ordination and co-operation depend upon adequate and effective system of communication at all levels of management. Communication brings, about mutual understanding between the officers and personnel at all levels and fosters the spirit of co-operation. In the words of Mary Cushing Niles, 'Good communications are essential to co-ordination.'
(iv) Job Satisfaction : Good communication promotes mutual understanding and this leads to Job satisfaction among employees. Their complaints and grievances are brought to the notice of the top officials. The very fact that they could put their grievances before management. leads to cooling down. of temper among employees. This helps in developing good employer-employee relationship,
(v) Basis of Leadership : It is impossible to think of leadership in the absence of effective communication between the leader and his followers. For leadership to exist a two-way communication, establishing close relations between persons is essential. The efficacy of leadership depends on unambiguous and comprehensive system of communication. It is through effective communication only that we have been able to develop-democratic leadership in business and political system.
(vi) Establishment of Human Relations : According to Robert D Berth, human relations are impossible without communication and communication is impossible without human relations. Importance of human approach has been accepted almost unanimously at present. Employees should be dealt with as human beings, not as machines. If they are to be aroused to work successfully, some motivation and encouragement are necessary. The views and opinions of the managers are to be conveyed to the employees and the beliefs and complaints of the employees in turn are to be received by managers. This is how a sound human relation gets established through communication.
