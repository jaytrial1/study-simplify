# CHARACTERISTICS OF COMMUNICATION
(i) Goal-oriented Activity : Its aim is not to convey or receive information only. Rather its purpose is to realise the basic objectives of the business unit. Communication is indispensable for realisation of objectives.
(ii) Two-way Process :* Communication is a two-way process. It includes the acts of giving and receiving information both. The top officials do not just issue instructions or orders to their subordinates; rather they also receive and try to understand the information that the subordinates supply in turn. That communication is a two-way process means also that the manager, after issuing orders, try to know the reactions of the subordinates and also whether they have fully understood the orders or not.
(iii) Internal Process : Communication is internal to the organisation in the sense that it is confined to only members to the organisation who use it. Advertisements in the newspaper intended for the customers are not included in the concept of communication. But when a manager asks his accountant to prepare some statement, it is a part of communication.
(Iv) Perpetual Process : Communication is a perpetual process. The exchange of views and opinions goes on continuously among the employees of a business enterprise. Communication begins as a business is launched and it ends only when the business is wound up.
(v) Administrative Process : Communication is administrative in character. In ordinary life, a letter or an advertisement is treated as a means of communication, but in business the word communication refers to administrative or managerial communication only. Through communication, the problems of management are solved.
(vi) Human Process : Communication is a kind of human process. It is effected between two human beings. The intercom, telephone and radio are merely physical instruments which help human beings communicate with one another.
(vii) All Pervasive : Communication is an all pervasive process in the sense that communication can be among the persons in the same level of management or among the persons in different levels of management.
