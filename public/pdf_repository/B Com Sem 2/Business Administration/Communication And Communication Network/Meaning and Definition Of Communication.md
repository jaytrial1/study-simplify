# MEANING \& DEFINITION OF COMMUNICATION

Several authors have attempted to define communication. Some of the notable definitions are as follows :
(i) In simple words, 'Communication means exchanging information among individuals working in any organisation.'
(ii) According to Louis A. Allen, "Communication is the use of all the things one person does, when he wants to create understanding in the mind of another. It is a bridge of meaning. It involves a systematic and continuous process of telling, listening and understanding."
(iii) Elliott Jaques described Communication as "the sum total of directly and indirectly, consciously and unconsciously transmitted feeling, attitudes and wishes."
(iv) According to the American Society of Training Directors, 'Communication is the interchange of thoughts or information in order to establish better human relations, mutual understanding or trust.'
(v) Newman and Summer hold that, 'Communication is an exchange of facts, ideas, opinions or emotions by two or more persons.'
(vi) According to Haimann, 'Communication means the process of passing information and understanding from one person to another. Communication, fundamental and vital to all managerial functions, is the process of imparting ideas and making oneself understood by others."

The above definitions clarify the basic theme of communication. It is not enough that one person speaks or writes something, it is also necessary that other person listens or reads it and understands it as well.

Since management basically means to get things done through other persons, a manager has to convey information to all employees in the organisation. It is estimated that on an average, a manager spends almost 90 per cent of his time in communicating information to his subordinates. This does not mean however, that the manager is an expert in communicating. Communication takes place when both the parties involved, that is, one who gives information and the other who receives it, are equally active and mutually helpful.
