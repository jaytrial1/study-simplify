# COMMUNICATION NETWORK
A communication network is the sum total of various interrelated and interdependent channels through which information flows among the members of the organisation. It includes both formal and inform channels of communication. The efficiency of the organisation depends on the functions of a large number of small groups in a business enterprise and also on the interaction between them. The activities of these small groups is to a large extent conditioned by the flow of information between them. Hence, building communication network is an area where much can be done to improve the efficiency of these groups.

Formal channels are provided in the structure of organisation to establish a link between two or more persons. Information is transmitted through these channels. This arrangement of communication resembles a net. It consists of the centers of communication and channels of information for the transmission of information among them. They together make up a network and hence, it is known as 'communication network.' There are several channels linking various posts in the organisation; the sum total of these channels is called communication network. In view of its considerable, significance in management, the behavioural scientists conducted experiments in the laboratory to analyse and explain the impact of communication network. The attempts were made to measure the direction of communication, the working of each net in it, accuracy of problem resolution, the nature of leadership, flexibility and character of the members etc.

Communication network is made up of two channels-(i) formal communication channel, and (ii) informal communication channel. Both these channels are interrelated and interdependent. These channels exert significant influence on the smoothness, rapidity and accuracy of the information flow. In fact, the effectiveness and problems of communication depend on the structure of this network.

In brief, the efficiency of communication network depends on the number of communication centres and their nature. If the number of communication centres is too large, many more channels will be needed to establish contact between them and consequently, the structure of communication will become more complicated. If the number of communication channels is too small, information will be delayed or fail to reach its final point. Similarly, the nature of communication network also affects the efficiency of communication. The four forms of communication network are as follows :

Various forms of Communication :
(1) Circle
(2) Chain
(3) Wheel
(4) Free-flow or All Channel.

(1) Circular : In circular network, each person is able to communicate with two other persons around him. For instance, a person in a conference can talk with only two persons-one to the left and the other to the right of him. He cannot talk with other persons of the group. In the figure 1, A can discuss with B and E, B can discuss with A and C and C can discuss with $B$ and $D$ only. There is therefore no centralisation but all oersons hold equal place and hence, there is a highly decentralised pattern in it.
(2) Chain : In this type of network, communication flows in a chain line in a specified formal channel. For instance, in figure 2 A reports to B, B reports to C and C reports to D . It thus represents a great deal of centralisation. A person takes decision and sends this information to the final person through several persons in between.
(3) Wheel : Wheel network is also called star network. It represents a dictatorial and autocratic pattern. When four persons communicate with the fifth person, it is known as wheel or circular network. For instance, in figure 3, B, C, D and B all these persons can talk with A. There is a high degree of centralisation in this network. As compared to chain network, it contains a lower number of channels. The members cannot generally communicate with each other, they communicate only with the leader of the group.
(4) Free Flow or All Channel : In this network, every person can freely communicate with all other person in the group. Hence, it consists of as many channels of communication as possible. It represents complete decentralisation.
