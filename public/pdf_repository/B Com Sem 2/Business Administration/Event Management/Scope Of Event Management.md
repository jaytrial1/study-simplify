# SCOPE OF EVENT MANAGEMENT
Event management is a profession that requires public assembly for the purpose of celebration, education, marketing and reunion.

The scope of event management is very wide in terms of various kinds of events.

Following points indicate the scope of event management.
(1) Minor event management
(2) Corporate event management
(3) Social event management
(4) Political event management
(5) Educational event management
(6) Sports event management
(7) Entertainment event management
(8) Religious/cultural event managment
(9) Non-profit event management
(1) Minor Event Management :

Minor events are small events occurring in day to day activities of people's life at which peoples give practically no thoughts. Such events can range from small gathering of peoples for their demands, office lunch break and employees sharing and praising each others' Tiffin and so on.

Management of these events are quite easy because small group of peopleconnected with these events. There is no need to prepare event blueprint and event portfolio. Event budget is not important consideration to execute action in such event management.

(2) Corporate Event Management : 

A corporate event can be defined as a gathering that is sponsored by a business for its employees, business partners and clients. These events for largest audience. Such events can range from conferences, meetings. trade shows, product launching, executive programmes and so on.

Following are the popular kinds of corporate events :
(1) Board Meetings :

Board meetings are the popular corporate event. It covers company's recent progress and discuss future goals and strategies.
(2) Seminars and conferences : :

Usually, seminars and conference are shorter events including 1 or 2 days. The main objectives of these events is to provide relevant information to concern parties like employees, business partners and clients. Such arrangement is necessary to execute action in this event management. For un example, preparing a list of targeted audiences, decide venue, arrangement of multiple sessions, catering and food, stage management etc.
(0) Trade shows :

Hosting a trade show is a great way to position company's brand as an industry leader and to generate new leads.

Trade shows event require great event planning. Event planning for trade shows involves negotiating sponsorship rates for trade show booth space and advertising and promotion at the event. It requires also logistic management.
(4) Executive Programmes :

Executive programmes are special events and basically arranged for training and development executives and officers.
(5) Business Dinners :

It is the most traditional business events. It can be held for a range of reasons like celebrating milestones, simply getting together with clients and employees, etc.

(6) Networking Events :

These events are ideal for making new contacts, leading to new clients, vendors and partners.
(7) Product launches :

Most product launch events happen in Metro cities or in cities where the company is located. These launches give the public and the media a chance to hear everything about the product. Event management of product launches is essential components of the marketing mix of large companies. When Pepsi or Coca-Cola anounces a new type of soft drink, the companies hold product launches. It is the great way to introduce the product to investors, business partners and media. Sometimes, this is not a formal event. For example, a company announcing a new soft drink may set up a booth at a major sporting event or large fair. These events require sound planning and great arrangement at convention hall. Timing and venue both are important consideration in such kinds of event management.
(8) Team Building Events :

Team buildings events are meant to do just in buildings strongest teams. It provides unique opportunity for employees to spend time together in a non-work environment. They working together to solve problems or puzzles and complete tasks.

(3) Social Event Management :

Social event management refers to manage social events like marriage, anniversaries, weddings, birthday parties, social gatherings, festival celebrations and other social celebrations.

These are usually organised to celebrate major life events and religious ceremonies.

Festival celebrations are very popular such as Navratri celebration, Ganesh Chaturthi Celebration, Janmashtami Celebration, etc.

Social event management is also known as personal event management.
(4) Political Event Management :

Political event management is not easy to compare other event management. Political event management is broad in nature. They include any function initiated and organised by a political party or organisation. Political event management includes political campaign management, political rallies management, management of gatherings of winning candidates, etc.
(5) Educational Event Management :

Educational event management includes various events related to academics and knowledge based events. Educational events are those where students, academicians and industry experts meet and interact on diferent academic and knowledge oriented topics. These include management of seminars, workshops, conferences, job fairs, campus placement, etc.

(6) Sports Event Management :

Sports event management is related to various major activities of different indoor and outdoor categories of sports. Now - a - days it is getting more - n - more popularity due to its high-voltage charms. These are events where the main attractions are sports matches - the winter olympics, world olympics, Cricket World Cup, Wimbledon, and other happenings that involve sporting activities all fall under this category.
(7) Entertainment Event Management :

Entertainment event management is easy to compare other event management. Entertainment events are done to provide a more relaxed environment that entertains the attendees. Concerts, stage plays stand-up comedies, awards distribution etc. are some good examples of these events.
(8) Religious / Cultural Event Management :

Religious / Cultural events includes festivals, religious ceremonies, cultural fairs, heritage museum tours, and cultural shows. All of these highlight the unique qualities of different cultures and religious. This kind of event aims to promote a locality's culture and appeal to the place as a tourist destination.
(9) Non-Profit Event Management :

The main purpose of this event management is to raise funds, spread awareness, and support a certain causes. These include - fund raising galas, charity auctions, and volunteer drives.

The events management industry contributes to the society's economic success, as it opens employemnt and investment opportunities.
