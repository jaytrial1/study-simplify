# COMPONENTS OF EVENT MANAGEMENT
There are number of activities and important things, that play very important role in successful event planning and management. All of the events consist of most important and inter-related elements which make it complete and successful event. These elements are in the form of components given as under.


(1) Event Blueprint : 

The first and formost step or component of event management is to prepare event blueprint. Event blueprint is the basic outline of the specific event. It depicts the structure, planning and strategy to manage a particular event successfully. It clears the concept of event and the initial vision.
(2) Event planning :

Event planning is the basic component of event management. Event planning is the process of managing a project such as a meeting, tardeshow, ceremony party, team building activity and convention.
Event planning Includes :
(1) Establishment of objectives of particular event.
(2) Budgeting
(3) Establishing timelines.
(4) Selecting and reserving the event sites
(5) Acquiring permits,
(6) Developing a theme of event,
(7) Arranging for activities,
(8) Arranging for equipment and facilities.
(9) Food planning
(J0) Managing risk and developing contingency plans.

(3) Event Programme :

The 'Programme' is the schedule of activities from the starting point of the event to end point of the event. In short, it is a planned series of particular event. For a conference, the programme stipulates the times of lectures and workshops, what topics are offered and who is presenting.

Event programme is the central organising component of the event. The important factors to consider in preparing the event programmes are :
(1) 'Consultation with all parties directly involved in the event.
(2) Calculating the time of each and every activity of event.
(3) The order of activities.
(4) Ensuring that the venue is available for the full duration of the event.
(5) Choosing the proper date for event.

(4) Event Budget :

The preparation of a budget is an essential part of event management. The event budget is a projection or forecast of the income and expenditure of the future event and its activities. The event budget is important to the success or failure of project. The success of an event is not just a question of the "idea" and "innovation", but it is a question of the "money." For this reason, the economic and financial forecast is crucial for every event manager. The event budget is the best way to determine whether an event is feasible and how great the risks are. Event budget will directly impact most of the other components.

Following are the basic rules to prepare a event budget :-
(1) It should be realistic about incomes of event.
(ii) It should be necessary to calculate expenditure that the event will incur based on plans made.
(iii) It should have a contigency plan.
(iv) Budget to avoid making a loss.

(6) Event Location and Venue :

Even location and venue is one of the key components and key planning areas in event management. An event venue is the place or location where the event is scheduled to take place.

There are two types of venues for particular event :
(a) In-house venue, and (b) External venue.

Following are the major consideration for selecting an event venue are as follows :
(1) Size of the audience and audience comfort
(2) Parking area
(3) Technical support
(4) Safety and security
(5) Layout and suitablity
(6) Kinds of events
(7) Logistic facilities

Example : 

Event management of wedding should be done by considering size of the audience (Guest), parking area and layout and sutiability. Generally, wedding are organised in convention hall or party hall. Sometimes, they are arranged in 3 star or 5 star hotels also.

(8) Event Security :

Security is one of the most important components of event management and it requires proper attention from event manager's side. Proper event security ensures you safety and proper results expected from the specific event. For proper safety and security special security staff and devices should be arranged.
(9) Contingency Plans :

Contingency plan is recovery of failure in some specific areas of event management. Event managers have to be prepared for continuity of event even if sumething goes wrong. No one can give guarantee of success of event. So, some contingent plans should be prepared.
