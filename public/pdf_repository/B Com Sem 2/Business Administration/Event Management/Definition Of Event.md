# DEFINITION OF EVENT

(1) In simple terms :
(a) "Event can be defined as something that happens according to a plan or an activity."
(b) "An event is a unique moment in time celebrated with ceremony and traditions to satisfy specific needs of people."

(2) According to Philip Kotler, "Events are defined as occurrences to communicate particular messages to target auidence."

Examples of events :
(1) Celebration of Vibrant Navatri
(2) Award ceremony
(3) A launching of a new product
(4) Opening ceremony of Cricket World Cup, etc.
