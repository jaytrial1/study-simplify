# INTRODUCTION

An event is a unique moment in its universal and it means occasion when something happens or something needs to be done to organised the same. Events are something like occurrences, happenings, incidents and occasions.

Events are attracting world wide attention and it is getting corporatised. So, it is becoming more of professional occasions. There are various kinds of events like social gathering, marriages, birthday party, corporate function, product launching etc.

So, the recent growth in events around the world is increasing in such a way that every event needs to be manage properly. Event management is considered as one of the strategic marketing tools by companies.
