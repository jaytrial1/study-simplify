# NATURE AND CHARACTERISTICS OF EVENT MANAGEMENT
The following are the characteristics of event management:

(1) Goal-Oriented:

Event management is a goal-oriented activity. Every event has certain goals or objectives to be achieved. Therefore, each and every activity of an event is directed to achieve the goals. Any event does not include unnecessary formalities and activities.

(2) Creative and Innovative process:

Management of events requires forecasting, imagination and foresightness. To manage any event successfully, the event management team must be creative, innovative and dynamic. The team must have new ideas to manage and deal with the event. So that we can say that event management is a creative and innovative process.

(3) It requires professionalism:

There is need for some sorts of professional touch to all the components of event management. So, we can manage an event successfully. The elements of professionalism involves systematic planning and control of activities related to an event, proper training to team members and proper directing to the employees.

(4) It is a systematic process:

Event management is considered as a systematic process because it involves a series of interrelated functions like establishing the objectives of event, planning, organising, staffing, directing, co-ordinating, communicating and controlling of people and resources.

(5) Group Efforts:

Any event cannot be managed successfully without collective and group efforts. The scope of event management is very wide.

So, at every level of event management proper group or team efforts are required.

(6) Requires Sound Leadership:

Sound leadership plays a vitte role in event management. The event managers have to influence, motivate and inspire the employees in order to manage the event successfully. Thus, event management requires sound and effective leadership on the part of event managers.

(7) Wider Scope :

Event management includes management of different events like social, political, commercial, religious, sports events, etc. So, the scope of event management open for all events. It is due to recent growth of various events across the world.
(8) It requires effective promotions :

Event management requires effective promotion in the form of publicity of the event, advertising of the event and maintaining good public relation. To communicate and popularised any event, it requires proper promotional activities.
