# CHARACTERISTICS OF EVENTS
The characteristics of events are as follows :
(1) An event is a unique moment.
(2) Events offer a unique experience.
(3) Events are for fullfilment of specific needs and objectives.
(4) Events have a shorter life span compare to projects.
(5) Events involve huge requirement of funds to manage the resources.
(6) Events require sound planning of various activities and resources.
(7) Events may face the element of risk and uncertainity.
