# DEFINITION OF EVENT MANAGEMENT
In simple terms,
(a) "Event management is the process of desiging and producing events."
(b) "Event management is the appication of the management practice of project management to the creation and development of events."
(c) "Event management is the planning and management of an event or activity which involves identifying target audience, devising the event concept, feasibility studies and co-ordinating various technical aspects."
(d) "Event management is a multi-purpose organ that manage the various components of events"
