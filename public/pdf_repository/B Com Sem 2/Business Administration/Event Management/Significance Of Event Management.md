# SIGNIFICANCE OF EVENT MANAGEMENT
(1) Helps to achieve specified goal :

The main objective of any event management is to achieve specified need and goals of the client or people. The management of a diverse set of functions focuses on the need of co-ordination and co-operation in order to achive the common objective. It is possible through group efforts of diversified team of event management company.
(2) Professional growth opportunities :

Event management offers huge opportunities for professional growth specially for event organisers and planners by continuing working on different events to get all round experience. With the help of skills and abilities, event planners can become entrepreneurs with their own event management firms.
(3) Provides Career Opportunities :

Apart from event manager, there are many other job opportunities in the event management such as -

- Operation and logistic managers,
- Entertainment managers,
- Sponsership managers,
- Event Designer,
- Event Co-ordinator, etc.

It does not require a formal degree or educational qualification. Professionals working in other areas can easily shift their careers in the field of event management.

(4) Develops leadership qualities :

To manage an event, the managers require good and sound leadership qualities to influence, motivate and inspire the subordinates to work effectively to make event more successfull. In all aspects of event management, managers have to develop some sorts of leadership quality and that is develop through event management.

(5) Increasing public relations : 

Event management helps to enhance good relation with various internal and external stakeholders from the organisation point of view. If the event is well-managed, then there is possibility of good corporate image and it leads to greater success and organisation can maintain long-lasting relation with public.

(6) Develop team spirit :

Event management is a group efforts. Therefore, there is need for team work between managers and their subordinates in the organisation to make the event more successful. If, there is proper co-ordination between the various departments in the organisation then it leads to team spirit among the employees.

(7) Proper execution of an event :

If event is managed through proper planning and controlling, the event can be easily executed effectively and efficiently.

(8) Global reach :

Event management is a global profession in which an local person or organisation can take the business across the globe. They should be proper service to international targeted audiences or executing an event in a foreign location.
