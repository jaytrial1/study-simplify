# DIFFERENCE BETWEEN PERT AND CPM : 

The purpose of both the methods is identical and both were developed almost at the same time "Fundamentally, PERT and CPM are techniques of project management useful in the basic managerial functions of planning. scheduling and control."
(1) CPM was developed in 1957 in Du Pont Company by Walker and Kelly, while PERT was developed by the Navy Special Project Office for the Polaris project in 1958. This technique succeeded in shortening the original project completion time of Polaris by two years.
(2) PERT is more used in research and development projects particularly in Aerospace industries and military activities. CPM is used more in projects such as construction and such activities where the activities are of repetitive nature.
(3) There are some differences in use of technical terms. e. g. in PERT, the point at which each activity is completed is called 'event', while in CPM, it is called 'nodes'. The different portions of project are called Activities and in CPM they are known as 'job'.

Except these points, there is not much difference. Both the methods are meant for finding out the longest path and efforts made to control them. Arrow diagrams are used in both methods and purposes of both are to complete the project in the least possible time.
