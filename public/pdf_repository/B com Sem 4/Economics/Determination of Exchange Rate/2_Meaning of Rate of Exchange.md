2. Meaning of Rate of Exchange

The rate at which the currency of one country is exchanged for the currency of another country is known as the rate of exchange. Thus, the rate of exchange is the rate at which two or more currencies are exchanged for one another. For example, if the exchange rate between dollar and rupee is one dollar equal to sixty five rupees ($ \$ 1=$ Rs. 65) or between pound sterling and rupee one pound-sterling is equal to One Hundred Five rupees ($£ 1=$ Rs. 105), it means that the price of one dollar or one pound-sterling in terms of rupees is Rs. 65 or Rs. 105 respectively. The rate of exchange, thus is the price of one currency expressed in terms of another currency, it is the reflection of the external value of the domestic currency. It should also be noted here that exchange rate is not always constant, it goes on changing from time to time on account of changes in the demand for and supply of foreign currency.
