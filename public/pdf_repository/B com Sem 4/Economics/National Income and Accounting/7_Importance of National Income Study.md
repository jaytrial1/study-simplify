
A thorough study of the national income of any country not only gives an idea of the economic situation and progress of that country, but also provides guidance as to what changes are necessary in the economy to achieve rapid progress in the future. Moreover, the progress made in various sectors of the economy such as agriculture, industry, transport, trade etc. can also be known from the study of national income. How much savings and investment should increase to increase the rate of economic progress, how much has happened in the past and how much can happen in the future can also be calculated. Information about the economic trends of a nation can be obtained from the study of national income.

The importance of the study of national income can be explained by the foliowing points :

1.  The composition of the entire economy is understood through information about national income. The share of different sectors of the economy - agriculture, industries, transport, services etc. in national income and the relative changes in it can be known through the study of national income. One section of economy with another section as well How they are related and interdependent with the economy as a whole can be seen through the analysis of national income. An analysis of this kind is very useful in studying the progress of different sectors of the economy and their comparative importance.
2.  The concept of national income is a measure of economic growth and development. A high and steadily increasing national income is a symbol of economic prosperity of a country. This is why it is said that the economic prospenty of a country is reflected in its national income.
3.  The modern age is the age of planning. Various countries of the world are making continuous efforts to achieve economic development through planning. Estimates of national income are more important in a planned economy. These estimates are very useful in formulating plans and setting development targets. It also facilitates evaluation of development programmes. Moreover, their contribution to actual economic policy formulation important.
4.  Estimates of national income are sometimes used to measure changes in a country's economic welfare over time. Many departmental changes take place in the process of development Also, the benefits of economic development are shared among different social groups. A study of national income is also necessary to know whether the benefits of development are being distributed equally or unequally.
5.  A discussion of the economics of developed and underdeveloped countries uses estimates of national income, international standards of welfare, and a comparative study of the stages of development of different economies. Estimates of national income and per capita income are very useful for making a comparative study of the growth rate and per capita income and standard of living of different countries.
6.  Also, the calculation of national income gives an idea of the distribution of income among different sections of the society. Per capita consumption is a common indicator of economic well-being.
7.  In a mixed economy like India, an idea of how and how much the activities of public and private enterprises contribute to the economic progress of the country also comes from the estimation of national income. Hence planning can be done for the development of public as well es private sector as required in future.
8.  The concept of national income is also important for achieving balanced regional development in a large country. From the income of various states of our country, we also get an idea of which states need to pay more attention to the economic development of the country.
9.  In the words of Paul Samuelson, "The concept of national income is one of the most important in all of economics. It is of fundamental importance in devising effective solutions to the problems of unemployment, inflation and economic development". Thus, national income estimates are a very valuable tool in the economist's toolbox.
10. All economic activities are aimed at providing material goods and immaterial services to satisfy human needs. If we measure the total output of goods and services every year, we can know to what extent we have achieved our goal. A country's economy can be said to be stagnant based on changes in national income over a period of time. Apart from that the rate of mobility can also be known, in short the national income estimates for each year give an idea of the prevailing stability or instability in the economy from which the guidance for the formation of economic policy is obtained.

Thus, the study of the concept of national, income is important and useful in many ways, but the extent to which estimates of national income can be used for different purposes depends on how accurate the nationa income calculation is, how much detail it provides, for what time it is available. It depends on all these.
