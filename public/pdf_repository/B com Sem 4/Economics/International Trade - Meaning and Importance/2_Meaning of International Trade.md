2. Meaning of International Trade

Man does not live in isolation. He lives in a society of human beings that support one another's wants. The division of labour, specialisation and the use of machinery have all combined to make it possible for many men and women to produce far in excess of what they can themselves consume. Hence, the need for exchange. Production, therefore, is not only for direct consumption but also for the market. Market is nothing but a mechanism that facilitates exchange or trade. Thus, trade means exchange between the two parties.

If an exchange on trade takes place among the citizens of the same country it is known as internal trade or inter - regional trade. However, if trade takes place between different citizens or governments of two different countries, it is known as international trade. In other words, trade which takes place amongst the citizens or regions of the same country is known as internal or inter-regional trade, while trade that takes place between citizens or governments of the different countries is called international trade. Thus, trade between Gujarat and Maharashtra is called internal or inter-regional trade, while trade between India and U.S.A. would be known as international trade.

# Division of Labour and Specialisation form the Basis of Trade
The Basis of Trade is Division of Labour and Specialisation

All trade - whether internal or international is the result of division of labour and specialisation in the field of production. If every individual is self-reliant as to his different wants, then he does not need exchange. Likewise if a country or a region can produce all the goods and services by itself, then the need for trade does not arise. But, in real life it is not so. A particular country or a region produces certain goods and commodities in which it has specialisation and imports the rest of the commodities from other country or region. Thus, for example, there is more production of textiles in Gujarat while there is more production of wheat in say Punjab or Madhya Pradesh and so the trade takes place between Gujarat and Punjab or between Gujarat and Madhya Pradesh. Thus, inter-regional trade (inter trade) and international trade depend on the same theoretical principle and base and still, however, the need for a separate theory of international trade has been accepted.

Under the international division of labour, the different countries of the world tend to produce those goods and commodities for which there exists favourable natural factors wealth, adequate water resources and adequate technical know-how and skill etc. Thus, when a country instead of producing all the goods and commodities which it needs, focusses its attention on the production of those goods and commodities for which there

Likewise, if other countries too do the same thing and specialise in the production of those goods and commodities in which it has comparative advantage, then it is known as international division of labour.
