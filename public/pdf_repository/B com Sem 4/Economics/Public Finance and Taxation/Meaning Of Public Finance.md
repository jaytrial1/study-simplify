# 2_Meaning of Public Finance
Public finance, which is on the border line of both economics and politics, is concerned with the revenue and expenditure of governments. We give below some of the definitions of public finance:
(i) Findlay Shirras : 'Public finance is the study of the principles underlying the spending and raising of funds by public authorities.'
(ii) Bastable : 'Public finance deals with expenditure and income of public authorities of the state and their mutual relations as also with the financial administration and control.'
(iii) Dalton : 'Public finance is one of those subjects which lie on the border line between economics and politics. It is concerned with the income and expenditure of public authorities and with the adjustment of the one to the other.'
(iv) Musgrave : has referred to the allocative, redistributive and stabilising functions of the State and, therefore, of public finance.
(v) Taylor : Public finance is concerned with the operation of 'fisc', that is the State treasury......... "Public finance is the fiscal science, its policies are fiscal policies and its problems are fiscal problems."

In short, public finance is a study of how the government raises its revenue and how it spends that revenue.

It follows from the above definitions that public finance can broadly be divided into four major branches :
(a) Public Revenue
(b) Public Expenditure
(c) Public Debt.
(d) Financial Administration.
