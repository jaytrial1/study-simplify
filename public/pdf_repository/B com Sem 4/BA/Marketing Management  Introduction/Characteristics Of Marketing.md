3\. CHARACTERISTICS OF MARKETING :

On the basis of above definitions, it is possible to derive following characteristics of marketing:

(1) Marketing is an economic function: Its basic task is the exchange of goods and services, The essence of marketing is selling whereby goods and services are offered to those who need them and pay for them in terms of money.

(2) Marketing is a legal function: It results in transfer of goods and services. The transfer may take place more than once. But the main point is that ultimately, the ownership vests with the final consumers or users, and the seller loses ownership.

(3) Marketing consists of various activities: The idea that marketing means only selling is an outdated one. Now it is defined to include many activities such as planning of sales, product development, market research, price determination, distribution of goods, sales promotion, after-sales services etc. In short, marketing includes activities from investigating consumers' needs to transfer of ownership of goods to them so that their needs are satisfied.

(4) Marketing is a system: The functions and activities included in marketing are interrelated and interdependent. A change in one activity invariably affects the other. Moreover they are influenced by the external environment and they also exert influence on it.

(5) Its function is to satisfy the consumers: To use the term marketing in the sense of selling only is a narrow interpretation. Satisfaction of the consumers is the most important aspect of marketing. Hence surveys and research are conducted to find out consumers preferences and then attempts are made to produce goods and services accordingly.

(6) Useful for the non-business units It is wrong to believe that marketing is useful for selling goods or services only. The religious institutions for example, try to spread their ideas and thus collect funds by donations. The educational institutions market education and hospitals market medical services to the people,

(7) Marketing is a social process: Paul Mazur has remarked in this context that marketing is the delivery of a standard of living to the society. It aims not only at profit maximisation, but at providing those goods and services to the people which can satisfy their needs.

(8) Everything Business does is Marketing: Peter Drucker has rightly stated, Marketing is not a function of business, but a view of the entire business as the economic organ, to provide goods and services. This is the marketing view of business. Everything the business does in that respect is marketing.
