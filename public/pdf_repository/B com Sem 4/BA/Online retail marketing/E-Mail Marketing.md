10\. E-MAIL MARKETING:

E-mail marketing is the effective component of online retail marketing. In the broadest sense, every E-mail sent to a potential or current customer could be considered E-mail marketing. E-mail marketing has the highest returns on investment than other marketing methods. Creating a marketing message via E-mail is almost as easy as writing an E-mail. This method is fast and efficient.

E-mail marketing is known as permission marketing and it is one of it's advantages because it allows customers to decide whether they want to be reached via E-mail.

The disadvantage of E-mail marketing is that it can transmit many software viruses and it makes customers become suspicious even to trusted sources and markets.
