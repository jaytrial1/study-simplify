1\. INTRODUCTION: MEANING:

Marketing control is an exhaustive term, as it includes control over all those marketing activities to see whether they were going on as planned and whether they result in high profitability. To be more precise, the term marketing control includes such activities as control over salesmen, advertising control, selling cost control, distribution cost control and control over sales organisation etc. In short, marketing control refers to the control over every activity related to marketing and sales. The main objective of marketing control is to achieve the sales target, to reduce selling costs, to maintain equilibrium in marketing activities and to maintain equilibrium also in the volumes of sales in different sales territories.

Marketing control helps not only to maintain profitability at a higher level, but also, it is in the interest of salesmen because in the absence of control, their efforts may get weakened, their sense of responsibility may erode and if sales decline, their income may fall. Conversely, marketing control, if effective, will inculcate discipline among the salesmen and thereby will raise the prestige of the firm.

The process of marketing control, like the process of controlling any other activity, can be divided into various stages. e.g., to establish the standards, to collect information of the actual performance, to compare actual performance with the standards laid down and finally to take corrective measures. Some specific methods are employed for marketing control. Among them, the main method is that of marketing cost analysis, in addition to budgetary control and control through ratios and reports. Since marketing control is a very complicated job, large firms keep a separate department to accomplish it successfully.
