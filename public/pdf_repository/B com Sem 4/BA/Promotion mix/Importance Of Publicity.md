6\. IMPORTANCE OF PUBLICITY:

(1) Newsworthiness: A product is newsworthy if it is possible to publish interesting stories about it. The objective of publicity is to build the prestige of the product. Of course, this is not possible in all cases. But when a new product is to be introduced in the market, it is inaugurated by some distinguished personality and the reports of the inauguration are published in the newspapers. This is how a new product immediately earns publicity.

(2) Stimulus for Salesmen and Dealers: The other objective of publicity is to stimulate salesmen and dealers to sell as much as possible. If a new product is publicised as soon as it is introduced in the market, the customers are aware of it when a salesman approaches them. The background being prepared in advance, the salesman's job is rendered easier. Similarly, dealers also need not give much information to the buyers who are well-informed, and hence dealers' job is also rendered less strenuous.

(3) Creating Credibility: Customers doubt the validity of claims made in advertising in favour of a product, as the manufacturer himself praises his product. As against this, publicity of a product through newspapers, radio and television carries much credibility because it is delivered through other non- commercia. agencies. Customers generally believe as correct the information published by newspapers, radio, television etc.

(4) Economy: When a company has a smaller promotion budget, publicity is most preferable. It enables the company to realise the goal of sales expansion at a lower cost. Of course, publicity is not completely costless. Some expense is to be incurred to invite journalists, to offer them gifts and presents and to maintain contacts with them. Despite this, publicity is undoubtedly less costly as compared to advertising and other media of sales promotion.

5\) Makes Salesmen's Work Easy: As due to publicity, the customers are convinced about the company's products, the work of salesmen becomes easy.

(6) Public Relations: The customers are not tempted to buy company's goods in publicity, as it is done in advertisement. Thus it works as a public relation activity.

(7) Change in Customer's Attitude: It becomes easy to change the attitude of customers through publicity. This becomes useful to society to spread some important messages like family planning, polio programmes.
