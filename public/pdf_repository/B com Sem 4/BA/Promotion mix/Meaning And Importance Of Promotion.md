2\. MEANING AND IMPORTANCE OF PROMOTION:

(A) Meaning: When intensive efforts are made to increase the sales of a particular product, it is called 'Promotion'.

'Promotion mix includes all those activities which are undertaken to promote the sale of products ultimately. They include activities like advertising, personal selling, sales promotion and publicity. Thus, without making any specific changes in product mix, prices or distribution channels, if an unusual increase in sales is achieved during a specific time period with the help of extensive use of advertising, personal selling, sales promotion and publicity, it is called promotion. During this promotion campaign, not only regular customers but even prospective customers are also attracted towards the company. The promotion activities create favourable psychological impact upon customers, which leads to increase in sales.

In promotion, consumers are informed and reminded about the products and are requested and persuaded to purchase their products.

(1) In the words of Philip Kotler, "Promotion compasses all tools in the marketing mix whose major role is persuasive communications."

(2) Mason & Rath write, "Promotion consists of those activities that are designed to bring a company's goods and services to the favourable attention of customers."

(B) Importance of Promotion Promotion works as oxygen for any business enterprise. The promotion has a strategic position in the business unit, as the spine has to the human body. The promotional activities enhance sales of a firm to new heights and sales is the life blood of any business. This proves how important the promotion activities are.

Promotion is important because of following reasons:

(1) Reducing distance between Producer and Consumers: Due to globalisation of trade, in many cases there is a distance of thousands of miles between a producer and consumers. When one country of the world concentrates on the production of some few products and its customers are spread throughout the world, the tools of promotion act as a link between producer and consumers. The consumers get valuable information about various products through various tools of promotion, which help them in making buying decisions quickly. This leads to permanent relation between producer and consumers.

(2) Increased Use of Communication: In fact, success of promotion depends upon efficient communication. The customers have to be informed about the outstanding features of the product, the policies of the company etc. Hence, the company has to make its communication system more efficient and effective.  
Unless the communication structure is well-established, the information about product's features, use value and benefits do not reach the consumers. In fact, providing information is the base of increasing sales. Hence, for making promotion successful, a proper communication system has to be established and then only promotion activities will start.

(3) Providing Information to Middlemen: Information should not only

reach consumers, but the middlemen like agents, wholesalers, distributors and retailers have also to be kept informed about the product, company and promotional activities. Nowadays there are wonderful media available which can send information to all middlemen within a very short time. In fact, the promotion activities greatly contribute to establishing a communication network.

(4) Increased Sales: The ultimate goal of all promotional activities is to increase sales. Due to them, demand has increased. By creating a favourable impression upon customers, these activities will work wonders in increasing sales of those products, whose sales are declining. Many prospective customers are attracted towards the company and its products due to promotion and thus boost up sales.
