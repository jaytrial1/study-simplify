6\. NEED FOR BRANDING :

A brand is a name, term sign symbol, design or a combination of them, just to identify the goods or services of one sellers group of others.

(1) The need for branding is required to distinguish the goods and services of one producer from those of another just to satisfy the needs of consumer as well as the producer.

(2) The need for branding is required because to protect the product of the producer as well as consumer of the same against the inferior quality. So the manufacturer put the trade-marks on their products.

(3) Branches help to identify the sources or maker of a product and allow consumers either individual-or firms-to assign responsibilities for its performance to a particular manufacturer or distributor. That is why branding is must.

(4) Today brands play a vital role which improves consumers' lives and enhances the financial value of firms. So for this reason too branding is required for the goods and services.

(5) Branding is needed because the consumers may evaluate the identical product differently depending on how it is branded.

(6) Branding is needed to create mental structures that help consumers organized their knowledge about products and services in a way that clarifies their decision making and, in the process, provides value to the firm.

(7) Branding is needed for the firm to simplify product handling or tracing. It also helps to organize inventory and accounting records.

(8) It is needed to give the firm legal protection for unique features or aspects of the product.

(9) Branding is needed because the competitors cannot easily imitate the product or services of the a particular organization.

(10) Branding should be must just to create a good and powerful competitive advantage in both market-place and in the mind of people.

For example: One of the master marketers at creating brands is "Procter and Gamble".
