Following are the most popular online retail marketing business models:

(1) Affiliate Marketing:  
Affiliate marketing has become one of the most effective business w models to advertise online. It is a popular tool for company's business th to compete in the present tech-hungry world. th

Affiliate marketing is an agreement between a merchant and a website owner or publisher. It is a joint and mutually beneficial business partnership between merchants and website owners whereby an affiliate is compensated for every visitor or client provided through his effort.

The basic parties in affiliate marketing are the merchant and the affiliate. The merchant is the individual or company that wants to sell its product or service. The affiliate is the individual or company that allows their website to be used for the marketing of the merchant's product.

The affiliate agrees to promote the merchant's product on their website or by other means such as email marketing by linking to the merchant's website. In exchange, the merchant pays a commission to the affiliate on all sales generated by the affiliate. Every time someone clicks on the affiliate's link and makes a purchase, the affiliate gets a commission. In most cases, the merchant will pay the affiliate only when a consumer clicks on the product link and actually makes a purchase. Some merchants, however, do choose to pay a commission merely for clicks.  
Affiliate networks help suitable affiliates and merchants find each other amongst the thousands of affiliate programmes on the internet. Affiliate networks are where merchants and potential affiliates can find each other.

Below are some of the popular affiliate marketing networks:  
(1) Amazon.com  
(2) LinkShare.com  
(3) Clickbank.com  
(4) Commission Junction.com

Advantage of Affiliate Marketing:

Affiliate marketing business models are frequently described as a win-win situation for both the merchant and the affiliate because of the pay-for-performance scheme. Obviously, both the merchant and the affiliate enjoy the benefits of affiliate marketing.

Following are the importance of affiliate marketing:  
(1) Affiliate marketing provides the merchant with a much wider market in which to advertise their products or services.  
(2) It gives the product or service the maximum exposure. So that the merchants can gain more customers.  
(3) The affiliate can earn commission. The more referrals there are the more profit for the affiliate. Prospective consumer clicks on the affiliates ad link, he is redirected to the merchant's website. If the consumer goes on to buy that particular product the affiliate earns a commission.  
(4) It is an effective way to earn money from in home.  
(5) It does not require a huge investment.

Disadvantages of Affiliate Marketing:  
(1) The merchant depends on website holders for selling products and services.  
(2) Critics argue that this kind of business model is SCAM of some sort.  
(3) Link hijackers can hijack affiliate links and get paid for the commissions instead.  
(4) Sometimes, high commission costs are incurred by website holders.
