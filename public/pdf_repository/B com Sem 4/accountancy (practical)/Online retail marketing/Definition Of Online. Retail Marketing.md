2\. DEFINITION OF ONLINE. RETAIL MARKETING:

(1) "Online retail marketing means advertising and marketing of goods and services through the internet."

(2) "Online retail marketing means the sale of products and services through the internet."

(3) "The promotion of products and services through one or more forms of electronic media, is called online retail marketing."

(4) "Online retail marketing refers to advertising and marketing efforts that use the web and email to drive direct sales through electronic media, in addition to sales leads from websites, emails or blogs."

(5) "Online retail marketing refers to a set of powerful tools and methods used for promoting goods and services through a digital channel."

(6) "Online retail marketing is the process of marketing and selling of goods and services over the internet."
