4\. IMPORTANCE/ADVANTAGES OR SIGNIFICANCE OF MARKETING CONTROL":

Marketing control can be beneficial if it is executed effectively. It is essential not only for realising the targets of sales within a definite time period; it is essential also to reap the following advantages as it includes control over all activities related to sales and marketing:

(1) Sales activity can be undertaken as per the schedule so that reasonable profits are assured.

(2) Once the sales quota is fixed, it is possible to assess the efficacy of sales activity by comparing the actual results with the targets.

(3) Marketing control requires strict control over the activities of the salesmen. Consequently, sales tend to increase, and per unit sales costs decline. If a sense of discipline is cultivated among the salesmen, it will add to the prestige of the firm.

(4) Marketing control envisages control not only on the salesmen's activities, but on the activities of all officers and employees directly or indirectly connected with the marketing of the product. Hence, an effective marketing control enhances the efficiency of the whole marketing organisation of the firm.

(5) As a result of marketing control, salesmen are accustomed to deal politely with the customers. This also tends to strengthen the goodwill of the firm.

(6) Correct choice of distribution channel is an integral part of marketing control. The choice is made keeping in view the type of product and market condition both. Therefore, the distribution channel proves to be most efficient.

(7) As a result of marketing control, the targets of sales laid down in the sales plan can be achieved as per the schedule.

(8) Through market research, useful information can be availed of, to make necessary changes in price policy and export policy of the firm.

In sum, marketing control is a term wide enough to encompass control over various aspects of marketing such as publicity, selling costs, distribution network etc. Hence, marketing control can contribute a lot in building the prestige of the firm and also in raising its profitability.
