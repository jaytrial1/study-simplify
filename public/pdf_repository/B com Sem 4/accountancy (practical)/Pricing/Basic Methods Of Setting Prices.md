5\. BASIC METHODS OF SETTING PRICES:

A company formulates a definite price policy in accordance with its objectives. The market trends are also taken into account while actually setting the price. The following are the basic pricing methods:

(1) Cost-oriented pricing

(2) Demand-oriented pricing

(3) Competition-oriented pricing.

If price is fixed on the basis of cost, it is called cost-oriented pricing. If price is fixed on the basis of level of demand, it is known as demand-oriented pricing. If price is fixed on the basis of competitors' prices, it is described as competition-oriented pricing.
