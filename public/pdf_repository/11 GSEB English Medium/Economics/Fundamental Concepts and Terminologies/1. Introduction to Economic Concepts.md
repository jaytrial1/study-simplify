# Introduction

Economics is the study of the rational behavior of human beings. In the context of rationality, activities conducted are called *economic activities*. To begin the study of economics as a science, it is necessary and helpful to get clarity about some frequently used concepts and basic terminologies.

This chapter provides a clear understanding of:
*   Goods, services, and factors of production
*   Concepts of and difference between price and value
*   Wealth and welfare
*   The trade cycle
