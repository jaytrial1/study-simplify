# Individual Demand and Market Demand

This section distinguishes between the demand of a single consumer and the total demand of all consumers in the market.

Demand can be analyzed at the level of an individual consumer or the entire market.

*   **Individual Demand:** The quantity of a good that a single consumer is willing and able to buy at a specific price at a given point in time.
*   **Market Demand:** The sum total of the individual demands of all consumers in the market for a good at a specific price at a given point in time. It is obtained by horizontally summing the individual demand curves.
