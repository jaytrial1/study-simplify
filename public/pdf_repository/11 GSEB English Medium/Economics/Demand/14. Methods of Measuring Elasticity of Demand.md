# Methods of Measuring Elasticity of Demand

This section lists the common methods used to calculate the elasticity of demand.

While the Law of Demand shows the direction of change, elasticity quantifies the extent of change in demand due to changes in determinants like price. There are several methods to measure elasticity of demand, including:

1.  Method of proportionate change (or percentage method)
2.  Total outlay method (or total expenditure method)
3.  Geometric method (or point method)
