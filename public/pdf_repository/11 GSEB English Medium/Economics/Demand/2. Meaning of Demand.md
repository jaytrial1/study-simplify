# Meaning of Demand

This section defines demand in economics and identifies its key components.

Demand refers to the *quantity* of a good or service that a buyer *desires*, has the *ability* to purchase, and is *willing* to buy at a *specific price* at a *given point in time*.

For something to be considered demand, all five factors must be present: desire, willingness to buy, ability to buy, a particular price, and a particular point in time.
