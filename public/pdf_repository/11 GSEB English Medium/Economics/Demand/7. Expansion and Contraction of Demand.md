# Expansion and Contraction of Demand

This section explains how changes in the price of a good, while other factors remain constant, lead to movements along the demand curve.

Expansion and contraction of demand refer to the changes in the *quantity demanded* of a good caused *only* by changes in its *own price*, assuming all other determinants of demand are constant.

*   **Expansion of Demand:** Occurs when the price of a good falls, leading to an increase in the quantity demanded. This is represented as a downward movement *along the same demand curve*.
*   **Contraction of Demand:** Occurs when the price of a good rises, leading to a decrease in the quantity demanded. This is represented as an upward movement *along the same demand curve*.
