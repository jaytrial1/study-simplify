# Introduction to Markets

Generally, a market is understood as the place where commodities and services are bought and sold. However, in economics, the concept of a market is more extensive than this common idea.

## Meaning of a Market

A market is the system through which buyers and sellers come into contact with each other, either directly or indirectly, for the sale or purchase of goods or services.

According to Professor Samuel, "Market is the functional system where the buyers and sellers contact each other to decide the price and the quantity of goods or services."
