# Exercise Questions

1.  Choose correct option for the following from the options provided:
    (1) How many types of markets are there according to location?
        (A) One
        (B) Three
        (C) Four
        (D) Seven
    > **Answer:** (C) Four
    (2) 'Negligible Transportation Expense' is the characteristic of which market?
        (A) Perfect Competition
        (B) Monopoly
        (C) Monopolistic Competition
        (D) Oligopoly
    > **Answer:** (A) Perfect Competition
    (3) 'Product Differentiation' is the characteristic of which market?
        (A) Perfect Competition
        (B) Monopoly
        (C) Monopolistic Competition
        (D) Oligopoly
    > **Answer:** (C) Monopolistic Competition
    (4) In which market 'Firm is an industry'?
        (A) Perfect Competition
        (B) Monopoly
        (C) Monopolistic Competition
        (D) Oligopoly
    > **Answer:** (B) Monopoly
    (5) 'Selling Cost' is an important characteristic of which market?
        (A) Monopoly
        (B) Bilateral Monopoly
        (C) Monopolistic Competition
        (D) Perfect Competition
    > **Answer:** (C) Monopolistic Competition
