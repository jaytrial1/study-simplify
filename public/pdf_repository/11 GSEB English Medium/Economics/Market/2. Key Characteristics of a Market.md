# Characteristics of a Market

A market possesses specific characteristics:

*   **Numerous Sellers and Buyers:** A market requires the presence of buyers and sellers. Their interaction through purchase and sales facilitates purposeful exchange. Sellers aim for maximum profit, while buyers seek satisfaction from the product.
*   **Goods and Services:** Products and services must be available according to demand and necessity. Producers and sellers offer various types and forms to attract buyers and maximize profit. Buyers seek an ideal set of products and services for their satisfaction.
*   **Contact:** Buyers and sellers connect through various direct or indirect means.
    *   **Tele-Shopping:** Buyers order products or services via telephone.
    *   **Online Shopping:** Buyers order products or services selected on websites via the internet.
    These methods allow for indirect contact between buyers and sellers.
*   **Price:** The price of the product or service must be determined at a given time. Price is decided based on demand and supply factors. It depends on the demand by buyers and the ability of producers and sellers to supply.
*   **Information about the Market Situation:** Buyers and sellers need information about the current market conditions. This is crucial for making decisions related to production, distribution, and purchase, especially during times of recession, inflation, or calamities.
