## Market Based on Competition

Markets based on competition are typically classified by the number of sellers and buyers, with the number of sellers being more significant. The major types include perfect competition, monopolistic competition, and oligopoly. The two primary classes based on competition are Perfect Competition and Imperfect Competition.
