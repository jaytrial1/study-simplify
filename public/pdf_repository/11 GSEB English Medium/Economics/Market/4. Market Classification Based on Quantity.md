## Market Based on Quantity

Markets classified by the quantity of goods traded fall into two main categories:

*   **Wholesale Market:** Sales and purchases are conducted on a large scale. Wholesale traders buy goods in bulk from the market and sell them to retail traders. Wholesale traders serve as an important link between producers and retail traders. For example, a wholesale grain market.
*   **Retail Market:** Sales and purchases occur on a small scale. Retail traders are an important link, providing goods and services directly to customers. They buy goods in large quantities and sell them to customers in smaller quantities according to their demand and needs.
