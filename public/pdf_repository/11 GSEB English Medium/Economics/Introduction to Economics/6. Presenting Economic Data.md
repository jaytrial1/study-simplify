This section describes the different ways economic information or data can be presented to facilitate analysis and understanding. It outlines the three main methods: descriptive presentation, data tables, and graphical/diagrammatic representation, illustrating with examples like the relationship between price and demand.

# Presentation of Information (Data) in Economics

Economic information can be presented in three ways:
1.  **Descriptive manner**
2.  By the way of **data tables**
3.  By the way of **graphs (diagrams)**

*   **Descriptive example:** The relation between price and demand is stated as, "when price of a commodity falls, its demand expands and vice-versa".
*   **Data table example:**

| Price of the <br> Commodity <br> $(\boldsymbol{x})$ | Demand for the <br> Commodity by <br> Individual <br> (in units) |
| :--: | :--: |
| 20 | 20 |
| 15 | 40 |
| 10 | 60 |
| 5 | 80 |

This table also shows that as price falls, demand expands.
