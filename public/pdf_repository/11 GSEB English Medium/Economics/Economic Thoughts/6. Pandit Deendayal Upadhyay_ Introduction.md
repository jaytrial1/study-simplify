# Pandit Deendayal Upadhyay: Introduction

**Pandit Deendayal Upadhyay** is described as a profound philosopher, an integral humanist, and eminent in politics and economics. Born on September 25, 1916, he lived for only 52 years but dedicated his life to philosophizing and working for India's development, focusing on integrating people from the lowest strata into the economic progress, considering the economic conditions of his time.

He presented his ideas on philosophy, economics, sociology, and literature. He gained recognition for his work in social organization, journalism, and politics. Some of his notable writings include:
*   Rashtra Jivan Ki Samasyayen
*   Devaluation: A Great Fall
*   Political Diary
*   Rashtra Chintan
*   Integral Humanism
*   Rashtra Jivan Ki Disha
*   Bharatiya Arthniti: Vikas Ki Disha
*   Akhand Bharat Kyon?

His views on politics, economics, and society are found within these works.
