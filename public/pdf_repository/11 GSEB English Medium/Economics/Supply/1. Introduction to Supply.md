# Introduction to Supply

Demand and supply are equally important determinants of price. To fully understand the concept of demand, it is necessary to understand the concept of supply.

Considering the demand for various goods in the market, it becomes a function of the market to ensure the supply of these goods. Supply must be altered in accordance with changes in demand. Just as there is a different demand at every price, there also is a corresponding supply to every price.
