This section defines the supply function as a mathematical representation showing how the supply of a good is determined by its various factors.

# The Supply Function

A *supply function* specifies a functional (mathematical) relationship between the supply of a good and its determinants.

It represents that the supply of one good is determined by many factors. In other words, the supply function is a mathematical representation of the relationship of the supply of a good with its determinants.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{S}_{x} & =f\left(\mathrm{P}_{x}, \mathrm{~T}, \mathrm{P}_{\mathrm{F}}, \mathrm{P}_{e}, \mathrm{U}\right) \\
\text { Here, } \mathrm{S}_{x} & =\text { Supply of X commodity } \\
f & =\text { Functional Notation } \\
\mathrm{P}_{x} & =\text { Price of X commodity } \\
\mathrm{T} & =\text { Level of Technology } \\
\mathrm{P}_{\mathrm{F}} & =\text { Factor Prices } \\
\mathrm{P}_{e} & =\text { Expectations Regarding Future Prices } \\
\mathrm{U} & =\text { Other Factors }
\end{aligned}
$$
