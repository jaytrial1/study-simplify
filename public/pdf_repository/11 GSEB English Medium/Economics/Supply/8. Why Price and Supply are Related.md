This section explains the underlying reasons for the positive relationship between the price of a good and the quantity supplied by producers.

# Why Price and Supply are Related

There are two reasons for the positive relationship between price and supply:

1.  Sellers pursue the objective of *profit maximization*, and when price rises, they see the possibility of increasing profits. Thus, they supply more at a higher price.
2.  When price rises, those producers who were not willing to sell their stocks at a lower price start selling the stocks.
