# Introduction

In economic analysis, understanding the concepts of *cost* and *revenue* is crucial for comprehending the behavior of a firm. Changes in the quantity of production lead to changes in total cost. Cost represents the debit side, while revenue represents the credit side of the firm's operations. The concepts of revenue and cost help determine the optimal production level and serve several purposes:

*   They help firms decide the *price* of their commodity.
*   They provide guidelines to maximize the firm's *profit*.
*   *Marginal cost* is particularly useful in explaining profit maximization.
*   The concept of marginal cost helps in understanding the firm's behavior.
*   Concepts of cost and revenue play an important role in decisions regarding the amount of factors of production invested, employment levels, and overall production and investment.
*   The concept of *opportunity cost* is important in managerial economics for understanding the alternative uses of factors of production.
*   The concept of *monetary cost* is useful for guiding the administration of a firm.
