# The Difference between Sole Proprietorship and Partnership Firm
| Sr <br> No. | Basis of <br> Difference | Sole Proprietorship | Partnership Firm |
| :--: | :--: | :--: | :--: |
| (1) | Meaning | When a person wishes to establish <br> the business, arranges capital and <br> bears loss and profit by himself it is <br> called sole proprietorship. | Partnership is a relation between two <br> or more persons who have agreed to <br> run business by all, or by one, or on <br> behalf of others and agree to share <br> the profit-loss |
| (2) | Number of <br> members | There is only one person | Minimum two persons. According <br> to companies Act 2013 clause no 464, <br> Number of partners in partnership firm <br> cannot be more than 100 members. As <br> per Government decision According <br> to companies Act(Year 2014) clause <br> No. 10 (Miscellaneous Rule) number <br> of maximum partners in partnership <br> firm is 50 . |
| (3) | Capital | Capital is brought by the owner. | Partners bring capital as per the <br> partnership deed. |
| (4) | Establishment | Any person can establish a lawful <br> bussiness | Establishment is by oral agreement <br> or written agreement. |
| (5) | Decisions | In sole proprietorship, as there is only <br> one owner, decision can be taken <br> quickly. | In partnership firm, as there are more <br> than one partner, there is a delay in <br> taking decision. |
| (6) | Profit-Loss | Only the owner has to bear <br> Profit-Loss | Profit-Loss is distributed among the <br> partners as per partnership deed. |
| (7) | Registration | No provision for Registration. | As per partnership Act registration <br> can be done ; but not compulsory. |
| (8) | Maintainance of <br> secrecy | As there is only one owner secrecy <br> maintained. | As there are more than one partner <br> maintenance of secrecy is less. |
| (9) | Transfer of <br> Share | As and when owner decideds he can <br> transfer the share. | If there is consent of all partners then <br> only a partner can transfer his share. |
| (10) | Risk | Risks of business are borne by the <br> owner. | Risks of firm are distributed among <br> the partners. |
