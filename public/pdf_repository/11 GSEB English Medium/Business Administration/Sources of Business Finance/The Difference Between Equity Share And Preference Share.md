# The Difference between Equity Share and Preference Share
| Sr. No. | Basis of <br> difference | Equity Share | Preference Share |
| :--: | :--: | :--: | :--: |
| (1) | Compulsory | It is compulsory to issue <br> each company. | It is not compulsory to issue such <br> share by each company. |
| (2) | Rate of <br> Dividend | Rate of dividend is not fixed. | Rate of dividend is fixed. |
| (3) | Rights | There is a right to attend company's <br> meeting, to vote and elect managers. | There is right to vote only in <br> matters related to their interests. |
| (4) | Risk | Proportion of risk is more | Proportion of risk is less. |
| (5) | Investors | These shares are preferred more by <br> investors who wish to take risks and <br> speculate (gamble) in shares. | These shares are preferred more by <br> such investors who wish to get <br> fixed income and security of capital. |
| (6) | Market Price | There is increase-decrease in market <br> price of shares. | Proportionately the price of a share <br> remain fixed. No increase-decrease <br> in market price. |
| (7) | Addition in <br> capital | In this type of shares, capital can be <br> raised by right shares and bonus <br> shares. | Capital can not be raised. |
