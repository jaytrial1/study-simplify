# Difference Between Owner's fund and Borrowed Fund
| Sr. No. | Basis of <br> difference | Owner's fund | Borrowed fund |
| :--: | :--: | :--: | :--: |
| (1) | Meaning | Funds invested by shareholders is <br> called owner's fund. | Funds borrowed from other sources <br> and invested in business is called <br> borrowed fund. |
| (2) | Source | Can be received from equity shares, <br> ploughing back of profit, depreciation <br> fund and reserves. | Can be received from debenture <br> commercial banks and financial <br> institutions. |
| (3) | Return | Return is in form of dividend of <br> owner's fund. | Return is in form of interest at <br> fixed rate. |
| (4) | Rate of return | Rate of return is uncertain or not <br> fixed. | Rate of return is fixed. |
| (5) | Repayment of <br> capital | Owner's fund is repaid at last. | Repayment of borrowed funds is <br> made earliar than owner's fund. |
