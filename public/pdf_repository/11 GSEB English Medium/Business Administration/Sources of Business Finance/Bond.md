# Bond
Bond is an important source for long term financial requirements of the company. Bond is a borrowed capital for the company. Bond is a debt and bond holders are creditors of the company. They are paid interest at fixed rate and time.

The basic price of bond is usually more than debentures. Mostly these are issued by Government, Municipal corporation and Companies. Bonds are known by the name given in the issue e.g. Sardar Sarovar Bond.

Bonds are issued for a fixed time limit. Governments and Companies when want to implement large size project then to satisfy long term requirement bond are more suitable.
