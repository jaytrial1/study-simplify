# Distinguish Between Departmental Store and Chain Store 

| Sr. No. | Points of <br> Difference | Departmental Store | Chain Store |
| :--: | :--: | :--: | :--: |
| (1) | Objective | To supply all types of goods to <br> customers at the same place | To supply goods by reaching near <br> the customers at various places |
| (2) | Method of <br> sales | Sales of goods at the same place in <br> different departments | Sales at different places through <br> branches |
| (3) | Risk | Greater risk as the sales activity is <br> at the same place. | Comparitively risk is less as the <br> sales activity is at different places <br> by branches |
| (4) | Sales of goods | Sales of goods is either on cash <br> basis or instalment system | Sales of goods is on cash |
| (5) | Opportunity <br> of selections <br> for the <br> customers | Wider opportunity of selection to <br> customers as many types of <br> products are sold in departmental <br> stores | Less opportunity of selection as <br> limited products of the same class <br> are sold in the chain stores |
