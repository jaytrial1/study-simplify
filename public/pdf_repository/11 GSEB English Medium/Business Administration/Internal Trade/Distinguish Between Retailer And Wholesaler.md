# Distinguish between Retailer and Wholesaler

| Sr. No. | Points of <br> difference | Retaile Trade | Wholesale Trade |
| :--: | :--: | :--: | :--: |
| (1) | Capital <br> Investment | Less capital investment is <br> required for business | More capital investment is <br> required for business |
| (2) | Purchase-Sales | Purchase the good in small or <br> large quantity and sells to the <br> customers in retail as per <br> requirements | Purchase the goods in large <br> quantity and sells the good in <br> small quantity to the retailers |
| (3) | Distribution <br> channel | Connecting link between <br> customers and wholesalers | Connecting link between retailer <br> and producer |
| (4) | Sales of <br> products | Sells various products of many <br> producers or wholesalers | Sells the various products of <br> limited producers |
| (5) | Number of <br> traders | Comparatively retailers are more <br> in number | Comparatively wholesalers are <br> less in number |
