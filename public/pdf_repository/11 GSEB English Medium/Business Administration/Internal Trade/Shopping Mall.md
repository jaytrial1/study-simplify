# Shopping Mall
Shopping mall is developed due to change in departmental stores in morden time. The constructions of shopping mall is larger than super market. In which many small-big shops are commenced under the ownership of a single person or more than one person. Various types of branded goods of daily requirement and luxurious products are available to the customers together at the same place. Efforts are made to attract the customers through various types of proposals of sales promotion. Competition among identical products of various producer is the specific charactristic of shopping mall. So customers have wide opportunities of selection. Morden facilities such as refresment house, entertainment department for children, Wi-fi, auditorium etc; are in shopping mall.
