# Difference between Public Sector and Private Sector
| Sr. No. | Point of <br> difference | Public Sector | Private Sector |
| :--: | :--: | :--: | :--: |
| (1) | Ownership | Ownership lies with centre or state <br> or jointly owned by centre and state <br> government | Owned by individual or group of <br> individuals |
| (2) | Control | As it is owned by the government <br> it can be controlled easily by the <br> government | Through various laws government <br> can control the private sector. |
| (3) | Main objective | Main objective is welfare. | Generally the main objective <br> is profit |
| (4) | Profitability | Due to government control it is not <br> keen for profit maximisation | Private sector is keen for profit <br> maximisation |
| (5) | Nature | Government department, Public <br> Corporation and Government <br> Companies are examples of <br> public sector | Individual ownership, Partnership, <br> Hindu Undivided Family, <br> Co-Operative Society, Joint <br> Stock Companies are examples <br> of private sector. |
