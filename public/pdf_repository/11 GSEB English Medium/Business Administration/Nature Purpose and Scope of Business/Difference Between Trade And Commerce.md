# Difference between Trade and Commerce
| Sr. <br> No. | Points of <br> difference | Trade | Commerce |
| :--: | :--: | :--: | :--: |
| (1) | Meaning | Exchange of goods or <br> services in return <br> of money | Commerce includes trade <br> and auxiliary <br> services to trade |
| (2) | Scope | Scope of trade is limited | Scope of commerce is wider. <br> Trade is included in <br> commerce |
| (3) | Parties | Both the parties of trade <br> are mostly known to each <br> other and are in proximity. | Both the parties may be at distant <br> places and unknown, even though <br> they can obtain auxiliary services |
