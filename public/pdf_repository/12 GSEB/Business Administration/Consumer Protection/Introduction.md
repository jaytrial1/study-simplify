# Introduction

It is proven that in a free market, consumer is the king of the market. The earlier slogan that "Consumer should be aware" has changed to "Seller should be aware". It is found that to increase their share and to survive in the ever increasing competition, producers of goods and services adopt unethical, exploitative and unfair practices. Due to such malpractices, consumers are being cheated. Consumers are forced to pay a higher price and at the same time they are experiencing insecurity due to defective products, health hazards due to adulteration and also due to wrong and misleading advertisements, sales of duplicate products, black marketing and hoarding of goods. The needs to protect consumers from unethical practices are on the rise. Such consumers who fall prey to various malpractices tend to put their lives to danger by unknowingly consuming such products. This is absolutely not justifiable.
