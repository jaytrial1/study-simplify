# Exercises and Activities

1.  Answer the following questions.
    (i) What is agriculture?
    > **Answer:** Agriculture is a primary activity that includes growing crops, fruits, vegetables, flowers, and rearing of livestock.
    (ii) Name the factors influencing agriculture?
    > **Answer:** Favourable factors influencing agriculture include topography of soil and climate.
    (iii) What is shifting cultivation? What are its disadvantages?
    > **Answer:** Shifting cultivation is a primitive subsistence agriculture practice where a plot of land is cleared by felling and burning trees, crops are grown until soil fertility is lost, and then the land is abandoned for a new plot. The text does not explicitly list disadvantages, but implies environmental impact through burning and moving on when soil loses fertility.
    (iv) What is plantation agriculture?
    > **Answer:** Plantation agriculture is a type of commercial farming where a single crop like tea, coffee, sugarcane, cashew, rubber, banana or cotton is grown on a large scale, requiring large amounts of labour and capital.
    (v) Name the fibre crops and name the climatic conditions required for their growth.
    > **Answer:** The fibre crops mentioned are jute and cotton. Jute requires high temperature, heavy rainfall, and humid climate, growing well on alluvial soil in tropical areas. Cotton requires high temperature, light rainfall, two hundred and ten frost-free days, and bright sunshine, growing best on black and alluvial soils.

2.  Tick the correct answer.
    (i) Horticulture means
    (a) growing of fruits and vegetables
    (b) primitive farming
    (c) growing of wheat
    > **Answer:** (a) growing of fruits and vegetables
    (ii) Golden fibre refers to
    (a) tea
    (b) cotton
    (c) jute
    > **Answer:** (c) jute
    (iii) Leading producers of coffee
    (a) Brazil
    (b) India
    (c) Russia
    > **Answer:** (a) Brazil

3.  Give reasons.
    (i) In India agriculture is a primary activity.
    > **Answer:** Agriculture is considered a primary activity because it is directly involved with the extraction and production of natural resources, such as growing crops from the land.
    (ii) Different crops are grown in different regions.
    > **Answer:** Different crops are grown in different regions because agricultural activity is concentrated in areas where suitable factors for growing those specific crops exist, such as favourable topography, soil type, and climate (temperature, rainfall, sunshine).

4.  Distinguish between the followings.
    (i) Primary activities and tertiary activities
    > **Answer:** Primary activities are concerned with the extraction and production of natural resources (like agriculture, fishing, gathering), while tertiary activities provide support services to primary and secondary sectors (like transport, trade, banking, insurance, advertising).
    (ii) Subsistence farming and intensive farming.
    > **Answer:** Subsistence farming is practiced to meet the needs of the farmer's family using low technology and household labour for small output. Intensive farming is a type of subsistence farming where a farmer cultivates a small plot using simple tools and more labour, often growing more than one crop annually due to favourable climate and fertile soil.

    (ii) Find out the difference between the life style of farmers in the USA and India on the basis of pictures collected from magazines, books, newspapers and the Internet.
