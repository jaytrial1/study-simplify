# Understanding the Farm System

Agriculture, or farming, can be viewed as a **system**.

### Inputs

Important inputs for farming include:

*   Seeds
*   Fertilisers
*   Machinery
*   Labour

### Processes

Some of the operations involved in the farm system are:

*   Ploughing
*   Sowing
*   Irrigation
*   Weeding
*   Harvesting

### Outputs

The outputs produced by the farm system include:

*   Crops
*   Wool
*   Dairy products
*   Poultry products
