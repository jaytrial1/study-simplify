This section introduces the concept of economic activities and their classification into primary, secondary, and tertiary sectors, using the example of wheat production.

# Introduction to Economic Activities

The process of transforming a plant, like wheat, into a finished product, such as bread or biscuits, involves three types of **economic activities**:

*   **Primary activities:** These are connected with the extraction and production of natural resources. Examples include *agriculture*, *fishing*, and *gathering*.
*   **Secondary activities:** These involve the processing of natural resources. Examples include *manufacturing of steel*, *baking of bread*, and *weaving of cloth*.
*   **Tertiary activities:** These provide support services to the primary and secondary sectors. Examples include *transport*, *trade*, *banking*, *insurance*, and *advertising*.

Agriculture is classified as a **primary activity**.
