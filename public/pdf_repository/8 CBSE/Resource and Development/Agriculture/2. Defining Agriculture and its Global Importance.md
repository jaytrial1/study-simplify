# Defining Agriculture and its Global Importance

**Agriculture** is a primary activity that includes growing crops, fruits, vegetables, flowers, and rearing livestock. Globally, 50 percent of people are involved in agricultural activity. In India, two-thirds of the population still depends on agriculture.

Favourable conditions like **topography**, **soil**, and **climate** are vital for agricultural activity. The land used for growing crops is known as **arable land**.

### Key Terms Related to Agriculture

Different specialized fields within agriculture are identified by adding prefixes to "culture":

*   **Agriculture:** Science and art of cultivating the soil, raising crops, and rearing livestock.
*   **Sericulture:** Commercial rearing of silk worms.
*   **Pisciculture:** Breeding of fish in tanks and ponds.
*   **Viticulture:** Cultivation of grapes.
*   **Horticulture:** Cultivation of vegetables, flowers, and fruits for commercial use.
