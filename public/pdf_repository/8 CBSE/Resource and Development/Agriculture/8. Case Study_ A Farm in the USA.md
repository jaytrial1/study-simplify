# Case Study: A Farm in the USA

Farms in the USA are typically much larger than in India, with an average size of about 250 hectares. Farmers often live on the farm. Major crops include corn, soybean, wheat, cotton, and sugarbeet.

Joe Horan, a farmer in the Midwest USA (Iowa State), owns about 300 hectares.

*   He grows corn, ensuring soil and water meet the crop's needs.
*   He takes measures to control pests.
*   He sends soil samples to a testing laboratory to check nutrient levels and plan a scientific fertiliser program.
*   His computer is linked to a satellite, providing a precise picture of his field, which helps him use chemical fertilisers and pesticides precisely where needed.
