# Case Study: A Farm in India

Munna Lal is a small farmer in Adilabad village, Ghazipur district, Uttar Pradesh, India, with about 1.5 hectares of farmland. His house is in the main village.

*   He buys **high yielding varieties (HYV)** of seeds every alternate year.
*   His land is fertile, allowing him to grow at least two crops annually, usually wheat or rice and pulses.
*   He seeks advice from friends, elders, and government agricultural officers.
*   He rents a tractor for ploughing, though some neighbours still use bullocks.
*   He rents a tubewell in a nearby field for irrigation.

Munna Lal also owns two buffaloes and a few hens. He sells milk to a cooperative store in the nearby town. He is a member of a co-operative society which advises him on animal fodder, livestock health safety measures, and artificial insemination.

His family members help with farm activities. He sometimes takes credit from a bank or the agricultural co-operative society to buy HYV seeds and implements.

He sells his produce in the mandi (market) in the nearby town. Many farmers lack storage facilities, forcing them to sell produce even when market prices are low. The government has recently taken steps to develop storage facilities.
