# Major Crops of the World

A wide variety of crops are cultivated to meet the needs of the growing population and supply raw materials for agro-based industries.

Major crop types include:

*   **Food crops:** Wheat, rice, maize, millets.
*   **Fibre crops:** Jute, cotton.
*   **Beverage crops:** Tea, coffee.

### Rice

*   Major food crop and staple diet in tropical and sub-tropical regions.
*   Requires high temperature, high humidity, and rainfall.
*   Grows best in alluvial clayey soil that retains water.
*   Leading producers: China, India, Japan, Sri Lanka, Egypt.
*   In favorable conditions (e.g., West Bengal, Bangladesh), two to three crops can be grown annually.

### Wheat

*   Requires moderate temperature and rainfall during the growing season and bright sunshine at harvest time.
*   Thrives best in well-drained loamy soil.
*   Grown extensively in USA, Canada, Argentina, Russia, Ukraine, Australia, and India.
*   In India, it is grown in winter.

### Millets

*   Also known as **coarse grains**.
*   Can be grown on less fertile and sandy soils.
*   A hardy crop needing low rainfall, high to moderate temperature, and adequate rainfall.
*   Jowar, bajra, and ragi are grown in India.
*   Other producing countries include Nigeria, China, and Niger.

### Maize

*   Requires moderate temperature, rainfall, and lots of sunshine.
*   Needs well-drained fertile soils.
*   Grown in North America, Brazil, China, Russia, Canada, India, and Mexico.
*   Also known as **corn**. Various colourful varieties are found globally.

### Cotton

*   Requires high temperature, light rainfall, 210 frost-free days, and bright sunshine.
*   Grows best on black and alluvial soils.
*   Leading producers: China, USA, India, Pakistan, Brazil, Egypt.
*   A main raw material for the cotton textile industry.

### Jute

*   Also known as the **'Golden Fibre'**.
*   Grows well on alluvial soil.
*   Requires high temperature, heavy rainfall, and a humid climate.
*   Grown in tropical areas.
*   Leading producers: India and Bangladesh.

### Coffee

*   Requires warm and wet climate and well-drained loamy soil.
*   Hill slopes are more suitable for growth.
*   Leading producers: Brazil, followed by Columbia and India.

### Tea

*   A beverage crop grown on plantations.
*   Requires cool climate and well-distributed high rainfall throughout the year for tender leaf growth.
*   Needs well-drained loamy soils and gentle slopes.
*   Requires a large number of labourers to pick the leaves.
*   Leading producers of best quality tea: Kenya, India, China, Sri Lanka.
