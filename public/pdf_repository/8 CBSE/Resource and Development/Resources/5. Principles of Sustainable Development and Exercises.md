# Principles of Sustainable Development and Exercises

## Some Principles of Sustainable Development

*   Respect and care for all forms of life
*   Improve the quality of human life
*   Conserve the earth's vitality and diversity
*   Minimise the depletion of natural resources
*   Change personal attitude and practices towards the environment
*   Enable communities to care for their own environment.

## Exercises

1. Answer the following questions.
(i) Why are resources distributed unequally over the earth?
(ii) What is resource conservation?
(iii) Why are human resources important?
(iv) What is sustainable development?

> **Answer:**
> (i) Resources are distributed unequally over the earth because the physical factors like terrain, climate, and altitude vary greatly from place to place. Different combinations of these factors lead to different types and amounts of resources being available in different regions.
> (ii) Resource conservation is the practice of using resources carefully and giving them time to get renewed or replenished. It involves managing resources wisely so they are not wasted or depleted too quickly.
> (iii) Human resources are important because people have the knowledge, skill, and technology needed to identify, extract, process, and create new resources from natural substances. Without human effort and ingenuity, many natural substances would not become useful resources.
> (iv) Sustainable development is balancing the need to use resources for current needs with the need to conserve them for future generations. It means using resources in a way that meets present needs without compromising the ability of future generations to meet their own needs.

3. Activity
"Rahiman paani raakhiye,
Bin paani sab soon.
Paani gaye na ubere
Moti, manus, choon..."
[Says Rahim, keep water, as without water there is nothing. Without water pearl, swan and dough cannot exist. ]

These lines were written by the poet Abdur Rahim Khankhana, one of the nine gems of Akbar's court. What kind of resource is the poet referring to? Write in 100 words what would happen if this resource disappeared?

> **Answer:** The poet is referring to **water**.
> If water disappeared, life as we know it would cease to exist. Water is essential for all living beings – humans, animals, and plants. Without it, crops would wither, rivers and lakes would dry up, and ecosystems would collapse. Humans would face severe thirst and starvation. Industries and agriculture, which rely heavily on water, would shut down. The Earth would become a barren, lifeless planet, similar to the nightmare Mona had. The lines about pearl, swan, and dough illustrate how even things seemingly unrelated to basic survival depend on water.

## For Fun

1. Pretend that you live in the prehistoric times on a high windy plateau. What are the uses you and your friends could put the fast winds to? Can you call the wind a resource?
Now imagine that you are living in the same place in the year 2138. Can you put the winds to any use? How? Can you explain why the wind is an important resource now?

> **Answer:**
> **Prehistoric Times:** On a windy plateau, prehistoric people might use the wind for simple things like drying clothes or food faster, scattering seeds, or perhaps using rudimentary sails on rafts if there was water nearby. They might feel its force and coolness. Yes, they could call the wind a resource because it has some utility for them, even if limited.
> **Year 2138:** In 2138, living on a windy plateau, we would use the wind primarily to generate electricity through advanced wind turbines. This clean energy could power homes, vehicles, and industries. We might also use wind patterns for advanced weather prediction or even wind-powered transportation systems. The wind is a much more important resource now because technology has allowed us to harness its energy on a large scale to meet significant societal needs like electricity production, which were not possible in prehistoric times.
