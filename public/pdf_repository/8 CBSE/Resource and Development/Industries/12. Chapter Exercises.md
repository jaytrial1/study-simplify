# Exercises
This section contains review questions and activities related to the chapter content.

1.  Answer the following questions.
    (i) What is meant by the term 'industry'?
    > **Answer:** Industry refers to an economic activity that is concerned with the production of goods, the extraction of minerals, or the provision of services.
    (ii) Which are the main factors which influence the location of an industry?
    > **Answer:** The main factors influencing the location of an industry are the availability of raw material, land, water, labour, power, capital, transport, and market.
    (iii) Which industry is often referred to as the backbone of modern industry and why?
    > **Answer:** The iron and steel industry is often referred to as the backbone of modern industry because almost everything we use is either made of iron or steel or has been made using tools and machinery made from these metals. It provides basic infrastructure for many other industries.

2.  Distinguish between the following.
    (i) Agro-based and mineral based industry
    > **Answer:** Agro-based industries use plant and animal products as raw materials (e.g., food processing, cotton textiles), while mineral-based industries use mineral ores as raw materials (e.g., iron and steel, cement). Products of mineral-based industries often serve as raw materials for other industries.
    (ii) Public sector and joint sector industry
    > **Answer:** Public sector industries are owned and operated entirely by the government (e.g., SAIL), whereas joint sector industries are owned and operated jointly by the state (government) and individuals or a group of individuals (e.g., Maruti Udyog Limited was an example).
