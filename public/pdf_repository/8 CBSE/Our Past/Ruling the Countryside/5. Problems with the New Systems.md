This section describes the negative consequences that arose after the implementation of the Mahalwari and Ryotwari systems.

# Problems with the New Systems

Within a few years of implementing the new Mahalwari and Ryotwari systems, it became evident that they were not working well.

*   **Excessive Revenue Demand:** Driven by the desire to increase land income, revenue officials fixed the revenue demand too high.
*   **Peasant Distress:** Peasants found it impossible to pay the high demands.
*   **Flight from Villages:** Many *ryots* fled the countryside, leading to villages becoming deserted in many regions.

The optimistic vision of officials that these new systems would transform peasants into prosperous, enterprising farmers did not materialise.
