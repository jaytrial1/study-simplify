This section details the Company's decision to introduce the Permanent Settlement to improve agriculture and secure revenue, and its key features.

# The Permanent Settlement

Facing an economy in ruins and uncertain revenue income, many Company officials felt that investment in land was necessary and agriculture needed improvement. After about twenty years of discussion, the Company introduced the **Permanent Settlement** in 1793.

Under the Permanent Settlement:

*   *Rajas* and *taluqdars* were recognised as **zamindars**.
*   Zamindars were tasked with collecting rent from peasants and paying revenue to the Company.
*   The amount of revenue to be paid was fixed permanently, meaning it would not increase in the future.

The Company believed this system would:

*   Ensure a regular flow of revenue into its treasury.
*   Encourage zamindars to invest in improving the land, as they would benefit from increased production without facing higher revenue demands from the state.
