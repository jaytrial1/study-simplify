# How did Colonial Rule Affect Tribal Lives?

The lives of tribal groups changed significantly under British rule.

## What happened to tribal chiefs?

Before the British, tribal chiefs held considerable economic power and administered their territories, sometimes having their own police and making rules for land and forest management. Under British rule, their powers changed. They kept their land titles and could rent out land, but lost administrative authority, being forced to follow British laws. They had to pay tribute to the British and discipline their own people on the British's behalf. This eroded their traditional authority and ability to perform their former roles.

## What happened to the shifting cultivators?

The British disliked mobile groups without fixed homes, preferring settled peasant cultivators who were easier to control and administer, and who provided a regular revenue source. They introduced **land settlements**, measuring land, defining individual rights, and fixing revenue demands. Some tribals became landowners, others tenants who paid rent to landowners, who in turn paid revenue to the state.

However, settling jhum cultivators was difficult, especially where water was scarce and soil dry. Plough cultivation often resulted in poor yields for them. Jhum cultivators in north-east India resisted, and due to widespread protests, the British eventually allowed shifting cultivation in some forest areas.
