### Let's Discuss

3. Why did William Jones feel the need to study Indian history, philosophy and law?
> **Answer:** William Jones felt the need to study Indian history, philosophy, and law because he had a deep respect for ancient cultures. He believed that to truly understand India and form the basis for its future development, it was necessary to discover and study the ancient texts that revealed the real ideas and laws of Hindus and Muslims. He saw it as a way for the British to learn from Indian culture and help Indians rediscover their own heritage.

4. Why did James Mill and Thomas Macaulay think that European education was essential in India?
> **Answer:** James Mill and Thomas Macaulay thought European education was essential because they believed Eastern knowledge was full of errors and unscientific. Mill thought education should be useful and practical, introducing Indians to Western science and technology. Macaulay saw India as uncivilised and believed English education was a way to civilise people, change their tastes and values, and make them aware of Western developments in science and philosophy.

5. Why did Mahatma Gandhi want to teach children handicrafts?
> **Answer:** Mahatma Gandhi wanted to teach children handicrafts because he believed education should develop a person's mind and soul, not just literacy. Learning a craft and working with their hands would help children understand how things operate and develop their minds and capabilities. He felt it was a way to connect education to practical life and make students self-reliant.

6. Why did Mahatma Gandhi think that English education had enslaved Indians?
> **Answer:** Mahatma Gandhi thought English education had enslaved Indians because he felt it created a sense of inferiority, making them admire Western civilisation and despise their own culture. It distanced them from their social surroundings, made them "strangers in their own lands" who couldn't relate to the masses, and charmed them into admiring British rule, thus making them willing to be enslaved.
