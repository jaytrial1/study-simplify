# Introduction to British Educational Policies in India

In earlier chapters, you have seen how British rule affected rajas and nawabs, peasants and tribals. In this chapter, we will try and understand what implication it had for the lives of students. For, the British in India wanted not only territorial conquest and control over revenues. They also felt that they had a cultural mission: they had to "**civilise the natives**", change their customs and values.

What changes were to be introduced? How were Indians to be educated, "civilised", and made into what the British believed were "**good subjects**"? The British could find no simple answers to these questions. They continued to be debated for many decades.
