## How Do New Laws Come About?

The Parliament has an important role in making laws. There are many ways through which this takes place and it is often different groups in society that raise the need for a particular law. An important role of Parliament is to be sensitive to the problems faced by people. State one reason why you think the Sedition Act of 1870 was arbitrary?
> **Answer:** The Sedition Act of 1870 was considered arbitrary because it allowed the British government to arrest persons without needing absolute evidence and for reasons that were not clearly defined beforehand. This gave the authorities excessive power to detain anyone they suspected of stirring up resistance, often without a fair trial.

In what ways does the Sedition Act of 1870 contradict the rule of law?
> **Answer:** The Sedition Act of 1870 contradicts the rule of law because the rule of law states that laws should be clear, known, and applied equally to everyone, and that no one should be punished unless they have violated a clear law. The Sedition Act was vaguely defined, giving arbitrary power to the government to arrest people without proper evidence or trial, which goes against the principles of fairness, equality, and due process under the law.

Domestic violence generally refers to the injury or harm or threat of injury or harm caused by an adult male, usually the husband, against his wife. Injury may be caused by physically beating up the woman or by emotionally abusing her. Abuse of the woman can also include verbal, sexual and economic abuse. The Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act 2005 extends the understanding of the term 'domestic' to include all women who 'live or have lived together in a shared household' with the male member who is perpetrating the violence.
