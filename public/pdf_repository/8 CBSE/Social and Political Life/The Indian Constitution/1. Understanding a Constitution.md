# Understanding a Constitution
This section introduces the concept of a constitution using analogies and defines what a constitution is in the context of a society and modern countries.

In this chapter, we begin by thinking about the rules of games like football. Just as games have rules that define them and distinguish them from others, a society also has fundamental rules that make it what it is. These are like the *constitutive rules* of a game. In large societies with different communities living together, these rules are agreed upon through consensus. In modern countries, this consensus is often written down in a document called a **Constitution**.

We have encountered the Indian Constitution in previous classes. This chapter will explore why a constitution is needed, how the Indian Constitution was written, who wrote it, and its key features. These features are essential for democracy in India.
