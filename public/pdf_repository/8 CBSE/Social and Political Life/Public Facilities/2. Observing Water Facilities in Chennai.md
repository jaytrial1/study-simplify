Why does water flow in a trickle in summer in most places in India? Find out.
> **Answer:** Water flow often reduces to a trickle in summer in many places in India because this is typically the dry season. Rivers, lakes, and groundwater levels, which are primary sources of water, decrease significantly due to less rainfall and increased evaporation. The demand for water also increases in summer for irrigation, cooling, and drinking, putting further strain on the limited supply.

Discuss: Is there a general shortage of water for everyone in Chennai? Can you think of two reasons why different people get varying amounts of water?
> **Answer:** Yes, there appears to be a general shortage of municipal water supply for everyone in Chennai, as the text mentions the municipal supply meets only about half the city's needs on average. Two reasons why different people get varying amounts of water could be: 1) **Location:** Areas closer to water storage and distribution points might receive more regular supply than those further away. 2) **Socioeconomic Status/Ability to Pay:** Wealthier residents can afford to supplement inadequate municipal supply by digging private borewells, buying water from private tankers, or purchasing bottled water, while poorer residents are limited to public taps or waiting for subsidized tankers, often receiving much less water.
