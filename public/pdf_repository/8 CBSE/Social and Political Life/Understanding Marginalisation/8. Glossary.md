This section provides definitions for key terms used in the chapter.

# Glossary

**Displaced:** In this chapter, this refers to people who are forced or compelled to move from their homes due to large development projects such as dams or mining.

**Hierarchy:** A system or arrangement where people or things are ranked one above another based on status or authority. In a hierarchy, those at the bottom usually have the least power. The caste system is an example of a hierarchical system.

**Ghettoisation:** The process by which a particular community becomes concentrated in a specific area or locality. This can happen due to social, cultural, or economic reasons, or sometimes out of fear or hostility, leading the community to group together for security. Often, communities in such areas have limited options to move elsewhere and may become isolated from the rest of society.

**Mainstream:** Literally, the main current of a river. In this chapter, it refers to the dominant cultural context where customs and practices are primarily those of the majority community. It also refers to the powerful or dominant group considered to be at the centre of society.

**Militarised:** An area where there is a significant presence of armed forces.

**Malnourished:** A person who does not receive adequate nutrition or food, leading to health problems.
