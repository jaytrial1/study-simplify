This section provides an interesting fact about reproduction within a honey bee colony, highlighting the role of the queen bee and the development of males and females from fertilised and unfertilised eggs.

# Did You Know?

An interesting organisation is observed in a honey bee hive, a colony of several thousand bees. Only one bee in the colony lays eggs. This bee is called the **queen bee**. All other female bees are worker bees. Their main job is to build the hive, look after the young and feed the queen bee adequately to keep her healthy so that she can lay eggs. A queen bee lays thousands of eggs. The fertilised eggs hatch into females, while the unfertilised eggs give rise to males, called drones. It is the job of the worker bees to maintain the temperature of the hive at around $35^{\circ} \mathrm{C}$ to incubate the eggs.
