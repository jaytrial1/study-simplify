# Characteristics of Synthetic Fibres

Imagine that it is a rainy day. What kind of umbrella would you use and why? Synthetic fibres possess unique characteristics which make them popular dress materials. They dry up quickly, are durable, less expensive, readily available and easy to maintain. Perform the following activity and learn for yourself.

## Activity 3.2: Comparing Water Absorption and Drying

Take two cloth pieces of the same size, roughly half a metre square each. One of these should be from natural fibre. The other could be a synthetic fibre. You can take help of your parents in selecting these pieces. Soak the pieces in different mugs each containing the same amount of water. Take the pieces out of the containers after five minutes and spread them in the sun for a few minutes. Compare the volume of the water remaining in each container.

Do synthetic fabrics soak less/more water than the natural fabrics? Do they take less/more time to dry?

> **Answer:** Synthetic fabrics generally soak less water than natural fabrics. They also take less time to dry.

What does this activity tell you about the characteristics of the synthetic fabrics?

> **Answer:** This activity shows that synthetic fabrics are less absorbent and dry faster than natural fabrics.

Find out from your parents about the durability, cost and maintenance of these fabrics, compared to the natural fabrics.

> **Answer:** Parents or grandparents would likely confirm that synthetic fabrics are often more durable, less expensive, and easier to maintain (e.g., less wrinkling, easier washing) compared to natural fabrics like cotton or wool.
