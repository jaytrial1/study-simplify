5. A pendulum oscillates 40 times in 4 seconds. Find its time period and frequency.
> **Answer:**
> Frequency = Number of oscillations / Time taken = 40 / 4 seconds = 10 Hz.
> Time period = 1 / Frequency = 1 / 10 Hz = 0.1 seconds.
> The time period is 0.1 seconds and the frequency is 10 Hz.

6. The sound from a mosquito is produced when it vibrates its wings at an average rate of 500 vibrations per second. What is the time period of the vibration?
> **Answer:**
> Frequency = 500 vibrations per second = 500 Hz.
> Time period = 1 / Frequency = 1 / 500 Hz = 0.002 seconds.
> The time period of the vibration is 0.002 seconds.

8. What is the difference between noise and music? Can music become noise sometimes?
> **Answer:** Music is sound that is pleasant to the ear, often characterized by regular patterns and harmonious tones. Noise is sound that is unpleasant, unwanted, or jarring, often irregular and chaotic. Yes, music can become noise sometimes, especially if it is played at an excessively loud volume.

9. List sources of noise pollution in your surroundings.
> **Answer:** Sources of noise pollution in surroundings can include: vehicles (cars, trucks, buses), construction work (drilling, hammering), loudspeakers (from events, shops), factories, generators, barking dogs, loud music from neighbours, aircraft, bursting crackers.

10. Explain in what way noise pollution is harmful to human.
> **Answer:** Noise pollution is harmful to humans as it can cause health problems such as lack of sleep, increased blood pressure (hypertension), anxiety, stress, and headache. Continuous exposure to loud noise can also lead to temporary or permanent hearing impairment.

11. Your parents are going to buy a house. They have been offered one on the roadside and another three lanes away from the roadside. Which house would you suggest your parents should buy? Explain your answer.
> **Answer:** I would suggest my parents buy the house three lanes away from the roadside. This is because houses directly on the roadside are exposed to much higher levels of noise pollution from traffic (horns, engines, etc.). A house further away will experience significantly less noise pollution, leading to a quieter environment, better sleep, and reduced risk of health problems associated with excessive noise.

12. Sketch larynx and explain its function in your own words.
> **Answer:**
> (A sketch would show the voice box located at the top of the windpipe with two vocal cords stretched across it, leaving a narrow gap.)
> The larynx, or voice box, is the part of the throat where sound is produced in humans. It contains two vocal cords. When air from the lungs is forced through the narrow gap between the vocal cords, the cords vibrate. These vibrations produce sound. Muscles attached to the vocal cords can change their tension and the size of the gap, which changes the pitch and quality of the sound produced.

13. Lightning and thunder take place in the sky at the same time and at the same distance from us. Lightning is seen earlier and thunder is heard later. Can you explain why?
> **Answer:** Lightning is seen earlier than thunder is heard because light travels much, much faster than sound. Both events (the lightning flash and the thunder sound) happen at the same time during a thunderstorm. However, the light from the lightning reaches our eyes almost instantaneously, while the sound waves from the thunder take a noticeable amount of time to travel through the air to our ears.
