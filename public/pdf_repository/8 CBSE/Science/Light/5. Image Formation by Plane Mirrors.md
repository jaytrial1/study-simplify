# Image Formation by Plane Mirrors

As demonstrated in Activity 16.3, a plane mirror forms a virtual image behind the mirror. You may recall from Class VII that the image formed by a plane mirror has specific features:
*   The image is **erect** (not upside down).
*   The image is of the **same size** as the object.
*   The image appears at the **same distance behind the mirror** as the object was in front of it.
*   The image **cannot be obtained on a screen** (it is virtual).
*   In an image formed by a mirror, the left of the object appears on the right and the right appears on the left. This is known as **lateral inversion**.
