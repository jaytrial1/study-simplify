# Extended Learning - Activities and Projects

1. Prepare Index Cards for any four metals and four non-metals. The card should have information like name of metal/non-metal; its physical properties, chemical properties and its uses.
2. Visit a blacksmith and observe how metals are moulded.
3. Suggest an experiment to compare the conductivity of electricity by iron, copper, aluminium and zinc. Perform the experiment and prepare a short report on the results.
4. Find out the locations of the deposits of iron, aluminium and zinc in India. Mark these in an outline map of India. In which form are the deposits found? Discuss in the class.
5. Discuss with your parents/neighbours/goldsmiths why gold is preferred for making jewellery.
