# Materials: Metals and Non-Metals

You are familiar with a number of materials like iron, aluminium, copper, etc. Some materials have been given in Table 4.1.

Table 4.1 : Appearance and Hardness of Materials

| Object/ <br> Material | Appearance <br> (Shiny/Dull) | Hardness <br> (Very hard/ <br> Not very <br> hard) |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Iron | Shiny | Very hard |
| Coal | Dull | Not very hard |
| Sulphur | Dull | Not very hard |
| Aluminium | Shiny | Very hard |
| Copper | Shiny | Very hard |

> **Answer to Table 4.1:** Based on the general properties discussed later in the chapter, the table is completed as shown above. Iron, Aluminium, and Copper are generally shiny and very hard. Coal and Sulphur are generally dull and not very hard.

Can you name the materials which are metals? The rest of the materials in Table 4.1 are non-metals. Metals can be distinguished from non-metals on the basis of their physical and chemical properties. Recall that lustre and hardness are physical properties.

> **Answer:** Based on the definition provided later in the chapter, the materials that are generally hard, lustrous, malleable, ductile, sonorous, and good conductors are metals. From the list, Iron, Aluminium, and Copper are metals.
