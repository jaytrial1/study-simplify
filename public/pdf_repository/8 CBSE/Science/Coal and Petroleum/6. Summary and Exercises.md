# Exercises

1.  What are the advantages of using CNG and LPG as fuels?
    > **Answer:** CNG and LPG are considered cleaner fuels compared to coal and petroleum. CNG is less polluting and can be supplied directly through pipes, making it easy to use in homes and factories. Both produce less harmful emissions upon burning.

2.  Name the petroleum product used for surfacing of roads.
    > **Answer:** Bitumen is the petroleum product used for surfacing of roads.

3.  Describe how coal is formed from dead vegetation. What is this process called?
    > **Answer:** Coal was formed about 300 million years ago from dense forests buried under soil due to natural processes like flooding. As more soil accumulated, the buried vegetation was compressed, and temperature increased with depth. Under high pressure and temperature, the dead plants slowly converted into coal. This slow process of conversion of dead vegetation into coal is called carbonisation.

    (a) Fossil fuels are $\qquad$ and $\qquad$ .
    (b) Process of separation of different constituents from petroleum is called $\qquad$ .
    (c) Least polluting fuel for vehicle is $\qquad$ .

    > **Answer:**
    > (a) Fossil fuels are **coal**, **petroleum** and **natural gas**.
    > (b) Process of separation of different constituents from petroleum is called **refining**.
    > (c) Least polluting fuel for vehicle is **CNG**.

6.  Explain why fossil fuels are exhaustible natural resources.
    > **Answer:** Fossil fuels are exhaustible natural resources because they are present in limited quantities in nature. Their formation process took millions of years, but the rate at which they are being consumed by humans is much faster than the rate of their formation. At current rates of usage, known reserves will be depleted in a few hundred years.

7.  Describe characteristics and uses of coke.
    > **Answer:** Coke is a tough, porous, and black substance. It is an almost pure form of carbon. Coke is used in the manufacture of steel and in the extraction of many metals.

8.  Explain the process of formation of petroleum.
    > **Answer:** Petroleum was formed from the remains of tiny organisms that lived in the sea millions of years ago. When these organisms died, their bodies settled at the bottom of the sea. Over time, these remains were covered by layers of sand and clay. Under conditions of high pressure, high temperature, and the absence of air, the dead organisms were slowly transformed into petroleum and natural gas over millions of years.
