This section provides suggestions for activities and projects to further explore astronomy concepts.

# Extended Learning - Activities and Projects

1.  **The North-South line at your place:** Learn to draw a North-South line using the shadow of a stick. Fix a vertical stick (point O). Mark the tip of the morning shadow (point A). Draw a circle with radius OA centered at O. As the shadow shortens and then lengthens, mark where it touches the circle again (point B). Draw the bisector of angle AOB. This is the North-South line. Use a magnetic compass to confirm which side is North.

2.  **Visit a planetarium:** If possible, visit a planetarium to see simulations of stars, constellations, and planets on a large dome.
3.  **Observe meteors:** On a clear, moonless night, watch for meteors (shooting stars). September-November is a good time.
4.  **Identify celestial objects:** Learn to identify naked-eye planets and prominent constellations like Ursa Major and Orion. Try to locate the Pole Star and Sirius.
5.  **Position of the rising Sun - Uttarayan and Dakshinayan:** Observe the sunrise point over several weeks from a place with a clear eastern horizon, using a marker like a tree. Note the direction of sunrise weekly. You will see it shifts. From summer solstice (around June 21), it shifts south (dakshinayan) until winter solstice (around December 22). Then it shifts north (uttarayan). The Sun rises exactly east only on equinox days (around March 21 and September 23). The rising Sun's direction is not a reliable guide for direction; the Pole Star is better.
6.  **Model of the solar system:** Form a group and create a scale model of the solar system showing planets and their relative sizes using materials like newspaper, clay, or plasticine, based on provided data (Table 17.1). Exhibit the models.
7.  **Scale model of distances:** Try making a scale model showing planet distances from the Sun (Table 17.1). Note any difficulties faced, likely due to the immense relative distances.
8.  **Astronomy riddle:** Solve the riddle: "My first is in VAN but not in PAN / My second is in EARTH and also in HEAVEN / My third is in ONE and not in TWO / My fourth is in BUN and also in FUN / My last is in STAR but not in RADAR / I am a planet that moves round the Sun." (The answer is VENUS). Try making similar riddles.
