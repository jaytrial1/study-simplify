**Q: Is there a change in the shape of the moon everyday?**
> **Answer:** Yes, there is a change in the shape of the moon everyday.

**Q: Are there days when the shape of the moon appears to be perfectly round?**
> **Answer:** Yes, there are days when the shape of the moon appears to be perfectly round.

**Q: Are there days when the moon cannot be seen at all even if the sky is clear?**
> **Answer:** Yes, there are days when the moon cannot be seen at all even if the sky is clear.

The day on which the whole disc of the moon is visible is known as the **full moon day**. After this, the bright part of the moon appears thinner each night. On the fifteenth day, the moon is not visible; this is the **new moon day**. The day after the new moon, a small portion appears as a **crescent moon**. The visible part then grows larger daily until the next full moon.

The various shapes of the bright part of the moon seen during a month are called **phases of the moon**.

**Q: Why does the moon change its shape every day?**
> **Answer:** The moon changes its shape every day because we only see the part of the moon that reflects sunlight towards us, and the amount of this illuminated part visible from Earth changes as the moon orbits the Earth.

The moon does not produce its own light, unlike the Sun and other stars. We see the moon because sunlight falling on it is reflected towards us. We only see the part of the moon from which the Sun's light is reflected towards us.

**Q: Can you now guess the relative positions of the Sun, moon and the Earth on the day of the full moon and on the day of the new moon? Sketch these positions in your notebook.**
> **Answer:** On the full moon day, the Earth is between the Sun and the moon, with the side of the moon facing Earth fully illuminated by the Sun. On the new moon day, the moon is between the Sun and the Earth, with the side of the moon facing Earth not illuminated by the Sun.

**Q: In which part of the sky would you look for the full moon?**
> **Answer:** On the full moon day, the moon rises in the east around sunset and sets in the west around sunrise. So, you would look for it in the eastern sky after sunset and in the western sky before sunrise.

The size of the illuminated part of the moon visible from Earth increases daily after the new moon day. After the full moon day, the visible sunlit part decreases daily.

**Q: I have heard that we never see the back side of the moon from the Earth. Is it true?**
> **Answer:** Yes, it is true. We always see the same side of the moon from the Earth.

## Activity: Moon's Rotation and Revolution

Draw a circle of about 1m diameter on the ground. Ask a friend to stand at the center. You revolve around your friend while always keeping your face towards them. Can your friend see your back? How many rotations did you complete in one revolution? The moon revolves around the Earth similarly.

**Q: How many rotations did you complete in one revolution [in the activity]?**
> **Answer:** In this activity, you would complete one rotation on your own axis for every revolution around your friend, while always facing them.

The moon completes one rotation on its axis as it completes one revolution around the Earth.

## The Moon's Surface

The moon's surface is dusty and barren with many craters of different sizes and a large number of steep, high mountains, some as high as Earth's highest mountains.

**Q: Can any life exist on the moon?**
> **Answer:** Based on the information that the moon has no atmosphere and no water, it is unlikely that life as we know it can exist on the moon.
