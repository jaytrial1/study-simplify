# Observing the Night Sky and Celestial Objects

Paheli and Boojho experienced the beauty of a clear night sky filled with bright stars in a village, a scene often obscured in big cities by bright lights, smoke, and dust. Observing the night sky is a fascinating experience in clear, dark conditions.

The sky is dotted with countless stars of varying brightness and color. Some star-like objects do not twinkle; these are **planets**.

The **moon** is the brightest object in the night sky. The stars, planets, the moon, and many other objects in the sky are called **celestial objects**.

The study of celestial objects and associated phenomena is called **astronomy**. In ancient India, ancestors made methodical observations of the sky, which helped them create calendars and almanacs used for daily life, understanding climate and rainfall, timely sowing, crop choice, and fixing dates for seasons and festivals.
