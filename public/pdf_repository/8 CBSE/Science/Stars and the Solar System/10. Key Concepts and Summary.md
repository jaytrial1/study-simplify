# Key Concepts and Summary

*   The phases of the moon occur because we only see the part of the moon that reflects sunlight towards us.
*   Stars emit their own light; our sun is a star.
*   Distances of stars are conveniently expressed in light years.
*   Stars appear to move from east to west due to Earth's rotation.
*   The Pole Star appears stationary because it aligns with Earth's rotation axis direction.
*   Constellations are groups of stars forming recognizable shapes.
*   The solar system includes eight planets, asteroids, comets, and meteors revolving around the Sun.
*   A satellite is a body revolving around another body.
*   The Moon is Earth's natural satellite. Some planets have natural satellites.
*   Venus is the brightest planet.
*   Jupiter is the largest planet.
*   Artificial satellites are man-made, orbit Earth closer than the moon, and are used for weather forecasting, communication, and remote sensing.
