# Historical Perspectives and Notable Figures

## Did You Know?

Historically, it was believed Earth was the universe's center, with the moon, planets, Sun, and stars orbiting it. About 500 years ago, Nicolaus Copernicus (1473-1543), a Polish priest and astronomer, proposed the revolutionary idea that the Sun was the solar system's center with planets revolving around it. He hesitated to publish his work, which only appeared in 1543, the year he died.

In 1609, Galileo designed his own telescope. Observing Jupiter's moons, Venus's phases, and Saturn's rings, he argued that all planets must orbit the Sun, supporting Copernicus's model.

This shows how scientific ideas and concepts evolve and change with new evidence.

## Kalpana Chawla: The First Indian Woman in Space

Kalpana Chawla was the first Indian woman astronaut, born March 17, 1962, in Karnal, Haryana. She earned degrees in Aeronautical and Aerospace Engineering in India and the USA. She joined NASA in 1988 and was selected for her first space flight in 1996. She was the first Indian-born woman and second Indian person in space. Tragically, she was one of seven astronauts who died in the Space Shuttle Columbia disaster on February 1, 2003. She remains a role model for young women globally.
