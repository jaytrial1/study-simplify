This section covers the additional services provided by commercial banks beyond their primary deposit and loan functions.

# Secondary Functions of Commercial Banks

In addition to primary functions, commercial banks perform the following secondary functions:

## 1. Overdraft Facility

*   Allows a customer to withdraw more money from their current account than the balance available, up to an agreed limit.
*   Generally offered to trusted customers for short periods.
*   Interest is charged on the amount overdrawn.

## 2. Discounting Bills of Exchange

*   Provides facility for the holder of a bill of exchange to receive payment from the bank before the bill's maturity date.
*   The bank deducts a commission and pays the balance to the holder.
*   On maturity, the bank collects the full amount from the party who accepted the bill.

## 3. Agency Functions

Commercial banks act as agents for their customers, charging a commission for these services:

*   **Transfer of Funds:** Facilitating easy and economical transfer of money between places using instruments like demand drafts and mail transfers.
*   **Collection and Payment of Various Items:** Collecting cheques, bills, interest, dividends, etc., and making payments for taxes, insurance premiums, etc., on behalf of customers based on standing instructions.
*   **Purchase and Sale of Foreign Exchange:** Authorized banks buy and sell foreign currency for customers, supporting international trade.
*   **Purchase and Sale of Securities:** Buying and selling stocks, shares, and government securities on behalf of customers.
*   **Income Tax Consultancy:** Providing advice and preparing income tax returns for customers.
*   **Trustee and Executor:** Safekeeping customers' wills and executing them after their death.
*   **Letters of Reference:** Providing information about the financial standing of customers to traders and vice versa.

## 4. General Utility Functions

Commercial banks offer various general utility services:

*   **Locker Facility:** Providing safe deposit vaults for customers to store valuables securely.
*   **Traveller's Cheques:** Issuing traveller's cheques to avoid the risk of carrying cash during travel.
*   **Letter of Credit:** Issuing letters to certify the creditworthiness of customers.
*   **Underwriting Securities:** Undertaking to sell securities (shares/debentures) on behalf of companies. Public trust in banks helps in the sale of such securities.
*   **Collection of Statistics:** Gathering and publishing data related to trade, commerce, and industry, and advising customers on financial matters.

Commercial banks receive deposits and use them for loans. The loans often exceed the initial deposits, which is the basis of **Money Creation**.
