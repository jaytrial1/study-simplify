This section introduces the concept of a central bank as the apex institution controlling a country's monetary and banking system, using the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) as an example.

# Central Bank

A **Central Bank** is the 'Apex' body that controls, operates, regulates and directs the entire banking and monetary structure of the country.

It is called the apex (supreme) body because it holds the highest position in the country's monetary and banking system. Most financially developed countries have a central bank. India's Central Bank is the **Reserve Bank of India (RBI)**, established on April 1, 1935, under the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934.

Central banks have different names globally: RBI in India, Bank of England in the UK, and Federal Reserve System in the USA.
