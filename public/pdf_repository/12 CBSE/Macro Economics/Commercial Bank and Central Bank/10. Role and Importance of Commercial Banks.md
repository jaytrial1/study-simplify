# Role and Importance of Commercial Banks

Commercial banks play a vital role in economic development:

1.  **Accelerating the Rate of Capital Formation:** Encourage saving habits and mobilize public savings. These savings are directed towards productive investment, contributing to capital formation and economic development.
2.  **Provision of Finance and Credit:** Provide essential finance and credit to trade and industry, supporting both domestic and foreign trade activities.
3.  **Developing Entrepreneurship:** Promote entrepreneurship by underwriting shares of companies and assisting in financing new ventures or reviving sick industries.
4.  **Promoting Balanced Regional Development:** Extend credit facilities to rural areas by opening branches in backward regions and channelizing funds from developed to underdeveloped regions.
5.  **Help to Consumers:** Provide credit for purchasing durable consumer goods (vehicles, appliances), making them accessible to consumers with limited immediate paying capacity and thereby boosting demand.
