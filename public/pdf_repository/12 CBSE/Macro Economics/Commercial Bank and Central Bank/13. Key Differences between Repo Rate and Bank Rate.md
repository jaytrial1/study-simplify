This section, from the 'Power Booster', clarifies the distinctions between two important monetary policy rates used by the central bank.

# Key Differences between Repo Rate and Bank Rate

| Basis               | Bank Rate                                            | Repo Rate                                                                |
| :------------------ | :--------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Loan Charge On      | Charged by RBI for lending loans to banks.           | Charged on repurchase of securities sold by banks to RBI.                |
| Collateral          | No collateral or security required from banks.       | Approved securities are required as collateral.                          |
| Tenure              | Used for banks' long-term financial requirements.    | Used for banks' short-term financial needs.                              |
| Rate                | Generally higher than the Repo Rate.                 | Always lower than the Bank Rate.                                         |
| Repurchase Agreement | No repurchase agreement involved.                    | Involves an agreement for the bank to repurchase the securities later. |
