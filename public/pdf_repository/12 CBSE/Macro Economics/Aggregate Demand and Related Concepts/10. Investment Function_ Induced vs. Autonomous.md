This section defines investment expenditure and differentiates between two types: induced investment (dependent on income/profit) and autonomous investment (independent of income/profit).

# Investment Function

**Investment** refers to the expenditure incurred on the creation of new capital assets. It involves adding to physical capital and changes in inventory, including expenditure on assets like machinery, buildings, equipment, and raw materials, which increases an economy's productive capacity. Investment expenditure is classified under two heads:

(i) Induced Investment
(ii) Autonomous Investment

## Induced Investment

**Induced investment** refers to investment that depends on profit expectations and is directly influenced by the income level. It is *income elastic*, meaning it increases with increasing income and vice-versa.

## Autonomous Investment

**Autonomous investment** refers to investment that is not affected by changes in the level of income and is not solely induced by profit motive. It is *income inelastic* or unaffected by changes in income.

*   Autonomous investments are generally made by the Government on infrastructural activities.
*   The level of autonomous investment depends on the social, economic, and political conditions of the country. These investments change when there are changes in technology, discovery of new resources, or growth of population, etc.

## Induced Investment Vs Autonomous Investment

| Basis             | Induced Investment                                       | Autonomous Investment                                   |
| :---------------- | :------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------ |
| Motive            | Driven by profit motive and profit expectations.         | Done for social welfare, not primarily for profit.      |
| Income Elasticity | Income elastic; increases with income.                   | Unaffected by changes in income level.                  |
| Investment curve  | Slopes upwards as it is income elastic.                  | Parallel to X-axis as it is income inelastic.           |
| Sector            | Generally done by the private sector.                    | Generally done by the government sector.                |
