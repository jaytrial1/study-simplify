This section provides a summary of the important formulas discussed in the chapter relating to national income, aggregate demand/supply, and propensities to consume and save.

# Summary of Key Formulas

Here are some important formulas from the chapter:

*   National Income Identity: $Y = C + S$
*   Aggregate Demand (Two-Sector Model): $AD = C + I$
*   Aggregate Supply Identity: $AS = C + S$
*   Average Propensity to Consume (APC): $APC = \frac{C}{Y}$
*   Relationship between APC and APS: $APC = 1 - APS$
*   Average Propensity to Save (APS): $APS = \frac{S}{Y}$
*   Relationship between APS and APC: $APS = 1 - APC$
*   Marginal Propensity to Consume (MPC): $MPC = \frac{\Delta C}{\Delta Y}$
*   Relationship between MPC and MPS: $MPC = 1 - MPS$
*   Marginal Propensity to Save (MPS): $MPS = \frac{\Delta S}{\Delta Y}$
*   Relationship between MPS and MPC: $MPS = 1 - MPC$
*   Linear Consumption Function: $C = \bar{c} + b(Y)$ (where $\bar{c}$ is autonomous consumption, $b$ is MPC)
*   Linear Saving Function: $S = -\bar{c} + (1-b)Y$ (where $-\bar{c}$ is dissaving at zero income, $1-b$ is MPS)
