# Components of Budget
The budget's structure is divided into two main components:

1.  **Revenue Budget:** Deals with the government's revenue. It shows how revenue is collected and allocated. It consists of:
    *   Revenue Receipts
    *   Revenue Expenditures
    *   *In essence, it's a statement of estimated revenue receipts and estimated revenue expenditure for a fiscal year.*

2.  **Capital Budget:** Deals with the government's capital aspects. It consists of:
    *   Capital Receipts
    *   Capital Expenditures
    *   *In essence, it's a statement of estimated capital receipts and estimated capital expenditure for a fiscal year.*

Alternatively, budget components can be categorized into:
1.  Budget Receipts
2.  Budget Expenditures
