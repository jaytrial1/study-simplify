# Introduction to Government Budget
In the modern world, governments aim to maximize the welfare of their country. This requires significant expenditure on infrastructure, economic, and welfare activities. Effective planning and policy are needed to manage these expenditures. A budget serves as a document outlining detailed programs and policies for a specific fiscal year.

# Meaning of Government Budget
A government budget is an annual statement presenting estimated receipts and expenditures for a fiscal year (April 1st to March 31st). The figures shown are estimates, not actual amounts.

**Important Points about Government Budget:**

*   Budgets are prepared by governments at all levels: Central, State, and Local. This chapter focuses on the Central Government budget, known as the 'Union Budget'.
*   Estimated expenditures and receipts are planned to align with government objectives.
*   In India, the Union Budget is conventionally presented by the Finance Minister in the Lok Sabha on the first day of February each year.
*   A copy of the Union Budget is also laid in the Rajya Sabha.
*   Parliamentary approval is required before the budget takes effect on April 1st.
*   The budget reviews the government's financial performance in the previous year and outlines financial policies for the upcoming fiscal year.
