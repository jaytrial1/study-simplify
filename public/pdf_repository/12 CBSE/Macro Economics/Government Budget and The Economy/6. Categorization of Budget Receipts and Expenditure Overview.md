The sources of government revenue ("Rupee Comes From") and areas of expenditure ("Rupee Goes To") are often visualized using charts.

*Image Description: The image displays two pie charts representing the Indian government's budget for 2024-25. The "Rupee Comes From" chart shows sources of revenue, with the largest share from Borrowing and Other Liabilities (27%), followed by Income Tax (19%), GST & Other Taxes (18%), and Corporation Tax (17%). Other sources like Non-Tax Receipts (9%), Union Excise Duties (5%), Customs (4%), and Non-Debt Capital Receipts (1%) contribute smaller portions. The "Rupee Goes To" chart shows expenditure allocation, with the largest shares going to States' Share of Taxes and Duties (21%) and Interest Payments (19%). Central Sector Schemes (16%), Other Expenditure (9%), Finance Commission & other transfers (9%), Centrally Sponsored Schemes (8%), Defence (8%), Subsidies (6%), and Pensions (4%) constitute the remaining expenditure.*
