This section demonstrates how the Value Added, Income, and Expenditure methods, despite their different approaches, ultimately yield the same measure of National Income, providing a visual chart and a numerical example for clarity.

# Reconciliation of Three Methods

Since all three methods are used to measure the value of the same physical output, they will provide the same National Income. The following chart shows the reconciliation of three methods used for calculating National Income.

*Flowchart showing the reconciliation of the three methods of national income calculation: Value Added Method (summing GVAMP to get GDPMP, then adjusting to NNPFC), Income Method (summing factor incomes to get NDPFC, then adding NFIA to get NNPFC), and Expenditure Method (summing final expenditures to get GDPMP, then adjusting to NNPFC). The diagram highlights that all three methods converge on the same final measure, NNPFC.*
