# Evolution of Money

Money has evolved through various stages influenced by time, place, and circumstances.

1.  **Commodity Money:** In early times, widely demanded and commonly accepted commodities like furs, skins, salt, rice, wheat, utensils, or weapons were used as money. This stage was essentially barter exchange.
2.  **Metallic Money:** With societal progress, metals like gold, silver, and copper became money due to their durability, divisibility, and ease of handling and measurement. This was the primary form of money for a long period.
3.  **Paper Money:** Carrying large amounts of metal coins was inconvenient and risky. The invention of paper money, regulated by the country's central bank (like the RBI in India), marked a significant step. Today, currency notes issued by central banks form a large part of the money supply.
4.  **Credit Money:** Credit money emerged alongside paper money. People deposit cash in banks and use cheques to withdraw or make payments. A cheque itself isn't money but functions similarly by transferring value.
5.  **Plastic Money:** The most recent form includes Credit cards and Debit cards, reducing the need to carry physical cash for transactions.
