# Monetary System in India

India's current monetary system is managed by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) and operates under an **Inconvertible Paper Currency Standard**, with the 'rupee' as the standard currency.

Key features of the Indian monetary system:

*   The monetary authority is the **Reserve Bank of India (RBI)**.
*   India follows a **'Paper Currency Standard'** as its primary currency is paper notes.
*   Coins are **limited legal tender** for small payments, while paper notes are **unlimited legal tender** for payments of any amount.
*   The RBI has the sole authority to issue currency notes of ₹2 and higher denominations. The Ministry of Finance issues ₹1 notes and all coins. However, the RBI is responsible for circulating all currency and coins.
*   India follows the **'Minimum Reserve System'** for note issue. The RBI must maintain a minimum reserve of gold and foreign exchange worth ₹200 crores, based on which it can issue any amount of currency notes.
