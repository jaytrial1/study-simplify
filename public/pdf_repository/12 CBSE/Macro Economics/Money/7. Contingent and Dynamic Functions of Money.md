# Contingent and Dynamic Functions of Money

Beyond the primary and secondary functions, money also performs contingent functions and can be viewed through static and dynamic roles.

## Contingent Functions of Money

These are additional roles money plays:

*   **Distribution of National Income:** Money facilitates the distribution of national income among factors of production (rent for land, wages for labor, interest for capital, profit for enterprise), all expressed in monetary terms.
*   **Maximisation of Satisfaction:** Consumers maximize satisfaction by equating the marginal utility of a good with its price (in money). Producers maximize profit by equating a factor's marginal productivity with its price (in money).
*   **Basis of Credit Creation:** Money, as a store of value, encourages savings in bank deposits. These deposits form the basis for commercial banks to create credit.
*   **Productivity of Capital:** Money, being highly liquid, allows capital to be easily transferred from less productive to more productive uses, increasing overall capital productivity.
*   **Bearer of Options:** Holding money provides purchasing power and flexibility. The holder has numerous options for its use and can change decisions based on needs and priorities.
*   **Guarantee of Solvency:** Having sufficient money relative to liabilities ensures solvency for individuals and institutions, preventing bankruptcy. People hold money to meet unexpected needs.

## Static and Dynamic Functions of Money

According to Paul Einzig, functions can be:

*   **Static Functions:** Those that help the economy function smoothly, like medium of exchange, measure of value, store of value, and standard of deferred payments. Without these, the economy cannot operate.
*   **Dynamic Functions:** Those that influence the level of economic activity and trends. For example, money's role as a basis for credit creation and liquidity can lead to changes in income, output, employment, and price levels.

Both static and dynamic functions are crucial for a healthy economy.
