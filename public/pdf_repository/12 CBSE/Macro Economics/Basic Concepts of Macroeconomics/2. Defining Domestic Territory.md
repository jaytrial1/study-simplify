# Domestic Territory (Economic Territory)

Domestic territory is a crucial concept in national income accounting. It is defined as the territory administered by the government within which persons, goods, and capital circulate freely. While in simple terms it might seem to mean the political borders of a country, for national income accounting purposes, it is interpreted more broadly.

## What is Included in Domestic Territory:

In addition to political frontiers, domestic territory also includes:

*   Ships and Aircraft owned and operated by normal residents between two or more countries.
    *   *Example:* Planes operated by Air India between Russia and Japan are part of the domestic territory of India. Similarly, planes operated by Singapore Airways between India and Japan are part of the domestic territory of Singapore.
*   Fishing vessels, oil and natural gas rigs, and floating platforms operated by the residents of a country in international waters where they have exclusive rights of operation.
    *   *Example:* Fishing boats operated by Indian fishermen in international waters of the Indian Ocean will be considered a part of the domestic territory of India.
*   Embassies, consulates, and military establishments of a country located abroad.
    *   *Example:* The Indian Embassy in Russia is a part of the domestic territory of India.
    *   *Note:* A 'Consulate' is an office or building used by a consul, an officer commissioned by the government to reside in a foreign country to promote the interest of their home country.

## What is Not Included in Domestic Territory:

Domestic Territory does not include:

*   Embassies, consulates, and military establishments of a foreign country.
    *   *Example:* The Japanese Embassy in India is a part of the domestic territory of Japan.
*   International organisations like UNO, WHO, etc., located within the geographical boundaries of a country.

According to the United Nations, Economic Territory or domestic territory is the geographical territory, administered by a government within which, persons, goods, and capital circulate freely.
