# Basic Concepts of Macroeconomics

## Learning Objectives

This chapter covers the following basic concepts of macroeconomics:

*   Domestic Territory (Economic Territory)
*   Normal Residents
*   Factor Income and Transfer Income
*   Final Goods and Intermediate Goods
*   Consumption Goods and Capital Goods
*   Gross Investment, Net Investment and Depreciation
*   Net Indirect Taxes (NIT)
*   Net Factor Income from Abroad (NFIA)
