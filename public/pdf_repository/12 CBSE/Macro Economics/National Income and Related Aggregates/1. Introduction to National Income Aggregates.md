This section introduces the concept of national income in macroeconomics and lists the various aggregates used to measure it.

# Introduction

National Income is a crucial concept in macroeconomics. It is measured using various aggregates or variants, each with a specific meaning, measurement method, and use. The primary aggregates of national income include:

*   Gross Domestic Product at Market Price (**GDP$_{\text{MP}}$**)
*   Gross Domestic Product at Factor Cost (**GDP$_{\text{FC}}$**)
*   Net Domestic Product at Market Price (**NDP$_{\text{MP}}$**)
*   Net Domestic Product at Factor Cost (**NDP$_{\text{FC}}$** or Domestic Income)
*   Gross National Product at Market Price (**GNP$_{\text{MP}}$**)
*   Gross National Product at Factor Cost (**GNP$_{\text{FC}}$**)
*   Net National Product at Market Price (**NNP$_{\text{MP}}$**)
*   Net National Product at Factor Cost (**NNP$_{\text{FC}}$** or National Income)

These aggregates are used to measure the value of diverse goods and services produced in an economy, expressed in monetary terms, as quantities cannot be simply added together.
