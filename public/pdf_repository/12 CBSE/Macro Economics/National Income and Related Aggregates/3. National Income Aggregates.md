This section defines the four main national aggregates of national income: GNP at Market Price, GNP at Factor Cost, NNP at Market Price, and NNP at Factor Cost, explaining how they relate to the domestic concepts through Net Factor Income from Abroad (NFIA) and outlining the relationships among the national aggregates.

# National Income Aggregates

These aggregates focus on the income earned by the normal residents of a country, regardless of where it is earned.

## Gross National Product at Market Price (GNP$_{\text{MP}}$)

This refers to the gross market value of all the final goods and services produced by the **normal residents** of a country during a period of one year.

$\text{GNP}_{\text{MP}} = \text{GDP}_{\text{MP}} + \text{Net factor income from abroad}$

It is important to note that GNP$_{\text{MP}}$ can be less than GDP$_{\text{MP}}$ if Net Factor Income from Abroad (NFIA) is negative, and greater than GDP$_{\text{MP}}$ if NFIA is positive.

## Gross National Product at Factor Cost (GNP$_{\text{FC}}$)

This refers to the gross money value of all the final goods and services produced by the normal residents of a country during a period of one year.

$\text{GNP}_{\text{FC}} = \text{GNP}_{\text{MP}} - \text{Net Indirect Taxes}$

## Net National Product at Market Price (NNP$_{\text{MP}}$)

This refers to the net market value of all the final goods and services produced by the normal residents of a country during a period of one year.

$\text{NNP}_{\text{MP}} = \text{GNP}_{\text{MP}} - \text{Depreciation}$

## Net National Product at Factor Cost (NNP$_{\text{FC}}$)

This refers to the net money value of all the final goods and services produced by the normal residents of a country during a period of one year.

$\text{NNP}_{\text{FC}} = \text{GNP}_{\text{MP}} - \text{Net Indirect Taxes} - \text{Depreciation}$

NNP$_{\text{FC}}$ is also known as **National Income**.

## Relationship between Four National Concepts

**GNP$_{\text{MP}}$, GNP$_{\text{FC}}$, NNP$_{\text{MP}}$**, and **NNP$_{\text{FC}}$** are the four national concepts. The term 'National' signifies that the production of only normal residents of the country is included, even if they are located outside the domestic territory.
