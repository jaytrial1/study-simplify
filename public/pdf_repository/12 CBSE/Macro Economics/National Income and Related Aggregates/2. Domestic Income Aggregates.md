This section defines the four main domestic aggregates of national income: GDP at Market Price, GDP at Factor Cost, NDP at Market Price, and NDP at Factor Cost, explaining the meaning of each component (Gross, Domestic, Market Price, Product) and the relationships between them.

# Basic Aggregates of National Income

In an economy, goods and services produced by various units over a year are measured in monetary terms. We begin with the basic domestic aggregates.

## Gross Domestic Product at Market Price (GDP$_{\text{MP}}$)

This refers to the gross market value of all **final goods and services** produced **within the domestic territory** of a country during a period of one year.

*   **'Gross'**: Signifies that **depreciation** has not been accounted for; it is included.
*   **'Domestic'**: Signifies inclusion of goods and services produced by all units located within the domestic territory, regardless of whether they are residents or non-residents.
*   **'Market Price'**: Signifies inclusion of **net indirect taxes (NIT)** (indirect taxes paid minus subsidies received).
*   **'Product'**: Signifies that only **final goods and services** are included.

Other aggregates can be derived from GDP$_{\text{MP}}$ by making adjustments.

## Gross Domestic Product at Factor Cost (GDP$_{\text{FC}}$)

This refers to the gross money value of all the final goods and services produced within the domestic territory of a country during a period of one year.

$\text{GDP}_{\text{FC}} = \text{GDP}_{\text{MP}} - \text{Net Indirect Taxes}$

## Net Domestic Product at Market Price (NDP$_{\text{MP}}$)

This refers to the net market value of all the final goods and services produced within the domestic territory of a country during a period of one year.

$\text{NDP}_{\text{MP}} = \text{GDP}_{\text{MP}} - \text{Depreciation}$

## Net Domestic Product at Factor Cost (NDP$_{\text{FC}}$)

This refers to the net money value of all the final goods and services produced within the domestic territory of a country during a period of one year.

$\text{NDP}_{\text{FC}} = \text{GDP}_{\text{MP}} - \text{Net Indirect Taxes} - \text{Depreciation}$

NDP$_{\text{FC}}$ is also known as **Domestic Income** or **Domestic factor income**.

## Relationship between Four Domestic Concepts

**GDP$_{\text{MP}}$, GDP$_{\text{FC}}$, NDP$_{\text{MP}}$**, and **NDP$_{\text{FC}}$** are the four domestic concepts. The term 'Domestic' indicates that the contribution of producers within the country's domestic territory is included, irrespective of their residency status.
