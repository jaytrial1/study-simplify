7_Appiko Movement

In the early 1970s, the "Chipko Movement" was initiated to create growing awareness towards rapid deforestation. In Karnataka, a similar movement took a different name, 'Appiko', which means to hug.

# Appiko Movement Details

## Initial Protest

- On 8-9-1963, when the felling of trees started in Sirsi district, 160 men, women and children hugged the trees and forced the woodcutters to leave.

## Assurance from Forest Officials

- These people left the trees only when they were assured by the forest officials that the trees will be cut scientifically and in accordance with the working plan of the district.

## Impact and Spread

- These people saved 12,000 trees and within months, this movement spread to many adjoining districts.
