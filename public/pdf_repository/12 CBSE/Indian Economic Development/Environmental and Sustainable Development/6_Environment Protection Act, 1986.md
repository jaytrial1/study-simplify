6_Environment Protection Act, 1986

# Overview

1.  According to the act, Environment includes water, air and land and the inter-relationship, which exists among and between water, air, land and human beings and other creatures, plants, micro-organisms and property.
2.  The main objective of the Act is to provide protection and improvement of the environment and for matters connected therewith.
3.  The Act is applicable to the whole of India.
4.  The Act provides power to make rules to regulate environmental pollution and to notify standards and maximum limits of pollutants of air, water, and soil.
