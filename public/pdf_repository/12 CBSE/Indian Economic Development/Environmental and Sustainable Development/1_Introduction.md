1_Introduction

Since independence, India has launched a number of economic plans to attain economic development at a quicker pace. However, the economic development that we have achieved so far has come at a very heavy price – at the cost of environmental quality.

Liberalisation, Privatisation, and Globalisation (LPG) have resulted in the development of the economy, but with adverse consequences on our environment. So, we have to bear in mind the adverse consequences of the past development path on our environment and consciously choose a path of sustainable development.

In this chapter, we will discuss:

- Meaning, functions, and role of the environment
- Environmental challenges facing India
- Steps and strategies to achieve sustainable development.

# Environment

## Meaning, Functions, and Role of Environment

The environment encompasses all living and non-living things surrounding us. It includes physical, chemical, and other natural forces.

### Functions of the Environment
- The environment provides resources for production.
- The environment assimilates waste.
- The environment sustains life by providing genetic and biological diversity.
- The environment provides aesthetic services.

## Environmental Challenges Facing India

India faces numerous environmental challenges that threaten its sustainable development.

### Air Pollution
#### Causes of Air Pollution
- Industrial emissions
- Vehicular emissions
- Burning of fossil fuels
#### Effects of Air Pollution
- Respiratory diseases
- Climate change
- Damage to infrastructure

### Water Pollution
#### Causes of Water Pollution
- Industrial effluents
- Sewage disposal
- Agricultural runoff
#### Effects of Water Pollution
- Waterborne diseases
- Damage to aquatic ecosystems
- Scarcity of clean water

### Deforestation
#### Causes of Deforestation
- Agricultural expansion
- Urbanization
- Logging
#### Effects of Deforestation
- Soil erosion
- Loss of biodiversity
- Climate change

## Steps and Strategies to Achieve Sustainable Development

Sustainable development aims to meet the needs of the present without compromising the ability of future generations to meet their own needs.

### Strategies for Sustainable Development
- Use of Non-conventional Sources of Energy
- LPG (Liquefied Petroleum Gas), Gobar Gas in Rural Areas
- CNG (Compressed Natural Gas) in Urban Areas
- Wind Power
- Solar Power
- Mini-Hydel Plants
- Traditional Knowledge
- Biocomposting
