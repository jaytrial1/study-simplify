5_Privatisation

Privatisation refers to the shedding of the ownership or management of a government-owned enterprise.

Privatisation implies a greater role for the private sector in the economic activities of the country. Over the years, the Indian Government has diluted its stake in several public enterprises, including IPCL, IBP, Maruti Udyog, etc.

# Methods of Privatisation

Privatisation can be done in two ways:

1.  Transfer of ownership and management of public sector companies from the government to the Private Sector.
2.  Disinvestment: Privatisation of the public sector undertakings (PSU) by selling off part of the equity of PSUs to the public. This process is known as disinvestment.

The purpose of privatisation was mainly to improve financial discipline and facilitate modernisation. It was also believed that private capital and managerial capabilities would help in improving the performance of the PSUs.

# Maharatnas, Navratnas and Miniratnas

The government also made attempts to improve the efficiency of public sector undertakings by giving them autonomy in taking managerial decisions.

## Special Status for PSUs

For instance, some PSUs have been granted special status as Navratnas and Miniratnas.

## Navratnas

*   In order to infuse professionalism and enable PSUs to compete more effectively in the liberalised global environment, the government started granting 'Navratnas' status to PSUs.
*   They were given greater managerial and operational autonomy in taking various decisions, to run the company efficiently, and to increase their profits.
*   The granting of Navratna status resulted in better performance of these companies.

## Miniratnas

*   Apart from this, other profit-making enterprises were granted greater operational, financial and managerial autonomy and they were referred to as 'Miniratnas'.

## Current Status (October 2024)

As of October 2024, there are:

*   14 Maharatnas (like Indian Oil Corporation Limited, Steel Authority of India Ltd., etc.)
*   24 Navratnas (like Bharat Electronics Limited, Container Corporation of India Limited, etc.)
*   51 Miniratnas I CPSEs (i.e. Category I of Central Public Sector Enterprises (CPSEs)) (Like Airports Authority of India, Antrix Corporation Limited, etc.).
*   11 Miniratnas II CPSEs (Like Bharat Pumps & Compressors Limited, Engineering Projects (India) Limited, etc.).
