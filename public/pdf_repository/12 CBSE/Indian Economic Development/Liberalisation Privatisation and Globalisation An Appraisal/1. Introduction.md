1_Introduction

Since independence, India followed the mixed economic system, combining the advantages of the market economic system (capitalist economy) with those of the planned economic system (socialist economy).

But, in reality, the public sector dominated the control and regulation of our economy, and the private sector was ignored. There was a huge investment in the public sector and very low investment in the private sector.

The dominance of the public sector for about 4 decades led to the establishment of various rules and laws, which hampered the process of growth and development.

# Dominance of Public Sector

According to some scholars, the increasing role of the public sector has helped the Indian economy to:

## Advantages

(i) Achieve growth in savings.

(ii) Develop a diversified industrial sector.

(iii) Achieve food security through sustained expansion of agricultural output.
