# Improving Agricultural Marketing

The adoption of the following measures can improve agricultural marketing:

## Infrastructure Development

### Storage Facilities

*   Extension of storage facilities at the farm level.
*   Storage and warehousing facilities in the markets and consumption centers.

### Transportation

*   Improvement of transport facilities between the villages and the mandis.

## Market Regulation and Structure

### Regulated Markets

*   Establishment of regulated markets.

### Cooperative Marketing

*   Establishment of co-operative marketing societies.

## Financial Support

### Access to Credit

*   Provision of cheap credit, especially from institutional sources.

## Quality and Information

### Grading and Standardization

*   Provision for Grading of the produce to ensure good quality to the consumers and better prices for the producers.

### Market Information

*   Timely access to market information.
