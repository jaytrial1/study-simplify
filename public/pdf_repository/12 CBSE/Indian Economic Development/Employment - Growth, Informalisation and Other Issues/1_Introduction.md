1_Introduction

Work plays an important role in our lives, as individuals and as members of society. Every rational man aims to get engaged in certain work to earn a living. Some work on farms, in factories, banks, shops and a few others work at home.

Work at Home includes not only traditional work like weaving, lace making or variety of handicrafts, but also modern jobs like programming work in the IT industry. Earlier factory work meant working in factories located in cities, whereas, now technology has enabled people to produce those factory-based goods at home in villages. During the Covid-19 pandemic, millions of workers delivered their products and services by working from home.

# Important Points about Work

## Earning a Living

- People work to 'earn a living'. Employment is an activity from which a person earns a means of livelihood.

## Self-Worth and Social Connection

- Some people get money by inheriting it. However, it does not completely satisfy anybody. Being employed in work gives us a sense of self-worth and enables us to relate ourselves meaningfully with others.

## Contribution to National Income

- Every working person contributes to national income by engaging in various economic activities.

## Supporting Dependents

- We work not only for ourselves, but also for those who depend on us, like our family. It gives us a sense of accomplishment when we work to meet their requirements.

## Insights into Employment Quality and Human Resources

- The study of working people gives us insights into the quality and nature of employment in our country and helps in understanding and planning our human resources.

## Analyzing Industry Contributions

- It helps in analyzing the contribution made by different industries and sectors towards national income.

## Addressing Social Issues

- It also helps us to address many social issues, such as the exploitation of marginalised sections of society, child labour, etc.

## Multifaceted Nature of Employment in India

- The nature of employment in India is multifaceted (i.e. have many aspects or phases) as some people get employment throughout the year, others get occasionally and some others remain unemployed.
