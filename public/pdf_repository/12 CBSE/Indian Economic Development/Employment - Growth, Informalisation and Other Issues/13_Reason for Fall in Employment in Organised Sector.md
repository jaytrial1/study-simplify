# Reasons for Fall in Employment

The various reasons for the fall in employment in the organised sector are:

## Public Sector Changes

*   There is a drastic fall in employment in the public sector due to privatisation and disinvestment.

## Industrial Unit Adjustments

*   Existing industrial units are reducing excess labour in order to remain competitive.

## Technological Advancements

*   Firms are adopting new technologies to ensure competitiveness, which are more automated and, therefore, not job-creating.

## Combined Effect

Due to the combined effect of all these reasons, there is a very slow expansion in employment opportunities in the organised sector.
