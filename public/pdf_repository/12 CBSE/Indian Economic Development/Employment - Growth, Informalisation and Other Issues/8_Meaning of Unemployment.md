8_Meaning of Unemployment

Unemployment is a serious problem found in almost every country, regardless of its economic development. It's a multifaceted process, affecting both economic and social activities. The National Statistical Office (formerly the National Sample Survey Organisation) defines unemployment as a situation where people are not working due to a lack of available jobs but are actively seeking employment through various means or expressing their willingness to work under prevailing conditions.

Unemployed workers waiting for casual jobs

In essence, unemployment occurs when people are willing and able to work at the existing wage rate but cannot find employment. This affects both unskilled and skilled workers. Note that individuals unwilling to work at existing wage rates are not considered unemployed.

Some economists define an unemployed person as someone unable to find even one hour of work in half a day.

Prolonged Unemployment may make a Person Unemployable (Just For Reference)

# Consequences of Unemployment

## Economic Impact

-   From an economic standpoint, unemployment signifies the inefficient use of human resources, leading to reduced production and a lower standard of living.

## Social Impact

-   Unemployment reduces the standard of living, and adversely affects worker morale and self-confidence.
-   Prolonged unemployment can lead to a loss of skills and work habits, potentially making the individual unemployable.

# Unemployment in India

## Temporary Nature

Unemployment is temporary even in India. Due to challenging economic conditions, individuals often cannot remain unemployed for extended periods and may be forced to accept undesirable or hazardous jobs.

# Sources of Unemployment Data

Data on unemployment is collected from three primary sources:

1.  Reports of the Census of India
2.  National Statistical Office's Reports on Employment and Unemployment Situation
3.  Directorate General of Employment and Training data on Registration with Employment Exchanges

These sources provide varying estimates of unemployment but offer valuable insights into the characteristics of the unemployed and the types of unemployment prevalent in the country.
