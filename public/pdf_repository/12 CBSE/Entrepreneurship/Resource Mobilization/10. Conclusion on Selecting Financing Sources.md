This section concludes the chapter by emphasizing that finance has a cost and risk, and advises entrepreneurs on the importance of carefully selecting and combining different sources of funds.

## Conclusion

Finance can be procured, just like any other resource, against a cost. Procurement of finance involves risk and formalities to comply with. Entrepreneurs need a careful attitude to sensibly make a choice of sources to generate funds. No one source can be deemed to be the best source. Thus, it is always advisable to select a combination of sources so that both cost and risk can be kept at the lowest.
