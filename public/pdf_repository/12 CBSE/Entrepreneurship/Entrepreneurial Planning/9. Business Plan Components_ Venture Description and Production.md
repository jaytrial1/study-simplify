# Business Plan Components: Venture Description and Production

## I. Introductory profile/general introduction

This is the title or cover page, providing a brief summary. It includes:
a) **Entrepreneur's bio-data:** Name, address, qualifications, experience, capabilities. For partners, names, number, addresses, designation.
b) **Industry's profile:** Name and address of the enterprise, contact details, nature of business, branches/sister concerns.
c) **Constitution and organisation:** Legal form (sole, partnership, company, etc.) and registration details.
d) **Product details:** Product utility, range, design, and precise Unique Selling Proposition (USP).

## II. Description of venture/business venture

This section starts with the "mission statement" describing the enterprise's size, scope, and nature, and what the entrepreneur hopes to accomplish. Key elements covered:
a) **Site:** Location, ownership (owned/leasehold), industrial area, required NOCs.
b) **Physical infrastructure:** Availability of:
    i) **Raw material:** Indigenous or imported, sources of supply.
    ii) **Labour:** Type, training provision, number required.
    iii) **Utilities:** Power, fuel, water, gas, electricity (type, load sanctioned, sources, quality, quantum needed, suppliers).
    iv) **Pollution control:** Sewage system, treatment plant, water harvesting, waste disposal/emission arrangements.
    v) **Transport and communication system:** Requirements, modes, potential bottlenecks.
    vi) **Machinery and equipments:** Complete list (size, capacity, type, cost, suppliers).
    vii) **Production process:** Process involved, installed/licensed capacity, technology (local/imported), shifts involved.

This section helps the entrepreneur assess feasibility and evaluate the idea, potentially saving time and cost later.

### Describing the venture

*   What is the mission of the new venture?
*   What are your reasons for going into business?
*   Why will you be successful in this venture?
*   What development work has been completed to date?
*   What are your product(s) and/or service(s)?
*   Describe the product(s) and/or service(s), including patent copyright, or trademark status.
*   Where will the business be located?
*   Is your building new? old? in need of renovations? (if renovation is needed, state costs.)
*   Is the building leased or owned? (State the terms)
*   Why is this building and location right for your business?
*   What office equipment will be needed?
*   Will equipment be purchased or leased?
*   What experience do you have and/or will you need to successfully implement the business plan?

## III. Production Plan

"The planning of industrial operations involves four considerations, namely, what work shall be done, how the work shall be done and lastly, when and by whom the work shall be done." - Kimball and Kimball Jr.

Production is the most important activity where raw material is transformed into finished product using energy, capital, manpower, and machinery. Manufacturing operations are complex and need detailed planning. The nature of the venture decides the importance and detail required. There are three situations:

a) **No manufacturing involved:** If the venture is a trading firm or service provider, this section is eliminated.
b) **Partial manufacturing:** If some or all manufacturing is subcontracted/outsourced, the plan should describe:
    i) Name and location of subcontractor(s)
    ii) Reasons for selection
    iii) Cost and time involved
    iv) Any completed contracts
    A clear mention of what the entrepreneur does internally versus outsourcing is required.
c) **Complete Manufacturing:** If manufacturing is done entirely by the entrepreneur, describe:
    i) Physical plant layout
    ii) Machinery and equipment required
    iii) Raw materials, suppliers (names, addresses, terms)
    iv) Cost of manufacturing
    v) Future capital equipment required

The objective of a production plan is "Picturising ahead every step in a long series of separate operations, each step to be taken in the right place of the right degree, and at the right time and each operation to be done at maximum efficiency", as quoted by Alford and Beathy.

A production plan helps plan work to clearly understand:
a) Production schedule and/or budget
b) Machinery, equipment requirement
c) Manufacturing method and process involved
d) Plant layout
e) Time, motion and work study
f) Manpower requirement
g) Inventory requirement

### Production plan

*   Will you be responsible for all or part of the manufacturing operation?
*   If some manufacturing is subcontracted, who will be the subcontractors? (Give names and address)
*   Why were these subcontractors selected?
*   What are the costs of the subcontracted manufacturing? (Include copies of any written contracts)
*   What will be the layout of the production process? (Illustrate steps if possible)
*   What equipment will be needed immediately for manufacturing?
*   What raw materials will be needed for manufacturing?
*   Who are the suppliers of new materials and what are the appropriate costs?
*   What are the costs of manufacturing the product?
*   What are the future capital equipment needs of the venture?

### If a retail operation or service:

*   From whom will merchandise be purchased?
*   How will the inventory control system operate?
*   What are the storage needs of the venture and how will they be promoted?
*   How will the goods flow to the customer?
*   Chronologically, what are the steps involved in a business transaction?
*   What are the technology utilization requirements to service customers effectively?
