### Facts Needed for Market Planning

*   Who are the users, where are they located, how much do they buy, from whom do they buy, and why?

*   What are the pricing changes in the market, who has initiated these changes, and why?
*   What are the market's attitudes concerning competitive products?
*   What channels of distribution supply consumers, and how do they function?
*   Who are the competitors, where are they located, and what advantages/ disadvantages do they have?

*   What are the company's strengths and weaknesses?

## Steps in preparing the marketing plan

Potential investors view the marketing plan as critical. Entrepreneur should make it comprehensive and detailed. Procedure involves:

### 1) Business situation analysis

'Where we have been?'. For a new venture, focus shifts to:
a) Entrepreneur's personal profile.
b) Emphasis on product development.
c) What 'need' it satisfies.
d) Any other enterprise/experience.
e) Any planned market segmentation.

### 2) Identify the target market

Clearly define the specific group of potential customers. This involves:
a) Deciding the general market/industry based on research/analysis.
b) Dividing the market into smaller groups based on:
    i) Consumer's characteristics (Geographic, Demographic, Psychographics).
    ii) Buying situations (conditions, usage, desired benefits).
c) Selecting segment(s) to target.
d) Developing a marketing plan integrating product, price, distribution, promotion.

### 3) Conduct SWOT analysis

Consider enterprise's:
a) Strengths
b) Weaknesses
c) Opportunities
d) Threats

Marketing plan considers strengths/weaknesses for success.

### 4) Establish goals

"Where do we want to go?" Answered by establishing:
a) Realistic, attainable, well-defined goals/objectives.
b) Quantifying goals for measurement/control.
c) Setting standards for qualitative goals.
d) Limiting goal number to avoid chaos/confusion.

### 5) Define marketing strategy

"How do we get there?" Demands outlining specific activities to meet goals/objectives. Strategy and action plan comprise decisions on the 4 P's:
a) Product
b) Price
c) Promotion
d) Place

### 6) Implementation and monitoring of the plan

Marketing plan is a commitment. Entrepreneur should be flexible and prepared to adjust the plan if necessary.

### Outline for a marketing plan

1.  Situation analysis
    *   Background of venture
    *   Strengths and weaknesses of venture
    *   Market opportunities and threats
    *   Competitor analysis
2.  Marketing objectives and goals
3.  Marketing strategy and action programs
4.  Budgets
5.  Controls

## IX. Assessment of risk

Hazards, risks, obstacles are always present. In a business plan, entrepreneur should:
1) identify potential hazards.
2) develop alternative strategies to prevent, minimize, or respond to the risk.

## X. Appendix

Contains backup material not necessary in the main text:
a) Letters from customers, distributors etc.
b) Primary or secondary research data.
c) Copies of contracts, agreements, price lists.

Businesses sometimes fail due to inability to plan effectively. Intelligent planning is possible with commitment and expert support.
