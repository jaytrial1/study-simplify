This section defines business, explores different perspectives on its meaning, and details the essential characteristics that distinguish a business activity.

# Nature and Characteristics of Business

Activities which are inspired mainly by economic consideration can be classified in three broad categories:

*   Manufacturing
*   Service
*   Trading

## Manufacturing

Production of merchandise for use or sale using labour and machines, tools, chemical and biological processing, or formulation. It commonly applies to industrial production where raw materials are transformed into finished goods on a large scale. These goods can be used for other products or sold to consumers through wholesalers and retailers. Manufacturing takes place under various economic systems, driven by profit in free markets and state direction in collectivist economies.

## Service

An intangible commodity, a type of economic activity that is intangible, not stored, and does not result in ownership. A service is consumed at the point of sale. Examples include postal service, medical consultation, banking, communication, transport, insurance, and warehousing. The service sector is crucial infrastructure for smooth business flow and has been growing rapidly in the Indian economy.

## Trading

The activity or process of buying, selling, or exchanging goods or services. It involves the amount of things or services bought and sold, and the money made from these transactions. Trading is also the act of exchanging one thing for another.

Thus, in nutshell, our key area of study is - **BUSINESS**. 'A state of being busy or occupied', is what 'Business literally means'. In a way all economic activities related to the production and distribution of goods and services undertaken for monetary gains are said to be business.

## Different Authors, Different Opinions

*   F.C. Hooper: Business is "whole complex field of commerce and industry, the basic industries, process and manufacturing and the network of ancillary services, distribution, banking, insurance, transport and so on which serve and interpenetrate the world of business as a whole."
*   Peterson and Plowman: Business may be defined as "an activity in which different persons exchange something of value whether goods and services for mutual gain or profit."
*   Wheeler: "An institution organized and operated to provide goods and services to the society under the incentive of private gain", is said to be business.

Business is a continuous human economic activity with an objective to earn profit by producing, buying and selling of goods and services. Irrespective of size, nature, scale or ownership, an activity is referred as business if it exhibits the following essential characteristics:

*   **Entrepreneur's presence:** There must be someone to take initiative for establishing a business and undertake the risk associated with it.
*   **Economic activity:** Activities related to the production and/or distribution of goods and services, with the motive of profit.
*   **Production or procurement of goods and services:** A business either produces or procures goods and services for offering to consumers. These can be Consumer's goods, Producer's goods, or Services.
*   **Sale or exchange of goods and services:** A business must involve sale, exchange or transfer of goods and/or services for satisfaction of human needs against a value/price. Goods/services for personal/self-consumption is not business.
*   **Regularity:** One time transaction is not business, even if profitable. Business must be done regularly.
*   **Utility creation:** Business activities result in creation of utilities:
    *   *Form utility:* Changing raw material into finished product.
    *   *Place utility:* Transporting goods from production to consumption place.
    *   *Time utility:* Storing goods when not required and supplying when needed.
*   **Profit earning:** Profit is the reward for undertaking business activity; no business survives long without profits.
*   **Uncertainty of return:** Business does not guarantee return of invested principal or profit.
*   **Element of risk:** Business involves risk (uncertainty). 'Higher the risk, higher the profit'. External factors affect business, making risk element important for vigilance.
