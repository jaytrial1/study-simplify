# Introduction to Management

Management is a universal concept, essential in every organization, whether business or non-business (like schools, hospitals, etc.). The success of an organization depends on effective management. For example, HCL's leadership in global technology is attributed to the managerial efficiency of Shiv Nadar and his team.

Consider the following scenarios:
-   A school without a principal
-   A cricket team without a captain
-   A country without a government

In all these cases, the situation is likely to be disorganized and chaotic due to the absence of management. Thus, management is vital for all organizations, regardless of size or type. It is necessary to ensure individuals make their best contribution towards group objectives.

Management involves a series of interrelated functions performed by all managers, although the time spent on different functions varies by level. For instance, top-level managers spend more time on planning and organizing than lower-level managers.

Management is always required when human and non-human resources work together to achieve an objective. With the increasing size and complexity of modern organizations, management has gained immense importance. Successful organizations achieve goals through a deliberate process called 'Management'.
