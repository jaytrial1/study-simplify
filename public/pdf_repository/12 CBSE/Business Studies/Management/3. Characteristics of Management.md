This section details the key features or characteristics that define the nature of management as an activity.

# Characteristics of Management

The important characteristics or features of management are:

1.  **Management is a goal-oriented process**: Management always aims to achieve specific organizational goals, which should be simple and clearly stated. Different organizations have different goals. Management integrates the efforts of all members towards achieving these goals. For instance, if Airtel's goal is to add 1 million subscribers in a year, all managerial activities are directed towards this goal.
2.  **Management is all Pervasive**: The activities involved in managing an enterprise are common to all organizations, whether economic, social, or political. Management is a universal concept required in all organizations, big or small, business or non-business. A company needs managing as much as a cricket team or a school. It is an essential element everywhere.
3.  **Management is Multidimensional**: Management is a complex activity with multiple dimensions:
    *   **Management of Work**: Every organization exists to perform some work (e.g., school provides education, factory produces goods). Management translates this work into goals, assigns means to achieve them (problems to solve, decisions to make, plans, budgets, responsibilities, authority).
    *   **Management of People**: People are the most important asset. Getting work done through people remains a major task despite technological advancements. Managing people has two dimensions: dealing with diverse needs of individuals and dealing with individuals as a group. The task is to make people work towards organizational goals by leveraging their strengths and minimizing weaknesses.
    *   **Management of Operations**: To survive and grow, organizations produce goods/services through a production process converting inputs into output. Management of operations combines management of work and people, deciding what is to be done (work) and who is to do it (people).
4.  **Management is a Continuous Process**: Management is an ongoing process. All functions (planning, organizing, staffing, directing, controlling) are performed continuously by managers.
5.  **Management is a Group Activity**: An organization comprises diverse individuals with different needs and purposes for joining. However, as members, they work together for the organizational goal, requiring teamwork and coordination. Management should enable members to grow and develop as needs and opportunities change.
6.  **Management is a Dynamic Function**: An organization interacts with its environment (social, economic, political factors). To survive and grow, it must adapt to the changing environment. Management is dynamic, adjusting the organization and its goals accordingly. For example, McDonald's changed its menu to adapt to the Indian market.
7.  **Management is an Intangible Force**: Management is invisible but its presence is felt when targets are met, employees are happy, and there is orderliness and coordination instead of chaos. Only the management itself is intangible; the people performing management functions are tangible and visible.
