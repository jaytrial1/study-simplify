This section describes the core activities or functions that managers perform within an organization.

# Functions of Management

Management is a dynamic process where every manager performs various functions, broadly classified into five categories:

1.  **Planning**: Determining in advance what to do, when, how, and by whom. It involves setting goals and developing efficient and effective ways to achieve them. Planning cannot prevent problems but can predict them and prepare contingency plans. Planning bridges the gap between the current state and desired future state.
2.  **Organising**: Once a plan is set, organizing examines required activities and resources. It's assigning duties, grouping tasks, establishing authority, and allocating resources for a plan. It decides who, where, and when a task will be done. Organizing groups tasks into manageable units and establishes authority/reporting relationships. Proper organization aids work accomplishment, efficiency, and effectiveness. Different businesses need structures suited to their work nature.
3.  **Staffing**: The managerial function of hiring and developing employees for positions created by organizing. It's finding the right person with the right qualifications for the right job at the right time. Also known as human resource function, it includes recruitment, selection, placement, and training.
4.  **Directing**: Supervising, motivating, leading, and communicating with employees to achieve objectives. This function influences human resource behavior. Motivation (creating desire to work) and leadership (influencing others) are key components. Good managers direct through praise and criticism to bring out the best in employees.
5.  **Controlling**: Monitoring organizational performance against goals. It involves setting performance standards, measuring current performance, comparing it to standards, and taking corrective action for significant deviations. It ensures everything is done according to plans.

**Levels and Functions of Management**

Managers at every level perform all five functions, but their relative importance varies. Top level focuses more on planning and organizing, middle level more on staffing, and lower level more on directing and controlling.
