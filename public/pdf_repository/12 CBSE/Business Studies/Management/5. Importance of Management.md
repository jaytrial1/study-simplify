This section outlines the reasons why management is considered essential for the success of organizations and society.

# Importance of Management

The importance of management is evident from the following points:

1.  **Management helps in achieving group goals**: Management is needed to achieve organizational goals. It brings human and non-human resources together and directs individual efforts towards common goals.
2.  **Management increases efficiency**: Management helps reduce costs and increase productivity through better planning, organizing, directing, staffing, and controlling organizational activities.
3.  **Management creates a dynamic organisation**: Organizations operate in changing environments. Employees often resist change. Management helps people adapt to changes, allowing the organization to maintain its competitive edge and promoting employee growth.
4.  **Management helps in achieving personal objectives**: Managers motivate and lead teams such that individual goals (good salary, growth prospects) are achieved while working towards organizational objectives. Through motivation and leadership, management aligns individual and organizational goals.
5.  **Management helps in the development of society**: Organizations have objectives towards various groups. While developing the organization, management also aims to develop society by fulfilling social obligations. Efficient management contributes by:
    *   Providing good quality products and services.
    *   Creating employment opportunities.
    *   Adopting new technology for people's benefit.
    *   Leading the path towards growth and development.
