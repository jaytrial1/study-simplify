This section defines privatisation and describes the steps taken to increase the role of the private sector.

# Privatisation

**Privatisation** means assigning a greater role to the private sector in the nation-building process while reducing the role of the public sector. This represented a reversal of the development strategy previously followed by Indian planners.

The Government initiated the following measures for privatization:

1.  **Disinvestment:** This involves selling a part or the whole of shares of public sector undertakings (PSUs), effectively transferring the enterprise to the private sector. It dilutes the government's stake. If the private sector acquires more than 51% shares, it leads to the transfer of ownership and management of the PSU to the private sector.
2.  **Setting up of Board of Industrial and Financial Reconstruction (BIFR):** BIFR was established to revive or close down loss-making and sick PSUs.
