# Technological Environment

**Technological Environment** includes forces related to scientific improvements and innovations that introduce new ways of producing goods and services and new methods and techniques for operating a business.

Technological change often leads to better service and cost efficiency. Technological advances create new products, production techniques, and ways of managing and communicating. For instance, computerized information kiosks and World Wide Web multimedia pages are now commonly used for advertising.

Continuous innovations in fields like lasers, robotics, biotechnology, and food preservation have created numerous opportunities and threats for various enterprises.

The Technological Environment has positively influenced business activities. It has led to shifts in demand from older technologies to newer ones (e.g., vacuum tubes to transistors, steam engines to diesel/electric engines, fountain pens to ballpoint pens). Technology is changing rapidly, and the technological environment significantly influences business.

**Examples:**

*   Color TVs with remotes replacing Black and White TVs.
*   Electrostatic copiers replacing manual photocopy machines.
*   Computers with multiple word processors replacing single-language word processors.
*   Airline companies using websites and the World Wide Web for customers to check flights, destinations, fares, and book tickets online.
*   Online shopping becoming an alternative to visiting physical markets. For example, Reliance Industries launched 'JioMart' online grocery service during Covid-2019.
*   Electronic payment services for utility bills replacing manual payments.

## Some Components of Technological Environment

1.  Research and development activities.
2.  Import and Export of Technology.
3.  Innovations in products and processes.
4.  New Equipment.
