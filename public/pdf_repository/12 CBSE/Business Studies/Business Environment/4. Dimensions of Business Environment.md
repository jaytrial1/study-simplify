# Dimensions of Business Environment

The business environment comprises external forces that directly or indirectly affect a business enterprise's operations. These forces are broadly categorized as:

1.  **General Environment:** This indirectly influences a business firm's operations. It includes economic, social, political, legal, and technological factors.
2.  **Specific Environment:** This directly influences a business firm's operations. It includes customers, owners and investors, suppliers, creditors, employees, trade unions, competitors, government, etc.

*Note: The specific environment is outside the scope of the Class XII syllabus addressed here. This discussion focuses on the General Environment.*

The various dimensions of the General Environment are crucial for decision-making and improving enterprise performance. Management benefits significantly from being aware of these dimensions.
