This section defines liberalisation and outlines the measures taken under the New Economic Policy to achieve it.

# Liberalisation

**Liberalisation** means removing unnecessary controls and restrictions on business activities. It involves lifting entry and growth restrictions on private sector enterprises and ending the system of licenses, permits, and quotas. The objective was to unleash the country's economic potential by encouraging private sector and multinational corporations (MNCs) to invest and expand.

The Indian industry was liberalized through the following measures:

1.  Abolition of licensing requirements in most industries.
2.  No restrictions on expanding or contracting business activities.
3.  Removal of restrictions on the movement of goods and services.
4.  Freedom in setting prices for goods and services.
5.  Reduction in tax rates and removal of unnecessary controls over the economy.
6.  Simplifying procedures for imports and exports.
7.  Introducing a liberal process to attract foreign capital and technology to India.
