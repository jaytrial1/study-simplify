This section focuses on the economic forces that impact business activities.

# Economic Environment

**Economic Environment** refers to all forces that have an economic impact on business activities.

Key economic factors affecting management practices include:

*   Interest rates
*   Inflation rates
*   Changes in disposable income
*   Stock market indices
*   Value of the rupee

Both short-term and long-term interest rates significantly influence the demand for products and services. Each economic factor can act as either an opportunity or a threat for a business.

**Examples:**

*   An increase in people's disposable income due to a rise in Gross Domestic Product (GDP) leads to increased demand for products.
*   Easy finance schemes resulting from banking sector reforms have boosted the demand for cars.
*   Rising inflation rates increase production costs by increasing the prices of raw materials and wages.
*   The demonetisation of ₹500 and ₹1,000 notes in 2016 significantly impacted the Indian Economic Environment.
*   The introduction of GST (Goods and Services Tax) influences almost all business enterprises.

## Some Components or Aspects of Economic Environment

1.  Relative roles of the private and public sectors.
2.  Growth rate of GNP and per capita income (at current and constant prices).
3.  Rates of savings and investments.
4.  Volume of imports and exports.
5.  Balance of payments and changes in foreign exchange reserves.
6.  Agricultural and industrial production trends.
7.  Money supply in the economy.
