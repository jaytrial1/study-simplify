# MEANING OF PRINCIPLES OF MANAGEMENT

Principles of Management are broad and general guidelines for decision making and behaviour of managers.
The principles serve as general practical guidelines to the manager for making decisions, taking action and solving various problems systematically. They are not rules, but only a guide to action.

Principles are applicable to different kinds of organisations such as business, hospitals, clubs and educational institutions.

- Management Principles have been derived and developed on the basis of observation and experimentation and personal experience of the management thinkers.


## Enrich Yourself: Derivation of Management Principles 

Principles of Management have been developed on the basis of observation and the guidelines developed through observation are repeatedly experimented to test their validity. Such guidelines are tested in different organisations with different environment. If they provide same result in all conditions, then the guidelines are designated as management principles.
For example, if it is observed that receiving orders from one boss (principle of 'Unity of Command') avoids confusion among subordinates and conflict among superiors, then it is experimented in different organisations. When it provides same results in all conditions, then the principle is accepted as a management principle.

- They contribute towards development of management both as a science and as an art. Derivation of these principles may be said to be a matter of science and their creative application may be regarded as an art.
- These principles are guidelines to action. While functions of management (i.e. Planning, Organising, Staffing, Directing and Controlling) are the actions to be taken while practising management, Principles help managers to take decisions while performing these functions.


## Management Principles Vs Pure Science Principles

The management principles are not as exact as the principles of Physics and Chemistry.

| Basis | Management Principles | Pure Science Principles |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 1. Flexibility | They are flexible. | They are rigid in nature. |
| 2. Application | They have to be applied creatively as they <br> deal with unpredictable human behaviour. | They are applied in static or absolute manner. |
| 3. Relation with <br> Environment | They have to keep pace with changing <br> environment as human behaviour is never <br> static. | They are not required to keep pace with <br> changing environment as they do not <br> change with time. |

## Management Principles Vs Management Techniques

The principles of management should not be confused with techniques of management.

| Basis | Management Principles | Management Techniques |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 1. Meaning | They are guidelines to take decisions or <br> actions while practicing techniques. | They are procedures, which involve a series <br> of steps to achieve desired goals. |
| 2. Flexibility | They are flexible. | They are not so flexible. |

## Management Principles Vs Values

While practicing principles of management, values cannot be neglected as businesses have to fulfil social and ethical responsibilities towards society. Management principles are different from values.

| Basis | Management Principles | Values |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 1. Meaning | They are guidelines to take decisions. | Values are general rules for behaviour of <br> individual in the society. |
| 2. Formation | They are formed after research in work situations. | They are formed through common practice. |
| 3. Nature | They are technical in nature. | They are ethical in nature. |
