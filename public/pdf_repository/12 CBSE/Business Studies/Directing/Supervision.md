# SUPERVISION 

The term 'Supervision' is made up of two words: Super and Vision. Super means over and above and Vision means seeing. So, supervision means overseeing employees at work. It is the function of keeping a watch on the working and performance of the employees.
Supervision refers to instructing, guiding and observing the subordinates at work to ensure that they are working as per plans and to help them in solving their work problems.

## SUPERVISION

The term supervision can be understood in two ways:

1. As an element of Directing: As supervision is an element of directing, every manager in the organisation supervises his subordinates. In this sense, supervision means guiding the efforts of employees to accomplish the desired objectives. It means overseeing subordinates \& giving them instructions to ensure optimum utilisation of resources \& achievement of work targets.
2. As a function performed by Supervisor: Supervisor is a managerial position in the organisation hierarchy at the operative level, i.e., immediately above the worker. The functions and performance of the supervisor are vital to any organisation as he is directly related with workers, whereas, other mangers have no direct touch with bottom level workers.

## Functions of a Supervisor

The functions of a supervisor can be understood from the following points:

1. Interpersonal contact with workers: Supervisor maintains day-to-day contact and friendly relations with workers. A good supervisor acts as a guide, friend \& philosopher to the workers.
2. Link between Workers and Management: Supervisor acts as a link between workers and management. On one hand, he communicates management's objectives, policies and programmes to workers and on the other hand, he conveys workers' ideas, suggestions, complaints and grievances to the management. By acting as a linking pin, supervisor helps to avoid misunderstandings and conflicts between management and workers.
3. Promotes Group Unity: Supervisor plays a key role in maintaining group unity among workers. He sorts out their internal differences and maintains harmony among workers.
4. Ensures Performance of work: Supervisor ensures performance of work according to the targets set. He takes responsibility for achievement of task \& motivates his workers effectively.
5. Provides Training to the Employees: Supervisor provides good on-the-job training to the workers. A skilled and knowledgeable supervisor can build efficient team of workers by arranging appropriate training programmes for the employees.
6. Influences Workers: Supervisor plays a key role in influencing the workers in the organisation. He builds up high morale among workers through good leadership.

7. Provides Feedback: A good supervisor analyses the work performed and gives feedback to the workers. He suggests ways and means of developing work skills.
