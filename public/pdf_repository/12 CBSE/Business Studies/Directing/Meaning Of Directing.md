# MEANING OF DIRECTING

Directing refers to the process of instructing, guiding, counselling, motivating and leading people in the organisation to achieve its objective.
Directing not only involves orders and instructions by a superior to the subordinates, but includes many elements like supervision, motivation, leadership and communication. It is one

of the key managerial functions performed by every manager. Directing is a managerial process which takes place throughout the life of an organisation.

## Definitions of Directing 

- Direction is a complex function that includes all those activities which are designed to encourage subordinates to work effectively and efficiently.
—Koontz and Donnell
- Directing deals with the steps a manager takes to get subordinates and others to carry out plans.
-William Newman
