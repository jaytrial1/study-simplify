This section introduces the management function of directing, defining its meaning and role within an organization.

# Introduction to Directing

After preparing plans, designing the organisation structure, and arranging necessary human resources, the next major managerial function is to direct the activities of employees. **Directing** aims to ensure that activities of all employees are mobilised towards organisational goals. It is the connecting link between the preparatory functions of management (planning, organising, staffing) and the function of controlling.

**Directing** refers to the process of instructing, guiding, counselling, motivating, and leading people in the organisation to achieve its objective.

Directing not only involves orders and instructions by a superior to the subordinates, but includes many elements like supervision, motivation, leadership, and communication. It is one of the key managerial functions performed by every manager. Directing is a managerial process which takes place throughout the life of an organisation.

## Definitions of Directing

*   **Koontz and Donnell:** Direction is a complex function that includes all those activities which are designed to encourage subordinates to work effectively and efficiently.
*   **William Newman:** Directing deals with the steps a manager takes to get subordinates and others to carry out plans.
