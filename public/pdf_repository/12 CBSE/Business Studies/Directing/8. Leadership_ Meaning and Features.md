This section defines leadership, highlights its key characteristics, and distinguishes between the roles of a manager and a leader.

# Leadership: Meaning and Features

**Leadership** is the process of influencing the behaviour of people at work towards achievement of organisational goals.

*   Leadership indicates the ability of an individual to maintain good interpersonal relations with followers and motivate them to contribute for achieving organisational objectives.
*   An individual, who possess attributes of leadership is known as '**Leader**'. The leader plays a key role in the success of an organisation.

In the words of George Terry, Leadership is the activity of influencing people to strive willingly for group objectives.

In the words of Koontz and Weihrich, Leadership is the art or process of influencing people so that they will strive willingly and enthusiastically towards the achievement of group goals.

## Features of Leadership

The various features of leadership are as follows:

1.  Leadership indicates ability of an individual to influence others.
2.  It aims to bring change in the behaviour of others.
3.  It indicates interpersonal relations between leader and followers.
4.  It is exercised to achieve common goals of the organisation.
5.  It is a continuous process.

Both leader and followers play key role in Leadership Process. Many times, the success of an organisation is attributed to the leader, but due credit is not given to his followers. Many followers related factors like their skills, knowledge, commitment, willingness to cooperate, team spirit etc., make a person an effective leader. A person can be a successful leader only through willing cooperation and team spirit of followers. So, it is to be recognised that both leader and follower play key role in leadership process.

## Difference between Managers and Leaders

The terms 'manager' and 'leader' are often used interchangeably. However, it is important to distinguish them for purposes of management analysis. The difference between the two will be clear from the following comparison:

| Basis                       | Manager                                                         | Leader                                                                     |
| :-------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1. Focus                    | Focuses on influencing the behaviour of employees to achieve goals of the organisation. | Focuses to satisfy behaviour of each and every member of group and realisation of group goals. |
| 2. Scope                    | Always a leader as he has to influence behaviour of his subordinates. | Not always a manager. Informal groups may be lead by non-managers.         |
| 3. Formal or Informal organisation | Exists only informal organisation.                              | May exist in formal as well as informal organisation.                      |
| 4. Authority                | Have formal authority to influence behaviour of the employees.    | Have only informal authority arising due to trust & faith shown by group members. |

Finally, we can conclude that leadership is a part of management. It is rightly said, "All managers are leaders, but all leaders are not managers".
