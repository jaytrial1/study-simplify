This section provides practical steps and strategies that can be implemented by organizations and managers to overcome communication barriers and enhance the effectiveness of communication.

# Measures to Improve Communication Effectiveness

The barriers to effective communication exist in all organisations to a greater or lesser degree. Organisations keen on developing effective communication should adopt suitable measures to overcome the barriers and improve communication effectiveness. Following measures can be used to improve effectiveness of communication:

1.  **Clarify the ideas before Communication:** The message or problem to be communicated must be absolutely clear in the mind of sender. The entire problem should be studied in depth, analysed and stated in such a manner that is clearly conveyed to subordinates. The sender should know the objective of his message and should arrange it in a proper order.
2.  **Communicate according to the needs of receiver:** The level of understanding of receiver should be clear to the communicator. Manager or sender should adjust his communication or message not according to his own level or ability, but as per education and understanding levels of receiver or subordinate.
3.  **Consult others before communicating:** Before communicating the message, it is better to involve others in developing a plan for communication. Participation and involvement of subordinates may help to gain ready acceptance and willing cooperation of subordinates.
4.  **Be aware of languages, tone and content of message:** The sender should take care that the content of the message, tone, language used, etc. are the important aspects of effective communication. The tone or language used for conveying the message must be easily understandable to the receiver and should not offend his sentiments. The message should stimulate the receiver to provide response and feedback.
5.  **Convey things of help and value to listeners:** While conveying the message, it is better to know the interests and needs of the receiver. If the message relates directly or indirectly to such interests and needs, it certainly evokes response from the receiver.
6.  **Ensure Proper Feedback:** The communicator may ensure the success of communication by asking questions regarding the message conveyed. The receiver of communication must be encouraged to respond to communication. The communication process may be improved by the feedback received to make it more responsive.
7.  **Communicate for Present as well as Future:** In order to achieve effectiveness in communication process, communication must meet the present needs and should also aim at future goals of the enterprise.
8.  **Follow up Communications:** There should be regular follow up and review on the instructions given to subordinates. Such follow up helps to remove hurdles or misunderstanding, if any in implementing the instructions.
9.  **Be a good Listener:** To make the communication process effective, the receiver should be a good listener.
    *   Patient and attentive listening solves half of the problems.
    *   Superiors must develop the habit of patient listening and should also give indications of their interest in the message.
