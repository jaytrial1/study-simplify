# ORGANISATION STRUCTURE 

Organisation structure is the outcome of the organising process. An organisation structure denotes the pattern of relationship among the positions and jobs within an organisation, which is created during the organising process. It provides the basis on which managers and nonmanagerial employees perform the job assigned to them.
Organisation Structure refers to the framework within which managerial and operating tasks are performed.

- It specifies the relationships between people, work and resources.
- As business operates in a dynamic environment, management must continuously review its plans and organisation structure of the enterprise should also be subjected to periodic review to determine if modification is required.
- A good organisation structure should be dynamic so that it can change and adjust as per situations and business environment.
According to Peter Drucker, "Organisation Structure is an indispensable means; and the wrong structure will seriously impair business performance and even destroy it".


## Need for Organisation Structure

A proper organisation structure is essential to ensure a smooth flow of communication and better control over business operations.

- In case of one man show, organisation structure is not needed as all activities are performed by the owner himself.
- However, when the organisation grows, it becomes difficult to coordinate the functions and activities of different members.


## Benefits of Organisation Structure

- A proper organisation structure ensures a smooth flow of communication and better control over the operations of a business enterprise.
- An organisation structure provides the framework which enables the enterprise to function as an integrated unit by regulating and coordinating the responsibilities of individuals and departments.


## How is Organisation Structure Created?

The structure of an organisation is deliberately designed and planned. It is greatly influenced effectively managed by a superior. This determines the levels of management in the structure.
