# IMPORTANCE OF ORGANISING 

Performance of the organising function can pave the way for a smooth transition of the enterprise in accordance with the dynamic business environment. Organising function helps in the survival and growth of an enterprise and this function must be properly performed to complete tasks and meet the goals successfully.
The need and importance of organising can be understood on the basis of following points:

1. Benefits of Specialisation: The process of organising leads to a systematic allocation of jobs amongst the work force. Such division of work reduces the workload and increases the productivity. Repetitive performance of a particular work also leads to specialisation.
2. Clarity in Working Relationships: Organising establishes the working relationships, which clarifies the lines of communication and specifies who is to report to whom.

- Each person knows his superior, from whom he has to take orders.
- This removes ambiguity in transfer of information and instructions.

- Clarity in superior-subordinate relationships helps in fixing responsibility and specification of the extent of authority to be exercised by an individual.

3. Optimum Utilisation of Resources: Organising ensures the optimum use of human, financial and material resources.

- In organising, work is assigned as per ability and proper assignment of jobs avoids overlapping of work and also makes possible the best use of resources.
- Avoidance of duplication of work helps in preventing confusion and minimising the wastage of resources and efforts.

4. Adaptation to Change: An organisation operates in a dynamic environment. Such an ever changing environment poses both challenges and opportunities to the business.

- The process of organising helps the enterprise to adjust itself as per changes in the business environment. It allows the organisation structure to be suitably modified and revision of inter-relationships amongst managerial levels to pave the way for a smooth transition.
- Organising provides stability to the enterprise, so that it can continue to survive and grow inspite of changes.

5. Effective Administration: Organising provides a clear description of jobs and clarifies authority and responsibility relationship.

- It helps to avoid confusion and duplication of efforts.
- Clarity in working relationships enables proper execution of work and brings effectiveness in administration.

6. Development of Personnel: Organising stimulates creativity amongst the managers through the use of delegation of authority.

- Effective delegation allows the managers to reduce their workload by assigning routine jobs to their subordinates. It gives them time to concentrate on strategic issues and explore new areas for growth.
- It also gives them the opportunity to innovate, which helps to strengthen the company's competitive position.
- Delegation also develops the subordinate by giving them the ability to deal effectively with challenges and realising their full potential.

7. Expansion and Growth: Organising process creates favourable conditions for expansion and diversification of enterprise by enabling it to deviate from existing norms and taking up new challenges.

- Organising allows a business enterprise to add more job positions, departments and even diversify their product lines. It helps in expansion and growth of business. help to increase customer base, sales and profit.
