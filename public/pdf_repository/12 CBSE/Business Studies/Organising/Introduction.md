# INTRODUCTION

After the plans have been drawn, the second important function of management is 'Organising'. The activities of an enterprise must be organised in such a manner that plans can be successfully implemented.

- Organising ensures that efforts are directed towards the attainment of goals in such a manner that the resources are used optimally and people are able to work collectively and effectively for a common purpose. In short, organising is a means for translating plans into action.
- Organising function leads to the creation of an Organisational Structure which includes:
- Designing of roles to be filled by suitably skilled people; and
- Defining the inter relationship between these roles so that ambiguity in performance of duties can be eliminated.
