# Profit Maximisation vs Wealth Maximisation

The objective of **profit maximisation** measures the performance of a company by looking at its total profit. It does not consider the risk, which the firm undertakes in maximizing the profits. Although profit maxmisation is an important objective of financial management, it has been severely criticised on following grounds:
*   It ignores the timing of returns.
*   It does not consider the uncertainty of future earnings.
*   It ignores the effect of dividend policy on market value of shares.
*   It does not differentiate between the short-term and long-term profits.
*   It ignores the interest of consumers, workers, and the society.

As an alternative to profit maximisation, Prof. Solomon suggested the concept of **wealth maximisation**. The wealth maximisation objective considers all future cash flows, dividends, earning per share, risk of a decision, etc. This objective also helps in achieving other objectives of financial management.
