# MEANING OF BUSINESS FINANCE

Business finance refers to the money required for carrying out business activities. It is basically concerned with acquisition of funds, use of funds and distribution of profits by a business enterprise.
Need for Business Finance
Almost all business activities require some finance. Finance is needed:

- To start and establish a business

- To run day-to-day activities of business like payment for raw material, salaries, etc.
- To modernize, expand and diversify the business
- To purchase assets (tangible assets like machinery, land, building, etc. and intangible assets like trademarks, patents, etc.).
Availability of adequate finance is very crucial for the survival and growth of a business.
