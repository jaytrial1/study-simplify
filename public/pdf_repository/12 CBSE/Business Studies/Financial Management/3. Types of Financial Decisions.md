## Investment Decision

A firm's resources are scarce in comparison to the requirements. So, the firm should invest them in a manner which generates highest possible return for their investors. **Investment Decision** is concerned with investment of firm's funds in different assets.

Investment Decisions can be categorised as:

1.  **Capital Budgeting Decisions or Long-term Investment Decisions:** These decisions relate to the investment in fixed assets. Such decisions involve commitment for long-term.
    *   **Examples:**
        *   Purchase of a new Fixed Asset like Plant and Machinery, Land, Building, etc.;
        *   Opening a new Branch;
        *   Launch of a new Product Line;
        *   Investing in Advanced Techniques of Production.
        *   Expenditure on Research and Development (R\&D) Programme.

## Importance of Capital Budgeting Decisions

Capital Budgeting decisions are among the most crucial and critical business decisions as a bad decision may lead to adverse financial consequences. Their importance will be clear from the following points:

1.  **Long-term Growth and effects:** These decisions have long-term implications for the firm. The funds invested in long-term assets are likely to yield returns in the future. Therefore, such decisions considerably affect the growth and future prospects of the business.
2.  **Large amount of funds involved:** These decisions result in a substantial portion of capital funds being blocked in the long-term projects.
    *   So, such decisions are very crucial & firm must carefully plan its investment programme.
    *   This may involve decisions like where to procure funds from and at what rate of interest.
3.  **Risk involved:** Capital budgeting decisions involve huge funds. It affects the returns of the firm as a whole in the long-term. So, these decisions influence the overall business risk complexion of the firm.
4.  **Irreversible Decisions:** Most of the capital budgeting decisions cannot be reversed or reverted without incurring heavy losses. Abandoning a project in midway involves adverse financial consequences. Therefore, they should be taken after evaluating each and every minute detail, otherwise firm may have to incur heavy losses.

## Factors Affecting Capital Budgeting Decision

A number of projects are often available to a business to invest in. In such a case, each project needs to be evaluated carefully and, depending upon the returns, a particular project is either selected or rejected. If there is only one project, its viability is seen in terms of rate of return and is compared with the industry's average.
There are certain factors which affect capital budgeting decisions:

1.  **Cash Flows of the Project:** When a firm takes an investment decision involving huge amount, it expects to generate some cash flows over a period.
    *   These cash flows are in the form of a series of cash receipts and payments over the life of an investment.
    *   Such cash flows should be carefully analysed before making a capital budgeting decision.
2.  **Rate of Return:** The firm should compare the rate of return expected and risk involved in different investment projects. For example, If there are two projects: A and B (with same risk involved), with a rate of return of $10 \%$ and $12 \%$ respectively, then under normal circumstance, firm will definitely select B.
3.  **Investment Criteria:** The decision to invest in a particular project involves a number of calculations regarding the amount of investment, interest rate, cash flows and rate of return. There are different techniques to evaluate investment proposals which are known as **capital budgeting techniques**. These techniques are applied to each proposal before selecting a particular project.
