This section continues the description of the selection process, covering the interview, background checks, decision making, and formalizing the employment.

3.  **Employment Interview**

All those applicants who qualify the tests are called for an interview. Interview is a formal, indepth conversation conducted to evaluate the applicant's suitability for the job.

*   The role of interviewer is to seek information and that of the interviewee is to provide the same.
*   Interview also gives a chance to the interviewee (candidate) to get information regarding salary, working conditions, posting, etc.

4.  **Reference and Background Checks**

A candidate is generally required to give names, addresses and telephone numbers of references for the purpose of verifying information and gaining additional information about him. The aim is to check the character background and social relations of the candidate. The candidates successful in the interview are generally investigated on the basis of references supplied by them. References can be of previous employers, known persons, teachers, university professors, etc.
However, references may be biased as the names are given by the candidate himself.

5.  **Selection Decision**

The candidates who cross all the hurdles are included in the final list of selection. Before making the final decision about selection, the views and opinion of the concerned manager (manager responsible for the performance of the new employee) is considered.

6.  **Medical Examination**

Before making the job offer, the candidate is required to undergo a medical test. The job offer is given to the candidate who is declared fit after the medical examination.

7.  **Job Offer**

The candidates, who have passed all the previous hurdles, are issued job offer. Job offer is made through a letter of appointment to confirm his acceptance.

*   The letter contains terms and conditions of employment, pay scale and other benefits associated with the job.
*   Such a letter generally contains a date by which the appointee must report on duty.
*   The appointee must be given reasonable time for reporting.

8.  **Contract of Employment**

After the candidate accepts the job offer, a written contract of employment is made between the employer and the candidate.

*   Candidate is also required to provide certain vital details in a form, duly authenticated and attested by him. Such attestation form is kept by the company as a valid record for future reference.
*   The contract of employment consist of necessary information regarding job title, duties, responsibilities, date of joining, pay and allowances, hours of work, leave rules, disciplinary rules, termination procedure, etc.

## Difference between Recruitment and Selection

| Basis      | Recruitment                                          | Selection                                                |
| :--------- | :--------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Purpose** | Aims to create a large pool of applicants.           | Aims to ensure that competent candidate is employed.     |
| **Nature** | Positive process; encourages people to apply.        | Negative process; rejects a larger proportion of candidates. |
| **Process** | Simple process; candidates don't cross many hurdles. | Complex process; candidates cross several hurdles.       |
| **Stage**  | Precedes selection process.                          | Starts after recruitment.                                |
