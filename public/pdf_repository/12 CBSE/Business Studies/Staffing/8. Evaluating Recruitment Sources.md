This section presents the advantages and disadvantages of using internal and external sources for recruitment and provides a comparative summary.

## Merits of Internal Sources

1.  **Motivation:** It motivates the employees to improve their performance. A promotion at a higher level may lead to a chain of promotion at lower levels. This motivates the employees to improve their performance through learning and practice. Employees work with their commitment and loyalty and remain satisfied within jobs and peace prevails in the enterprise because of promotional avenues.
2.  **Simplified Process:** It simplifies the process of selection and placement as the candidates are already known to the organisation and can be evaluated more accurately.
3.  **Training:** Through transfer, employees get training, which prepares them for higher jobs. There is no need for induction training as employees are already familiar with the organisation.
4.  **Optimal Utilisation:** Transfer has the benefit of shifting surplus workforce to those departments where there is shortage of staff.
5.  **Economical:** This process is economical or cheaper as compared to getting candidates from external sources as no money needs to be spent on searching for sources of recruitment and no time is wasted in long selection process.

## Demerits of Internal Sources

1.  **Lack of Fresh Talent:** It prevents induction of fresh talent. By stopping 'infusion of new blood' into the organisation, it may also lead to inbreeding, i.e. existing employees may continue to work and behave in old ways even after promotion and transfer.
2.  **Lethargy:** The employees may become lethargic if they know that they will automatically get timebound promotions.
3.  **Limited Scope:** A new company cannot use internal sources of recruitment. Also, no organisation can fill all its vacancies from internal sources.
4.  **Reduced Competition:** It reduces the spirit of competition among the employees as there is no competition from the outside world.
5.  **Reduced Productivity:** Frequent transfers of employees may often reduce the productivity of the organisation.

## Merits of External Sources

1.  **Qualified Personnel:** External sources help management to attract qualified and trained people to apply for vacant jobs in the organisation.
2.  **Wider Choice:** External sources provide wide choice and help the enterprise to make right choice of candidates.
3.  **Fresh Talent:** The present employees may be insufficient or they may not fulfil the specifications of the jobs to be filled. Through external recruitment, enterprise can expect to get fresh, talented candidates from outside. This means infusion of new blood and new ideas into the enterprise.
4.  **Competitive Spirit:** External candidates are expected to be trained and talented. As internal candidates have to compete with them, it induces the existing employees to work harder to show better performance.

## Demerits or Limitations of External Sources

1.  **Dissatisfaction among Existing Staff:** Recruitment from outside may cause dissatisfaction and frustration among the existing employees. They may feel that their chances of promotion are reduced.
2.  **Lengthy Process:** External recruitment is a time consuming process as vacancies are publicised and enterprise has to wait for the response of prospective candidates.
3.  **Costly Process:** It is very costly to recruit staff from external sources as lot of money is spent on advertising and processing the applications.

## Difference between Internal and External Sources of Recruitment

| Basis        | Internal Recruitment                                     | External Recruitment                                         |
| :----------- | :------------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Meaning**  | Making use of existing staff to fill up vacant jobs.     | Searching for employees from sources outside the organisation. |
| **Choice**   | Provides limited choice.                                 | Provides wide choice.                                        |
**Economy**  | Cheaper as there is no cost of publicising vacancy.      | Costly as lot of money is spent on publicising.              |
| **Time**     | Less time consuming.                                     | Requires a lot of time.                                      |
| **Motivation** | Motivates the existing staff to improve performance.     | Makes the existing staff dissatisfied.                       |
| **Quality**  | No scope for fresh talent.                               | Infuses new blood & new talent.                              |
