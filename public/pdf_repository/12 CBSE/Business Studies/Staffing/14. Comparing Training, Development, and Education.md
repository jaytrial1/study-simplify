This section clarifies the distinct meanings and purposes of training, development, and education, which are sometimes used interchangeably.

## Difference between Training and Development

| Basis                     | Training                                                                 | Development                                                                 |
| :------------------------ | :----------------------------------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Concept**               | Process of increasing knowledge & skills for a specific job.             | Process of learning and growth for overall potential.                       |
| **Purpose**               | Enables employees to do the present job better.                          | Aims for overall growth and prepares for future roles.                      |
| **Orientation**           | Job oriented process.                                                    | Career oriented process.                                                    |
| **Scope of learning**     | Narrow scope, part of development.                                       | Broader in scope, includes training.                                        |
| **Suitability**           | More suitable for technical staff.                                       | More suitable for managerial staff.                                         |
| **Duration**              | Short-term process.                                                      | Long-term process as it is career-oriented.                                 |
| **Level of trainees involved** | Mostly used for non-managerial personnel (operative employees).          | Generally applied to the growth of managerial personnel.                    |
| **Depth of knowledge imparted** | Knowledge is imparted for doing a particular job.                      | Knowledge is imparted for growth of an employee in all respects.            |
| **Initiative in learning** | The employer takes initiative in providing training to his employees.    | The individual takes the initiative himself for his growth and development. |

## Training, Development and Education

Training, development and education are sometimes interchangeably used. However, this is not correct. Let us now make comparison between training, development and education.

| Basis         | Training                                                    | Development                                                         | Education                                                       |
| :------------ | :---------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------ | :-------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Meaning**   | Process of increasing knowledge, skills & abilities for a particular job. | Process of learning and involves overall growth of the employees. | Process of increasing the knowledge and understanding of employees. |
| **Purpose**   | Enables employees to do the job better.                     | Enables the overall growth of the employees.                        | Develops general knowledge and understanding.                   |
| **Orientation** | Job oriented.                                               | Career oriented.                                                    | Knowledge oriented.                                             |
| **Scope**     | Narrow in scope.                                            | Broader in scope.                                                   | Its scope is broader than training.                             |
| **Beneficial**| Beneficial to achieve objectives of organisation.           | Beneficial to achieve the objectives of both organisation and individual. | Benefits a lot in achieving goals of the individuals.           |
