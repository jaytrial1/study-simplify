# TRAINING AND DEVELOPMENT 

Training refers to a process of increasing the knowledge, skills and abilities of employees for doing a particular job.

- It is a systematic and continuous process of improving skills.

- Training aims to enable the employee to perform present job better or to prepare him for a higher position with increased responsibilities.
Development refers to overall growth of the employee. It seeks to prepare employees for handling more responsible and challenging jobs by developing their hidden qualities and talents.


## Importance or Benefits of Training and Development 

Every organisation needs trained and experienced people to survive and grow in this competing environment. In the rapidly changing environment, the jobs have become more complex and training and development is essential to maintain a knowledgeable work force.
Training and development plays an important role in improving the effectiveness and efficiency of the organisation and the employees.

## Importance or Benefits to the Organisation

The trained employees perform better than the untrained employees and they make less wastage of resources and give higher productivity. The various benefits of training and development to the organisation are:

1. Systematic Learning: The systematic learning process of training teaches workers to make the most efficient use of resources. It is better than hit and trial methods which lead to wastage of efforts and money.
2. Increased Productivity: Well trained employees give better performance on the job, which increases the productivity both in terms of quantity and quality, leading to higher profits.
3. Prepare Future Managers: Training enables the employees to take more challenging jobs and prepare them to take position of future manager in case of emergency.
4. Boost Morale of Employees: Training increases morale of the employees and reduces absenteeism and employees' turnover.
5. Better Response to Fast Changing Environment: Training helps to keep pace with the rapid changes taking place in the technological and economic environment.

## Importance or Benefits to the Employees

1. Career Growth: Training helps employees to advance in their careers as it provides new, improved and advance knowledge and skills.
2. Increased Earnings: Training helps to improve the performance and productivity of workers, which help them to earn more.
3. Safety of Employees: The trained workers are less prone to accidents as they are more efficient to handle machines and materials.
4. High Morale: The trained employees have greater job satisfaction as they know their jobs. It helps to increase their morale.

Difference between Training and Development

| Basis | Training | Development |
| :--: | :--: | :--: |
| 1. Concept | It is a process of increasing knowledge \& skills. | It is a process of learning and growth. |
| 2. Purpose | Training aims to enable the employees to do the job better. | Development aims for overall growth of the employees. |
| 3. Orientation | It is job oriented process. | It is career oriented process. |
| 4. Scope of learning | It has narrow scope as it is a part of development. | It is broader in scope as it includes training. |
| 5. Suitability | It is more suitable for technical staff. | Development is more suitable for managerial staff. |
| 6. Duration | It is a short-term process. | It is a long-term process as it is careeroriented. |
| 7. Level of trainees involved | It is mostly used for non-managerial personnel (operative employees). | It is generally applied to the growth of managerial personnel. |
| 8. Depth of knowledge imparted | The knowledge is imparted for doing a particular job. | The knowledge is imparted for growth of an employee in all respects. |
| 9. Initiative in learning | The employer takes initiative in providing training to his employees. | The individual takes the initiative himself for his growth and development. |

## Training, Development and Education 

Training, development and education are sometimes interchangeably used. However, this is not correct. Let us now make comparison between training, development and education.

| Basis | Training | Development | Education |
| :--: | :--: | :--: | :--: |
| 1. Meaning | It is a process of increasing the knowledge, skills \& abilities of employees to do a particular job. | It is a process of learning and involves overall growth of the employees. | It is the process of increasing the knowledge and understanding of employees. |
| 2. Purpose | It aims to enable the employees to do the job better. | It aims to enable the overall growth of the employees. | It aims to develop general knowledge and understanding. |
| 3. Orientation | It is job oriented. | It is career oriented. | It is knowledge oriented. |
| 4. Scope | It is narrow in scope. | It is broader in scope. | Its scope is broader than training. |
| 5. Beneficial | It is beneficial to achieve objectives of organisation. | It is beneficial to achieve the objectives of both organisation and individual. | It benefits a lot in achieving goals of the individuals. |
