This section provides a summary of the three primary components or aspects that constitute the staffing function.

# Aspects or Components of Staffing

The three important aspects of staffing are:

1.  **Recruitment:** Recruitment is a positive step which aims at attracting number of candidates to apply for the given job.
2.  **Selection:** Selection is a negative process, which aims at selecting the most suitable person out of the various candidates, who have applied for the job.
3.  **Training:** Training is concerned with upgradation of the knowledge and skills of the employees so that their ability to perform can be enhanced.

It must be noted that several factors such as supply and demand of specific skills in the labour market, unemployment rate, labour market conditions, legal and political considerations, company's image, policy, human resource planning cost, technological developments and general economic environment etc., will influence the way recruitment, staffing and training will be actually carried out.
