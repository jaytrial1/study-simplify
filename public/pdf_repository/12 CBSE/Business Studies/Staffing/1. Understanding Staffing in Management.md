This section introduces the concept of staffing within an organization and its fundamental importance after planning and organizing have been completed.

# Introduction to Staffing

It is important to have a good organisation structure, but it is even more important to fill this structure with the right people. An organisation can make optimum use of physical and financial resources only through competent human resources. As Narayana Murthy (Former CEO of Infosys) stated, "Our assets walk out of the door each evening. We have to make sure that they come back the next morning."

Thus, after preparing plans and designing suitable structure of organisation, the function of staffing is performed by the managers. Staffing is primarily concerned with finding the right person for the right position at the right time. The foundation of any organisation is the talented and hardworking people, who are the principal assets of any firm. So, adequate staffing or the provision for appropriate human resources is an essential requirement for any organisation's success.

# Meaning of Staffing

The term 'Staffing' stands for manning various positions in the organisation. Staffing is that part of the process of management which is concerned with obtaining, utilising and maintaining a satisfactory and satisfied work force.

*   Staffing is the process of obtaining and maintaining capable and competent people to fill all positions.
*   The scope of staffing is not restricted to just filling the job positions. It includes a series of activities like manpower planning, recruitment, selection, placement, promotion, appraisal and development of personnel, to fill the roles designed into the organisation structure.
*   Staffing recognises the importance of every single person employed by an organisation as it is the individual worker, who is the ultimate performer.
*   Staffing fills the Organisation Structure: An organisation structure is only an empty shell without the right kind of persons. Staffing aims to fill these empty shells with the right people.

## Staffing is needed in both New and Existing Organisations

*   In a new enterprise, the staffing function is performed after planning and organising. After making the plans and creating the organisation structure, the management gets to know the human resource requirements. After that, management starts with the activities relating to recruiting, selecting and training people, to fulfil the requirements of the enterprise.
*   In an existing enterprise, staffing is a continuous process because new jobs may be created and some of the existing employees may leave the organisation.
