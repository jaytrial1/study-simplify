# STAFFING AND HUMAN RESOURCE MANAGEMENT

The staffing function deals with the human element of management. Managing the human component is the most important task as success of an organisation is determined to a great extent on the competence, motivation and performance of its human resources.
Staffing Function is performed by all managers at all levels. However, its scope is different in small and large organisations.

- In small organisations, the function of staffing is the responsibility of all managers and managers perform all duties related to employees salaries, welfare and working conditions.
- However, as organisations grow in size, a separate department called the 'Human Resource Department (HRD)' is formed, with specialists to manage the people.
Human Resource Management (HRM) is a specialised area which requires expertise of many people. The number of human resource specialists and size of this department gives an indication of the size of the business as well. In a large company, Human Resources Department will contain specialists for each function of this department. HRM may be defined as the art of procuring, developing and maintaining competent workforce to achieve the goals of an organisation in an effective and efficient manner.


## Specialised Activities to be Performed by Human Resource Personnel

Human Resource Management includes many specialised activities and duties which the human resource personnel must perform. These duties are:

- Recruitment i.e., search for qualified people.
- Analysing jobs, collecting information about jobs to prepare job descriptions.
- Selection.

- Developing compensation and incentive plans.
- Placement and orientation.
- Training and development of employees for efficient performance and career growth.
- Performance Appraisal.
- Maintaining labour relations and union management relations.
- Handling grievances and complaints.
- Providing for social security and welfare of employees.
- Defending the company in law suits and avoiding legal complications.


## Evolution of Human Resource Management 

The present day HRM has evolved from a number of inter-related developments:

1. Labour Welfare Officer: The industrial revolution led to the emergence of trade unions. These unions felt the need of a person, who could act as a mediator between the owners and workers. It resulted in appointment of 'Labour Welfare Officer'. However, his role was limited to the welfare activities of employees and he was looked down by both the workers and the owners.
2. Personnel Manager: With the introduction of factory system, number of persons began to be employed under one roof. So, there was a need for a person, who could assume the responsibility of recruitment, selection and placement of personnel. This led to the emergence of personnel officer, in the first place and personnel manager, later on.
3. Human Resource Manager: The fast changing technological developments necessitated the need for new skill development and training of employees. People came to be recognised as a valuable resource. Increase in the scope of work led to replacement of personnel manager with human resource manager.

## Conclusion

Staffing is an inherent part of Human Resource Management as it is the practice of finding, evaluating and establishing a working relationship with people.

Staffing is both a Line as well as a Staff Activity

- Staffing is a line activity because being a management function, it is performed by all managers.
- Staffing is a staff activity because of advisory role played by HRD. It is a distinct functional area of management, just like marketing management and financial management.
