This section focuses on the money market, its characteristics, key participants, and the types of instruments traded.

# The Money Market

The **Money Market** is a market for short-term funds, dealing in monetary assets with a maturity period of **up to one year**.

*   It does **not** have a physical location; most transactions occur over telephone and the internet.
*   The money market helps businesses meet their short-term financial needs (like working capital) and allows temporary deployment of excess funds for earning returns.

## Major Participants of Money Market

Key participants in the money market include:
*   Reserve Bank of India (RBI)
*   Commercial Banks
*   Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC)
*   General Insurance Corporation of India (GIC)
*   Non-Banking Finance Companies
*   State Governments
*   Large Corporate Houses
*   Mutual Funds

## Instruments of Money Market

Investment in the money market is facilitated through various **money market instruments**. Some common instruments are:
*   Treasury Bill
*   Commercial Paper
*   Call Money
*   Certificate of Deposit
*   Commercial Bill

## Features of Money Market Instruments

Money market instruments possess specific features:
1.  **Highly Liquid:** They are considered close substitutes for money due to their high liquidity, often facilitated by institutions like the Discount Finance House of India (DFHI). Their short maturity also leads to them being called 'Near Money'.
2.  **Less Risky:** Although generally unsecured, they are considered less risky due to their short duration and the financial soundness of their typical issuers.

## Features of Money Market

Based on the nature of its instruments and operations, the money market has the following characteristics:
1.  It is a market for short-term funds with instruments maturing within one year.
2.  Instruments traded are typically quite expensive, requiring significant investment outlay.
3.  Money market instruments are highly liquid.
4.  Money market instruments are generally less risky.
5.  Expected returns are relatively lower due to the short investment duration.
