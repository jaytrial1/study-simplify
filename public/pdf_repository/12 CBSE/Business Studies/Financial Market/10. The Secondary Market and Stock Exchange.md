# The Secondary Market and Stock Exchange

The **Secondary Market** is a market for the **sale and purchase of previously issued securities**.

*   It allows existing investors to sell their holdings (disinvest) and new investors to buy existing securities (enter the market).
*   It provides **liquidity and marketability** to existing securities.
*   It contributes to economic growth by facilitating the channelisation of funds to productive investments through disinvestment and reinvestment.
*   It is commonly known as the **'Stock Exchange'** or **'Stock Market'**.
*   Securities trading, clearing, and settlement are conducted within the regulatory framework set by SEBI.
*   Modern technology allows trading through stock exchanges from anywhere in the country via trading terminals.

In the secondary market, securities are exchanged **between investors**. The company that originally issued the securities is **not involved** in these transactions.

## Definition of Stock Exchange

According to the Securities Contracts (Regulation) Act, a **Stock Exchange** is an institution or body constituted for the purpose of assisting, regulating, or controlling the business of buying and selling or dealing in securities.

*   It provides a platform for trading existing securities.
*   It offers liquidity by enabling the exchange of securities for money and vice versa.
*   It promotes transaction safety and enhances the creditworthiness of listed companies.
