This section outlines the key functions performed by a stock exchange in the financial market.

# Functions of Stock Exchange

An efficient stock exchange fosters a positive environment for the primary market. Its important functions include:

1.  **Providing Liquidity and Marketability to Existing Securities:** The primary function is offering a ready market for buying and selling existing securities.
    *   This ready market ensures liquidity and easy marketability, allowing investments to be converted to cash when needed.
    *   It gives investors the flexibility to disinvest and reinvest their funds.
2.  **Pricing of Securities:** Share prices are determined by the forces of demand and supply on the stock exchange.
    *   The exchange provides continuous valuation and quotes prices for various securities, enabling investors to know the current worth of their investments.
    *   *Example:* Securities of profitable companies tend to be valued higher due to higher demand, while those of loss-making companies are valued lower due to low demand.
3.  **Safety of Transaction:** Stock exchange membership and dealings are strictly regulated by the legal framework.
    *   This ensures that investors have a safe and fair trading experience.
    *   Companies must fulfill stringent requirements to list their securities and operate within the exchange's legal framework even after listing.
4.  **Contributing to Economic Growth:** By providing a platform for buying and selling existing securities, the stock exchange facilitates the channelisation of savings into the most productive investment avenues through disinvestment and reinvestment.
    *   This process promotes **capital formation** and contributes to overall economic growth.
5.  **Spreading of Equity Cult:** The stock exchange encourages wider share ownership through regulating new issues, promoting better trading practices, and educating the public about investments.
    *   **Equity Cult** refers to investor behavior where interest in a stock is high, sometimes disproportionately to the company's fundamentals, driving investment in the equity market.
6.  **Providing Scope for Speculation:** Within legal provisions, the stock exchange allows for a controlled degree of speculative activity.
    *   Healthy speculation is considered necessary for maintaining liquidity and price continuity in the market.
