This section describes the primary market, its function in issuing new securities, and its contribution to capital formation.

# The Primary Market

The **Primary Market** is where securities are **sold for the first time**. It is also known as the **new issues market**.

*   Its core function is to facilitate the transfer of investible funds from savers to entrepreneurs (for new ventures or expansion) through the initial issue of securities.
*   Investors in this market include banks, financial institutions, insurance companies, mutual funds, and individuals.
*   Common securities issued include Equity shares, Preference shares, and Debentures.
*   Funds raised are used for purposes like setting up new projects, expansion, diversification, modernisation, mergers, and takeovers.

The Primary Market directly contributes to **Capital Formation** by channeling surplus funds from savers to entrepreneurs for setting up new projects, expansion, and diversification.
