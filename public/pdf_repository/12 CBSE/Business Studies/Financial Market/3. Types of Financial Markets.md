# Types of Financial Markets

Financial markets are broadly classified into two major segments based on the maturity of the financial instruments they handle:

1.  **Money Market:** This market deals with financial instruments that have a maturity period of **less than one year**. It is the market for short-term funds.
2.  **Capital Market:** This market deals with financial instruments that have a maturity period of **more than one year**. It is the market for medium and long-term funds.
