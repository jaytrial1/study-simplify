This section describes the shift from traditional floor trading to modern electronic systems and highlights the benefits of screen-based trading.

# Evolution of Stock Trading and Electronic Systems

Historically, securities trading on stock exchanges occurred on the trading floor using an **open outcry system** or auction method. Brokers would shout prices and deals were struck based on bids and offers.

In the present scenario, this auction method has been replaced by an **online screen-based electronic trading system**. Trading has moved from the physical stock market floor to brokers' offices.

*   A stock exchange has a central computer system connected to many terminals across the country.
*   Each broker (member of the exchange) has access to a terminal linked to the main system.
*   In screen-based trading, the broker enters buy or sell orders (including quantity and price) into the computer.
*   The system automatically matches matching buy and sell orders based on the best available prices. Unmatched orders remain visible on the screen.
*   The entire transaction process is visible on the computer screen, showing price movements in real-time during trading hours.

## Advantages of Electronic Trading Systems (Screen-based Trading)

Electronic trading offers significant advantages:
1.  **Transparency:** Participants can see prices of all securities in the market as transactions occur.
2.  **Efficiency in Price Fixing:** Efficient information flow helps in determining prices accurately. Screens display prices and market developments influencing share prices.
3.  **Operational Efficiency:** Reduces time, cost, and risk of error in transactions.
4.  **Increased Participation and Liquidity:** Allows a large number of participants from anywhere to trade through brokers, without needing to know each other. This enhances market liquidity.
5.  **Single Trading Platform:** Connects trading centers nationwide onto one platform, enabling online buying and selling of shares.
