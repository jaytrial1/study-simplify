This section discusses the transition from holding securities in physical certificates to the electronic Demat system and the problems associated with the older method.

# Holding Securities: Physical vs Electronic Form

In the past, a physical share certificate served as proof of ownership. Dealing in shares in physical form has largely been replaced by the **Demat System** for electronic storage due to several problems.

## Problems with Dealing in Physical Form

Dealing with physical share certificates presented several issues:
*   Theft of certificates.
*   Risk of fake or forged transfers.
*   Delays in the transfer process.
*   Extensive paperwork required for handling physical certificates or debentures.

To address these problems, **'Dematerialisation (Demat Account)'** was introduced with the help of a **'Depository'**.
