# Personal Selling

## Concept of Personal Selling

Personal Selling involves oral presentation of message in the form of conversation with one or more prospective customers for the purpose of making sales.
Under personal selling, companies appoint sales persons, who contact with prospective buyers to create product awareness and preferences with the aim of making sale.

## Features of Personal Selling

1. Personal Form: Personal selling involves direct face-to-face communication between the seller and the buyer. It leads to interactive discussion and personal contact between the two.
2. Development of Relationship: Personal selling helps in developing personal and friendly relationships between the salesperson and prospective customers. Such a healthy relationship has an important place in sales.
3. Two-Way communication: Personal selling is a two-way communication process as both buyer and seller can talk to each other. As a result, it gives better response.
4. Oral Conversation: Personal selling involves oral conversation between the sales person and the buyer.
5. Flexibility: It is highly flexible as salesperson can adjust the message according to the situation.

Advertising Vs Personal Selling

| Basis | Advertising | Personal Selling |
| :--: | :--: | :--: |
| 1. Kind of Communication | Impersonal communication. | Personal form of communication. |
| 2. Uniformity of message | It transmits uniform messages to all customers. | Message is not uniform and has to be adjusted as per customer. |
| 3. Flexibility | It is inflexible as message can't be adjusted. | It is highly flexible as message can be adjusted. |
| 4. Reach | It reaches large number of people. | It reaches limited people because of time and cost consideration. |
| 5. Cost | Cost per person reached is very low. | Cost per person reached is quite high. |
| 6. Time | It covers market in short time. | It needs lot of time to cover the entire market. |
| 7. Feedback | Direct feedback is not possible. | It provides direct and immediate feedback. |
| 8. Role | Advertising is more useful in creating \& building interest of the consumers in the firm's products. | Personal selling plays important role at the awareness stage of decision making. |
| 9. Type of customers | It is more useful for the ultimate consumers, who are large in numbers. | It is more useful for industrial buyers or to intermediaries such as dealers and retailers, who are less in numbers. |
| 10. Medium | The important medias are T.V., radio, newspapers, magazines, etc. | Personal selling makes use of sales staff. |
