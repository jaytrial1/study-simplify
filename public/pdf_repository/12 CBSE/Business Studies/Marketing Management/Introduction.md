# INTRODUCTION

Marketers undertake various activities to increase the demand for their products and earn profit by satisfying customers' needs and wants. People purchase products because they satisty some of their needs. Activities performed by the marketers to facilitate exchange of goods and services between producers and the users of such products are referred to as Marketing Activities.
Marketing is one of the important functional areas of management. Goods once produced are not going to serve any purpose unless these are exchanged for money or money's worth. This involves movement of goods from producers to the ultimate consumers. To bring producer

and consumer together, certain activities and functions are performed, which is the subject matter of marketing.
