# Sales Promotion 

## Concept of Sales Promotion

Sales Promotion refers to short-term incentives, which are offered to encourage the buyers to make immediate purchase of a product or service.
Sales promotion activities include offering cash discounts, sales contests, free gift offers, and free sample distribution. It supplements other promotional efforts such as advertising and personal selling. Sales promotion tools are used for:

- Customers in the form of discounts, free samples, contests, etc.
- Traders or Middlemen in the form of cooperative advertising, dealer discounts, dealer incentives, contests, etc.
- Sales Person in the form of bonus, contests, special offers, etc.

Sales promotions include only those activities that are used to provide short-term incentives to boost the sales of a firm.

## Advertising Vs Sales Promotion

| Basis | Advertising | Sales Promotion |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 1. Objective | It aims to attract customers towards a <br> particular brand. | It aims to convince customers to actually <br> purchase the product. |
| 2. Regularity | It is recurring in nature. | It is non-recurring. |
| 3. Usefulness | It is useful in creating demand and goodwill. | It is useful in increasing immediate sales. |
| 4. Time | It has long-term perspective. | It has short-term perspective. |
| 5. Media | TV, Newspaper, Magazines, etc. | Rebates, Free Samples, contests, etc. |
