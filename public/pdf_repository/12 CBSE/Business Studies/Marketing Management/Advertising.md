# Advertising

## Concept of Advertising

Advertising refers to an impersonal form of communication, which is paid for by the marketers (sponsors) to promote some goods or services.
In the words of David Ogilvy, If you're trying to persuade people to do something, or buy something, it seems to me you should use their language, the language in which they think.
In the words of Andy Travis, We find that advertising works the way the grass grows. You can never see it, but every week you have to move the lawn.

- Advertising is the most commonly used tool of promotion. Advertisement provides information or details regarding benefits, price and availability of the goods and services.
- The basic purpose is to increase the demand and sale of goods and services.
- Some common modes of advertising are: Newspapers, Television, Magazines, Radio, etc.


## Features of Advertising

The important distinguishing features of advertising are as follows:

1. Paid Form: Advertising is a paid form of communication. The sponsor has to bear the cost of communicating with the prospective customers.
2. Impersonality: Advertising is an impersonal method of promotion as there is no direct face-to-face contact between customer and advertiser. It creates a monologue (one-sided communication) and not a dialogue.

3. Identified Sponsor: Advertising is undertaken by some identified individual or company, who makes the advertising efforts and also bears the cost of it.

## Merits of Advertising 

Advertising, as a medium of communication, has the following merits:

1. Mass Reach: Advertising is a medium through which a large number of people can be reached over a vast geographical area. For example, advertisement on newspaper or TV reaches lakhs of people.
2. Enhancing Customer Satisfaction and Confidence: Advertising creates confidence amongst prospective buyers as they feel more comfortable and assured about the product quality and hence feel more satisfied.
3. Expressiveness: With the use of latest techniques, graphics and multimedia, modern advertising has become one of the most forceful mediums of communication. The special effects are created to make even simple products and messages more expressive, appealing and attractive.
4. Economy: Advertising is economical because of its mass reach. The total advertising cost is spread over a large number of people. As a result, the per-unit cost of reaching a prospective buyer is very less as compared to other promotional techniques.
