# PROMOTION

After product planning, pricing and distribution, the next task of marketing manager is to formulate a suitable strategy to communicate about the product to prospective buyers. In the how it can satisfy their needs and wants or may not be convinced about its utility and benefits. and persuade them to buy these products. Promotion makes use of various tools to encourage exchange of goods and services in the market.

## Elements of Promotion Mix 

Promotion mix refers to combination of promotional tools used by an organisation to achieve its communication objectives.
The various promotional tools are:
A. Advertising
B. Personal Selling
C. Sales Promotion
D. Public Relation**
*It must be noted that Public Relation and Publicity taken together become the fourth ingredient of promotion mix. However, the scope of syllabus is restricted to public relation only.
These tools are also known as elements of promotion mix. These tools can be used in different combinations to achieve the goals of promotion. For example, firms selling consumer goods may use more of advertising, while firms selling industrial goods may use more of personal selling. The combination of these elements to be used by a firm depends on various factors such as nature of market, nature of product, promotion budget, objectives of promotion, etc.
