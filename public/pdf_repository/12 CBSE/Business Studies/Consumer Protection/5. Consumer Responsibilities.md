This section outlines the duties and responsibilities that consumers should uphold alongside their rights to ensure effective consumer protection.

# Consumer Responsibilities

In addition to rights, consumers also have certain responsibilities. Rights and responsibilities are two sides of the same coin. Unless a consumer fulfills these responsibilities, enforcing rights may not be possible. Consumers should keep the following responsibilities in mind:

1.  **Knowledge about the market:** Be aware of available goods and services for intelligent choices.
2.  **Quality Conscious:** Buy standardised goods with quality assurance marks. Look for marks like:

3.  **Use Products Safely:** Learn about risks, follow instructions, and use products safely.
4.  **Refer Labels Carefully:** Read labels for information on prices, weight, manufacturing & expiry dates, etc.
5.  **Insist on Cash Memo:** Always ask for a cash memo as proof of purchase for filing complaints.
6.  **Be Honest:** Be honest in dealings, buy legal goods, and discourage malpractices like black-marketing and hoarding.
7.  **File a Complaint:** File a complaint in an appropriate consumer forum for defects or deficiencies. Filing complaints, even for small losses, encourages businessmen to supply quality products.
8.  **Form Consumer Organisations:** Form consumer societies to educate members and safeguard interests.
9.  **Respect the Environment:** Save the environment by avoiding waste, littering, and pollution.
10. **Assert yourself:** Ensure you get a fair deal.

Rights and Responsibilities go simultaneously. To ask for rights, responsibilities should be fulfilled first.
