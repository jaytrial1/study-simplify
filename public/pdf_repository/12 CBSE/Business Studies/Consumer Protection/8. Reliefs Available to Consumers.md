This section details the various remedies or forms of relief that a consumer court or redressal agency can grant if a consumer's complaint is found to be genuine.

# Reliefs (Remedies) Available to Consumers

If the consumer court or redressal agency is satisfied about the genuineness of the complaint, it can grant one or more of the following reliefs to the aggrieved party:
1.  To remove the defects in goods or deficiencies in services.
2.  To replace the goods with new, defect-free goods.
3.  To return the price or charges paid, along with interest.
4.  To pay compensation for any loss or injury due to the opposite party's negligence.
5.  To discontinue and not repeat unfair or restrictive trade practices.
6.  Not to offer hazardous or unsafe goods for sale.
7.  To withdraw hazardous goods from sale.
8.  To cease manufacture of hazardous goods and desist from offering hazardous services.
9.  To pay a sum determined by the agency if loss/injury was suffered by a large number of unidentifiable consumers (minimum 25% of the value of defective goods/service).
10. To issue corrective advertising to neutralise misleading advertising effect, at the cost of the responsible party.
11. To provide adequate costs to parties.
12. To cease and desist from issuing any misleading advertisement.
