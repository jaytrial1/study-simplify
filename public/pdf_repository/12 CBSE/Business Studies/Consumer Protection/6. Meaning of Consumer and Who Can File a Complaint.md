This section defines who is considered a 'consumer' under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, and lists the various entities or individuals who are eligible to file a complaint.

# Meaning of Consumer

A consumer is generally understood as a person who uses or consumes any product. However, the term 'Consumer' in the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 is comprehensive.

As per the Act, a '**Consumer**' is a person who buys any goods or avails services for a consideration, paid or promised, or partly paid or partly promised, or under any scheme of deferred payment. It includes any user of such goods or beneficiary of services if such use is made with the buyer's approval. It includes offline or online transactions, teleshopping, direct selling, or multilevel marketing.

**Note:** A person obtaining goods or services for resale or commercial purpose is not treated as a consumer. However, "commercial purpose" does *not* include using goods or services for earning livelihood through self-employment. For example, a person buying a computer for job work at home is a consumer.

## Examples for Better Understanding

A person is a consumer in the following cases:
*   Buying a car in cash or under finance.
*   Using a friend's car with their approval.
*   Hiring a Taxi from a Tourist Company.
*   Using a Taxi hired by a friend.

## Who are NOT Termed as Consumers under the Act:

**In case of Consumer of Goods:**
*   Any person obtaining goods without consideration.
*   Any person using goods without the buyer's approval.
*   Any person obtaining goods for resale or commercial purpose.

**In case of Consumer of Services:**
*   Any person hiring or availing services without consideration.
*   Any person availing service without the hirer's approval.
*   Any person availing service for resale or commercial purpose.

## Who can file a complaint?

A complaint before the appropriate consumer forum can be filed by:
1.  A consumer.
2.  Any voluntary consumer association registered under law.
3.  The Central Government or any State Government.
4.  The Central Authority*.
5.  One or more consumers with the same interest (numerous consumers).
6.  The legal heir or representative in case of a consumer's death.
7.  The parent or legal guardian if the consumer is a minor.

*The Consumer Protection Act, 2019 established the **Central Consumer Protection Authority (CCPA)**, known as the 'Central Authority', to regulate violations of consumer rights, unfair trade practices, and false/misleading advertisements, and to promote, protect, and enforce consumer rights.

## Against whom a complaint can be filed?

A complaint can be filed against:
*   The seller, manufacturer, or dealer of **defective goods**. 'Defect' means any fault, imperfection, or shortcoming in quality, quantity, or purity.
*   The provider of services if they are **deficient**. 'Deficiency' means any imperfection, shortcoming, or inadequacy in the quality, nature, and manner of performance of services.
