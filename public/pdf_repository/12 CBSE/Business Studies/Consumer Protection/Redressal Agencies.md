# REDRESSAL AGENCIES 

For the redressal of consumer grievances, the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 has set up three. tier machinery:

## District Commission (or District Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission)

The State Government shall establish a District Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission, to be known as the District Commission, in each district of the State. State Government can set more than one District Commission in each district, if it deems fit.

- Composition: Each District Commission shall consist of:
(a) A President; and
(b) Not less than two and not more than such number of members as may be prescribed, in consultation with the Central Government.
They all are appointed by the state government.
- Jurisdiction: The District Commission shall have jurisdiction to entertain complaints where the value of the goods or services paid as consideration does not exceed ₹ 1 crore.
- Settlement through Mediation: At the first hearing of the complaint or at any later stage, if it appears to the District Commission that there exists elements of a settlement which may be acceptable to the parties, except in such cases as may be prescribed, it may direct the parties to give in writing, within 5 days, consent to have their dispute settled by mediation. In the event of failure of settlement by mediation, the party proceeds with the complaint.

- District Commission refers the complaint to the opposite party and if required, goods or a sample can also be sent to laboratory for testing.
- After considering the laboratory report and hearing all facts, the order is passed.
- Appeal: Any person aggrieved by an order made by the District Commission may prefer an appeal against such order to the State Commission on the grounds of facts or law within a period of 45 days from the date of the order.
- If the aggrieved party is required to pay any amount in terms of an order of the District Commission, then he/she can appeal to State Commission only when he/she has deposited $50 \%$ of that amount in the manner prescribed.


## State Commission (or State Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission)

The State Government shall establish a State Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission, to be known as the State Commission, in the State.

- Composition: Each State Commission shall consist of:
(a) A President; and
(b) Not less than four or not more than such number of members as may be prescribed, in consultation with the Central Government.

They all are appointed by state government.

- Jurisdiction: The State Commission shall have jurisdiction to entertain:
(a) Complaints where the value of the goods or services paid as consideration exceeds $\$ 1$ crore, but does not exceed $\$ 10$ crore; and
(b) Appeals against the orders of any District Commission within the State.

Commission refers the complaint to the opposite party and if required, goods or a sample can also be sent to laboratory for testing. After considering the laboratory report and hearing all facts, the order is passed.

- Appeal: Any person aggrieved by an order made by the State Commission may prefer an appeal against such order to the National Commission within a period of 30 days from the date of the order in such form and manner as may be prescribed.
- If the aggrieved party is required to pay any amount in terms of an order of the State Commission, then he/she can appeal to National Commission only when he/she has deposited $50 \%$ of that amount in the manner prescribed.


## National Commission (or National Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission) 

The Central Government shall establish a National Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission, to be known as the National Commission.

- Composition: Each National Commission shall consist of:
(a) A President; and
(b) not less than four and not more than such number of members as may be prescribed. They all are appointed by central government.
- Jurisdiction: The National Commission shall have jurisdiction to entertain:
(a) Complaints where the value of the goods or services paid as consideration exceeds $\$ 10$ crore; and
(b) Appeals against the orders of any State Commission.

On receiving the complaint, National Commission refers the complaint to the opposite party and if required, goods or a sample can also be sent to laboratory for testing. After considering the laboratory report and hearing all facts, the order is passed.

- Appeal: Any person aggrieved by an order made by the National Commission may prefer an appeal against such order to the Supreme Court within a period of 30 days from the date of the order.

- The Maharashtra State Consumer Commission asked a leading soft drink maker to pay a claim of $\$ 1,000,000$ to Chandrashekar, when he complained that he has found some solid objects inside a sealed bottle.

Always Remember: Only those cases can be appealed in Supreme Court in which value of goods or services paid as consideration exceeds ₹ 10 crore and aggrieved party is not satisfied with the order of National Commission. Moreover, in a case decided by the District Commission, the appeal of the same filed before the State Commission and thereafter, the order of the State Commission can be challenged before the National Commission and no further.

Author's Note: As per Consumer Protection Act, 2019, State Commission, National Commission and Supreme Court may also entertain an appeal after the expiry of appeal period, if they are satisfied that there were sufficient causes for not filing the appeal within the prescribed period. However, in the absence of any information to the contrary, time limit for further appeal is always taken as:

- 45 Days (for appeal to State Commission against order of District Commission)
- 30 Days (for appeal to National Commission against order of State Commission)
- 30 Days (for appeal to Supreme Court against order of National Commission)

Summary of Three Redressal Agencies

| Rank | District Commission | State Commission | National Commission |
| :--: | :--: | :--: | :--: |
| Committee | President and atleast 2 other members | President and atleast 4 other members | President and atleast 4 other members |
| Committee Appointment | State Government | State Government | Central Government |
| Jurisdiction | Entertain complaints where value of goods or services paid as consideration does not exceed ₹ 1 crore | Entertain complaints where value of goods or services paid as consideration exceeds ₹ 1 crore but does not exceed ₹ 10 crore | Entertain complaints where value of goods or services paid as consideration exceeds ₹ 10 crore |
| Further appeal | State Commission (Within 45 days from the date of the order). | National Commission (Within 30 days from the date of the order). | Supreme Court (only if matter of original jurisdiction) (Within 30 days from the date of the order). |
