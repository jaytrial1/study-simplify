# Consumer Awareness - Role of Consumer Organisations and NGOs

Consumers' awareness about their rights and responsibilities helps achieve consumer protection objectives. It also requires consumers to form organisations for protection and promotion of their interests.

In India, several consumer organisations and Non-Government Organisations (NGOs) work to protect and promote consumer interests. NGOs are non-profit organisations aiming for public welfare, with their own constitution and freedom from government interference.

## Functions Performed by Consumer Organisations and NGOs

Various functions performed by Consumer Organisations and NGOs include:
1.  **Educating the public:** Organising training programmes, seminars, and workshops on consumer rights.
2.  **Publishing information:** Publishing periodicals and publications about consumer problems, legal reporting, reliefs, etc.
3.  **Comparative testing:** Carrying out tests on consumer products in accredited laboratories and publishing results.
4.  **Encouraging protest:** Encouraging consumers to protest and take action against unscrupulous practices.
5.  **Providing legal assistance:** Offering aid, legal advice, etc., for seeking legal remedies.
6.  **Filing complaints:** Filing complaints in consumer courts on behalf of consumers.
7.  **Public interest litigation:** Taking initiative to file cases in consumer courts for the general public interest, not just individuals.
