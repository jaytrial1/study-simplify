# Consumer Protection Act, 2019

The Consumer Protection Act, 2019 replaced the 1986 Act and aims to widen its scope in addressing consumer concerns. The Act protects and promotes consumer interests and establishes authorities for timely and effective administration and settlement of disputes. Its scope is wide, applying to all undertakings regardless of size, nature, or ownership.

The main features of the Act are:
*   It grants certain rights to consumers to empower and protect their interests.
*   It provides safeguards against defective goods, deficient services, unfair trade practices, and other exploitation.
*   It provides a three-tier machinery (District, State, and National Commissions) for simple, quick, and easy remedies.
*   It provides for the formation of consumer protection councils at District, State, and apex levels.
