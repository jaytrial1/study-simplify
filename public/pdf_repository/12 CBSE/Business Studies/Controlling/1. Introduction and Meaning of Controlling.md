This section introduces the concept of controlling in management and defines its core meaning.
# Introduction and Meaning

Every organisation aims to achieve certain objectives or goals. The **controlling** function aims to ensure whether everything is being done according to predetermined goals.

**Controlling** refers to the comparison of actual performance with the planned performance and taking corrective actions in case of deviations between actual and planned performance. Controlling cannot prevent deviations entirely, but it can minimise them by taking actions and decisions that reduce their recurrence.

According to Koontz and O' Donnel, **Managerial Control** implies the measurement of accomplishment against the standard and the correction of deviations to assure attainment of objectives according to plans.

According to Dale Henning, **Control** is the process of bringing about conformity of performance with planned action.
