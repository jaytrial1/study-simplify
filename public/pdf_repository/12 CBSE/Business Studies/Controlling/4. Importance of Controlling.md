This section details the various benefits and significance of having an effective controlling system in an organisation.
# Importance of Controlling

Controlling is an indispensable function of management. A good control system helps an organisation in the following ways:

1.  **Accomplishing Organisational Goals:** It measures progress towards goals and involves taking corrective actions in case of deviation, keeping activities on the right track.
2.  **Judging Accuracy of Standards:** An effective control system helps verify whether the standards set are accurate and objective. It helps revise standards based on changes in the business environment.
3.  **Making Efficient Use of Resources:** Controlling helps reduce wastage and spoilage of resources by ensuring activities are performed according to standards, leading to optimum use of human and physical resources.
4.  **Improving Employee Motivation:** It communicates employee roles and performance standards in advance, motivating them to perform better.
5.  **Ensuring Order and Discipline:** An efficient control system creates an atmosphere of order and discipline, minimising undesirable activities like theft, fraud, dishonesty, and corruption.
6.  **Facilitating Coordination in Action:** Controlling provides direction to all activities and efforts for achieving organisational goals. Predetermined standards govern each department and individual, ensuring coordination and accomplishment of overall objectives. Coordination among interdependent departments is necessary, and controlling provides this.
