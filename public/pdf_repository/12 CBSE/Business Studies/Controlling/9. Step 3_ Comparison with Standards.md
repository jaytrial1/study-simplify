# Step 3: Comparison of Actual Performance with Standards

The third step in the controlling process is to compare the actual performance with the standards.

*   This comparison reveals the **deviation** between planned and actual performance.
*   Comparison is easiest when standards are quantitative.
*   If actual performance matches the standards, controlling ends for that specific activity.
*   If there is a mismatch or deviation (actual performance falls short), the extent of deviation is determined.
*   Minor deviations may be ignored, but major deviations require immediate action.
