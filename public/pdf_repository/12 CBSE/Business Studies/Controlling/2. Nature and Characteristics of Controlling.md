This section outlines the key features and characteristics that define the controlling function in management.
# Nature and Characteristics of Controlling

*   **Goal-Oriented Function:** Controlling ensures that resources are used effectively and efficiently to achieve predetermined goals.
*   **Pervasive Function:** Control is exercised at all levels of management, though its nature and extent may differ. It is required in both business and non-business organisations.
*   **Backward and Forward Looking:**
    *   It is **backward-looking** as it measures and compares actual performance with standards fixed in the past.
    *   It is also **forward-looking** as it aims to improve future performance based on experience gained in the past.
*   **Continuous Function:** Controlling involves continuous review of performance and revision of standards.
