Prepare a model to demonstrate movement of particles in solids, liquids and gases.

## For making this model you will need:

*   A transparent jar
*   A big rubber balloon or piece of stretchable rubber sheet
*   A string
*   Few chickpeas or black gram or dry green peas.

## How to make?

*   Put the seeds in the jar.
*   Sew the string to the centre of the rubber sheet and put some tape to keep it tied securely.
*   Stretch and tie the rubber sheet on the mouth of the jar.
*   Your model is ready. Now run your fingers up and down the string by first tugging at it slowly and then rapidly.

### Activity Explanation (Inferred Outcome)

*   When you tug the string slowly: The rubber sheet vibrates gently, causing the seeds to move slightly, vibrating in their positions. This represents the particle movement in a **solid**.
*   When you tug the string rapidly: The rubber sheet vibrates vigorously, causing the seeds to move around more freely, sliding past each other and changing positions within the jar. This represents the particle movement in a **liquid**.
*   If you could pull hard enough to make the seeds bounce wildly and fill the whole jar (though the model might not fully achieve this), it would represent the rapid, random movement of particles in a **gas**.
