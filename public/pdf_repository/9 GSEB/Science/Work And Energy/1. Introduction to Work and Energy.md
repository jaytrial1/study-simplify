# Work and Energy

In previous chapters, we discussed motion, its causes, and gravitation. Now we will explore 'work', a concept crucial for understanding many natural phenomena. Energy and power are closely related to work and will also be studied in this chapter.

All living beings require food for energy to perform basic survival activities, known as 'life processes'. We also need energy for other activities like playing, singing, reading, writing, thinking, jumping, cycling, and running, with more strenuous activities requiring more energy.

Animals also engage in activities requiring energy, such as jumping, running, fighting, moving away from enemies, finding food, or finding shelter. We also use animals for tasks like lifting weights, carrying loads, pulling carts, or ploughing fields, all of which require energy.

Machines also need energy to work. Engines, for example, require fuel like petrol and diesel. Both living beings and machines need energy to perform actions and tasks.

### Think it over !

Think of machines. List the machines that you have come across. What do they need for their working? Why do some engines require fuel like petrol and diesel? Why do living beings and machines need energy?

> **Answer:**
> Machines like cars, fans, computers, washing machines, etc., need energy to work. They get this energy from sources like electricity or fuel. Engines require fuel like petrol and diesel because these fuels contain chemical energy that is converted into mechanical energy through combustion to make the engine work. Living beings and machines need energy because energy is the capacity to do work. Without energy, they cannot perform any activities or tasks.
