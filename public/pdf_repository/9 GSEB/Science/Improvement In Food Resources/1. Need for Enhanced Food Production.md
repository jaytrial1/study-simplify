# Improvement in Food Resources

All living organisms need food for development, growth, and health, as it provides essential nutrients like proteins, carbohydrates, fats, vitamins, and minerals. We obtain most of our food from agriculture and animal husbandry.

Efforts are constantly being made to improve agricultural and animal husbandry production. Why is this necessary? Why can't we manage with current production levels?

> **Answer:** India has a very large and growing population. To feed over one billion people, we need significantly more grain each year. Since there is limited scope to increase the area of land under cultivation, it is necessary to increase the efficiency of production per unit area for both crops and livestock.

Increasing food production to meet demand has seen success through the *Green Revolution* (increased food-grain production) and the *White Revolution* (better milk production and availability).

However, these revolutions have led to intensive use of natural resources, increasing the risk of environmental damage and ecological imbalance. Therefore, sustainable practices are needed in agriculture and animal husbandry to increase production without degrading the environment.

Simply increasing grain production and storage doesn't solve malnutrition and hunger. People need the financial means to purchase food. Food security depends on both *availability* and *access*. A large part of the population depends on agriculture for livelihood. Increasing their income is crucial to combat hunger.

Scientific management practices are essential for high yields. For sustained livelihood, practices like *mixed farming*, *intercropping*, and *integrated farming* (combining agriculture with livestock, poultry, fisheries, or bee-keeping) should be undertaken.
