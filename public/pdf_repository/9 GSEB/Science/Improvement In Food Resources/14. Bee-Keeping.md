# BEE-KEEPING

Beekeeping for honey production is a growing agricultural enterprise, requiring low investment and providing additional income for farmers. Beehives also produce wax used in medicines.

Local bee varieties for commercial honey production include *Apis cerana indica* (Indian bee), *A. dorsata* (rock bee), and *A. florae* (little bee). The Italian variety, *A. mellifera*, is also used commercially due to its high honey collection capacity, less stinging, long stay in hives, and good breeding.

Commercial honey production often involves establishing *bee farms* or *apiaries*.

1. What are the desirable characters of bee varieties suitable for honey production?

> **Answer:** Desirable characters for honey production include a high honey collection capacity, stinging less, staying in the beehive for long periods, and breeding well. The Italian bee variety, Apis mellifera, possesses these traits.

2. What is pasturage and how is it related to honey production?

> **Answer:** Pasturage refers to the availability of flowers for bees to collect nectar and pollen. It is directly related to honey production because the quantity of pasturage determines how much nectar bees can collect, influencing the yield of honey. The type of flowers available in the pasturage also determines the specific taste and quality of the honey produced.
