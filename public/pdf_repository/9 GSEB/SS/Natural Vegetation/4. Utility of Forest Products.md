This section details the various ways forests are useful to humans, providing timber, medicinal plants, and other valuable products.

# Utility of Forest Products

Forests benefit mankind in numerous ways.

## Commercial Products

*   *Timber wood* from *teak* and *saal* is used for making furniture.
*   *Boats* are made from *sundari* trees found in the Sundarbans.
*   *Sports goods* and *packing boxes* are prepared from *pine* and *chid* wood.
*   *Turpentine* is extracted from the liquid of *chid* trees.
*   *Sandalwood* is used for perfumed oil and cosmetics.
*   *Baskets*, *toys*, and *home decoration items* are made from *bamboo*.
*   Forests also provide products like *lac* (sealing wax), *resin*, *gum*, *rubber*, *honey*, and *cane*.

## Medicinal Plants

Many plants found in forests have medicinal uses:

| Vegetation    | Medicinal Utility                     |
| :------------ | :------------------------------------ |
| *Sarpagandha* | Used for high blood pressure          |
| *Limdo*       | Acts as a bacterial resistant         |
| *Tulsi*       | Treats cold, cough, and fever         |
| *ArjunSadad*  | Used for heart ailments               |
| *Bili*        | Helps with gas and cough impurities   |
| *Galo*        | Used for diabetes, fever, joint pain  |
| *Harde*       | Treats constipation, hair diseases    |
| *Amla*        | Cures gas, acidity, aids digestion    |
| *Karanj*      | Treats skin and dental-gum diseases   |

Other products derived from forests include *leaf plates* from *khakhro* leaves, *catechu* from *kher* trees, and *bidis* from *timru* leaves. Forests also provide livelihood and food for people living in or near them. Thus, forests play a significant role in the social and economic development of society.
