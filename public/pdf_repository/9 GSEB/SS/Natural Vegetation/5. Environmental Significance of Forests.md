# Environmental Significance of Forests

The environmental importance of forests is substantial:

*   Forests help bring rain.
*   They prevent the atmosphere from becoming adverse.
*   They provide essential oxygen.
*   Forests help control floods.
*   They absorb harmful gases like carbon dioxide.
*   Forests prevent soil erosion.
*   Forests maintain groundwater levels.
*   Forests restrict the spread of deserts.
*   Forests help reduce air pollution.
*   Forests enhance natural beauty.
*   Forests purify the air.
*   They provide a natural habitat for wildlife.
*   Forests are ideal locations for adventure tourism activities.
*   Some forests are designated as National Parks and Sanctuaries to protect biodiversity.
