This section focuses on the structure and composition of the Indian Parliament, including the Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha.

# The Legislature at the Central Level - Parliament

India is a republic with a parliamentary democracy. The central representation of the people is in the Parliament, and state representation is in the Legislative Assembly. The Parliament is the apex legislative body of the nation.

The Indian Parliament consists of the **President**, the **Rajya Sabha**, and the **Lok Sabha**.

Legislatures can be of two types:

*   **Unicameral Legislature:** Consists of one house.
*   **Bicameral Legislature:** Consists of two houses.

At the Central level, the Legislature is bicameral, consisting of the Upper House (Rajya Sabha) and the Lower House (Lok Sabha). At the State level, the Legislature can be unicameral (Legislative Assembly only) or bicameral (Legislative Assembly and Legislative Council). Most states have only a Legislative Assembly. States like Bihar, Uttar Pradesh, and Maharashtra have both.

## Lok Sabha

The Lok Sabha is the Lower House of the Parliament. Its members are direct representatives of the people, elected through direct elections.

*   **Total Members:** 545.
*   **Elected Members:** 543 (from constituencies across India).
*   **Nominated Members:** 2 (selected by the President from the Anglo-Indian community).

The President is a part of the Parliament but does not regularly attend its sittings, except for addressing the first meeting and joint sessions. The President has the power to summon, prorogue (withhold proceedings), and dissolve the Lok Sabha. A Bill passed by both Houses becomes an Act only after the President signs it.

The Lok Sabha is **not a permanent house**. Its term is typically **five years**, but it can be dissolved sooner.

The Indian Constitution is supreme, and the Parliament must exercise its powers within constitutional limits. Laws framed by the Parliament can be challenged in court if they violate the Constitution, and the court can declare them null and void.

### Qualification for Membership of Lok Sabha

To be a member of the Lok Sabha, a person must:

*   Be a citizen of India.
*   Be 25 years of age or more.
*   Not be of unsound mind or insolvent.
*   Not be an employee of the Central or State Government (holding an office of profit).
*   Not be a proven criminal.

There must be a maximum period of six months between two sittings of the Parliament. The President can dissolve the Lok Sabha on the counsel of the Prime Minister. The term can be extended by one year in case of a national emergency. The President's decision to dissolve the Parliament cannot be challenged in court.

Members of the Cabinet Ministry can attend proceedings of both Houses, even if they are not members of the Lok Sabha, but they can only vote in the House they are members of. Gujarat State has 26 representatives in the Lok Sabha.

### Quorum

**Quorum** is the minimum number of members required to be present for a House to conduct its business. It is $\frac{1}{10}$th of the total number of members of that House.

*   For Lok Sabha (total 545 members): Quorum is 55 members ($\frac{1}{10}$th of 545).
*   For Rajya Sabha (total 250 members): Quorum is 25 members ($\frac{1}{10}$th of 250).

## Rajya Sabha

The Rajya Sabha is the Upper House of the Parliament and represents the States and Union Territories of India.

*   **Total Members:** 250.
*   **Elected Members:** 238 (elected indirectly by the members of the State Legislative Assemblies and Union Territories using the method of equivalent representation).
*   **Nominated Members:** 12 (nominated by the President from individuals with special knowledge or practical experience in literature, science, arts, culture, sports, and social service).

A person cannot be a member of both Houses simultaneously.

### Qualification for Membership of Rajya Sabha

To be a member of the Rajya Sabha, a person must:

*   Be a citizen of India.
*   Be 30 years of age or more.
*   Not be of unsound mind, insolvent, or a criminal.
*   Not be a government employee taking salary or holding any office of profit.

Gujarat State has 11 representatives in the Rajya Sabha.

The Rajya Sabha is a **permanent House** and cannot be dissolved.

*   Term of a member: 6 years.
*   Retirement: $\frac{1}{3}$rd of its members retire every two years, and an equal number are elected to replace them.
*   Eligibility: Members are eligible for re-election and re-nomination.

### Sessions

Generally, both Houses of Parliament meet three times a year for sessions:

1.  Budget Session
2.  Monsoon Session
3.  Winter Session

### Leadership of Rajya Sabha

The **Vice President of India** is the *ex-officio* Chairman of the Rajya Sabha. The Vice Chairman is elected by the members of the Rajya Sabha from among themselves. Dr. Sarvapalli Radhakrishnan was the first Chairman of the Rajya Sabha.
