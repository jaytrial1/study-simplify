# The First World War

In the First World War, 24 nations supported the Allied Nations, while 4 nations supported the Axis Nations (Central Powers). Initially, Germany achieved significant victories in Europe, devastating the French army. Germany also engaged in extensive submarine warfare, sinking ships belonging to the Allied Nations. Tanks and poisonous gases caused widespread casualties among soldiers and civilians.

In 1917, the **Russian Revolution** began, leading Russia to withdraw from the war. Germany was particularly strong at this point. A key event that altered the course of the war was Germany sinking the American steamer **'Lusitania'**, resulting in the deaths of 147 American soldiers. Consequently, America joined the Allied Nations in April 1917. This brought countries like Panama, Greece, Cuba, China, and Siam into the Allied fold, significantly strengthening their position.

The German army could not withstand the arrival of American troops, and the war's momentum shifted. Bulgaria surrendered in September 1918, followed by Turkey and Austria in October 1918. The German leader Kaizer abdicated and fled. Germany surrendered to the Allied Nations on November 11, 1918, signing a ceasefire treaty that ended the war.
