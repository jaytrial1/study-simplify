This section introduces the context of European expansionism and colonialism, setting the stage for the conflicts that followed, including the First World War and the Russian Revolution.

# Introduction to European Expansionism

The desire of Western European countries to expand their empires and acquire new territories led them to Asia and Africa. This resulted in the establishment of colonies and the expansion of their rule. Consequently, intense competition arose among European nations for acquiring colonies.

This pursuit of colonisation by Western European nations caused significant economic, social, and cultural damage to the Asian and African countries. The global political outcome of this competition included events such as the **First World War** and the **Bolshevik Revolution** in Russia.
