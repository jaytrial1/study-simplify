# Man-Induced Disasters: Industrial Accidents

This section introduces man-induced disasters and focuses on industrial accidents, their causes, impacts, and safety measures, using the Bhopal Gas Tragedy as a key example.

**Man-induced disasters** are events that occur directly or indirectly due to human actions, whether knowing or unknowing, unawareness, ignorance, or failure of human systems. These cause heavy damage to human life and property.

## Industrial Accidents

In industrial townships, mills, and factories, accidents can happen despite precautions. Such accidents lead to deaths of people and livestock, damage to property, and severe environmental effects. **Human errors** are often at the root of industrial accidents. Accidents can occur at any stage involving human management of machines, processes, and material handling.

### Bhopal Gas Tragedy

The **Bhopal Gas Tragedy** occurred on December 3, 1984, at the Union Carbide plant in Bhopal, Madhya Pradesh, which produced insecticides. A poisonous gas called **MIC (Methyl Isocyanate)** was used and stored in large tanks. In the early morning hours, this gas began leaking from the tanks for about 40 minutes. The gas spread quickly over the densely populated area of Bhopal.

According to official figures, about 2500 people died immediately, and thousands more were affected. The gas also killed thousands of birds and animals. Pregnant women, unborn babies, and newborns were particularly vulnerable. Drinking water reservoirs and land were also affected. The tragedy resulted in 10,000 people becoming permanently handicapped and 1.5 lakh people partially handicapped.

### Rescue Operations during Gas Leakage: What to do

*   Install modern warning systems for advance information about gas leakage.
*   Pre-determine and support high-level safety measures in factories.
*   If gas leaks during transfer, drive the gas tanker away from human settlements.
*   Observe the wind direction and run *against* the wind.
*   Do not self-treat symptoms like breathlessness or burning eyes; seek immediate medical guidance.
*   Immediately shift fainted or weak people from the affected area.
*   Inform the Fire Brigade and Police.
*   Park vehicles in a way that does not obstruct rescue operations.

### Rescue Operations during Gas Leakage: What not to do

*   During rescue operations, people not involved should not gather around.
*   Do not enter the affected area until authorities declare it totally safe.
*   Do not join rescue operations without proper training and necessary equipment.
