# Man-Induced Disasters: Riots

This section defines riots, discusses their characteristics, and the devastating impact they have on society, property, and national unity, along with safety measures.

**Riots** are a problem faced by many countries. Often, participants are unaware of the root cause or purpose, merely joining the crowd or imitating others. Such crowds, lacking a common purpose, intentionally disturb peace and can destabilize the administrative setup.

When riots take a political or communal turn, they risk social well-being and harmony. Innocent citizens suffer greatly, laborers face difficulty sustaining life, and significant life and property are damaged. Riots challenge the unity and integrity of a nation, necessitating strong action to curb them.

### What to do:

*   Prevent the spread of rumors and violence-inciting talks.
*   Form and actively participate in a peace committee in your residential area.
*   Perform your civil duties by helping those affected by the riot.

### What not to do:

*   Do not be instrumental in spreading rumors.
*   Do not violate instructions or curfew declared by authorities to maintain law and order.
*   Do not believe and spread baseless talks appearing on social media.

Sometimes, terrorists hijack buses, trains, or planes, taking passengers hostage to demand the release of accomplices or other concessions. In such situations, families of hostages should remain calm and support the administration's efforts. Plans, such as commando operations or negotiations, are implemented to rescue the citizens.
