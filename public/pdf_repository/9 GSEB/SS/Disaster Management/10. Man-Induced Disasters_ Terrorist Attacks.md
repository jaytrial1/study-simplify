# Man-Induced Disasters: Terrorist Attacks

This section defines terrorism, highlights its global spread, provides examples of major terrorist incidents, and outlines safety precautions for citizens.

**Terrorism** is defined as immoral destructive acts performed by a person or group, such as property destruction, creating fear, genocide, or abduction, often to draw attention to demands. Terrorism is considered an enemy of mankind, disregarding race, community, or region. It has spread globally, and perhaps no country is entirely safe today.

## Some Terrorist Incidents of the World:

*   **September 11, 2001 (USA):** A terrorist organization hijacked four passenger planes. Two were crashed into the Twin Towers of the World Trade Center in New York City, killing all on board and many in the towers within minutes. Many others were injured, and buildings collapsed. A third plane hit the Pentagon, and a fourth crashed into a field in Pennsylvania after passengers attempted to regain control. Around 3000 people were victimized, and over 6000 were injured in these attacks.
*   **December 13, 2001 (India):** Five terrorists attacked the Indian Parliament building, firing indiscriminately. Indian security forces defended the building, resulting in the deaths of the terrorists and the martyrdom of about 8 security guards, with 16 wounded.
*   **November 26, 2008 (Mumbai, India):** A series of bomb blasts and firing occurred at hotels and other key locations in Mumbai. Around 137 persons were injured. Terrorists held people hostage in some hotels. Indian forces, including NSG commandos and military personnel, engaged the terrorists for two days, eventually killing all of them.
*   **December 2014 (Peshawar, Pakistan):** A terrorist attack on an Army school in the cantonment area killed about 141 persons, including 132 innocent students.

### What to do:

*   Inform the police about any suspicious behavior.
*   Do not touch any unclaimed items in public places like shopping malls, cinemas, gardens, or religious sites.
*   Cooperate with security checks.
*   If you rent out your house, register the tenants with the nearest police station as required by law.
*   Public places should be well-equipped with **CCTV cameras**.
*   While travelling on a bus or train, inform authorities immediately if someone leaves luggage behind.
*   If your single neighbor doesn't mingle locally and works on their computer late at night, inform the police about their behavior.
*   Inform Police about unclaimed vehicles.

### What not to do:

*   Do not accept any goods or parcels from unknown persons.
*   Do not rent your house to an unknown person without a thorough inquiry.
*   Do not sell your home, mobile phone, or vehicle to any unknown person without verifying identity.
