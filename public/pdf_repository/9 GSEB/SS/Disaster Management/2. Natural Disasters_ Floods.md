# Natural Disasters: Floods

This section describes floods, their causes, and essential safety measures to take or avoid during and after a flood event.

A **flood** generally means an extensive land area remaining submerged under water for several days. People often associate floods with rivers overflowing their banks. Floods are a natural result of continuous heavy rainfall. They become dangerous and cause large-scale damage to lives and property when human constructions ignore natural drainage patterns and land slopes.

### What to do:

*   Collect precious and personal items and move to a safer place.
*   Put water, dry breakfast, candles, lanterns, and matchboxes in a plastic box to keep them dry and keep them with you.
*   Ensure children are not hungry.
*   After the flood recedes, drink only **boiled water**.
*   Keep a radio and mobile phone with you.
*   Be aware of snakes moving to dry places; keep a bamboo stick to drive them away.

### What not to do:

*   Do not eat food prepared in flood water.
*   Do not move out from a safer place without getting authentic information about routes and the current situation.
