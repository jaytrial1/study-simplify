This section elaborates on the various advantages of adopting natural farming practices, covering aspects like cultural preservation, economic self-reliance, soil health, environmental protection, human health, water conservation, animal welfare, and farmer prosperity.

# Benefits of Natural Farming

Let's understand why natural farming is considered the best option.

## Sustaining the Farming Culture

India's farming culture is ancient and glorious. The high costs and lower returns associated with excessive chemical use threaten this heritage. Natural farming, with its negligible cost and stable production of quality products, offers a way to preserve this culture. Experiences of successful farmers demonstrate its effectiveness in this regard.

## Promoting Self-Reliant India

Natural farming involves negligible costs and maintains production levels while providing affordable, quality products. This significantly benefits farmers financially. Since natural farming requires minimal external resources, farmers can become self-reliant. It is seen as a strong option for building a truly self-reliant India.

## Ensuring Longevity of the Land

The fertility of the land is declining due to the excessive use of chemicals. In some areas, land has even become infertile. Chemical fertilizers and pesticides greatly reduce soil productivity over time.

Natural farming, however, can make the soil fertile and productive for a long time. It leads to significant improvement in soil fertility.

## Controlling Global Warming

Global warming is a major concern. Rising temperatures and climate change lead to disasters like extreme weather events. Industrialization, vehicles, materialistic lifestyles, and chemical farming are considered contributing factors. The SPNF method avoids compost and chemical fertilizers. By returning crop residues to the soil as mulch instead of burning them, it helps reduce greenhouse gas emissions.

## Fostering Healthy Life

Many people suffer from serious diseases like cancer, diabetes, and heart disease, often linked to consuming food containing excessive chemicals. Natural food is essential for longevity and healthy living. Natural farming uses no chemical fertilizers or pesticides, resulting in completely chemical-free products. It integrates natural systems to ensure crops acquire all necessary nutrients from the soil, making the produce rich in nutrients and potentially having medicinal properties.

## Conserving Water

Water is life, and its scarcity is a major problem. While wastage and poor planning contribute, chemical farming hardens the soil, preventing water percolation. Uneven land also causes rainwater to flow away.

This leads to falling groundwater levels and water pollution. Natural farming softens and fertilizes the soil. Using mulch increases the soil's moisture-holding capacity, conserving water. Increased activity of soil worms and microorganisms improves soil structure, making it more porous, which allows rainwater to percolate deep underground. Farmers observe that this method stores rainwater and significantly increases soil moisture retention, reducing the water requirement for crops compared to chemical farming.

## Saving the Cow Lineage (Gauvansh)

In Indian culture, cows are revered. Beyond religious beliefs, indigenous cows are crucial for natural agriculture. One gram of indigenous cow dung contains countless useful microorganisms vital for farming. Cow urine, milk, and ghee are also valuable. Natural farming gives indigenous cows respect and importance based on their scientific value, potentially improving farmers' economic situation through cattle rearing. The medicinal properties of indigenous cow products further increase their importance and the need for cattle breeding.

## Improving the Lives of Farmers

Farmers are called the 'father of the world', but modern farming costs often exceed returns, causing distress. Natural farming prevents the exploitation of nature and produces food that benefits human and soil health. Low production costs and increased profit margins lead to financial prosperity for farmers. Many farmers are adopting natural farming and inspiring others, bringing positive changes to their lives. Natural farming and the resulting natural food are seen as the best ways to maintain human health, soil health, and the environment.

Let's collectively contribute to a prosperous and self-reliant nation through natural farming, bringing about a revolution for a more glorious India.
