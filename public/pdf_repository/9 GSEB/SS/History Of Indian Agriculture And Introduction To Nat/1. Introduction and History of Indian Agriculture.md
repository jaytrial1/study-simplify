# Introduction and History of Indian Agriculture

Humans initially lived a nomadic life, moving from place to place in search of food. Gradually, they began to cultivate various crops, leading to a settled lifestyle. Through the efforts of sages, people started living in groups, developing family and social systems that formed human civilization and culture. Thus, Indian culture is also known as the '*sage-agriculture civilization*'. Over time, agriculture has changed to meet the needs of a growing population, adapting to new demands and requirements.
