# Understanding Natural Farming

**Natural farming** is described as a chemical-free or traditional farming method. It is considered an **agroecology-based diversified farming system** that integrates crops, trees, and livestock with functional biodiversity.

This approach is based on understanding and utilizing the natural systems that have sustained life for millions of years before humans. Research into these natural systems led Padmashree Dr. Subhash Palekar to develop the method known as "*Subhash Palekar Natural Farming*" (SPNF).

Both state and central governments are actively promoting natural farming to increase its adoption and benefits for farmers. Notable initiatives include declaring Dang district in Gujarat as a 'natural farming district' and establishing the University of Natural Farming and Organic Agriculture for education, research, and extension. The "Gujarat Natural Farming Development Board" has also been created to expand natural farming and provide guidance and incentives to farmers.
