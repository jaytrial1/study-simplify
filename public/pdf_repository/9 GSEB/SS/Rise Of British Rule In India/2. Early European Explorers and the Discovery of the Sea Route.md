# Early Explorers and the Discovery of the Sea Route

Inspired and supported by the Portuguese King Prince Henry, explorers began searching for new sea routes.

*   Bartholomew Diaz discovered the **Cape of Good Hope**.
*   Christopher Columbus, funded by the King of Spain, set sail in 1492 aiming for India but instead reached islands in the Atlantic Ocean, now known as the West Indies. Columbus believed he had reached India, but Amerigo Vespucci later clarified that it was a new land, which was then named **America** after him.

## Vasco da Gama's Arrival in India

The Portuguese sailor **Vasco da Gama** successfully discovered the sea route to India in 1498 A.D. With the help of an Indian sailor, he reached Calicut on the western coast and was welcomed by the local ruler, **King Zamorin**, who permitted him to trade. This event was crucial as it opened the door for other European nations to come to India.
