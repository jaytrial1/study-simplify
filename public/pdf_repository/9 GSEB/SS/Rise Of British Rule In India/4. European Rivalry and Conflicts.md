# European Rivalry and Conflicts

In the 18th century, the English and French were actively competing to establish their rule globally and conquer territories. This rivalry extended to India.

Between 1746 and 1763, three **Karnataka Wars** were fought between the French East India Company and the British East India Company in India. The French East India Company lost these wars, which facilitated the expansion of the British East India Company.

By the end of this struggle for power:
*   The Portuguese retained control over Diu, Daman, and Goa.
*   The French held trade centers at Chandranagar, Mahe, Karaikal, and Pondicherry.
*   The Dutch eventually left India.
