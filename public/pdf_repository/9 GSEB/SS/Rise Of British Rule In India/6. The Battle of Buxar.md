This section explains the events leading to and the outcome of the Battle of Buxar, which further solidified British power through the acquisition of Diwani rights.

# The Battle of Buxar

After making Mir Jafar the Nawab of Bengal, the British extracted significant wealth from him under various pretexts. Driven by a desire for greater power, the East India Company replaced Mir Jafar with **Mir Qasim** as Nawab. However, Mir Qasim proved to be more ambitious and became a threat to the Company. Consequently, Mir Qasim was removed, and Mir Jafar was reinstated as Nawab.

Mir Qasim sought refuge with the Nawab of Awadh. At this time, the Mughal emperor **Shah Alam** was also in Awadh. The three leaders – Mir Qasim, the Nawab of Awadh, and Emperor Shah Alam – decided to form a united front and declared war against the Company.

The East India Company fought against this united army at **Buxar** on **22nd October, 1764**. The united army was defeated. This victory granted the Company the **Diwani rights** (the right to collect revenue) for Bengal, Bihar, and Orissa.
