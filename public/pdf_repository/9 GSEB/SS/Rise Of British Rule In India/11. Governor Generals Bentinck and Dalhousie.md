# Governor Generals Bentinck and Dalhousie

Compared to his predecessors, **William Bentinck** is known as a "liberal Governor General". He believed that the imperialist policies of earlier Governor Generals caused dissatisfaction and hindered unity. Therefore, he adopted a more liberal approach.

**Dalhousie** arrived in India as Governor General in 1848. He was a staunch imperialist. Dalhousie implemented the **Policy of Annexation** for the expansion of the Company's rule and British imperialism in India.
