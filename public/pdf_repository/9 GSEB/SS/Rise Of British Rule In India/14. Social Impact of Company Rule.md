This section examines the social changes and impacts brought about by British administration, including the rise of newspapers, social reforms, and the introduction of English education.

# Social Impact of the Company Rule

During the British administration, the development of newspapers fostered a spirit of freedom of speech and thought among the people.

Indian society at the time had several problematic social customs, rites, and rituals, including:
*   Sati System
*   Female infanticide
*   Child marriage

Indian reformers like **Raja Ram Mohan Roy**, **Durgaram Mehta**, and **Behramji Malbari** played crucial roles in advocating for change and helped in getting laws passed with the assistance of the British to curb these practices.

The administrative framework in India was anglicised, creating a demand for people proficient in English. English education was introduced in India largely due to the efforts of **Lord Macaulay**. Based on recommendations by **Charles Wood**, universities were established in Mumbai, Madras, and Kolkata.

English education led to the emergence of a new class of educated Indians. Over time, this group became proponents of social reforms and contributed to the reform movement.
