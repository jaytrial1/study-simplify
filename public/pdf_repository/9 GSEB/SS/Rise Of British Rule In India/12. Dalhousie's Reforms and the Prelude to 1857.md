This section covers the various reforms introduced by Lord Dalhousie and the overall impact of 100 years of British rule leading up to the 1857 uprising.

# Dalhousie's Reforms and the Prelude to 1857

Despite his imperialist policies, Dalhousie also introduced several reforms.

*   He oversaw the laying of the **first railway line in India** (between Mumbai and Thane) in 1853 A.D.
*   He introduced the **wireless telegraph system** connecting India and England.
*   He established the **Public Works Department**.
*   He promoted **English education**.
*   He passed laws banning **child marriage** and advocated for **widow remarriage**.

Within 100 years, from the Battle of Plassey in 1757, the British rule and empire expanded significantly. However, this expansion generated dissatisfaction among Indian states, which ultimately culminated in the **first struggle for independence in 1857 A.D.**
