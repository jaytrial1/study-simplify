# Expansion of Company Rule under Governor Generals

The English government passed a **Regulating Act in 1773 A.D.** This Act made the Governor of Bengal the **Governor General** and brought all the Company's trade and political activities under his direct control. The Governors and councils of Mumbai and Madras were placed under him.

**Warren Hastings** became the first Governor General of India.

During his tenure, the **First Maratha War** and the **Second Mysore War** with **Haider Ali** of Mysore took place. The Company faced challenges fighting against these two powers simultaneously.

**Cornwallis** succeeded Warren Hastings as Governor General. During his time, the **Third Mysore War** occurred with **Tipu Sultan**, famously known as the 'Tiger of Mysore'. The Company allied with the Marathas and the Nizam to defeat Tipu Sultan. Tipu was unable to win against the combined forces and had to accept a treaty.

After Cornwallis, **Sir John Shore** became Governor General. His rigid policies led to a decline in the East India Company's influence, while the Marathas grew stronger.
