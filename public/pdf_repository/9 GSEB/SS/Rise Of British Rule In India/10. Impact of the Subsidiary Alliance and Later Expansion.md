# Impact of the Subsidiary Alliance and Later Expansion

The Subsidiary Alliance System was described as "sweet poison". By implementing it, Wellesley annexed many provinces and significantly expanded the East India Company's rule.

Governor General Wellesley asked **Tipu Sultan** to accept the System, but he refused. As a consequence of his refusal, the Company, with support from the Nizam, attacked Mysore in 1799. During this **Fourth Mysore War**, Tipu Sultan died fighting the British.

After Wellesley, **Hastings** became Governor General. He waged war with Nepal, resulting in the recruitment of Gurkhas into the army. Hastings also fought against the Pindharas and brought them under control. The **Third Maratha War** established the supremacy of the Company over the Marathas.
