# Terms and Conditions of the Subsidiary Alliance System

The Subsidiary Alliance System had several key conditions:

*   The East India Company would provide a trained army to protect the state that accepted the System from internal and external attacks.
*   In return, the state would either pay for the maintenance of this army or cede territories with equivalent income to the British.
*   The state would not wage war or sign treaties with other states without the Company's permission.
*   An English representative would be stationed in the state assembly.
*   The state would not employ other foreigners.

## States that Accepted the Subsidiary Alliance System

The following states and rulers accepted the Subsidiary Alliance System:

*   Nizam of Hyderabad
*   Mysore
*   Awadh
*   Gaikwad of Baroda
*   Scindia of Gwalior
*   Bhosle of Nagpur
*   Holkar of Indore
