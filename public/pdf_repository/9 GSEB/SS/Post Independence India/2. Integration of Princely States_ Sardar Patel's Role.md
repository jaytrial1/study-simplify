This section details the efforts led by Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel to persuade the rulers of princely states to accede to the Union of India.

# Integration of Princely States: Sardar Patel's Role

Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel appealed to the Kings and Nawabs of the princely states to voluntarily give up their states in the interest of their subjects and the Union of India, stimulating a sense of nationalism. Kings, Rajwadas, and Nawabs contributed to building a strong, integrated, and prosperous India. Sardar Patel assured them that their rights and interests would be protected.

Sardar Patel, along with his Home Department secretary Mr. V.P. Menon, prepared a draft for the **'Attachment bond'** and the **'Status Quo Agreement'**. These documents were finalized with input from concerned ministers and a joint committee of kings. The rulers were generally satisfied with this draft.

Except for Kashmir, Hyderabad, and Junagadh, 559 princely states accepted the instrument of accession and joined the Union of India.
