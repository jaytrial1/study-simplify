# World After 1945

The history of the world is marked by numerous wars. The most destructive in modern times were the First and Second World Wars. Following the devastation of World War I, the League of Nations was formed to ensure lasting peace, but it failed, leading to World War II in 1939. The horrors of the Second World War spurred renewed efforts for peace and the protection of independence, culminating in the establishment of the **United Nations** on October 24, 1945.
