# India's Contribution at the International Level

India has made significant contributions internationally both during its freedom struggle and after independence. India has consistently opposed **imperialism, colonialism**, and **racial discrimination**, supporting movements against these evils. India was actively involved in the formation of the United Nations and participates in its activities. India has played a crucial role in UN peacekeeping efforts by sending military troops to conflict zones like Gaza, Cyprus, Congo, and Sri Lanka.

India has persistently advocated for **disarmament** to achieve world peace and security, taking a leading role in global debates and offering suggestions. India has presented proposals for **total nuclear disarmament** in the United Nations General Assembly, reflecting humanity's desire for peace. India has consistently argued at the UN that resources spent on nuclear weapons and military should instead be used for the upliftment of poor people globally. World peace remains a primary concern in India's foreign policy. India actively works to foster an atmosphere of cooperation, trust, and understanding among nations.
