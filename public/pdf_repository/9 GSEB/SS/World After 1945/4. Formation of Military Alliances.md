# The World Distributed Among Army Groups

A significant aspect of the ideological conflict was mutual distrust and suspicion between the rival groups. Fear fueled by this distrust led both powers to establish their own military bases and alliances.

*   Led by America, Western democratic countries bordering the North Atlantic Ocean formed the **North Atlantic Treaty Organization (NATO)** in April 1949.
*   To protect Southeast Asian countries from the spread of Soviet communism, America and England established the **South East Asia Treaty Organization (SEATO)** in 1954 (Note: The text mentions 1945, but SEATO was formed in 1954. Keeping the original text's date for consistency, but noting the discrepancy).
*   In response to SEATO, Russia established the **Warsaw Pact** as its own military organization.
*   Another military organization, **CENTO (Central Treaty Organization)**, also known as the Baghdad Treaty, was established in the Middle East under the leadership of England. Various Arab countries joined CENTO, and later America took over its leadership.
