# The United Nations Charter

The United Nations Charter begins with a Preamble that outlines its objectives. The main goals are:

*   To establish international peace and security. This involves taking collective action to prevent or remove threats to peace and suppress acts of aggression. It also emphasizes resolving international disputes through peaceful means.
*   To develop friendly relations among nations and take appropriate steps to maintain world peace.
*   To address economic, social, and cultural problems through UN intervention and promote human rights and fundamental freedoms for all people, without discrimination based on caste, language, gender, or religion.
*   To serve as a central coordinating body for the actions of different nations working towards common goals.

The Charter emphasizes that the rejection of war and the constant pursuit of peace are the primary objectives of the United Nations.
