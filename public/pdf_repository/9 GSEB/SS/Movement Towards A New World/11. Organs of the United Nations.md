This section lists the principal organs that constitute the structure of the United Nations, designed to fulfill its aims.

# Organs of United Nations

The United Nations is structured into six principal organs to achieve its objectives:

1.  General Assembly
2.  Security Council
3.  Economic and Social Council
4.  Trusteeship Council
5.  International Court of Justice
6.  Secretariat

The **International Court of Justice** is located in Hague, Netherlands. All other principal organs are situated in New York, America.
