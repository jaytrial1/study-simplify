# Movement Towards A New World

## Global Trends between the two World Wars

After the First World War, many people faced economic hardship. The governments in power were largely unsuccessful in improving these conditions, leading to a loss of trust in democratic systems. This environment facilitated the rise of dictatorships in some countries, which became a significant factor contributing to the Second World War.

Several key events and movements characterized this period:
*   Fascism in Italy
*   Nazism in Germany
*   Militarism in Japan
*   Global Depression 1929-32
