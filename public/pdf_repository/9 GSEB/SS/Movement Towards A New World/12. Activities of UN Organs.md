This section describes the functions and composition of each of the six main organs of the United Nations, including the specialized agencies working under the Economic and Social Council.

# Activities of the Organs of United Nations

## General Assembly

The **General Assembly** is the largest organ of the United Nations, comprising representatives from all member nations. Each nation can send up to five representatives, but has only one vote during elections.
*   It can discuss, advise, suggest, or recommend on any matter related to international relations.
*   It approves the annual budget presented by the Secretary General and allocates expenses.
*   It seeks solutions to global economic problems, human rights issues, disarmament, and other international matters.
*   Decisions on general matters are made by a two-thirds majority vote.

## Security Council

The **Security Council** is considered the most important organ of the United Nations, primarily responsible for maintaining international peace and security. It has five permanent members: America, Britain, France, Russia, and China. There are also 10 non-permanent members elected for two-year terms by the General Assembly.

The Council possesses significant power to maintain peace and security. It resolves disputes between nations peacefully through negotiations and mediation. A key feature is the "**Veto**" power, or "right to deny," held by the five permanent members. If any permanent member votes against an important international matter, the Council cannot make a decision on it. Russia has historically used this Veto power most frequently.

## Economic and Social Council

This Council, also known as **ECOSOC**, has 54 members. The General Assembly appoints one-third of its retiring members for three-year terms. ECOSOC works to improve the standard of living worldwide without discrimination based on religion, caste, or region. It operates through various sub-councils and specialized agencies globally.

*   **WHO (World Health Organisation)**: Aims to improve global health.
*   **IMF (International Monetary Fund)**: Works to establish financial stability.
*   **FAO (Food and Agriculture Organisation)**: Helps improve agricultural production, forestry, and fisheries and raise nutrition levels.

## Trusteeship Council

The **Trusteeship Council** includes the five permanent members of the Security Council and representatives selected by the General Assembly. It oversaw territories that had not gained independence, were under previous mandates, or were territories of nations defeated in World War II. Its role was to supervise their social, economic, and political development towards self-governance or independence. (Note: The UN Trusteeship Council suspended operation on 1 November 1994, after the independence of Palau, the last remaining trust territory).

## International Court of Justice

The **International Court of Justice** is headquartered in Hague, Netherlands. It consists of 15 judges appointed for nine-year terms. Its functions include resolving disputes between nations, issuing verdicts on international disputes brought before it, and providing legal advice.

## Secretariat

The **Secretariat** is the administrative arm of the United Nations, headed by the **Secretary General**. The Secretary General is appointed by the General Assembly for a five-year term. The Secretariat includes a large staff of secretaries, administrators, assistants, translators, and experts who support the Secretary General's work. The Secretariat is located in New York. The United Nations, through its organs and activities, continuously strives to achieve world peace, unity, and universal brotherhood.
