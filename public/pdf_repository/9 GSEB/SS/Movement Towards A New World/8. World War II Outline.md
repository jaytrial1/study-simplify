This section provides a brief overview of the key players and events of World War II, including the formation of the opposing alliances, Japan's attack on Pearl Harbour leading to US entry, and the use of atomic bombs ending the war.

# World War II - An Outline

With the start of World War II, the world was again divided into two major blocs. The **Allied Nations** were led by England and France, while the **Axis Nations** comprised Germany, Italy, and Japan. Other nations joined these groups. A pivotal moment occurred when Japan attacked the American Navy at **Pearl Harbour**. This attack ended America's neutral stance, leading it to retaliate and join the Allied Nations, significantly strengthening their side.

As the war intensified, America dropped **Atom bombs** on the Japanese cities of **Hiroshima** and **Nagasaki** to force Japan's surrender. This resulted in the deaths of one lakh twenty-four thousand Japanese citizens and widespread destruction. Japan surrendered on August 11, 1945, bringing World War II to an end.
