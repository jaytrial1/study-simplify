This section highlights the critical task of integrating princely states into the Indian Union and touches upon the long-term challenges faced by the newly independent nation.

A large number of *refugees* migrated from both countries, leaving behind their homes and properties. India skillfully and patiently handled the tremendous problem of their *rehabilitation* and *restoration*.

The then Deputy Prime Minister and Home Minister, *Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel*, played a crucial role in merging about *562 small and big Princely States* into the 'Indian Union'. His strong determination, iron will, and diplomatic negotiations were instrumental in solving this complex problem.

However, long-term planning was necessary to address other significant challenges, including:
*   The effects of communal riots due to partition.
*   The shock of Mahatma Gandhi's death.
*   Making Independent India self-reliant through good governance.
*   Improving the weak condition of Indian agriculture.
*   Creating an atmosphere of peace and tranquility in the country.
*   Ensuring the internal and external security of the country.
