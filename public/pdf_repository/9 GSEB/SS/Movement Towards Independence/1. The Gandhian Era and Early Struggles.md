# Movement Towards Independence

The period from 1920 to 1947 is recognized as the *Gandhian age*, marked by *Gandhian movements* for the struggle for Independence. This phase of the national movement aimed at achieving both constitutional rights and the freedom of India. The struggle involved a series of significant events that had a widespread impact across the nation.
