This section discusses the government's attempts to suppress the Quit India movement and how, despite the harsh measures, the movement fostered significant political awareness and signaled India's demand for complete independence.

# Failure of the Government in crushing the struggle

The Government attempted to suppress the *Quit India Movement* with extreme force. The struggle, starting from smaller actions, escalated to widespread disruption of the government system. Over *70,000 people were imprisoned*, *538 gunshots* were fired, *1028 people lost their lives*, and *3200 people were injured*. Students were particularly affected by shootings in places like Ahmedabad and Patna.

Despite the brutal repression, the movement successfully created a unique *political consciousness* among the people. It sent a clear warning to the British Government and a message to the world that India would no longer tolerate being enslaved. The struggle effectively signaled the Indian people's demand for the British to *Quit India*. The previous idea of *Dominion Status* became almost irrelevant after this struggle. India finally achieved independence after five more years of struggle.
