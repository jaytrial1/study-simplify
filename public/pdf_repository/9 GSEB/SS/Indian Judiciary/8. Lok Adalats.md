This section describes Lok Adalats, their purpose, functioning, and benefits.

# Lok Adalats

Gujarat State is first to start **Lok Adalats** to provide speedy and economical justice to the poor, weak and exploited section of the society. Under this the court provides free support and guidance through '**Legal Aid Service Center**' situated in Ahmedabad. Lok Adalats function voluntarily even on Sundays and holidays at the district and taluka headquarters as per the convenience of the petitioners.

The Lok Adalats generally hear the cases of accident and is related compensation, divorce, alimony, general debts, personal complaints, police complaints etc. Lok Adalats consist of advocates, social workers, educationists, reputed citizens, businessmen, police officers, insurance officers, judges and judicial officers. Here cases are settled amicably such that both the parties are satisfied. The cases fought in the Lok Adalats are neither won nor loss. Lok Adalats save time and money. Many pending cases are solved. All the judgments of the Lok Adalats are backed by the law. Therefore the Lok Adalats have become a centre of attraction.
