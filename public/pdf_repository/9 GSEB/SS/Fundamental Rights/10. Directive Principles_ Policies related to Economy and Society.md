This section details the Directive Principles focused on economic and social policies, including equitable distribution of wealth, protection of labour, promotion of cooperation, welfare of weaker sections, uniform civil code, and provisions for support in old age, illness, etc.

# Directive Principles: Policies related to Economy and Society

These Directive Principles are divided into various sections.

## Principles related to Economic Policy

For the modification of Economic Policy, there are certain principles which are given below:
*   Equal distribution of wealth and resources among all classes for the welfare of the citizens.
*   Assets and production of resources should not be distributed to a certain group or section; this should be administered by the State.
*   Equal pay for equal work for both men and women.
*   Provision for good health for labourers working at places should be created. Because of financial constraints, men, women, and young children working at unhygienic places or where health matters are retorted should not be allowed to happen.
*   State should promote co-operation in administration of industrial units by labourers.
*   Protection of children, youth against exploitation and against moral and material abandonment and to live in a healthy and dignified environment; these principles should be developed by State.
*   Relief and leave at the time of delivery should be provided to women. Worker's Insurance, Bonus, Maternity leave, Gratuity, and other legal rights should be included in the policy.
*   To organize agricultural and animal husbandry on modern and scientific lines and to prohibit the slaughter of cows and other useful milk-producing cattle; a draft should be drawn for the livestock breed like donkeys, bullocks, cows for halting the slaughter.
*   Equal legal rights to all. State should organize free legal services to the desired citizens who are financially feeble as well as incapable in getting so.

## Principles related to Social policy

Weaker sections of the society, that is the Schedule Class, Schedule Tribe, and backward class, educational and economic rights to be conferred which should be taken in concern by the State. They should be protected against social exploitation and injustice.

**Uniform Civil Code** should be the concern for all the citizens of the country, thereby dispersing and administering justice to all which should be taken in concern by the State. Steps should be taken for social justice in matters of Marriage, divorces, adoptions, juvenile, assets and properties by the State.

The State should make provisions for the support and education in matters: right to employment, old age, illness or inauspicious situations, poverty etc.
