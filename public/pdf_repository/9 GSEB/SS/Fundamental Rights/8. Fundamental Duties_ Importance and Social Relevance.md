# Fundamental Duties: Importance and Social Relevance

January 6th is celebrated as '**Fundamental Duties Day**' in India. Of all these Duties, some Duties are backed by the law. The person violating it can be sentenced. A teacher should make efforts so that the students follow the Duties and they become aware and conscientious. It is our Duty and Responsibility as a citizen to see that various social evils and customs prevailing in the society should be eradicated like untouchability, drudgery, child-labour, women exploitation, dowry, female-foeticide, ill-customs, superstition. These Duties are directive in conveying us that what a sensible and aware citizen must do. If citizens propagate the importance and utilization of Fundamental Duties, then future citizens develop the feeling of national responsibility, international brotherhood, and a just and exploitation-free society.
