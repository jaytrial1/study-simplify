This section introduces the concept of physiography and outlines the major physical divisions of India based on its landforms.

# Introduction to Physiography and Divisions of India

**Physiography** refers to the irregular landforms with undulations on the surface of the earth, including mountains, plateaus, and plains.

Based on physiography, India can be divided into the following major regions:
*   Northern mountainous region
*   Great northern plains
*   Peninsular plateau
*   The Coastal plains
*   Archipelagos
