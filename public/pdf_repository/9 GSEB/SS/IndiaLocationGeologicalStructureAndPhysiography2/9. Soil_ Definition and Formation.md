# Soil: Definition and Formation

**Soil** is a basic resource, especially important for agriculture. It is a thin layer formed of organic and inorganic matter on the surface of the earth. It is the thin layer on the earth's crust.

## Soil Formation

Soil formation is a long-term process resulting from the **denudation** (erosion and weathering) of rocks. Factors like temperature, rain, snow, air, vegetation, and insects erode the rock surface, turning it into a powdery form, which creates the land layer.

This initial layer contains gravels, smaller stones, pebbles, and soil particles, collectively known as '**Regolith**'. Regolith initially contains only mineral contents. Eventually, biotic matter (like decomposed plants and animals), air, and water mix with the regolith, and this mixture forms the soil.
