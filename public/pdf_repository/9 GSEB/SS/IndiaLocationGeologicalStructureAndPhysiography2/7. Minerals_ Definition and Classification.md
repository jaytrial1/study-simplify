# Minerals: Definition and Classification

A '**Mineral**' is a matter formed due to natural organic or inorganic processes, possessing a specific chemical composition. Minerals are found within the earth in solid, liquid, and gaseous forms. Their availability depends on the geological structure of the earth's surface.

Minerals like iron, copper, nickel, gold, and silver are found in igneous rocks. Coal, mineral oil, and natural gas are typically available from stratified rocks. Slate, marble, and diamond are found in metamorphic rocks.

About 200 minerals are used directly or indirectly in daily life. While a universally accepted classification is difficult, a general classification is as follows:

## Classification of Minerals

1.  **Metallic minerals**:
    *   Precious metallic minerals: Gold, silver, platinum
    *   Light metallic minerals: Magnesium, bauxite, titanium
    *   Minerals of general use: Iron, copper, lead, zinc, tin, nickel
    *   Minerals used in alloys: Chromium, manganese, tungsten, vanadium
2.  **Non-Metallic minerals**: Limestone, chalk, asbestos, mica, fluorspar, gypsum, sulphur, diamond
3.  **Energy resources**: Coal, mineral oil and natural gas, uranium, thorium
