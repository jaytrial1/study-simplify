# Written Constitution and its Size

## Written Document

Except for Britain and Israel, India, along with other nations, has its Constitution in written form. The Constituent Assembly drafted the Constitution in written form considering India's social, geographical, and historical circumstances.

## Size of the Constitution

The Indian Constitution is divided into 22 sections comprising 395 articles and 8 appendices (Note: The text later mentions 12 appendices). The Constitution includes provisions for State administration, inter-state relations, fundamental rights, directive principles, judiciary, election commission, government institutions, minorities, scheduled castes, and deprived groups. Compared to other Constitutions, the Constitution of India is the largest, most extensive, and detailed written document.
