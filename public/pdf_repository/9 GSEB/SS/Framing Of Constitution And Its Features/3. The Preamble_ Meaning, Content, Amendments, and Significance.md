## Amendments to the Preamble

The Preamble remained unchanged until 1976. In the **42nd Constitutional Amendment** in 1976, the words **'Socialist'**, **'Secular'**, **'Unity'**, and **'Integrity of the Nation'** were added.

## Significance of the Preamble

The Preamble verbalizes the fundamental objectives, aims, ideas, and principles of the Constitution. It gives a clear insight into the **'Rule of Welfare'** and reflects the psyche of the Constitution framers.

The Preamble is considered the soul of the Constitution and is an important necessity. It provides proper guidance for understanding the framing or interpretation of any law and helps understand the policy behind any law. The Preamble is a useful tool to avoid trouble in interpreting the Constitution.

When ambiguity or obscure details arise in the law, the Preamble helps in understanding and interpreting it. Thus, the Preamble serves as a **compass** to understand the provisions of Constitutional law.

The Preamble represents the nation's unity, integrity, and the standard crust of brotherhood among citizens with noble sentiments and ideals. It is backed by high ideals and goals.
