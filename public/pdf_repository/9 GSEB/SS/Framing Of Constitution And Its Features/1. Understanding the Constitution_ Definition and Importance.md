This section defines what a constitution is and explains its significance as the fundamental law of a country.

# Understanding the Constitution

## Constitution Defined

A **Constitution** is defined as 'Any systematic collection of rules designed to govern a country's regime'.

## Importance of the Constitution

The Constitution is the basic and most important document of a country. All other laws of the country are based on the Constitution. The Constitution is superior to all other laws. It is considered a **live and basic document** because it involves periodically changing requirements, people's aspirations, expectations, interests, and emotions.
