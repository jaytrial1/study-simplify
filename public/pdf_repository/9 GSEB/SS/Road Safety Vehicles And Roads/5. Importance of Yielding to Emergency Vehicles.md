This section highlights the legal consequences of failing to give way to emergency vehicles.

# Importance of Yielding to Emergency Vehicles

Do you know?

According to Section 194 (E) of the Motor Vehicle Act, if a driver fails to draw the vehicle on the side of the road, when emergency vehicles such as a fire brigade vehicle or an ambulance are passing, will be punishable with imprisonment for a term which may extend to six months, or with a fine of ten thousand rupees or with both. So, always give way to emergency vehicles and be a good Samaritan by helping to save someone's life.
