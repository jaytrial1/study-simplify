This section explains the meaning of the different colors of traffic signals and how drivers should respond to them.

# Interpreting Traffic Signals

*   It is the duty of every motorist to follow traffic rules.
*   There are three colours of traffic signals at junction: **red**, **green**, and **yellow** (amber).
*   When our vehicle is approaching a traffic control signal, the driver should slow down his vehicle and follow the traffic signals as follows:

## Red Traffic Signal:

*   Stop the vehicle before the stop line when there is a red light in front of your vehicle on a crossroad.
*   Stop your vehicle before a pedestrian crossing when the stop line is not marked or visible.
*   When there is no pedestrian crossing, stop before the primary traffic signal.
*   A driver wishing to turn left when the red light is on, can turn left after giving priority to vehicles coming from his right, pedestrians crossing the road, and cyclists.
*   When there is a continuous flashing red light at crossroads, the motorist should proceed as mentioned below:
    *   If there is no stop line or any primary traffic signal, one should stop the vehicle before the pedestrian crossing.
    *   Drive the vehicle ahead only when deemed safe after giving priority to pedestrians and traffic on the main road.

# Green Traffic Signal:

*   Drive the vehicle forward only when there is a green light in front of your vehicle at a pedestrian crossing or four lanes are clear.
*   When the traffic regulation signal displays the direction indicator green arrow, one can move in the direction of the green arrow.
*   Give way to pedestrians at pedestrian crossing and vehicles already at crossroads at the time of green light at the traffic signal.
*   When a continuous flashing green arrow displays at a crossroad, the driver should turn in the direction of the green arrow when deemed safe after giving the right of way to pedestrians, cyclists, and intermingling traffic on the road.

## Yellow (Amber) Traffic Signal:

*   Stop before the stop line when there is a yellow light in front of your vehicle at crossroads or when there is none before a pedestrian crossing or primary traffic signal.
*   But if your vehicle has crossed the stop line or is so close to the stop line that a sudden stop is likely to cause a collision with the vehicle coming from behind, it is safe to cross the signal without stopping.
*   Drive cautiously after giving way to pedestrians and vehicles already present at the crossroads when the yellow light is flashing continuously at the crossroads.
