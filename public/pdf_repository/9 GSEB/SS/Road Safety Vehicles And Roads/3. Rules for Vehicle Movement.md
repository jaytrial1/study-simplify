This section details the rules for driving vehicles on different types of roads, including two-way roads, multi-lane roads, and one-way streets.

# Rules for Vehicle Movement

*   Only a person holding a **driving licence** for the particular vehicle should drive the same on the public road.
*   On a two-way public road, a vehicle should be driven on the **left side** of the road, and allow oncoming vehicles to pass on its right.
*   When another vehicle is overtaking our vehicle, there is a curve ahead, a sloped road, or the road ahead is not clearly visible due to any obstacle, then the driver must compulsorily drive his vehicle on the **left side** of the public road.
*   When a road has more than one lane, **heavy vehicles** should be driven on the **left side** of the road.
*   Do not drive in the direction where vehicular traffic is prohibited on a **'One-way'** declared road.
*   Do not drive on the **wrong side** against the flow of traffic.
*   When a vehicle moves backward (in reverse), keep a distance from it and do not move towards it until it completes the action of reverse movement.
