This section provides instructions on how to safely execute left turns, right turns, and U-turns.

# Executing Turns and U-Turns

## To turn Left or Right, take a U-turn

The driver should think in advance about the direction he wants to go and give proper signal in time to safely reach the right lane and turn as mentioned below:

## Turn to the Left

*   A driver wishing to turn left must clearly and timely indicate his intention to turn left by a directional or hand signal.
*   The driver should move towards the left lane in time before turning left.
*   After giving the signal to turn left, one should look towards the left lane and behind, then only move, when it is safe.

## Turn to the Right

*   The driver should prioritize pedestrians before turning when pedestrians are crossing the road.
*   A driver wishing to turn right must clearly and timely indicate his intention to turn right with a directional or hand signal.
*   The driver should get into the right-most lane in time before turning right.
*   After giving the signal to turn right, one should look towards the right lane and behind, then only move, when it is safe.

# Taking a 'U'-turn:

*   Never take a U-turn at the places mentioned below:
    *   Where a U-turn is prohibited by a road sign or traffic signal.
    *   On busy public roads with continuous traffic.
    *   On a main road, highway or expressway.
    *   Crossing single or double continuous white or yellow stripes.

## How to take a U-turn:

*   Take a U-turn only when it is safe to do so, keeping an eye on the traffic coming from the opposite direction and the traffic on our side and behind.
*   Before a driver takes a U-turn, give way to pedestrians and cyclists.
*   The driver should allow the oncoming traffic to pass and take the U-turn in such a way as not to cause inconvenience to others.
