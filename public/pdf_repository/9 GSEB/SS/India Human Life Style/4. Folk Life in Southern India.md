This section describes the lifestyle, including food, dress, dwellings, language, festivals, and dances, of the states and union territory in Southern India.

# Folk Life - Southern India

South India includes the states of Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Kerala, and the Union Territory of Puducherry. It is a peninsula, and all states except Telangana have a sea coast.

## Food

In the southern states, *rice* and *fish* form the main diet. Famous rice preparations include *idli*, *dhosa*, and *meduvada*, often served with coconut chutney. *Rasam*, which is similar to *dal* (gravy), is also part of the meal. In Kerala, coconut leaves are sometimes used as plates.

## Dressing

Due to the hot and humid weather in the south, people wear loose garments.
*   Men commonly wear *lungi* or a short *dhoti*, *paheran*, and a *khes* (shawl) on the shoulder, sometimes with a headgear.
*   Women wear *sari* in a southern style, *petticoat*, and *blouse*.
Keralite women often wear strips of flowers (*veni*) in their hair.

## Dwellings

People live in houses made of brick and cement. Modern housing is prominent in cities like Bengaluru and Chennai. People living near the coast may use coconut leaves in their huts. Creating a *rangoli* design outside the house is a daily routine in South India.

## Language

Languages spoken in South India belong to the *Dravidian family* of languages. Kannada is spoken in Karnataka. Telugu is spoken in Andhra Pradesh and Telangana. Tamil is spoken in Tamil Nadu, and Malayalam is spoken in Kerala.

## Festivals and Celebrations

Andhra Pradesh is known for its *Kuchipudi* dance. Festivals celebrated include *Shivratri*, *Makar Sankranti*, and *Baisakhi*. Mysore in Karnataka celebrates *Dashera*, *Eid*, and *Navratri*. *Kathakali* is a well-known dance of Kerala, where festivals like *Onam*, *Christmas*, *Eid*, and *Shivratri* are also celebrated. *Bharat Natyam* is a very famous dance style of Tamil Nadu, and *Pongal* is its main festival.
