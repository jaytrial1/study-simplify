# Introduction to India's Diversity

India is a country known for its vast diversity, stemming from its varied relief features and climate. This diversity is reflected in the daily lives of its people, including their food, clothing, housing, languages, dialects, festivals, and holy days. Despite these differences, India exhibits a strong sense of unity in diversity.

Relief and climate significantly influence lifestyle. For example, coastal regions where rice and fish are staple foods contrast with areas like Gujarat, Madhya Pradesh, and Punjab, where wheat is the main crop and thus the primary food source. Similarly, clothing varies with climate; people in colder, mountainous regions wear heavy woollens that cover the entire body, while those in hot regions prefer light, loose cotton garments. While traditional attire varies regionally, modern clothing like pants and shirts for men and salwar-kameez for women are now commonly seen across all states.

India can be broadly divided into four zones based on lifestyle:
*   Western India
*   Northern India
*   Southern India
*   Eastern India
