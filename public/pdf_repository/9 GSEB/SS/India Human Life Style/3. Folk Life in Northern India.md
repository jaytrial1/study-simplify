This section covers the lifestyle aspects, including food, dress, dwellings, language, festivals, and fairs, of the states and union territories in Northern India.

# Folk Life - Northern India

North India includes the Union Territories of Jammu & Kashmir and Ladakh, and the states of Punjab, Haryana, Himachal Pradesh, Uttar Pradesh, Uttarakhand, and Delhi. Punjab is known as the land of five rivers. Jammu & Kashmir is often called the heaven on earth due to its natural beauty. Uttarakhand is a mountainous region known as Devbhumi, and Himachal Pradesh is also mountainous. Uttar Pradesh is part of the fertile Ganga-Yamuna plain. Delhi is the National Capital.

## Food

*Wheat* is the staple food in Punjab and Haryana. Punjabis frequently use *tandoori roti* and various *parathas*. *Paneer*-based mixed vegetables are a special preference. *Lassi* is a well-known drink. In Jammu & Kashmir, the main food includes *rice*, *meat*, and *fish*. The staple food in Uttar Pradesh is *roti*, *dal*, and *rice*.

## Dressing

The dress worn by Punjabis and Haryanvis is known as the *Punjabi Dress*.
*   Women wear *salwar* and *kameez*.
*   Men wear a loose shirt (*jhabbho*) and *salwar*, sometimes with a Punjabi-style headgear and an embroidered jacket over the shirt.

Kashmiri people wear the *Kashmiri dress* and during winter, they wear clothes that cover the entire body. The dress in Himachal Pradesh and Uttarakhand is similar to that of Jammu & Kashmir. Men wear a cap, and women wear a scarf on their heads. In Uttar Pradesh, men wear *dhoti* and *paheran* and tie a scarf on the head. Women wear *sari*, *petticoat*, and *blouse*.

## Dwelling

Punjab and Haryana receive less rainfall, resulting in houses with flat rooftops. Urban houses are built with bricks and cement. In Jammu & Kashmir, wood is used extensively in house construction. In Himachal Pradesh and Uttarakhand, houses are often two-storeyed, with animals kept on the ground floor to provide warmth to the wooden first floor, which is useful during snowfall. These houses have sloping roofs, often covered with smooth rocks as tiles to allow snow to slide off easily. Urban houses in Uttar Pradesh are built with bricks and cement.

## Language

Punjabi is spoken in Punjab, and Haryanvi is spoken in Haryana. Hindi and Urdu are used in Uttar Pradesh. Urdu is a major language in Jammu & Kashmir, where Kashmiri and Dogri languages are also spoken. Laddakh and Tibetan languages are spoken in Ladakh. In Uttarakhand, besides Hindi, *Garhwali* and *Kumauni* dialects are spoken. The state language of Himachal Pradesh is *Pahadi*. Local dialects are also spoken in remote areas.

## Festivals and Celebrations

Major festivals in Punjab are *Baisakhi* and *Lohri*. *Bhangra* is a famous folk dance. *Eid* and *Moharram* are celebrated in Jammu & Kashmir. *Losar* and *Hemis* are celebrated in Ladakh. *Dashera* is celebrated in a special way in Kullu, Himachal Pradesh. *Holi* is a major festival in Uttar Pradesh. *Kathak* is a well-known dance style from Uttar Pradesh. Other widely celebrated festivals include *Shivratri*, *Ram Navami*, *Janmashtami*, *Dashera*, *Eid*, *Muharram*, and *Christmas*.

## Fairs

Kullu's *Dashera* Fair is well known. A *Martyrs Fair* is held in Punjab. *Kumbh Mela* and *Magh Mela* are famous fairs in Prayagraj (Allahabad) in Uttar Pradesh. In Uttarakhand, *Kumbh* and *Ardha Kumbh* fairs are also famous.
