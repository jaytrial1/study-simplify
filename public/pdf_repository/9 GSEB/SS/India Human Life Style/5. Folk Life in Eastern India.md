This section details the lifestyle, including food, dress, dwellings, language, festivals, and fairs, of the states in Eastern and Northeastern India.

# Folk Life - Eastern India

The states included in Eastern India are Bihar, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, Odisha, West Bengal, Assam, Arunachal Pradesh, Nagaland, Manipur, Mizoram, Tripura, Sikkim, and Meghalaya. Odisha and West Bengal have a sea coast.

## Food

People in Bihar, Jharkhand, Chhattisgarh, and West Bengal eat *roti* and vegetables but consume more *rice* in their meals. Rice is a staple diet, especially in mountainous regions. Pulses and green vegetables are also part of their meals. *Rosogulla* and *Sandesh* are favourite sweets.

*Satthu* is a special dish eaten in Bihar. Tea is a widely consumed drink in Assam.

## Dressing

*   Bihari people wear *dhoti*, *zabbho* (loose shirt), a *khes* on the shoulder, and a *Pagh* (headgear).
*   Women wear *sari*, *petticoat*, and *blouse*.
The attire in Jharkhand, Assam, and Odisha is similar. Bengali women wear *sari* in a Bengali style, and men wear a *dhoti* with pleats and a silken *zabbho*.

## Dwellings

People in plain regions live in houses made of bricks and cement. In mountainous areas, wood and bamboo are used in construction. Slanting roofs are common in regions with heavy rainfall. In Bengal, many houses have a small pond (*Pukur*) in the backyard where fish are reared for daily consumption.

## Language

Hindi is spoken in Jharkhand, Bihar, and Chhattisgarh. *Maithili*, *Bhojpuri*, and *Magdhi* are dialects spoken in Bihar. Assamese is spoken in Assam, Odia in Odisha, and Bengali in West Bengal. Garo and Khasi languages are spoken in Meghalaya, and Mizo dialect is used in Mizoram.

## Festivals and Holy Days

*Bihu* is a famous dance style of Assam, and *Odissi* is the famous dance style of Odisha. The *Chariot Procession of Jagannathpuri* is world-renowned. Festivals like *Chhath* and *Bhaiyaduj* in Bihar, and *Durga Puja* in Bengal are celebrated with great enthusiasm. Each state has its own festivals. India is a secular country, celebrating religious and national festivals without bias.
