# Threats to Wildlife

The existence of wildlife is under threat due to human greed and development. The continuous decrease in forest cover leads to the loss of natural habitats, leaving animals unprotected. *Hunting* for skin, meat, teeth, hair, and bones is a major problem.

Heavy grazing by domestic animals in forests reduces food availability for herbivores, causing their numbers to decline. This, in turn, affects carnivores, which may then venture into human settlements in search of food, attacking domestic animals. This interaction creates *human-animal conflict*, often leading to the destruction of wildlife. Human activities within forest areas also negatively impact wild animals.

### Human-Animal Conflict Examples

As forest areas decrease, wild animals occasionally enter human habitations. Human interference in wildlife areas results in clashes. In such instances, animals can become victims of human aggression and lose their lives.

*   In South and South-Eastern India, *elephants* seeking food cause damage to agricultural fields.
*   *Leopards* have been known to injure or kill people in Saurashtra and South Gujarat.
*   *Wolves* pose similar threats in the forests of north-eastern Gujarat.

Precautions are necessary to prevent such incidents.
