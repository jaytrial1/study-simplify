This section outlines the various methods and approaches required to protect wildlife and their habitats.
# Strategies for Conservation

Conserving and increasing forest areas requires long-term planning. Strong legal provisions for conservation and their strict implementation are essential. Social NGOs should prioritize wildlife issues and organize public awareness programs. Including these problems in school syllabi can educate future citizens.

Before implementing developmental projects, their potential environmental and ecological effects must be examined. Cutting large trees outside forests should be stopped as they provide nesting places for birds. Protecting ponds, farm ponds, and wetlands is crucial for migratory birds and other aquatic wildlife. Urgent action is needed to reduce pollution. Anticipatory planning, patrolling, and safety measures are required to manage forest fires.
