# Wildlife in Gujarat

### Like to know... (Gujarat Specific Facts)

*   The *Lion* is the State Animal and the *flamingo* is the State Bird of Gujarat.
*   *Asiatic Lion*, *Ghudkhar* (wild ass), and *Patti lizard* are found only in Gujarat in India.
*   Species like *Wild buffalo* (Indian bison), *elephant*, *cheetah*, *large Indian squirrel*, and *tiger* have become extinct in Gujarat.
*   The *Desert Sanctuary of Kachchh* is the largest sanctuary in Gujarat.
*   The *Porbandar Bird Sanctuary* is the smallest sanctuary in Gujarat.
