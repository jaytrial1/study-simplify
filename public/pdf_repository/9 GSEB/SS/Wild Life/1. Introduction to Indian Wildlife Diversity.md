# Introduction to Indian Wildlife Diversity

India boasts a diverse range of wildlife, reflecting its varied relief and climate. Globally, approximately *15 lakh species* of wildlife have been recorded, with *81,251 species* found in India. This includes reptiles, mammals, fish, insects, and more, with explorations ongoing to discover additional species. India ranks sixth globally in terms of biodiversity richness. Despite having less forest cover compared to its size, India's wildlife diversity is notably high.
