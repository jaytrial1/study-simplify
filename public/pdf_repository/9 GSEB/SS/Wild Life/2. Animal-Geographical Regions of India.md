This section outlines how India's wildlife is categorized into specific geographical zones based on animal characteristics and distribution.
# Animal-Geographical Regions of India

Just as natural vegetation is classified into regions, animals can also be spatially distributed. India's wildlife is divided into *9 zones* based on similarities in characteristics and regional existence. These zones are:
1.  Himalayan region
2.  Ladakh and dry cold area
3.  Forested Region in lower Himalayas
4.  High lands without forest cover in Upper Himalayas
5.  Northern Plain
6.  Desert of Rajasthan
7.  Peninsular Plateau
8.  Sea coast
9.  Nilgiri Hills

The study of biodiversity is conducted according to these *Animal-Geographical regions*.
