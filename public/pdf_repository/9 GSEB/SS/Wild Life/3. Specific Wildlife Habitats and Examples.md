# Specific Wildlife Habitats and Examples

India's diverse landscapes, including alluvial plains, peninsular plateaus, mountains, swamps, coasts, and various forest types (rain, deciduous, coniferous), provide a vast environment for wildlife.

Examples of animals found in these regions include:
*   *Asian elephant* in peninsular rain forests.
*   *One horned rhino* in the swampy Brahmaputra river area.
*   *Snow leopards* in the higher Himalayas.
*   *Wild goats* and *musk deer* in Jammu - Kashmir.
*   *Wild buffalo* (*Indian Bison*) and *tiger* in Central India and West Bengal.
*   *Ghudkhar* (*wildass*) in the Little Rann of Kachchh.
*   *Flamingo* in the waterlogged Greater Rann of Kachchh.
*   *Great Indian Bustard* in grassland areas.

Waterlogged areas attract a large number of *migratory birds* from cold regions, such as *Siberian crane*, *pelican*, *Tibetian duck*, *kunj*, and *karkara*.

*Flying squirrels* are found in the dense forests of the Western Ghats. The *Nicobari dove* is a rare bird specific to the Nicobar island. Rare species of *corals* are found in the Gulf of Kachchh and Lakshadweep Islands.

Beyond mammals and birds, other notable species include *king cobra*, various *snakes*, *python*, and *iguana* (*patlagho*). Aquatic life along coasts and water bodies includes various *fishes*, *sea snakes*, *dolphin*, *shark*, *dugang* (*sea cow*), *octopus*, and *whale*.

Animals also inhabit agricultural areas outside forests, such as *fox*, *wolf*, *nilgai*, *deer*, *mongoose*, *rabbits*, *wild hog*, and *hedgehog*. Common birds in these areas include *nightingale*, *parrot*, *peacock*, *weaver bird*, *chibari*, *pilak*, *vulture*, *kabar*, and *dhor bagla*.

### Interesting Facts

*   The largest bird seen in India is the *Crane*.
*   The smallest bird seen in India is the *Fulsunghano*.
*   The largest butterfly seen in South India is the *Birdwing*.
*   The smallest butterfly seen in India is the *Sirasjevel*.
