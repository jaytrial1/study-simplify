# Introduction to Drainage Systems

The term 'drainage system' refers to the systematic pattern of rivers in an area. When observing the physiography of India, it is seen that a river and its many tributaries come from different directions and merge into a single river. The water of these rivers is emptied into a water reservoir, sea, or a desert. Thus, when a river flows through its system in any area, it is called a **river basin**. When a mountain or an upland separates the flow of water from one another, it is called **Water-divider**.
