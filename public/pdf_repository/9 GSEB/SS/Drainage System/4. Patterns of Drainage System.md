# Pattern of Drainage System

The river system of a main river and its tributaries forms distinct patterns:

## (A) Dendritic Pattern:
*   Resembles the branches of a tree.
*   Forms where the river and its tributaries follow the slope of the land.
*   Example: Ganga.

## (B) Trellis Pattern:
*   Forms a net-like shape.
*   Streams from mountainous regions meet one another due to slopes, often joining the main river at right angles.
*   Example: Many streams from the Himalayan region.

## (C) Rectangular Pattern:
*   Main river and its tributaries meet at right angles.
*   Mostly develops in areas with strong jointing or faulting in the rock structure.
*   Example: Rivers flowing through rift valleys, like Narmada.

## (D) Radial Pattern:
*   Streams flow out in all directions from a central mountain or elevated point.
*   Example: Rivers of Saurashtra.
