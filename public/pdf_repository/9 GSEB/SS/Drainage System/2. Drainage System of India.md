# Drainage System of India

The drainage system of India follows its physiography. Accordingly, the drainage system of India is divided into two parts:
1.  Himalayan Rivers
2.  Peninsular Rivers

Rivers of India are divided into two zones according to the physical features, resulting in large differences between the Himalayan and Peninsular rivers.

**Himalayan Rivers:**
*   Contain water throughout the year (perennial).
*   Water volume increases due to heavy monsoon rain and melting snow in summer.
*   Major rivers like Sindhu (Indus) and Brahmaputra originate in the northern part of the Himalayas.
*   These rivers have cut deep gorges through the mountains.
*   Himalayan rivers cover a long distance from their origin to the sea.
