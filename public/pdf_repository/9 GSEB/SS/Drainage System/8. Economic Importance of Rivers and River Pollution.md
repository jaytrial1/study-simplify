This section highlights the historical and modern significance of rivers for human activities and discusses the growing problem of river pollution and its prevention.

# Economic Importance of Rivers

Rivers have been historically important to humans. They are a natural resource of water, crucial for many human activities.
*   Satisfy basic human needs.
*   Agriculture and industries have developed along rivers.
*   Ancient civilizations flourished along river banks.
*   Major cities like Delhi, Kolkata, Ahmedabad, Bharuch, and Surat have developed along rivers.
*   Rivers are the base for an agrarian country like India.
*   Modern uses include drinking water, irrigation, hydel power generation, and navigation.

# River Pollution

River water is heavily used in domestic activities, agriculture, and industrial production. Despite rivers being considered holy ('Lokmata'), industrial effluents and sewage water are released into them, along with solid waste.
*   Industrialization and urbanization increase water pollution.
*   River water pollution is a national problem.
*   Government is making efforts to prevent pollution.
*   Public participation is also needed.

## Measures to Prevent Water Pollution:
*   Strict implementation of rules to prevent water pollution.
*   Implementing water purification projects through initiatives like the National River Conservation Project (NRCP).
*   Strict rules for industrial units to prevent releasing untreated effluents.
*   Industrial units should filter harmful contents from chemical effluents before discharge.
*   Citizens should avoid mixing domestic waste into rivers.

*   National River Conservation Project (NRCP) was introduced as the second stage of the Ganga River Purification effort.
