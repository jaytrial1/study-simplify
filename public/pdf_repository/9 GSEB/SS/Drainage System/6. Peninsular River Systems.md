This section details the major rivers of the Indian peninsula, noting their origins, flow directions, and basins, highlighting the role of the Western Ghats.

# Peninsular River Systems

Western Ghats are the main water divide for peninsular rivers. Major rivers of the peninsula include Mahanadi, Godavari, Krishna, Kaveri, etc., which flow eastwards into the Bay of Bengal. Some rivers flow west of the Western Ghats. Large rivers like Narmada and Tapi flow from east to west into the Arabian Sea.

## Narmada Basin:
*   Originates near Amarkantak plateau in Madhya Pradesh.
*   Flows westward in a rift valley.
*   Flows faster through rocky terrain near Jabalpur (marble rocks), forming the Dhuandhar Falls.
*   Tributaries are shorter and mostly meet at right angles.
*   Basin area: Madhya Pradesh and Gujarat.
*   Length: About 1312 km.

## Tapi Basin:
*   Originates in Betul District, Madhya Pradesh, in the Satpuda ranges.
*   Flows in a rift valley, parallel to Narmada, into the Arabian Sea.
*   Basin area: Madhya Pradesh and Maharashtra.
*   Length: Almost 724 km.
*   The coastal plain between the Arabian Sea and Western Ghats is narrow, resulting in shorter westward-flowing rivers like Sabarmati and Mahi (Mahisagar).

## Godavari Basin:
*   Largest peninsular river.
*   Starts from the slopes of Western Ghats near Nasik in Maharashtra.
*   Length: About 1465 km.
*   Flows east to the Bay of Bengal.
*   Largest basin among peninsular rivers.
*   Basin area: About 50% in Maharashtra, rest in Madhya Pradesh and Andhra Pradesh.
*   Major tributaries: Purna, Vardha, Pranhita, Manjara, Vainganga, and Penganga.
*   Known as 'Ganga of South' due to its length and large basin.

## Mahanadi Basin:
*   Originates in the mountainous area of Chhattisgarh.
*   Flows through Odisha and meets the Bay of Bengal.
*   Length: About 860 km.
*   Basin area: Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, and Odisha.

## Krishna Basin:
*   Originates near Mahabaleshwar in Maharashtra.
*   Length: About 1400 km.
*   Major tributaries: Tungabhadra, Koyna, Ghatprabha, Musi, and Bhima.
*   Basin area: Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Andhra Pradesh.

## Kaveri Basin:
*   Originates in the Brahmagiri Range in Western Ghats.
*   Length: About 760 km.
*   Major tributaries: Amaravati, Bhavani, Hemvati, and Kalini.
*   Basin area: Kerala, Karnataka, and Tamil Nadu.
*   Meets the Bay of Bengal south of Cuddalore in Tamil Nadu.

Other smaller rivers flowing eastwards to the Bay of Bengal include Damodar, Brahmani, Vaitarni, and Suvarnarekha.
