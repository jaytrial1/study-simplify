# Introduction to Indian Democracy

India holds the distinction of having the highest number of voters globally and is the largest democratic country in the world by population. This remarkable democracy has thrived for over six decades, navigating numerous political changes and challenges. The resilience and continuity of Indian democracy are attributed to the dedication and insight of its citizens. This chapter will delve into the characteristic features of India's democratic system.
