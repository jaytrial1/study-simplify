# Public Opinion in Democracy

In a democratic country like India, the elected representatives represent the people. In a representative system, the ruling party aims to regain and retain power, which necessitates staying connected with the populace. To govern effectively and address national problems, it is crucial for the government to consider public opinions and thoughts when framing policies. **Public opinion** is highly important for political parties, representatives, and organizations. Collective public opinion holds significant weight in a democracy.

Strong and organized public opinion is vital for a healthy democracy. It can highlight government malfunctions and create conditions where the government cannot afford to misrule or neglect the country. An alert and informed public is difficult for the government to mislead. Various modern mediums play a crucial role in shaping public opinion.
