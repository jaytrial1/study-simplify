# Political Parties and the Opposition

India is a **multi-party democracy**, hosting numerous political parties. The Election Commission sets specific criteria for validating political parties, classifying them as either **National parties** or **Regional parties** based on factors like vote share in previous general elections across a certain number of states.

According to criteria, a party securing more than four percent of the total votes in the previous general elections in at least four major states is recognized as a national party; others are regional parties. As of a certain date (March 10th, 2014, according to the text), a large number of parties were registered, with new ones added yearly.

The two major national parties mentioned are the **Indian National Congress (INC)** and the **Bhartiya Janata Party (BJP)**. Other national parties listed are the Bahujan Samajwadi Party (BSP), Communist Party of India Marxist (CPIM), and National Congress Party (NCP). Examples of regional parties include Shiv Sena, Trinamool Congress, Samajwadi Party (SP), Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam (DMK), All India Anna Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam (AIADMK), Aam Aadmi Party (AAP), Janata Dal (United) (JDU), and Rashtriya Janata Dal (RJD). A party's status can change based on its performance in elections.

The party or coalition forming the government is known as the **ruling party**. The party or parties not in power constitute the **opposition party**.

The opposition party plays a crucial role by democratically opposing objectionable plans and policies of the government, helping to shape public opinion. However, in a healthy democracy, it is desirable that the opposition supports the ruling party on social and public causes aimed at national welfare. The success of democracy relies on a balanced relationship between the ruling and opposing parties. An effective and strong opposition is considered a prerequisite for a good democracy. Democracy is often likened to a chariot, with the ruling party and the opposing party acting as its two wheels.
