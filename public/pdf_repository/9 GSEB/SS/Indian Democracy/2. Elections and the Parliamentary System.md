# Elections and the Parliamentary System

Democracy exists in many countries worldwide, yet their governance structures differ. In a democracy, each voter casts their own vote, and the right to vote is universal, regardless of variations in national governance. The democratic process is sustained through the act of voting during elections.

India operates under a **Parliamentary System**. The Parliament serves as the national institution representing the people. The Parliament of India comprises the President, the Vice President, the **Rajya Sabha** (Upper House), and the **Lok Sabha** (Lower House). At the state level, there is the **Legislative Assembly**, and some states also have a **Legislative Council**. The voter plays a central role in the formation of these bodies. In India, all governing bodies are elected through voting, thereby realizing democracy through elections.
