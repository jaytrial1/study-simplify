# The Importance of the Voter

The voter is a vital component of a democracy. India has adopted **Universal Adult Suffrage**, granting the right to vote to every person aged 18 or above who is listed in the voter roll. This right is extended without discrimination, provided the person is not legally bankrupt or mentally unstable. The Constitution guarantees the right to vote despite issues like illiteracy, poverty, or social standing.

The system of Universal Adult Suffrage is founded on the principle of '**One Person, One Vote**'. It is considered both a right and a duty for every citizen to cast their vote without being influenced by greed or fear. Voters should be aware and vigilant about their right. The Constitution ensures this right for all citizens, irrespective of caste, creed, religion, or education. Voting is a fundamental process in democracy, and every voter should exercise this right.
