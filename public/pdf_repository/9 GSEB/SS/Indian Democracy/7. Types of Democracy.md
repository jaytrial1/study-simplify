# Types of Democracy

While many countries around the world are democracies, there is diversity in their systems. India follows a **Parliamentary democratic system**. In contrast, countries like the USA have a **Presidential parliamentary system** (Note: The text seems to conflate Presidential and Parliamentary systems here; it likely means Presidential system as an alternative to Parliamentary). We will look at two main types: Parliamentary Democracy and Presidential Democracy.

## Parliamentary Democracy

India has adopted the Parliamentary Democracy. In this system, the President appoints the leader of the party or coalition that secures a majority of seats in the Lok Sabha as the **Prime Minister**. The Prime Minister then forms the government. The Parliamentary Government is directly accountable to the Lok Sabha and remains in power as long as it maintains the confidence of the majority of Members of Parliament.

## Presidential Democracy

Presidential Democracy is another significant type of democracy. In this system, the **President is directly elected by the people**. The President serves for a fixed term as defined by the Constitution. Presidential Democracy is common in many countries, including the USA.
