# Seasons of India

Weather in India remains almost the same for approximately two months, which is considered a **season**. Traditionally, there are six seasons in India: Hemant, Shishir, Vasant, Grishma, Varsha, and Sharad. Since the difference between successive traditional seasons is often subtle, taking two seasons together results in three main seasons:

1.  Cold season (Winter)
2.  Hot season (Summer)
3.  Rainy season (Monsoon)

Changes in these seasons are distinctly experienced in India. Cold weather starts with winter, temperature gradually increases during summer, and with the onset of monsoon, air gathers moisture and brings rain.

The Indian Meteorological Department (IMD) of the Government of India in Delhi has divided the climate of India into four seasons:

1.  Cold Weather season - Winter (December to February)
2.  Hot Weather season - Summer (March to May)
3.  Advancing Monsoon - Rainy season (June to September)
4.  Retreating Monsoon season (October to November)
