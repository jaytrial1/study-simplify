This section provides information about the national agency responsible for weather forecasting and climate studies in India.

# Indian Meteorological Department

The **Indian Meteorological Department (IMD)** broadcasts weather news via radio, television, newspapers, and websites. Established in 1875 at Kolkata, its main office was in Pune until 2005 and is now in New Delhi. It has six regional offices located at Chennai, Guwahati, Kolkata, Mumbai, Pune, and Nagpur, as well as offices in every state capital. IMD has established Observation Stations from India up to Antarctica. Weather forecasts are made based on information collected from these stations.
