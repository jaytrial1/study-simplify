This section describes the period after the main rainy season when monsoon winds withdraw, leading to changing weather conditions.

# Season of Retreating Monsoon Winds (October - November)

The duration between October and November is known as the **Retreating Monsoon** season. During this time, vertical sunrays gradually shift southwards, creating low pressure over the Indian Ocean in the south. High pressure gradually develops over North India. By the end of September, a new pressure system develops over the Arabian Sea and Bay of Bengal, weakening the monsoon winds that have reached the interior.

As pressure conditions change, these winds start flowing back towards the sea during October-November. This period is therefore called the season of 'Retreating Monsoon'. A season of dry and cool winds begins, replacing the hot summer conditions.

*   **Clear sky** and **increasing day temperature** are main characteristics.
*   The soil retains moisture.
*   Night is cool and pleasant.
*   Daytime weather can be uncomfortable due to high temperature and humidity. This situation is known as **'October Heat'**.
*   In Gujarat, it is locally known as **'Bhadarvi Taap'**.
