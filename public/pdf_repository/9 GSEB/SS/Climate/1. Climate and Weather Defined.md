# Understanding Climate and Weather

**Climate** is defined as the average of atmospheric conditions over a long period, typically determined from the average weather conditions of summer, winter, and rainy seasons of a place for about 35 years or more. In essence, it is the average of long-term atmospheric conditions.

**Weather**, in contrast, is the average of short-term conditions of the atmosphere. Weather can change at any time during a day. Changes in weather conditions depend on factors such as air temperature, air pressure, humidity, rain, fog, or the amount of clouds. Weather affects our daily agricultural activities. The Indian Meteorological Department publishes a Daily Weather Map and a Report for the entire nation.
