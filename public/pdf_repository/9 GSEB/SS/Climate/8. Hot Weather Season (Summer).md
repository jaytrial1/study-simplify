This section describes the summer season in India, characterized by rising temperatures and hot, dry conditions.

# Hot Weather Season - Summer (March to May)

The warm and dry season between March and May is called **Summer** in India. During this period, vertical sun rays gradually shift northwards, causing the landmass to become warmer and temperatures to increase continuously.

*   March is the hottest month in southern India, with temperatures exceeding 40°C at many places.
*   Central and North-West India experience maximum temperatures during April and May, sometimes reaching around 45°C to 50°C.
*   The entire nation experiences very high temperatures during summer.

Due to the altitude of the peninsula and plateau, summer in South India is slightly milder, with lower temperatures compared to North India (e.g., Delhi/Allahabad ~34°C compared to Madurai ~30°C and Bengaluru ~27°C). Physical features and proximity to the sea have a dominating effect on temperatures in the south.
