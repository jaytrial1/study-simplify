# Resistance Movements Against Forest Control

Across India and the world, forest communities resisted the changes imposed by colonial forest policies. Leaders of these movements against the British, such as Siddhu and Kanu in the Santhal Parganas, Birsa Munda of Chhotanagpur, and Alluri Sitarama Raju of Andhra Pradesh, are still remembered in songs and stories.
