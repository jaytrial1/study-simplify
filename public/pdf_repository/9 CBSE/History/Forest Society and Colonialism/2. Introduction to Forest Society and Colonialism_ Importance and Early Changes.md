# Introduction to Forest Society and Colonialism: Importance and Early Changes

Forests provide a vast array of products essential to our daily lives, from paper, furniture, and building materials to dyes, spices, food items like coffee and rubber, medicinal herbs, fuel, fodder, and packaging. The biodiversity found in forests, such as the Amazon or the Western Ghats, can be incredibly rich, with hundreds of plant species in a small area.
