This section describes how the Nazi regime implemented its racial ideology once in power, identifying and persecuting groups deemed 'undesirable,' particularly focusing on the systematic exclusion, ghettoization, and annihilation of Jews, as well as the persecution of other groups like Gypsies and Poles.

### Establishment of the Racial State

Once in power, the Nazis rapidly pursued their goal of creating an exclusive racial community of pure Germans by physically eliminating those considered 'undesirable' in their expanded empire. They desired a society solely of 'pure and healthy Nordic Aryans,' who were seen as 'desirable' and worthy of prosperity. All others were classed as 'undesirable,' meaning even Germans considered impure or abnormal were denied the right to exist. Under the *Euthanasia Programme*, Helmuth's father and other Nazi officials condemned many Germans deemed mentally or physically unfit to death.

Jews were the primary target, but not the only group classified as 'undesirable'. Many Gypsies and blacks in Nazi Germany were considered racially 'inferior' threats to Aryan purity and were persecuted. Russians and Poles were seen as subhuman and denied basic humanity. When Germany occupied Poland and parts of Russia, captured civilians were forced into slave labour, many dying from hardship and starvation.

Jews suffered the most. Nazi hatred of Jews was partly rooted in traditional Christian hostility, stereotyping them as Christ-killers and usurers. Historically, Jews were barred from owning land, surviving through trade and moneylending, and confined to separate areas called *ghettos*. They faced periodic violence and expulsion. However, Hitler's hatred was based on pseudoscientific racial theories claiming conversion couldn't solve the 'Jewish problem,' only total elimination could.

From 1933 to 1938, Nazis terrorized, *pauperised*, and segregated Jews, forcing them to leave Germany. The next phase (1939-1945) focused on concentrating them in specific areas and eventually killing them in gas chambers, mainly in Poland.

### The Racial Utopia

Under the cover of war, the Nazis implemented their murderous racial ideal, with *genocide* and war becoming intertwined. Occupied Poland was divided; much of the northwest was annexed to Germany. Poles were expelled from their homes and properties, which were given to ethnic Germans brought from occupied Europe. Poles were then herded into the *General Government*, a region serving as the destination for all 'undesirables'. Polish intellectuals were murdered to keep the population intellectually and spiritually submissive. Polish children resembling Aryans were forcibly taken from mothers, examined by 'race experts,' and raised in German families if they passed, or left to perish in orphanages if they failed. The General Government, with large ghettos and gas chambers, became a primary killing field for Jews.

**Persecution** - Systematic, organised punishment of those belonging to a group or religion

## Stage 1: Exclusion 1933-1939

YOU HAVE NO RIGHT TO LIVE AMONG US AS CITIZENS

The Nuremberg Laws of citizenship of September 1935:

1.  Only Persons of German or related blood would henceforth be German citizens enjoying the protection of the German empire.
2.  Marriages between Jews and Germans were forbidden.
3.  Extramarital relations between Jews and Germans became a crime.
4.  Jews were forbidden to fly the national flag.

Other legal measures included:

*   Boycott of Jewish businesses
*   Expulsion from government services
*   Forced selling and confiscation of their properties

Besides, Jewish properties were vandalised and looted, houses attacked, synagogues burnt and men arrested in a pogrom in November 1938, remembered as 'the night of broken glass' (*Kristallnacht*).
