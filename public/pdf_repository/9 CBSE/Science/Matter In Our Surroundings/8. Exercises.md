# Exercises

1.  Convert the following temperatures to the celsius scale.
    (a) 293 K
    (b) 470 K

> **Answer:**
> (a) 293 K - 273 = 20°C
> (b) 470 K - 273 = 197°C

2.  Convert the following temperatures to the kelvin scale.
    (a) 25°C
    (b) 373°C

> **Answer:**
> (a) 25°C + 273 = 298 K
> (b) 373°C + 273 = 646 K

3.  Give reason for the following observations.
    (a) Naphthalene balls disappear with time without leaving any solid.
    (b) We can get the smell of perfume sitting several metres away.

> **Answer:**
> (a) Naphthalene balls disappear over time due to sublimation. Naphthalene is a substance that changes directly from the solid state to the gaseous state at room temperature without going through the liquid state.
> (b) We can smell perfume from several metres away because perfume is volatile and its particles diffuse rapidly through the air. The particles mix with air particles and move quickly in all directions due to their kinetic energy, reaching our nostrils even from a distance.

4.  Arrange the following substances in increasing order of forces of attraction between the particles - water, sugar, oxygen.

> **Answer:** The forces of attraction between particles are weakest in gases, intermediate in liquids, and strongest in solids. Oxygen is a gas, water is a liquid, and sugar is a solid.
> Increasing order of forces of attraction: Oxygen < Water < Sugar.

5.  What is the physical state of water at-
    (a) 25°C
    (b) 0°C
    (c) 100°C ?

> **Answer:**
> (a) At 25°C, water is typically in the liquid state (room temperature).
> (b) At 0°C, water is at its freezing point (or melting point of ice). It exists as both solid (ice) and liquid water during the phase transition at standard atmospheric pressure.
> (c) At 100°C, water is at its boiling point. It exists as both liquid water and gaseous water vapour (steam) during the phase transition at standard atmospheric pressure.

6.  Give two reasons to justify-
    (a) water at room temperature is a liquid.
    (b) an iron almirah is a solid at room temperature.

> **Answer:**
> (a) Water at room temperature is a liquid because:
>     *   It has a fixed volume but no fixed shape; it takes the shape of the container.
>     *   It flows easily.
> (b) An iron almirah is a solid at room temperature because:
>     *   It has a definite shape and distinct boundaries.
>     *   It has a fixed volume and is rigid.

7.  Why is ice at 273 K more effective in cooling than water at the same temperature?

> **Answer:** Ice at 273 K (0°C) is more effective in cooling than water at the same temperature because ice absorbs additional heat energy (latent heat of fusion) from the surroundings to melt and change into liquid water. This extra absorption of heat provides more cooling effect than water at 0°C, which does not undergo a phase change and only absorbs heat to increase its temperature.

8.  What produces more severe burns, boiling water or steam?

> **Answer:** Steam at 100°C produces more severe burns than boiling water at 100°C. This is because steam particles possess extra energy in the form of latent heat of vaporisation compared to water particles at the same temperature. When steam condenses on the skin, it releases this additional latent heat, causing more severe burns.

9.  Name A,B,C,D,E and F in the following diagram showing change in its state

> **Answer:**
> A: Fusion (Melting) - Solid to Liquid
> B: Vaporization (Boiling/Evaporation) - Liquid to Gas
> C: Condensation - Gas to Liquid
> D: Solidification (Freezing) - Liquid to Solid
> E: Sublimation - Solid to Gas
> F: Deposition - Gas to Solid
