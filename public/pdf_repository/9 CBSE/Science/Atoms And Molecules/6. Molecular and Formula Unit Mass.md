This section explains how to calculate the mass of molecules and ionic compounds based on the atomic masses of the constituent atoms.

# Molecular and Formula Unit Mass

## Molecular Mass

Extending the concept of atomic mass, we can calculate **molecular mass**. The molecular mass of a substance is the sum of the atomic masses of all the atoms in one molecule of that substance. It is the relative mass of a molecule expressed in atomic mass units (u).

### Example Calculation: Molecular Mass

Example 3.1 (a) Calculate the relative molecular mass of water ($\mathrm{H}_{2}\mathrm{O}$).
(b) Calculate the molecular mass of $\mathrm{HNO}_{3}$.

**Solution:**
(a) Atomic mass of hydrogen = 1 u, oxygen = 16 u
So the molecular mass of water, which contains two atoms of hydrogen and one atom of oxygen is:
$= (2 \times \text{atomic mass of H}) + (1 \times \text{atomic mass of O})$
$= (2 \times 1 \text{ u}) + (1 \times 16 \text{ u})$
$= 2 \text{ u} + 16 \text{ u} = 18 \text{ u}$

(b) The molecular mass of $\mathrm{HNO}_{3}$ is the sum of the atomic masses of Hydrogen, Nitrogen, and three Oxygen atoms.
$= (\text{atomic mass of H}) + (\text{atomic mass of N}) + (3 \times \text{atomic mass of O})$
$= (1 \text{ u}) + (14 \text{ u}) + (3 \times 16 \text{ u})$
$= 1 \text{ u} + 14 \text{ u} + 48 \text{ u} = 63 \text{ u}$

## Formula Unit Mass

The **formula unit mass** of a substance is the sum of the atomic masses of all atoms in a **formula unit** of a compound. It is calculated just like molecular mass, but the term "formula unit" is used specifically for substances whose constituent particles are ions (ionic compounds). For example, sodium chloride (NaCl) is an ionic compound, and its formula unit mass is calculated using the atomic masses in its formula unit:

Formula unit mass of NaCl = (1 $\times$ atomic mass of Na) + (1 $\times$ atomic mass of Cl)
$= (1 \times 23 \text{ u}) + (1 \times 35.5 \text{ u})$
$= 23 \text{ u} + 35.5 \text{ u} = 58.5 \text{ u}$

### Example Calculation: Formula Unit Mass

Example 3.2 Calculate the formula unit mass of $\mathrm{CaCl}_{2}$.

**Solution:**
Formula unit mass of $\mathrm{CaCl}_{2}$ = (atomic mass of Ca) + (2 $\times$ atomic mass of Cl)
$= (40 \text{ u}) + (2 \times 35.5 \text{ u})$
$= 40 \text{ u} + 71 \text{ u} = 111 \text{ u}$

### Questions

1. Calculate the molecular masses of $\mathrm{H}_{2}, \mathrm{O}_{2}, \mathrm{Cl}_{2}, \mathrm{CO}_{2}, \mathrm{CH}_{4}, \mathrm{C}_{2}\mathrm{H}_{6}$, $\mathrm{C}_{2}\mathrm{H}_{4}, \mathrm{NH}_{3}, \mathrm{CH}_{3}\mathrm{OH}$. (Use atomic masses from Table 3.2)
    > **Answer:**
    > Atomic masses: H=1u, O=16u, Cl=35.5u, C=12u, N=14u
    > $\mathrm{H}_2$: $2 \times 1 = 2 \text{ u}$
    > $\mathrm{O}_2$: $2 \times 16 = 32 \text{ u}$
    > $\mathrm{Cl}_2$: $2 \times 35.5 = 71 \text{ u}$
    > $\mathrm{CO}_2$: $(1 \times 12) + (2 \times 16) = 12 + 32 = 44 \text{ u}$
    > $\mathrm{CH}_4$: $(1 \times 12) + (4 \times 1) = 12 + 4 = 16 \text{ u}$
    > $\mathrm{C}_2\mathrm{H}_6$: $(2 \times 12) + (6 \times 1) = 24 + 6 = 30 \text{ u}$
    > $\mathrm{C}_2\mathrm{H}_4$: $(2 \times 12) + (4 \times 1) = 24 + 4 = 28 \text{ u}$
    > $\mathrm{NH}_3$: $(1 \times 14) + (3 \times 1) = 14 + 3 = 17 \text{ u}$
    > $\mathrm{CH}_3\mathrm{OH}$: $(1 \times 12) + (3 \times 1) + (1 \times 16) + (1 \times 1) = 12 + 3 + 16 + 1 = 32 \text{ u}$

2. Calculate the formula unit masses of $\mathrm{ZnO}, \mathrm{Na}_{2}\mathrm{O}, \mathrm{K}_{2}\mathrm{CO}_{3}$, given atomic masses of $\mathrm{Zn}=65 \text{ u}, \mathrm{Na}=23 \text{ u}, \mathrm{K}=39 \text{ u}, \mathrm{C}=12 \text{ u}$, and $\mathrm{O}=16 \text{ u}$.
    > **Answer:**
    > $\mathrm{ZnO}$: $(1 \times \text{atomic mass of Zn}) + (1 \times \text{atomic mass of O}) = (1 \times 65) + (1 \times 16) = 65 + 16 = 81 \text{ u}$
    > $\mathrm{Na}_2\mathrm{O}$: $(2 \times \text{atomic mass of Na}) + (1 \times \text{atomic mass of O}) = (2 \times 23) + (1 \times 16) = 46 + 16 = 62 \text{ u}$
    > $\mathrm{K}_2\mathrm{CO}_3$: $(2 \times \text{atomic mass of K}) + (1 \times \text{atomic mass of C}) + (3 \times \text{atomic mass of O}) = (2 \times 39) + (1 \times 12) + (3 \times 16) = 78 + 12 + 48 = 138 \text{ u}$
