# Cattle farming

Cattle husbandry serves two purposes: milk production and draught labour (tilling, irrigation, carting). Indian cattle species are *Bos indicus* (cows) and *Bos bubalis* (buffaloes). Females producing milk are *milch animals* (dairy animals), while those used for farm work are *draught animals*.

Milk production is related to the *lactation period* (period of milk production after calf birth). Increasing this period can increase milk production. *Exotic* (foreign) breeds like Jersey and Brown Swiss have long lactation periods. *Local* breeds like Red Sindhi and Sahiwal are disease resistant. Cross-breeding exotic and local breeds combines these desired qualities.

## uestion

1. Which method is commonly used for improving cattle breeds and why?

> **Answer:** Cross-breeding between exotic (foreign) breeds and local breeds is commonly used. Exotic breeds are selected for desirable traits like long lactation periods (high milk yield), while local breeds are chosen for traits like excellent disease resistance. Cross-breeding aims to produce offspring with both high yield potential and good disease resistance.

Proper housing and cleaning are essential for humane farming, animal health, and clean milk production. Animals need regular brushing. Sheds should be well-ventilated, roofed for protection from weather, and have sloping floors for dryness and easy cleaning.

Dairy animals have two food requirements:
*   **Maintenance requirement:** Food to support a healthy life.
*   **Milk producing requirement:** Food needed during lactation.

Animal feed includes:
*   *Roughage:* High fibre (e.g., fodder).
*   *Concentrates:* Low fibre, high protein and nutrients (e.g., grains, oil cakes).

Cattle need *balanced rations* with proportionate nutrients. Feed additives with micronutrients promote health and milk output.

Cattle suffer from diseases that reduce milk production or cause death. Healthy animals feed regularly and have normal posture. Parasites can be *external* (on skin, causing skin diseases) or *internal* (worms in stomach/intestine, flukes in liver). Infectious diseases are caused by bacteria and viruses. Vaccination prevents many major viral and bacterial diseases.
