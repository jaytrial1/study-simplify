# Storage of grains

Significant losses can occur during agricultural produce storage. Causes include:
*   **Biotic factors:** Insects, rodents, fungi, mites, bacteria.
*   **Abiotic factors:** Inappropriate moisture and temperature.

These factors degrade quality, cause weight loss, poor germination, and discolouration, reducing marketability. Proper treatment and warehouse management control these factors.

Preventive and control measures before storage include:
*   Strict cleaning of the produce.
*   Proper drying (first in sunlight, then in shade).
*   *Fumigation* using chemicals to kill pests.

## uestions

1. Why should preventive measures and biological control methods be preferred for protecting crops?

> **Answer:** Preventive measures and biological control methods are preferred because they reduce or eliminate the need for chemical pesticides. Excessive use of chemicals can be poisonous to other organisms, cause environmental pollution, and lead to pests developing resistance. Preventive measures like proper farming practices avoid infestations, while biological control uses natural enemies, minimizing harm to the environment and non-target species.

2. What factors may be responsible for losses of grains during storage?

> **Answer:** Losses of grains during storage can be caused by biotic factors such as insects, rodents, fungi, mites, and bacteria, and abiotic factors like inappropriate levels of moisture and temperature in the storage area.
