10. Which type of cell division is required for growth and repair of body and which type is involved in formation of gametes?

> **Answer:**
> 1. **Plant Cells vs. Animal Cells:**
>    *   **Cell Wall:** Plant cells have a rigid cell wall outside the plasma membrane (composed mainly of cellulose); animal cells do not.
>    *   **Shape:** Plant cells typically have a fixed, regular shape due to the cell wall; animal cells usually have an irregular or round shape.
>    *   **Plastids:** Plant cells usually contain plastids (like chloroplasts for photosynthesis); animal cells do not.
>    *   **Vacuoles:** Plant cells often have a large central vacuole occupying a significant portion of the cell volume; animal cells have smaller, scattered vacuoles, or sometimes none.
>    *   **Centrioles:** Animal cells have centrioles (involved in cell division); plant cells usually lack centrioles (except in some lower plants).
> 2. **Prokaryotic Cells vs. Eukaryotic Cells:** (Answer provided previously in the table)
> 3. If the plasma membrane ruptures or breaks down, the cell will lose its ability to regulate the entry and exit of substances. The contents of the cell will leak out, and the cell will not be able to maintain its internal environment, leading to the death of the cell.
> 4. If there was no Golgi apparatus, the cell would not be able to effectively package, modify, and transport proteins and lipids synthesized by the ER. Lysosomes would also not be formed. This would disrupt many cellular processes, including secretion, membrane synthesis, and waste digestion, severely impacting the cell's ability to function and likely leading to its death.
> 5. The organelle known as the powerhouse of the cell is the **mitochondrion**. It is called the powerhouse because it is responsible for generating most of the cell's supply of adenosine triphosphate (ATP), which is used as a source of chemical energy to power the cell's activities. This energy is released through cellular respiration.
> 6. The lipids and proteins constituting the cell membrane are synthesised in the **endoplasmic reticulum (ER)**. Lipids are manufactured in the smooth endoplasmic reticulum (SER), and proteins are manufactured on the ribosomes attached to the rough endoplasmic reticulum (RER). These components are then often transported and modified by the Golgi apparatus before being used to build the cell membrane (membrane biogenesis).
> 7. An Amoeba obtains its food through a process called **endocytosis**. The flexible plasma membrane of the Amoeba extends outwards to engulf food particles from its external environment, forming a food vacuole inside the cell.
> 8. **Osmosis** is the movement of water molecules through a selectively permeable membrane from a region of high water concentration (or low solute concentration) to a region of low water concentration (or high solute concentration). It is a special type of diffusion involving water.
> 9. **Potato Osmosis Experiment:**
>    (i) Water gathers in the hollowed portion of cups B (sugar) and C (salt) because the solutions inside the cups have a lower concentration of water (higher solute concentration) compared to the pure water in the trough. The potato tissue acts as a selectively permeable membrane, allowing water to move by osmosis from the trough (higher water concentration) into the cups (lower water concentration).
>    (ii) Potato A is necessary for this experiment as a **control**. It contains no solute inside, so there should be no significant net movement of water into the cup, demonstrating that the gathering of water in B and C is due to the presence of the solute and osmosis, not just the cup shape or other factors.
>    (iii) Water does not gather in the hollowed out portion of A because it is empty (no solute to create an osmotic gradient). Water does not gather in D because the potato cup is boiled. Boiling kills the potato cells, destroying their selectively permeable membranes. Without functional membranes, osmosis cannot occur effectively to draw water into the cup.
> 10. The type of cell division required for **growth and repair** of the body is **mitosis**. The type of cell division involved in the formation of **gametes** is **meiosis**.
