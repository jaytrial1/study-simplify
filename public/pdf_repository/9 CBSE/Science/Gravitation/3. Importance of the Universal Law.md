# Importance of the Universal Law

The universal law of gravitation is crucial because it successfully explains several phenomena that were previously not understood as being connected:

*   **The force that binds us to the Earth:** Gravity is what keeps us grounded.
*   **The motion of the Moon around the Earth:** The Earth's gravity provides the necessary centripetal force.
*   **The motion of planets around the Sun:** The Sun's immense gravity keeps the planets in orbit.
*   **The tides due to the Moon and the Sun:** The gravitational pull of the Moon and the Sun on Earth's water causes tides.
