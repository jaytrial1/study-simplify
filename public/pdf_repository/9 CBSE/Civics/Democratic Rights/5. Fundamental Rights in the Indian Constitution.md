# Fundamental Rights in the Indian Constitution

In India, like many democracies, rights are included in the Constitution. Some rights deemed **fundamental** to life are given a special status and are called **Fundamental Rights**. The Preamble of our Constitution promises equality, liberty, and justice for all citizens, and the Fundamental Rights translate this promise into action. They are a crucial basic feature of India's Constitution.

Our Constitution provides for **six Fundamental Rights**. Can you recall them? Let's examine what each means for an ordinary citizen.
