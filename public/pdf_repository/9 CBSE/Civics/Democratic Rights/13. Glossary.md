# Glossary

**Amnesty International:** An international volunteer organization campaigning for human rights, publishing reports on violations globally.
**Claim:** A demand for legal or moral entitlements made by a person on fellow citizens, society, or the government.
**Covenant:** A promise made by individuals, groups, or countries to uphold a rule or principle, legally binding on signatories.
**Dalit:** A term for persons belonging to castes traditionally considered low and untouchable, also known as Scheduled Castes or Depressed Classes.
**Ethnic group:** A human population sharing a common ancestry, united by cultural practices, religious beliefs, and historical memories.
**Traffic:** Selling and buying of men, women, or children for immoral purposes.
**Summon:** An order issued by a court requiring a person to appear before it.
**Writ:** A formal document containing an order of the court to the government, issued only by a High Court or the Supreme Court.
