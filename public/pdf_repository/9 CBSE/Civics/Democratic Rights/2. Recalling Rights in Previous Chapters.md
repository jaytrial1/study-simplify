# Recalling Rights in Previous Chapters

Throughout this book, the concept of rights has been mentioned repeatedly. Can you recall the dimension of rights discussed in each of the five preceding chapters?

Chapter One: Chile under Pinochet and Poland under Jaruzelsky were not democratic because ...
> **Answer:** In Chile under Pinochet and Poland under Jaruzelsky, citizens were denied basic rights like freedom of speech, assembly, and political participation, which are essential for a democratic system.

Chapter Two: A comprehensive definition of democracy includes ...
> **Answer:** A comprehensive definition of democracy includes not just elections but also the protection of basic rights and freedoms for all citizens, ensuring equality and dignity.

Chapter Three: Our Constitution makers believed that fundamental rights were quite central to the Constitution because ...
> **Answer:** Our Constitution makers believed that fundamental rights were central because they provide the foundation for equality, liberty, and justice, protecting citizens from arbitrary state power and ensuring a dignified life.

Chapter Four: Every adult citizen of India has the right to ... and to be ...
> **Answer:** Every adult citizen of India has the right to **vote** and to be **elected** to government.

Chapter Five: If a law is against the Constitution, every citizen has the right to approach ...
> **Answer:** If a law is against the Constitution, every citizen has the right to approach **the courts** (judiciary).
