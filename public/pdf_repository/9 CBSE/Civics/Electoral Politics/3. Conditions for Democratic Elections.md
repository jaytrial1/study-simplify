# Conditions for Democratic Elections

Elections can be conducted in various ways, and while all democratic countries hold them, many non-democratic countries also do. To distinguish democratic elections, we need a simple list of minimum conditions:

-   First, **everyone should be able to choose**. This means universal adult franchise – everyone has one vote, and every vote has equal value.

-   Second, there should be **something to choose from**. Parties and candidates must be free to contest and offer genuine choices to voters.
-   Third, the choice must be offered at **regular intervals**. Elections must be held periodically, every few years.
-   Fourth, the **candidate preferred by the people should be elected**.
-   Fifth, elections should be conducted in a **free and fair manner**, allowing people to choose as they truly wish.

These conditions may seem simple, but many countries fail to meet them. This chapter will assess Indian elections against these criteria.
