# Working of Institutions in a Democracy

Democracy involves more than just electing rulers; it requires rulers to follow rules and procedures, working within institutions. This chapter examines how institutions function in a democracy by looking at how major decisions are made, implemented, and resolved in India. We will focus on the legislature, executive, and judiciary.

We will explore what each institution does, how it connects to others, and how its functioning impacts democracy. The goal is to understand how these institutions collectively govern. We will use examples from the national government (Central Government, Union Government, or Government of India) and encourage thinking about state-level examples.
