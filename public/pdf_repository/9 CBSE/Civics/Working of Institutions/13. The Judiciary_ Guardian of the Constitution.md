This section introduces the judiciary as the system of courts and its crucial role in resolving disputes and upholding the law and the Constitution.

# The Judiciary

Let's revisit the Mandal case. The dispute was ultimately settled by the Supreme Court. This highlights the essential role of an independent and powerful **judiciary** in a democracy.

Imagine the Mandal case without a Supreme Court, or with a court powerless to judge government actions, or one that people didn't trust, or whose decisions were not accepted. The dispute might never have been resolved peacefully.

The judiciary refers to all the courts at different levels in a country. India has an **integrated judiciary**:

*   **Supreme Court:** At the national level, its decisions bind all other courts.
*   **High Courts:** In the states.
*   **District Courts:** At the district level.
*   Courts at the local level.

The Supreme Court is the highest court of appeal in civil and criminal cases and can hear appeals against High Court decisions. It can take up disputes:

*   Between citizens.
*   Between citizens and the government.
*   Between two or more state governments.
*   Between governments at the union and state level.
