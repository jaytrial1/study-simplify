# Need for Political Institutions

Governing a country involves many activities: providing security, education, and health facilities; collecting taxes; spending money on administration, defence, and development; and implementing welfare schemes. Decisions must be taken, implemented, and disputes resolved. It is crucial to assign responsibilities clearly and ensure activities continue even when leaders change.

In modern democracies, various arrangements are made to handle these tasks. These arrangements are called **institutions**. A democracy functions well when these institutions perform their assigned roles. The Constitution outlines the basic rules, powers, and functions of each institution.

In the Mandal case example, we saw several institutions at work:

*   The **Prime Minister** and the **Cabinet** are institutions that take important policy decisions.
*   The **Civil Servants** implement the ministers' decisions.
*   The **Supreme Court** is an institution that settles disputes between citizens and the government.

Can you think of some other institutions in this example? What is their role?
> **Answer:** Other institutions involved include:
> *   **Parliament:** Discussed the Mandal report, put pressure on the government, and could have potentially blocked the decision if it disagreed.
>   *   **The President:** Formally announced the government's intention and formally issues orders and laws.
>   *   **The Mandal Commission:** An ad-hoc institution appointed to study and recommend policy.

Working with institutions can be challenging due to rules, regulations, meetings, committees, and routines, which can cause delays and complications. However, these aspects are useful. They provide opportunities for wider consultation in decision-making and make it difficult to rush through bad decisions. This is why democratic governments rely on institutions.

Which institutions are at work in the running of your school? Would it be better if one person alone took all the decisions regarding management of your school?
> **Answer:** Institutions in a school include the Principal, Vice-Principal, teachers (as a collective body), school management committee/board, parent-teacher association, and student council. Institutions facilitate this collaborative approach.
