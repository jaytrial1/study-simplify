# A Major Policy Decision: The 1990 Reservation Order

On August 13, 1990, the Government of India issued an Office Memorandum (O. M. No. 36012/31/90-Est (SCT), dated 13.8.1990). Signed by a Joint Secretary, this short order announced a major policy decision that became highly controversial.

The order stated that 27 per cent of vacancies in civil posts and services under the Government of India would be reserved for **Socially and Educationally Backward Classes (SEBC)**. This extended job reservation, previously available only to Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes, to a new category. Only individuals belonging to these backward classes were eligible for this quota.

The decision was based on the report of the **Second Backward Classes Commission**, also known as the **Mandal Commission**, established in 1979 under B.P. Mandal. The Commission was tasked with identifying socially and educationally backward classes and recommending measures for their advancement. Its 1980 report recommended the 27% job reservation.
