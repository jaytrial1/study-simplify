# Introduction to India's Diverse Landforms

This section introduces the varied physical landscape of India and briefly touches upon the geological processes that shaped it.

India is a vast country with a great variety of landforms. Depending on where you live, you might be familiar with flat plains or rugged mountains and valleys. In fact, India possesses nearly all major physical features found on Earth, including mountains, plains, deserts, plateaus, and islands. These features have been shaped over millions of years by geological processes.

The rocks found across India vary significantly, from hard marble used in structures like the Taj Mahal to soft soapstone used in talcum powder. This variation in rock types contributes to the different colors and compositions of soil found in various places. India's large landmass was formed during different geological periods, which profoundly influenced its relief. Beyond deep geological formations, processes like *weathering*, *erosion*, and *deposition* have further modified the landforms to their current state.
