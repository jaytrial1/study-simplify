# The Purvachal (Eastern Hills and Mountains)

This section describes the extension of the Himalayas in the northeastern part of India.

The Brahmaputra River marks the easternmost boundary of the Himalayas. Beyond the Dihang gorge, the Himalayas take a sharp southward bend and run along India's eastern boundary. These are known as the **Purvachal** or the Eastern hills and mountains.

Running through the northeastern states, these hills are mostly composed of strong *sandstones* (sedimentary rocks). They are covered with dense forests and generally run as parallel ranges and valleys. The Purvachal comprises several distinct hill ranges:

*   Patkai hills
*   Naga hills
*   Manipur hills
*   Mizo hills
