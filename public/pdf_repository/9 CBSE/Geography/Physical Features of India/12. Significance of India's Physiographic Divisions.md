# Significance of India's Physiographic Divisions

This section summarizes how the unique features of each physical division contribute to India's natural resources and development potential.

A detailed look at India's different physiographic units reveals the unique features of each region. It is clear that each region complements the others, enriching the country with natural resources.

*   The mountains (Himalayas) are major sources of **water** and **forest wealth**.
*   The northern plains are the **granaries** of the country, providing the base for early civilizations due to their fertility.
*   The plateau (Peninsular Plateau) is a storehouse of **minerals**, crucial for industrialization.
*   The coastal regions and island groups provide sites for **fishing** and **port activities**.

Thus, the diverse physical features of India offer immense future possibilities for development.
