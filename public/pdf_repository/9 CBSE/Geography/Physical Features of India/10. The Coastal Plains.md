# The Coastal Plains

This section describes the narrow coastal strips that flank the Peninsular Plateau along the Arabian Sea and the Bay of Bengal.

The Peninsular plateau is bordered by a stretch of narrow coastal strips running along the Arabian Sea in the west and the Bay of Bengal in the east.

*   **The Western Coastal Plain:** Located between the Western Ghats and the Arabian Sea, this is a narrow plain. It has three sections:
    *   Northern part: **Konkan** (Mumbai-Goa)
    *   Central stretch: **Kannad Plain**
    *   Southern stretch: **Malabar coast**

*   **The Eastern Coastal Plain:** Located along the Bay of Bengal, these plains are wide and level.
    *   Northern part: **Northern Circar**
    *   Southern part: **Coromandel Coast**

    Large rivers like the Mahanadi, Godavari, Krishna, and Kaveri have formed extensive *deltas* on this coast. Lake Chilika is an important feature along the eastern coast.

    ### Key Concepts & Examples

    *   **Chilika Lake:** The largest salt water lake in India, located in the state of Odisha (formerly Orissa), south of the Mahanadi delta.
