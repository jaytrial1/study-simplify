# The Indian Desert

This section describes the location, climate, and features of the arid region in northwestern India.

The Indian Desert lies towards the western margins of the Aravali Hills. It is an undulating sandy plain covered with **sand dunes**. This region receives very low rainfall, less than 150 mm per year, resulting in an *arid climate* with low vegetation cover.

Streams appear during the rainy season but quickly disappear into the sand due to insufficient water to reach the sea. The **Luni** is the only large river in this region.

### Key Concepts & Examples

*   **Barchans:** Crescent-shaped sand dunes that cover larger areas in the desert.
*   **Longitudinal dunes:** Dunes that become more prominent near the Indo-Pakistan boundary.

Visiting Jaisalmer can provide an opportunity to see groups of barchans.
