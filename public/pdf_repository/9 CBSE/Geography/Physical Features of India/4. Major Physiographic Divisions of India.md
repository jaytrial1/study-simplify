# Major Physiographic Divisions of India

This section provides an overview of the six main physical divisions that characterize the diverse landscape of India.

India's varied physical features can be broadly categorized into the following six major physiographic divisions:

1.  The Himalayan Mountains
2.  The Northern Plains
3.  The Peninsular Plateau
4.  The Indian Desert
5.  The Coastal Plains
6.  The Islands
