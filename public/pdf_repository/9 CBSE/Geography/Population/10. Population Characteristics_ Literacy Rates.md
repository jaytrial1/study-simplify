# Population Characteristics: Literacy Rates

**Literacy** is a very important quality of a population. Obviously, only an informed and educated citizen can make intelligent choices and undertake research and development projects. Low levels of literacy are a serious obstacle for economic improvement.

According to the Census of 2011, a person aged 7 years and above who can read and write with understanding in any language, is treated as literate.

There has been a steady improvement in the literacy levels in India. The literacy rate in the country as per the Census of 2011 is 74.04 per cent; 82.14 per cent for males and 65.46 per cent for females.

**Find out:** Why do such differences exist (between male and female literacy rates)?
> **Answer:** The differences between male and female literacy rates are often due to historical and social factors such as gender inequality, cultural biases favoring education for boys over girls, limited access to schools for girls, safety concerns, and the expectation of girls contributing to household chores or getting married early.
