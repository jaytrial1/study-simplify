# Population Characteristics: Sex Ratio

**Sex ratio** is defined as the number of females per 1000 males in the population. This information is an important social indicator to measure the extent of equality between males and females in a society at a given time. The sex ratio in the country has always remained unfavourable to females.

**Find out:** Find out why this is so?
> **Answer:** A sex ratio unfavourable to females can be caused by various factors including higher female infant mortality rates, gender-biased healthcare and nutrition, female foeticide and infanticide, and social preferences for male children, which can lead to neglect of female children.

**Do You Know?**
*   Kerala has a sex ratio of 1084 females per 1000 males. Puducherry has 1038 females for every 1000 males, while Delhi has only 866 females per thousand males and Haryana has just 877 females per thousand males (based on 2011 data).

**Find out:** What could be the reasons for such variations?
> **Answer:** Variations in sex ratio across states can be due to a combination of factors, including regional differences in cultural practices (like son preference), levels of female education and empowerment, access to and quality of healthcare for women and girls, economic conditions, and the impact of migration patterns (e.g., male-dominated migration to urban areas).
