# The Importance of Biodiversity and Conservation

Let us discuss: What will happen if plants and animals disappear from the earth's surface? Can human beings survive under such a situation? Why is bio-diversity necessary and why should it be conserved?
> **Answer:** If plants and animals disappear, human survival would be impossible. Plants are the primary producers of food and oxygen. Animals are part of the food chain and perform essential ecological roles like pollination and decomposition. Biodiversity is necessary because every species plays a role in maintaining the balance and health of ecosystems, which provide essential services like clean air, water, food, and medicine. Conserving biodiversity is crucial to ensure these ecosystems continue to function and support life, including human life.

We have historically relied on biodiversity for food (cultivated plants), medicine (medicinal plants), and animal resources (milch animals, draught power, transportation, meat, eggs, fish). Insects are vital for pollination and biological pest control. Every species contributes to the ecosystem.

Due to excessive exploitation by humans, the ecosystem has been disturbed. Many plant and animal species are now endangered, and some have become extinct.
