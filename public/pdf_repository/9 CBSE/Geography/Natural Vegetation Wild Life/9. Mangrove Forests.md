# Mangrove Forests

Mangrove tidal forests are found in coastal areas affected by tides, where mud and silt accumulate. These are dense forests with roots of the plants submerged under water.

**Locations:** Deltas of major rivers like the Ganga, Mahanadi, Krishana, Godavari, and Kaveri.

In the **Ganga-Brahmaputra delta (Sundarbans)**, **sundari trees** are common, providing durable hard timber. Other trees like palm, coconut, keora, and agar also grow in parts of the delta.
