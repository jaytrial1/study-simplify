# India's Natural Vegetation and Wildlife

India is a vast country with immense variety in its plant and animal forms, making it one of the world's twelve mega biodiversity countries. With approximately 47,000 plant species, India ranks tenth globally and fourth in Asia for plant diversity. It has about 15,000 flowering plants, accounting for 6% of the world's total. India also has many non-flowering plants like ferns, algae, and fungi, along with around 90,000 species of animals and a rich variety of fish.

**Natural vegetation** refers to a plant community that has grown naturally without human intervention and has remained undisturbed for a long time. This is also known as **virgin vegetation**. Cultivated crops, fruits, and orchards are considered vegetation but not natural vegetation.

## Key Concepts

*   The virgin vegetation purely native to India is called **endemic** or **indigenous species**.
*   Plants that originated outside India are termed **exotic plants**.
*   The term **flora** is used for plants of a specific region or period.
*   The term **fauna** refers to animal species of a particular region or period.
