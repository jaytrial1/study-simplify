# Types of Vegetation in India

Based on variations in climate and terrain, India's natural vegetation can be broadly classified into five major types:

1.  Tropical Evergreen Forests
2.  Tropical Deciduous Forests
3.  Tropical Thorn Forests and Scrubs
4.  Montane Forests
5.  Mangrove Forests

These types are distributed across the country in different regions, as shown in Figure 5.3.

> **Answer:** States in the Northeast and along the Western Ghats have high forest cover primarily due to high rainfall and suitable climatic conditions for dense forest growth, combined with mountainous terrain that limits agriculture and urbanisation in many areas. States in the plains or arid/semi-arid regions have lower forest cover due to less rainfall, fertile land used extensively for agriculture, and higher population density leading to deforestation for settlement and development.
