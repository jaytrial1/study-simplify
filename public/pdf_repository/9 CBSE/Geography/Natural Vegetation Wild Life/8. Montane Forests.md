# Montane Forests

In mountainous areas, vegetation changes with increasing altitude due to the decrease in temperature. This creates a succession of vegetation belts, similar to moving from tropical to tundra regions at different latitudes.

*   **Wet Temperate Forests:** Found between 1000 and 2000 metres. Dominated by evergreen broad-leaf trees like oaks and chestnuts.
*   **Temperate Forests:** Found between 1500 and 3000 metres. Contain coniferous trees such as pine, deodar, silver fir, spruce, and cedar. These cover southern slopes of the Himalayas and high altitude areas in southern and north-east India.
*   **Temperate Grasslands:** Common at higher elevations.
*   **Alpine Vegetation:** Found at altitudes generally above 3,600 metres. Temperate forests and grasslands give way to Alpine vegetation.
    *   **Common trees:** Silver fir, junipers, pines, birches. These trees become progressively stunted as they near the snow-line.
    *   They merge into Alpine grasslands through shrubs and scrubs. These grasslands are used extensively for grazing by nomadic tribes like the Gujjars and the Bakarwals.
*   **Tundra Vegetation:** At higher altitudes, mosses and lichens are found, forming part of the tundra vegetation.
