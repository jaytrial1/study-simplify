# The Thorn Forests and Scrubs

In regions receiving less than 70 cm of rainfall, the natural vegetation consists of thorny trees and bushes. This type is found in the **north-western parts of India**, including semi-arid areas of Gujarat, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Uttar Pradesh, and Haryana.

**Main plant species:** Acacias, palms, euphorbias, cacti.

**Characteristics of plants:**
*   Trees are scattered.
*   Roots are long, penetrating deep to find moisture.
*   Stems are succulent to conserve water.
*   Leaves are mostly thick and small to minimise evaporation.

These forests transition into thorn forests and scrubs in increasingly arid areas.
