# Tropical Evergreen Forests

These forests are found in areas receiving heavy rainfall (more than 200 cm) with a short dry season. They are primarily located in the **Western Ghats**, **Lakshadweep**, **Andaman and Nicobar Islands**, upper parts of **Assam**, and the **Tamil Nadu coast**.

These forests are very dense, with trees reaching heights of up to 60 metres or more. The region is warm and wet throughout the year, supporting luxuriant vegetation including trees, shrubs, and creepers, creating a multi-layered structure. Trees do not shed their leaves at a specific time, so the forests remain green year-round.

**Commercially important trees:** ebony, mahogany, rosewood, rubber, cinchona.
**Common animals:** elephants, monkeys, lemurs, deer, one-horned rhinoceros (in Assam and West Bengal), birds, bats, sloth, scorpions, snails.
