# Understanding Climate and Weather

In previous chapters, we explored the landforms and drainage of our country. These are fundamental aspects of the natural environment. This chapter focuses on the third basic element: the atmospheric conditions that prevail over India. We will learn why we experience different conditions throughout the year, such as wearing woollens in December, feeling hot in May, or receiving rain in June-July. Understanding India's climate provides the answers to these questions.

**Climate** refers to the sum total of weather conditions and variations over a large area for a long period of time (more than thirty years). **Weather** refers to the state of the atmosphere over an area at any point of time. The elements of weather and climate are the same: temperature, atmospheric pressure, wind, humidity, and precipitation. Weather conditions can fluctuate frequently, even within a day. However, there is a common pattern over weeks or months, leading to the division of the year into **seasons** like winter, summer, or rainy seasons based on generalised monthly atmospheric conditions.

The world is divided into numerous climatic regions. India has a specific type of climate, which we will explore in detail.
