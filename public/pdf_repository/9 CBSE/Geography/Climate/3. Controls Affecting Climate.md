This section outlines the major geographical and atmospheric factors that control the climate of any place, including latitude, altitude, pressure, distance from the sea, ocean currents, and relief.

# Climatic Controls

There are six major controls that influence the climate of any place:
1.  **Latitude:** Due to the Earth's curvature, solar energy received varies with latitude, causing air temperature to decrease from the equator towards the poles.
2.  **Altitude:** As one goes to higher altitudes, the atmosphere becomes less dense and temperature decreases. Hills are cooler in summer.
3.  **Pressure and Wind System:** This depends on latitude and altitude and influences temperature and rainfall patterns.
4.  **Distance from the Sea (Continentality):** The sea has a moderating influence. As distance from the sea increases, this influence decreases, leading to **continentality** (extreme temperatures: very hot summers, very cold winters).
5.  **Ocean Currents:** Along with onshore winds, ocean currents affect coastal climates. A coastal area will be warmed or cooled if warm or cold currents flow past it and winds blow onshore.
6.  **Relief Features:** High mountains act as barriers to winds (cold or hot). They can cause precipitation on the windward side if they are high enough and in the path of rain-bearing winds, leaving the leeward side relatively dry.

## Find out

*   Why most of the world's deserts are located in the western margins of continents in the subtropics?
> **Answer:** Most of the world's deserts are located in the western margins of continents in the subtropics due to the influence of subtropical high-pressure belts (causing descending, dry air) and cold ocean currents along these coasts (which prevent moisture-laden winds from reaching the land).
