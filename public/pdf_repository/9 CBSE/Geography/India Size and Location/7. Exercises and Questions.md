3.  The sun rises two hours earlier in Arunachal Pradesh as compared to Gujarat in the west but the watches show the same time. How does this happen?
    > **Answer:** This happens because India follows a single standard time based on the Standard Meridian of India ($82^{\circ} 30^{\prime}$ E). Although there is a natural time difference of about two hours between the easternmost (Arunachal Pradesh) and westernmost (Gujarat) points due to the Earth's rotation and the difference in longitudes, all clocks across the country are set to the time at the standard meridian to maintain uniformity.

4.  The central location of India at the head of the Indian Ocean is considered of great significance. Why?
    > **Answer:** India's central location at the head of the Indian Ocean is significant for several reasons:
    > *   It provides India with a long coastline, facilitating extensive maritime trade and contact with East Asia, Southeast Asia, West Asia, Africa, and Europe.
    > *   The trans-Indian Ocean routes connect countries of Europe in the West and East Asia, giving India a strategic advantage in international trade and communication.
    > *   The Deccan Peninsula's protrusion into the ocean gives India easy access to both the Arabian Sea and the Bay of Bengal routes.
    > *   This prominent position in the Indian Ocean was instrumental in naming the ocean after India.
    > *   Historically, it facilitated the exchange of ideas and commodities between India and other parts of the world.
