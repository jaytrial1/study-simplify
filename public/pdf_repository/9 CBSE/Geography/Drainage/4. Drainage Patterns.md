# Drainage Patterns
The streams within a drainage basin form certain patterns based on the slope of the land, underlying rock structure, and climate. These patterns include **dendritic**, **trellis**, **rectangular**, and **radial**.

*   The **dendritic pattern** develops where the river channel follows the slope of the terrain. The stream and its tributaries resemble the branches of a tree.
*   A **trellis pattern** develops where hard and soft rocks exist parallel to each other. Tributaries join the main river at approximately right angles.
*   A **rectangular pattern** develops on strongly jointed rocky terrain, where streams follow the joints.
*   The **radial pattern** develops when streams flow in different directions from a central peak or dome-like structure.

A combination of several patterns may be found in the same drainage basin.
