# Himalayan River Systems: Overview and Features
The major Himalayan rivers are the Indus, the Ganga, and the Brahmaputra. These are long rivers joined by many large tributaries. A river along with its tributaries is called a **river system**.

Himalayan rivers perform significant erosion in their upper courses, creating features like gorges.

In their middle and lower courses, these rivers form depositional features such as **meanders** (bends), **oxbow lakes** (cut-off meanders), and **deltas** (deposits at the mouth).
