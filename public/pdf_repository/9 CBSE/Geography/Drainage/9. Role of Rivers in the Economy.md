# Role of Rivers in the Economy
Rivers have been fundamentally important throughout human history. Water from rivers is a basic natural resource essential for various human activities. River banks have attracted settlers since ancient times, and these settlements have grown into big cities.

## Activity
Make a list of cities in your state which are located on the bank of a river.
> **Address to Activity:** Identify the major rivers flowing through your state using a map or atlas. Then, list the prominent cities situated along the banks of these rivers.

Using rivers for irrigation, navigation, and hydro-power generation is especially significant in a country like India, where agriculture is the major livelihood source for the majority of the population.
