# India's Self-Sufficiency in Foodgrains

Since gaining Independence, India has aimed for self-sufficiency in foodgrains. Indian policymakers adopted various measures, leading to the **Green Revolution**, a new strategy in agriculture that significantly increased the production of wheat and rice.

Indira Gandhi, the then Prime Minister, marked the success of the Green Revolution by releasing a special stamp titled 'Wheat Revolution' in July 1968. The success in wheat production was later seen in rice as well. While foodgrain production increased, it was not uniform across all crops.

The highest growth rate was seen in wheat and rice production. Uttar Pradesh and Madhya Pradesh were major contributors to wheat production (36 and 18 million tonnes respectively in 2020-21). West Bengal and UP were major rice producers (17 and 16 Million tonnes respectively in 2020-21). Total foodgrain production was 310 Million tonnes in 2020-21 and increased to 315 million tonnes in 2021-22.
