# Understanding Hunger

**Hunger** is another indicator of food insecurity. It's not just a sign of poverty but also contributes to it. Achieving food security requires eliminating current hunger and reducing the risk of future hunger.

Hunger has two main dimensions:
*   **Chronic hunger:** This results from diets that are persistently inadequate in terms of quantity and/or quality. Poor people often suffer from chronic hunger because their very low income prevents them from buying enough food for survival.
*   **Seasonal hunger:** This is linked to the cycles of food growing and harvesting. It's common in rural areas due to the seasonal nature of agricultural work and in urban areas among casual labourers (e.g., less construction work during the rainy season). This type of hunger occurs when a person cannot find work for the entire year.
