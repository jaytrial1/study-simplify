This section introduces the concept that the quality of a country's population, determined by literacy, health, and skills, is crucial for its growth rate.

# Quality of Population

The quality of a population is determined by:
*   **Literacy rate:** The percentage of people who can read and write.
*   **Health:** Indicated by factors like life expectancy.
*   **Skill formation:** The acquisition of skills by the people.

The quality of the population directly influences a country's growth rate. A literate and healthy population is considered an asset.
