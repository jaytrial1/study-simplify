# Economic Activities

Like Vilas and Sakal, people engage in various activities. These activities are classified into three main sectors:
1.  **Primary Sector:** Includes agriculture, forestry, animal husbandry, fishing, poultry farming, mining, and quarrying.
2.  **Secondary Sector:** Includes manufacturing.
3.  **Tertiary Sector:** Includes trade, transport, communication, banking, education, health, tourism, services, insurance, etc.

Activities in these sectors result in the production of goods and services and add value to the national income. These are called **economic activities**.

Economic activities have two parts:
*   **Market activities:** Involve remuneration (pay or profit) for the performer. This includes production of goods or services, including government service.
*   **Non-market activities:** Production for self-consumption. This includes consumption and processing of primary products and own account production of fixed assets.

## Activity

Visit a village or colony located near to your residential area and note down the various activities undertaken by the people of that village or colony.

If this is not possible, ask your neighbour what is their profession? In which of the three sectors will you categorise their work?

Say whether these activities are economic or non-economic activities:
*   Vilas sells fish in the village market.
*   Vilas cooks food for his family.
*   Sakal works in the private firm.
*   Sakal looks after his younger brother and sister.

> **Answer:**
> *   Vilas sells fish in the village market: **Economic activity** (involves earning income/profit).
>   *   Vilas cooks food for his family: **Non-economic activity** (production for self-consumption/family).
>   *   Sakal works in the private firm: **Economic activity** (involves remuneration/salary).
>   *   Sakal looks after his younger brother and sister: **Non-economic activity** (domestic chore/care for family, not for pay).
