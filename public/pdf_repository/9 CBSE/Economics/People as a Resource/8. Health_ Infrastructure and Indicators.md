# Health

Firm maximise profit: Do you think any firm would be induced to employ people who might not work efficiently as healthy workers because of ill health?
> **Answer:** No, a firm aiming to maximize profit would generally prefer to employ healthy workers. Ill health reduces a person's efficiency, increases absenteeism, and can lower overall productivity, which negatively impacts the firm's profitability.

A person's health helps them realize their potential and fight illness, enabling them to maximize their output. Health is essential for well-being. Improving the population's health status has been a national priority.

National health policy aims to improve accessibility of healthcare, family welfare, and nutritional services, focusing on underprivileged segments. Over the last five decades, India has built extensive health infrastructure and developed manpower in primary, secondary, and tertiary sectors (government and private).

These measures have led to improvements in key health indicators:
*   **Life expectancy** increased to over 67.2 years in 2021.
*   **Infant mortality rate (IMR)** decreased from 147 in 1951 to 28 in 2020.
*   **Crude birth rates** dropped to 20.0 (2020).
*   **Death rates** dropped to 6 (2020).

*   *Infant mortality rate is the death of a child below one year of age.*
*   *Birth rate is the number of babies born for every 1,000 people during a particular period of time.*
*   *Death rate is the number of people per 1,000 who die during a particular period of time.*

Increase in life expectancy and improved childcare are indicators of a country's progress. Increased longevity reflects good quality of life and self-confidence. Reducing infant mortality involves protecting children from infection, ensuring nutrition for mother and child, and overall childcare.
