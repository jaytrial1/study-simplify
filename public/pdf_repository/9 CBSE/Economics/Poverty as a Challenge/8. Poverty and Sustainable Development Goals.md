# Poverty and Sustainable Development Goals

The United Nations (UN) Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) propose to end poverty in all its forms by 2030. The SDGs are a shared blueprint for peace and prosperity, comprising 17 goals requiring urgent action globally. Developed countries are expected to support developing countries in achieving these goals, which include ending poverty, improving health and education, reducing inequalities, and promoting sustainable development.

The UN works with member countries to eradicate poverty through the 17 SDGs, which have specific targets. One target of **SDG 1: No Poverty** is to reduce the proportion of people living in poverty in all dimensions by at least half by 2030. India's decline in the proportion of poor reflects its commitment to achieving this goal.
