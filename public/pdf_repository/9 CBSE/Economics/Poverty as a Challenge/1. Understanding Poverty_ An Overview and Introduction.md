# Understanding Poverty: An Overview and Introduction

This chapter examines poverty, a major challenge faced by independent India. It explores this complex issue through examples, discusses how social scientists analyze it, and illustrates poverty trends in India and globally using the concept of the poverty line. The causes of poverty and government anti-poverty measures are also discussed. The chapter concludes by expanding the official definition of poverty to include the concept of human poverty.

In our daily lives, we encounter many individuals we perceive as poor. These may include landless laborers in villages or people living in overcrowded slums in cities. The NITI Aayog utilizes the **Multidimensional Poverty Index (MPI)** to analyze poverty in India. The ratio of multidimensional poverty has significantly decreased from approximately 55 percent in 2005-06 to 25 percent in 2015-16, and further to 15 percent in 2019-21. It is anticipated that multidimensional poverty will soon reach single digits.
