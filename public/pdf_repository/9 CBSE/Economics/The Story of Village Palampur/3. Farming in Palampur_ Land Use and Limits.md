# Farming in Palampur

Farming is the primary production activity in Palampur. About 75% of the working population depends on farming for their livelihood, either as farmers or farm labourers. Their well-being is directly linked to farm production.

However, there is a fundamental limitation in increasing farm production: the land area under cultivation is fixed. Since 1960, there has been no expansion in cultivated land in Palampur. Any wastelands were converted to cultivable land by then, leaving no further scope to increase production by bringing new land under farming.

The standard unit for measuring land is the **hectare**. In villages, local units like bigha or guintha might be used. One hectare is the area of a square with sides of 100 metres.
