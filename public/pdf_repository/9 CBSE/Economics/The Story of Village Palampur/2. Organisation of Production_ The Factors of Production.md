# Organisation of Production

The goal of production is to create the goods and services that people want. There are four key requirements for production:

1.  **Land:** This includes land itself and other natural resources such as water, forests, and minerals.
2.  **Labour:** This refers to the people who do the work. Production activities require different types of labour, from highly educated workers for specific tasks to manual labourers. Each worker contributes labour.
3.  **Physical Capital:** This is the variety of inputs required at every stage of production. Physical capital has two categories:
    *   **Tools, machines, and buildings:** These range from simple tools like a plough to complex machines like generators and computers, and buildings like factories. These items can be used in production over many years and are called **fixed capital**.
    *   **Raw materials and money in hand:** Production needs raw materials (like yarn for a weaver, clay for a potter) and money for payments and buying other necessary items. These are used up during production and are called **working capital**.
4.  **Human Capital:** This is the knowledge and enterprise needed to combine land, labour, and physical capital to produce an output (either for personal use or sale). This crucial fourth factor is referred to as human capital.
