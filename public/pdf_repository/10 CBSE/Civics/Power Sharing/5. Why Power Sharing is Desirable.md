# Why Power Sharing is Desirable

Two main sets of reasons support power sharing:

*   **Prudential Reasons:** Power sharing is beneficial because it reduces the likelihood of conflict between social groups. Conflict can lead to violence and political instability. Power sharing helps maintain political order stability. Although imposing the majority's will might seem effective short-term, it harms national unity long-term. The tyranny of the majority is not only oppressive for the minority but can also negatively affect the majority.

*   **Moral Reasons:** Power sharing is the fundamental spirit of democracy. A democratic government involves sharing power with those affected by its decisions. Citizens have a right to be consulted on their governance. A legitimate government is one where citizens participate and feel invested in the system.

Prudential reasons focus on better outcomes, while moral reasons emphasize the inherent value of power sharing itself.
