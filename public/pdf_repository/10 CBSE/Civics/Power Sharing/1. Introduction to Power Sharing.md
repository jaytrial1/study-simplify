# Overview of Power Sharing in Democracy

In a democracy, power is not held by a single government body. An intelligent distribution of power among the legislature, executive, and judiciary is fundamental to democratic design. This chapter examines the concept of power-sharing further, starting with examples from Belgium and Sri Lanka to understand how democracies manage demands for power-sharing. These examples provide general insights into why power-sharing is needed in a democracy and introduce various forms of power-sharing.
