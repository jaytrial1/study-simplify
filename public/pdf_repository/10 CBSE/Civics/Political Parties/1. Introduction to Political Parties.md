# Introduction to Political Parties

In our study of democracy, political parties have appeared many times. They played a role in the rise of democracies, in forming constitutional designs, in electoral politics, and in the making and working of governments. Political parties also serve as vehicles for federal sharing of power and as negotiators of social divisions. This chapter will examine the nature and working of political parties, particularly in India, by addressing fundamental questions about their necessity and ideal number.

Political parties are among the most visible institutions in a democracy. For many citizens, democracy is synonymous with political parties. Even in remote areas, people who may not know about the Constitution or the government structure are likely to know about political parties. However, this visibility doesn't always mean popularity; many people are critical of parties and blame them for problems in democracy. Despite this, parties have become widespread in democracies globally over the last century.
