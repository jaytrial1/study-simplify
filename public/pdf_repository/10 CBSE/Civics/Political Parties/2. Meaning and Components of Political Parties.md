# Meaning and Components of Political Parties

A political party is defined as a group of people who come together with the goal of contesting elections and holding power in the government. They agree on certain policies and programs for society, aiming to promote the *collective good*. Since different views exist on what constitutes the collective good, parties attempt to persuade people that their proposed policies are superior. They seek to implement these policies by gaining popular support through elections.

Parties therefore reflect fundamental political divisions within a society. They represent a *part* of society and involve **partisanship**. A party is identified by the section of society it represents, the policies it supports, and the interests it upholds.

A political party typically consists of three main components:
*   The **leaders**
*   The **active members**
*   The **followers**
