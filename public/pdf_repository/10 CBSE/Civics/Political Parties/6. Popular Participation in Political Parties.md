This section examines the level of public engagement and trust in political parties, particularly focusing on trends in India.

# Popular Participation in Political Parties

It is often believed that political parties face a crisis due to unpopularity and citizen indifference. However, evidence for India suggests this is only partially true. Surveys over decades show:

*   Political parties do not enjoy high trust in South Asia. More people express 'not much' or 'not at all' trust than 'some' or 'great' trust.
*   Globally, political parties are among the least trusted institutions in most democracies.
*   Despite low trust, the level of *participation* in party activities in India is relatively high. The percentage of people who are members of a political party is higher in India than in many developed countries like Canada, Japan, Spain, and South Korea.
*   Over the last three decades, the proportion of Indians reporting membership in political parties has steadily increased.
*   The proportion of those who feel 'close to a political party' has also risen in India during this period.

Despite some fluctuations, party identification has increased in India.
