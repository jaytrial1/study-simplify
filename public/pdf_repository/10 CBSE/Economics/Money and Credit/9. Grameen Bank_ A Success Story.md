# Grameen Bank of Bangladesh

The Grameen Bank of Bangladesh is a significant success story in providing credit to the poor at reasonable rates. Starting as a small project in the 1970s, it grew to over 9 million members in about 81,600 villages by 2018. Most borrowers are poor women.

These borrowers have demonstrated that poor women are reliable borrowers and can successfully manage small income-generating activities.

Professor Muhammad Yunus, the founder of Grameen Bank and a Nobel Peace Prize recipient, stated that making credit available to the poor on appropriate and reasonable terms allows millions of small people with their small pursuits to contribute significantly to development.
