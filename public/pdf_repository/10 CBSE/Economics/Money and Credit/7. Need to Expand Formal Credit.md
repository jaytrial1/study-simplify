# Need to Expand Formal Credit

Because of the problems associated with high-cost informal credit, it is necessary for banks and cooperative societies to **increase their lending**, particularly in rural areas. This would reduce dependence on informal sources.

Cheap and affordable credit is **crucial for the country's development**. It allows people to borrow for various needs like growing crops, starting businesses, setting up small-scale industries, or trading goods, leading to higher incomes.

Comparing poor urban households with rich urban households:
*   **Poor households:** 54% of loans are from informal sources, 46% from formal sources.
*   **Rich households:** Only 17% of loans are from informal sources, while 83% are from formal sources.

A similar pattern exists in rural areas: richer households access cheap formal credit, while poorer households rely on expensive informal sources.

This indicates two key points:
1.  The formal sector currently meets only about half of the total credit needs of rural people. The rest comes from informal sources.
2.  Formal sector loans are predominantly accessed by richer households, while the poor depend on informal sources.

It is important that formal credit sources not only expand but also ensure that **everyone receives these loans**, especially the poor. A more equal distribution of formal credit would allow the poor to benefit from cheaper loans.
