# Production Across Countries
Historically, production was largely confined within national borders, with trade being the main connection between countries. However, the emergence of **multinational corporations (MNCs)** has changed this. An MNC is a company that **owns or controls production in more than one nation**.

MNCs strategically set up offices and factories in locations offering **cheap labour** and other resources to lower production costs and boost profits.

## Spreading of Production by an MNC
Consider an MNC producing industrial equipment:
*   Designs products in the **United States**.
*   Components manufactured in **China** (for cheap manufacturing).
*   Assembled in **Mexico and Eastern Europe** (for closeness to US and European markets).
*   Finished products sold **globally**.
*   Customer care handled through **call centres in India** (leveraging skilled, English-speaking youth at lower costs).
