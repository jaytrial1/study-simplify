This section consolidates the understanding of globalisation as the rapid integration and interconnection between countries, driven significantly by MNCs and increasing flows of goods, services, investment, and technology.

# What is Globalisation?
Over the past few decades, MNCs have increasingly sought cheap production locations globally, leading to a rise in foreign investment. Simultaneously, foreign trade has grown rapidly, with a large portion controlled by MNCs.

The combined effect of greater foreign investment and trade is the increased integration of production and markets across countries. **Globalisation** is defined as this **process of rapid integration or interconnection between countries**.
MNCs are central to this process, facilitating the movement of goods, services, investments, and technology between countries. This results in regions worldwide being more closely connected than ever before.

## LET'S WORK THESE OUT

1. What is the role of MNCs in the globalisation process?
> **Answer:** MNCs play a major role in globalisation by:
> *   Controlling and spreading production processes across different countries.
> *   Making foreign investments to set up or acquire businesses globally.
> *   Facilitating foreign trade by buying and selling goods and services across borders.
> *   Transferring technology and management practices between countries.
> *   Linking markets and production sites worldwide.
2. What are the various ways in which countries can be linked?
> **Answer:** Countries can be linked through:
> *   Foreign trade (exchange of goods and services).
> *   Foreign investment (MNCs setting up or controlling production).
> *   Movement of technology.
> *   Movement of services.
> *   Movement of people (though this is often restricted).
