# Introduction
As consumers today, we experience a wide variety of goods and services in our markets, from the latest electronics to numerous car brands. This extensive choice is a relatively recent change in India, transforming markets rapidly over the past two decades. This chapter explores the reasons behind these transformations, the factors driving them, and their effects on people's lives.
