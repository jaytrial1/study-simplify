This section acknowledges that the benefits of globalisation have not been universally shared and discusses the need for 'fair globalisation' and the role of government and people's movements in achieving it.

# The Struggle for a Fair Globalisation
Evidence shows that globalisation has not benefited everyone equally. Those with education, skills, and wealth have often gained the most, while many others have not shared in the benefits.

Since globalisation is a reality, the focus is on making it more 'fair'. **Fair globalisation** aims to create opportunities for everyone and ensure a better distribution of its benefits.

The **government** has a major role to play:
*   Implement policies that protect the interests of all citizens, not just the powerful.
*   Ensure labour laws are properly enforced and workers' rights are upheld.
*   Support small producers to improve their competitiveness.
*   Use trade and investment barriers if necessary to protect domestic interests.
*   Negotiate for 'fairer rules' at the WTO.
*   Collaborate with other developing countries to counter the dominance of developed countries in global trade forums.
