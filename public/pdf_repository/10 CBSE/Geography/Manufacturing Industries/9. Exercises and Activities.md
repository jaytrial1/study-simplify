## Answer the following briefly in not more than 30 words.

(i) What is manufacturing?

> **Answer:** Manufacturing is the production of goods in large quantities after processing raw materials into more valuable finished products using machines and various techniques.

(ii) What are basic industries? Give an example.

> **Answer:** Basic industries are those that supply their products or raw materials to other industries for further manufacturing. An example is the iron and steel industry, which provides steel for machinery used in various sectors.

## Write the answers of the following questions in 120 words.

(i) How do industries pollute the environment?

> **Answer:** Industries pollute the environment in several ways. Air pollution occurs through the emission of undesirable gases like sulphur dioxide and carbon monoxide, and particulate matter from factories and power plants. Water pollution results from discharging untreated organic and inorganic industrial wastes, chemicals, and heavy metals into rivers, harming aquatic life. Thermal pollution, where hot water from factories is released into water bodies, reduces oxygen levels. Land pollution is caused by the dumping of solid wastes, harmful chemicals, and garbage, which contaminates soil and groundwater. Additionally, noise pollution from machinery and construction activities can lead to various physiological and psychological issues for humans and animals.

(ii) Discuss the steps to be taken to minimise environmental degradation by industry?

> **Answer:** Minimising environmental degradation by industry requires a multi-pronged approach. To reduce water pollution, industries should minimise water usage through reuse and recycling in successive stages, harvest rainwater, and treat hot water and effluents before discharge. Effluent treatment involves primary (mechanical), secondary (biological), and tertiary (biological, chemical, physical, and recycling) phases. For air pollution, smoke stacks should be fitted with electrostatic precipitators, fabric filters, and scrubbers, and cleaner fuels like oil or gas should replace coal. Noise pollution can be mitigated by fitting generators with silencers, redesigning machinery for energy efficiency and reduced noise, and using noise-absorbing materials. Legal regulation of groundwater overdrawing is also crucial. Implementing these measures integrates economic development with environmental concerns, promoting sustainable industrial practices.
