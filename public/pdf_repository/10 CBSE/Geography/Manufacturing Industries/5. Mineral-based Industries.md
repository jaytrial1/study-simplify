# Mineral-based Industries

Industries that use minerals and metals as raw materials are called **mineral-based industries**.

> **Answer:** Some industries that would fall into this category include: Iron and Steel, Aluminium Smelting, Cement, Machine Tools, Petrochemicals, Fertilizers (using mineral phosphates), and Glass manufacturing.

## Iron and Steel Industry

The iron and steel industry is considered a **basic industry** since all other industries—heavy, medium, and light—depend on it for their machinery. Steel is needed to manufacture a variety of engineering goods, construction material, defence, medical, telephonic, scientific equipment, and a variety of consumer goods.

## Activity

Make a list of all such goods made of steel that you can think of.

> **Answer:** Goods made of steel include:
> *   **Construction:** Beams, rods, reinforced concrete, roofing sheets, bridges.
> *   **Automotive:** Car bodies, engine parts, chassis, nuts, bolts.
> *   **Appliances:** Refrigerators, washing machines, ovens, dishwashers.
> *   **Kitchenware:** Utensils, pots, pans, knives, sinks.
> *   **Tools:** Hammers, wrenches, drills, saws, screwdrivers.
> *   **Machinery:** Industrial machines, agricultural equipment, gears, bearings.
> *   **Infrastructure:** Railway tracks, pipelines, electrical towers, streetlights.
> *   **Medical:** Surgical instruments, hospital beds.
> *   **Defence:** Weapons, tanks, ships.
> *   **Consumer Goods:** Furniture frames, bicycles, office supplies, packaging.

Production and consumption of steel are often regarded as the index of a country's development. Iron and steel is a **heavy industry** because all the raw materials as well as finished goods are heavy and bulky, entailing heavy transportation costs. Iron ore, coking coal, and limestone are required in the ratio of approximately 4:2:1. Some quantities of manganese are also required to harden the steel.

> **Answer:** Steel plants should ideally be located near:
> 1.  **Sources of raw materials:** Proximity to iron ore mines, coking coal fields, and limestone quarries to minimize transportation costs of bulky raw materials.
> 2.  **Water sources:** Abundant water supply is needed for cooling and other processes.
> 3.  **Cheap labour:** Availability of skilled and unskilled workers.
> 4.  **Efficient transport network:** Good railway and road networks are essential for bringing in raw materials and distributing finished products to markets and consumers.
> 5.  **Market proximity:** Being close to the major consumers of steel (e.g., automobile industry, construction sector) can also be a factor.

## Aluminium Smelting

Aluminium smelting is the second most important metallurgical industry in India. It is light, resistant to corrosion, a good conductor of heat, malleable, and becomes strong when it is mixed with other metals. It is used to manufacture aircraft, utensils, and wires. It has gained popularity as a substitute for steel, copper, zinc, and lead in a number of industries.
