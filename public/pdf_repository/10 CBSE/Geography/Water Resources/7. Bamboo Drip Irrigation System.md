This section introduces a unique, centuries-old traditional irrigation method from Meghalaya. It describes how the bamboo drip irrigation system ingeniously uses bamboo pipes and gravity to transport and precisely deliver water to plant roots, showcasing sustainable and efficient water management.

# Bamboo Drip Irrigation System

In Meghalaya, a 200-year-old system of tapping stream and spring water by using bamboo pipes is prevalent. This ingenious system transports about 18-20 litres of water over hundreds of metres, finally reducing the flow to a precise 20-80 drops per minute at the plant site.

## How it Works

*   **Diversion:** Bamboo pipes are used to divert perennial springs on the hilltops to the lower reaches by gravity.

*   **Precise Application:** Reduced channel sections and diversion units are used at the last stage of water application. The final channel section enables water to be dropped directly near the roots of the plant, ensuring highly efficient water use.
