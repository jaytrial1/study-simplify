This section covers significant food crops that are not grains, including sugarcane, various oilseeds, and important beverage crops like tea and coffee, detailing their cultivation requirements and production areas.

# Major Food Crops: Other than Grains

## Sugarcane

*   **Crop Type:** Tropical as well as subtropical crop.
*   **Geographical Conditions:**
    *   **Climate:** Hot and humid.
    *   **Temperature:** 21°C to 27°C.
    *   **Annual Rainfall:** Between 75 cm and 100 cm.
    *   **Irrigation:** Required in regions of low rainfall.
    *   **Soil Preference:** Can be grown on a variety of soils.
    *   **Labor Requirement:** Needs manual labor from sowing to harvesting.
*   **Global Rank:** India is the second largest producer of sugarcane, only after Brazil.
*   **Products:** Main source of sugar, gur (jaggery), khandsari, and molasses.
*   **Major Producing States:** Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Bihar, Punjab, and Haryana.

## Oil Seeds

*   **Global Rank (Groundnut):** In 2020, India was the second largest producer of groundnut in the world after China.
*   **Cropped Area:** Different oil seeds cover approximately 12 percent of the total cropped area of the country.
*   **Main Oil-seeds Produced in India:** Groundnut, mustard, coconut, sesamum (til), soyabean, castor seeds, cotton seeds, linseed, and sunflower.
*   **Usage:**
    *   Most are edible and used as cooking mediums.
    *   Some are also used as raw material in the production of soap, cosmetics, and ointments.

### Specific Oilseed Crop Seasons and Producers:
*   **Groundnut:** Kharif crop; accounts for about half of the major oilseeds produced in the country.
    *   **Largest Producers (2019–20):** Gujarat (largest), followed by Rajasthan and Tamil Nadu.
*   **Linseed and Mustard:** Rabi crops.
*   **Sesamum (Til):** Kharif crop in north India; Rabi crop in south India.
*   **Castor Seed:** Grown both as rabi and kharif crop.

## Tea

*   **Crop Type:** Example of **plantation agriculture**; important beverage crop.
*   **Origin in India:** Introduced initially by the British; today, most tea plantations are owned by Indians.
*   **Geographical Conditions:**
    *   **Climate:** Tropical and sub-tropical.
    *   **Soil:** Deep and fertile, well-drained soil, rich in humus and organic matter.
    *   **Temperature/Moisture:** Requires warm and moist, frost-free conditions all through the year.
    *   **Rainfall:** Frequent showers evenly distributed over the year ensure continuous growth of tender leaves.
*   **Labor Requirement:** Labour-intensive industry; requires abundant, cheap, and skilled labor.
*   **Processing:** Tea is processed within the tea garden to restore its freshness.
*   **Major Producing States:** Assam, hills of Darjeeling and Jalpaiguri districts (West Bengal), Tamil Nadu, and Kerala.
*   **Other Producing States:** Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand, Meghalaya, Andhra Pradesh, and Tripura.
*   **Global Rank:** In 2020, India was the second largest producer of tea after China.

## Coffee

*   **Quality:** Indian coffee is known globally for its good quality.
*   **Variety:** The **Arabica** variety, initially brought from Yemen, is produced in India and is in great demand worldwide.
*   **Cultivation Regions:**
    *   Initially introduced on the **Baba Budan Hills**.
    *   Even today, its cultivation is confined to the **Nilgiri** in Karnataka, Kerala, and Tamil Nadu.

## Horticulture Crops

*   **Global Rank:** In 2020, India was the second largest producer of fruits and vegetables in the world after China.
*   **Diversity:** India produces both tropical and temperate fruits.
*   **Prominent Fruits and Their Regions:**
    *   **Mangoes:** Maharashtra, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Uttar Pradesh, and West Bengal.
    *   **Oranges:** Nagpur and Cherrapunjee (Meghalaya).
    *   **Bananas:** Kerala, Mizoram, Maharashtra, and Tamil Nadu.
    *   **Litchi and Guava:** Uttar Pradesh and Bihar.
    *   **Pineapples:** Meghalaya.
    *   **Grapes:** Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, and Maharashtra.
    *   **Apples, Pears, Apricots, and Walnuts:** Jammu and Kashmir and Himachal Pradesh (in great demand worldwide).
