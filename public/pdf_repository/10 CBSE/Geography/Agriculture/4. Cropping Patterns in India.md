# Cropping Patterns in India

India's physical diversities and cultural plurality are clearly reflected in its agricultural practices and cropping patterns. Various types of food and fiber crops, vegetables and fruits, spices and condiments constitute some of the important crops grown in the country. India has three distinct cropping seasons:

*   **Rabi**
*   **Kharif**
*   **Zaid**

## Rabi Crops

*   **Sowing Season:** Winter (October to December)
*   **Harvesting Season:** Summer (April to June)
*   **Important Crops:** Wheat, barley, peas, gram, and mustard.
*   **Major Producing Regions:** Large parts of India, but particularly important in northern and north-western states such as Punjab, Haryana, Himachal Pradesh, Jammu and Kashmir, Uttarakhand, and Uttar Pradesh.
*   **Contributing Factors:**
    *   Availability of precipitation during winter months due to **western temperate cyclones** helps in the success of these crops.
    *   The success of the **Green Revolution** in Punjab, Haryana, western Uttar Pradesh, and parts of Rajasthan has also been a significant factor in the growth of these rabi crops.

## Kharif Crops

*   **Sowing Season:** With the onset of monsoon (different parts of the country)
*   **Harvesting Season:** September-October
*   **Important Crops:** Paddy (rice), maize, jowar, bajra, tur (arhar), moong, urad, cotton, jute, groundnut, and soyabean.
*   **Major Producing Regions:**
    *   Most important rice-growing regions: Assam, West Bengal, coastal regions of Odisha, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Tamil Nadu, Kerala, and Maharashtra (particularly the Konkan coast), along with Uttar Pradesh and Bihar.
    *   Recently, paddy has also become an important crop of Punjab and Haryana due to irrigation.
*   **Unique Feature:** In states like Assam, West Bengal, and Odisha, three crops of paddy are grown in a year: **Aus**, **Aman**, and **Boro**.

## Zaid Crops

*   **Season:** Short season during the summer months, in between the rabi and kharif seasons.
*   **Important Crops:** Watermelon, muskmelon, cucumber, various vegetables, and fodder crops.
*   **Special Note:** Sugarcane takes almost a year to grow, spanning across seasons.
