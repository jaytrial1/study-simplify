# Non-Food Crops

## Rubber

*   **Crop Type:** Equatorial crop; also grown under special conditions in tropical and sub-tropical areas.
*   **Geographical Conditions:**
    *   **Climate:** Moist and humid.
    *   **Rainfall:** More than 200 cm.
    *   **Temperature:** Above 25°C.
*   **Significance:** Important industrial raw material.
*   **Main Producing Regions:** Kerala, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Andaman and Nicobar Islands, and Garohills of Meghalaya.

## Fibre Crops

Cotton, jute, hemp, and natural silk are the four major fiber crops grown in India.
*   The first three (cotton, jute, hemp) are derived from crops grown in the soil.
*   Natural silk is obtained from cocoons of silkworms, which are fed on green leaves, especially mulberry.
*   The rearing of silkworms for the production of silk fiber is known as **sericulture**.

### Cotton

*   **Origin:** India is believed to be the original home of the cotton plant.
*   **Significance:** One of the main raw materials for the cotton textile industry.
*   **Global Rank:** India is the second largest producer of cotton after China.
*   **Geographical Conditions:**
    *   **Soil:** Grows well in drier parts of the **black cotton soil** of the Deccan plateau.
    *   **Temperature:** High.
    *   **Rainfall/Irrigation:** Light rainfall or irrigation.
    *   **Frost-free Days:** 210 frost-free days.
    *   **Sunshine:** Bright sunshine for its growth.
*   **Crop Type:** Kharif crop.
*   **Maturity Period:** Requires 6 to 8 months to mature.
*   **Major Producing States:** Maharashtra, Gujarat, Madhya Pradesh, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Tamil Nadu, Punjab, Haryana, and Uttar Pradesh.

### Jute

*   **Common Name:** Known as the **golden fibre**.
*   **Geographical Conditions:**
    *   **Soil:** Grows well on well-drained fertile soils in the flood plains where soils are renewed every year.
    *   **Temperature:** High temperature is required during the time of growth.
*   **Major Producing States:** West Bengal, Bihar, Assam, Odisha, and Meghalaya.
*   **Uses:** Used in making gunny bags, mats, ropes, yarn, carpets, and other artifacts.
