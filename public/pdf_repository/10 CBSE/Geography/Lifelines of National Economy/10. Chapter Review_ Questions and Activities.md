2.  Answer the following questions in about 30 words.
    (i) State any three merits of roadways.
    > **Answer:** Roadways offer door-to-door service, lower construction costs than railways, and can traverse dissected and undulating terrain. They are economical for short distances and provide feeder services to other transport modes.

    (ii) Where and why is rail transport the most convenient means of transportation?
    > **Answer:** Rail transport is most convenient in the northern plains due to vast level land, high population density, and rich agricultural resources, facilitating efficient movement of freight and passengers over long distances.

    (iii) What is the significance of the border roads?
    > **Answer:** Border roads are strategically important for national security and economic development in difficult border areas. They improve accessibility, facilitate troop movement, and integrate remote regions into the national economy.

    (iv) What is meant by trade? What is the difference between international and local trade?
    > **Answer:** Trade is the exchange of goods and services among people, states, or countries. *Local trade* occurs within cities, towns, or villages, while *international trade* involves exchange between two or more countries, typically via sea, air, or land routes.

3.  Answer the following questions in about 120 words.
    (i) Why are the means of transportation and communication called the lifelines of a nation and its economy?
    > **Answer:** Transportation and communication are called the lifelines of a nation and its economy because they are fundamental for development and integration. Transport facilitates the movement of raw materials from supply to demand locales, goods to markets, and people across regions, enabling production and consumption. Efficient transport networks like roads, railways, waterways, and airways reduce costs, save time, and connect diverse regions, fostering trade and economic growth. Communication systems like postal services, internet, radio, and television enable rapid information exchange, crucial for business, governance, and social awareness. They bridge geographical distances, promote national integration, and connect a country to the global economy, making them indispensable for socio-economic progress and prosperity.

    (ii) Write a note on the changing nature of the international trade in the last fifteen years.
    > **Answer:** Over the last fifteen years, international trade has undergone significant transformation, largely driven by globalization, technological advancements, and shifting economic powers. There's been a notable increase in the trade of services, particularly in information technology, finance, and business process outsourcing, with India emerging as a software giant. The rise of e-commerce platforms has also revolutionized global trade, making it accessible to smaller businesses and individual consumers. Supply chains have become more complex and globalized, leading to greater interdependence among nations. However, recent years have also seen a rise in protectionist tendencies and trade disputes, alongside a greater focus on resilient and diversified supply chains following global disruptions like the pandemic. Environmental and ethical considerations are also increasingly influencing trade policies and consumer preferences, leading to a demand for sustainably sourced goods.
