# Summary of Control and Coordination

*   **Control and coordination** in living organisms are primarily carried out by the **nervous system** and **hormones**.
*   Nervous system responses can be categorized as **reflex actions** (involuntary, rapid), **voluntary actions** (conscious control), or **involuntary actions** (automatic, unconscious).
*   The nervous system uses **electrical impulses** for rapid message transmission, receiving information from sense organs and acting through muscles.
*   **Chemical coordination** through hormones is observed in both plants and animals.
*   Hormones are produced in one part of an organism and transported to another part to achieve their specific effects.
*   The action of hormones is regulated by **feedback mechanisms**, ensuring precise timing and amount of secretion.
