This section highlights how the First World War stimulated industrial production in India and the subsequent shift in market dominance after the war.

# Industrial Growth During World War I and Post-War Developments

By the first decade of the twentieth century, several changes impacted the pattern of industrialisation in India:
*   **Swadeshi Movement:** As the Swadeshi movement gained momentum, nationalists mobilised people to boycott foreign cloth.
*   **Industrial Pressure:** Indian industrial groups organised to protect their collective interests, pressuring the government for increased tariff protection and other concessions.
*   **Decline in Yarn Exports:** From 1906, the export of Indian yarn to China declined as products from Chinese and Japanese mills flooded the Chinese market.

Consequently, Indian industrialists began shifting from yarn to cloth production. Cotton piece-goods production in India doubled between 1900 and 1912.

Despite these changes, industrial growth remained slow until the First World War. The war, however, created a dramatically new situation. With British mills preoccupied with war production for the army, Manchester imports into India declined significantly. Suddenly, Indian mills found a vast home market to supply. As the war prolonged, Indian factories were called upon to meet war needs, producing:
*   Jute bags
*   Cloth for army uniforms
*   Tents and leather boots
*   Horse and mule saddles
*   A host of other items

New factories were established, and existing ones ran multiple shifts. Many new workers were employed, and everyone was made to work longer hours. Industrial production boomed throughout the war years.

After the war, Manchester could never regain its former position in the Indian market. Unable to modernise and compete with the US, Germany, and Japan, Britain's economy struggled. Cotton production collapsed, and exports of cotton cloth from Britain fell dramatically. Within the colonies, local industrialists gradually consolidated their position, substituting foreign manufactures and capturing the home market.
