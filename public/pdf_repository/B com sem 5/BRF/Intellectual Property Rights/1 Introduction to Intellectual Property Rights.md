# Introduction to Intellectual Property Rights

Intellectual Property Rights (IPRs) are the legal rights granted to individuals who are the owners and inventors of a work, having created something with their intellectual creativity. Individuals in fields such as music, invention, and literature can be granted these rights, which they can then utilize in business practices.

The creator or inventor receives exclusive rights against any misuse or unauthorized use of their work. However, these rights are granted for a limited period to maintain balance.

The World Intellectual Property Organization (WIPO) outlines the following activities covered by intellectual property rights:

*   Scientific discoveries.
*   Industrial designs.
*   Protection against unfair competition.
*   Literary, artistic, and scientific works.
*   Trademarks, service marks, commercial names, and designations.
*   Inventions in all fields of human endeavor.
*   Performance of performing artists, phonograms, and broadcasts.
*   All other rights resulting from intellectual activity in the industrial, scientific, literary, or artistic fields.
