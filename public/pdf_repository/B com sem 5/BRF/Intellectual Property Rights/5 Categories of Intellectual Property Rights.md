5_Categories of Intellectual Property Rights

This section lists the seven categories of Intellectual Property Rights covered by the TRIPS Agreement and provides brief explanations for industrial designs, trade secrets, and geographical indications.

# Categories of Intellectual Property Rights

The TRIPS Agreement covers seven categories or types of Intellectual Property Rights, which are as follows:

1.  Patents
2.  Copyrights
3.  Trademarks
4.  Industrial Designs
5.  Trade Secrets (Know-how)
6.  Geographical Indications
7.  Integrated Circuits

## Industrial Designs

**Design** refers only to the features of shape, pattern, configuration, etc., produced by any industrial process. It is important to note that design does not mean the article itself; it refers to the features that form the conception or idea of a shape.

## Trade Secrets

A **trade secret** is information that is not generally available and that confers a competitive advantage on its possessor.

Examples include:

*   A manufacturing process.
*   A chemical formula.
*   A business method.
*   A machine design.
*   The formula of Coca-Cola.
*   Survey methods used by researchers.

## Geographical Indication

A **Geographical Indication** is a sign used on goods that have a specific geographical origin and possess qualities, reputation, or characteristics that are essentially attributable to that origin. Most commonly, a geographical indication includes the name of the place of origin of the goods. It may be registered in respect of goods.
