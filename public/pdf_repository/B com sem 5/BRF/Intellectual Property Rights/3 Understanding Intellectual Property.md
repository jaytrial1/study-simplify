# Understanding Intellectual Property

Every kind of intellectual property is by nature **intangible property**. Intellectual property is the creation of the **human intellect (mind)**. It refers to knowledge, ideas, inventions, innovations, creativity, and research—all products of the human mind, similar to any other property. It is an **intangible right** exercisable in respect of a tangible work. Furthermore, it is treated as **movable property** and can be assigned and transferred.

The main types of Intellectual Property are copyrights, trademarks, patents, and designs.

The basic features of Intellectual Property are:

*   Intellectual property is in the nature of **intangible property**.
*   It is a creation of the **human intellect (mind)**.
*   It is treated as a **movable property** and can be assigned and transferred.
*   The owner of Intellectual Property has **absolute and exclusive right** over it.
*   It exists **only under law**; it is a legal right.
