9_Chapter Summary

This section provides a concise summary of the key concepts covered in the chapter, including the meaning of intellectual property and intellectual property rights, types of IPRs, and brief overviews of patents, copyrights, and trademarks.

# Chapter Summary

## Intellectual Property Rights in India

*   The Copyright Act, 1957.
*   The Trademark Act, 1999.
*   The Patent Act, 1970.
*   The Designs Act, 2000.
*   Geographical Indications of Goods (Registration and Protection) Act, 1999.

## Meaning of Intellectual Property

Intellectual property is the creation of the human mind. It refers to knowledge, ideas, invention, research etc. It is an intangible right exercisable in respect of a tangible work.

## Meaning of Intellectual Property Rights

IPRs are the rights given to persons over the creations of their minds.

## Types of IPRs

1.  Patents
2.  Copyrights
3.  Trademarks
4.  Industrial Designs
5.  Trade Secrets (know-how)
6.  Geographical Indications
7.  Integrated Circuits.

## Patents

*   **Meaning**: A patent is a contract between the inventor and Government, whereby the inventor gets exclusive rights for a limited period in return for disclosing full details of the invention.
*   **Features of Patent**:
    1.  Patent must be in respect of invention.
    2.  Patent for respective/particular invention.
    3.  Patent may be for substance or process.
    4.  It grants exclusive right to the inventor for his creation/invention.
    5.  Patent registration can be filed individually or jointly.
    6.  Patent is a property right.
    7.  Clear specifications and claims.
    8.  General principles applicable to working of patented inventions.
*   **Remedies**:
    *   **Infringement**: Not defined under the Patent Act, but occurs when a third party uses patented rights without permission.
    *   Remedies available against patent infringement include administrative remedy, civil remedy (injunctions), damages, and accounts of profits.

## Copyright

*   **Meaning**: Copyright is the exclusive right to do or authorize others to do certain acts in relation to (i) literary, dramatic or music works, (ii) artistic work, (iii) cinematograph film and (iv) sound recording.
*   **What works are protected?** Copyright subsists in:
    *   Original literary, dramatic, musical and artistic works.
    *   Cinematograph films.
    *   Sound recording.
    *   Computer programmes.
*   **Ownership of Copyright**: The ownership of copyright vests with the author. Authors include:
    *   Author of the work.
    *   The Composer.
    *   The Artist.
    *   The person taking the photograph.
    *   The producer.
    *   The person who has collected and edited the work.
    *   Computer Programmer (Creator).
    *   The Paper Setter.
    *   First Owner (e.g., Government, International Organizations).
*   **Duration/Terms of Copyright**: Each work under the Copyright Act subsists for a particular term, generally 60 years from specific events (e.g., author's death, publication).
*   **Meaning of Fair Use**: Fair Use means reasonable use, defined under the Copyright Act for specific purposes like research, criticism, review, and reporting.

## Trademark

*   **Meaning**: A trademark is a marketable and commercial asset of the owner. It is a combination of pictures, colors, drawings, words, letters, numbers, etc. Section 2(1)(Zb) of the Trademark Act defines Trademark or Mark.
*   **Elements/Characteristics of a Trade Mark**:
    1.  It must be a mark of any manner.
    2.  It is a mark capable of being represented graphically.
    3.  It must be used in relation (connection) to goods.
    4.  It is a commercial asset of the owner.
    5.  The registered trademark is a valuable property.
    6.  It must have a distinguishable feature from the trademark of another.
    7.  It must show some nexus between the goods or services provided and the work.
    8.  It is available to the owner for perpetuity.
*   **Roles or Functions of Trademark**:
    1.  It provides for the mark of origin of goods or services.
    2.  It is a means of advertising the product.
    3.  It provides the feature of quality of the product.
    4.  It builds a brand image of goods or services on the mind of final users or consumers.
*   **Trademark Act of 1999**: The Trademark Act is a special Act aiming to protect trademarks from misuse, deception, and confusion.
