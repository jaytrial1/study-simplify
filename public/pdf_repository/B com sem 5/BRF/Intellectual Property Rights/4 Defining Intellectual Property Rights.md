# Defining Intellectual Property Rights

According to the World Trade Organization (WTO), "Intellectual Property Rights are the rights given to persons over the creations of their minds. They usually give the creator an exclusive right over the use of his/her creation for a certain period of time."

IPRs provide exclusive rights to the creator or inventor, encouraging more individuals to invest time, efforts, and money in such creations and innovations. They cover all areas of technology, property, patents, trademarks, designs, copyrights, and so on.
