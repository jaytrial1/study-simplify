# Introduction

This section introduces the concept of contract discharge, explaining how contractual obligations are brought to an end, freeing parties from their mutual commitments.

We have so far considered how a contract is formed and how the legal bond (vinculum juris) is created between the contracting parties. Now, we will examine how this tie is unfastened and how parties become free again. This process is commonly known as the "discharge" or "dissolution of contracts." Discharge of a contract brings an end to the agreement, and the parties are subsequently released from their mutual obligations. The most common way a contract is discharged is by performing one's part of the promise at the proper time and place. Other modes of discharge are discussed below.
