# Modes of Contract Discharge

This section outlines the various methods by which a contract can be discharged, leading to the termination of contractual obligations.

A contract may be discharged in any one of the following ways:
1.  By performance
2.  By mutual consent or agreement
3.  By subsequent impossibility
4.  By lapse of time
5.  By operation of law
6.  By breach of contract

Let us discuss each of them under a separate head.
