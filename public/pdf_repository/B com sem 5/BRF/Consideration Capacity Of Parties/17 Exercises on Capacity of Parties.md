17_Exercises on Capacity of Parties

This section presents a collection of questions and problems designed to test understanding of the capacity of parties to enter into contracts, particularly focusing on minors and persons of unsound mind.

# Exercises on Capacity of Parties

1.  What do you understand by 'capacity to contract'? What is the effect of any agreement made by persons not qualified to contract?
2.  Who are parties competent to contract? Discuss fully.
3.  Discuss with suitable illustrations the validity of contracts by minors. Explain the meaning of 'age of majority'. Discuss the legal validity and effects of a minor's agreement.
4.  Discuss the law relating contracts by persons of unsound mind.
5.  Write a note on the contractual capacity of:
    (a) Aliens, (b) Foreign Sovereigns, (c) Married women, (d) Corporations, (e) Lunatics.
6.  Discuss in brief:
    (1) All agreements with minors are void.
    (2) Competent parties.
    (3) A minor on attaining majority cannot adopt or ratify an agreement entered into while he was a minor.
    (4) Contract made with minor is void.
7.  A minor agrees to mortgage his property and receives Rs. 8,000 as earnest money. Thereafter he does not execute the mortgage deed. The mortgagee wants the refund of Rs. 8,000. Will he succeed?
8.  **True or False:**
    (1) An agreement with a minor is voidable.
    (2) Agreement with a minor is valid.
9.  Explain the law related to agreements with the Minors.
10. Which parties are not competent for contract? State the Provisions of Indian Contract Act regarding contract with Minor.
11. Define minor and discuss the Provision relating agreements by and with minor.
12. Which parties are not competent for contract?
13. "An Agreement with a minor is" Explain this statement on the basis of the law related to Agreements with and by the Minor.
14. Give a list of person who are disqualified because of his status.
15. What is competence of parties? Write a note on a person who is not competence for contract due to this status.
16. Define competent party. Discuss the law relating agreements with and by the minor.
17. In which circumstances a party is said to be discharged from the contract by impossibility.
18. Answer the following by selecting the correct alternative among those given below:
    (1) An agreement with a minor is -
    (a) void ab initio
    (b) voidable agreement
    (c) unenforceable agreement
19. (A) State provisions regarding minor, under the Indian Contract Act.
    (B) Define Adult.
20. Who can be called as a person of unsound mind? Discuss his capacity to make contract.
21. Who can make contract?
22. Discuss the minor's capacity to make Contract as per Indian Contract Act.
23. Discuss in brief the provisions of Indian Contract Act regarding contract with Minor.
