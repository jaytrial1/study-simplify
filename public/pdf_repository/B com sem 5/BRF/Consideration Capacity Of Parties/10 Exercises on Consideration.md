10_Exercises on Consideration

This section lists various questions and problems related to the concept of consideration, serving as practice material for learners.

# Exercises on Consideration

1.  Define consideration and discuss the essential elements of consideration.
2.  Define consideration and point out the differences between English Law and Indian Law in this respect.
3.  Explain the term "Consideration" and state the exception to the rule "No consideration, no contract."
4.  "A contract without consideration is void" Discuss this statement with exceptions to the same.
5.  Discuss the rule that a stranger to a contract cannot sue on the contract and the exceptions to the rule.
6.  Define consideration. Discuss its essential factors.
7.  Discuss:
    (a) "Past consideration is no consideration."
    (b) "The law will not enforce a promise given for nothing."
    (c) "Insufficiency of consideration is immaterial but an agreement without consideration is void."
    (d) "Though consideration need not be adequate, it must be real (Anson)."
    (e) "Consideration must move from the promisee," Explain with reference to English law and India law.
    (f) "A stranger to the consideration may sue on a contract but not a stranger to the contract."
    (g) Different types of the consideration.
    (h) Consideration.
    (i) Consideration is essential for validity of contract.
    (j) Consideration is foundation of a valid contract.
8.  **Problem:**
    (1) A agrees with B to sell his horse worth Rs. 1,000 for Rs. 10 only. Is this agreement valid? Discuss.
9.  Write short notes on:
    (1) Exceptions of agreements without Consideration.
    (2) Legal rules for valid consideration.
10. **True or False:**
    (1) Agreements without consideration are void. To this rule there are no exceptions.
    (2) Consideration must move at the desire of promisee.
    (3) There are no exceptions to the rule that agreement without consideration is void.
    (4) Consideration must be adequate.
    (5) Consideration need not be adequate.
11. Answer the following by selecting the correct alternative among those given below:
    (1) In a contract, consideration must be fulfilled by -
    (a) the promisor
    (b) the promisee
    (c) by the promisor as well as promisee.
12. Define Consideration. "Contract made without consideration is void" Explain the statement with its exceptions.
13. Answer in brief:
    (1) Define 'Consideration'.
    (2) What is meant by consideration?
