9_Summary of Consideration Principles

This section provides a concise summary of the definition of consideration, its essential elements, and the key exceptions to the rule that a contract requires consideration.

# Summary of Consideration Principles

## Consideration

### Definition: Section 2(d)

1.  When at the desire of the promisor,
2.  The promisee or any other person,
3.  (a) has done or abstained from doing, or
    (b) does or abstains from doing, or
    (c) promises to do or abstain from doing
4.  something.
5.  An act, abstinence or promise is called a consideration for the promise.

### 'No Consideration, No Contract' Exceptions: Section 25

(i) **Natural Love and Affection**
    (a) parties standing in near relation.
    (b) out of natural love and affection.
    (c) contract reduced to writing and registered.
(ii) **Compensation for Voluntary Service.**
    (a) for gratuitous or voluntary services.
    (b) legally compellable duty.
(iii) **Promise to pay time-barred debt**
    (a) a promise made by a person or his agent.
    (b) which is in writing and signed by him.
    (c) to pay a time-barred debt.
(iv) **Agency** (Sec. 185)
(v) **Completed Gift** (Expl. (1) Sec. 25)
