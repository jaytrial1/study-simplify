5_Summary of Unlawful Agreements

This section provides a concise summary of the conditions under which consideration is deemed illegal and lists various agreements considered to be against public policy.

# Summary of Unlawful Agreements

### Consideration, When Illegal?

1.  Is forbidden by law.
2.  Is of such a nature that, if permitted, would defeat the provisions of any law.
3.  Is fraudulent.
4.  Involves or implies injury to the person or property of another.
5.  Is regarded by court as immoral or opposed to public policy.

### Agreements Against Public Policy:

1.  Trading with an enemy.
2.  Interfering with the administration of justice.
3.  Trafficking in public offices.
4.  Creating interest opposed to duty.
5.  In restraint of parental rights.
6.  Interfering with marital duties.
7.  Marriage brokage agreements.
8.  Restraining personal liberty.
9.  In fraud of creditors.
10. Tending to create monopoly.
