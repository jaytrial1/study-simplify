4_Consequences of Illegality

This section explains the legal ramifications of an agreement being deemed illegal, emphasizing that such contracts are generally void and unenforceable.

# Consequences of Illegality

The general effect or consequence of illegality is to render the contract *void and unenforceable*. The legal maxim "Ex turpi causa non oritur actio" (out of a base cause no action arises) applies, meaning no court will assist a party whose cause of action is founded upon an immoral or illegal act.

Therefore, in the case of a strictly illegal contract:
*   No action would lie.
*   Property transferred cannot be recovered.
*   Money paid cannot be reclaimed.
*   Services rendered need not be paid for.
