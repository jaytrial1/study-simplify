15_Practice Questions and Exercises

This section provides a series of practice questions designed to test understanding of the concepts covered in the chapter on legality of object and consideration, and performance of contracts.

# Practice Questions and Exercises

1.  Explain in what cases the consideration and object of an agreement are said to be unlawful.
2.  Enumerate the various kinds of illegal objects which may vitiate a contract. What is the effect of illegality of objects?
3.  A contract shall not be enforced if the court regards it as opposed to public policy. Mention some of the agreements which are opposed to public policy.
4.  Write short notes on:
    (a) Maintenance and Champerty
    (b) Stifling prosecution
    (c) Marriage brokage contracts
    (d) Public Policy
5.  Rekha joins service. One of the conditions of her contract of service is that she will have to leave the service if she marries. She gets married. Can she be legally terminated?
6.  Define consideration. Discuss its essential factors.
7.  "The contract opposed to public policy is illegal." Discuss this statement.
8.  Which objects are unlawful? Discuss the agreements opposed to public policies.
9.  When can the consideration and objects of the contract be treated as illegal? Explain.
10. Discuss in brief the agreements against public policy.
11. What do you mean by performance of a contract? Under what circumstances a contract need not be performed?
12. Explain the term 'tender'. What are the essentials of a valid tender?
13. What are the rules laid down in the Act as to the devolution of joint rights and liabilities?
14. State in brief the provisions of the Contract Act relating to the time and place of the performance of a contract.
15. What are reciprocal promises? Discuss the provisions in the Indian Contract Act relating to the performance of reciprocal promises.
16. State the rules regarding appropriation of payments.
17. (a) What is Tender? Discuss the essential factors of the Tender.
    (b) Short note:
    (i) Clayton's Law on Appropriation of Payment.
    (ii) Anticipatory breach of contract.
    (iii) State the persons who are entitled to perform the contract.
    (iv) Devolution of joint rights and liabilities.
18. Discuss:
    (a) By whom a contract must be performed?
    (b) Can a contract be assigned? If so, how?
    (c) Essentials of a valid offer of performance.
    (d) Indian Contract Act contains no provisions relating to appropriation of payments where a debtor owing several distinct debts to one person makes a payment to him.
19. Write short notes:
    (1) Rules as regards appropriation of payment. (Clayton's case)
    (2) Mention the requisites of valid tender.
    (3) Clayton's Law on Appropriation of Payment.
20. Answer in brief:
    (1) What is Tender?
    (2) What do you mean by performance of a contract?
    (3) Who can perform a contract?
    (4) What is meant by Tender?
