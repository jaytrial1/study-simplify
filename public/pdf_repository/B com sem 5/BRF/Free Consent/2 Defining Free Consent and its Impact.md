2_Defining Free Consent and its Impact

This section elaborates on the concept of "free consent" by listing the factors that can vitiate it, and explains the critical distinction between void and voidable contracts when consent is not freely given.

# Defining Free Consent and its Impact

According to Section 14, "Consent is said to be free when it is not caused by:
*   Coercion, as defined in Section 15, or
*   Undue influence, as defined in Section 16, or
*   Fraud, as defined in Section 17, or
*   Misrepresentation, as defined in Section 18, or
*   Mistake, subject to provisions of Sections 20, 21, and 22.

Consent is considered to be "so caused" if it would not have been given *but for* the existence of such coercion, undue influence, fraud, misrepresentation, or mistake.

## Effect When Consent is Not Free

A crucial distinction exists between a *mistake* vitiating a contract and the other grounds (coercion, undue influence, fraud, and misrepresentation).
*   In the case of a **mistake**, the contract is **void** from the beginning. Ownership of goods will not pass, even if they change hands, and the true owner can recover the property.
*   In other cases (coercion, undue influence, fraud, misrepresentation), the contract is only **voidable**. This means the contract is valid until the aggrieved party chooses to set it aside. If a third party acquires rights in the subject matter before the contract is set aside, ownership would pass to them.

> **Answer:** Free consent means that the agreement was not caused by coercion, undue influence, fraud, misrepresentation, or mistake. If consent is caused by any of these factors, it is not free.

> **Answer:** When consent is not free, the contract is generally **voidable** at the option of the party whose consent was not free (e.g., in cases of coercion, undue influence, fraud, or misrepresentation). This means the contract is valid until that party chooses to set it aside. However, if consent is vitiated by certain types of **mistake**, the contract is considered **void** from the outset, meaning it never existed in the eyes of the law.
