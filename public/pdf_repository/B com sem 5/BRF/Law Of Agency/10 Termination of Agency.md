# Termination of Agency

An agency relationship, like other contracts, can be terminated by the actions of the parties involved or by operation of law.

*   **Q:** Describe the various modes by which an agency may be terminated.
*   **Q:** Termination of agency (1) by act of the parties (2) by operation of law.
*   **Q:** When agency can be terminated?

> **Answer:** An agency can be terminated by:
> **1. Act of the Parties:**
>    *   **Agreement:** By mutual consent of the principal and agent.
>    *   **Revocation by the Principal:** The principal can revoke the agent's authority at any time before the authority has been exercised to bind the principal. However, if authority has been partly exercised, it cannot be revoked regarding acts already done (Sec. 204). Reasonable notice must be given (Sec. 206), and compensation may be due for premature revocation without sufficient cause (Sec. 205).
>    *   **Renunciation by the Agent:** The agent can renounce the agency by serving proper and reasonable notice to the principal. Compensation may be due for renunciation without sufficient cause (Sec. 205).
> **2. Operation of Law:**
>    *   **Performance of the contract:** When the business of the agency is completed.
>    *   **Efflux of Time:** If the agency was for a fixed period, it terminates upon the expiry of that period.
>    *   **Death or Insanity:** The death or unsound mind of either the principal or the agent terminates the agency (Sec. 201).
>    *   **Insolvency of the Principal:** The principal being adjudicated insolvent terminates the agency (Sec. 201). (Insolvency of the agent does not necessarily terminate it).
>    *   **Destruction of the Subject-Matter:** If the subject matter of the agency is destroyed.
>    *   **Principal Becoming an Alien Enemy:** If war breaks out between the countries of the principal and agent.
>    *   **Dissolution of a Company:** If the principal is a company and it is dissolved.
>    *   **Termination of Sub-agent's Authority:** A sub-agent's authority automatically terminates when the original agent's authority ends (Sec. 210).
>
> The termination of an agent's authority takes effect for the agent when it becomes known to them, and for third persons when it becomes known to them (Sec. 208).

"An agency is terminated by the principal revoking his authority; or by the agent renouncing the business of the agency; or by the business of the agency being completed; or by either the principal or agent dying or becoming of unsound mind; or by the principal being adjudicated an insolvent under the provisions of any Act for the time being in force for the relief of insolvent debtors." (Sec. 201).

## Termination of Agency by Act of the Parties

1.  **Agreement:** Just as agency is established by agreement, it can be terminated by mutual agreement.
2.  **Revocation by the principal:** The principal can revoke the agent's authority at any time before it has been exercised to bind the principal.
    *   "The principal cannot revoke the authority given to his agent after the authority has been partly exercised, so far as regards such acts and obligations as arise from acts already done in the agency." (Sec. 204).

    **Example:** A authorizes B to buy 1,000 bales of cotton and pay from A's money. B buys the cotton in his own name, making himself personally liable. A cannot revoke B's authority regarding payment for the cotton.
3.  **Renunciation by the Agent:** An agent can revoke and terminate the agency by serving the principal with proper and reasonable notice.

    *   **Compensation for revocation:** "Where there is an express or implied contract that the agency should be continued for any period of time, the principal must make compensation to the agent, or the agent to the principal, as the case may be, for any previous revocation or renunciation of the agency without sufficient cause" (Sec. 205).
    *   **Reasonable Notice:** "Reasonable notice must be given of such revocation or renunciation; otherwise the damage thereby must be made good to the one by the other." (Sec. 206).
    *   **Revocation: Express or implied:** "Revocation and renunciation may be express or may be implied in the conduct of the principal or agent respectively." (Sec. 207).

## Termination of Agency by Operation of Law

1.  **Performance of the contract:** When the transaction for which the agency was created is completed, the agency terminates.
2.  **Efflux of Time:** If the agency was for a fixed period, it terminates upon the expiry of that time, even if the work is not completed.

    **Example:** An agent appointed to sell a house within six months finds the agency ends after six months, even if the sale isn't effected.
3.  **Death and Insanity:** The death or insanity of either the principal or the agent terminates the agency.
4.  **Insolvency:** The insolvency of the principal terminates the agency. However, the insolvency of the agent does not necessarily terminate the agency.
5.  **Destruction of the subject-matter:** If the specific subject matter of the agency (e.g., a horse to be sold, a house) is destroyed, the agency is necessarily terminated.
6.  **Principal becoming an alien enemy:** If the principal and agent belong to different countries and war breaks out between them, the contract of agency is terminated.
7.  **Dissolution of a company:** If the principal is a company, the agency ends as soon as the company is dissolved.
8.  **Termination of sub-agent's authority:** "As soon as the agent's authority is terminated, the sub-agent's authority is automatically terminated." (Sec. 210).

### When Termination of Agent's Authority Takes Effect

"The termination of the authority of an agent does not, so far as regards the agent, take effect before it becomes known to him, or, so far as regards third persons, before it becomes known to them." (Section 208).

**Example:** A directs B to sell goods for him, agreeing to give B 5% commission. A later revokes B's authority by letter. Before receiving the letter, B sells the goods for 100 rupees. The sale is binding on A, and B is entitled to his 5% commission.
