# Distinctions from Other Legal Roles

It is important to differentiate an agent from other roles such as a servant or an independent contractor, as their legal implications vary significantly.

*   **Q:** State difference between: Agent, Servant and Independent Contractor.

## Agent Differs from Servant

| Aspect                 | Agent                                                                                                     | Servant                                                                   |
| :--------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1. Authority           | Agent has authority to bind his Principal.                                                                | Servant does not have such authority.                                     |
| 2. Contractual Relations | Agent creates contractual relations between principal and third party.                                    | No such authority.                                                        |
| 3. Control             | Agent is not under direct control of his principal regarding the mode of work.                            | Servant works under the direct control of his master, including how work is done. |
| 4. Remuneration        | Agent is paid commission for the services he renders to the principal.                                    | Servant is paid in the form of salary.                                    |
| 5. Wrongs              | Principal is liable for the wrongs of his agents if they are done within the *scope of his authority*.    | A master is liable for the wrongs of his servant if they are committed in the ordinary course of servant's employment. |
| 6. Employment          | An agent may work for several principals at the same time.                                                | Servant usually serves only one master.                                   |

## Agent Differs from Independent Contractor

| Aspect                 | Agent                                                                                                     | Independent Contractor                                                               |
| :--------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1. Instructions        | An agent acts according to the instructions of the principal.                                             | Independent contractor exercises their own discretion as to the mode and time of doing work. |
| 2. Representation      | An agent represents his principal in dealings with third persons.                                         | An independent contractor does not represent their employer in dealings with other persons. |
| 3. Personal Liability  | He is not personally liable provided he works within his authority.                                       | He is personally liable for all acts done by him.                                    |
| 4. Remuneration        | He usually gets commission.                                                                               | He usually gets a fixed contracted amount.                                           |
