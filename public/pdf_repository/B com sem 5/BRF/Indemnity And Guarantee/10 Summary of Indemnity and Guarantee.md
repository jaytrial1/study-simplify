10_Summary of Indemnity and Guarantee

This section provides a concise overview of the key concepts discussed throughout the chapter, summarizing definitions, rights, liabilities, and discharge conditions for both contracts of indemnity and guarantee.

# Summary of Indemnity and Guarantee

## Indemnity and Guarantee

### Contract of Indemnity

*   **Definition:** A promise to save another from loss caused by the conduct of the promisor himself, or by the conduct of any other person (Section 124).
*   **Rights of the Indemnity-Holder (when sued):** The indemnity-holder may recover:
    *   Damages compelled to pay.
    *   Costs incurred in defending a suit.
    *   Sums paid by way of compromise.

### Contract of Guarantee

*   **Definition:** A contract to perform the promise or discharge the liability of a third person in case of his default (Section 126).
*   **Purpose:** May be given for:
    *   Repayment of a debt.
    *   Price of goods sold on credit.
    *   Good conduct or honesty of a person employed (fidelity guarantee).

### Continuing Guarantee

*   **Definition:** A guarantee which extends to a series of transactions (Section 129).
*   **Revocation of Continuing Guarantee:**
    1.  By notice from the surety to the creditor (Section 130).
    2.  By death of the surety (Section 131).

### Nature of the Surety's Liability

*   "The liability of the surety is co-extensive with that of the principal debtor" (Section 128).
*   The surety is liable for what the principal debtor is liable.
*   It is not necessary to sue the principal debtor first.
*   The creditor can pursue remedies concurrently against both the principal debtor and the surety.

### Rights of the Surety

1.  **Against the Principal Debtor:**
    *   Right of subrogation (Section 140): Surety steps into the shoes of the creditor upon payment.
    *   Right of indemnity (Section 145): Principal debtor implicitly promises to indemnify the surety for rightful payments.
2.  **Against the Creditor:**
    *   Before payment, can call upon the principal debtor to pay.
    *   On payment of debt, entitled to all securities held by the creditor (Section 141).
3.  **Against Co-sureties:**
    *   Co-sureties are liable to contribute equally (Section 146).
    *   Co-sureties bound in different sums are liable to pay equally within the limits of their respective obligations (Section 147).
    *   Release of one co-surety by the creditor does not discharge others (Section 138).

### Discharge of Surety

1.  **By Revocation:**
    *   By surety (for future transactions, Section 130).
    *   By death of surety (for future transactions, Section 131).
    *   By novation (Section 62).
2.  **By Conduct of the Creditor:**
    *   Variance in terms of contract without surety's consent (Section 133).
    *   Release or discharge of principal debtor (Section 134).
    *   Compounding with principal debtor, or promising to give time/not to sue (Section 135).
    *   Creditor's act or omission impairing surety's eventual remedy (Section 139).
    *   Loss of security (Section 141).
3.  **By Invalidation of Contract:**
    *   Guarantee obtained by misrepresentation (Section 142) or concealment (Section 143).
    *   Failure of a co-surety to join as a surety (Section 144).
    *   Failure of consideration.

### Surety, When Not Discharged

1.  Mere agreement with a third person to give time to the principal debtor (Section 136).
2.  Mere forbearance on the part of the creditor to sue (Section 137).
3.  Release of one co-surety by the creditor (Section 138).
