# Introduction to Special Contracts

We have so far discussed the principles applicable to contracts in general. We shall now take up certain special contracts, viz. contracts of indemnity and guarantee, bailment and agency. These are all contracts to which the general principles so far discussed relating to the formation and discharge of contracts are fully applicable. But there are some special features in regard to these contracts and certain special rules or principles have to be enunciated with reference to them, over and above those which have been already discussed.

These provisions are contained in Sections 76 to 238 of the Indian Contract Act. The three special contracts covered are as follows:

*   **Contract of Indemnity and Guarantee** (Sections 124 to 147)
*   **Contract of Bailment** (Sections 148 to 181)
*   **Contract of Agency** (Sections 182 to 238)

In this chapter, we shall discuss contracts of indemnity and guarantee. In simple words, a *contract of indemnity* is one where one person promises to compensate another person if they suffer some loss due to the behavior of the promisor or a third person. In contrast, a *contract of guarantee* involves one person promising another that if a third person (the principal debtor) fails to pay a certain sum of money or perform a promise, they will do so.
