6_Understanding Acceptance

This section defines acceptance as the offeree's agreement to the terms of a proposal, explaining how it can be expressed and its crucial role in forming a legally binding agreement.

# The Concept of Acceptance

An agreement is formed when an offer is accepted. An offer itself does not create legal rights until it is accepted. *Acceptance* is the offeree's expression of willingness to be bound by the terms of the offer.

Section 2(b) states: "The proposal is said to be accepted when the person to whom the proposal is made signifies his assent thereto. A proposal when accepted becomes a promise."

Acceptance can be expressed in the following ways:
1.  By express spoken words.
2.  In writing.
3.  By conduct.

**Examples:**
1.  When A asks B, "Will you buy this car for Rs. 20,000?" and B says 'Yes', this is an express oral acceptance.
2.  If A writes a letter with the offer and B replies in the affirmative in writing, this is an express written acceptance.
3.  A widow promised to settle immovable property on her niece if the niece stayed with her. The niece stayed with her until her death. Held, the niece was entitled to the property as there was an implied acceptance by her conduct. (V. Rao Vs. A. Rao (1916) 39 Mad. 509)

## Effect of Acceptance

An offer alone or an acceptance alone is 'inactive', 'inert', and 'ineffective' in forming a contract. A legal right is created only when there is a lawful offer by one party and its lawful acceptance by another.

Anson famously compared acceptance: "Acceptance is to offer what a lighted match is to a train of gun-powder. It produces something which cannot be recalled or undone. But the power may have lain till it has become damp or the man who laid the train may remove it before the match is applied. So, an offer may lapse for want of acceptance or be revoked before acceptance. Acceptance converts the offer into a promise and then it is too late to remove it."

*   **Q:** Define: Acceptance
*   **Q:** What is acceptance?

> **Answer:** Acceptance is the act by which the person to whom a proposal is made signifies their assent to that proposal. It is the offeree's expression of willingness to be bound by the terms of the offer, transforming the proposal into a promise and forming the basis of a contract.
