12_Summary of Contractual Elements

This section provides a concise overview of the legal rules and conditions pertaining to proposals, their lapse, and the various aspects of acceptance and communication, summarizing the key points of the chapter.

# Summary Chart

## A) Proposal (Offer) [Sec. 2(a)]

### Legal Requirements:
1.  Capable of creating a legal relationship.
2.  Must be definite and certain.
3.  Must be communicated.
4.  May be conditional.
5.  May be general or specific.
6.  Made with a view to obtaining assent.
7.  Must not thrust the burden of acceptance.

### Lapse of Offer:
1.  By notice of revocation.
2.  By lapse of time (stipulated or reasonable).
3.  By failure to fulfill a condition precedent.
4.  By death or insanity of the proposer (if known before acceptance).
5.  By counter-offer.
6.  By rejection.

## B) Acceptance [Sec. 2(b)]

### Essential Elements:
1.  Must be absolute and unqualified.
2.  Expressed in some usual manner.
3.  By the party named in the offer (or any person for general offers).
4.  Communicated to the proposer:
    *   **a) By express conduct:**
        *   By performance of condition.
        *   By acceptance of consideration.
        *   By accepting a benefit or service.
    *   **b) By implied conduct.**
    *   **c) Waiver of the communication by the offerer.**
    *   **d) Acceptance by post** (complete against proposer upon posting).
5.  Must be within a reasonable time.
6.  Cannot be in ignorance of the offer.

## C) Communication

### Rule 1: Communication of offer, when complete:
*   When it comes to the knowledge of the person to whom it is made.

### Rule 2: Communication of acceptance, when complete:
*   **a) As against the proposer:** When it is put in a course of transmission to him so as to be out of the power of the acceptor.
*   **b) As against the acceptor:** When it comes to the knowledge of the proposer.

### Rule 3: Communication of revocation, when complete:
*   **a) As against the person who makes it:** When it is put into a course of transmission to the person to whom it is made, so as to be out of the power of the person who makes it.
*   **b) As against the person to whom it is made:** When it comes to his knowledge.

### Rule 4: Revocation of a proposal:
*   It may be revoked at any time before the communication of its acceptance is complete as against the proposer, but not afterwards.

### Rule 5: Revocation of an acceptance:
*   It may be revoked at any time before the communication of the acceptance is complete as against the acceptor but not afterwards.
