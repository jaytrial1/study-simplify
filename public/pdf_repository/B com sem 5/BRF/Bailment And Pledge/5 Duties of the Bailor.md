5_Duties of the Bailor

This section outlines the responsibilities and obligations of the bailor towards the bailee and the bailed goods, including disclosing faults and covering expenses.

# Duties of the Bailor

### 1. Duty to Disclose Fault in Goods Bailed
*Section 150* states: "The bailor is bound to disclose to the bailee faults in the goods bailed, of which the bailor is aware, and which materially interfere with the use of them, or expose the bailee to extraordinary risks; and if he does not make such disclosure, he is responsible for damage arising to the bailee directly from such faults."

**Example:**
A lends a horse, which he knows to be vicious, to B. A does not disclose this fact. The horse runs away, and B is thrown and injured. A is responsible to B for the sustained damage.

If the goods are bailed for hire, the bailor is responsible for such damage, whether they were aware of the faults or not (Section 150).

**Example:**
A hires a carriage from B. The carriage is unsafe, though B is not aware of it, and A is injured. B is responsible to A for the injury.

### 2. To Bear Extraordinary Expenses of Bailment
*Section 158* states: "Where, by the conditions of the bailment the goods are to be kept or to be carried, or to have work done upon them by bailee for the bailor, and the bailee is to receive no remuneration, the bailor shall repay to the bailee the necessary expenses incurred by him for the purpose of the bailment."

**Example:**
If a horse is lent for a journey, the ordinary and reasonable expenses of feeding the horse would be payable by the bailee. However, if the horse becomes sick and extraordinary expenses are incurred for its treatment, or if the horse is stolen and recovery expenses are incurred, the bailor should pay those expenses.

### 3. To Indemnify the Bailee
*Section 164* states: "The bailor is responsible to the bailee for any loss which the bailee may sustain by reason that the bailor was not entitled to make the bailment, or to receive back the goods, or to give directions respecting them."

**Example:**
A gives B's car to C for use without B's knowledge or permission. B sues C and receives compensation. C is entitled to recover his losses from A.

**Q: What are the duties of the bailor?**
> **Answer:** The duties of the bailor include: disclosing known faults in the goods that could interfere with their use or pose extraordinary risks, bearing extraordinary expenses incurred by the bailee for the purpose of the bailment (especially in gratuitous bailments), and indemnifying the bailee for any loss sustained due to the bailor's lack of title or authority to bail the goods or give directions.

**Q: Explain the duties of Bailor.**
> **Answer:** The bailor has several key duties:
> 1.  **Duty to disclose faults:** The bailor must inform the bailee of any known defects in the goods that could cause harm or pose unusual risks. If the bailment is for hire, the bailor is liable for defects even if unknown.
> 2.  **Duty to bear extraordinary expenses:** In gratuitous bailments, the bailor must reimburse the bailee for necessary and extraordinary expenses incurred for the purpose of the bailment.
> 3.  **Duty to indemnify the bailee:** The bailor must compensate the bailee for any loss the bailee suffers if the bailor did not have the right to make the bailment, receive the goods back, or give directions concerning them.
