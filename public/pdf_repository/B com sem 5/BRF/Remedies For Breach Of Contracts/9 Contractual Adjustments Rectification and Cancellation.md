9_Contractual Adjustments: Rectification and Cancellation

This section covers remedies that involve adjusting or nullifying a contract: rectification, which corrects errors in a written agreement to reflect original intent, and cancellation, which declares a contract null and void.

# Contractual Adjustments: Rectification and Cancellation

Two further reliefs available are:

1.  To have the contract *rectified*.
2.  To have it declared a *nullity* or *cancelled*.

If the Court finds that the written contract does not express the parties' original intention and they are willing to rectify it, the Court usually orders *rectification*. If rectification is not possible, the Court may declare the contract null and void and cancel it.
*   **Example:** A court ordered the rectification of a contract containing "549 L. for 4,50,000 Gallons" by adding words to read "549 L. per annum for a supply of 4,50,000 Gallons per diem," to reflect the true agreement.
