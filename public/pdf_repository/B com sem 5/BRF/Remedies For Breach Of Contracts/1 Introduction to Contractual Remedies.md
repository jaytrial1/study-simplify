# Introduction to Contractual Remedies

Parties to a contract are expected to fulfill their respective promises. When one party fails to carry out their promise, it constitutes a *breach of contract*.

In such an event, the aggrieved party is entitled to claim *compensation* for the breach. Section 73 of the relevant act outlines the quantum of damages, aiming to compensate the loss suffered by the injured party rather than penalize the breaching party.
